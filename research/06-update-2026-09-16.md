# WoW: Forever — Update, Sept 15–16, 2026

Covers Sept 15–16, 2026 (research run on Sept 16, one day before beta opens). Baseline is
**everything already in this repo through `06-since-announcement.md` (dated Sept 14)**, not just
the Sept 12 announcement — that file already absorbed the Deep Dive, the Hardcore panel, and six
developer interviews through Sept 14. This file only adds what is new since then. 24 sources.

No source found reports the beta being delayed, rescheduled, or the Nov 4 launch date changing.

## What changed since Sept 14

### Official announcements

- **The Sept 17 dev Q&A is two segments, not one.** Blizzard's own event page splits it: "Thursday,
  September 17 at 10:00 a.m. PDT for modern WoW, and 10:30 a.m. PDT for WoW Forever." The Sept 12/14
  notes in `01-official-facts.md` only recorded one 10:00 a.m. PDT slot for the whole thing. (Blizzard,
  "Watch the World of Warcraft Live Q&A September 17," https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302544/,
  read Sept 16)

### Beta/PTR and datamining

- **Beta-adjacent data is already circulating a day before public beta opens.** Wowhead published
  "New Forsaken Paladin Mounts Datamined from Forever Beta Client" — the headline implies press/partner
  builds of the beta client are already out and being mined, ahead of the Sept 17 public opt-in opening.
  Full article body was not retrievable (paywall/JS), so mount specifics are unconfirmed. (Wowhead,
  https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/new-forsaken-paladin-mounts-datamined-from-forever-beta-client-382940,
  ~Sept 15–16)
- **Talent scope, with numbers for the first time:** across 27 trees, 470 total talents, 129 are newly
  added and 231 were rewritten from their Classic Era version. Warlock changed hardest (only 3 of 52
  talents untouched). Hunter gets new spells Sniper Shot, Strider Kick, Summon Hawk and new talents
  Rapid Recuperation, Lacerating Strikes, Expose Prey, Lone Wolf (Marksmanship can now play petless).
  Warrior gets Spearing Strike plus new talents Bloodthrill, Improved Slam (Arms), Vanguard (Charge in
  Defensive Stance), Improved Shield Wall cooldown reduction. This is a real number where the prior
  notes only had qualitative "extensive talent revamp." (PC Gamer, "Talent calculators have already
  been assembled for World of Warcraft: Forever, and there's some real interesting stuff in here,"
  https://www.pcgamer.com/games/world-of-warcraft/talent-calculators-have-already-been-assembled-for-world-of-warcraft-forever-and-theres-some-real-interesting-stuff-in-here/,
  on or before Sept 16 — community, transcribed from fan calculators, not Blizzard)

### Class, systems and ruleset details

- **Dungeon leveling is being deliberately nerfed to fight instance-spam boosting.** Kill XP from
  dungeon mobs is being "drastically lowered," while dungeon quest XP is being made "much, much, much
  higher." Intent, per the devs: your first clear of a dungeon should feel amazing, but re-running the
  same dungeon for XP becomes a net loss versus questing in the open world. This is a new, specific,
  and contested system detail not in the prior passes. (Warcraft Tavern, "Dungeon Mob XP Drastically
  Lowered in WoW Forever," https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/dungeon-mob-xp-drastically-lowered-in-wow-forever/;
  Icy Veins, "WoW Forever Cuts Dungeon Kill XP but Makes Dungeon Quests More Rewarding,"
  https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/wow-forever-cuts-dungeon-kill-xp-but-makes-dungeon-quests-more-rewarding/;
  Massively OP, "...aims to combat dungeon boosting, says 'no plans' for WoW Tokens," Sept 14,
  https://massivelyop.com/2026/09/14/world-of-warcraft-forever-aims-to-combat-dungeon-boosting-says-no-plans-for-wow-tokens/;
  all Sept 14 — community)
- **No WoW Token, no character boosts, confirmed on the record.** Associate Production Director
  Clayton Stone, in an interview with Destin Legarie: "no plans for anything like a WoW token or a
  boost" in any shop, because "the journey really is what the game is." (Wowhead, "No WoW Token or
  Boosts & The Future of Forever — Destin Interview," https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/no-wow-token-or-boosts-and-the-future-of-forever-destin-interview-with-blizzard-382882,
  Sept 14 — community, quoting Blizzard)
- **Every raid boss gets a "chase item"** — a low-drop-chance, unusually strong item on every boss's
  loot table, from a Sun interview at BlizzCon. (Wowhead, "Chase Items on Every Raid Boss: The Sun
  Interview on WoW Forever," https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/chase-items-on-every-raid-boss-the-sun-interview-on-wow-forever-382892,
  ~Sept 14–15 — community, quoting Blizzard)
- **No unconventional class roles at launch** (no Season of Discovery-style Shaman tank); **every raid
  tier will offer at least one 10-player and one 20-player option**; **mount training now costs more
  gold**. All three from Tim Jones and Mike Nuthals to Kotaku. (Kotaku, "8 New Things We Just Learned
  About World Of Warcraft: Forever From Its Lead Designers," https://kotaku.com/new-things-learned-world-of-warcraft-forever-2000734005,
  Sept 14 — community, quoting Blizzard)
- **PvP progression detailed:** honor earned through Battlegrounds feeds a per-season/patch-cycle
  ranking system up to 14 ranks, unlocking cosmetic rewards and stat-bearing PvP armor; Darkspear
  Islands is the new progression-relevant BG alongside the originals. (readdork, "World of Warcraft:
  Forever PvP progression and add-on restrictions detailed," https://readdork.com/gaming/world-of-warcraft-forever-pvp-progression-addon-restrictions-4548e3a68a,
  Sept 15; Massively OP, Sept 15, https://massivelyop.com/2026/09/15/wow-forever-discusses-classes-addons-party-forming-pvp-progression-and-how-it-effectively-negates-wow-2/
  — both community, quoting Blizzard)
- **No auto-teleport group finder, no Meeting Stones.** Forever ships Classic's basic LFG tool (lists
  groups, does not form or teleport them); Meeting Stones (introduced in TBC) are absent, so Warlock
  Ritual of Summoning is the only way to pull people to an instance. Lead designer Mike Nuthals frames
  this as preserving travel and downtime as part of the social ritual. (Warcraft Tavern, "No Group
  Finder or Summoning Stones in WoW Forever," https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/no-group-finder-or-summoning-stones-in-wow-forever/,
  Sept 14; Kotaku, Sept 14 — both community, quoting Blizzard)

### Logging, API and addons — the single biggest development this pass

- **Blizzard has finally said something about addon policy — the prior notes' "biggest open risk" is
  now partially resolved.** `04-data-api-addons.md` (Sept 12) and `06-since-announcement.md` (Sept 14)
  both stated flatly that Blizzard had said nothing at all about Forever's addon policy. That changed
  Sept 14: lead encounter designer Mike Nuthals told Kotaku that Forever brings over "the in-game or
  in-combat add-on disarmament where the programmatic, the calculations done to automate marking or
  communicating for players... so that players will not have access to those kind of computational
  add-ons" — i.e., retail's existing restriction on secure/protected in-combat automation (auto target
  marking, automated raid-cooldown assignment, etc.), applied to Forever for the first time in a
  Classic-lineage game. (Kotaku, https://kotaku.com/new-things-learned-world-of-warcraft-forever-2000734005,
  Sept 14 — community, direct Blizzard quote; corroborated by GamesRadar, "World of Warcraft: Forever
  takes page out of retail's book...," https://www.gamesradar.com/games/world-of-warcraft/world-of-warcraft-forever-takes-page-out-of-retails-book-as-blizzard-confirms-access-to-add-ons-will-be-restricted-in-the-throwback-mmo/,
  and readdork, Sept 15)
- **What this restriction is, precisely, per Blizzard's own words: it targets in-combat automation of
  decisions (marking, communication, assignment), not information display.** A WeakAuras author quoted
  by gamefragger lists what should still be fine under that reading: custom timers/bars/icons/text, boss
  cast-bar countdowns, personal cooldown/buff tracking, raid-frame auras through Blizzard's own filters,
  manual raid notes, and general UI reskinning. (gamefragger, "World Of Warcraft: Forever Will Restrict
  Add-Ons The Same Way Retail Did," https://gamefragger.com/pc/warcraft/world-of-warcraft-forever-will-restrict-add-ons-the-same-way-retail-did-a29806,
  Sept 16 — community)
- **Contradicts/at-risk claim, flagged as inference, not a Blizzard statement:** the same gamefragger
  article separately asserts "Details-style live combat-log parsing and instant death logs" will be
  restricted, alongside auto mechanic-assignment tools. This is the article's own extrapolation — it
  explicitly notes "no official allow-list was published" — not a quote from Nuthals, Jones, or any
  Blizzard source. No other outlet makes this specific claim, and no source in this pass found a
  Blizzard statement addressing combat-log reading, Warcraft Logs-style export, or damage/death meters
  by name, one way or the other. **Treat this as the single most consequential open question for the
  site and do not build around either answer yet.**
- **Community reaction is already anxious, with no blue-post clarification yet.** A Blizzard forum
  thread ("Addons/Weakauras restricted") relaying the Kotaku quote has no official reply as of this
  research pass; players' loudest worries are healing addons (VuhDo, mouseover healing) and whether the
  default UI is legible enough without them — not logging specifically. (Blizzard Forums,
  https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/addonsweakauras-restricted/2350657, checked Sept 16 — community)
- **A community addon author is already building the test.** `Caeth/CleanCombatLog` on GitHub is an
  explicit "Thursday beta/API test build" (i.e., timed to Sept 17) whose entire purpose is to find out
  whether `COMBAT_LOG_EVENT_UNFILTERED` registers in the Forever client, via a `/ccl cleu` test command;
  the README states the interface number and outcome are both unknown as of writing. This is the
  addon-side mirror of the open question above and is worth watching directly on beta day. (GitHub,
  https://github.com/Caeth/CleanCombatLog, checked Sept 16 — community, unreleased/untested)
- **No statement found, from Blizzard or Warcraft Logs/Archon, about Warcraft Logs support for
  Forever**, and no statement found from DBM's maintainer (MysticalOS) about Forever compatibility.
  `03-competitive-landscape.md`'s "expected Dec 9" for Warcraft Logs remains an inference, still
  unconfirmed by Archon itself.

### Competitive landscape

- **The talent-calculator field is far more crowded than the Sept 12–14 passes captured.** In addition
  to Wowhead, Icy Veins, wowtbc.gg and talentsforever.com (already noted), at least four more
  fan calculators are live: wowforevertalents.com, wowclassicforever.info, wowforever.tech, and
  wowforever.netlify.app, plus wowforeverguides.com's version and a leveling-map site
  (wowforeveratlas.com). talentsforever.com's own about page claims 470/470 talents "confirmed" as of
  its Sept 14 update. This reinforces, more strongly than before, the existing recommendation not to
  compete head-on for "talent calculator." (Search aggregation of live sites, checked Sept 16 — community)

## Implications for foreversixty

- **Dates: no change.** Beta Sept 17, launch Nov 4, raids Dec 9, Hardcore "winter" all stand
  unmodified. The only date-shaped addition is the two-part Sept 17 Q&A split (10:00 modern / 10:30
  Forever) — worth watching specifically the 10:30 segment for any addon or logging follow-up.
- **The logs engine's core risk just went from "totally unknown" to "explicitly named but unresolved."**
  Blizzard itself confirmed *some* in-combat addon automation is blocked, using the same "in-combat
  add-on disarmament" language retail already uses (which historically has **not** blocked passive
  combat-log-reading addons like Details/Recount/Skada in retail — that automation restriction there
  targets things like auto-loot-rolling and secure macro chaining, not log capture). The single claim
  that log parsing itself is restricted is one outlet's inference, not corroborated. Recommendation:
  do not change the logs engine's design pre-emptively; instead, treat Sept 17 as the actual test date
  — run `Caeth/CleanCombatLog`'s own `/ccl cleu` test (or equivalent) the moment beta opens, and watch
  the 10:30 a.m. PDT Forever Q&A segment and the Blizzard forum thread above for a blue-post answer.
- **New leveling-guide risk:** any leveling route content built for Forever must account for the
  dungeon-XP nerf — a "dungeon farm to 60" or "chain-run this dungeon" route is now explicitly a worse
  strategy than quest-completion routing through new zones. Build guides around first-clear + quest
  XP, not repeat clears.
- **Chase items and the "every tier gets a 10 and 20 player raid" detail** are worth folding into any
  future gear-planner or raid-content pages once beta/live data confirms specifics — neither is
  actionable yet, both are still single/dual-sourced developer-interview claims pending beta
  confirmation.
- **No new risk to the site's domain/branding or legal posture** was found this pass; nothing in
  `05-business-legal.md` is contradicted.
