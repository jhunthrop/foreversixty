## 2. Fan-site economics

- **Display ads:** Raptive and Mediavine dominate; 2025–26 RPMs reported $15–50, gaming niche lower ($2–6 unoptimized), sources conflict. [Raptive](https://raptive.com/gaming/), [MonetizePros](https://monetizepros.com/ad-serving-and-optimization/mediavine-vs-raptive/). Playwire works with Raider.io. [Case study](https://www.playwire.com/raider.io-case-study)
- **Premium:** Raider.io $5/mo; Wowhead Premium.
- **Patreon:** Sixty Upgrades (solo dev "Grits"). [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/sixtyupgrades)
- **Affiliate:** RestedXP paid guides with up to 50% affiliate commission.
- **Gold-seller sponsorships are taboo** (r/classicwow Rule 4, Blizzard ToS).

**Ownership and comps:**
- Wowhead: ZAM 2007, Tencent 2012. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wowhead)
- Icy Veins: Enthusiast Gaming, €7M (~$8.3M), 2021; ~$2.4M ad revenue in 2020, so ~3–4x revenue. [Press release](https://www.enthusiastgaming.com/enthusiast-gaming-closes-acquisition-of-icy-veins/), [EsportsInsider](https://esportsinsider.com/2021/03/enthusiast-gaming-set-to-acquire-icy-veins-for-e7m)
- Raider.io: independent, ~30M pageviews/mo. [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/raider-io)
- Warcraft Tavern: WordPress + Beaver Builder, passion project since 2017. [About](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/about/)

**Traffic (SimilarWeb, order-of-magnitude only):** wowhead.com 30–58M/mo; warcraftlogs.com 12.6M; icy-veins.com 9.4M; warcrafttavern.com ~906K.

Implication: a small team's historical ceiling is ~1M monthly visits (Tavern) without heavy investment; realistic outcomes range from solid side income to an Icy Veins-scale acquihire, not venture scale, unless you become the dominant tools site.

## 3. Legal / IP

From Blizzard's [Legal FAQ](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/legal/c1ae32ac-7ff9-4ac3-a03b-fc04b8697010/blizzard-legal-faq) and [Trademark Usage Guidelines](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/legal/38fd0408-8431-469a-99bc-2cd9eb9462c8/blizzard-entertainment-trademark-usage-guidelines):

- **No Blizzard mark in your domain name.** Explicit. "Acme Warcraft" is their own example of what not to do.
- Non-commercial use only per the letter; ad-supported fan sites operate under de facto tolerance, not permission.
- Icons: extract your own from client files; retain copyright notices.
- No merch with Blizzard marks.
- [Developer API ToU](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/legal/a2989b50-5f16-43b1-abec-2ae17cc09dd6/blizzard-developer-api-terms-of-use): no paid features on API data; no API data for marketing; no interstitial donation asks; no selling/sharing data; 30-day retention; 36,000 calls/hour.
- **Private servers vs. fan sites are different risk categories.** Turtle WoW and Epoch ran Blizzard's game; content sites have operated 10–20 years without takedowns. Docket: [Blizzard v. Turtle WoW](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/71235075/blizzard-entertainment-inc-v-turtle-wow/).
- Descriptive use in page titles and URL paths ("forever talent calculator") is standard nominative use; Wowhead does `/forever/talent-calc`.

### Checklist
- [ ] Brand-neutral domain (no WoW, Warcraft, Forever-as-title)
- [ ] "Not affiliated with Blizzard" footer on every page
- [ ] No emulation, no client/server code, no redistributable asset packs
- [ ] Extract own icons; keep copyright notices
- [ ] Never paywall API-backed features; purge API data after 30 days
- [ ] No RMT/gold affiliates
- [ ] No Blizzard marks on merch
- [ ] Monitor enforcement posture weekly post-launch

## 4. Go-to-market

- **SEO:** Wowhead shipped `/forever/talent-calc` same day; head terms will be Wowhead's within a week. Winnable: long-tail combo queries ("undead paladin forever build", "skyborne hunter guide"), realm list / population / queue queries (historically won by small fast trackers), and new-content queries (Skyborne, new dungeons, new raids) where nobody is entrenched.
- **Reddit r/classicwow:** "a community, not a platform"; drive-by promotion removed; Content Creator Guidelines wiki; ~1,150 mod actions/week. Participate for weeks before promoting. [Policy thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/classicwow/comments/9lgeh0/what_is_the_selffan_promotion_policy_here_for/)
- **Addon as distribution:** Deathlog has 25M+ CurseForge downloads and publishes its DB freely; RestedXP funnels addon users to paid guides. Strongest structural lever for a new entrant.
- **Creators:** no hard data on partnership economics; avoid anything implying Blizzard affiliation.
- **Tech precedent:** aoWoW (PHP) is how Turtle WoW and others stood up Wowhead-clone databases in days. [GitHub](https://github.com/TrinityCore/aowow)

## GTM sketch: Sept 12 → Dec 9

- **Week 1 (Sept 12–18):** brand-neutral domain, hosting, analytics; fork/bootstrap DB engine; "everything we know" + FAQ; start participating (not promoting) on Reddit/Discord.
- **Weeks 2–3 (Sept 19–Oct 2):** beta live; Skyborne guide, new-zone datamining, build planner (differentiate on sharing/comparison, not head-term SEO); start addon; "Forever vs Era vs SoD" comparison content.
- **Weeks 4–6 (Oct 3–23):** class-by-class beta builds (long-tail); realm list page pre-built; addon to CurseForge/Wago; outreach to 2–3 mid-tier creators.
- **Weeks 7–8 (Oct 24–Nov 3):** launch checklist, day-one leveling, best routes 1–60; queue tracker live and load-tested; over-provision 5–10x.
- **Nov 4–Dec 9:** leveling and dungeon guides, population/realm health, pre-raid BiS planner, then raid-prep sprint timed to Dec 9 unlock; plan Hardcore deathlog for winter.

Cross-cutting: Wowhead, Icy Veins, and Tavern will publish every content type the same day or faster. Realistic wins are live-data tools, long-tail coverage, and addon distribution.
