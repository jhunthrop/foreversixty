## 2. Historical precedent

| Era | Winners | Speed | What they did right | Monetization |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Classic 2019 | Warcraft Tavern (Oct 2018), Sixty Upgrades (June 2019), WCL Classic (~May 2019) | Tavern ~10 mo pre-launch; Sixty Upgrades ~2 mo | Tavern grew by **acquiring** niche rivals (GnarlyGuides, WowClassicBiS, Barrens.chat); Sixty Upgrades solved founder's own problem, free | Ads + affiliate; Patreon; premium tiers |
| Classic 2019 (late) | NexusHub | 7 mo post-launch | Partnered with TSM for distribution | Free |
| TBC Classic 2021 | RestedXP v1 | 6 days pre-launch | Free addon funnels to paid guides | Freemium |
| TBC Classic 2021 | ironforge.pro | ~mid-2021 | Filled the population-data gap Blizzard leaves | Tiny Patreon |
| WotLK Classic 2022 | None new | — | Incumbents extended; first-mover advantage compounds | — |
| Hardcore 2023 | Deathlog addon, wowdeathmap.com | Deathlog 5 days post-launch | **Clearest playbook**: tiny addon + public data layer (leaderboard, heatmap, Discord bot); streamer deaths went viral | CurseForge rewards |
| SoD 2023-24 | AtlasForge.gg (all-in-one armory/map/DB/AH/pop); Questie's spoiler policy | Icy Veins hub 3 days pre-launch | Consolidate several tools in one destination; transparency builds trust | Ads |
| Anniversary / Cata 2024 | None new | — | Blizzard's built-in LFG absorbed gaps | — |

Lessons:
1. Every big independent win shipped a genuinely free, narrow tool that solved one real problem, then monetized adjacently. None gated the core value.
2. Since 2022 no new site broke through; Forever is the first real re-opening of the field since 2021.
3. Addon + public data layer is the fastest path to virality.
4. Consolidation beats fragmentation.
5. No durable community/social/guild-finder product has ever won; every winner was a data or guide tool. That is the biggest structural gap.

## 4. Gap analysis: ranked opportunities

1. **Hardcore deathlog + public data layer**, ready before Hardcore ships (winter). Best-attested playbook; unusual lead time. Dev 2–4 weeks now, activate day one.
2. **Legacy / alt and account-progression tracker.** Matches Forever's alt-friendly pillar; no incumbent tracks cross-character progression. Buildable on beta data before Nov 4.
3. **Realm/community finder** (vibe matching, not raid logistics). Green-field. 4–8 weeks; needs a seed community, so launch alongside beta.
4. **Exploration / quest-completion tracker** for the expanded world. 3–6 weeks after beta data is minable.
5. **Skyborne and new-combo build planner** with paywall transparency. 2–3 weeks.
6. **Leveling routes for the new zones**; RestedXP's head start does not carry over. Only buildable during beta; ~7 weeks hard floor.
7. Realm population tracking: **saturated, not recommended as lead.**
8. Darkspear Islands BG stats: moderate, PvP historically less monetizable; 8–10 weeks out.
9. AH price data if NexusHub is truly dead (verify first). 4–6 weeks post-launch.
10. Camping / social "third places": most speculative; bolt onto 1–6, not standalone.

**Recommendation:** #1 + #2 + #5 as three narrow, free, shareable tools under one destination, echoing the AtlasForge/Tavern consolidation lesson.
