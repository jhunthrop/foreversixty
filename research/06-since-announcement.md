## 2. Rulesets replace realms — detail beyond section 13

- The four rulesets in Blizzard's own words: **Normal** (PvP requires mutual consent), **PvP** ("every trip into contested territory" carries danger), **Roleplaying**, and **Hardcore** (after launch). (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard)
- Wowhead reads the absence of a PvP toggle on the Normal ruleset as a **soft confirmation that retail's War Mode is not in Forever**. That is Wowhead's inference, not Blizzard's statement. **Single-source, and an inference.** (Wowhead Deep Dive liveblog, https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-liveblog-382862 — community)
- The stated reason for going realmless: in Classic, players "naturally gravitated towards the same few very large realms at the expense of smaller ones." (Wowhead Deep Dive liveblog — community; Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard)
- **Two-part names are unique per region**, which Wowhead notes means *more* competition for a given name, not less; Blizzard's own framing is the opposite, that two-part names give "more room to create original character names, such as 'Ana Forever'." Both readings come from the same change. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard; Wowhead Deep Dive liveblog — community)
- A **language preference** set at creation is used by the game to lean toward grouping and phasing you with players who share it, alongside a faction preference. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard; Wowhead Deep Dive liveblog — community)
- The two-part name prompt was live in the BlizzCon demo's character creation. (MMORPG Network demo report, https://mmorpgnetwork.us/forever-demo-from-the-blizzcon-floor/ — community)
- Whether a player can switch an existing character's ruleset is **not settled**. A Wowhead commenter reports seeing Sodapoppin toggle between rulesets at the login screen on stream; Blizzard has said only that dead Hardcore characters transfer out. Treat as **unconfirmed**. (Wowhead Deep Dive liveblog comments — community)

## 4. Classes, talents and abilities

- Kris Zierhut, in an interview quoted by Icy Veins, on the milestone structure: "We still have what we call them gold medal talents. 11, 21, 31 point, one point talents. Those are all still there. We did add a new one point talent at the 16 point mark. So now each tree has four special one point abilities." This is the first direct developer quote on the 16-point milestone. (Icy Veins, "Start Planning Your WoW Forever Character With Our New Talent Calculator", https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/start-planning-your-wow-forever-character-with-our-new-talent-calculator/ — community)
- Each tree still has **seven rows** and characters still have **51 talent points**. (Icy Veins talent calculator news — community)
- The design goal Blizzard repeated is to **"unsolve"** the game. (PC Gamer 10 big details — community; Wowhead Deep Dive liveblog — community)
- **A locked "Secondary" tab was visible in the demo talent window**, which Talents Forever reads as dual spec being planned. **Single-source, and an inference from a UI element.** (Talents Forever changelog, 2026-09-13 — community)
- Power Up Gaming counted the trees from the demo and published a per-class new/changed/same breakdown: Warrior 11/30/13, Paladin 20/18/14, Hunter 15/16/19, Rogue 10/25/18, Priest 14/27/12, Shaman 15/25/10, Mage 6/32/16, Warlock 23/26/3, Druid 15/23/14. **Single-source**, and derived from demo footage rather than the client. (Power Up Gaming, "New WoW Forever Talents: Changes vs Classic Guide", https://powerupgaming.co.uk/2026/09/14/new-wow-forever-talents-changes-vs-classic-guide/ — community)

### Per-class, from the demo and from Blizzard's Paladin worked example

**Paladin** (Blizzard's own example; the fullest detail of any class)
- Holy Strike baseline at level 6, instant, 12 s cooldown, weapon damage plus Holy; Rank 5 by level 38 in the demo. Judgement no longer consumes Seals, 10 yd range, 10 s cooldown. Seal of Fury is the tank seal: damage regardless of weapon speed, a small absorb worth half the damage with a shield equipped, and Judging it taunts at 10 yd (other taunts are 5 yd) for 4 s. Consecration baseline at 20, much heavier damage on the first four targets. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard; Wowhead Deep Dive liveblog — community; Talents Forever — community)
- Holy: Improved Holy Strike, Voice of Truth (silence and interrupt immunity window), Reverence, Infusion of Light, Divine Favor moved up, Holy Shock easier to reach with a shorter cooldown, Consecrated Ground, Light's Vigil. Protection: Improved Seal of Fury, Shield Specialization (blocks restore mana), Swift Judgement (resets Judgement when it misses), Templar's Bulwark (a large absorb that triggers Forbearance), improved Reckoning, Iron Creed. Retribution: Vindication, Sacred Arbiter, Champion of the Light, Instrument of Law (instant Hammer of Wrath), Twist of Light (seal twisting without a swing-timer addon). (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard; Wowhead Deep Dive liveblog — community)
- Illumination now refunds roughly 50% of the mana instead of the full cost; Redoubt triggers on any damaging melee hit rather than requiring a crit; Holy Shield's block-chance bonus is lower; Templar's Bulwark's absorb is reported as 100% of max health. **Single-source.** (Power Up Gaming — community)
- **Naming conflict:** Inven Global's Deep Dive write-up calls the tank seal **Seal of Wrath**; Blizzard's recap, Wowhead and Talents Forever all call it **Seal of Fury**. Treat Seal of Fury as correct. (Inven Global, https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25897/world-of-warcraft-forever-details-revealed-major-overhaul-for-gear-and-classes — community)
- Paladins deliberately keep their weaknesses: no standard interrupt, limited ranged tools. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard; guided.news deep dive — community; Inven Global 25897 — community)

**Warrior**
- Victory Rush is baseline in the Arms tab: instant, 30 s cooldown, heals 10% of max health, usable within 20 s of a non-trivial kill. Tactical Mastery is a passive rather than a talent. Slam reaches rank 3 by level 38 (Classic: 46) and has a 15 s cooldown. Thunder Clap works in Battle or Defensive Stance; rank 4 slows attacks 20% for 22 s and hits up to 4 targets (Classic: 10% for 30 s). Shield Wall is 15 min / 60% for 12 s (Classic: 30 min / 75% for 10 s) and appears no longer to share a cooldown with Retaliation and Recklessness. Shield Block has 2 charges and 7 s baseline. Intercept and Bloodrage are still in the game. **Single-source** (stream transcription). (Talents Forever — community)
- New talents: Spearing Strike (Arms, bonus damage vs Giants, Dragonkin and mounted targets, dismounts), Bloodthrill (Arms, melee hits on Rend targets can proc Overpower), Raging Blows (Fury, Whirlwind also strikes off-hand), Vanguard (Protection, Charge usable in Defensive Stance). Changed: Improved Slam preserves the swing timer, Death Wish's penalty is +5% damage taken instead of −20% armor, Improved Shield Wall cuts the cooldown instead of extending the duration. **Single-source.** (Power Up Gaming — community)

**Hunter**
- Aimed Shot is trainable rather than a Marksmanship talent, 2 s cast and 6 s cooldown shared with Multi-Shot (Classic was a 3 s cast). Aspect of the Beast now also grants 50 melee attack power at rank 1. Call Pet is renamed after the pet, e.g. Call Owl. **Single-source.** (Talents Forever — community)
- New: Lone Wolf (Marksmanship, +20% damage with no active pet), Summon Hawk (a dive-bomb sharing a cooldown with Arcane Shot, up to 2 active), Resourcefulness (Survival). Changed: Endurance Training also grants pet armor, Efficiency covers melee abilities, Trueshot Aura moved up and grants ranged attack power only, Entrapment's root is guaranteed but short. **Single-source.** (Power Up Gaming — community)

**Rogue**
- **Rogues can use one-handed axes**; the Hack and Slash talent lists Axe/Sword as one weapon group. **Single-source.** (Talents Forever — community)
- New: Mutilate (Assassination, 2 combo points, stronger on poisoned targets), Venom (Assassination finisher), Restless Blades (Combat), Cutthroat (Subtlety). Changed: Improved Expose Armor reduces Energy cost and refunds a combo point on 5-point casts, Weapon Expertise now reduces enemy dodge and parry instead of granting weapon skill, Hemorrhage boosts your Rupture damage. Four prerequisite arrows in the Rogue trees were corrected after stream review (Deflection→Riposte, Precision→Dual Wield Specialization, Blade Flurry→Weapon Expertise, Dirty Deeds→Quietus). **Single-source.** (Power Up Gaming — community; Talents Forever — community)

**Priest**
- New: Penance (Discipline), Divine Aegis (Discipline), Litany of Light (Holy), Prayer of Mending (Holy). Changed: Meditation is 17% mana regen while casting at rank 1 (Classic 5%), Spirit of Redemption lasts 15 s (was 10), Spiritual Guidance scales healing fully but damage only weakly, Vampiric Embrace is shorter but can trigger Spirit Tap on death. **Single-source.** (Power Up Gaming — community)
- Shadow Word: Death and Devouring Plague are normal Shadow spells; Devouring Plague is no longer an Undead racial. Fear Ward is baseline for every Priest at a 3 min cooldown. **Single-source.** (Talents Forever — community)

**Shaman**
- Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning cast half a second faster than Classic before any talent; Chain Lightning rank 1 is a 2 s cast, 6 s cooldown, 3 targets, each jump 30% weaker. Ghost Wolf's base cast is 2 s, and Improved Ghost Wolf makes it instant at 2/2 and usable indoors. Fire Nova is a spell rather than a totem: 30 yd, instant, 10 s cooldown, detonating around your active Fire totem. Two new abilities: **Totemic Projection** (ground-targeted, moves your totems up to 30 yd away) and **Call of the Elements** (drops up to four totems from the Totem Bar in one 3 s cast). Windfury Weapon rank 1 is a 20% chance of 2 extra attacks at 103 attack power, 60 minutes, and suppresses your own Windfury Totem benefit on that hand. **Single-source.** (Talents Forever — community)
- New talents: Lava Burst (Elemental), Maelstrom Weapon (Enhancement), Spirit Weapons (Enhancement), Water Shield (Restoration). Changed: Call of Flame also boosts Flame Shock, Fire Nova and Lava Burst; Elemental Fury moved earlier and is multi-rank; Stormstrike now increases your own Nature damage. **Single-source.** (Power Up Gaming — community)

**Mage**
- New abilities: **Frostfire Bolt** (both Fire and Frost, named by four talents) and **Comprehend Scroll** (decipher an untranslated scroll, seen on a Skyborne Mage). **Single-source.** (Talents Forever — community)
- New talents: Arcane Blast, Hot Streak. Changed: Improved Counterspell's silence is guaranteed but short at rank 1. Mage has the fewest new talents (6) and the most changed (32) of any class. **Single-source.** (Power Up Gaming — community)

**Warlock**
- **Banes and Curses are now separate categories, so one of each can run on a target.** Curse of Agony is renamed Bane of Agony, Curse of Doom is Bane of Doom, Enslave Demon is Subjugate Demon. A new **Incubus** summon is the male counterpart to the Succubus, named by four Demonology talents. **Single-source.** (Talents Forever — community)
- New talents: Pandemic (crit damage bonus for Corruption, Banes, Drain Soul/Life, Siphon Life and a new Drain Hope), Drain Hope, Malevolence (DoTs can crit). Warlock has the most new talents (23) and the fewest unchanged (3). **Single-source.** (Power Up Gaming — community)

**Druid**
- Omen of Clarity and Nature's Grasp are trainable rather than talents. **Revive** is a new 10 s out-of-combat resurrection; Rebirth keeps its 30 min cooldown. **Lacerate** is in as a baseline Bear bleed, learned after level 30. **Cure Poison is gone**; Abolish Poison is the only poison cure. **Single-source.** (Talents Forever — community)
- New talents: Genesis (Balance), Nature's Majesty, Berserk (Feral). Changed: Feral Charge has a separate Cat version, Primal Fury also grants combo points on Cat crits, Heart of the Wild moved much earlier, Furor's Cat energy recovery scales with time spent out of form. **Single-source.** (Power Up Gaming — community)

## 5. Stats, items and combat

- Blizzard's own wording adds detail section 13 compresses: weapon skill "still works as it always has," but items carry less of it per item "so they are not always the obvious best choice"; unified hit and crit are framed as helping hybrids such as Retribution Paladins and Enhancement Shaman while also making Taunts, Poisons and Traps more reliable for Warriors, Hunters and Rogues. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap via MMO-Champion, https://www.mmo-champion.com/content/14512-BlizzCon-2026-World-of-Warcraft-Forever-Deep-Dive-Panel — community, quoting the Blizzard text; Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard)
- **Unique dungeon boss items are Rare (blue) quality**, and every dungeon drop is now at least Rare. (Inven Global 25897 — community; Wowhead Deep Dive liveblog — community)
- Named new or updated items beyond the ones in section 13: **Graverobber's Shovel**, **Sharpened Cutlery**, and updated **Tidal Charm** and **Whitemane's Chapeau**. **Single-source.** (Output Lag live panel log, https://outputlag.com/news/everything-new-world-of-warcraft-forever/ — community)
- PC Gamer's summary of the item pass: "Most items in the game have been reworked (or new ones have been added) so that they're useful to all classes and roles" and "All bosses have better loot overall." (PC Gamer 10 big details — community)
- Combat pacing: ~10–15 s to kill an ordinary creature solo; Polymorph, Banish, Fear and Root keep a place in dungeons; damage dealers must respect threat and use area damage only when safe. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard)

## 7. Camping and professions — detail beyond section 13

- **Campsites offer vendors, repairs, profession workspaces and one-hour buffs.** You must sit at the fire for a few moments and become rested before the buffs apply. Some parts of Azeroth are described as better suited to a quiet rest than others. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard; guided.news deep dive — community)
- Named profession objects: Blacksmith **Sharpening Wheel** (+Attack Power), Tailor **Faction Banner** (+Spirit), Herbalist **Incense Candle** (+Intellect), Alchemist **Alchemy Lab**, Leatherworker **Tanning Rack** (required for advanced Leatherworking recipes), Cook **upgraded Campfires** for 5 or 10 objects. These effects are variants of, and **do not stack with**, the equivalent class buffs. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard)
- Crafted food grants a small **experience** bonus as well as stat bonuses; fishing and cooking were updated alongside camping. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard; Wowhead Deep Dive liveblog — community)
- Kris Zierhut's design intent for camping, in his own words: another player walks by, adds to your fire, "and that's the moment the friendships, relationships, and guilds are born." (IGN interview with Kris Zierhut and Mike Nuthals, https://sea.ign.com/world-of-warcraft-forever/248775/world-of-warcraft-forever-devs-explain-why-the-game-makes-wow-2-unlikely — community)

## 8. Transmog — it is opt-out, not opt-in

- The system is **on by default and can be disabled**, which is the reverse of how the first pass recorded it. A mode selector appears the first time you log in; choosing **Classic Mode** disables transmog automatically on entering the world. You can also change the setting later by talking to an NPC, and configure outfits at a transmogrifier in certain cities. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard; Wowhead Deep Dive liveblog — community)
- With transmog disabled you still **collect appearances**, you can still hide your own helm or cloak and display your own transmog, and you still see other players' hidden helm and cloak settings. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard)
- Appearance grants: Poor and Common quality grant on loot without binding; a bind-on-pickup Uncommon or Rare dungeon drop grants its appearance to **all eligible looters** when looted; epic raid drops grant only to the player the item binds to. Armor type rules apply (plate to plate, cloth to cloth) and level restrictions lift at 60. PvP gear transmog still requires the matching rank. Cosmetic-only items exist but will be rare. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard; Wowhead Deep Dive liveblog — community)

## 9. Hardcore — what the Sept 13 panel actually announced

The Hardcore panel ran Sunday Sept 13 at 4:15 p.m. PDT on the Warcraft Stage. Speakers named in coverage: Production Director Clayton Stone and Senior Game Designer Josh Greenfield (Aggrend).

- **Hardcore is confirmed for Forever, after launch, with no date given at the panel.** Blizzard's roadmap places it in winter; PC Gamer's headline frames it as "by winter," which is the roadmap restated, not a new date. (Wowhead Hardcore panel liveblog, https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/whats-next-hardcore-panel-liveblog-382875 — community; MMO-Champion, https://www.mmo-champion.com/content/14518-BlizzCon-2026-World-of-Warcraft-The-Past-Present-and-Future-of-Hardcore — community; PC Gamer, https://www.pcgamer.com/games/world-of-warcraft/world-of-warcraft-forever-will-get-a-hardcore-mode-by-winter-say-devs-whichll-be-interesting-given-all-the-new-and-undiscovered-ways-to-die/ — community; Warcraft Tavern, https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/world-of-warcraft-forever-was-designed-with-hardcore-in-mind/ — community)
- **A dead Hardcore character can transfer to any other ruleset, including PvP and Roleplaying** — not forced into PvE as in Classic Hardcore. Once transferred it can never return to Hardcore. (MMO-Champion — community; Warcraft Tavern — community; Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard; guided.news deep dive — community)
- **A new layer-refresh safety system:** when a layer refresh is coming, the game shows a UI notification; you move somewhere safe and click a button, and the refresh happens then. Warcraft Tavern reports this applies to **all versions of the game, not only Hardcore**. (MMO-Champion — community; Wowhead Hardcore liveblog — community; Warcraft Tavern — community)
- **Legacy in Hardcore is one-directional and roguelike.** You earn Legacy points in Hardcore and each death leaves account-wide bonuses that make the next character easier. Legacy points, mounts, pets, appearances and achievements earned outside Hardcore do **not** carry into Hardcore; everything earned in Hardcore **does** carry out. Hardcore will have its own Legacy challenges and perks. (MMO-Champion — community; Wowhead Hardcore liveblog — community; Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard)
- **Mak'gora is getting "a dedicated space."** Greenfield teased Mak'gora tournaments as something the team is actively thinking about. **Single-source-ish** — three outlets report it, all from the same panel moment, and all describe it as a tease rather than an announcement. (MMO-Champion — community; Wowhead Hardcore liveblog — community; Warcraft Tavern — community)
- History given on the panel: the Road to Ragnaros community event and the Soul of Iron buff led to the prototype; Greenfield tracked roughly **1,500 people** playing Hardcore by the start of 2023; Chase McGuire prototyped official Hardcore in two weeks; Holly Longdale greenlit it. Transferring dead characters off was allowed deliberately because "this isn't Diablo and you may lose a character you had put 200+ hours on." (MMO-Champion — community)
- Blizzard says Forever's content was designed with Hardcore in mind: new item effects are meant to make dungeon runs worth the risk when death is permanent. (Warcraft Tavern — community)
- No new information on Hardcore rules themselves (level restrictions, grouping, duelling, the Mak'gora exception, self-found) was given; Blizzard said it plans "a separate presentation" for the remaining Hardcore rules. (guided.news deep dive — community)

## 11. Raids, group content and encounter design

- **The launch raids require no attunement.** Clayton Stone declined to say whether later raids will. (But Why Tho interview with Anna Resendez and Clayton Stone, https://butwhytho.net/2026/09/wow-classic-devs-world-of-warcraft-forever/ — community)
- **No flex raiding, and none planned.** Ana Resendes: "We're starting with 10- and 20-man, and no plans for flex at the moment… as you get more gear, maybe you might need less people." Difficulty and scale never adapt to group size or level. (But Why Tho — community; Inven Global 25888 — community)
- **No automated group finder.** Blizzard will provide tools that help players find each other but explicitly not "pressing a single button to automatically assemble a group and immediately teleport into content." (Inven Global 25888 — community)
- **No official Race to World First support**, though Blizzard expects the community to compete. (Inven Global 25888 — community)
- Encounters are deliberately grounded: "not gonna start with super complex mechanics where people are being thrown around in the room." Internal playtests compare new dungeons against Stratholme in the same client for parity in difficulty, damage numbers, pacing and complexity. (But Why Tho — community; ScreenRant — community)
- **Forever's launch is deliberately conservative relative to Season of Discovery.** Clayton Stone: "we certainly heard feedback from players that with Season of Discovery, we pushed the envelope about as far as we could push it… for where we're starting, we're really working on filling out some of their skills that they didn't necessarily have before." He does not rule out bringing SoD-style class changes in later. This is the clearest answer yet to the community's "will Forever die like SoD" fear and to "where are the SoD tank and healer specs." (But Why Tho — community)

## 12. New classes are explicitly not ruled out

- The What's Next panel showed a **fan survey with 35,305 responses** on new classes: 42.2% said no, 47.3% said yes in some form. Clay Stone said **nothing is off the table, not even a new class**. (Output Lag — community; Wowhead What's Next liveblog, https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/world-of-warcraft-forever-whats-next-liveblog-382824 — community)
- This directly contradicts the single-source "no new classes" line carried in the first pass. See Corrections.

## 13. Controller support and the client

- Controller support is a first for the franchise and shipped in the demo in an **alpha state**. Buttons map to face buttons and the D-pad and combine to swap ability sets, comparable to Final Fantasy XIV; a radial menu on select/start navigates map, quest log and bags. One or two people playtest every internal leveling and dungeon playtest on a controller. Tim Jones on consoles: "I'm sorry. Nothing to announce." (ScreenRant — community; MMORPG Network — community)
- The demo client carried a retail-style **Edit Mode** for UI customisation. **Single-source.** (MMORPG Network — community)
- Kris Zierhut on why there is no WoW 2: the engine and server infrastructure have been improved continuously for 25 years — "Why would you throw that away and start over?" (IGN — community)

## 14. Addons, the API, combat logging and the Battle.net API

**Nothing.** This pass found no statement of any kind, from Blizzard or from a developer interview, about addon support in Forever, the Forever addon API, combat logging, `/combatlog` export, or a Battle.net API namespace for Forever. Searches were run against Blizzard news, the Blizzard forums, all four panel liveblogs and recaps, six developer interviews and the general press.

What exists is adjacent and must not be mistaken for a Forever statement:
- Retail Patch 12.0 (Midnight) removed `COMBAT_LOG_EVENT_UNFILTERED` from addons and introduced Secret Values; Ion Hazzikostas defended the change in January 2026. This is **retail**, and nothing says it applies to Forever's client. (PC Gamer, https://www.pcgamer.com/games/world-of-warcraft/blizzard-let-its-ui-mods-go-farther-than-we-should-have-says-game-director-ion-hazzikostas-but-the-best-time-to-plant-a-tree-is-20-years-ago-the-second-best-time-is-today/ — community)
- Blizzard's Hardcore panel credited **community addons** as the origin of official Hardcore ("Hardcore is inspired by the community and the add-ons made to enable the rules and playstyle"), which is warm toward addons but is not a policy statement. (Wowhead Hardcore liveblog — community)
- Blizzard's Paladin talent copy mentions Twist of Light letting players seal-twist **"without a swing-timer addon"**, which presumes addons exist in Forever but does not say what they may do. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard; Wowhead Deep Dive liveblog — community)
- The retail Midnight controller-support-via-addons remark in ScreenRant confirms addons have historically filled that gap; again, not a Forever policy. (ScreenRant — community)

## Corrections to earlier research

1. **Transmog is opt-out, not opt-in.** `01-official-facts.md` §8 and `everything-we-know.md` both say "opt-in transmog," sourced to one outlet. Blizzard's own Deep Dive recap makes it a system that is on by default and can be **disabled**, with Classic Mode disabling it automatically at first login. The site's changelog entry `2026-09-12-transmog-opt-in.md` should stay as a dated record of what was reported that day, and a new entry should record the correction. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard)

2. **"No new classes" is wrong.** §7 carries "No new classes" sourced to gamefragger. Clay Stone said on the What's Next panel that nothing is off the table, not even a new class, alongside a 35,305-response fan survey. (Output Lag — community; Wowhead What's Next liveblog — community)

3. **Realms do not exist; rulesets do.** The Key dates table in `00-synthesis.md` still reads "Realm list becomes public" for Oct 27 and "Hardcore realms" for winter, and §11 item 1 still asks for "Realm count, types (PvP/PvE/RP), regions." There is no realm list. Oct 27 is name reservation only; the ruleset list is already public. "Hardcore realms" is "the Hardcore ruleset." (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard)

4. **The Skyborne are not a neutral race in Blizzard's terminology.** §7 and the site's Skyborne page call them neutral by analogy with Pandaren. Blizzard's transcript is explicit: "the Skyborne are not a neutral race. At character creation, players will choose whether their Skyborne joins the Horde or the Alliance." The mechanic is the same, the wording is not, and Blizzard's wording matters because the two factions' Skyborne have different racials. (Blizzplanet transcript — community; Blizzard What's Next recap — blizzard)

5. **Only one new race/class combination was confirmed on Sept 12; six are confirmed now, and "Night Elf Paladin" was never one of them.** §11 items 11 and 13 can be closed: Dwarf Shaman and Undead Paladin are both officially confirmed; Night Elf Paladin is a retail Patch 12.2 combination and does not apply to Forever. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard)

6. **The beta is not paywalled.** A free opt-in exists. The paid packs guarantee a seat. (Wowhead 382860 — community; gamefragger — community)

7. **"Nora Mills, Lead Software Engineer" appears to be wrong.** §12 lists Nora Mills. The two named lead software engineers in coverage are **Ana Resendez** (Blizzard's own Deep Dive recap) and **Nora Valletta** (ScreenRant). Blizzplanet's transcript has Tim Jones hand over to "Nora." Use Nora Valletta.

8. **The Deep Dive's Paladin tank seal is Seal of Fury, not Seal of Wrath.** Inven Global's write-up uses Seal of Wrath; three other sources including Blizzard use Seal of Fury.

9. **Two dungeon level ranges on the site are probably wrong.** City of Dalaran is 28–30, not 20–35; Krol'dok Stronghold is 40–55, not 35–40, and it is an outdoor dungeon in Riverglades. Both figures are single-source either way; Output Lag's come off the panel slides in real time. (Output Lag — community)

10. **The beta end date is reported two ways.** Blizzard's pre-purchase post says the beta runs "through October 21, 2026, PST." The roadmap slide, as logged by Output Lag and reproduced by Icy Veins, says September 17 to **October 22**. guided.news notes exact start and end times were never given, so the test may run into Oct 22 in other time zones. Keep Oct 21 as Blizzard's stated date and note the conflict. (Blizzard 24301508 — blizzard; Output Lag — community; Icy Veins roadmap, https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/warcraft-forever-roadmap-unveils-raid-unlocks-and-major-updates/ — community; guided.news deep dive — community)

11. **Launch time is stated as both PST and PDT by Blizzard.** Three Blizzard posts say 3:00 p.m. PST; the "Carve a New Path" post says 3:00 p.m. PDT. November 4 is after the US DST change, so PST is the consistent reading. (Blizzard 24301508, 24301145 — blizzard; Blizzard 24302093 — blizzard)

12. **§11 item 6, "Legacy vs Warbands," is effectively answered.** Legacy points are earned account-wide and spent per character, with a 16-point per-character cap against 65 earnable at launch, three trees, and a separate cosmetic reward track. It is not Warbands. (Blizzard Deep Dive recap — blizzard)

13. **§11 item 4, self-found, is partly answered.** There is no self-found ruleset; the four rulesets are Normal, PvP, Roleplaying and Hardcore. PvP flagging rules are still unstated beyond "mutual consent" on Normal.
