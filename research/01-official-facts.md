## 2. Lore / Setting

- After *Warcraft III: Reforged — Forsaken Kingdom*, before Molten Core: "year one of vanilla." [Blizzard 24301145](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301145/), [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/world-of-warcraft/forever-blizzcon-2026-wow-classic-plus)
- Standalone narrative branch; permanent third pillar alongside Modern and Classic. [guided.news](https://guided.news/en/news/wow-classic-launches-in-november-everything-you-need-to-know-about-world-of-warcraft-forever/)
- Level cap 60 indefinitely; no Outland/Draenor-style jumps. [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/world-of-warcraft/forever-blizzcon-2026-wow-classic-plus), [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25876/)
- No flying, no level scaling. [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25876/)
- "Time bubble": horizontal growth (more raids/dungeons at 60), not vertical. [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/wow-classic-plus-world-of-warcraft/)
- Pillars: approachability, the world as protagonist, the journey, nurturing social experiences. [Blizzard 24302093](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302093/)

## 5. Raids

- **Barrow Deeps**: 10-player, Night Elf prison cavern, "notorious Demon Hunter" teased, three entrances.
- **Hyjal Summit**: 20-player on Mount Hyjal; unknown antagonist siphoning the mountain.
- **Onyxia's Lair** stays as 40-player.
- New legendary and tier sets from both raids.
- **Raids unlock Dec 9, 2026**, not at launch.
- "Timbermaw" raid tease appears only as a Wowhead nav title; unconfirmed.

Sources: [Blizzard 24303862](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24303862/), [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25876/), [gamefragger](https://gamefragger.com/pc/warcraft/world-of-warcraft-forevers-first-panel-just-mapped-nine-dungeons-hyjal-and-a-level-60-roadmap-a29773).

## 6. PvP

- **Darkspear Islands**: 15v15 battleground off Kalimdor; EotS-style open space with Arathi Basin-style control points.
- Original battlegrounds remain.
- Reworked Honor system and regular PvP season refreshes; no numbers yet.
- Community has noted "no arenas" with no official response.

Sources: [Blizzard 24303862](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24303862/), [Icy Veins roadmap](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/warcraft-forever-roadmap-unveils-raid-unlocks-and-major-updates/).

## 7. Race / Class

**Skyborne** (official spelling; "Skyborn" appears in some outlets):
- Neutral elven race from Zephras Isle; choose faction at creation (Pandaren-style).
- Default classes: Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Druid (sky-blue druid forms). Horde-only Shaman, Alliance-only Mage.
- Deep customization, new voice lines and dances.
- Paywalled ($29.99 minimum). Largest community flashpoint.

Sources: [Wowhead Skyborne First Look](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/skyborne-first-look-new-neutral-race-in-world-of-warcraft-forever-382829), [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25876/).

**New race/class combos:**
- Undead Paladin: confirmed via cinematic. [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/undead-paladins-coming-to-world-of-warcraft-forever-382823)
- Dwarf Shaman: shown in demo per hands-on reports. [Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/undead-paladins-other-race-class-combos-in-world-of-warcraft-forever/)
- Night Elf Paladin: only in reader comments; unconfirmed for Forever.
- Do not conflate with retail Patch 12.2 "Eclipse" Paladin combos (Night Elf, Forsaken, Darkspear). [Wowhead 382828](https://www.wowhead.com/news/three-new-race-class-combos-for-paladins-coming-in-patch-12-2-382828)
- No new classes. [gamefragger](https://gamefragger.com/pc/warcraft/world-of-warcraft-forevers-first-panel-just-mapped-nine-dungeons-hyjal-and-a-level-60-roadmap-a29773)

**Talents and classes:** extensive talent revamp, meaningful trade-offs; Paladin gets a tanking Seal, better group healing, fuller Retribution (single-source); racials reworked (Dwarf bonus vs beasts, Undead spell-drain, single-source). [Blizzard 24303862](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24303862/), [guided.news](https://guided.news/en/news/wow-classic-launches-in-november-everything-you-need-to-know-about-world-of-warcraft-forever/)

## 8. Systems

- **Legacy progression**: account-wide benefits for alt levelers, without skipping the journey. Relationship to retail Warbands unclear. [Blizzard 24303862](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24303862/), [Wowhead Legacy explainer](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/warcraft-forever-legacy-system-explained-382842)
- **Camping**: craft campfires in the world; others join, contribute via professions, share buffs. Presented by Clayton Stone. [Wowhead liveblog](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/world-of-warcraft-forever-whats-next-liveblog-382824)
- **Opt-in transmog**: toggle; disabling shows everyone's real gear. [Out of Games](https://outof.games/news/9946-world-of-warcraft-forever-whats-next-at-blizzcon-2026-ended/)
- New professions, reputations, dungeon-themed items, legendary questlines (few specifics). [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25876/)
- Riding skill changes teased.
- Official gamepad/controller support. [Blizzard 24303862](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24303862/)

## 9. Visual / UX

- HD/SD character model toggle with restored Classic animations on HD.
- Modern and Classic visual presets.
- Rendering upgrades: flowing rivers (Elwynn), mist (Darkshore), moonlight (Ashenvale).
