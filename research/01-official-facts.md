## 2. Lore / Setting

- After *Warcraft III: Reforged — Forsaken Kingdom*, before Molten Core: "year one of vanilla." [Blizzard 24301145](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301145/), [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/world-of-warcraft/forever-blizzcon-2026-wow-classic-plus)
- Standalone narrative branch; permanent third pillar alongside Modern and Classic. [guided.news](https://guided.news/en/news/wow-classic-launches-in-november-everything-you-need-to-know-about-world-of-warcraft-forever/)
- Level cap 60 indefinitely; no Outland/Draenor-style jumps. [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/world-of-warcraft/forever-blizzcon-2026-wow-classic-plus), [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25876/)
- No flying, no level scaling. [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25876/)
- "Time bubble": horizontal growth (more raids/dungeons at 60), not vertical. [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/wow-classic-plus-world-of-warcraft/)
- Pillars: approachability, the world as protagonist, the journey, nurturing social experiences. [Blizzard 24302093](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302093/)

## 7. Race / Class

**Skyborne** (official spelling; "Skyborn" appears in some outlets):
- **Correction (Sept 14):** Blizzard's own transcript says "the Skyborne are not a neutral race" — the faction is chosen at creation and the two halves get different racials. Elven race from Zephras Isle; Horde Skyborne work with the Earthen Ring, Alliance Skyborne with the Kirin Tor. ([Blizzplanet transcript](https://warcraft.blizzplanet.com/blog/comments/blizzcon-2026-world-of-warcraft-whats-next-panel-transcript/4))
- Default classes: Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Druid (sky-blue druid forms). Horde-only Shaman, Alliance-only Mage.
- Deep customization, new voice lines and dances.
- Paywalled ($29.99 minimum). Largest community flashpoint.

Sources: [Wowhead Skyborne First Look](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/skyborne-first-look-new-neutral-race-in-world-of-warcraft-forever-382829), [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25876/).

## 8. Systems

- **Legacy progression**: account-wide benefits for alt levelers, without skipping the journey. Relationship to retail Warbands unclear. [Blizzard 24303862](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24303862/), [Wowhead Legacy explainer](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/warcraft-forever-legacy-system-explained-382842)

- ~~**Opt-in transmog**~~: **corrected Sept 14 — transmog is opt-*out*.** It is on by default, a mode selector appears at first login, picking Classic Mode disables it automatically, and an NPC can change it later. Disabling still shows everyone's real gear. [Blizzard Deep Dive recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-panel-recap), [Out of Games](https://outof.games/news/9946-world-of-warcraft-forever-whats-next-at-blizzcon-2026-ended/)

- Official gamepad/controller support. [Blizzard 24303862](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24303862/)

## 13. Deep Dive panel, Sept 13, 2026 (added Sept 14)

Sources: Blizzard, "World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap", https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-panel-recap (blizzard); Wowhead liveblog, https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-liveblog-382862 (community); Icy Veins, "The Racial Rework Gives Every Race a Reason to Matter", https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/the-racial-rework-gives-every-race-a-reason-to-matter-in-warcraft-forever/ (community, demo access); PC Gamer, https://www.pcgamer.com/games/world-of-warcraft/10-big-world-of-warcraft-forever-details-from-blizzcon-no-separate-realms-optional-transmog-and-massively-improved-items/ (community).

- **Rulesets replace realms.** Players choose Normal, PvP, Roleplaying, or Hardcore; each is one large ecosystem, not a realm. No cross-ruleset grouping; factions stay separate. Hardcore arrives after launch, fully enclosed; a dead Hardcore character may transfer to another ruleset; other rulesets' progress does not flow into Hardcore. Collections and Legacy points are account-wide across rulesets (Hardcore excepted). PvP ruleset has faction-balance systems like Season of Discovery. (Blizzard recap)
- **Character names are two-part** (first and last), unique per region; a language preference is chosen at creation. (Blizzard recap)
- **Six new race and class combinations:** Gnome Priest, Human Hunter, Dwarf Shaman, Orc Mage, Troll Warlock, Undead Paladin, "with more planned for future updates." (Blizzard recap)
- **Racials:** every race has two active and two passive abilities tuned for similar offensive power. Dwarf: Stoneform (removes and grants immunity to bleeds, poisons, diseases; now reduces physical damage taken), Find Treasure (stacks with other tracking), Mace Specialization (crit chance with all spells and abilities while a mace is equipped), Big Game Hunter (damage vs Beasts). Undead: Will of the Forsaken (removes Charm, Fear, Sleep; no longer grants immunity), Cannibalize (restores Mana and Health), Underwater Breathing, Touch of the Grave (attacks may drain life). Skyborne (Icy Veins, from the demo): Walk on Air (glide downward 10 s), Read Ley Line (100% health and mana regeneration for 15 s), Wind Blessed (1% haste), Elemental Insight (5% damage to Elementals). Skyborne choose Horde or Alliance at creation. (Blizzard recap; Icy Veins)
- **Talents:** familiar structure, same number of rows, one-point milestone talents at 11, 21, and 31 points plus a new 16-point milestone; some talents unchanged from 1.12; key buff talents (Divine Spirit, Blessing of Kings, Improved Mark of the Wild, Improved Battle Shout) are baseline. (Blizzard recap; Wowhead)
- **Paladin (worked example):** Holy Strike baseline at level 6 (instant, 12 s cooldown); Judgement no longer consumes Seals; Seal of Fury for tanks (damage regardless of weapon speed, small absorb, Judging it taunts at 10 yards); Consecration baseline at 20 with heavier damage on the first four targets; Holy: Improved Holy Strike, Voice of Truth, Reverence, Infusion of Light, Holy Shock easier to reach, Consecrated Ground, Light's Vigil; Protection: Improved Seal of Fury, Shield Specialization, Swift Judgement, Templar's Bulwark, Iron Creed; Retribution: Vindication, Sacred Arbiter, Champion of the Light, Instrument of the Law, Twist of Light (seal twisting without a swing-timer addon). (Blizzard recap; Wowhead)
- **Stats and items:** spell, melee, and ranged hit chance are one stat; likewise crit; expertise (reduces parry and dodge chance) added; weapon skill per item reduced; bonus healing includes one third as much bonus damage, available from level 10; caster weapons grant spell damage and healing; trinkets more situational (Worgenbane Talisman); items with biome effects (Woodlands, Mountains, Deserts) and creature-type effects (X'caliboar); every dungeon drop re-examined, hundreds of new drops, unique dungeon boss items now rare quality, set bonuses improved, quest rewards expanded (Mor'ladim adds the Ladimore Heirloom Ring; Nesingwary adds the Master Hunter's Spellsword), world-drop epics improved, hundreds of new rare-creature drops. (Blizzard recap; Wowhead)
- **Combat pacing:** an ordinary creature takes about 10 to 15 seconds solo; crowd control and threat matter in dungeons. (Blizzard recap)
- **Legacy system:** account-wide Legacy Points from leveling; 65 earnable at launch, 16 spendable per character; three trees at launch: Professions, Adventure, Resourcefulness; perks such as Well Rested, Bountiful Harvest, Reagent Economy; a Legacy Reward Track for cosmetics (Spectral Bear Cub); mounts granted on learning riding. A community Legacy planner already exists at wowforevertalents.com. (Blizzard recap)
- **Camping:** a Basic Campfire takes up to three crafted objects, one per player, one-hour cooldown; every profession has three objects at different skill levels (first at skill 20; advanced from Blueprint recipes dropped by dungeon bosses); Cooks upgrade campfires to 5 or 10 objects; buffs require sitting at the fire. (Blizzard recap)
- **Professions:** over 600 new recipes; crafted food grants experience and stat bonuses. (Blizzard recap)
- **Transmog:** global opt-out and a Classic Mode; appearances granted on loot for poor and common items, to all eligible looters for uncommon and rare dungeon drops, only to the binding player for epic raid drops; armor-type restrictions kept; level restrictions lifted at 60. (Blizzard recap)

Impact on the site: rankings and character pages key on region + ruleset, not realm (Phase 3 contract updated Sept 14); the gear stat model needs unified hit and crit plus expertise (Phase 2 stat weights); the Legacy trees are a second planner; four more new combos than the What's Next recap listed.
