## Who they are

The best Fury warrior on Forever plays two weapons as one rage engine. From forty, Bloodthirst goes out the moment it is ready, until Execute takes over at twenty percent, and everything else in the kit is arranged around that button. They never sit on a full rage bar, never let Battle Shout fall off, and never go into a boss without the food, elixirs and stones the pull asks for. What sets them apart is not a secret talent or a trick: it is the same short priority, pressed in the same order, for the whole fight.

## What they know

**The priority, in words.** This is the order they play. Bloodrage whenever it is ready and the bar is under eighty, because rage limits everything else Fury does. From thirty, Death Wish for the execute phase as the burst window, and from fifty Recklessness with it. Battle Shout kept up for the group. From twenty-four, in execute range, Execute comes first, ahead of Bloodthirst and Whirlwind. Otherwise Bloodthirst whenever it is off cooldown, since it is the button you lose the most by delaying. Whirlwind on cooldown right behind it, even on a single target. From thirty-one, Reckless Swings on cooldown, right behind Whirlwind once you have it at thirty-six: the next four white swings each bring five more rage, and the quarter of base health it costs is the healers' to cover. With a shaman's Windfury Totem in the group, Hamstring right behind it while the target is above twenty-five percent: ten rage for a main-hand hit that can trigger the totem's strike; without the totem, never. From thirty-six, Berserker Rage right behind it whenever you are under eighty rage, held for the fear on Magmadar, Onyxia and Nefarian. Before thirty-six you have no Whirlwind and fight in Battle Stance, so Overpower whenever it lights, Heroic Strike above sixty, and Execute from twenty-four carry you. From thirty-six you live in Berserker Stance with Whirlwind, and Bloodthirst joins at forty. Heroic Strike is the rage dump, only above sixty rage and never from twenty-five percent down, so the bar is full when Execute arrives.

**Cooldowns and how they line up.** Death Wish is on a three-minute cooldown. When the fight is long enough for a second use after this one, press it now; otherwise hold it for execute range, where Execute takes the extra damage, and never hold it so long that the fight ends before it runs out. From fifty, Recklessness is a once-a-fight button and goes in execute range too. On a short pull, press both in the closing seconds rather than not at all. Death Wish also raises the damage you take while it runs, which is worth knowing on a boss that hits the raid. Bloodrage is a minute-long cooldown that turns a sliver of health into rage, and it is pressed early enough that the rage lands while Bloodthirst still wants it. Battle Shout goes up just before the pull, so the first swings already carry it.

**What changed for Forever.** Whirlwind now strikes with both weapons. Unbridled Wrath's chance is up to sixty percent. Bloodthirst keeps its forty-five percent of attack power, adds a little flat damage, and gives ten percent movement speed for ten seconds. Reckless Swings is a Forever talent: twenty rage from four white swings every fifteen seconds, paid for in health. Fury is the dual-wield spec, so both weapons matter, and Dual Wield Specialization is what makes the off hand pull its weight.

**The mistakes seen most, by finding.**
- core_casts: Bloodthirst sitting ready with rage in the bar, because Whirlwind went first, Heroic Strike spent the rage it needed, or the warrior was walking.
- resource: rage at the cap. A full bar is rage thrown away; Heroic Strike is the answer, and Bloodrage pressed on a full bar is a wasted cooldown.
- idle: late to the boss after a move or a phase change, swinging at nothing while the boss walks.
- uptime: Battle Shout lapsing in a long fight, usually after a death or a phase change.
- adherence: buttons outside the list taking globals Bloodthirst and Whirlwind could have used.
- ranks: a rotation spell at an older rank than your level calls for, because the trainer visit was skipped.
- preparation: a missing food, elixir or sharpening stone, or a combat potion never used.
- death: dying inside Death Wish, or standing where the boss cleaves.

**Consumables and world buffs.** Before a boss you want Smoked Desert Dumplings, Elixir of the Mongoose, Juju Power, R.O.I.D.S. and Juju Might, a sharpening stone on each weapon (Elemental from fifty), and from forty-six the Mighty Rage Potion as often as its timer allows. No flask. World buffs are never expected: Forever raids have none, so a missing one is never held against anyone.

**Ten players against forty.** In a forty-player raid the buffs pile up: several warriors, a shaman or a paladin, every class's raid buff. On a ten-player night there may be no second warrior, so your Battle Shout is the only one the melee gets, and losing it costs more people than you. Fewer healers means a death lands harder too, and Death Wish's extra damage taken is a real cost.

## Drills

- drill.core_casts: From forty, on trash pulls, press Bloodthirst the moment it comes off cooldown, before anything else; Whirlwind and Heroic Strike wait their turn.
- drill.uptime: Put Battle Shout where you can see its timer. Recast it the moment it drops, and again the moment you are back up after a death or a phase change.
- drill.idle: On every move, keep the boss in range and swing the moment you arrive. Practise on trash: the first button after a reposition is Bloodthirst from forty, not a walk to the side.
- drill.resource: Watch the rage bar on trash pulls. Above 60 with Bloodthirst cooling down, queue Heroic Strike; at the cap, it should already be queued.
- drill.ranks: Visit your trainer every time you level, then check Bloodthirst, Whirlwind, Execute, Heroic Strike and Battle Shout against the rank your level calls for.
- drill.preparation: Before every boss, check food, elixirs, a stone on both weapons, and from forty-six a Mighty Rage Potion on your bars. Do it on the walk to the pull, not after it.
- drill.death: Before the pull, find the spot behind the boss, out of its cleave. With Death Wish running, keep a healing potion on a key you can reach without looking.
- drill.adherence: On trash pulls, play only Bloodrage, Death Wish, Battle Shout, Execute, Bloodthirst, Whirlwind, Reckless Swings, Berserker Rage and Heroic Strike for three minutes. Anything else on your bars waits.
