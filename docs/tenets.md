# Tenets

1. **The standard is the best WoW addon ever made.** Every surface we ship, in game and on the site, is judged against Details!, WeakAuras and ElvUI at their best, not against "it works". A plain table where an icon, a quality colour or a tooltip was available is a defect.
2. **An item is never just a name.** Wherever an item appears (BiS lists, planner slots, reports, tooltips, the addon), it shows its icon, its quality colour and, on hover, the real item: stats, item level, required level, source. In game that is the client's own item tooltip; on the site it is the shared item tooltip, the same way talents already work.
3. **An ability is never just a name.** Rotation lines carry the spell's icon, rank and the condition in plain words.
4. **Nothing is clipped, off-frame or hidden behind a disclosure when it is the point.** A runner-up, a swap note or a "load it" button is shown, not tucked away.
5. **Accuracy first, then polish, and both before ship.** A wrong number with a beautiful icon is still wrong; a right number in a plain list is still unshipped.
6. **Design before build, and two reviewers see it before you do.** A player-facing surface starts from an experience spec and mock written by the `ux-designer` agent and reviewed by the `wow-player` persona; the lane builds to the spec. Before merge, both review the rendered result on screenshots at phone and desktop with every state, never a diff. A green test suite is not "done"; two SHIP verdicts on screenshots are.
7. **Data reads like the game, not like the pipeline.** A source is one line a player recognises (boss and instance, quest and giver, vendor and faction, "World drop"), never a dump of every mob. A weight carries its unit and its DPS meaning. A pick shows its alternatives. Pipeline counters and coverage numbers stay out of the player's view.
