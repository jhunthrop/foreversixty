How to read the deltas:

- **Arcane's leveling build gains about 18%.** This is mostly Missile Barrage's second rank. The 70658 search had already found the same +18.9% ("deep Arcane"), and the 70685 respec takes that rank.
- **Arcane raid** gains Precision's extra 1% Arcane hit and two more ranks of Arcane Meditation.
- **Fire raid** loses about 1% after the item effect, to Critical Mass's two lost ranks.
- **Frost** holds within error in both bands.
- **The raid column's drop of about 5% is the item database's, not the talents'** (see Concerns).

The **Fire leveling adoption** passes every condition of the adoption rule:

- It is the variant that keeps every unmodeled guide talent.
- Fire holds 31 points, against 17/31/3, and Combustion is kept.
- It gains 4.9%, beyond error.
- It drops nothing in `data/curated/apl/mage-fire.json`.

What moves: Accelerant 5 → 1, Master of Elements 2 → 3, Arcane Meditation 0 → 2 and Arcane Resilience 1 → 2. Accelerant measures zero at 60 because Scorch is the level-60 filler (the APL's 2026-10-07 round). Fireball is cast only before Scorch is trained.

The **Frost leveling** winner qualifies on the rule alone:

- What it moves: Winter's Chill 5 → 0 and Frostbite 1 → 0, into Arcane Meditation 1 plus the filler that opens its row.
- Why it is held: its point order makes the level-40 ladder rung sim below level 30 (`TestRotationLadder` dps_regression, 56.4 against 57.2).
- A follow-up could re-spend Frost with a ladder-aware point order.

The guides' prose states the gains only as relative figures.
