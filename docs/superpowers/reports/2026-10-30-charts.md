# Chart lane report, 2026-10-30

Spec: `design/specs/2026-10-30-controls-charts.md` Part B, §C and §D. Branch `wt/charts`, not pushed.

## The library and the budgets

- **uPlot 1.6.32**, pinned exact (released 2025-03-14).
- **The chart chunk** holds uPlot, `TimeSeriesPlot`, `ChartTooltip` and `OverviewBrush`. It loads lazily: the report island starts the import when it mounts, in parallel with the summary fetch (`lib/chart/uplot-loader.ts`).
- **Measured** at gzip level 9:
  - 31,697 B at the stop;
  - 31,858 B on the final fixture build.
- **The cap.** The spec's 30 KB cap stopped the lane, and the coordinator ruled option 1: a 32 KB (32,768 B) cap.
  - The ruling is recorded in spec §B3 and §B11.
  - `scripts/check-island-size.mjs` now has a real entry for the chart chunk. It finds the chunk by uPlot's class name, because the file name is content-hashed.
  - Moving the tooltip and the brush into the entry measured 28,957 B for the chunk. But Rollup then split a 19 KB shared chunk out of `report-island.js`, so the page would have loaded no less. That move was not made.
- **`report-island.js`** is 124,315 B against its 143,360 B budget, which is unchanged.
  - Rollup now splits an 18,856 B shared chunk off the entry; the entry imports it statically.
  - So the boot cost is about 143.1 KB, against the spec's quoted ~129 KB.
  - The growth is the legend, the Players control, the legend sheet, the data adapters and the chart frame.
  - The budget script only weighs `report-island.js` itself, so it does not see this cost. It should learn to count the static imports.

## What shipped

- **Shared base** (`components/chart/`):
  - **`TimeSeriesChart`** is the frame. It reserves the loaded chart's exact height and shows the loading skeleton and the error state with Try again.
  - **`TimeSeriesPlot`** is the lazy chunk, with uPlot inside. It draws:
    - the crosshair, its time pill and the per-line dots;
    - drag to zoom with its label, through one `onWindow` shared with the brush, the presets and the URL;
    - double-click and Escape back to the whole fight;
    - the marker lane, with the 22 px step and leader, death clusters and gold clusters;
    - phase bands and their backed labels;
    - log scale, keyboard arrows and the live region;
    - a 300 ms press-and-hold scrub on touch, the event line and the one-time hint.
  - **`ChartTooltip`** is in the top layer. It shows the ±2 s events, sorted rows, the dead last, a 12-row cap, a flip in the right 55 % and the share column.
  - **`ChartLegend`**: toggle, hover highlight, `Only`, Alt-click, pinned chips, `YOU`, and on a phone one row with `+n`.
  - **`PlayersControl`** and **`LegendSheet`**.
  - **`OverviewBrush`**: the two existing range inputs are the handles. Pan by dragging the window body. Keys: arrows ±1 s, Shift ±10 s, PageUp and PageDown ±30 s, Home and End.
  - **`SyncedCrosshair`** puts the shared crosshair on the lane and SVG views.
- **Data adapters** (`lib/chart/`, every one unit-tested):
  - `drawn.ts`: top 8 with pinned players, the trailing 5 s average, medians, the sum reconciliation, stopping a line at a death.
  - `rate.ts` and `threat.ts`.
  - `events.ts`: the ±2 s window, collisions and clusters.
  - `report-markers.ts`, `tooltip.ts`, `choice.ts`.
  - `time-axis.ts` and `value-axis.ts`, `layout.ts`, `palette.ts`.
  - `cursor.svelte.ts`, `legend-state.svelte.ts`, `copy.ts` (the §B8 copy).
- **Damage, healing and damage taken** (`report/TimeChart.svelte`, rewritten; the hand-drawn canvas is deleted):
  - top 8 of N, plus the Source player and you, plus the raid median and a class median;
  - the head's figure is the sum of every player's rounded figure;
  - the submerge draws flat at zero;
  - `players=` and `scale=log` are in the URL;
  - the sample report keeps its 96 px strip.
- **Threat** (`report/ThreatChart.svelte`, with `ThreatTable` restyled):
  - "Estimated threat on {enemy}";
  - the tanks, the taunters and the attacked players are always drawn;
  - taunt markers, and target-change markers drawn from `target_changes` when the summary has it; tested with a fixture;
  - the share is of every player's estimated threat;
  - the caveat line links to the glossary's new `#glossary-threat` entry, which opens on the hash;
  - the old `threat-chart-note` (kept only for the night and for reports parsed before threat was kept second by second) and `threat-incomplete` paragraphs are replaced;
  - the Threat tab draws one chart, not two;
  - on a phone, the tab change scrolls the Source row to the top.
- **Deaths:** a death marker is an `<a>` to `?tab=deaths&death={guid}-{at_ms}`, keeping the fight and the window. It opens in place, and its accessible name is the killing-blow line.
- **The rest of the inventory:**
  - Resources: the synced crosshair, and the value at the cursor in the readout slot.
  - Timelines and Raid cooldowns: the synced crosshair, and labels from `time-axis.ts`.
  - The character history chart: its guide line takes the §B5.8 crosshair. It has no floating tooltip to restyle; its readouts sit in the panel.
  - The sim distribution: the band, the 2 px mean line and the axis labels.

## Fix round 2 (both reviewers on HOLD, coordinator rulings)

The verdicts are `design/reviews/2026-10-30-controls-charts/build-charts-ux-verdict.md` and `build-charts-player-verdict.md`. The rulings are recorded at the top of the spec, under "Fix round 2".

1. **Phone chart head.**
   - The head wraps: the title on its row, then Players, Window and Log scale each on a full-width row.
   - The Window field shows `Whole fight` whole. The e2e test measures both fields at 240 px or wider at 390.
2. **Threat title and the Enemy field agree.**
   - With `Every enemy` picked, the chart sums each player's estimated threat over every enemy and is titled `Estimated threat, every enemy`.
   - With an enemy picked, it reads `Estimated threat on {enemy}`.
   - The phone keeps the name. The tooltip head reads `{phase} phase · estimated threat, {subject}`.
   - To keep the 640 px fold, the phone drops the head's `Cumulative · …` meta; the brush under the plot carries the window there.
3. **`All N`.** The Players field never names a limit above the roster. Unit tests cover the copy; a server-render test covers `All 3`.
4. **Pinned first.**
   - The legend lists the medians, then the pinned players, then the rest. On a phone the pinned players come first of all, so the tanks are named before `+N`.
   - The phone foot keeps the estimate wording: `An estimate: 1 per damage, 0.5 per heal.` and the link.
5. **Damage defaults.**
   - The default draws the top 4 and the bottom 4, plus you, the Source player and the median. The head field reads `Top 4, bottom 4 of 40`.
   - The bottom is taken from the metric's own role, so on Damage done a healer's small damage never counts as a low performer.
   - A preset row sits in the Players control and the legend sheet: `Top 4 + bottom 4`, `Top 8`, `Bottom 8`, and `Custom`.
   - The URL carries `players=top` and `players=bottom`.
   - The median is now 2 px at 85 %. A hovered chip dims every other line but never the median, and `Only` keeps the median.
   - The threat chart keeps its top 8 by standing.
6. **Units and reconciliation.**
   - The head reads `Raid {n} DPS over the whole fight` (or `over {start} to {end}`).
   - Every legend chip carries `DPS`. On a phone only the first chip does, to keep the pinned names in the row.
   - The tooltip head reads `· DPS, 5 s average`.
   - The head, the legend and the Players list share one definition. A unit test, plus an e2e test, checks that the 40 players' figures sum to the head.
7. **Resources.** No damage chart is drawn above the rows, and the rows get a column head (`Player`, `Power`, `Over the fight`, `Time empty`).
8. **Death link.** `build-charts-death-link-before-1440.png` (the marker's tooltip) and `build-charts-death-link-after-1440.png` (the Deaths tab, that death open and in view).
