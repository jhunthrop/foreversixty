## Section 1. Subscribe and join (spec design/specs/2026-10-27-subscribe-and-join.md §12, rows 1–17)

### Overall verdict: NOT SHIPPABLE on these captures

The structure is right. A member who cannot pay gets one sentence and a link to send their GM, and a GM gets one button. Guild chat would still screenshot four things:
- "You lead OLYMPUS XXVII" printed next to "Member · Not verified".
- "Officer has started a claim".
- "Employed Millennials's".
- An empty gold ring where the Horde crest belongs.

The cards are also 376 px tall (532 px on phone) whatever is in them, so most of every card is dead space. The guild-step fixes below and a recapture are needed before I would post this in guild chat.

Systemic defects (each one appears in several rows; the row table refers to them by letter):

- **A. Fixed card height (product).** web/src/lib/billing/checkout-layout.ts:44, GUILD_CARD_MIN = 'min-h-[532px] md:min-h-[376px]'. Every card is as tall as the tallest possible card. Examples: a ready card with one sentence and one button (01b-claimed-crop-1440, 05-ready-and-on-plan-crop-1440), a phone card with 250 px of empty box above and below the trial button (01b-claimed-360, 06-after-success-crop-390), and a lone frozen card (08-frozen-crop-1440). It reads as unfinished. Fix: reserve that height on the loading skeleton only, as spec §5.7 says (156 / 260). Real cards size to their content.
- **B. The "you" row contradicts the step (fixture, and a check the product should make).** The row reads "Obnoxious Yell · Member · Not verified" on:
  - a card that says "You lead OLYMPUS XXVII" (01b-guild-master, 06-confirm-open);
  - "You hold OLYMPUS XXVII's claim" (01b-claimed, 05, 10);
  - "As an officer you can start one" (07-start-claim);
  - "Thornwick holds …" as shown to an officer (08-claimed-ask-officer);
  - "You pay for it" (09-you-pay, 09-ending, 09-you-pay-raid-chosen).

  guild-card-model.ts:51-52 reads rank and verified from me.characters[].guild, and the fixtures (web/src/test-support/subscribe-fixtures.ts) change only me.guilds[].rank. In production both move together. Fix: make the fixtures consistent (leader or officer, Verified, verified_count at least 1 on a claimed guild), then recapture. Separately, the Employed Millennials card has no "you" row at all, because the owner fixture has no character in that guild. Spec row 1 says the owner is a member in both. Add the second character so the card shows who the player is in that guild.
- **C. Empty crest rings (fixture, and a fallback worth checking).** Every checkout card shows a bare 40 px gold ring. The same guild's own page (16-guild-note-no-approver-1440) shows the Horde crest in its ring. The stepGuild fixture sets no faction. Fix: give the fixtures faction 'horde' and confirm the FactionMark fallback renders. A Forever guild always has a faction, so an empty ring should never reach a player.
- **D. The lead sentence tells the player the next step is someone else's when it is theirs (product copy).** 01b-guild-master, 06-confirm-open, 07-start-claim, 07-confirm-claim, 08-blocked-rate-limited and 08-blocked-holds-other all lead with "Only a guild's guild master can claim it here and subscribe it. … so the next step is theirs." The card under it says "You lead OLYMPUS XXVII. Claim it here" or "As an officer you can start one". Fix: when any card is claim, start_claim or confirm_claim, lead with "You can claim a guild here. Each card below has its next step." When the only blocked guild is the viewer's own (claim_blocked), lead with "Here is the next step for each of your guilds."
- **E. "Millennials's" (product copy).** 01-member and every row with the second guild show "Only Employed Millennials's guild master can claim it" and "LINK FOR EMPLOYED MILLENNIALS'S GUILD MASTER". Fix: avoid the possessive for any name ending in s, e.g. "Only the guild master of Employed Millennials can claim it and subscribe it. Send them this link." and "Link for the guild master of Employed Millennials". Apply the same rule to "{Guild}'s claim" and "{Guild}'s plan".
- **F. "Officer has started a claim on OLYMPUS XXVII." (product).** 07-confirm-claim. guild-card-model.ts:159 and claim-flow.ts:26 fall back to the rank word "Officer" as if it were a name. The fallback also reaches the confirm ("Confirm Officer's claim …?") and the done line ("Confirmed. Officer holds …"). Fix, with no name: "An officer has started a claim on {Guild}. As an officer you can confirm it."; confirm "Confirm the claim on {Guild}? The officer who started it will run the guild here and can subscribe it."; done "Confirmed. The claim on {Guild} is settled."
- **G. The verify line tells the guild master to wait for the guild master (product copy).** On 08-blocked-rate-limited and 08-blocked-holds-other the viewer is the GM, and the card ends "… or once the guild master claims OLYMPUS XXVII and approves you." On 08-claimed-ask-member the guild is already claimed and still reads "once the guild master claims". Fix: no verify sub-line on claim_blocked. On a claimed guild, use "Not verified yet. You are verified when you turn up in the guild's logs, or once the claim holder approves you."
- **H. The link field does not look like a field (product).** On the checkout cards the URL is bare mono text with no box (01-member-crop-1440: "https://foreversixty.gg/guild/us/pvp/o"), because the field's bg-raised matches the card's ground. On the guild page the same control (17-rotated) has its box. Fix: inside a card, give the field a visible ground (--card-top) or a stronger border, so the player can see what Copy link copies.

## Section 2. Launch pricing, Premium value amendment (spec design/specs/2026-10-27-launch-pricing.md B9, rows 15–22)

### Overall verdict: SHIP WITH FIXES (blocked on the real example run)

In five seconds I know what is free, what Premium does for my character, and what it costs: $40 a year, $50 after 6 Jan 2027. Every Premium line says what it does for me ("Try up to 5,000 gear, talent or drop combinations … Your browser stops at 400."). The scope note is honest about healers and tanks. The guild region gives a GM the per-raider figure for 40, 20 and 10, the trial terms, and "One officer pays". The interval switch moves nothing. Two things stop it:
- The "real server run" is a broken image. The data file is the placeholder (web/src/data/premium-examples.json: "placeholder": true, simId "placeholder-run", no web/public/premium/ files), so the page claims "A REAL SERVER RUN" over nothing, and "Open this result" points at a run that does not exist.
- On /sim that empty box is 1214 px wide.
