## Part 1: Subscribe and join (SJ §12 screenshot table, rows 1–17)

### Shared fixes (referenced by row)

**G1. Guild cards carry a 376 px floor (532 px phone). Blocker.**
- What the viewer sees: every guild card is the height of the tallest possible card. A `ready` card has about 250 px of empty navy under one sentence and one button (rows 1b, 5, 6, 10). On a 390 phone the `ready` card is 530 px tall with the button floating in the middle (row 5 @390, row 6 after-success @390).
- Spec: §7 says a `ready` card is 156 px desktop and 260–360 px phone. §5.7 says the skeleton reserves 156 / 260.
- Change:
  - Remove `GUILD_CARD_MIN` from the real card (`GuildStepCard.svelte:48`). Cards size to their content.
  - Set the skeleton card to `h-[260px] md:h-[156px]` (§5.7).
  - Set `CHECKOUT_FRAME_PX.card` to `{ phone: 260, md: 156 }`.
  - Re-measure CLS from loading to ready at 390 and 1440. The cap is 0.02.
- This also fixes the §1 answer at 1280 x 800. Today the second card starts at y 692 in row 5.

**G2. The pre-paint frame reserve stays on `main` after the rows render.**
- What the viewer sees:
  - Row 10 @1440: about 470 px of empty page between the foot and the site footer.
  - Row 11 Empty A @360: the panel ends at y 430 and the footer starts at y 1515.
  - Row 2 @1440: about 380 px of empty page.
- Change:
  - The `min-height` in `checkout.astro:109–123` holds only while the step is loading.
  - When rows, an empty state, the error or the premium panel render, remove it: clear the custom property, or drop the class.
  - Under G1 the reserve becomes the skeleton height, so releasing it moves nothing that is on screen.

**G3. The copy-link field is invisible on a card.**
- What the viewer sees: on a guild card the URL looks like loose mono text. Pixel check, row 1 @1440: the field's ground is `#0d111a` and the card's is `#0d111a`, with no border pixels. There is nothing to show that the URL is a field you can select.
- Change: inside a `card`, give `CopyLinkField`'s input `--bg` (`#07090d`) as its ground. That is the same contrast `GuildInviteLink` has on the page ground. Keep `border-line-warm`. Leave `GuildInviteLink`'s own render as it is, so capture 17 stays identical (P4 aside).

**G4. The copy field shows a visible label on the checkout card.**
- What the viewer sees: an 11 px label in tracked caps (`LINK FOR OLYMPUS XXVII'S GUILD MASTER`) sits between the step sentence and the URL. It wraps to two lines for Employed Millennials. It adds 34–51 px per card. At 360 it pushes `Copy link` to y 599–643, past the 640 fold that §1 requires.
- Spec: §5.5 and §9.1 call it the "sr label".
- Change: add a `labelVisible` prop to `CopyLinkField`, default `true` so `GuildInviteLink` keeps its `INVITE LINK` label. Set it to `false` on the checkout card and on the claim page member hand-off. With `false`, the label span gets `sr-only`.

**G5. The copy buttons are not full width on a phone.**
- Spec: §5.2 says buttons and the copy field go full width below 768. The §5.8 phone wireframe shows `[ COPY LINK ]  [ SHARE ]` across the card.
- Today: `Copy link` is `w-fit`, 97 px wide (rows 1, 7 and 8 @360/390).
- Change: below 768, the button row in `CopyLinkField` is `grid grid-cols-1` (`grid-cols-2` when Share exists), and each button is `w-full justify-center`. From 768, keep `w-fit`.

**G6. The lead sentence tells a player who can act that the next step is someone else's.**
- What the viewer sees:
  - Row 1b: the card says `You lead OLYMPUS XXVII. Claim it here, then subscribe it.` The lead above it says `Neither of your guilds has been claimed yet, so the next step is theirs.`
  - Rows 6, 7 and 8 (claim_blocked): the single-guild form `Your guild has not been claimed yet, so the next step is theirs.` appears above `You lead …`, `Start a claim` and `Confirm the claim` cards.
- Spec: row 1b requires the some-claimed form.
- Change: choose the lead in this order.
  1. Any card is `ready`: `Choose the guild this plan is for.`
  2. Any card is `claim`, `start_claim`, `confirm_claim`, `claim_link_bnet` or `claim_blocked`: `Only a guild's guild master can claim it here and subscribe it. Here is the next step for each of your guilds.` (the existing §9.1 string).
  3. Otherwise, the §9.1 table as written.
- SJ §9.1 is amended to match.

**G7. The fixtures contradict themselves, so the captures show states that cannot happen.** All of these are in `web/src/test-support/subscribe-fixtures.ts`:
- **a. Rank and verified on the You row.** The You row reads `me.characters[].guild.rank/verified`. `stepGuild()` sets only the guild entry. So every card reads `Member · Not verified`, including:
  - `You lead …` (1b, 6, 8 claim_blocked)
  - `You hold OLYMPUS XXVII's claim` with `1 verified` (1b claimed, 5, 6 after, 10)
  - officer steps (7, 8 claimed_ask officer)
  - `You pay for it` (9)

  Fix: `stepGuild` takes the character too, or the builder derives `OWNER_CHARACTER.guild` from the first guild's `rank` and `verified`. Claim holders and payers are `leader` and `verified: true`. The officer steps are `officer`.
- **b. The owner's second character.** Capture 1 must match production. The owner has a character in Employed Millennials, and spec E2E 1 says each card shows it with `Member · Not verified`. Today that card has no You row at all (rows 1, 1b, 5). Add the owner's second character with `guild.id = 3`.
- **c. Faction.** `stepGuild` has no `faction`, so every crest is an empty 40 px gold ring, which reads as a missing image. Set the faction production holds (`horde` for both of the owner's guilds), and `crest_url` if production has one. The empty ring stays legal for a guild with neither.
- **d. Seats.** A Guild plan has 1 Premium seat (`PLANS.guild.seats`). Rows 5 and 9 show `1 of 3` and `All 3` on `Guild plan`. Set `seats_total: 1` on the Guild fixtures, or make those fixtures `raid_guild`.
- **e. Payer name and verified count.**
  - `billed_by` and `holder_name` go only to a verified viewer (§8). Rows 8 claimed_ask officer and 9 show them to a viewer marked `Not verified`. Make those viewers verified.
  - A claimed guild has at least 1 verified member, the holder. Rows 8 claimed_ask and checkout_open show `No one verified yet`. Give them `verified_count ≥ 1`.
