# Client 1.60.1.70425: the engine, the guides and the addon follow the new trees

2026-10-16. Follows `2026-10-07-live-trees-rework.md`.

## What happened

The client watcher (client.yml) found 1.60.1.70425, fetched its tables and activated it
(911a9b1b) the way it was built to. Every downstream run on that main failed, because
the move assumed the talent trees had not changed shape. They had:

| Class | Tree change in 1.60.1.70425 |
|---|---|
| Mage | Arcane gains Arcane Wrath (tier 3, behind Arcane Blast); Missile Barrage moves to tier 0 with two ranks; Wake of Fire becomes Improved Fire Blast; Critical Mass 3 to 5 ranks; Fire Power 5 to 3 ranks and a new per-effect mechanic. |
| Priest | Shadow Affinity's node becomes Improved Mind Blast (5 ranks) and the tier-2 Improved Mind Blast node is gone; Improved Fade becomes Dark Inspiration; Mental Strength moves to tier 1, Improved Power Word: Shield to tier 2. |
| Warlock | Malediction becomes Grim Reach (range, 2 ranks). |
| Shaman | Tidal Focus and Totemic Focus swap positions; Elemental reshuffles (Elemental Warding and Call of Flame move, Lava Burst to tier 4, Elemental Mastery becomes the capstone node, Call of Thunder is gone, Improved Fire Nova becomes Trial by Fire). |
| Druid | Predatory Instincts becomes Natural Instinct (adds healing from Intellect). |

The guide re-encoder carried talents by id, which put ranks on whatever talent now wore the
id (Totemic Focus's five points landed on Tidal Focus, Call of Thunder's point on Earthbound,
Call of Flame's three on Elemental Alacrity), and left the clamped builds short of 51 points.
The addon's default builds were derived from those codes. The engine fork's compiled
talent layout was still 70374's, so every mage, priest, warlock and shaman talent string the
site built read wrong in the sim.

## What changed

**Guides.** `web/scripts/reencode-guide-builds.mjs` now carries every talent by its name
within its tree, and exits non-zero without writing when a name is gone, a rank no longer
fits or the result is short or illegal, so an activation stops before it can commit a wrong
build. The eight builds that could not carry cleanly were resolved by hand, each change
named in the guide's prose:

- mage/fire (both): Critical Mass 5/5 (Combustion's prerequisite), Fire Power 3/3.
- priest/shadow: leveling Improved Mind Blast 4/5 (Shadow Affinity's point); raid Shadow Reach 1/2.
- warlock/affliction: leveling Pandemic 3/3 and Bane 5/5, raid Pandemic 3/3 (Malediction's points).
- warlock/demonology: Bane 5/5. warlock/destruction: Cataclysm 3/3.
- shaman/elemental: leveling Lava Burst (Call of Thunder's point), raid Thundering Strikes 3.
- shaman/enhancement: Elemental Alacrity 3 and Tidal Focus 5 (Elemental Warding and Totemic Focus moved a tier deeper).
- priest/holy, shaman/restoration: the id-carry had moved Meditation and Tidal Focus; restored.

**Activation.** `tools/activate-build.sh` rebuilds `addon-builds.json` from the moved guides
(`sim/cmd/addon-builds`) instead of carrying the old build's forward, and re-stamps the API's
Battle.net import fixture. client.yml's activate job gets a Go toolchain for it.

**API.** `bnetbuild`'s tables test reads the newest build directory instead of pinning a
literal.

**Sim.** The Immovable Object's block value pin follows the client (25 since 70425).

**Engine fork** (wowsims-forever, branch `forever`). `make talents TALENT_BUILD=1.60.1.70425`,
`make proto`, `make spellconst BUILD=1.60.1.70425`; the conformance client fixtures and
trainables copied from the 70425 build; the conformance goldens regenerated with zero
mismatch rows. Besides the layout, the client changed these rates and mechanics, and the
engine follows each (sim/core/testdata/conformance/TALENTS.md carries the row-by-row read):

- Arcane Blast: stacks grant 15% of Intellect as spell power (was +10% other-spell damage), 15 sec from the first stack, not renewed; 1.5 sec cast; rows 32/78/101/168/236, coefficient 0.429.
- Arcane Wrath: new talent nuke (3.5 sec cast, 6 sec cooldown, +5% of maximum mana as spell damage), registered under its talent; feeds Missile Barrage.
- Missile Barrage: two ranks, 20/5/10% a rank on Arcane Blast / Arcane Missile hits / the bolts.
- Fire Power: 1% a rank per Fire effect on the target, four at most (Fireball, Pyroblast and Flamestrike dots, Ignite, Improved Scorch).
- Critical Mass five ranks; Heating Up gains a stack on every landed Fire Blast; Ice Lance x3 on Frozen (was x4); Winter's Chill stacks to five at every rank and feeds Cone of Cold (+40% a stack) instead of Frost crit.
- Priest: Mental Strength 2% a rank; Improved Mind Flay is a compounding per-tick ramp; Dark Inspiration (+3% Spirit a rank for 10 sec from Mind Blast or a full Mind Flay); Divine Aegis capped at 20 a level; Devouring Plague 24 sec cooldown, Shadow Word: Death 18 sec, Vampiric Embrace 30 sec; Holy Fire rank 5 ticks 9; Inner Fire uses Forever's flat-armour aura.
- Warlock: Shadow Mastery 2% a rank. Shaman: Call of Flame lengthens Flame Shock 3 sec a rank; Trial by Fire adds Magma Totem duration; Water Shield spends a globe on any spell crit; Lava Burst ranks at 30/40/50 (135/165/230 mana); Chain Lightning 145/200/250/315 and Fire Nova 65/120/210/290/375 mana; Stormstrike 8% of base mana; Mana Tide Totem four ranks.
- Paladin: Champion of the Light 10/20/30%; Shield Specialization 4% mana; Judgement of Righteousness and Judgement of Fury coefficient 0.429; Consecration's first-targets coefficient 0.042; Seal of Fury rank 3 at 26 (its judgement at 25).
- Hunter: Resourcefulness 50/100%; Multi-Shot instant; pet Dismember 15 sec cooldown. Druid: Natural Instinct's healing from Intellect.

Not modeled, named: Frost Blast (a new baseline Frost nuke, 1326053) has no spell in the
engine yet and no curated rotation casts it; Dark Inspiration's party share; Reckoning's
four-attack store; Arcane Wrath is registered but no curated rotation casts it yet.

## Follow-ups

- Curated rotations: mage-arcane should consider Arcane Wrath; mage-frost Frost Blast once it exists; the talent search should re-run on the new trees (bis.yml's forced ranking covers the picks, not the builds).
- The fork's item-database suite (`make test`, which is `go test --tags=with_db`) was run in a second pass: the preset goldens regenerate for the new rates and the geared-character tests pin the client's numbers (fork commit eb3dc7793).
