# wow-player review: Protection warrior prose, 2026-11-12

Surfaces read: the guide (web/src/content/guides/warrior/protection.md), the coach priority paragraph (data/curated/coach/warrior-protection.md) and the two card notes in the APL json (Bloodrage 2687, Thunder Clap 11581). No em dashes found in any changed line, and no site or sim talk except the two phrases flagged below.

## What is true and worth saying

- Bloodrage costing a fifth of base health is real information. Classic players remember it as a small health cost; a number is new and a tank should know it.
- Revenge growing with attack power is the best addition here. It tells a tank why strength and attack power on gear are not wasted.

## MUST

1. Card note, Bloodrage: "so it will not fire when you are nearly dead". "Fire" is addon and sim speak; the player is pressing a button. It also says "nearly dead", but the rule is health below the cost. Replacement:
   "It costs a fifth of your base health, so you cannot press it when you are that low."
2. Guide, The pull: "which is nothing to a tank at full health and the reason it refuses to fire when you are nearly dead." Same problem ("refuses to fire"), and "nothing" is overstated: the healers see it, and it comes back every minute. Replacement for the whole added sentence:
   "Bloodrage takes a fifth of your base health, so pull with it before the first hit lands and let the healers top you up, and do not press it mid-fight when you are low."
3. Coach: "it costs a fifth of your base health, which a tank at full health never notices." Same overstatement, and the coach is the voice a guildmate would use: a guildmate would say it costs health and when to avoid it. Replacement:
   "Bloodrage while rage is under sixty; it costs a fifth of your base health, so not when you are already low."
4. Card note, Thunder Clap: "and its damage grows with your attack power." This reads as if attack power matters for Thunder Clap. At about one sixteenth of attack power per hit it is a few dozen points of damage, not a reason to do anything. Either say the size or drop it. Replacement:
   "Keep it on the boss: its attack speed slow is a straight cut to the damage you take. The damage itself adds a sixteenth of your attack power, which is small. Refresh it in its last 3 seconds."

## SHOULD

5. Guide, talent bullet: "Revenge and Thunder Clap both grow with your attack power, Revenge by a quarter of it." This is in the bullet about talent points, where it does not belong, and it gives a share for Revenge but none for Thunder Clap. Move it to the Rotation section next to the Revenge line and use:
   "Revenge adds a quarter of your attack power to every hit, which is why attack power on gear counts for a tank. Thunder Clap adds about a sixteenth."
   If it stays in the talent bullet, use the same wording after "These are the three buttons you press most after Shield Slam."
6. Guide, Improved Bloodrage bullet: "so with the talent you buy more rage for the same health." This explains the obvious; "half again as much rage" already says it, and the health cost is now stated in the pull paragraph. Remove the added sentence. Replacement bullet:
   "**Improved Bloodrage** (2 points): half again as much rage from every Bloodrage."
7. Players cannot read "base health" off the character panel. If the level 60 figure is known, give it once in the pull paragraph ("about X health at 60"). Not verified by me; the accuracy reviewer should supply the number or confirm it is omitted on purpose.

## NIT

8. The Bloodrage cost now appears in four places (guide pull, guide talent bullet, coach, card). After fixes 2 and 6 it is three, which is enough: guide pull, coach, card.
9. Coach paragraph still says only "its attack-speed slow" for Thunder Clap and nothing on Revenge scaling. Fine for the priority paragraph; no change needed.

## Verdict

NO-SHIP as written. The facts are right and the Revenge point is useful, but "will not fire" and "refuses to fire" read like the sim talking, "never notices" and "nothing" overstate a cost healers do see, and the Thunder Clap line implies a benefit that is tiny. Apply MUST 1 to 4 and SHOULD 5 and 6 and it is a SHIP.
