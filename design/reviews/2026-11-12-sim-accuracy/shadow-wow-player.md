# Wand Shoot notes, Shadow priest and mage: wow-player review

## Surfaces reviewed
- Shadow priest Rotation and priority page, Shoot row note (new text).
- Mage (Arcane, Fire, Frost) Rotation and priority page, Shoot row note (new text).
- Shadow guide prose, web/src/content/guides/priest/shadow.md, leveling lines 39, 121-133.

## What a player reads
Shadow: wand only when nothing else is castable, it cannot start inside the global
cooldown so it does not fit between spells, the first shot lands half a second after
you press it, then the wand's speed until you cast, and under 28 that is most of your
damage so let it run. That matches how a priest actually wands: press Shoot, a short
windup, then a steady stream. "Let it run" is the right advice at leveling.

Mage: wand when dry and waiting on a gem, a rune or Evocation, same windup rule, no
"most of your damage" line. Correct, a mage wands only as a last resort.

## Lines to fix
- Shadow: "below 28 that is most of your damage". Says "below 28" with no unit. Fix:
  "below level 28". Minor.
- Shadow guide line 39, "so early on you wand between casts", sits slightly against
  the note's "it never fits between spells". A player reads it as "wand between
  casts" vs "never between spells". Fix the guide to "so early on the wand carries you
  between DoTs" or similar; the note itself is right (you wand when nothing else is
  castable, and keep wanding). Minor, not a blocker.
- Neither note is meta, over-explained, or contains an em dash. No sim talk.

## Data I believe
Half a second windup, first shot at global cooldown end after an instant, a windup
after a channel: consistent with the 0.50 s start-to-fire gap the logs show. Nothing
here a player would contradict.

## Verdicts
- priest-shadow: SHIP, the note now matches how wanding feels and agrees with "Until 28 the wand is your filler"; fix "below 28" to "below level 28" when next touched.
- mage: SHIP, accurate, short, and keeps the right advice to wand only when out of mana.
