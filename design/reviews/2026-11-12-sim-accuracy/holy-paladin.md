Lane brief: `design/reviews/sim-accuracy/brief.md`. Lane `sim-holypal`. Site from origin/main aafe382b5, fork from master 0fb4dcebb (the pin at the start). ## Method

1. **Blizzard since 10 Nov.** "WoW: Forever Hotfixes" (t/2403040) posts 7 and 8 (12 Nov, the "November 11, 2026" notes), read as the Discourse JSON: the paladin lines are the Judgement and Unsealed Fury lines the paladins lane landed; nothing for Holy, nothing on healing, Illumination, auras or Spirit. "Class Tuning Incoming" (t/2404092) has no Blizzard post after 6 Nov and no paladin line.
2. **Client and push.** wago.tools CSVs for 1.60.1.70790 and 1.60.1.70888, both with the push layered (`useHotfixes=1`): SpellEffect, SpellAuraOptions, SpellPower, SpellClassOptions, SpellMisc, SpellLevels, SpellCooldowns, SpellCategories, SpellDuration, Spell and SpellName, read for the 65 ids the Holy card and builds touch (every Holy Light, Flash of Light, Holy Shock cast, heal and damage row, Light's Vigil, Oath of Light, Divine Favor, Illumination and its energizes, Infusion of Light, Spiritual Focus, Healing Light, Holy Power, Boundless Light, Reverence, Benediction, Conviction, Divine Intellect, Concentration Aura, Blessing of Wisdom, Lay on Hands). No row differs between the two builds (two SpellEffect rows of Light's Vigil change order only). The site's `hotfix-push.json` carries no row for any of them. The engine was then read line by line: `sim/paladin/{healing,holy_light,flash_of_light,holy_shock,lights_vigil,oath_of_light,divine_favor,healing_talents,blessings,blessing_ranks,items,item_sets_pve,item_sets_tier1}.go`, the Holy half of `talents.go`, `holy/holy.go`, `core/{mana,cast,spell_result,spell_outcome,buffs}.go` for the regen, pushback, heal crit and aura models, and `sim/request/request.go` for what the site asks of the healer.
3. **Real fights.** foreverlogs.gg's 25 public reports (2815 to 2853, 5 to 12 Nov): every paladin on every roster (354 rows) and every damage, cast, resource, buff and healing event each did (224,588 events, `meta_events`, 500 a page). Six paladins heal: three in the Holy tree, Onyooms (2834, level 30 to 33 by her ranks: 241 Flash of Light rank 2 and 67 Holy Light rank 4 heals, 10 Divine Favors, 13 Oaths of Light, 24 Illumination returns), Kiyo (2818, 22 to 25, 158 heals) and Holy (2825, 20, 124 heals), plus Bool (2815, 20 Holy Lights), Sallie (2845) and Ploppa (2842, 42 to 43, a Retribution paladin healing with Holy Light rank 6 and Flash of Light rank 4, 203 heals). No logged paladin in the Holy tree is above 33, so Holy Shock, Holy Power, Boundless Light and Light's Vigil still have no log. The healing stream carries effective amounts and no overheal event, so overheal cannot be read; crit shares, crit sizes, proc ids, amounts and timings can. Our API's public reports are unchanged since 8 Nov (gxrgvz4yzsi2, z2opt2x2gs5u) and hold no Holy paladin of use (the 11 Nov lane).
4. **Sims** on the Windows box through `tools/remote-test.sh` (`--pin-engine` on the old pin, `--engine-sha` on the lane's fork branch; `nice -n 15`, GOMAXPROCS=5, one job at a time; the runner and job files are in `holy-paladin/`): `spec-breakdown -heal` before and after at raid 60 and bare 60 (both factions, seeds 1 and 50001) and the bare bands 20 to 50 (both factions), 10,000 iterations; the Holy rank rows at their boundary levels on the fixed engine (both factions, seeds 1 and 50001); the leveling order's Illumination step at 24; the fork's core, paladin, priest, healer and common suites (`-tags with_db`) in a clone on the box; the Holy ladder golden.

Where it comes from. Concentration Aura gives back the casting time the pulses took: at raid 60 Flash of Light casts a fight go 136.5 to 142.1 and Holy Shock 7.8 to 8.0 for +0.8%; the bare bands below 50 spend their whole bar on Flash of Light and gain the same few casts. The Illumination regen was worth far more than its mana return: with Illumination at five points every crit opened 15 s of half the paladin's Spirit regeneration while casting, which on a healer critting every few seconds was a standing +50% casting regen on top of Reverence's 30%. Without it the raid 60 bar runs dry at 195 s instead of 242 and the bare 60 bar at 50 s instead of 55, and the bare 30 to 50 bands, which lived on that regen for two thirds of their fights, lose a fifth of their healing. Band 20 has one point of Illumination and loses 2%. The ranker's Holy picks and weights move with it (MR85): crit is worth less as a mana stat, Spirit is worth less while casting, mp5 and Intellect more.
