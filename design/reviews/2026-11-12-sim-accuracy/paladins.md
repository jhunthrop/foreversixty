Lane brief: `design/reviews/sim-accuracy/brief.md`. Lane `sim-paladins`. Site from origin/main e9eddbd7f, fork from master 4f8bb748d (the pin at the start). Client on the site: 1.60.1.70790; live client 1.60.1.70888. Scope: Retribution and Protection, end to end: engine against the client, the hotfix push and Blizzard's notes through 12 Nov; engine against the first logs since the 11 Nov lane; builds, rotations and rank rows under the adoption rule, with Protection re-judged on the 48 s dungeon pull (MR50). Holy was audited 2026-11-11 and is touched only where a shared spell moves it.

**Totals: 4 confirmed engine bugs fixed (2 new data from Blizzard's 11 November hotfix, 2 old core bugs the hotfix exposed) plus 1 old naming gap closed from the logs. Judgement is a ranged attack (no melee proc fires on it, its hit is the ranged table's); Unsealed Wrath stores no copy of the Seal from a Windfury Totem extra attack; a relic in the ranged slot no longer doubles the wearer's weapon skill (Hammer of Wrath never missed below the cap); the four ranged-hit items give nothing without a bow, gun, crossbow or thrown weapon; Seal of Fury's absorb is the client's own spell Light's Fury. Retribution raid 60 -3.8% Alliance and -4.3% Horde, bare 45 to 60 -0.1 to -0.8%, bare 20 to 40 unchanged; Protection unchanged to the hundredth; Holy unchanged. Protection's leveling order and its mana gates hold on the 48 s pull; its Consecration rank 1 row is now 30 to 38 (the 40 to 48 and 50 to 57 rows are gone), Retribution's rank 3 row ends at 57 for the Alliance and its Hammer of Wrath rank 1 rows are gone. 0 open engine items for the owner beyond the standing Templar's Bulwark ruling. Engine pin 4f8bb748d to 442d43ace (main had moved to the shadow lane's fea547248 by the landing; the pin is the lane's ten commits rebased on it).**

## Method

1. **Blizzard since 10 Nov.** "WoW: Forever Hotfixes" (t/2403040) posts 7 and 8 (12 Nov, the "November 11, 2026" notes), read as the Discourse post JSON for the verbatim text. Paladin lines: "Judgement changed to now be considered a Ranged Attack. This means it no longer counts as a Melee attack for the purposes of item and effect procs and uses the Paladin's Ranged Hit chance." and, under Retribution, "With the fix to Windfury Totem activating from Holy Strike, we have changed Unsealed Fury. Unsealed Fury no longer generates echoes from Windfury Totem attacks." (Blizzard's "Unsealed Fury" is the Unsealed Wrath talent; the client has no spell of the former name.) Nothing for Protection or Holy. "Class Tuning Incoming" (t/2404092) has no post after 6 Nov.
2. **Client and push.** Both lines are server side: the 70888 tables still file the judgements on the melee table (SpellCategories DefenseType 2, SpellMisc Attributes_0 0x200000 since 70707), so the engine carries the ranged attack by hand with the post named in the comment, as the 10 Nov shaman and hunter rows do. The engine was read against `data/builds/1.60.1.70790/spellconst/paladin.json` and `talents/paladin.json` for every spell, talent and aura the two cards and builds touch; the 11 Nov lane's table stands and is not repeated. One client row the engine had not read: 1310927 "Light's Fury", the Seal of Fury absorb (aura 69, every school, 10 s), which the engine had named with an unused id.
3. **Real fights.** foreverlogs.gg reports 2849, 2850 and 2853 (11 and 12 Nov, the three since the 11 Nov lane: 113 paladins, 31,054 events) and, for Protection, the three Seal of Fury paladins of 2834 (Heaven at 30 to 35 with Seal of Fury rank 3, Seal of Command, Holy Strike rank 4 and Consecration rank 2, 17,009 events; Ewli at 34 to 38 with Holy Shield rank 1 and Seal of Fury rank 4; Arkhram at 40 with Holy Shield rank 2 and Reckoning), every damage, cast, resource, buff and healing event, plus the debuff stream for the five paladins judged against their own Judgement of the Crusader. Our API's public reports are unchanged since 8 Nov (gxrgvz4yzsi2, z2opt2x2gs5u). No paladin in any public log is above 43, so Hammer of Wrath, the twist and Shieldsworn still have no log; the 11 Nov Judgement change cannot be seen in a log yet (no logged paladin fights under a Windfury Totem or with a proc weapon).
4. **Sims** on the Windows box through `tools/remote-test.sh` (`--pin-engine` on the old pin, `--engine-sha` on the lane's fork branch; `nice -n 15`, GOMAXPROCS=5, one sim at a time; the job files and runner are in `paladins/`): spec-breakdown before and after at raid 60 and the bare bands 20 to 60, both factions, seeds 1 and 50001, 10,000 iterations; the Protection walk at every level 20 to 59 on the pull; the order steps, the mana gates and the Consecration rank rows level by level; the Retribution rank rows at their boundary levels on the new engine; the fork's paladin and core suites (`-tags with_db`) in a clone on the box; the three paladin ladder goldens.

## Confirmed and fixed

| # | finding | evidence | origin | fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| J1 | **Judgement was a melee attack.** Judgement of Command carried `ProcMaskMeleeMHSpecial`, so Windfury Totem (20% an attack), the weapon's enchant and Hand of Justice fired on every one, and all three damage judgements rolled the main hand's attack table (a Human's sword or mace racial reached them). Blizzard's 11 Nov note makes Judgement a ranged attack: no melee proc, the ranged hit table. Now the Judgement ability and every judgement it resolves to (Righteousness, Command, Fury, Light, Wisdom) carry `CastType` ranged and the damage ones `DefenseType` ranged with `ProcMaskRangedSpecial`; the miss is the ranged table's (the class's base skill, no weapon racial), never dodged, parried or blocked, as before; the crit roll is unchanged. Red first: `TestJudgementsAreRangedAttacks` (the three damage judgements and the two debuff judgements). The ranged table it moved to was wrong for every published paladin until R1 and R2 below. | t/2403040 post 8 | new data (11 Nov hotfix) | E 8dec0b88c (`judgement.go`, `soc.go`, `sor.go`, `seal_of_fury.go`, `utility_seals.go`) |
| J2 | **Unsealed Wrath stored a copy from a Windfury Totem extra attack.** An extra attack is a cast of the main hand swing, so `isSealSwing` counted it and a stack was stored (and the 15 s refreshed).
