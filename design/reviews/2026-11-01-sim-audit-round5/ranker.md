# Sim-accuracy round 5: ranker lane

Lane `ranker`, site branch `wt/r5-ranker`. Owns `sim/cmd/leveling-bis`. No engine change.

Measurements need item data that has the round-4 stat fixes. data.yml has failed on main since round 4 merged (addon-data drift and a dps-per-point test), so the committed `items/<class>.json` and `simdb.bin` still date from 2026-10-31. "Published" below is the committed BiS set measured on that regenerated data.

### With the warlock lane's engine fix (fork fce7dd53d)

Fork fce7dd53d (lane/r5-warlock) removes a hidden third of item healing that the engine also counted as spell damage. Glowing Brightwood Staff carries 150 healing, so on f8a5f94ce it dealt about 50 spell power the item does not have. Both rankers were re-run on f8a5f94ce plus fce7dd53d (cherry-picked onto a local branch, not pushed), with spec-breakdown built on the same engine:

| spec | faction | published | before | after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arcane | alliance | 573.9 / 574.2 | 577.0 / 576.5 (+0.5%) | 577.0 / 576.5 (+0.5%) |
| Arcane | horde | 577.1 / 576.9 | 579.4 / 579.5 (+0.4%) | 579.4 / 579.5 (+0.4%) |
| Destruction | alliance | 668.0 / 667.3 | 670.2 / 670.3 (+0.3%) | 670.2 / 670.3 (+0.3%) |
| Destruction | horde | 665.3 / 665.2 | 672.5 / 672.1 (+1.1%) | 672.5 / 672.1 (+1.1%) |

So the band-60 raid losses of Arcane and Destruction are the engine's healing bug, not the ranker. With the fix, Kindling Stave (Arcane) and Elemental Mage Staff (Destruction) are right, and the after-ranker shows it: Glowing Brightwood Staff is now verified and loses by 3.0 to 5.5 DPS, and Kindling Stave loses to Elemental Mage Staff for Destruction by 2.8 to 4.3. K2 stands on its own (the stat runner-up was never simmed, whatever the engine), but on the fixed engine it no longer changes a band-60 raid pick. With the fix, the guard still keeps the published set at band 60 bare for Arcane alliance (288.0 +/- 0.8 against 284.9, 8 slots) and Destruction alliance (359.5 +/- 0.4 against 356.9). Those are ranker losses, not engine ones. Enhancement and the bare warrior band carry no healing items in the differing slots, so the fix does not touch them.

The first table (f8a5f94ce, no fix): Arcane alliance keeps Glowing Brightwood Staff because, once it is measured (K2), the bugged engine scores it highest. Destruction now picks Kindling Stave over Spire of Hakkar, and the sim agrees (+0.4%); Elemental Mage Staff is measured and loses by 11 to 14 DPS. Enhancement keeps the published set through the guard (`kept_alternative`: the previous published set).
