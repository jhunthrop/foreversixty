A group utility totem, aura, or buff (Windfury Totem, Strength of Earth) can measure as a pure DPS loss here purely because this character is the only one who would benefit from the GCD spent refreshing it - the real payoff is the melee group standing in it, which a solo sim cannot see. Treat any finding that drops a group buff/totem line as a hint to verify by hand, not a rotation change to paste blind.
