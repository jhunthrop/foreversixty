# wow-player verdict: SHIP (one minor fix)

Question: first-time player landing, is this better? Yes.

The returning-1440 header has a Premium button. The first-visit-1440 header has none, and the nav, search and Sign in sit cleanly with no gap or leftover spacing. A first visit from the addon page or a BiS search should be about the class, not a pitch. In the first-visit 390 menu, nothing about Premium appears and the menu reads like a game menu: Best in slot, Guides crests, Tier list, Sim, Planner, Logs.

Nothing feels off at first sight. A button that shows up on day three reads as normal, not as a bait-and-switch.

Minor fixes:

- A signed-out player who clears cookies sees the button vanish and come back. Harmless, but it needs a fallback that shows it, not hides it, if the visit counter cannot be read.
- Keep the footer link and the in-context offers as they are. They are the only Premium path for a first-time visitor.
