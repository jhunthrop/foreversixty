The builds themselves are mostly right now. The trees show 70707 talents at legal ranks (Precision, Accelerant, Critical Mass 3/3, Shieldsworn, Holy Binding, Lacerate, Primal Bite as capstone, Bloom, Unsealed Wrath offered and declined with a reason). What blocks eleven pages is the Leveling card, which still reads like a different build, plus a few removed spells and stale lines in the rotations.

## NEW in this change: MUST (blocking)

### M1. Leveling card disagrees with the build above it (11 pages, all but priest holy)
The bands show ranks above the talent's maximum and names that are not the talent in that slot. Looks like ranks keyed by the old tree position while the names come from the new one. A player loading "LOAD IN PLANNER" would get the wrong points. Quotes:
- arcane: "Missile Barrage 5/2", "Arcane Blast 3/1", "Arcane Power 3/1", "Arcane Instability 5/3", "Arcane Mind 1/5", "Arcane Impact 1/3" (build has Mind 5/5, Impact 3/3), plus "Burning Soul 3/3" and "Ignite 2/5" which the build does not take.
- fire: "Flame Throwing 5/2", "Critical Mass 5/3", "Arcane Resilience 5/3", "Missile Barrage 5/2", "Incineration 2/5" (key table says 5/5), "Burning Soul 1/3", "Improved Fire Blast 1/2".
- frost: "Missile Barrage 5/2", "Incineration 1/5", "Accelerant 1/5", "Ignite 3/5", "Burning Soul 3/3" against a build with Incineration 3/5 and Accelerant 2/5.
- balance: "Furor 5/5" in the 50 to 59 band. Furor is a Feral talent now and the build has Heart of the Wild there. "Reflection 3/3 Subtlety 1/3" also looks swapped.
- cat: "Furor 5/5" from level 20 (the build has Furor 0/5, Heart of the Wild 5/5), "Predatory Strikes 1/3" and "Predatory Discipline 3/1" (ranks swapped).
- bear: "Predatory Discipline 3/1" where the key table has "Predatory Strikes 3/3"; "Primal Bite 5/1"; "Shifting Power 1/1" although the prose says it is left out; "Natural Instinct 1/2" and "Rend and Tear 2/5" against 2/2 and 5/5 in the build.
- restoration: "Subtlety 3/3" where the prose and tree have Reflection 3/3.
- holy paladin: "Divine Strength 5/5" in the first band (a healer; the tree has Spiritual Focus 5/5), "Infusion of Light 5/2", "Sacred Duty 5/2", 15 Protection points where the page says "12 in Protection".
- protection: "Sacred Duty 5/2", "Shield Specialization 3/2", "Toughness 2/5", "Iron Creed 1/3", and "Improved Hammer of Justice 3/3", "Stoicism 1/2", "Reckoning 5/5", "Spiritual Focus 5/5" which the prose says are "Left out to fit the budget".
- retribution: "Sanctified Judgement 5/3", "Unyielding Faith 3/2", "Turn Evil 3/2", "Champion of the Light 1/5", "Spiritual Focus 5/5", "Illumination 3/5". Unsealed Wrath 1/1 is listed although the page says "neither build takes it".
- discipline: "Divine Aegis 2/3" and "Holy Binding 1/1" in the 60 band, against Divine Aegis 3/3 and no Holy Binding in the build ("the client removed Binding Heal, and its point measured about +1.7% healing as Divine Aegis's third rank").

### M2. Removed spell still in the cooldown lists
"Voice of Truth 3 min cooldown" on paladin holy, protection and retribution. The talent was removed in 70707. A player would look for the button.

### M3. Rotation lines that no longer match the build
- frost, Priority row 4: "Cone of Cold ... On five Winter's Chill stacks". The builds hold Winter's Chill 3/5 and 2/5. If ranks cap stacks, as the 70658 wording did ("up to 5 stacks at rank 5"), five stacks cannot happen; the page also says "skip it on most bosses" and "the guide keeps the ranged build". Either drop the row or say what it needs.
- frost, rotation row 3 "Arcane Missiles ... Whenever Missile Barrage is up" while the Level 60 build shows Missile Barrage 0/2 (only the raid build has it). The key-talent chip for it also reads "0/2". Say which build the rotation belongs to.
- arcane, Priority row 5 "The filler once two Blasts are up" against row 4 "To 3 stacks always, and on past them". Row 4 is also cut off: "(more mana…".
- balance, Priority row 2 cut off: "(3.0 sec with Improved…".
- restoration, Priority row 9 "Wild Growth ... On a party member when two are hurt" and prose "Party members get Wild Growth when two are hurt", while the build leaves Wild Growth out (capstone 0/1) and says the points "did better elsewhere". A player cannot cast it.
- discipline, "Brings: ... Prayer of Mending on the tank" and "Renew and Prayer of Mending go on the tank". Prayer of Mending is the Holy capstone and sits at 0/1 in this build; neither it nor Renew is in the priority list. (Predates 70707 but makes the rotation text wrong.)

### M4. Prose that contradicts its own page
- fire, Accelerant: "so Accelerant speeds up nothing the level-60 rotation casts", yet the raid build is "3 points ... to Accelerant (3)" and shows 4/5. Four dead points by the page's own words. Same on frost: Accelerant 2/5 on a Frost mage.
- arcane: "all three ranks of Arcane Meditation" and "both builds make the same ... Arcane Meditation picks", but the third top-row Arcane talent shows 1/3 in both trees. One of the two is wrong.
- retribution: nightly absolute numbers in prose, "153.8 DPS ... 165.6 DPS ... 190.0 DPS". Relative gains only. Also "added to the client this week" (twice) and "an earlier version of this page".
- frost: literal markdown asterisks in the page text: "into **Cold Snap**".
- balance: "An earlier search dropped Moonfire upkeep ..." is drafting history.

## PRE-EXISTING site-wide (SHOULD follow-ups, not caused by 70707)
- "DPS per point" and "1 point of mp5 = 0.71 DPS" on every healer weight table; "1 point of expertise = 987.4 DPS" (protection), "687.7 DPS" (bear). A healer or tank stat has no DPS. Give the real unit or drop the line.
- Stat-priority prose that contradicts its table: restoration says "MP5 first, Spirit behind it, then healing power, Intellect" while the table is MP5, Spirit, Intellect, Healing power; priest holy says "MP5 and Spirit ... then Intellect, then healing power" while the table is MP5, Healing power, Intellect, Spirit.
- Cooldown lists that give every spec of a class every spell: Balance and Restoration druids show Frenzied Regeneration, Dash, Challenging Roar, Rebirth; priests show Elune's Grace, Feedback, Desperate Prayer, Divine Grace (race-gated).
- Raw tooltip formulas in player text: "[(172 + (Healing * 0.42899999)) * (1 * 1)]" (Prayer of Mending), "Shields up to (20 * 60) damage" (Divine Aegis), "(3 /- 3 * 6)" (Vindication). The client shows numbers; a player will screenshot this.
- Fire priority list names Scorch twice (rows 2 and 5); Fire also tells you to cast Fireball "only before Scorch is trained".
- Bear: Expertise ranked first (weight 1.00) while the page says "no item carries it yet".
- Mana-bound healer stat order (MP5 above healing power) reads wrong to a player even if the sim says so.
