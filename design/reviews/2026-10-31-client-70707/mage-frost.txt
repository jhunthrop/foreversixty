OVERVIEW

The beta stops at level 30. Everything above it on this page, the talents, rotation, stat weights and best in slot, comes from this site's simulator running on the client's own data, not from play. Class mechanics carry over from 1.12 where the demo showed no change.

STRENGTHS
Frostbolt slows enemies and keeps them at range
Frost Nova and Ice Block cover emergencies
Ice Shards adds up to 100% Frost critical strike damage
Frost Channeling cuts Frost mana cost by 15% and threat by 30%
WEAKNESSES
Less sustained output than Fire
No burst cooldown: Icy Veins is not in this client’s trees
Shatter works on a boss only through Fingers of Frost
IN A RAID
Role: Caster DPS with personal safety and utility
Brings: Arcane Intellect for the group

Key talents
Improved Frostbolt
5/5
Up to 0.5 seconds off Frostbolt's cast time at rank 5, the tree's most direct throughput gain
Ice Shards
5/5
Up to 100% more Frost critical strike damage at rank 5, the biggest single damage multiplier in the tree
Frost Channeling
3/3
Up to 15% off the mana cost of your Frost spells at rank 3, and 30% less threat from them
Shatter
3/3
Up to 50% more critical strike chance against a frozen target at rank 3. A raid boss cannot be frozen by Frost Nova, so against a boss this talent only works through Fingers of Frost, below
Fingers of Frost
2/2
Each Chill effect (Frostbolt, Frostfire Bolt, Cone of Cold, Improved Blizzard) has a 15% chance to grant a 15 second effect that treats your next 1 spell (2 at rank 2) as if the target were Frozen. That switches on Shatter's crit bonus and Ice Lance's frozen damage bonus against a target that Frost Nova cannot freeze. It needs Ice Lance, so that is a one-point cost
Winter's Chill
3/5
Reworked in Forever: every Frostbolt and every Fingers of Frost Ice Lance stacks Winter's Chill on the target, up to 5, and only your own next Cone of Cold spends them, taking 20% more damage and 7% more of your spell damage per stack. A raid frost mage stands at range and does not cast Cone of Cold, so the raid build keeps just the 2 points Ice Barrier needs below it; the leveling build keeps 3, since you are often in Cone of Cold range while leveling
Missile Barrage
0/2
Missile Barrage 2/2 gives Frostbolt a 20% chance to make your next Arcane Missiles free, halve its channel and fire the missiles every half second, and Precision (below) now covers those Arcane missiles' hit. Together with an Arcane Missiles cast on every proc it was worth about +7% in our audit run
Ice Barrier
1/1
The shield also raises your Frost damage by 15% for a minute, and by another 5% while the shield holds, so it is a damage talent first. Both builds take it: the leveling build moves two Winter's Chill points into Cold Snap, its prerequisite, and Ice Barrier itself, about +12% in our level-60 search run

Outside Frost, Precision (3/3) is Elemental Precision renamed, now adding 6% hit chance to all your spells. The client moved Improved Fire Blast two rows down the Fire tree, out of reach of a ten-point dip, so its point went to Incineration, which also raises Ice Lance’s critical strike chance; the raid build’s Incineration goes to 5/5, taking that point and one of the three Arcane Focus held before the client removed it. Precision now does Arcane Focus’s job for the Missile Barrage missiles, and its other two points go to Wand Specialization, which the Missile Barrage row needs above it. Accelerant speeds up nothing Frost casts. The leveling build’s three points open the Fire row that Flame Throwing and Impact sit in; the raid build’s one is a spare point, since moving it elsewhere measured within the run’s error.

Put up Ice Barrier before the pull and recast it whenever its minute runs out, for its Frost damage bonus. Frostbolt is the filler. Cast Arcane Missiles whenever Missile Barrage procs. Wand only when you are out of mana: a wand shot cannot start during the global cooldown. When Fingers of Frost procs, cast Ice Lance while the effect is up: the held charge makes the target count as frozen, so Ice Lance deals four times its damage and Shatter’s crit bonus applies, and each cast spends one charge. In the simulator the Ice Lance line gains roughly 3% over Frostbolt alone, beyond the run’s error. Frostbolt’s own Chill can roll the next charge, so keep casting while charges are held rather than waiting. Frostfire Bolt, which you can train from level 40, is not a Frost filler: swapping it in for Frostbolt measured roughly a third lower damage, because its direct hit is smaller and the Frost talents above scale Frostbolt, not it. Frost Nova and the other freezes are not part of the boss rotation, since a boss cannot be frozen. Keep Ice Barrier up at all times: it is the largest single Frost damage bonus you have. Cone of Cold only reaches 10 yards (12 with Arctic Reach), and raid frost mages stand at range, so skip it on most bosses. If a fight lets you stand that close (no cleave, tail or melee-range mechanic), Cone of Cold is worth a global on five Winter’s Chill stacks: standing in range with more Winter’s Chill points measured about 6% ahead of the ranged build in our raid run, but it puts you in melee range, so the guide keeps the ranged build.

PROFESSIONS

Tailoring and Enchanting is the standard caster pairing in Classic-era play: Tailoring’s crafted spellcaster gear fills early slots, and Enchanting lets you apply your own weapon and gear enchants instead of paying for them.
