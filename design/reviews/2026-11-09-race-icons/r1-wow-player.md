Verdict: SHIP

Judged only the race-icon change, on the hunter and mage landings and the hunter-bm and warrior-fury Races lanes, at 1440 and 390 (the 390 captures I opened were hunter and hunter-bm).

Question the surface answers: which races can I play this class as, and which one is best. The icons help; the answer (Recommended pill, faction colour, racial line) is unchanged and still first.

What works
- These are the client's own Achievement_Character portraits, the same faces as the character-create screen. Human, Dwarf, Gnome, Night Elf, Orc, Tauren, Troll and Undead are all correct and recognisable at 24px (landing) and 28px (spec rows).
- Faction order and colour are right: Alliance then Horde, blue and red names.
- Race lists match the class pairs. Hunter: Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Orc, Tauren, Troll, plus Skyborne. Mage: Human, Gnome, Orc, Undead, Troll. No Dwarf or Night Elf mage, no Tauren mage. Warrior Fury shows Gnome, which is right for Forever.
- The racial spell icons sit under the portrait on the spec page and the two icon sizes do not clash.
- 390: the two-column landing list wraps "High Order Skyborne" cleanly; spec rows hold up with the portrait left of the name.

Should fix (minor, not blocking)
1. High Order Skyborne (blue) and Windshaper Skyborne (red) share the Blood Elf face. A Classic player will read a golden-haired blood elf in the Alliance list and think Blood Elf. The ChrRaces fallback justifies it, but the two lines are told apart only by name colour, and Blood Elf is not a playable race in Forever. A one-line note is optional.
2. Mage landing: the Skyborne blurb says Horde Windshapers cannot be mages, and the list agrees. Good, no change.

Nothing here contradicts the Forever facts.
