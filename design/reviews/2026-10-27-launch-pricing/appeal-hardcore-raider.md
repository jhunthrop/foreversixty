# Appeal review: Kae, 27, Fury Warrior (parses everything, pugs MC on alts)

Screens read: founder yearly and monthly at 1440, founder yearly at 390, after the founder window at 1440.

**1. First three seconds.** I see "$0" and "This list never shrinks", then "$40 /year" with a struck-out $50. I relax: logs, parses, BiS and sims are free. Then I see "$120" and "$200" and think "guild stuff, officer problem, not me."

**2. Scary numbers?** $40 a year isn't. I already pay WCL and Raider.IO about $5 a month each, so "$3.33 a month" is cheaper than what I pay now. $150 and $250 aren't scary either, because those are the GM's bill split across 25+ people. After the window, "$50" on its own looks like a jump if you've never seen the $5 monthly price. Showing monthly first wouldn't change my mind. Seeing "$3.33 a month" next to $40 already does that job.

**3. What Premium does for my DPS.** Only half clear. "Server sims: 5,000 combinations, any precision" reads like a spec sheet. "History charts of your score" doesn't tell me which score. "Supporter mark" is just a badge. What I want to read is "sims every gear, talent and consumable combo for your character and tells you the biggest DPS upgrade." The free list (sims, parses, BiS) already covers most of what I need.

**4. Subscribe today?** No, not yet. I'd use free first. I'd pay if a server sim found me 2%+ over my browser sim, or if Premium coached my logs ("you clipped 14 Bloodthirsts").

**5. Three honest changes.**
- Rewrite each Premium line as what I get out of it. Keep one plain line on what the server sim can do that the browser sim can't.
- Show one real example result (a sample sim or chart for a Fury warrior) so I can see what I'm paying for.
- On phone, Free shows twice (a short row at the top, the full card at the bottom). Keep one, and put it at the top.
