# Appeal review: budget player (Theo, 20, Frost Mage, phone first)

**1. First three seconds.** The big gold "$40 /year" with "$50" crossed out. I wince a bit. Then I see "Everything a player needs is free" and "no ads" and relax. The thin "Free $0" strip above it is easy to skip. It looks like a banner, not a plan.

**2. Scary numbers?** $40 on its own is fine: one tank of gas, and "$3.33 a month" right under it helps. What sets off my cash-grab alarm is the crossed-out "$50". Every store does fake sales with that, even if yours is real. $120 and $200 don't scare me because they're clearly guild stuff, not for me. Monthly ("$5 /month") feels friendlier. But I'd still compare the two and pick the year if I was sure. Showing monthly first wouldn't change my answer, just how it feels.

**3. Free vs paid clear?** Mostly yes. "This list never shrinks" is the best line on the page. But on my phone the actual free list (BiS, talents, browser sims, logs, guides) sits *below Raid Guild*, way down. I have to scroll past three paid plans to see what I already get. Paid is clear: server sims, history charts, Discord pings, a badge.

**4. Would I subscribe?** Not now. Browser sims are free, and the history charts and pings are nice but not needed. I'd pay $5 once if a server sim found a real upgrade for me or my logs told me what I'm doing wrong. A free sample of one server sim would sell it.

**5. Three honest changes**
- Put the full free list first on phone, before Premium, at the same size.
- Say what the $50 is: "$50 after 6 Jan 2027" instead of only crossing it out.
- Show one real example of what Premium gets you (a server-sim result or a parse history chart), or one free server sim.
