# Appeal review: Marcus, casual Holy Paladin, solo payer

**1. First three seconds.** I see "$40 /year" with "$50" crossed out, then "$120" and "$200" beside it. My first thought is "oh, this is a subscription page." Then I read "Everything a player needs is free" and calm down. I don't feel pushed.

**2. Scary numbers?** $120 and $200 sit right next to my plan and make it look like a pricey site. Then I read "For a guild" and realise they aren't for me. $40 a year is fine. That's less than a month of Spotify plus WoW. Seeing monthly first ("$5 /month") feels smaller, but I'd pay yearly anyway. What actually put me off was the guild numbers being as big as mine, not the yearly totals.

**3. Premium vs free.** Not really. "Server sims: 5,000 combinations, any precision, no tab left open" means nothing to a healer. "History charts of your score and parses" is a bit closer. Nothing says what it does for a Holy Paladin. The free list already covers everything I use (BiS, guides, talents). That's honest, but it means I have no reason to pay.

**4. Subscribe today?** No. I would if Premium did something for me, like healing log coaching ("what to fix after Onyxia") or alerts when a BiS upgrade drops. Or if it said plainly "this keeps the site running." I'd pay $40 for that, knowing the founder price ends 6 January.

**5. Three honest changes:**
1. Make Premium the plan that stands out, and make the guild prices smaller or move them lower down, labelled "for guild leaders". Then $200 isn't the third number I see.
2. Write the Premium list in player words, one line per benefit, such as "See if your gear and parses are improving week to week."
3. Add a plain line under Premium: "Free stays complete. Premium pays for the servers and keeps the site going." Supporting the site is the reason I'd actually pay.

Also, on the phone the full Free card shows up again below Raid Guild. That looked like a bug to me.
