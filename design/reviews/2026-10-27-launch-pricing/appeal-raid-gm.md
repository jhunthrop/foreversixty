# Appeal review: Dana, GM of a 40-person raiding guild (2026-10-27)

**1. First three seconds.** "$200 /year" with "$250" crossed out. My first thought is "that's a lot for a website." A second later I see "$16.67 a month" and "First 7 days free", and I calm down. The FOUNDER tag reads as a real deadline, not a gimmick.

**2. Scary or fair?** Fair. I already pay about that much for bot hosting. $200 split over 40 raiders is $5 a head for the year, about a nickel per raider per raid night. Put that line on the card and I'd show it to my officers when I ask them to chip in. Showing monthly first wouldn't change my mind, because I'd pay yearly anyway. It would just make the first look less scary.

**3. Guild vs Raid guild.** The choice makes itself: Guild stops at 25 members and I have 40. The one feature that sells it to me is "Loot council". "The readiness board, with consumables" means nothing to me yet. I'm also not sure what "verified members" means. Do all 40 have to sign in with Battle.net? What about the 80 Discord members who don't raid?

**4. Buy today?** Not yet. Seven days covers two raid nights, which isn't enough to test loot council properly. My card goes in at the start, and nothing promises me a reminder before day 8. A reminder email before the trial ends, plus a look at loot council and the bot in action, would get me to buy.

**5. Three honest changes.**
- Add a cost-per-raider line under the price, e.g. "$5 per raider a year for a 40-person guild".
- Promise "we email you 2 days before your trial ends" right next to "First 7 days free".
- Add one screenshot, or a link to a demo, of loot council and the Discord bot. Also say plainly who counts as a member, and whether officers can pay together or chip in.
