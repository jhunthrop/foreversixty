# Character history: wow-player review (2026-10-25)

Question: am I getting better, and where? The 1440 owner capture answers it: a rising score line and a rising parse line, with "Up 1090 since 12 Oct" under the chart. The tier bands read like item quality colours and the gap shading is easy to follow.

Verdict: SHIP WITH FIXES

## FIX list
1. history-owner-1440 (and -390): the page contradicts itself. The Forever Score card sparkline says "Up 590 since 12 Oct" and stops at "4 Nov". The History chart under it says "Up 1090 since 12 Oct" and runs to 11 Dec. Same player, same page, two numbers. Make both read from one series, or drop the sparkline text when History is shown.
2. history-owner-1440: the parse readout says "88 parse · 11 Dec 2026 · kill 5 of 5", but the 11 Dec row in "Kills of Golemagg" says Parse % 80. A WCL player will catch that in seconds. The chart point, the readout and the row must show the same number (88). Every row also reads 80 / 1,402 / 91%. That is fixture data, but it hides whether the columns sort or colour correctly, so use varied fixtures for the next capture.
3. history-owner-1440: the owner is a Holy/Discipline priest. The Every Ranked Fight table says HPS, but both History tables say DPS and "Execution 91%" on Golemagg. The page's own note says healers have no execution. Show HPS for healing specs, and no Execution column for healers and tanks.
4. history-owner-1440: "Execution" is not explained anywhere in History. The only definition is far down the page, under "Every ranked fight". Add a tooltip on the header, or one line under the tables. Also say what Parse % is ranked against. WCL players expect "vs same spec", and one clause would stop the argument.
5. history-owner-1440: "Fights behind the score" shows Golemagg on 26 Oct and 19 Oct. The Golemagg tab lists only five kills (17 Nov to 11 Dec), and Gehennas has one kill, on 19 Oct. Either the fight list or the kill counts is wrong. Reconcile them.
6. history-owner-1440 and -390: names are chopped: "Golemagg the Incinerat[e]" runs into the "Private" tag, and "Incinerato" is cut at 390 beside "Guild". Shorten the boss name (Golemagg) or put the tag in its own column or an icon. Never clip the name and the tag together.
7. history-gap-note-1440/390: "Score not kept" shows only as a small grey line under the chart, "Gap 1 Dec to 8 Dec: Score not kept". The gap is a narrow shaded strip with no label inside it, while "No rated fights" is labelled inside its gap. Put the reason in the strip, or give it a hover label, and add "Report flagged" the same way. Also make it plain that "Score not kept" means the site did not store it, not that the player did something wrong.
8. history-owner-1440: the Y axis is cropped to roughly 700 to 1900 with only 1000 and 1500 labelled. Legendary never shows, although the spec says 0 to 2100. That is acceptable, but add the 2000 line or a "scale starts at 700" cue so nobody reads the chart as 0 to 2100. At 390, the "Uncommon" band label touches the plot edge, and the date axis shows only three dates with no year.
9. history-owner-1440: "what do I click to act on a bad fight" has no answer. Boss names are gold like links, but nothing says where they go. Say it ("Open fight" or a trailing arrow) and offer one concrete next step from a low row, for example the logs page or "Sim this gear". The user is paying for this, so give them something to act on.
10. history-single-kill-section-1440: the one-kill state leaves a big empty hole between "One kill so far. A line needs two." and the kills table, and the tables on the two sides no longer line up. Collapse the gap, and show the single point on a tiny axis instead of only words.
11. history-owner-1440: the Score over time card has a two-line caption but Parse by boss has none, so the readouts and charts sit at different heights. Add a caption to the right side ("Percentile among same-spec kills"), which also covers fix 4. Parse points are all gold except the last; the colours (purple 75 to 94, orange 95 to 98, blue 50 to 74) appear only in the numbers. Colour the points by parse tier as WCL does, or say it is deliberately one colour.
12. history-owner-390: the phone layout is usable but long. The tab pills handle two bosses, but with 10 or more bosses they will need to scroll or wrap, so capture that. The step buttons are small; make them 44 px.
13. history-free-owner-1440: the upsell is a bare grey sentence floating under the score card with no heading or panel: "History charts draw your score and parses over time. See plans." Give it the History heading and a locked-chart ghost, so the player sees what $4 buys.
14. Wording: "parse" is correct (a percentile), and the colours match WCL. "Output" does not appear anywhere on screen, which is right. Keep it that way.
