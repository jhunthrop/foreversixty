# wow-player review: unknown-realm question on /setup (2026-11-13)

Seen rendered: question-1440, question-390, question-close, refused-close, answered-1440. (2000 and the other answered captures read as the same block.)

## 1. The question
"Which realm type is <character> on?" The answer is the first thing on the page, above Sign in, with the four buttons directly under the line. A player knows what to tap in about a second.

## 2. What reads wrong (most damaging first)
1. Refusal line starts in lowercase and reads like a raw server string: "another account already has a character of that name on that realm type. Try again." (refused-close.png). Capitalise it, and say what to do: "Another account already has <name> on that realm type. Pick another type." A player would screenshot a lowercase red sentence under a clean block. MUST FIX.
2. "RP PvP realms count as Roleplay." is a grey 13 px footnote under the buttons. The player who is on an RP PvP realm decides before they reach it, hesitates between PvP and Roleplay, and may tap PvP. It is the one real ambiguity here and it is the least visible line. Put it above the buttons with the "Blizzard does not list..." line, or directly under the Roleplay button. SHOULD, and I would do it in the same pass as the must-fix.
3. Nothing says the answer can be changed or that a wrong tap has a cost. One-tap, once, saves immediately. Add "You can change this later" if that is true, or "Pick carefully" if it is not. A Hardcore player tapping Normal by accident has no way back on screen.
4. The block has no mark that it needs doing. It uses the same panel chrome and weight as the done steps below it. A gold edge or small "Needs your answer" tag would separate it from Sign in and The companion. SHOULD.
5. 390 px: Hardcore wraps alone onto a second row (question-390.png). A 2 by 2 grid reads tidier. SHOULD.

## 3. Missing
- No hint where a player finds their realm type ("shown on the realm list at login"). Players who log in through the companion may not know the word for their realm. SHOULD.
- Nothing says the answers are pooled ("two players agreeing resolves it for everyone"). That is a reason to answer honestly and a reason a player would not guess a button. One short line is enough. SHOULD.

## 4. Words
- Labels Normal, PvP, Roleplay, Hardcore match the site's one list. Right for Forever. "Normal" not "PvE" is right here.
- "realm type" is the right phrase for a player (not "ruleset"). Keep it.
- "Rankings and BiS follow your answer." is fine and states the consequence. Keep.
- Hardcore needs no gloss for this audience.
- No wrong game facts found. Check the class colour: Newly Rolled renders a yellow-green that I did not read as the druid orange or the hunter green; confirm it is the client class colour for that character's class.

## 5. Verdict: FIX
Must-fix: capitalise and rewrite the refusal sentence (item 1).
Shoulds: move or promote the RP PvP line (2), say whether the answer can be changed (3), mark the block as needing an answer (4), 2 by 2 buttons at 390 (5), where to find the realm type and the pooled-answer line (section 3).
With item 1 done this is SHIP.
