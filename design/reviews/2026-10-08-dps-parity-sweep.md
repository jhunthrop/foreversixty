# DPS parity sweep: six modelling gaps closed against the client

2026-10-08, client 1.60.1.70009. Fork branch `dps-sweep` (`/Users/jh/code/wowsims-forever/.worktrees/dps-sweep`), site branch `dps-sweep`. Neither is merged or pushed. Each gap was checked against the client's rows first, fixed with a test written first, and the fork's conformance report reads `declared, matches` for every spell it touches.

Movement is the BiS ranker (`leveling-bis -spec <spec> -bands 60`, the band-60 raid-preset gear set, both factions) before and after, run from binaries built at the fork's `forever` head and at this branch, writing to a scratch directory. "Search" is `rotation-search -preset raid` (800 confirm iterations, seed 7) on the old and new engine. Percentages are relative.

## 1. Frost mage: Cone of Cold, Frostbite, the Manaflare Regalia

**Client rows.** Cone of Cold 120, 8492, 10159, 10160, 10161 (levels 26/34/42/50/58): instant, 10 s category cooldown, 210 to 555 mana, effect 0 a 40% snare (aura 33, 6 s), effect 1 school damage with coefficient 0.129 on every target in the cone, `SpellClassMask_0` 1573376 which carries the Chill bit (1048576); learnable through the Frost skill line. Frostbite 11071 (5/10/15% a rank): aura 109 with the Chill mask, trigger 12494, a root (aura 26) of 5000 ms. The Tier 1 mage set is Manaflare Regalia, ItemSet 2098 (the client has no "Arcanist" bonus for Frostfire Bolt; Arcanist Regalia 201 is the vanilla set): 2P hit 1% (1300947), 3P Counterspell cooldown -5 s (1301013), 4P 21 spell damage against Elementals (1301079), 5P (1301488) "Your Frostfire Bolt spell has a 10% increased chance to trigger Missile Barrage, gains 10% increased critical strike chance while your Combustion spell is active, and has a 10% increased chance to trigger Fingers of Frost". The six pieces carry no stats in this build's item rows.
**Engine before.** Cone of Cold had a mask bit and a talent naming it (Improved Cone of Cold) and no spell. Frostbite was `_ = mage.Talents.Frostbite`. The set was not registered at all.
**Change.** `cone_of_cold.go` registers the five ranks (binary, like Frostbolt: the same damage-plus-snare structure; hits every target). `chill.go` is one hook for every landed Chill application that rolls Fingers of Frost and Frostbite; Frostbite roots for 5 s, counts as Frozen for Shatter and Ice Lance, and obeys Frost Nova's immunity rule (a target above the level cap cannot be frozen), so it does nothing to a raid boss. The set is registered by name; the 5P reads "10% increased chance" as ten percentage points (Missile Barrage 20 to 30, Fingers of Frost 15 to 25 on Frostfire Bolt) and adds 10 crit points to Frostfire Bolt while Combustion is up. The 4P has no sim path (no Elemental target, no spell power by mob type). The fork's item database gets the six pieces (`tools/database/overrides.go`) so the set resolves under `with_db`.
**Movement.** Ranker: 0.00% (raid and bare, both factions): the rotation never casts Cone of Cold, Frostbite cannot freeze the boss, and the pieces have no stats, so no set is ever worn (`engineImplementedSetIDs` in the ranker is the sets implemented on 2026-09-28 and does not offer 2098 either). Search: Cone of Cold offered, -32.6 +/- 2.2 (hurts: 555 mana for one cone a 10 s cooldown on a single target); no mutation beats the incumbent. The ladder at levels 38 to 50 moves by about 1% (the targets there are freezable, so Frostbite fires); the golden is regenerated. `TestP1Mage.results` gains the Manaflare set case. The 10% reading of the set bonus and the Cone's binary flag are unconfirmed.

## 2. Warlock: Demon Armor, Life Tap, Dark Pact

**Client rows.** Demon Armor has five ranks: 706, 1086, 11733, 11734, 11735 at levels 20/30/40/50/60, armor 210/300/390/480/570 and Shadow resistance 3/6/9/12/15 (effects 0 and 1). The live Affliction tree has no Dark Pact and the client has no Dark Pact spell. Improved Life Tap is "+10/20% Mana awarded by your Life Tap". Life Tap 1454 to 11689 reads "Converts ${($m1+$SPI*1)*(1+$18182m1/100)} Health into ${...} Mana for you. Spirit increases the amount converted": effect 0 is a dummy amount (420 at the spell's level 56, +1 a level, so 424 at 60) with a spell power coefficient of 0, and one point per point of Spirit. Cataclysm's live text is "Reduces the Mana cost of your Destruction spells by 3% / 6% / 10%". Soul Harvest needs a kill, Demonic Aegis is defensive.
**Engine before.** The Demon Armor table skipped rank 2, so levels 30 to 39 wore rank 1. Life Tap scaled with spell power at 0.8, used 420, and ran its result through the Shadow damage multipliers (Shadow Mastery and the rest inflated the mana). Cataclysm took 1% a rank (3% at the top). `dark_pact.go` was a commented-out stub with a field and a registration call.
**Change.** Demon Armor reads the generated rank tables plus a Shadow resistance column, held to the client's effects by a test. Life Tap restores the rank amount at the caster's level plus Spirit times Improved Life Tap, nothing else (`LifeTapAmount`, held to the client by `AssertTable`). Cataclysm is 3/6/10. Dark Pact is deleted.
**Movement.** This is the one change in the sweep that moves a number a player will notice. Ranker, raid preset: Affliction -1.1% (Alliance) and -1.9% (Horde), Demonology -3.5% and -3.6%, Destruction -3.0% (both factions) (bare band 60: -0.9% to -4.7%). Search baselines on the old and new engine: Affliction 800.8 to 784.4, Demonology 752.1 to 733.4, Destruction 808.0 to 786.9 (-2.0%, -2.5%, -2.6%). The cause is Life Tap: a tap now returns noticeably less mana than the spell power version did, so the mana-bound rotations lose casts. The Spirit reading rests on the spell text alone; if beta data show spell power still counts, `lifeTapMana` is the single place to add it back. Adopted from the new search (rule: beyond error): Affliction recasts Immolate in its last 3 seconds (+3.2 +/- 1.5; the old engine measured +1.1, inside error), and Demonology fills with Searing Pain where Shadow Bolt stood (+18.0 +/- 1.6 on the new engine; the old engine already measured +10.4 +/- 1.6, so this was a pending finding the Life Tap change widened; the Shadow Bolt line behind an unconditional Searing Pain could never fire and is removed). Held: Destruction's "replace Bane of Doom with Curse of the Elements" (+12.1), for the reason 2026-10-07-raid-builds.md gives (the raid preset's own Curse of the Elements collides with the cast one). Builds: with Searing Pain as the filler Improved Shadow Bolt no longer earns a point, and the Demonology raid build moves to `0553/203511310122000135/005003` (-5 Improved Shadow Bolt, -3 Improved Imp, +3 Aftermath, +3 Malediction, +2 Master Summoner; 790.8 against 776.2 for the old raid build, +1.9% beyond error, the winner of the unconstrained search, holds the Demonology tree, drops nothing the rotation casts). On the old engine the old raid build was ahead (824.0 against 810.6). Affliction's and Destruction's raid builds stay the best found (876.3 against the search's winner, which is the same build; 806.8 against 801.6). Ladder goldens for the three specs are regenerated; the Demonology ladder now lists Searing Pain as the filler from level 20. The guide rotation prose for Affliction and Demonology and the Demonology raid-build paragraph are rewritten with relative gains only.

## 3. Shaman: Elemental Fury and the totems

**Client rows.** Elemental Fury 16089 (20% a rank, 100% at 5/5): an add-percent aura on crit damage bonus (aura 108, misc 15), "your Searing and Magma Totems and your Fire, Frost, and Nature spells". Its class mask (mask 0 `0xD1300403`, mask 1 `0x1000`) matches the Searing Totem's Attack spells (3606 to 10436, bit `0x40000000`), the Magma Totem's pulse (8187, 10579 to 10581, `0x40000004`), Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, the three shocks, Fire Nova and Lava Burst, and not Healing Wave (`0x40`) or Chain Heal (`0x100`).
**Engine before.** The talent selected spells carrying the shaman or totem flag; the totems' damage is dealt by child spells that carry neither, so Searing and Magma Totem never got the bonus. Healing spells (flagged, magic) would have.
**Change.** The Searing Totem's attack gets a spell code; `elementalFuryApplies` selects by it, by the Magma Totem's code and by the shaman flag, and excludes healing. Tests: the bonus on both child spells at every rank, and the predicate on named cases.
**Movement.** Ranker, Elemental raid: +0.81% (both factions), bare band 60 +0.9% to +1.0%. Search baseline 447.1 to 450.6 (+0.8%). `TestElemental.results` moves +0.4% to +11% across the Phase 1 cases (the most on the multi-target rows where Magma Totem is cast) and is adopted for that reason. The search finds no better rotation. Not part of this change: a build move the build search finds on both engines (swap Improved Healing Wave for Totemic Focus, +3.7% over the raid build, 31/2/18, keeps every unmodeled talent); it predates this sweep and is left to the build lane.

## 4. Druid: Lacerate's crits and Furor's cap

**Client rows.** The druid's Lacerate is 414644, 1235826, 1235827 (levels 42/50/58, class set 7): a periodic damage aura (aura 3, base points 10/12/15 a 3 s tick), a dummy effect of 10 that the text calls "$s2% weapon damage per existing application", up to 5 stacks, and the hidden companion 414647 (effect 31, 20% weapon damage). The periodic spell carries the same attribute set as Rake, Rip and Rend (`Attributes_8` `0x1200`); the companion has no cannot-crit attribute. Nothing in the rows, the 1 October notes or the research states that a druid bleed can crit, and `core.Dot.CanCrit` is opt-in per dot until measured. Furor (17056) reads "regain 20% of the Energy you had when you were last in Cat Form, plus 2 Energy for each second you spent not in Bear Form, Cat Form, or Dire Bear Form, up to a maximum of 20 Energy", and the tooltip formula derives all three numbers from one value (`100*($m2/100)`), so the cap is `100 x` the rank's fraction of the bar: 20 at rank 1, 100 at rank 5.
**Engine before and change.** The engine already lets the direct hit crit (a special melee attack), keeps the bleed from critting, and caps the whole Furor refill at 20 a rank (the cap read as covering the sum; the other reading, a cap on the per-second part only, would let rank 1 refill 40 and make the cap bite only after ten seconds out of form). Both readings match the client text as far as it can be matched, so nothing changes. `TestLacerateHitCritsAndTheBleedDoesNot` pins the first, `TestFurorCatFormEnergyFollowsTheLiveText` already pinned the second.
**Movement.** None (ranker 0.00% for Feral, Bear and Balance). Open: the direct hit is modelled as the companion's flat 20% of weapon damage, while the visible text says 10% per existing application; the two differ (50% against 20% at five stacks, nothing against 20% on the first cast). The earlier lane chose the companion and marked it unconfirmed; it stays so.

## 5. Rogue and warrior: the Twin Blades of Hakkari

**Client rows.** ItemSet 461 (items 19865 and 19866, the Warblades of the Hakkari): one bonus, spell 15763 at two pieces, aura 30 (skill) on skill line 43, base points 6, "Increased Swords +6". Zul'Gurub is not Phase 1: `research/01-official-facts.md` lists Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia opening on 9 December, and `data/curated/phases.json` has no phase for it; the blades are `client_unconfirmed` items from the classic-db table.
**Engine before and change.** `sim/common/item_sets` already registers "The Twin Blades of Hakkari" by name with `SwordsSkill += 6`, which is the client's bonus, and the site's item database carries the set name on both blades. The gap was a test: `TestTwinBladesOfHakkariAddsSixSwordsSkillAtTwoPieces` holds the pair to +6 over one blade. The bonus has no Phase 1 effect.
**Movement.** None (ranker 0.00% for the three rogue and two warrior specs).

## 6. Hunter: Hawk rank 6 and the pet abilities

**Client rows.** Aspect of the Hawk ranks 13165 to 14322 give 20/35/50/70/90/55 ranged attack power and rank 7 (25296, level 60) 120: rank 6 (14322, level 58) is weaker than rank 5 (14321, level 48), so `data/curated/inferior-ranks.json` marking it inferior is right and nothing else is needed. Dismember (1264758 .. 1264933), Pinch (1264735 .. 1264742) and Dust Cloud (1265899 .. 1265904) each have a SkillLineAbility row on their family's skill line, so a hunter teaches them. Sonic Blast (1264478 .. 1264482) has no row for any rank, like Hydra Shot.
**Engine before.** The three periodic family abilities were modelled (Savage Rend, Tendon Rip, Web); the earlier audit left Dismember, Pinch, Sonic Blast and Dust Cloud out on a damage-per-focus argument.
**Change.** Dismember (35 focus, 6 s, a flat 50 to 58 hit at rank 5), Pinch (50 focus, 30 s, 88 to 102) and Dust Cloud (10 focus, 505 armor for 30 s, renewed as it lapses) are registered for the Crocolisk, Crab and Tallstrider and cast ahead of the focus dump like the others; a pet's autocast uses an ability whenever it is ready. Dust Cloud shares Faerie Fire's minor armor reduction category through a new `core.MinorArmorReductionAura`, so it does not stack with Faerie Fire (unconfirmed: the rows state the amount and duration only). The Bat gets no Sonic Blast. The four family abilities share one rank table and config.
**Movement.** None: every preset uses the Cat, which gains nothing (ranker 0.00% for the three hunter specs).
