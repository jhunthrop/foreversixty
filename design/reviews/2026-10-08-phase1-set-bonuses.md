# Phase 1 set bonuses: inventory, models, and what the ranker sees

2026-10-08, client 1.60.1.70009. Fork branch `sets` (`/Users/jh/code/wowsims-forever/.worktrees/sets`, head `108076f92`), site branch `sets` (`/Users/jh/code/forever/.worktrees/sets`). Neither is merged or pushed. The ranker before/after runs are `leveling-bis -repo-root . -all -bands 60` (both presets, both factions, all 28 specs) from binaries built at the fork's `forever` head (`b4fa0ac3f`) and at `108076f92`, writing to a scratch directory.

## Verdict

1. **The engine modelled almost none of Forever's Phase 1 set bonuses correctly.** Of the 178 sets the ranker can offer a level 60 character, 84 were not registered at all (17 Tier 1 sets, 52 PvP rank sets, 4 dungeon sets, 4 class quest sets, 3 crafted or misc, 4 others) and 42 more were registered with vanilla thresholds or amounts that Forever re-itemised (the 10 Tier 0 dungeon sets are 2/3/4/5/6 pieces in the client, not vanilla's 2/4/6/8; 18 PvP sets, 7 class quest sets, 5 dungeon sets such as the Postmaster, Necropile, Bloodmail and Deathbone, and Imperial Plate carry other amounts). Only 52 were right. Counted by bonus spell (467 reachable in Phase 1): 224 had no registration, 41 were wrong, 50 lacked their threshold, 53 were hand-written and unchecked, 99 were right.
2. **Now every one of them is built from the client's rows.** Of the 467 reachable bonuses, 320 are flat stats applied straight from the row, 43 are hand-written models with their amounts, chances, durations and cooldowns read from the rows, 103 are declared "no sim effect" with a reason, and 1 is an old registration left alone (Green Dragon Mail's Meditation, which matches its row). A new set bonus conformance report (`sim/core/testdata/conformance/sets.golden.md`) states each verdict; the test fails on a mismatch or a missing threshold, and today there are none.
3. **The ranker prices pieces one at a time and only sees a bonus by accident of order.** A set enters a published pick through two doors, both narrow (section 3). The smallest change that would let it price a set from nothing is a per-set trial over each set's best piece in each slot; cost about 43 extra 100-iteration runs per band, faction and preset before any prefilter.
4. **Ranker movement is small.** 95 of 112 spec, preset and faction rows are unchanged to within 0.2%. The largest are Holy paladin +2.8% (Alliance, raid), Prot warrior +0.9% and +1.4%, and on the bare preset Shadow priest +4.5%, Restoration druid about +2% and Balance druid (Horde) +3.6% (section 4).

## 1. Inventory

**Phase 1 here means a piece has a loot source the catalogue does not gate "later".** `data/curated/phases.json` has pre-beta, beta, launch (4 Nov 2026 23:00) and raids-1 (9 Dec 2026); `data/curated/loot/forever-raid-phases.json` stamps `opens` onto raid sources. The roadmap in `research/01-official-facts.md` lists Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia for 9 December. **Molten Core, Blackwing Lair, Zul'Gurub, Ahn'Qiraj (both), and Naxxramas are all `later`**: the roadmap names none of them for Phase 1, and the Era raids' tier sets are not in this client's item table at all ("every tier-1 and tier-2 set piece are gone"). Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit have no loot in either database, so the only Phase 1 raid drops the catalogue carries are Onyxia's Lair (`opens: raids-1`), whose Tier 2 helms are one piece per set, so no Tier 2 bonus (3 pieces and up) is reachable. The ranker refuses any source with a non-empty `opens` at every band (`band.go:130`), so even those one piece are outside its pool. The Forever Tier 1 sets (ItemSets 2098 to 2115) are the tier the 9 December raids are named for, but no source states where their six pieces drop; the catalogue sources four of their six pieces each, as crafted ilvl 61 pieces, and those pieces carry no stats in this build (checked for all 17: every Tier 1 item row has an empty stat block), so no Tier 1 piece ever scores above zero and the ranker cannot wear them for their stats. Their bonuses still stand and are modelled.

PvP rank rewards stay in the pool (rank 10 and below by default; `pvpRankCap`, `band.go:283`); rank 11 and up, which is every Champion's, Lieutenant Commander's, Field Marshal's and Warlord's set, can appear only as a labelled alternative.

The client carries 536 sets. 223 have a piece with a Phase 1 source and are in the generated table (`sim/core/client_sets_gen.go`, written by `tools/gen_set_rows.py`); **178 are in the ranker's band 60 pool for at least one class** (the inventory below). The table also carries each set's `PhaseOnePieces`, the most pieces any one class can wear from Phase 1 sources, which splits a set's bonuses into reachable and unreachable (85 bonus rows are unreachable: Eskhandar's 4P, the Tier 1 5Ps with four pieces sourced, the one- and two-piece Tier 0.5 quest entries).

**What matters to a sim.** A bonus matters when it changes damage, healing, threat, mitigation or mana the engine measures: flat stats of every kind; creature-type attack power and spell damage (the default sim target is humanoid, so "against Humanoids" is live and the rest are inert unless a test sets the target type); procs that return mana, energy or rage, raise spell power, add damage or attack speed; cooldown, cost, cast time and crit modifiers on abilities the rotations cast. It does not matter when it changes a utility ability the sim never casts for those ends (Intercept, Blink, Gouge, Hammer of Justice, Psychic Scream, traps, totem radius, Fear Ward), movement, a heal on being struck, a stun on a boss, or a disguise. In the table, the bracket after each bonus is the verdict: `stat` (flat, applied from the row), `model` (hand-written, tested), `none` (no sim effect, reason in the golden), `legacy` (old registration not re-checked; its row matches), `(unreachable)` (more pieces than Phase 1 offers).

Engine before and after: "registered" means the harness found every reachable bonus at the client's threshold with the client's amount (or a hand-written one it cannot check); "registered, wrong" means at least one reachable bonus had a different amount or no registration at its threshold. The "before" column is the engine at `b4fa0ac3f` measured by the same harness.

## 2. What was modelled

**Mechanism.** `core.NewClientItemSet` (fork `sim/core/client_sets.go`) builds a set from its client row. A bonus spell that decodes as a flat stat is applied from its row; every other bonus must be in the set's `Effects` (a hand-written model) or `NoSim` (a one-line reason), or the engine stops at init. The generated table (`client_sets_gen.go`, 223 sets and 375 spells, including the spells each bonus triggers or names) carries each spell's effects, proc chance, duration, cooldown and internal cooldown, so a model reads its numbers instead of typing them. Creature-type attack power and spell damage use `core.ApplyMobTypeAttackPower` and `core.ApplyMobTypeSpellPower`, extracted from the existing item effects. `core.ClientSetTestGear` wears zero-stat synthetic pieces of a set, so a test reads a bonus alone.

**Hand-written models** (each has a test that wears exactly the threshold in pieces, checks one piece fewer has nothing, and holds the effect to the row):

- Procs that give back resources: mana on a spellcast or auto-attack (Magister's, Devout, Beaststalker and their Tier 0.5 variants: 5% for the trigger's 200 mana), energy and rage on a melee hit (Shadowcraft, Darkmantle, Battlegear of Valor and Heroism, Wildheart and Feralheart with its three returns), spell power bursts (Lightforge, Soulforge, The Elements, The Five Thunders: Crusader's Wrath and The Furious Storm), Roar of the Crowd (Gladiator 4P, +5% attack speed for the trigger's duration), Devious Strike (Defias Leather 4P, the bleed) and +1 dagger skill (5P), Enraging Light (Scarlet Crusade 4P, holy damage per melee hit, 3x against undead), Bloodmail and Necropile Drain, Deathbone Surge, Volcanic, Stormshroud, Spider's Kiss, Ironweave 3P spell penetration, Plagueheart (Corruption damage, threat, Life Tap health), Undead Slaying (one helper for four sets), and the rating-based Necropile 2P and Bloodmail 5P.
- PvP: shock crit +2% (Elemental and Enhancement, three set families) and Immolate cast time -0.2 s (warlock), through the existing `core.EquipSpellMod` / class mask machinery.
- Tier 1 5P: Eviscerate cost, Life Tap +20% mana at no extra health, Aimed Shot and Multi-Shot cooldown, Recklessness and Shield Wall cooldown, Penance and Prayer of Mending, Devouring Plague, Judgement, Holy Shock, Forbearance, Riptide, Stormstrike, Lava Burst, Swiftmend, Berserk, Insect Swarm, Tiger's Fury, and Manaflare's 3P and 5P (moved onto the row machinery with its tests). The 4Ps (attack power and spell damage against a creature type, healing and damage, 1.2% expertise) are flat and automatic; Manaflare's 4P, which the dps-sweep review marked "no sim path", now has one (a test points the target at an Elemental).
- Old registrations that were stubs and are now real: Garments of the Oracle 5P and Vestments of Faith 2P (Renew duration and cost), Epiphany (it filtered on damage procs and never fired on a heal), Stormcaller 5P Chain Heal cast time, Earthshatterer 4P Mana Spring +25%, Freethinker 3P Holy Light cast time. Corrected against the rows: Volcanic and Stormshroud (10% not 5%, 40 and 20 damage not 15 to 25, Revitalize 4% not 2%); Undead Slaying (the old code also raised the crit multiplier 2%, doubling the effect on crits); Bonescythe 6P threat (x0.92, not divided by 1.08), Head Rush's 500 ms cooldown, Revealed Flaw's 180 s cooldown (a real behaviour change); Spider's Kiss shreds the target's armor, not the wearer's.

**Tests.** 90 test functions added or changed, in `sim/common/item_sets`, `sim/common/clientsetbonus`, every class and spec package that has a set, and `sim/core`. Tier 1 and PvP also have a table test that each registered set's thresholds equal the client's. A mutation check (doubling a stated chance) failed 10 tests.

**Conformance.** The fork's conformance tool (`sim/conformance`) covers class spells only (cost, cooldown, cast time, GCD, duration, damage) and does not cover set bonuses. This lane extends it: `BuildSetsReport` wears each registered client set one piece short of and at every threshold on a level 60 preset, compares the engine's stat change with the same amount worn as one plain item (so health from stamina and block from defense are measured the same way on both sides), and writes `sim/core/testdata/conformance/sets.golden.md` with the verdicts `declared, matches`, `modelled`, `no sim effect`, `registered, not verified` and `unreachable in Phase 1`. `TestSetBonusConformance` fails on `mismatch` or `threshold missing`. Today: 655 bonus rows over 215 registered sets, 374 `declared, matches`, 75 `modelled`, 125 `no sim effect`, 79 `registered, not verified` (old hand-written bonuses of sets outside Phase 1 or whose row carries no number; the 61 reachable ones were read one by one), 2 `unreachable`, 0 mismatch, 0 missing. At the start (`b4fa0ac3f`) the same harness read, over the same table, 41 mismatches, 54 missing thresholds, 159 unverified, 139 matches and 7 unreachable. Eight client sets are not registered; none has a piece the ranker can offer (Scale of the Gods, five Tier 2.5 quest sets, Dreamwalker Raiment).

**Manaflare Regalia and the other Tier 1 sets carry no stats in this build**, so the registered bonuses are exact and unreachable for the ranker through scoring; this is stated here and in each set's test.

**Assumptions the rows could not settle** (each is in the code as a named constant or a comment):

- Rows that give 100% with no stated chance: Roar of the Crowd, Rogue Armor Energize, Warrior's Resolve, Crusader's Wrath and The Furious Storm. The engine keeps the one proc per minute it used for the energize and rage bonuses and vanilla's 4% for the two spell power bursts. Bloodmail's bleed (row 100%, no cooldown) uses one per minute. Necropile Drain's row has a 10 ms cooldown and 100%: modelled as every harmful cast, shadow damage that cannot crit; the heal back is not modelled.
- Roar of the Crowd's "twice as likely with no ally within 20 yards" is not applied (a sim raid always has allies close). Devious Strike's "from behind" is every melee hit (damage dealers stand behind the boss).
- Wildheart's energy return (spell 27784) has `Misc0` 0, which the client's tables read as mana; the text says energy, and energy is modelled.
- Hunter Tier 1 5P: the row's family mask reaches Aimed Shot only; the text and the lane brief say Aimed Shot and Multi-Shot, and both are modelled (5P is unreachable in Phase 1 either way).
- Insect Swarm +3 s (Grovekeeper Eclipse 5P) is quantised to whole 2 s ticks (the engine's duration mod refuses half a tick), so it adds 2 s.
- Paladin Retribution Tier 1 4P (1301084): the spell's name and its row say Undead (mask 32); its tooltip says Demons. The row is followed.
- Hit and crit rating auras (aura 189 with the hit or crit masks) are decoded locally in `sim/common/item_sets/client_ratings.go` at 10 and 14 rating per percent; `core`'s flat decoder does not read them (a cleaner home).
- The client keeps two ids for several Tier 0.5 and PvP sets (Battlegear of Heroism 511 and 1778, Champion's Raiment 342 and 1754). Phase 1 items carry both, so each id is registered; the engine matches by id first and then by name.

**Deleted.** `sim/common/guardians/eskhandar.go` (the Spirit of Eskhandar 4P pet was a placeholder dealing 1 damage; the bonus is unreachable with three pieces), the vanilla `dungeon_set_1.go`, and the registrations of Lieutenant Commander's Arcanum, Champion's Guard (no client set of those names) and Field Marshal's Aegis (402; its pieces have no Phase 1 source). ## 3. Does the ranker count set bonuses?

**Not when it chooses; only in two narrow ways afterwards.**

1. **Scoring is per piece.** `score()` (`score.go:159`) is the dot product of one candidate's own stats and the spec's stat weights. A set bonus is a third thing neither piece carries alone, so it has no term. `pick()` takes the best score per slot. A 2P hit or a 4P attack-power-against-humanoids is invisible to it.
2. **`trySetCompletion` (`sets.go:108`, called at `main.go:728`) is the one place a set is priced as a set,** and it needs all of: the set's id is in `engineImplementedSetIDs` (`setids_generated.go`, a static map); at least two of the set's pieces are each the **number one scored** candidate of their own slot (`bestSetPieces`, `sets.go:64`: `best := list[0]`); the set is not already fully worn. It then equips those pieces together and runs one 100-iteration verify against the baseline, keeping the set only if it wins by `swapMargin` (1%, `verify.go:312`). A set whose pieces are individually below another item in every slot is never tried, however large its bonus. In the 28-spec run **no set completion fired** (no "completion" note in either log).
3. **`verifyBand`'s runner-up swaps (`verify.go:213`, `applySwaps` at `:332`) measure the whole set of picks.** A runner-up that completes a bonus with pieces already worn is credited with it and can swap in. That is how every set effect in the movement below happened (Holy paladin's Knight-Lieutenant's Lamellar Greaves, which with the Lieutenant Commander's Redemption piece already worn complete its 2P healing bonus, "beat the scored pick Incandescent Mooncloth Boots: +19.6 DPS (326.9 vs 307.3)"; Shadow priest's Virtuous Hands and Epaulets). It extends a set the score-based picker already half built; it cannot start one.
4. **The implemented-set list was stale.** `engineImplementedSetIDs` was generated once by a two-pass script against an older engine, so it called 232 sets implemented, by name only, and missed every set registered by id. It is now rebuilt from the engine's registrations (335 sets) by `TestEngineImplementedSetIDs` (`FOREVER_UPDATE_SETIDS=1 go test ./cmd/leveling-bis -run TestEngineImplementedSetIDs` rewrites it, and the test fails when the file drifts).

**Smallest change that would let a set enter a published pick** (proposed, not built): replace `bestSetPieces`' "number one of each slot" with "the best-scored piece of **this set** in each slot", try each implemented set with at least two such pieces (all pieces together, as now), and keep the existing verify and margin. The class pools have on average 43 sets with two or more pieces (49 druid, 54 hunter, 65 paladin, 24 mage, 20 warlock), so the cost is one extra 100-iteration run per set per band, faction and preset: about 43 x 0.08 s (the 18-slot verify takes 1.4 s) = 3.5 s, against the 1.4 s verify and 4 s trinket pass now. A cheap prefilter removes most of it: sum the set's flat bonus stats (`core.DecodeClientFlatBonus` already reads them, and the ranker links the engine) in score units and try only sets whose bonus beats the score the set's pieces displace. A threshold-aware version (try 2P, 3P, 4P subsets separately) would add a few more runs for sets whose lower thresholds carry the value.
