## Decisions

- **D1. R21-1 is filed as a regression of round 5's R5-2 fix.** "Outside Execute" was added to agree with arms.md:91 but Heroic Strike, on the same card, was never compared. The comparison holds on the flat bonuses alone from 48 to 59 and on the page's own 3.6 speed weapons from 40; at 60 Spearing Strike outhits it on the raid's dragons and giants. The fix reuses arms.md:46's wording, which is true at every band.
- **D2. R21-2 is decided on the client multipliers**, not a sim: the Execute formula, the rage pooling and the Recklessness press are the same for both specs, so the size of an Execute is the product of the damage modifiers, and Death Wish's 20% beats Two-Handed Specialization's 6% with or without Enrage. No sim can make 1.06 x 1.10 exceed 1.20.
- **D3. Rows that render before their talent or spell exists stay with the component lane** (Fury Death Wish and Reckless Swings at 10 to 30, Arms Reckless Swings and Spearing Strike on the leveling order, Protection Last Stand at 10 to 26), as in rounds 15 to 20.
