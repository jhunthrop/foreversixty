## Decisions

- **D1. Riptide "your cheapest heal per mana"** (restoration.md:71, card 11). Kept. On one target it is: r3 at 813 healing is about 5.5 healing a mana with Tidal Focus, Healing Wave r10 with Healing Way about 4.9. Only a Chain Heal rank 2 whose two jumps both land on hurt players, cast on the Riptide target, returns more (about 6.1), and that cast needs the Riptide first, which is the line's point.
- **D2. Chain Heal party jumps.** The tooltip's "If cast on a party member, the heal will only jump to other party members" is the vanilla sentence; round 1 and round 2 researched it (1.12.0 widened it to raid members in a raid). Not re-filed.
- **D3. "Your weapon imbues only proc on melee swings"** (elemental.md:96). Kept as round 7 D3 decided: the restriction to melee is the claim, and Rockbiter has no proc to contradict it.
