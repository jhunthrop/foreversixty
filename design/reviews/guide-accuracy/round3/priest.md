## Decisions (owner: nothing goes back as a taste call)

- **R3-D1 Healer card notes that name rank 1 casts are not re-filed.** The Holy Heal filler note ("rank 1 in a raid, the cheapest heal you have") and the Discipline party note ("Lesser Heal rank 1 ... for very little mana") disagree with the pages' Low ranks paragraphs. They describe the card actions round 1 handed to the sim round (round1/priest.md O1 (b)/(c), "card notes still describe the r1 casts; rewrite them with (b)/(c)"). They get rewritten when the ranks move, not before.
- **R3-D2 The Starshards line waits on the sim.** Leaving it off the Shadow page is not false while the rotation does not cast it. If SIM-R3-2 shows it gains beyond error, the Night Elf line and the rotation get it (text below). If it does not, the page stays silent about it, as a top player would about a spell you do not press.
- **R3-D3 Undead Cannibalize is left out.** On 70755 Cannibalize gives back health and mana (7% every 2 s for 10 s beside a corpse, `races.json`), which is real for leveling. The page saying nothing about it is not an error, and the Leveling section is not a race guide. No change.
- **R3-D4 Web read.** Forever guides agree with the current picks without numbers: Icy Veins' Forever Shadow guide names Gnome for Alliance and Undead for Horde, RankedBoost's Forever priest race tier list puts Undead first, Gnome and Troll in S, Night Elf in A. Neither measures anything and both predate 70755, so the sim decides. When it ties, the picks stay.
