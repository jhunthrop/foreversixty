## Decisions

- **Night Elf stays the Alliance pick for Arms and Fury, Undead the Horde pick.** The measurements that chose them fired Elune's Light on cooldown, which is how R3-1's fix tells the player to use it, so the decision stands without a rerun; the fix only removes advice that would have played the racial worse than it was measured.
- **Elune's Light timing: on cooldown, paired with Death Wish for Fury, never inside Recklessness.** Settled by the client text of both spells (100% crit under Recklessness leaves no table for a further 10%), not a taste call. Classic consensus is the same for every crit cooldown under Recklessness.
- **Sweeping Strikes "lasts 20 seconds" in What changed from 1.12 (index.md:32): not filed.** Forever's spell is 20 s (`spellconst` 12292 duration 20000), and Wowhead's Classic Era data also shows 20 s while older wiki text says 10 s; the bullet may name an unchanged value, but no source settles 1.12 cleanly, so it is not CONFIRMED.
- **Dwarf "trails because its 1% hit matters least once your gear has you near the cap" (arms.md:114, fury.md:117): not filed.** Fury's band 60 weights put 1% hit (45.2) above 1% crit (35.2), so the explanation is weak, but Dwarf does measure last, and the special-attack cap the BiS gear reaches is a real reason. Sim request 3 asks what actually costs Dwarf the gap.
