## Decisions

1. **Pounce in Forever needs you behind the target.** The client description of all three ranks and the Wowhead Forever tooltip agree. A client description beats Era memory (round 1 lesson). On a boss you cannot get behind, the cat opens with Claw, not Pounce.
2. **Feral Charge (Cat) into Ravage is not claimed.** No source says whether the leap keeps Prowl, so the R3-1 fix for feral.md:112 drops the "for a Shred or a Ravage" wording. Use: "Feral Charge's Cat Form leap lands you behind a mob for a Shred, and in Bear Form it is the charge you pull dungeon packs with."
3. **A failed Shred refunds 80%.** This is a Classic rule the engine models (shred.go `Refund: 0.8`), and Forever did not change it. It does not change hit's place in the stat order, only why hit matters.
4. **Restoration's spare Balance point goes into Improved Regrowth.** A healer gets nothing from Improved Moonfire. The planner rules and tier gates show the point is free. The swap is measured before the build string moves (Sims requested 3), but the prose fix is right either way.
5. **Skyborne stays the paid option on every druid page,** with Wind Blessed named as its throughput racial. Night Elf and Tauren stay the free picks until the race sims below say otherwise.
