## Decisions

- **D1. Mage races are set by the round 3 sims.** Horde: Undead for all three specs (beyond error for Arcane and Fire; Frost is a three-way tie and Undead is nominally ahead and brings Will of the Forsaken). Alliance: Gnome for Arcane and Frost (beyond error over Human); Fire is a Gnome/Human tie, so the frontmatter keeps Gnome and the prose says they are even until the bare tie-break (S1) decides. Skyborne is named only as the paid option. Replacement race paragraphs, top-player voice, no em dashes:
  - **arcane.md:99** "**Gnome** is the best Alliance Arcane mage: Eureka! is 10% more damage and 10% cheaper spells for 8 seconds on a 2 minute cooldown, and Expansive Mind adds 5% mana. Press it with Arcane Power on the pull and again the moment it is back. **Human** is close behind with 1% crit and 10% Spirit, which runs all the time thanks to Arcane Meditation and Mage Armor. On the Horde, **Undead** is the pick: Touch of the Grave gives your spells a 30% chance to drain 5% of your maximum health from the boss, at most once every 15 seconds, and Will of the Forsaken breaks fears. **Orc** is next: Blood Fury adds 10% spell power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes, so press it with Arcane Power on the pull and on cooldown after that. **Troll** Berserking adds 10% cast speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes and lines up with every Arcane Power. **Skyborne** is a paid unlock and only Alliance for mages; Read Leyline (50% more health and mana regeneration for 15 seconds, 2 minute cooldown) and Wind Blessed's 2% haste still do less damage than a Gnome or a Human."
  - **fire.md:101** "**Gnome** and **Human** come out even for Fire. Eureka! is 10% more damage and 10% cheaper spells for 8 seconds on a 2 minute cooldown, and Expansive Mind adds 5% mana: press it with Combustion on the pull and again the moment it is back. Human brings 1% crit, which Fire turns into Ignite, and 10% Spirit. On the Horde, **Undead** is the pick: Touch of the Grave gives your spells a 30% chance to drain 5% of your maximum health from the boss, at most once every 15 seconds, and Will of the Forsaken breaks fears. **Orc** is next: Blood Fury adds 10% spell power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes, so press it with Combustion on the pull and on cooldown after that. **Troll** Berserking adds 10% cast speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes and lines up with every Combustion, but falls behind both. **Skyborne** is a paid unlock and only Alliance for mages, and it does the least Fire damage of the lot."
  - **frost.md:102** "**Gnome** is the best Alliance Frost mage: Eureka! is 10% more damage and 10% cheaper spells for 8 seconds on a 2 minute cooldown, and Expansive Mind adds 5% mana. **Human** adds 1% crit and is just behind. **Skyborne** edges a Gnome by a hair with Wind Blessed, 2% haste that never turns off, but it needs the paid Skyborne Heroic Pack, so take it only if you already own it. On the Horde, **Undead**, **Orc** and **Troll** come out even: Touch of the Grave drains 5% of your maximum health from the boss on a 30% chance at most once every 15 seconds, Blood Fury adds 10% spell power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes, and Berserking 10% cast speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes. Take Undead for Will of the Forsaken on fear fights."
  - Frontmatter: arcane, fire, frost `recommendedRaces: [gnome, undead]`.
- **D2. Frostfire Bolt line keeps its point, loses the wrong number.** The Frost build keeps Accelerant (SIM-5 still owns whether it should), so the prose may not call Frostfire Bolt a 3 second cast in that build (R3-6).
