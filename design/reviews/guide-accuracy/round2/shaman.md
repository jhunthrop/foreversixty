## Decisions

### D1. Lightning Overload's damage after 70755 (coordinator: decide, do not leave for the owner)

- **What the client says.** Talent 104759, 70755 text: "Gives your Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning spells a 30% chance to cast a second, similar spell on the same target at no additional cost that causes half damage and no threat." The overload is its own spell per rank (408439..408477 for Lightning Bolt, 408479..408484 for Chain Lightning), each with its own school-damage effect. 70755 raised Lightning Bolt rank 10 (15208) from 196 to 353 base at 0.714 and Chain Lightning rank 4 (10605) from 123 at 0.571 to 510 at 0.714, and left the overload rows at 70753's values (408477 98 at 0.357; 408484 62 at 0.2855; `spellconst/shaman.json` 70753 vs 70755).
- **Description versus rows.** The description rule says a description beats an inferred flag. Here the description is a fixed sentence with no `$s` value, while the rows are the damage effects the server rolls for the overload spell; they are not an inference. Classic 1.15's engine (Season of Discovery, same spell family) deals each overload spell's own row, so the server will too unless Blizzard also hotfixed them, and the 70755 push changed the bolt rows without touching these.
- **Web.** No Forever combat log exists yet (Warcraft Logs has no Forever host before launch, per the WCL key note in memory); no blue post, guide or discord note measures the overload. Searches: Warcraft Wiki "Lightning Overload" (half damage, no threat, by design); Forever guides (Warcraft Tavern deep dive, conquestcapped talents page) quote the tooltip only.
- **Decision.** The overload hits for its own, smaller row: at 70755 numbers about 40% of a Lightning Bolt with gear (98 + 0.357 SP against 353 + 0.714 SP), not half. The guide does not quote a share. elemental.md:62 already says "a free, smaller second bolt with no threat", which is the stable truth either way. Exact guide change: none. Optional polish for Chain Lightning accuracy: "a free, smaller copy of the spell with no threat".
- **Sim round.** None needed now: the pinned engine already deals the overload rows (`sim/shaman/lightning_bolt.go:41-62`, `chain_lightning.go:34-46`) at 10/20/30% (`talents.go:644`). Re-check against the first level-60 Forever log: overload hit over bolt hit should read about 0.4 (rows) and not 0.5; if it reads 0.5, switch the engine to half the cast spell.
