## Decisions (open choices settled with Forever sources, no owner taste calls)

1. **Feral Charge in the cat raid build: stays out.** The Cat Form leap is a 30 second repositioning tool with no damage. The raid build's tier gates leave no dead point to trade, because Feral Instinct and Ferocity are the gate for row 3. On a boss that turns, Claw (with Ferocity) already covers it. The prose (DRUID-R2-1) names the leap as the swap for a fight where you keep losing the boss's back. Sources: Blizzard deep dive (Feral Charge), Wowhead Forever tooltip 414924.
2. **Nature's Reach melee hit: the client row decides it, yes.** Blizzard's wording ("now also grants increased hit chance and applies to all your damaging spells") reads as spells, but the 70755 row has the melee hit aura and the tooltip is unscoped. Per the round 1 lesson, the client description and row beat an inference from prose. The guide states nothing false today, so the only change is the build re-run (S7). Do not add a "hit for cats" line until the build takes it.
3. **Swiftmend consuming the HoT: no.** The developer note, the deep dive and the beta logs together outweigh the stale word "consumed" in the tooltip.
4. **Downranking threshold (17 or 18 levels): no guide sentence depends on it** (DRUID-R2-10). Use 18 in the engine round unless a level 60 log shows otherwise.
5. **Ferocity over Furor in the cat raid build: keep.** Both are tier 0 gate points. Ferocity cheapens the Claw you use on a turning boss. Furor only pays when you leave Cat Form, which a raid cat rarely does, and the raid prose already says when to take it.
