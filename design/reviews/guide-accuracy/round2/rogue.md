## Sim findings (for the next sim round; no engine changes now)

- **SIM-R2-1 (CONFIRMED).** Assassination APL: the Cold Blood line (priority 2, CP ≥ 4) fires ahead of the Slice and Dice line, so whenever Slice and Dice is the finisher, Cold Blood goes onto the next Mutilate. Gate Cold Blood on "Slice and Dice active with ≥ 3 s left" (that is, the Eviscerate condition), or move it between the Slice and Dice and Eviscerate lines. See ROGUE-R2-1.
- **SIM-R2-2 (NEEDS-SIM).** Combat raid 60: measure the three stealth openers on the same boss pull, Garrote vs Ambush vs Backstab. It decides ROGUE-R2-5.
- **SIM-R2-3 (LIKELY, leveling order, needs a ladder row).** Combat leveling takes Puncturing Wounds at 19-21, before Precision (22-24). The prose and the APL level on a sword with Sinister Strike, and Puncturing Wounds only touches Backstab and Mutilate. Test Precision first (and Puncturing Wounds after Precision, or Deflection/Improved Sprint for the gate) on the leveling ladder at 20-30.
- **Carried, still open on d1d9f2775:** the Mutilate off-hand penalty (`mutilate.go` still swings the off hand at full weapon damage, and its comment still says the penalty "does not apply"), SIM-7 (`preparation.go` resets only Cold Blood, Shadowstep and Vanish), and SIM-1 to SIM-5, SIM-8 and SIM-10 as filed in round 1. SIM-6 is closed (Premeditation needs no Stealth on the pin).

## Round 1 research answers re-checked

- **Mutilate off-hand (item 1).** The client description ("90% weapon damage plus an additional 40 with each weapon") does not settle it, as round 1 said. wowsims/sod `mutilate.go` does use `OHNormalizedWeaponDamage` and the `dwsMultiplier()` on the off hand, with the flat inside the 0.95 (fetched 2026-11-04). The answer stands.
- **Occult Poison (item 2).** Warcraft Wiki, Occult Poison II: "The subject of this article is exclusive to Season of Discovery." The answer stands.
- **Rupture (item 4).** Warcraft Tavern Combat: "As a Combat Rogue, you won't ever bother with Rupture!" Warcraft Tavern Assassination: "you won't bother with Rupture since you won't have enough Energy or time to use it." Deep Dive: "damaging finishers like Rupture and Eviscerate are a lower priority". All quoted as claimed. Icy Veins returned HTTP 403 and could not be re-read, but it is not needed for the consensus. The answer stands. Note: both Warcraft Tavern pages predate 70755 (no Shank, Flash of Steel or Coup de Grace), and both open with Ambush. Our Garrote opener is backed by our own measured runs, not by those pages.
