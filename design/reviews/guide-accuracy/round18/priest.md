## Decisions

- **R18-D1. The page's own race's row is held to the prose; other races' rows stay SHOULDs.** Round 17 D6 kept a Gnome's Mind Blast exception out of a Human page. R18-2 is the other case: the Human row is what the page renders for its race, so the sentence contradicts the page's own card at 50 and 51. The Undead Mind Blast and Renew exceptions (S1, S2) stay optional clauses.
- **R18-D2. A card note that credits a talent before the shipped order takes it is a finding at the levels it renders on** (round 15 and 16 rogue precedent, round 17's rule). R18-3 and R18-4 are fixed with level-proof wordings ("once you have ...") rather than per-level notes, so the 60 reader loses nothing.
- **R18-D3. The Holy filler note is level-proofed, not the row removed.** The 59 row is measured (round 19 H2, both seeds) and the per-mana claim holds on the client numbers, so the note names both ranks instead of "your top trainer rank".
- **R18-D4. Lesser Heal at 60 stays with the owner** (sim round 18 D3, round 19 "D3 at 60"; R17-D7). The card's "a small top-up for little mana" does not contradict discipline.md:74, so not filed.
- **R18-D5. Inspiration missing from both talent lists is a SHOULD**: an omission, not a false statement, and the Holy stat paragraph already names it.
