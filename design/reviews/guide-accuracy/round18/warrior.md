## Decisions

- **D1. The six findings are filed under the every-band rule** (ROGUE-R15-2, ROGUE-R16-2, PRIEST-R17-5, PRIEST-R17-9; the round 18 brief: "a card note must be true at every level it renders on"). Rounds 10 to 17 checked the warrior notes against their conditions at 60 and did not read them band by band; this round did, against `addon-data.json`'s rows, the shipped orders and the trainer levels. Two of them (R18-5, R18-6) are wrong play as well as wrong credit: a level 30 to 49 Arms warrior told to leave Battle Stance for a Recklessness they do not have, and a level 10 to 22 Fury warrior told to pool rage from 25% for an Execute they do not have.
- **D2. Conditional notes pass.** "with Improved Bloodrage", "with Improved Berserker Rage", "only with Improved Slam", "raid build only" and "with Improved Overpower" are read as conditionals, as R15-2 read "in the raid build". The Fury Bloodrage and Berserker Rage notes are Shoulds, not findings, even though the Fury Bloodrage conditional is unmet on eighteen of twenty bands.
- **D3. Rows that render before their spell exists are not filed** (the component lane's whole-row issue, round 15; the brief's "raid-build cards shown at low bands"). Only the first band on which a player actually holds the ability counts against the note's wording, which is why the Reckless Swings half of R18-4 names band 32 alone.
