"Conflagrate rank 3 and Immolate rank 5 arrive with it, but on level 40 gear your mana runs short. At 40 keep casting Immolate rank 4 and move up to rank 5 at 41. At 41 drop Corruption from rank 4 back to rank 3 for that one level. Each is a small gain that costs you nothing, and from 42 the top rank of both wins again."

- Trainer rows (`trainables/warlock.json`): Conflagrate rank 3 is 17962 at 40 (ranks 1 and 2, 1293817 and 1293818, at 25 and 32); Immolate rank 4 is 2941 at 30 and rank 5 is 11665 at 40; Corruption rank 3 is 6223 at 24 and rank 4 is 7648 at 34, rank 5 at 44. So "arrive with it" (Incinerate 412758 at 40) holds for both, "from rank 4 back to rank 3" is the right direction, and at 42 rank 5 Immolate and rank 4 Corruption are the top ranks learned.
- Rows (`rank-choices.json` warlock-destruction): Immolate 2941 from 40 to 40; Corruption 6223 from 41 to 41. Nothing at 42 or above.
- The rendered card (`addon-data.json` warlock-destruction): band 40 casts Corruption 7648 and Immolate 2941; band 41 casts Corruption 6223 and Immolate 11665; band 42 casts Corruption 7648 and Immolate 11665. The sentence matches all three bands exactly.
- The reason holds per cast on the client numbers (round 17's rule): Immolate rank 4 is 56 + 95 for 155 mana against rank 5's 72 + 125 for 220, and the spell power share is the same on both ranks (0.2 direct, 0.13 a tick), so rank 4 does more per point of mana at any spell power a level 40 wears; Corruption rank 3 is 132 for 100 against rank 4's 168 for 160, same coefficient per tick. "Your mana runs short" is the per-cast reason and sim round 19 R1 is the measurement (about 33 Life Taps in 3 minutes at 40).
