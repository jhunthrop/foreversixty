## How a card note renders, and where (for the findings below)

- The addon shows, at each level band, the first sentence of every curated note whose cast spell has a trainable rank at that level (addon-data.json `condition`; addon/ForeverSixty/Rotation.lua `Rotation.model` shows a band's lines as they are, with no spellbook filter). Talent gates (`auraIsKnown` on a talent aura) are not resolved by level: data/pipeline/addonrotation.py `_level_truth` reads only `currentLevel` comparisons, mirroring web/src/lib/bis/apl-level-gate.ts, and every other clause is open. So the Arcane band at every level carries both the Heating Up-gated rows and their leveling counterparts.
- The website card renders at level 60 only (`GUIDE_ROTATION_LEVEL = 60`) for the described build (the raid build where one differs), drops rows whose gate that build settles against (`buildRunsGates`) and tags rows that need a raid-only talent.
- So a note's first sentence is read at every band from the spell's first trainable rank upward; the rest of the note at 60.
