## Decisions

- **D1. Round 12's SHAMAN-R12-S2 is withdrawn.** Its note said Enhancement's raid set "carries none" (no potion). `sim/request/raid-consumes.json` shaman-enhancement carries `major_frenzy_potion` from 55, so "Rage of the Farseer, trinkets, Blood Fury and your potion go together on the pull" (enhancement.md:68) has a real potion behind it, and the consumables table names it. Nothing to change.
- **D2. Healing Wave "drop a rank" (restoration.md:73, card .17) stands.** On base numbers Healing Wave 10 is the most mana-efficient rank (1615/620 against 1344/560 and 972/440), and rank 8 only passes it per mana above about 700 healing power. The advice is still right in play, because a heal that overheals wastes its mana, and the measured rank rows (rank 3 at 20 to 39, rank 5 at 40 to 49) come from the heal profile with overhealing counted. The page claims "save mana", not "more healing per mana on paper".
- **D3. Elemental "a bolt three or four ranks below your best ... does more in total before you run dry"** (elemental.md:116) stands on the measured `rank-choices.json` rows (rank 3 at 30 to 39, 4 at 40 to 49, 6 at 50 to 59), though on base numbers alone a higher rank is more mana-efficient; the gain comes from spell power on a cheaper cast inside the 17-level grace. Kept as measured.
- **D4. Windfury Weapon solo at 60** (enhancement.md:79, :121). The preset keeps Windfury Weapon without a totem by rule, not by a measured comparison, but my estimate puts the two extra 20% swings well ahead of Rockbiter's flat bonus without the totem's own extra attack (roughly 40% more white damage against about a third of that). No request filed; the page's call matches.
