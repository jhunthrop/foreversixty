## Findings

### WARLOCK-R17-1 (CONFIRMED, kind: consumable/race; a measured result the page now contradicts)
- **Where:** affliction.md:97.
- **Quote:** "Undead is as good as Orc for Affliction: Touch of the Grave gives your spells a chance to drain up to 5% of your maximum health from the target, at most once every 15 seconds, which helps a spec that pays for mana in health."
- **Evidence:** sim round 18 A4 (`design/reviews/2026-11-04-sim-audit-round18/warlock.md`), the race run guide round 15 requested on the shipped Wrack raid build `25520020135001351/00052/005004` with Heart of the Lion on the demon, band 60 raid, frozen BiS, 10,000 iterations, seeds 1 and 50001: Orc 750.6 / 750.8, Undead 747.0 / 747.2 (-0.5% at both seeds), Troll 739.1 / 739.2. The same gap on the other two pages reads "Undead is close behind Orc" (Demonology -0.5%, round 19 D2; Destruction -0.3%, round 19 X3). Round 14 accepted "as good as" on round 16 A1's 0.4%, and round 15 wrote the trigger: "If Orc now leads Undead beyond error, the line becomes 'Undead is close behind Orc', as on the other two pages." It does, at both seeds, with Command riding on the demon's larger share.
- **Fix** (affliction.md:97, replace the last sentence): "Undead is close behind Orc: Touch of the Grave gives your spells a chance to drain up to 5% of your maximum health from the target, at most once every 15 seconds, which helps a spec that pays for mana in health."

No other statement on the four pages, and no rendered warlock card clause, is false on 70755. None contradicts its own page, the header build card, the live BiS rows (either faction), the race table or the stat table.
