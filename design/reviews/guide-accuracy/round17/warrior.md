## Decisions

- **WARRIOR-R17-1 is filed as CONFIRMED, not a Should.** The project already treats "swings" against "white swings" as a mechanical distinction (fury.md:71 and :86, round 16's note on the Reckless Swings mask, and round 19's Unbridled Wrath and Reckless Swings edits), the Forever log count is hard evidence, and the line understates Flurry on exactly the swings a Fury warrior makes most.
- **arms.md:111's Artisan's Precision clause is a confirmed error on main** (a round 16 edit that contradicts the page's own hit cap statement and the BiS rows), listed under Covered by sim round 19 because r19 removes it. If r19 lands first, nothing remains to fix; if the guide round lands first, apply r19's sentence.
- **Round 19's warrior guide wording stands in every line.** Its race lines match its own measurements and the 1% rule; its Unbridled Wrath lines match #105 and the 70450 log, including "from either hand".
- **Race picks stay**: Night Elf / Undead on Arms and Fury, Night Elf / Tauren on Protection, now measured on the Deep Wounds engine.
- **Not filed, per the brief**: the BiS race captions, the leveling tree's rendering, the rendered Arms Reckless Swings row (regen), cut-off card notes, raid-build cards shown at low bands.
- **Provenance caution, not a finding**: round 19 attributes the Deep Wounds rules to "Blizzard on forever-bugs #234". The page shows the tracker maintainer (AidanZMoon) relaying what they "asked and confirmed"; the three rules are stated there, and the linked commit "Warrior: Remove AP scaling of Deep Wounds" is the tracker's, not Blizzard's. No guide line rests on the attribution.
