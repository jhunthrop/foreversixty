## Engine questions

- The engine's spell-power level penalty is `LevelPenaltyFromMaxLevel` only (maxLevel + 12, 5% a level). Vanilla also cut the spell-power share of ranks whose spell level is below 20 ((20 - level) x 3.75%). If Forever's client keeps that rule, every leveling rank ladder that casts a sub-20 rank (Elemental Lightning Bolt rank 3 at 26, 27 and 30 to 33; Restoration Healing Wave ranks 2 and 3 to 36) is measured with too much spell power. No page states a value that depends on it (the prose says "most of your spell damage", which holds either way), so this is for the sim lane only.
