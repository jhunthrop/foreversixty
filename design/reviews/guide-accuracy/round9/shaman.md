## Decisions

- **D1. Low ranks of heals: the engine pin contradicts the page, and the page stays.** `sim/core/spell.go:794-809` on d1d9f2775 says "Heals take no penalty in Forever", based on low-level beta logs (Healing Wave ranks 1 and 4 among them). restoration.md:76 and card 17 follow round 1's researched level-gap rule (full bonus for 17 levels, then 5% a level; Blizzard's 1 Oct note, ForeverChanges' measurement). Logs taken below about level 35 cannot tell the two rules apart for those ranks, and Blizzard's note says outright that low ranks lose bonus. So the page is the game-correct side. This is round 1's open sim-round item (round1/shaman.md Q3 (a)), not re-filed. If a level 60 log ever shows a full bonus on Healing Wave rank 4, both the paragraph and card 17 change together.
- **D2. Elemental Fury "doubles"** is exact on a spell's 150% crit (see above). Do not rewrite it as "+100% damage", which would read as 250%.
- **D3. Restoration Skyborne line** leaves out Read Leyline correctly: Windshaper Skyborne do not have it.
- **D4. "On a long fight, rank 10 alone runs you dry"** (elemental.md:80). The card's file note says the paced rotation runs out of mana for under 1 s of a 180 s raid fight. That is the paced rotation, not rank 10 alone, so the two do not contradict each other. Kept. The Earth Shock filler sim below measures the same mana budget.
