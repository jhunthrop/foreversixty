## Decisions

- **D1. Totemic Call.** Client description over the range field and older write-ups: the spell brings your totems to you. The 30 yd range field is not explained by the description, so the fix quotes no range.
- **D2. Blood Fury timing.** On its own 2 minute cooldown, lined up with Lightning Mastery or Rage of the Farseer on the pull. That is how the race sims that made Orc the pick ran it. No sim needed.
- **D3. Water totem quest.** Named in the Restoration text only, where the page's first Water totem comes up (Healing Stream at 20). Elemental and Enhancement name no Water or Air totem in their leveling text, so nothing to add there.
- **D4. Icy Veins calculator source** (index.md:11): kept. It is a 403 to automated fetches, not dead, and round 4 accepted the same entry on the priest index.
