## Decisions

- **D1. Heart of the Lion "Train at level 1".** Kept. The 70532 raw SkillLineAbility row for 409580 has AcquireMethod 0 (trainer), not 2 (learned automatically), and 70755 marks it active.
- **D2. Heart of the Lion 280.** Kept. Engine and Wowhead both scale from BaseLevel 0 (40 + 4 x 60); the 276 you would get from SpellLevel 1 is not what the client shows.
- **D3. Dual Wield at 20 for Survival.** Kept. The 70755 trainables carry no Dual Wield row for any class (it is a skill-line spell, SkillLineAbility 610 for 674 with AcquireMethod 2 and the hunter bit in ClassMask 13), so the data neither confirms nor contradicts the level; Classic hunters dual wield from 20 and no Forever source says otherwise.
- **D4. Explosive Trap "under a pack the tank is holding".** Kept. Traps still go at your own feet (Blizzard deep dive), but they work in combat in Forever, so stepping into the pack to drop it is legal play; the line does not claim you can throw it.
- **D5. Freezing Trap "laid before the pull".** Kept as true advice; Forever lets you trap in combat too, but the line does not say otherwise.
