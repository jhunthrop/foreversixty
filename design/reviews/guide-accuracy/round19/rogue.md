## Decisions

- **D1. ROGUE-R19-1 is CONFIRMED** under the round 18 rule as the brief restates it ("every card note, including its later sentences"): the second sentence's reason rests on Mutilate and Seal Fate on 18 bands where the order has given neither. - **D4. The 70790 activation is clean on the rogue side**: every rogue client file except two unrelated rings is byte-identical to 70755 under the label, and no rendered text names 70755.
