## Decisions

- **D1. The notes' later sentences now follow the round 18 rule on the druid pages too.** Round 18 audited the druid rank rows and passed the talent clauses; read band by band, four notes give a reason that rests on a talent the shipped order has not given yet (Rend and Tear twice, Bloom twice, Eclipse, Innervate), and one quotes a full-rank cost on the band that has one point (Maul, the warrior R19-2 case).
- **D2. R19-1 is the worst**: its first sentence, which is all the addon row shows, tells a level 20 to 34 Moonkin to cast Wrath every global, while the card it sits on never casts that Wrath below 35. The fix puts "From 35" first and gives the one-point number, so the leveling build's 0.17 seconds and the raid build's half second are both on the card.
- **D3. Conditionals that are unmet rather than wrong stay Shoulds** (Predatory Discipline, Improved Moonfire and Nature's Splendor), as in warrior round 19 D3.
- **D4. The 70790 activation is clean on the druid side**: every druid client file is identical to 70755 under the label, the only item change is two Sulfuras rings no druid page names, and no page or note names 70755.
