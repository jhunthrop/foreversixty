## How the rule is applied this round

The brief's round 19 lens: every sentence of a note must be true at every level its row renders on, crediting no talent, trained spell or rank before the shipped order or trainer gives it. Two refinements, both following round 18 D1 and the warrior round 19 report:
- A row the APL gates on a talent (`auraIsKnown` Heating Up) is read where that gate can hold; its later sentences describe the build that has the talent. The addon's missing talent gate is the component lane's (round 18 Sim findings).
- A conditional instruction on a proc or stack the character cannot have yet is idle, not false (SHOULD). A sentence that states what an active cast does, or a number for a talent the order has given only one rank of, is a finding.
