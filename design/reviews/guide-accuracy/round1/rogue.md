## What checks out

70755 moved seven talents out (Murder, Improved Eviscerate, Improved Sinister Strike, Improved Distract, Quietus, Cutthroat, Thousand Cuts) and eight in (Blood Splatter, Coup de Grace, Efficient Strikes, Composure, Flash of Steel, Quick Getaway, Boot Knife, Death Mark), and retuned Opportunity (20%), Dual Wield Specialization (50%), Aggression (15% to Sinister Strike and Eviscerate only), Flawless Execution (15), Venom (passive on Shank), Ghostly Strike (Stealth-free Ambush/Garrote) and Hemorrhage (Garrote too). None of the removed talents is named anywhere, and every 70755 value the prose quotes matches the rank text: Venom 30%/20%/6 s, Relentless Strikes 20%/point for 25, Seal Fate 100%, Lethality 20%, Vile Poisons 20%, Improved Poisons, Coup de Grace 20% plus Death Mark, Flash of Steel +15/-2 s, Boot Knife 2 points, Death Mark 25 energy/10 s/6 s/12% per point/double for bleeds/AP and crit cap, Hemorrhage 145%, Serrated Blades 9%/30%, Ghostly Strike 180%/15%/7 s/20 s, Initiative, Dirty Deeds, Blood Splatter 6/9 s, Hack and Slash, Weapon Expertise 2%, Puncturing Wounds 30%/45%, Aggression, Efficient Strikes 3/5.

**Shank.** Shank is in every guide and the index and comes in correctly: level 24, rank 2 at 44 (`trainables/rogue.json`), 5 energy, 15 then 25 energy back, 10 s cooldown, off-hand 50% weapon damage, cannot miss (`spellconst` 1329283/1329549; `sim/rogue/shank.go` header: Attributes_3 ALWAYS_HIT). Each tree's Shank talent is named: Venom, Flash of Steel, Boot Knife. The Flash of Steel arithmetic (40 energy every 8 s) is right.

**Spells and racials.** Spell levels and costs check out: Redirect at 1 (10 s cooldown, 15 s), Poised at 18, Instant Poison at 20, Deadly Poison at 30, Hemorrhage and Venom at 30, Mutilate/Death Mark/Adrenaline Rush at 40 (the tier gates agree), Kidney Shot 25 energy with a 20 s cooldown, Blade Flurry 25 energy with a 2 min cooldown, Cold Blood 3 min, Premeditation 2 min, Preparation 10 min, Cheap Shot 2 combo points. Mutilate needs two daggers (`mutilate.go:125`). Racials match `races.json` on 70755: Elune's Light, Berserking, Blood Fury, Eureka!, Ruthless, Artisan's Precision, Will of the Forsaken, Wind Blessed. One-handed axes are confirmed in `classes.json`.

**Gear, professions and poisons.** Gear and professions check out: Heartseeker is a blacksmithing dagger (`loot.json` crafted:blacksmithing), the two Hearts of the Mountain are enchanting crafts, and Devilsaur and Stormshroud are leatherworking crafts. Deadly Poison in the main hand with Instant in the off hand is backed by `design/reviews/2026-10-07-rogue-parity.md:65,114`.

**Bleeds and poisons crit** (`forever-dots-crit.md`). The engine does this for Garrote, Rupture and Deadly Poison (`Dot.OutcomeTick`, `core.periodicDamageCanCrit`). No guide line contradicts it.

**Voice.** No em or en dashes, and no sim, APL, search, band or preset talk in the prose.

## Sim findings (engine or rotation vs the real game; for the next sim round, no engine changes now)

- **SIM-1 (CONFIRMED).** Shank rank 1 is never cast from 24 to 43. All three APLs name only rank 2, 1329549 (learned at 44). The ladder goldens record `kind=unresolved_id action={SpellID: 1329549}` at levels 30, 38 and 40 for every spec (`rogue-assassination.golden.md`, `rogue-combat.golden.md`, `rogue-subtlety.golden.md` Violations), and no Shank casts appear below 50. - **SIM-2 (LIKELY).** Subtlety's Eviscerate line (CP ≥ 3 with Rupture ticking) sits above nothing that waits for Death Mark, so Death Mark at five points is reached only when a two-point jump lands on exactly five. The ladder shows zero Death Mark casts at level 40 (`rogue-subtlety.golden.md:187`). Fix to test: gate Eviscerate on Death Mark being on cooldown (or more than a global from ready). The guide prose already plays it this way.
- **SIM-3 (LIKELY).** Subtlety's Slice and Dice line (`rogue-subtlety.json` priority 7: CP ≥ 3, Slice and Dice down or under 3 s, Rupture ticking) is a strict subset of the Eviscerate line above it. It fires only when energy is 25 to 34 (Eviscerate is 35). Real play: Slice and Dice before Eviscerate when it is down, as the guide says (subtlety.md:76).
- **SIM-4 (NEEDS-SIM).** Questions to sim. (a) Combat raid build, raid gear: is Rupture at 5 combo points ever worth a global over Eviscerate at 5, now that bleeds always crit? (b) The same for Assassination with Blood Splatter (Rupture at 4+ when Slice and Dice is up and Rupture is down).
- **SIM-5 (NEEDS-SIM).** Subtlety: Ghostly Strike now lets the next Garrote go in without Stealth (1329326). Garrote gets Hemorrhage's 15%, Opportunity's 20% and Initiative's extra point, crits as a bleed, lasts 27 s with Blood Splatter and is stored at twice the rate by Death Mark. Test whether re-applying Garrote after Ghostly Strike whenever Garrote has dropped beats Hemorrhage in that slot. The engine already models the opener buff (`ghostly_strike.go` canOpen).
- **SIM-6 (CONFIRMED on the pinned engine; fixed on fork r14).** `premeditation.go` (2eaae03c6) still requires Stealth. Fork r14 E e01d5596a lifts the requirement, so the Subtlety APL also needs an in-combat Premeditation line (three points or fewer).

In the Subtlety build that is a Premeditation + Shank + Ghostly Strike reset once a fight.
- **SIM-8 (LIKELY, Subtlety Shank overflow).** Gate the Subtlety Shank line on CP ≤ 3 (Boot Knife adds 2), and Ghostly Strike on CP ≤ 4. See ROGUE-4.
