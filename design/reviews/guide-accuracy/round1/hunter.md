### Q1 / HUNTER-9 / S1: does Deadly Aspects' ranged proc work under Aspect of the Falcon? RESOLVED: no, Hawk is the ranged aspect.
- Answer: Forever's Aspect of the Falcon is the Season of Discovery spell (469145, same id, same text: the talent "can now proc from and benefit melee attacks"). In SoD, where it has been live since 2024, players report hundreds of auto shots under Falcon with no ranged proc, and that Hawk keeps the ranged proc ("Use hawk for pve if ranged, falcon for melee"). 70755 spellconst 469145: aura 99 (melee AP), aura 124 (ranged AP), aura 42 proc trigger 469144, i.e. one proc aura that adds melee as the proc source.
