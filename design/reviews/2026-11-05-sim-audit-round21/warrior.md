# Round 21, lane warrior (Arms, Fury, Protection)

Site `wt/r21-warrior` on int/r21 (d21fa8e0a). Engine `lane/r21-warrior` on fork int/r21 (c2909c2f6). Client 1.60.1.70790, which is still the newest Forever build on wago (`/api/builds`, product wowf, read 5 Nov 04:20 UTC). The lane base `sb-base` was built from the fork int/r21 and this site branch. It equals `$R/before.tsv` to the decimal for all twelve warrior values: the platform's Execute/Eureka! change moves no warrior race we publish.

**Result: 1 confirmed warrior bug, `new-data`, fixed in the engine. Defensive Stance's threat bonus is 80%, not 30% (Blizzard 3 Nov). Protection threat goes up 37.9% at raid 60.** Arms and Fury do not move. No spec outside the class moves. The Protection page now names the 80%, and the wow-player gave it a SHIP.
