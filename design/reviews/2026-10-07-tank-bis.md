# Tank BiS, 2026-10-07

Three tank specs are ranked: `warrior-protection`, `paladin-protection` and
`druid-feral-bear` (new: the Feral tree's Bear Form, beside the cat). Branch
`tanks` in both repositories. Nothing here was pushed or merged.

## The fight

One curated statement, `data/curated/tank-encounter.json`, embedded in
`sim/request` (`make tank-encounter-sync`, proved equal by
`TestTankEncounterCopyMatchesCurated`). Every number carries its reason in
the file. In short:

- The boss is the level 63 target the ranker already fights, tanked by the
  character, one melee swing every 2.0 s rolling 2,500 to 3,325 before armor
  (the engine's own rule: minimum to 133% of it), parry haste on. Crushing
  blows and critical hits come from the engine's attack table for the three
  level gap; none is a number in the file.
- The healers are assumed, not simulated: 440 healing per second in a 2.0 s
  cadence with 0.5 s variation, so a poorly mitigated set dies in a real
  share of fights and a well mitigated one rarely does. The TMI window is 6 s.
- Nothing public states a Forever boss, so both are stand-ins, chosen so the
  comparison between sets is meaningful, not to predict a named boss. I
  calibrated them on the Protection warrior and the bear at level 60
  (first 1,900 / 440 was too gentle: nothing died; 2,700 / 400 killed the
  raid-ready bear a third of the time; 2,500 / 440 is what shipped).
- Bands below 60 scale damage by (level / 60)^2 and healing by
  (level / 60)^1.5 (stated with reasons). The first attempt, one linear factor,
  killed every low-level tank in every fight. Low bands remain the least
  reliable part of the tank data.
- The tank carries the raid preset's buffs when ranked raid-ready and the
  class kit alone when bare. Consumables: `presets.raid.consumes.groups.tank`
  (keyed on the stamina reference stat): Elixir of Greater Defense, Elixir of
  Fortitude, Dirge's Kickin' Chimaerok Chops, Rumsey Rum Black Label, Juju
  Power, Juju Might, plus the class potion (Mighty Rage, Major Mana, the
  bear's Greater Stoneshield). I chose "a group inside the raid preset" over
  a separate `raid_tank` preset because the buffs and debuffs are the same
  raid, and the preset machinery already keys consumables on the reference
  stat. Flask of the Titans and Zanza are left out on purpose: an existing
  test (and the preset notes) keep Ahn'Qiraj-era consumables out.

## The scoring rule

`sim/cmd/leveling-bis/score_tank.go`, selected by `role: "tank"` in
`specs.json`. Every comparison the ranker makes (stat weights, trinket and
effect tournaments, runner-up verification, set completion) runs on one number:

    score = effective health * (threat per second / 100) ^ 0.25 * exp(-0.5 * TMI / 100)
    effective health = max health * (boss raw damage per second) / (damage taken per second)

- Mitigation first. Effective health is a straight multiplier, so a point of
  stamina and a point of armor are priced in one coin, and a 1% change in
  either moves the score 1%.
- TMI is the risk guard: each TMI point costs half a percent. It sees what
  effective health cannot (the same damage taken in spikes is worth less).
- Threat is last and a quarter-power: a 16% threat gain is worth a 4% gain in
  effective health.
- Chance of death is published, not scored: a rare event whose estimate at a
  few thousand iterations is noisier than the margins that decide a pick.

Weights are the derivative of the score by stat: the engine's own sweep gives
the slopes of damage taken, threat and TMI (with the sweep's paired random
streams), and the maximum health slope is read exactly from the engine's stat
computation. The reference stat is stamina (specs.json); its published weight
is exactly 1 and `reference_dps_per_point` carries tank score per stamina
point. The error of a weight sums the three terms' errors linearly (the upper
bound; they are positively correlated). The scale-factor anchor stays the
owner-ruled primary stat (strength for the warrior and paladin, agility for
the bear).

The weights character is dressed in a representative set instead of being
bare (a bare tank's armor and avoidance weights are measured where nobody
stands): per slot, among the items within a few item levels of the best, the
most armored. A shield tank swings a one-hander with a shield; the main-hand
pool drops two-handers for warrior and paladin.

Contract (what the web reads): a tank band entry keeps the DPS shape and adds
`role: "tank"`, `metrics: {dtps, tmi, chance_of_death, tps, effective_health,
dps}`, `score_unit: "tank_score"`; `set_dps` is the tank's own damage; every
`sim_dps`, `dps_delta` and `reference_dps_per_point` on such a band is in tank
score, not damage.

tables carries it, so it never moves a pick. The warrior's Block is not
separable from zero because the item tables fold a shield's block value into
its `block` line (see below).

### Forever threat changes found, and what was assumed

Found in the client tables and talent text:

- Defiance: "an additional 5% [a rank, three ranks] threat in Defensive Stance
  while a shield is equipped" (vanilla was 3% a rank, five ranks). Applied
  additively: 1.30 + 0.05 a rank, which gives Kenco's 1.45 at rank 3.
- Sunder Armor's flat threat: the client's effect 63 states 34, 75, 117, 158,
  206 by rank; the old code had vanilla's figure.
- Bear Form (Passive2) aura 10: +30% threat (the old code used 2.1021, which
  was Wrath's number).
- Righteous Fury (spell 25780): +60% threat on the Holy school only.
- Improved Righteous Fury: damage taken -2% a rank, no longer threat.
- Instrument of Law: -10% a rank threat while Righteous Fury is off.
- Holy Shield: "20% additional threat"; Iron Creed: +25% Holy Strike threat at
  rank 5 (5% a rank).
- Primal Bite and Shield Slam: "high" and "very high" threat in the text, no number.

Assumed (unconfirmed): Shield Slam flat threat 508 (kept); Revenge and
Demoralizing Shout threat formulas (kept, vanilla-shaped); Primal Bite and
Lacerate threat x2.0; Swipe x2.0 (kept; the only druid threat modifier in the
client is Season of Discovery's passive 436895); Demoralizing Roar flat threat
twice the spell level; Holy Shield and Iron Creed stack multiplicatively with
Righteous Fury; Improved Seal of Fury returns 1% of maximum mana (the tooltip
prints 0); Seal of Fury's shield lasts 10 s; Hammer of the Righteous as 120% of
weapon damage on three targets (the client has a flat 1 and dummies); Divine
Protection/Shield on their Classic Era ids; block value per strength 0.05;
Shield Block's two charges (single source, research/06); paw damage and speed
from Era (54.8 dps at 2.5 s); Lacerate stacks to five with a 20% weapon-damage
direct hit (hidden spell 414647); Frenzied Regeneration and Enrage on their Era
conversions; Thick Hide's per-defense armor as a third of 2.0 a rank; the form
passives' armor percentage applies to bonus armor as well; Natural Reaction,
Feral Swiftness and Furor name no form, so dodge is permanent.

Not modelled, each with its reason beside the code: Iron Will, Improved Disarm,
Vanguard, Improved Shield Bash, Concussion Blow (the boss never stuns, fears,
silences or disarms and Concussion Blow carries no damage or threat), Taunt,
Disarm, Shield Bash, Growl, Challenging Roar, Bash, Feral Charge (one stationary
boss), Bloodrage's health cost, Shield Slam's dispel, the bear idols, Consecration
beyond its Classic numbers, Benediction's Forever text.

### Things the lane found that need an owner decision

1. `GetRageConversion` (sim/core/rage.go) writes `attacker_level^2`, which in Go
   is XOR. At level 60 it computes about 198 where the square gives 230.6, so
   rage from damage taken is about 16% high for every rage class. I did not
   fix it: it moves every rage spec's golden.
2. The bear lane corrected four things the cat and balance share (Sharpened
   Claws 3% a rank, Heart of the Wild 2% a rank, Savage Fury 5% a rank on
   Claw/Rake, Thick Hide as form-conditional base armor). The cat ladder moved
   about -1% and balance about -0.7%; those ladder goldens were recommitted
   (`48dc6c3b`). Shred never applies Savage Fury and Rend and Tear does not
   read for the cat: not fixed.
3. The paladin lane's Judgement-no-longer-consumes-the-seal and Holy Strike
   weapon specialization raise Retribution's level 60 ladder DPS from 188 to
   197.6 (golden regenerated); the Retribution rotation notes and the old
   Protection guide sentence about Judgement were stale (the latter rewritten).
4. The item tables fold a shield's block value (mod 48) into the `block` stat
   (mod 15) in `data/pipeline/normalize/gear.py`, so shields' block value is
   either lost or counted as block chance; the ranker cannot weigh block value
   apart. Worth a data-lane look.
5. `data/builds/1.60.1.70009/addon-data.json` and `addon/ForeverSixty/Data.lua`
   now drift from curated specs and stat weights (three `data/tests` failures);
   the nightly regenerates them and I did not commit a local regeneration.
6. Web: `src/lib/home/upgrades.ts` prints `dps_delta` as "+N DPS together";
   on a tank band that is tank score. Not guarded (outside the BiS page) and
   needs a unit-aware label before a tank character reaches the home panel.
   `bulk-store.test.ts` fails five tests once `npm test`'s sync publishes real
   loot (raid ordering by date); unrelated to this work.
