# Premium differentiators — persona reviews, 2026-10-27

Five wow-player personas read the current free/Premium/guild feature list and named what is
missing that would make them pay. Brief: the site as it stands on 2026-10-27 (free: character
export, BiS by sim, planner, browser sims, logs and live logging, reports, rankings, guides,
guild pages; Premium: server sims, retention, history charts, compare builds, Discord pings,
supporter mark).

## The finding

Four of five personas name the same thing in their own words: **coach me on my own log.**
Nobody wants more reference data; they want the site to read their night and say what to do
differently. Today's Premium is "more of the free tools" (bigger sims, longer retention), which
none of them would buy on its own. The fifth (the casual altoholic) wants the account-wide
view the addon export already makes possible.

| Persona | Day-one feature | Would pay |
|---|---|---|
| Parse-chasing DPS | Sim my actual pull: my kill vs a sim of my real kit and buffs, gap split into gear, buffs, execution | $6, $10 bundled with the parse coach |
| Healer | Downrank coach and mana curve on my Onyxia log: which rank, when I went empty, why | $6; $8–10 with healer gear in mana per fight |
| Tank and raid leader | One-screen wipe review: what started the chain, who, the 10 s before | $8–10; a personal Raid Leader tier ~$12 |
| Casual altoholic | "I have an hour tonight" across all alts: bags, banks, professions, cooldowns, on the phone | $6, or $50/yr with a pause |
| PvP player | Rank planner from realm standings (estimated from exporters) | $6 while grinding |

## Ranked themes

1. **Log coaching per role** (DPS parse gap, healer downrank/mana, tank threat ceiling and
   wipe review, PvP death recap). Built on what we already own: the log pipeline and the sim
   engine. The strongest Premium differentiator.
2. **Sim against reality** — sim the real pull (fight length, uptime, buffs), split the gap.
   The engine and the parsed log exist; this joins them.
3. **Account-wide alt view** — bags, banks, gold, cooldowns, professions and recipes across
   alts, searchable on the phone, "as of last login". The addon export already carries most
   of it; quest completion (attunements) would need adding.
4. **Time-aware gearing plans** — pre-raid BiS ordered by DPS gained per hour played, within a
   gold budget; per-boss swap sets pushed to the addon; consumables return on gold.
5. **Raid leader tier** — wipe review, pull-over-pull progression curves, mechanic-failure
   record across weeks, consumable compliance. Split between a personal ~$12 tier and the
   guild plan.
6. **Healer-correct numbers** — healer score on assigned targets with absorbs and dispels,
   healer gear valued in mana per fight. Healers say every parse site ranks them unfairly.
7. **PvP** — rank planner, honour per hour, PvP fight recaps, PvP BiS mode. Rank standings
   are only as good as the number of exporters on a realm; label estimates.

## Features they would not pay for

Supporter mark (all), Discord pings (casual, healer), 2-year retention (casual, DPS — Warcraft
Logs keeps logs), character history charts (DPS), server sims (healer — the engine sims DPS
only; casual — browser sims are free).

## Data and honesty flags

- Rank 15–18 point conversion is unpublished for Forever; projections must say "estimated".
- Realm standings, enemy lookups and hotspot maps depend on exporter density; publishing
  inspect data on players who never installed the addon is a privacy and ToS question.
- No PvP simulator: the engine sims raid encounters, not two players reacting.
- Healer sims need engine work; a healer BiS ranked by the DPS engine is guesswork.
