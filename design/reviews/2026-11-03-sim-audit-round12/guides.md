- **hybrids** (data/curated/apl/druid-feral.json:289 and :308, plus the fork copy): both Claw and Shred notes give a reason the client contradicts. "until 24 rank 1 Shred hits for less than Claw" and "where it hits harder than rank 1 Shred" are both false, since rank 1 Shred is 155% weapon + 22 against Claw's 110% + 27 (5221, 1082). It is a proxy that may land near the right level by luck. The real trigger is Shredding Attacks' rank. Please decide the gate and reword both notes. Solo leveling targets face you, so Shred cannot land at all, and the guide now says so.
