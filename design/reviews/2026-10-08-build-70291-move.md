# Moving the site and the engine to client build 1.60.1.70291 (2026-10-08)

## Why

wago.tools listed 1.60.1.70291 as the newest `wow_classic_beta` build while every
site surface and the engine read 1.60.1.70009, four builds back. The class-spell
diff between the two (design/reviews/2026-10-08-build-70291-diff.md) carried
changes a player would feel: Bloodthirst 45% of attack power, Penance's
coefficient and base cut, Holy Shield 30, Combustion 3 charges, Water Shield's
cooldown gone, Wolfshead 5 energy, Bear Form's threat 50, the low-rank nukes'
per-level growth cut, and the hit and crit enchants turned into flat stats.

## Data: seven fetch attempts

`data.yml`'s fetch job had only ever run on a build directory that already
existed. A never-built directory exposed, in order: icons reading
spellranks.json before levels wrote it (simconst and levels moved before
icons); the curated Anachronos override naming a vendor the fresh build lacked
(the nightly fill-in caches are seeded from the newest numeric build; the
override itself was stale, Wowhead's Forever payload lost the Ahn'Qiraj
war-effort line, 12,060 to 10,678 items, so it was removed); simdb reading
combatratings.txt before gametables wrote it (reordered); and, after the first
green run, 2,388 of 25,518 items missing because the classic-db supplement
was seeded after normalize read it and the hotfix dump (1,002 ids the client
carries as runtime hotfixes only) was not seeded at all. The seed step now
runs before normalize and copies the hotfix dump marked `SEEDED_FROM`, which
`pipeline.hotfix_merge` merges add-only, and a step after the build diff
fails a run that lost more than 2% of the previous build's items. The final
build has 25,606 items (88 new, none lost), 276 changed, 162 new spells, 41
changed sets. `trainables/` is the one output the workflow does not produce;
`pipeline trainables` wrote it by hand.

## Engine (fork f80f8e819, lane build-70291)

spellconst and relic modifiers regenerated from 70291; the conformance
client copies, set rows, game tables and levels payload refreshed (a new
`tools/levels_from_site.py` renders the site's flattened levels.json back
into the payload shape). Goldens regenerated first as the worklist: 35
damage rows and 13 verdict rows differed. All fixed: the Wrath, Lightning
Bolt, Shadow Bolt, Lesser Heal, Heal and Penance ladders (Penance coefficient
0.19, mana 150/220/270/385), Mana Tide at 25, Wolfshead 5, Shifting Power's
1 s GCD and level 1, Improved Shifting Power's curve, Reckoning's 1.5 s
internal cooldown, Expose Prey's 10 s window, Water Shield with no cast
cooldown and the aura row's 3.5 s proc interval, Bear Form's threat aura 50,
the renamed trap spells resolved by rank. Every class now reads 0 mismatches
at 60; the only level-60 result movements are the feral tank's threat (+15%,
the client's 50 against the engine's 30) and elemental's 0.1% from Water
Shield. Already matching and merely re-commented: Bloodthirst 45%, Raging
Blows, Holy Shield 30, Dual Wield Specialization, Combustion 3, Berserker
Rage 30, Gore Drinker and Lingering Rage.

Left open, recorded in the conformance SUMMARY: talents not regenerated on
the fork (text, Impale's prerequisite and the Predatory Instincts rename
only); Natural Instinct's healing from Intellect; Thick Hide ranks 1 and 2;
Furor's bear wording; what replaces Flametongue Weapon's removed effect spell
415138; the Lightwell-style Renew 1289450; the hit and crit enchants (no
engine code reads them); Hawk and pet scaling auras; Lunar and Umbral Fire,
Thorns and Retribution Aura values, Penance's damage half, Enrage's 30%
trigger.

## Site (lane activate-70291)

active-build.json and the workflow gates moved; all 43 guide build codes
re-stamped (every talent keeps its tree, tier and column; only warrior
Impale gained a prerequisite every guide already satisfies; no talent
dropped); test pins moved; a BiS fallback for a build with no ranked files
yet (the newest other build's files are served, each still stamped with its
own build) in the web loader, the sync script, the e2e support and the Go
tools; the ladder goldens regenerated. The first nightly on 70291 replaces
the fallback with ranked files and re-renders the addon data.
