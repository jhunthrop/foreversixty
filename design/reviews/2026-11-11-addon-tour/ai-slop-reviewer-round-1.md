# ai-slop-reviewer, round 1 (boards in this folder)

**First impression.** Mostly a WoW player's frame: the Builds dialog, the panel and the spec descriptions have real content and no pitch. The new tour leads are where it slips; three of the five read like a marketing paragraph on top of a page that already shows the thing.

## Tells found

- Tell 19/18, `tourSiteLead` (onboarding-2): names no noun, a three-item "what you get" list restating the page title. Delete it or give the page one concrete line.
- Tell 21/22, `tourLoopLead` (onboarding-1): the first sentence names the ring and the arrow; the second repeats the diagram below. Keep the first.
- Tell 21, `tourBackLead`: a benefit list duplicating the "Your fights rated" row, and "Once the companion has your fights" re-explains the previous page. One concrete line.
- Tell 7: the grey "Welcome to Forever Sixty" subtitle on every page, then the lead, then a grey sub-line under every item, stacks three deep. Drop the repeated subtitle.
- Tell 22: "Loads the leveling build. You can change it later." (chip card) and "loads its leveling build" beside the question; the companion sentence in the Builds dialog; "Tab to switch" as an orphan hint.
- Tell 7/22, `tourSetupLead`: specific, but "path" is a term the player has not met. Cut it or say "build".
- Tell 17, borderline: "coming" in gold on page 4 beside gold for the job and the ring; the dialog keeps gold to Following. Keep gold's meaning consistent.
- Chip card: three solid bright yellow bars read as three CTA blocks; make them the same recipe as the panel and tour tiles.
- Tell 11, minor: three equal tiles and a 3x2 diagram, both real, not pushed.
