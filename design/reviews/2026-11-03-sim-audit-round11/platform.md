# Sim-accuracy round 11: platform lane

Site branch `wt/r11-platform` from origin/main 8f047a62f. No engine change; the lane go.work points at the shared baseline engine cfcb76e80. Scope: the CI guard for round 10's api breakage, then a fresh audit of every surface that turns sim inputs into a request or shows sim output: /sim and its drawer, the planner's live figure, Top Gear and the weights tool, the API sims path, the coach (Go and `tools/coach`), the Discord bot and the addon's rotation table. Nothing nightly-owned is committed: `addon-data.json`, `Data.lua`, `spellicons.json` and the simbuffs table are untouched, and every local regeneration lived in the lane scratch.

**Found: 10 CONFIRMED-BUG rows, all fixed.** By origin: R9 3 (P1, A1, C2), R10 1 (C4), old 6 (W1, S1, C1, C3, C5, A2). The integrator's follow-ups add a label fix to the auras lane's improved Mana Spring patch (M1, not counted: it never shipped) and a guard for the regression lane's C9 (G2). No row moves any published number: no engine, preset or rotation change (W1 adds a field to the weights result; the numbers are the same). Shared-data effects arrive at the owner's refresh (A1, A2 through `addon-data.json`; C2 through the same file).
