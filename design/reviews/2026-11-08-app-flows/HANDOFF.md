## API ready now
- `GET /v1/home`: `sections` composed per moment (raid live, raid day, the day after, guild, leveling, no guild); `activity` sections carry `limit`, `group` (raid night) and `exclude_kinds` (the day after: read past them to fill the limit).
- `GET /v1/me/activity?group&before&limit` and `POST /v1/me/activity/read {through}`: the Activity tab and its badge (migration 0084; mounted when `APP_NOTIFICATIONS_ENABLED`).
- `/v1/me` characters carry `avatar_url` (the profile button) and `faction`.
- Push: APNs secrets on Cloud Run, FCM admin on api-runtime, Firebase apps `gg.foreversixty.app` and `.debug`. What is left is client-only (entitlement, registration, categories and actions, the Late content extension, routes; `google-services.json`, FCM registration).
- Where to go tonight and Best run: no endpoint; compute from the cached `/data/{build}/bis/{spec}.json` and `loot.json` as `web/src/lib/bis/page-model.ts` does.
