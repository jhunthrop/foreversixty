Engine: r9/healers/engine (sim/druid). Client: r8/healers/raw CSVs (1.60.1.70707 layered with the hotfix push, fetched 2026-11-01). Trait rank values come from TraitDefinitionEffectPoints -> CurvePoint, not from the base points of the spell row. Wowhead Forever tooltips were fetched 2026-11-02.

## Aligned (what I checked)

- **Healing Touch** (all 11 rows, healing_tables.go:28-95 against SpellEffect/SpellPower/SpellMisc/SpellLevels): base, variance, per-level growth, SpellLevel and MaxLevel, mana, and cast index (1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, then 3.5 s). Coefficients are 0.429/0.571/0.714/0.857/1. Attr8 lacks 0x200, which is right for a direct heal. Ranks 10 and 11 (25297) both have SkillLineAbility rows (573, AcquireMethod 0) that are indistinguishable from each other; rank 11 is treated as a book rank, as in earlier rounds. Naturalist takes -100 ms a rank (curve 85364, eff0 mask 0x20 plus WG). Tranquil Spirit takes -2% a rank (curve 85404, misc 14, mask 0x20|0x80).
- **Regrowth** (9 rows): direct base, variance, growth and coefficient 0.286. HoT is 7 ticks at 3 s (DurationIndex 86 = 21 s, period 3000), base per tick, coefficient 0.071 a tick. Attr8 0x200, so the ticks crit; the engine map has 8936-9858. Nature's Splendor: 1223083 eff1 aura 107 misc 1 +6000 ms on mask 0x40, which is 2 ticks.
- **Rejuvenation** (11 rows): 4 ticks at 3 s (12 s), coefficient 0.2 a tick, no growth, mana. Attr8 0x200. Splendor eff0 +3000 ms on mask 0x12, which is 1 tick. Improved Rejuvenation is 5/10/15 (curve 85403, DOT op, mask 0x10).
- **Tranquility** (4 rows): area aura 35/8 to the party, period 2000, 10 s (5 ticks), base and growth, coefficient 0.067. CategoryRecovery 300 s; Improved Tranquility -30/-60% (curve 85408, misc 11), so 120 s. Mana 375/505/695/925. Attr1 0x40 (channelled). Attr8 0x200, so the ticks crit; the engine map has all 4. Genesis's mask leaves out 0x80, and the engine leaves it out too.
- **Swiftmend**: PowerCostPct 20, RecoveryTime 15 s, GCD 1.5 s, instant, learn level 1 (talent). It can crit (no ATTR2 CANT_CRIT). It eats whichever of Rejuvenation or Regrowth has the least time left (not stated by the client).
- **Innervate**: 29166, PowerCostPct 5, RecoveryTime 360 s, DurationIndex 18 (20 s). Aura 110 at 400% (engine SpiritRegenMultiplier +4) and aura 134 at 100% (ForceFullSpiritRegen). SpellLevel 40 and trainable at 40; the engine gates it at InnervateLevel (innervate.go:11). Not in Clearcasting's mask, and the engine has no SpellFlagOmen on it.
- **Nature's Swiftness**: RecoveryTime 180 s, no GCD, DurationIndex 21 (until used), ProcCharges 1. Cast time -100%. Its mask includes Healing Touch and Regrowth (Wild Growth: see D1).
- **Omen of Clarity / Clearcasting**: 16864 trainable at 20 (Balance skill line, passive). ProcCategoryRecovery 10 s, ProcTypeMask 0x14014 (positive spells included). The 4% per spell rate is fitted from logs, not stated by the client (as before). 16870 lasts 15 s with 1 charge. Its cost mask covers HT, Regrowth, Rejuvenation, Tranquility, Swiftmend and WG, and not Wrath. HoT-only casts roll on cast complete.
- **Bloom**: 1328621 effects 100 and 50 (aura 4). The heal is 1328622: no class mask (so no GoN, matching the engine's empty ClassSpellMask), Attr3 0x10000 (SUPPRESS_CASTER_PROCS, matching the engine's empty proc mask), and no CANT_CRIT. It procs only when the HoT runs its full duration.
