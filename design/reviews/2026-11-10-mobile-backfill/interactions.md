- **Reminder ask cadence.** At most twice, 30 days apart, after a first In.
- **Live notification.** Expanded pills answer in place, with In/Late/Bench/Out in the custom view.
- **Sign-out location.** Sign out is in Settings (You) only, not in the drawer.
- **Character switcher.** Lives in the drawer only. There is no switcher sheet and no switcher on the bar.

### iOS suspected gaps (detail)

1. **`/app/character/...` is a dead link on iOS.** `DeepLink.character` parses, but no branch of `RootView.openLink` handles it: the chain falls through to `openRaids`, and `RaidsRouter.handles` returns false. links.json says it should open Home on that character. The Next upgrade widget's tap uses exactly this link, so it does nothing. Refs: apps/ios/App/Shell/ShellRouting.swift:8-47,88-92; apps/ios/Widgets/Extension/ForeverSixtyWidgets.swift:144-150.
2. **`view=unanswered` is dropped.** `RaidsRouter.open` destructures `view` as `_`, so `/app/raid/{g}/{r}?view=unanswered` opens the plain raid page instead of Not answered. The push path avoids this by routing `raid.short` directly. Ref: apps/ios/App/Raids/RaidsTab.swift:95-97.
3. **Silent sign-up failures on Home and the Raid tab head.** `HomeFlowModel.answerError` and `RaidAnswerModel.error` are set but never drawn. Composer and view only read `pending` and `lateOpen`, so a refused answer just snaps back with no reason. (The raid-night page does draw its error.) Refs: apps/ios/App/Home/Flow/HomeFlowModel.swift:92,209; apps/ios/App/Raids/Flow/RaidAnswerModel.swift:14,46; apps/ios/App/Raids/RaidsTab.swift:181-190.
4. **No haptics on three of the four sign-up surfaces (R14).** Only SignupController (the raid page) plays selection, success and error. RaidAnswerModel (Raid tab head, raid night) and HomeFlowModel (Home card) play nothing. R14 also wants haptics on pull to refresh and on sign-in done; neither exists, and Haptics.swift:4-7 explicitly says "Pull to refresh … play nothing". The spec asks for a light impact; iOS uses `.selection` and `.success` instead. Character pick has no haptic despite the Haptics doc listing it. Refs: apps/ios/App/Common/Haptics.swift:4-8; grep for `haptic` shows no call in Home/Flow, Raids/Flow or Shell.
5. **Offline does not disable Home, Raid-tab and raid-night answers (R16).** RaidAnswerButtons has no `.disabled` and the answer models do not check connectivity. Only the raid page's SegmentsView disables with the offline line. Refs: apps/ios/App/Raids/Views/NextRaidBlock.swift:151-171; apps/ios/App/Raids/Logic/SignupController.swift:108-113.
6. **The spec ask is bypassed outside the raid page.** Home, the Raid tab head and the raid-night answers always send `spec: nil`. A multi-spec character with no raid spec answering In there never sees "Which spec tonight?". The raid page, the push (`askSpec`) and the intents all ask. Refs: apps/ios/App/Home/Flow/HomeFlowModel.swift:202; apps/ios/App/Raids/Flow/RaidAnswerModel.swift:38.
7. **Late minutes are missing on the raid page.** SignupController's request carries no `lateMinutes` and SegmentsView has no chips. Home, the Raid tab, the raid night page and the push all offer 15/30/60. Ref: apps/ios/App/Raids/Logic/SignupController.swift:169.
8. **Sign-in failure is invisible outside Home.** The drawer, Raid tab and Activity sign-in buttons call `session.signIn`, but the failure notice is only rendered by SignedOutPanel on Home. The drawer also stays open during and after sign-in. Refs: apps/ios/App/Shell/MenuScreens.swift:24; apps/ios/App/Raids/RaidsTab.swift:243; apps/ios/App/Activity/ActivityScreen.swift:33.
9. **The drawer's Refresh gives no feedback.** There is no spinner and no error; it only re-reads `/v1/me` and does not trigger a Battle.net re-import (R15's stamp). Ref: apps/ios/App/Shell/MenuScreens.swift:22.
10. **Drawer Account rows lack their second lines.** Notifications, Devices and Offline data show empty lines because `menuFacts` passes nil for them. Ref: apps/ios/App/Shell/MenuScreens.swift:33-41.
11. **No OS-denied notification state.** The `notif.denied.*` copy is generated but never used. NotificationSettingsScreen shows the switches as on even when the system permission is denied, and nothing links to Settings. Refs: apps/ios/App/Generated/Copy.swift:2053-2059 (unused); apps/ios/App/Account/NotificationSettings.swift:213-260.
12. **Activity has no offline handling and some silent failures.** There is no offline strip or stamp. A Mark all read failure rolls back silently, and loadMore swallows errors. A row tap that maps to no destination is a dead tap and does not mark the row read. Refs: apps/ios/App/Activity/ActivityModel.swift:154-173; apps/ios/App/Activity/ActivityScreen.swift:42-45.
13. **The Activity badge may be stale until the tab is first visited.** It is only fed by ActivityScreen's `.task`, while the Raid badge is loaded at root. Ref: apps/ios/App/RootView.swift:116-119,195.
14. **`/app/guild/{g}` ignores the guild id.** It just clears the Raid stack, so a link for a guild other than the current character's shows the wrong guild. Ref: apps/ios/App/Raids/RaidsTab.swift:102-103.
15. **Not answered's "Edit on the site" is a tap gesture on Text** with an `.isLink` trait, not a Button. It has no pressed state, and the 44 pt hit area is not guaranteed. Ref: apps/ios/App/Raids/Flow/NotAnsweredView.swift:40-46.
16. **Dead code and minor items.** HomeNoCharacterView (apps/ios/App/Home/Flow/HomeFlowView.swift:270-283) is unused. A paperdoll slot without a pick is a silent no-op tap (apps/ios/App/Bis/Gear/GearListView.swift:32). The Live Activity falls back to `?? 0` guild/raid ids, which builds a dead link (apps/ios/Widgets/Extension/ForeverSixtyWidgets.swift:159). There is no loot-interest control at all.

**Cross-platform comparison notes (iOS behaviour per flow):**
- Raid page sign-up: optimistic; selection haptic on press, success or error on result; disabled offline with `raid.offline`; refusal restores the answer with the API's sentence; spec sheet for 2+ specs; `Saved.` for 4 s; posts `raidSignupSaved`, which refreshes the badge, widgets and Live Activity; reminder ask after In or Late.
- Home and Raid-tab sign-up: optimistic via `pending`/`inputs.answer`; no haptic; not offline-aware; silent rollback; Late opens 15/30/60 inline; posts `raidSignupSaved`.
- Push answers: background PUT with a confirmation posted in place; Late chips in the content extension; spec needed opens the app at `?spec=ask`.
- Notification permission is only ever asked in context (first-run guild main, the raid-page reminder ask). PushRegistrar never prompts.
- Pull to refresh exists on Home, Raid root, raid page, raid night, Not answered, Gear, Activity and the no-characters view, with no haptic on any of them.
