# Player verdict: mobile follow-ups (2026-10-31)

Verdict: SHIP. No blockers. Three optional shoulds.

## 1. Band mismatch: fixed
- Home (iOS and Android), level-58 Fury warrior: "Best in slot for 50 to 59, together" and the card header "50 to 59". BiS opens on "50 to 59". Same band, same wording. The talents row also says "the 50 to 59 build", so Home no longer shows "60 · raid" to a level-58 character.
- Level-31 hunter (Yellfury): Home says "30 to 39". That is the right band.
- The upgrade rows are the fixture's hunter list on a warrior (Beaststalker's, Hyper Deluxe Sniper Rifle). I judged only the band choice and labels, as asked.

## 2. Selector truncation: fixed
- "Yellfury / Hunter · 31" is in full on iOS at the default size and at the largest accessibility text (raids and home). Android at the largest text is also in full.
- The pill grows a little at ax5 and is not clipped. "Hunt..." is gone.
- Yellfury shows "Hunter" with no spec. That is fine at 31 with no spec chosen. Obnoxious Yell shows "Fury · 58".

## 3. Buff tiles: fixed
- Every tile has a name under its icon. Fort and SPro, which were told apart by art alone, now read.
- Layout: iOS wraps 7 + 3 and Android wraps 8 + 2, with nothing clipped.
- "Bless" is dimmed, which matches "Paladin 0" and "Missing: Blessings".
- I cannot check the screen-reader full names from screenshots. That is outside what I saw.

## Optional shoulds
1. "MS" for Mana Spring Totem. In a raid with five warriors on the roster, a player reads "MS" as Mortal Strike or main spec. Use "MSpring" or "Mana".
2. The MotW and Bless placeholders are empty squares, and only Bless is dimmed. MotW has 3 druids, so it is present, but a bare square beside a dimmed one looks like a broken image or a missing buff. Show the same placeholder for both, with a letter or a generic buff glyph inside, until the art is published.
3. Tile "Bless" against the footer "Missing: Blessings". Make the tile name match, or add a tooltip or long-press that gives the full name. Do the same for the other terse labels ("Int", "SS", "Rez"). Players know MotW, SoE and WF from raid chat, but "Rez" does not say whether it is a Rebirth or a Soulstone.
