# Guild tab (iOS + Android), guild-officer review, 2026-11-11

Job of the page: the away-from-PC guild door. An officer clears what needs them (unanswered signups, applicants, setup) and glances at comp. A raider answers In/Late/Bench/Out, sees their job and prep, and sees where the guild stands.

Judged on: ios-full-guild-tab-officer.png, ios-full-guild-tab.png, ios/dark/leveler-01-top.png, GuildTabPresenter.swift, and the fields the API already serves (api/internal/guilds/home.go member view).

What works: signup is one tap with the state kept; Needs you (5 unanswered, 2 healers, healers short by 3) is the right first officer glance; Who is coming is comp against target with a Missing buffs line and an Out/Bench count; Your job and Before you pull answer the member's raid-night questions; Applications and Setup are one tap away; the under-60 "Raids open at 60 · you're 58" line is quiet and never blocks.

## Findings

1. SHOULD. The Roster section is only "Recently on" (5 names with seen-at). Where: GuildTabPresenter.recent(), GuildTabViews roster block. Cost: the officer's roster question is who is raid-ready and who is slipping, and this answers "who logged in". Fix: the member view already serves item_level, gear_gap, attendance and attendance_30 (present/nights), best_parse, rating (overall/output/survival/mechanics/preparation), last_report_at and addon seen_at/snapshot_at. Add one row "Slipping" (lowest attendance_30 with 3+ nights, or no addon sync this week, which the tab already shows as "Not synced this week"), and one row "Gear gap" (largest gear_gap). Ordering by attendance_30 is a client sort, so no new endpoint.
2. SHOULD. Loot is a single line, "Last raid: 4 pieces", with no names, no recipients, no tap target beyond the header link. Where: content.loot (badge.lastRaidLoot), Loot section. Cost: an officer cannot answer "who got what" or fairness at all; a raider cannot answer "what did I win". Fix: show the pieces and winners from the data behind the badge (loot award export/award_record.go, export_loot.go) as item icon + name + recipient rows, and flag "you won" for the viewer. Awards-per-raider fairness: the API has the awards; confirm the app can read them (needs a read-only endpoint if only exports serve them today, label needs data).
3. SHOULD. Attendance is invisible apart from the signup counts. Cost: "who's at risk" is the second officer job and attendance_30 is already in the roster payload. Fix: add an "At risk" row (members under a threshold, names + present/nights) under Who is coming, officers only.
4. SHOULD. Officer rows do not do the work: Plan a raid, Setup and Assignments all hand off to the browser, and "Preview mention" in Needs you is the only Discord action. Cost: the common actions (bench, assign, accept, post) are not in-app except applicant sheet; the app is a viewer for assignments ("Assigning is on the site for now"). This is acceptable per the app-priority ruling, but then the tab has to carry the read side fully. Fix: make sure Open in planner lands on the planner for that raid (it does: {guild}/raids/{raid}) and keep Preview mention state visible after posting (needs checking on device; the fixture could not exercise it).
5. SHOULD. The officer tab is long (about 6.4 screens at 390 pt in the officer render); the Officer section with Plan, Applications and Setup sits below Needs you and Your job, and Assignments follows it. For an officer who opens the app to act, Needs you and Officer should read together, and Your job (a member item) should not push them down. Fix: order officer sections directly under the raid head; Your job stays under the signup card only for non-officers or when the officer has a pick, collapsed to one line.
6. NICE. Recent logs show kills/fights/duration but no per-raider performance (deaths, parse vs spec median, who to talk to). home.go already returns best_parse (metric, value, percentile, encounter) and rating; a "Lowest preparation" or "Best parse this week" line would give the officer someone to talk to. Deaths and avoidable damage per raider: needs data in the tab (not in the member view).
7. NICE. Progression is one line (1 of 1 down, 6 pulls). Fine for now; the web guild page's per-boss detail is one tap away.
8. NICE. Below 60 the tab shows spread (30 at 60, 5 at 55 to 59, you 58) and Close to 60 rows. Good level-aware design; Loot is correctly dropped for levelers.
9. NICE (member). "What's my next upgrade and where does it drop" is not on this tab at all, and "my attendance" is not shown to the member (attendance_30 is in their own row). One line under Your job, "Your attendance 7 of 8 · 30 days", costs one row.

Not covered by captures (fixture limits): at-60 and no-guild-at-60 are parity renders only; Android light is the dark theme by design.

## Value test
Would a 40-man guild pay $15 to $25 a month for the tab alone? As a phone companion with signup, comp, prep and applicant action, it is a strong member surface and a decent officer inbox. Replacing the spreadsheet for officers needs the roster to answer ready/slipping and loot to answer who got what. Both are served by data the roster payload and loot exports already carry; the tab uses neither beyond a last-seen time and a piece count. The single addition that flips it: finding 1+3 (attendance and gear-gap "slipping" rows) together with finding 2 (loot recipients).

VERDICT: NOT YET
