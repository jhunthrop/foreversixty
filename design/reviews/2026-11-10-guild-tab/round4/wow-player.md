## Verdict: SHIP

The renders are now whole pages. I read every section on the at-60 and officer renders on iOS, the Android officer render from the raid head to Pages, and the top of the iOS leveler render. Nothing below blocks; the two "fix before launch" items should not wait for the next guild pass.

### Player question and first screen
"What do I do tonight, and how is my guild doing?" At 60 the Onyxia card with In / Late / Bench / Out and "As Thandrel, Damage" is first, then Your job and Who is coming. Right order for a raider.

### What a Classic player will find wrong, most damaging first

1. **Fix before launch: Gear gaps shows "41 DPS" with no direction and no unit.** Selka is a Restoration Druid; a healer's gap is not in DPS. Read cold, "41 DPS" looks like her own output. Healers should show healing, or the section should be DPS specs only.
2. **Fix before launch: the leveler render contradicts itself.** At 36 it says "Raids open at 60 · you're 36", yet In is lit and "As Felsnap, Damage" shows. A player who cannot enter should not see a live sign-up.
3. **Loot data does not match the raid night.** "Wed 30 Dec" is the Onyxia night, but Eskhandar's Collar (Winterspring rare) and Ancient Cornerstone Grimoire (Dire Maul) do not drop there; a Fury Warrior winning a caster book would be mocked; Head of Onyxia is a quest item. Use Ashkandi, Deathbone, Eye of Onyxia.
4. **Your award in gold is unlabelled.** "Thandrel" in gold is the only cue it is yours; use "You" or "· you".
5. **"item level 58" wraps** in Missing raids, leaving "level 58" alone on a line. Classic players write "ilvl".
6. **Android loses class colour on Who is coming names** (iOS has Aenwyn blue, Brightwen pink, Thalrin green; Android all tan). Parity break.
7. **Smaller:** Thalrin appears in three lists (acceptable density); "Missing Fort · WF 1 of 2" does not say what 1 of 2 counts; "Roster 31 of 38 at 60" repeats the guild card's "31 at 60".

### Parts that work
Recently on (crests, class colours, "just now / 12 min ago"); Missing raids and Your attendance ("7 of 8 nights"); Recent logs (name, place, "7/8 kills · 1 h 32 min", date, chevrons); Progression ("1 of 1 down · 6 pulls"); Assignments and Your job ("Whelps, west cave with Kaelra and Thalrin", the best line on the page); Before you pull; Who is coming under 60 with counts alone.

### Missing against the references
No marker on the officer's own awards beyond the one gold name; no rank on roster rows; an attunement line for levelers (routed to needs data, noted).

### Data I do not believe
The loot (item 3); gaps in DPS for a healer (item 1). Item levels 66 / 63 / 61 at 60 are plausible pre-raid.

### Not judged
The none and none-leveler renders and the iOS device walks; type size judged from the 2x renders only.
