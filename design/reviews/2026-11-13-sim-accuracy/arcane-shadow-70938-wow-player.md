Reviewer: the wow-player persona (`.claude/agents/wow-player.md`). Scope: the "Mana in a 3 minute fight" paragraphs of the three mage guides after the gem-order fix (M1), and the Shadow guide's Shadowform and Mental Agility lines after S1.

- **Arcane: SHIP.** Ruby, potion, then rune or Citrine is right, and "a smaller gem spent first wastes one of the two" is true to the shared cooldown. The pre-pull "Conjure a Mana Ruby and a Mana Citrine" stays right: the Citrine is the fallback when you have no rune.

Line 54's "half the mana on every Shadow spell" was flagged for a separate check: it is the 70938 client's Shadowform text ("reducing the Mana cost of all Shadow spells by 50%"), so it stands.
