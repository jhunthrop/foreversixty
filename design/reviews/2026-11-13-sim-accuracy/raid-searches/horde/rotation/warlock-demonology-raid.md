# Rotation search: Demonology, level 60

Generated by `sim/cmd/rotation-search` against client 1.60.1.70790. Gear and talents: the committed level-60 horde raid-preset BiS band's gear and the guide's own FS1 build, both held fixed - only the rotation varies. Seed 7 (the same for every sim). Run time 33s.

Baseline (the curated rotation): **800.8 ± 1.5 DPS** (800 iterations).

**Caveat:** this sims one solo character with no raid around it. A group utility totem, aura, or buff (Windfury Totem, Strength of Earth) can measure as a pure DPS loss here purely because this character is the only one who would benefit from the GCD spent refreshing it - the real payoff is the melee group standing in it, which a solo sim cannot see. Treat any finding that drops a group buff/totem line as a hint to verify by hand, not a rotation change to paste blind.

## Dangling gates in the winning rotation

These lines gate on a dot or aura that nothing else in the winning rotation ever applies, so they are legal but dead (never fire). The search's own measurement already reflects this - a dead line costs nothing - but a curation lane should decide by hand whether to drop the line or restore whatever used to apply its gate.
