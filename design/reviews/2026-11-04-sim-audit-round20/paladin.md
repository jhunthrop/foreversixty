# Sim-accuracy round 20: paladin

Lane paladin. Site wt/r20-paladin from int/r20 (04d7da663), fork lane/r20-paladin from int/r20 (da54b7b06). Client 1.60.1.70755 with the late hotfix re-layer. Every sim, sweep and sim-running test went through `$R/sim.sh`. Measured on the frozen BiS (`$R/bis-frozen`) and the guide builds (`$R/rt`); leveling bands on the band's own published build and the bare preset; Holy with `-heal`.

**Result:** 3 CONFIRMED-BUGs, all decided on the client and web research as the brief asked, all fixed end to end: the Seal of Righteousness spell damage share (S1, which also settles the rank 7 vs 8 question: rank 8), and Oath of Light's amount and level rule (O1), which rewrote the Holy rank rows at 43-60 (H1). Nothing else moved: Protection is aligned spell by spell, Retribution's other rows hold on the new Seal. Carried seeds closed: Oath of Light (decided), rank 7 vs 8 (decided), the one-hand/two-hand split (decided), the Judgement dummy coefficients (explained), the Holy Light 0%-bonus rows (gone).

Live logs: api.foreversixty.gg/v1/reports/recent still had only the 2026-10-20 report at 20:44 UTC; foreverlogs reports 2812-2814 (the last ids before launch) have no encounters; nothing at 40+ exists yet. Launch is 23:00 UTC. Warcraft Logs is still blocked (gcloud).

## Seed: Oath of Light (decided)

| # | finding | evidence and source | confirmed? | origin | fix sha |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| O1 | **Oath of Light adds its whole amount (400 / 115 / 230 at 60), not the amount times the heal's coefficient, and a rank learned far below the caster keeps only the share its healing power keeps.** The engine added `coefficient x amount` (0.714 x 400 = 286 on Holy Light) and never penalised it. The Oath is Blessing of Light rebuilt (dummy auras applied by server script, the same "by up to" text, Blizzard's note "the amount of bonus healing increases with the player's level"), so how Blizzard's server treats that script decides it. Two independent Classic sources on that server code: (1) a Classic Era bug report of 13 September 2019 with measurements, "Flash of Light appears to receive the +60 amount, rank independent" (the whole amount, not 0.429 x 60) and "it's only +60 for Holy Light (Rank 1)": 210 x 0.2875, the level-1 rank's sub-20 share, exactly, with a reply that the spell power penalty "applies to the blessing of light as well and always has"; (2) warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Blessing_of_Light: "in practice it actually adds the full amount", and patch 2.3.0 "Lower ranks of Flash of Light and Holy Light are now properly penalized when used with this Blessing". Forever has no sub-20 cut; its rule is MaxLevel + 12 then 5% a level (core.SpellLevelPenalty), which is what the Oath term now follows, like the heal's own coefficient. No Forever log with the Oath exists (the one post-Oath beta Holy paladin in the cache, report 2811, is from 23 October, before the 30 October push; 2812-2814 are empty), and no Forever web source mentions the Oath and ranks (searches 2026-11-04: r/wowforever, Blizzard forums, talentsforever, wowforevertalents, fextralife, icy-veins db, mobalytics).
