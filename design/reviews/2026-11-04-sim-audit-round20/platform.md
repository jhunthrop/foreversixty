# Sim-accuracy round 20: platform lane

Site branch `wt/r20-platform` (from int/r20 04d7da663). Fork branch `lane/r20-platform` (from int/r20 da54b7b06). The lane owns sim/core, racials, the web, the addon and the sim tooling. Client 1.60.1.70755 with the late re-layer.

Method: the seed items in the brief's order, then the shared plumbing the seeds touch. Every sim went through `$R/sim.sh` (frozen BiS, guide raid builds, 10,000 iterations at seeds 1 and 50001 for every before/after; 3,000 to explore). Every CONFIRMED-BUG has a test that was red before its fix.

**Confirmed and fixed: 4** (all `old`), plus one research finding (P9) that closes the low-rank proc curve item as aligned. One engine change moves numbers: Skyborne base stats (no published pick is Skyborne, so no published figure moves; a Skyborne sim moves under 0.2%). The Blizzard-only proc penalty (P9) moves nothing: no rotation casts Blizzard.
