# wow-player review: Tanks tab (/tiers/tank) and proposed fix

Verdict: SHIP with changes.

Question the tab answers: which tank takes the least punishment against a level 63 boss, and what do I give up for it. Damage taken is the sort and is the first column, which is right. Everything after it is where the page goes wrong.

## (a) Do the numbers read right
- Warrior first on damage taken: yes. Shield Block up 83% with 2 charges crowds crushes out (1.7%), plus Defensive Stance and Thunder Clap. Every Classic tank would expect it.
- Bear and paladin tied: believable. 351 vs 352 is a tie, not a rank. Both land at +53% after rounding, so say so (tie marker or same rank number). Bear: 15% crushes and no block eat the 16k armor. Paladin: Holy Shield is a partial block, so 11% crushes is right.
- Bear 9,171 vs warrior 7,484 health reads right with Dire Bear Form.
- Threat, bear 2,027 over warrior 1,677: players will not believe it (Classic lore: warrior out-threats bear with Sunder and Revenge). Accuracy reviewer: confirm the warrior rotation includes Sunder Armor and Revenge, and that bear rage from the extra damage taken is not the whole lead.
- Confirm no Blessing of Salvation on any tank and that Righteous Fury is on the paladin. Alliance raid buffs normally carry Salvation.
- Thunder Clap on a boss: 84 swings vs 98 is about 14% fewer. Check the slow rank and uptime against the real Classic values.

## (b) The proposal
- Real max health: correct and a MUST. 38,034 "Health" next to a 9,171 character panel is a screenshot-and-mock number. Bear best and green is right.
- Bars proportional, no floor: correct. Add a label or tooltip for what the bar is ("Damage taken, longer is better, warrior = 100%").
- "+53% taken" with the tooltip: clear. Keep the unit in the cell. Tooltip wording is fine.
- Leader cell: "best" is wrong here, because the bear is best in Health and Threat and the warrior reads "best" under a column that is not about either. Use "lowest" (or "least taken"). Do not use a dash.
- Column header: "Taken / s" has no noun. Use "Damage taken / s". "vs #1" should read "vs #1 taken".
- The rail note must define Taken / s, Health (max health, raid buffs), Threat / s, and the bar. Right now the 1440 capture shows no note at all, only "Sign in to mark your spec".
- Top copy: "Best in slot, raid buffs · level 63" reads as a level 63 character. Say "vs a level 63 boss".

## (c) Anything else
- Missing the numbers tank players check first: Armor (8,700 / about 16k), avoidance (39% / 33%), crushes (1.7% / 15% / 11%). They explain the order and are already in the sim. Add at least avoidance and crush chance.
- Threat leader (bear) green while the damage leader is warrior: fine, but only if the note says the list sorts on damage taken.
- Empty page below three rows. No alternatives, no Horde-specific explanation (Windfury, Strength of Earth vs Alliance blessings).
- Health column must not use effective health anywhere else on the site without that name.

## MUST
1. Health = real max health.
2. Leader cell "lowest" instead of "best"; header "Damage taken / s".
3. Rail note defines each column and the bar; header says "vs a level 63 boss".
4. Treat 351 vs 352 as a tie.
5. Accuracy check on bear threat over warrior, Salvation, Sunder.

## SHOULD
Add avoidance and crush % columns; bar tooltip; cell wording "+53% taken".
