## Description

## Your Forever Sixty character, in game and on the site

Forever Sixty is the companion addon for **[foreversixty.gg](https://foreversixty.gg)**, the planner, simulator and combat log site built for World of Warcraft: Forever. It carries your character to the site in one click, and brings the plan you made there back into the game.

No configuration. No network calls. Nothing runs in combat.

### Follow a build as you level

Plan a build on the site, copy its code, and load it in the addon. From then on the addon shows you the **next talent to take**, marks it in your talent window, and keeps a small tracker on screen so you never open the site to check. A short note on level-up names the talent to spend the point on.

### Know what to equip

The addon scores every piece in your bags against what you wear, using **the same stat weights the site's simulator uses** for your spec. Hover any item and the tooltip says whether your build plans it and whether it beats your current piece. The Gear page shows the plan beside what you have on, slot by slot, with an Equip button for anything better.

### Send your character to the site

One button copies a code that holds your talents, gear, bags and professions. Paste it into the planner or the simulator and your character is there, exactly as it stands. The export saves on logout, so the desktop companion can keep your character current on the site without you doing anything.

### Built to feel like part of the game

- A single window with an overview, talents, gear, export and settings, in the site's own dark and gold.
- A minimap button, a keybind, and `/fs` for the command-line inclined.
- Every feature can be turned off in Settings.

### What it does not do

It never talks to the internet, never reads your account, and never acts in combat. It reads what the game client already shows you, and nothing else.

### Get more from it

The addon is free. The site's planner, simulator and combat logs are free too, with no ads, ever. Guild leaders can claim their guild on the site for a roster, shared reports and officer tools.
