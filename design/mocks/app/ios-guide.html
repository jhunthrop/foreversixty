Overview

Protection is Forever's tanking spec, using a one-handed weapon and shield to hold enemy attention and survive heavy hits.

Strengths

  • Shield Slam is its highest-threat button and grows with Block Value
  • Sunder Armor stacks add threat on every spare global
  • Defensive talents keep mitigation and Rage generation high
  • Revenge comes up more often the better its avoidance

Weaknesses

  • Gives up the damage of the two-handed and dual-wield specs
  • Needs healers to outlast sustained damage
  • Threat leans on Sunder Armor filling every other global

In a raid

  • Brings: Sunder Armor on the target, Thunder Clap's attack-speed slow and Demoralizing Shout

Talents

Shield Specialization 5 ranks
Increases your chance to Block attacks with your shield by 5% and grants you a 100% chance to generate 5 Rage when you Block.

More Block chance, and every Block pays Rage. A block is no longer only mitigation: it feeds the Rage that Shield Slam, Revenge and Heroic Strike spend

Anticipation 5 ranks
Increases your Defense Skill by 20.

Defense Skill, which raises avoidance and lowers the chance to be critically hit

Improved Revenge 3 ranks
Increases damage dealt by your Revenge ability by 60%.

Cheaper or harder-hitting versions of the three abilities that make most of a tank's threat

Improved Thunder Clap 3 ranks
Reduces the Rage cost of your Thunder Clap ability by 6.

Cheaper or harder-hitting versions of the three abilities that make most of a tank's threat

Improved Sunder Armor 3 ranks
Reduces the Rage cost of your Sunder Armor ability by 3.

Cheaper or harder-hitting versions of the three abilities that make most of a tank's threat

Last Stand 1 rank
When activated, this ability temporarily grants you 30% of your maximum health for 20 sec. After the effect expires, the health is lost.

A short burst of health, then it is lost again. An emergency button, not a damage-taken talent

Master of Defense 2 ranks
Grants you a 100% chance to generate 5 Rage when you Dodge or Parry while a shield is equipped.

Rage whenever you Dodge or Parry with a shield equipped, the avoidance counterpart of Shield Specialization

Defiance 3 ranks
Increases all threat generated in Defensive stance by an additional 15% while a shield is equipped.

More threat in Defensive Stance while a shield is equipped, added on top of the stance's own threat bonus

Focused Rage 3 ranks
Reduces the Rage cost of your offensive abilities by 3.

Cheaper offensive abilities, so the Rage the talents above produce goes further

Bastion 5 ranks
Increases all damage you deal by 10% while a shield is equipped.

10% more damage from everything you do while a shield is equipped, and so 10% more threat from your damage (the flat threat on Shield Slam, Sunder Armor and Heroic Strike does not scale with it)

Improved Bloodrage 2 ranks
Increases all the Rage generated by your Bloodrage ability by 50%.

Half again as much Rage from Bloodrage

Concussion Blow 1 rank
Stuns the target for 5 sec.

One point, because Shield Slam requires it

Shield Slam 1 rank
Slam the target with your shield, causing 421 to 439 damage, increased by your Block Value, and has a 50% chance of dispelling 1 magic effect on the target. Causes a very high amount of threat.

The capstone: a shield hit that adds your Block Value, with very high threat
