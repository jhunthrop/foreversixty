Artifacts for Forever Sixty

Hand-built pieces drawn in the spirit of Blizzard's WoW: Forever page: warm leather and stone grounds, night-blue skies, bone-white titles, teal actions, scrollwork frames around real screenshots. Every piece here is CSS or SVG I wrote; nothing is generated art, nothing is copied from Blizzard's files. Icons are the game's own, served from our site.

What I took from their page: Friz Quadrata titles in mixed case with a soft shadow (we use Marcellus, open licence), Open Sans body, teal buttons rather than gold, warm umber texture behind content, ornate bone frames only around imagery, and titles that sit in the layout instead of shouting.

Rules these follow

  • Do: warm umber and night blue grounds, bone titles in mixed case, teal for the single action, frames only around real screenshots, the client's own tooltip.
  • Don't: Blizzard logos or key art, painted or generated illustrations, glowing gold, gradient cards, big hero headings, uppercase eyebrows everywhere.
