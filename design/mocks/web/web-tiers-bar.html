How to read this

How we check the sim ›

What the number is

Damage per second from our own sim against one target for 180 seconds. Every spec is simmed in its best Raid-ready, Phase 1 gear, with enchants a fresh level 60 can buy, raid buffs and consumables, as its best Alliance race, named under it.

What it leaves out

Cleave, add fights, movement and what a spec brings the raid are not counted, so a spec can sit lower here than it plays in your raid. At a fresh 60 the order differs: see the leveling bands ›

Letters and ties

S is within 5% of the top, A within 10%, B within 20%, C within 30%, D beyond. ≈ tie marks a spec within 1% of the one above; a tie across a line is a tie.
