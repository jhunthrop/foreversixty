      Priest

      Updated 4 Nov

      Two healing specs and one shadow caster, as always. Shadow is also how a priest levels. What changed is how much every priest now carries.

      Discipline
      Discipline heals the tank with Penance on cooldown, a shield to open Renewed Hope, and crits that leave Divine Aegis shields behind.
      Read the guide ›
      Holy
      Holy is the priest who heals everything: Prayer of Mending and Renew on the tank, Greater Heal as the filler, and Prayer of Healing for a hurt group.
      Read the guide ›
      Shadow
      Shadow keeps two DoTs ticking, hits Mind Blast and Shadow Word: Death on cooldown, and fills with Mind Flay, all from Shadowform.
      Read the guide ›

      What changed from 1.12

      • Every priest now has Fear Ward, Devouring Plague, Shadow Word: Death, Divine Spirit and Lightwell from the trainer.
      • Penance and Prayer of Mending are the deep Discipline and Holy talents, and the trainer teaches their higher ranks once you have them.
      • Shadowfiend, the Season of Discovery rune, is not in Forever.
      • Low-rank heals. In Forever a rank learned far below your level gets less of your healing bonus, and the Spell Healing tooltip on your character sheet says so. At 60, Lesser Heal and the first ranks of Renew and Heal get next to nothing from your gear: downrank only within the ranks you learned in your forties and fifties.

      Races

      Race picks.

      • Discipline: Human on Alliance for The Human Spirit and Divine Grace, with Gnome level with it at 60. Undead on Horde for Dark Sacrifice.
      • Holy: Human on Alliance for The Human Spirit and Divine Grace, with Gnome close. Undead on Horde for Dark Sacrifice.
      • Shadow: Human on Alliance for Ruthless, with Night Elf just as good and Dwarf close. Undead on Horde for Touch of the Grave, well ahead of Troll.
      • The Gnome priest is new. Gnomes bring Eureka! (8 seconds of 10% cheaper and 10% stronger direct heals and damage spells every 2 minutes, and 10% stronger Mind Flay and Penance ticks; never Shadow Word: Pain, Devouring Plague, Renew or a shield), Expansive Mind (5% more mana) and Escape Artist. Their priest spells are Contingency Plan, a ward that shields and heals an ally who drops under 35%, and Confounding Flash, which confuses nearby enemies. At 60 a Gnome heals as well as a Human as Discipline.

      Each race has its own two priest spells.

      • Human: Divine Grace (a free instant heal that clears Weakened Soul) and Feedback.
      • Dwarf: Desperate Prayer and Chastise.
      • Night Elf: Elune’s Grace and Starshards.
      • Undead: Dark Sacrifice (health for mana, once a fight) and Touch of Weakness.
      • Troll: Hex of Weakness and Shadowguard.

      Skyborne, Forever’s new neutral race, cannot be priests: they play warrior, hunter, rogue, shaman, mage or druid.
