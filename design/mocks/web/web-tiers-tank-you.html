How to read this

How we check the sim ›

What the number is

Damage taken per second, lower is better: it is the sim’s outcome against the boss, where effective health is only the hit-point pool going in. The best of each column is green.

The boss

A stand-in level 63 target with one melee swing every 2 seconds, not a named boss.

The gear

Every spec is simmed in its best Raid-ready, Phase 1 gear, with enchants a fresh level 60 can buy, raid buffs and consumables, as its best Alliance race, named under it. At a fresh 60 the order differs: see the leveling bands ›
