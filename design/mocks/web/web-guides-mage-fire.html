      Fire Mage

      DPS · Updated 6 Nov

      Fire casts a 3 second Fireball, keeps its own Improved Scorch stacks up, turns crits into fast Pyroblasts with Heating Up, and cashes Combustion into a run of Ignites.

      Overview

      In a raid

      • Role: Sustained ranged caster DPS with a Combustion window every 3 minutes
      • Brings: Arcane Brilliance, Remove Lesser Curse, Polymorph and Counterspell. Improved Scorch now only boosts your own Fire damage, so every Fire mage stacks their own.

      Strengths

      • Five points of Accelerant make Fireball a 3 second cast, and with Missile Barrage every Fireball has a 20% chance at a free, fast Arcane Missiles
      • Every Fireball, Scorch and Fire Blast crit pays twice, once through Ignite and once through Heating Up
      • Fire Power rewards you for every burn you keep on the boss
      • Fire Blast is instant and a 3-stack Pyroblast is short enough to cast between steps, so you keep doing damage through movement

      Weaknesses

      • Runs out of mana before a long boss dies without gems, potions and runes
      • No Presence of Mind and no Ice Block: they sit in the Arcane and Frost trees, which this build only dips into
      • Fire-immune and Fire-resistant bosses, Ragnaros first among them, shut the spec down

      The raid build moves 2 points from Wand Specialization (2) to Improved Channeling (2).

      Key talents

      Improved Scorch 3/3
      Every Scorch that lands adds 3% Fire damage taken from you, up to 5 stacks for 30 seconds. A missed Scorch adds no stack
      Heating Up 1/1
      Scorch, Fire Blast and Fireball crits cut your next Pyroblast's cast by 25%, up to 3 stacks, and every Fire Blast hit adds a stack on top. Three stacks make Pyroblast a 1.5 second cast
      Pyroblast 1/1
      The payoff for Heating Up, and the spell you cast before the pull
      Ignite 5/5
      Every Fire crit burns the boss for another 40% of the hit over 4 seconds
      Fire Power 3/3
      3% more Fire damage for each of your Fire effects on the target, up to 12%. Improved Scorch, Ignite and Pyroblast's burn are three of them
      Incineration 5/5 · Critical Mass 3/3
      10% more crit on Scorch, Fire Blast and Arcane Blast and 6% more on every Fire spell, which is what keeps Ignite and Heating Up rolling
      Master of Elements 3/3
      Every Fire crit refunds 30% of the spell's base cost. It is the steady trickle that makes the gems and potions last
      Improved Fire Blast 2/2
      Fire Blast every 6 seconds instead of 8, and 15 yards more range. With Heating Up, that is a Pyroblast stack every 6 seconds
      Combustion 1/1
      Each Fire hit adds 10% Fire crit until three non-periodic crits land, on a 3 minute cooldown
      Accelerant 5/5
      Fireball becomes a 3 second cast, which is what makes it your filler
      Missile Barrage 2/2
      Every Fireball has a 20% chance, and Arcane Blast 40%, to make your next Arcane Missiles free and twice as fast
      Arcane Blast 1/1
      Each cast adds 12% of your Intellect as spell damage to every spell for 18 seconds

      Combustion asks for 30 points above it. The Fire damage talents above it come to 26, and five ranks of Accelerant make it 31. Fire skips Blast Wave and Improved Flamestrike, which are AoE only, and Flame Throwing: 6 more yards are worth less to a raid caster than a faster Fireball. Two ranks of Burning Soul stay for the threat: 20% less threat from your Fire spells and a 47% chance to keep a cast from being pushed back.

      The other 17 points buy hit and a little Arcane. Three go into Precision in Frost for 6% hit. Fourteen in Arcane take Improved Channeling, which opens the tree and keeps your Missiles from being pushed back, Arcane Concentration (a free spell 10% of the time), Missile Barrage, Arcane Blast and a point of Arcane Subtlety. Wand Specialization and Arcane Resilience do nothing on a boss, and Arcane Meditation’s regeneration is worth less than Missile Barrage and Arcane Blast, so all three are left out.

      The level 60 build is the raid build with one change for the open world: Wand Specialization takes two of Improved Channeling’s points, because a leveling mage finishes mobs with the wand while the mana comes back, and 25% more wand damage is worth more there than pushback protection on Arcane Missiles. It spends its points in a different order on the way up, set out under Leveling. Swap those two points back into Improved Channeling when you start raiding. Open the planner to change the build.

      Cooldowns

      Evocation
      8 min cooldown. When you are nearly dry and more than 30 seconds of the fight remain
      Combustion
      3 min cooldown. Once the boss carries 5 Improved Scorch stacks, with your on-use trinkets and your damage racial if your race has one
      Trinkets, racials and potions
      On cooldown, with your biggest damage: Blood Fury (Orc), Eureka! (Gnome), Berserking (Troll)

      Opener. Mage Armor, Arcane Brilliance and your food buff go up before the pull. Conjure a Mana Ruby and a Mana Citrine and drink back to full. Start Pyroblast 6 seconds before the pull so it lands just after the tank’s first hit: the full cast happens while nothing else is going on, and its burn starts your Fire Power count. Then Scorch to 5 stacks, and from there Fire Blast whenever it is ready, unless 3 Heating Up stacks have a Pyroblast waiting. Put up Arcane Blast once the stacks are on.

      The loop. Keep 5 Improved Scorch stacks rolling: refresh them with one Scorch when 5 seconds or less are left, because a 3 second Fireball can carry you past a later refresh. Pyroblast only on 3 stacks of Heating Up. Fire Blast every time it is off cooldown. Arcane Missiles the moment Missile Barrage procs. Arcane Blast once whenever its buff has dropped: 12% of your Intellect as spell damage for 18 seconds is worth a 1.5 second cast, but a second Blast on top doubles its cost, so let the buff fall off first. Everything else is Fireball, except when Fire Blast comes back in less than a Scorch’s cast: then cast Scorch, so the Fire Blast and its Heating Up stack are not held behind a Fireball. A Pyroblast at the full 6 seconds, or on 1 or 2 stacks, loses to a Fireball.

      Combustion. Press it once the boss carries 5 stacks, together with your on-use trinkets and your racial: Eureka! on a Gnome, Blood Fury on an Orc, Berserking on a Troll. A fresh tank needs a few seconds before you do. Combustion lasts until three crits land, and every Fireball, Scorch or Fire Blast crit in it is an Ignite and a Heating Up stack, so the window usually ends in a 1.5 second Pyroblast.

      Ignite and threat. Ignite’s damage is your damage and your threat. Fire mages pull more than anyone else in the first twenty seconds, mostly off a Combustion window that crits three times in a row. You have no Ice Block and no Invisibility, so the only fix is not to pull: watch your threat, hold Combustion on a fresh tank, and stop casting for a global if you are close. Burning Soul takes some off the top.

      Mana in a 3 minute fight. Fire runs dry on a long fight, though not as fast as Arcane. In order: a Mana Ruby when you are 1,200 mana down, a Major Mana Potion the next time you are well down, then the Mana Citrine or a Demonic Rune two minutes after the Ruby. Mana gems and Demonic Runes share one 2 minute cooldown, so it is a gem or a rune, never both at once. The Robe of the Archmage is on its own 5 minute cooldown and shares nothing with the gems or the rune: use it whenever you are 625 mana down. Evocation is an 8 minute cooldown and comes once a fight: channel it near empty with more than 30 seconds left, never in the last 30. Keep Fire Blast on cooldown even when you are short: every hit is a Heating Up stack, and the mana you would save is worth less than the Pyroblasts you would lose. Fireball stays your filler when you are short too: Master of Elements hands back 30% of its cost on every crit, and the Missile Barrage procs it buys are free.

      AoE. Flamestrike is a 3 second cast that burns the ground for 8 seconds, so cast it where the tank is going to stand, then follow with Arcane Explosion in the pack or a Blizzard from range. Flamestrike costs 990 mana, so two of them on a big pull eat a third of your bar before the Arcane Explosions start. Let the tank get a few seconds of threat first, and Frost Nova and Blink out if the pack turns on you.

      Execute and movement. Fire has no execute. On the move, keep Fire Blast on cooldown and save a Pyroblast on 3 Heating Up stacks for the next stop. Scorch’s stacks last 30 seconds, so a short movement phase does not drop them.

      Frostfire Bolt is not a Fire filler. It hits for much less than Fireball, and its burn mixes Frost and Fire, which Fire Power does not count.

      1 hit rating = 0.62 DPS at level 60.

      Hit and Critical strike are per rating point: 10 hit rating is 1% hit and 14 critical strike rating is 1% critical strike.

      Spell haste: haste DPS per 1%, but no item at level 60 has it.

      The table above gives the order for your gear; this is why each stat counts. Hit matters up to the hit cap: every missed Fireball or Scorch is a missed Ignite and a missed Heating Up chance, and a missed Scorch is a missed stack too. Precision gives you 6%, your gear finds the rest, and once your gear reaches the cap more hit adds nothing. Fire power is nearly as good as spell power to you: everything you cast on a boss is Fire except Arcane Blast and the Missile Barrage Missiles. Crit feeds Ignite, Heating Up and Master of Elements at once, and Critical Mass and Combustion stack more of it on top of your gear. Intellect is your mana bar and adds spell crit, which Fire turns into Ignite and Heating Up. Fire drinks a Major Mana Potion on every boss, so a deeper bar keeps you casting to the end.

      Human is the Alliance pick for Fire, a hair ahead of Gnome: Ruthless adds 1% crit with all spells, which Fire turns into Ignite and Heating Up, and The Human Spirit adds 10% Spirit. Gnome is just behind: Eureka! makes your damage spells hit 10% harder and cost 10% less for 8 seconds on a 2 minute cooldown, and Expansive Mind adds 5% mana, so press Eureka! with Combustion on the pull and again the moment it is back. On the Horde, Undead is the pick: Touch of the Grave gives your spells a 30% chance to drain 5% of your maximum health from the boss, at most once every 15 seconds, and Will of the Forsaken breaks fears. Orc is next: Blood Fury adds 10% spell power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes, so press it with Combustion on the pull and on cooldown after that. Troll Berserking adds 10% cast speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes and lines up with every Combustion, but falls behind both. Skyborne is a paid unlock and only Alliance for mages, and played without Read Leyline it does the least Fire damage of the lot.

      Professions

      Tailoring and Enchanting are the standard pair, and your raid set needs both: Robe of the Archmage is a Tailoring craft and Frozen Heart of the Mountain an Enchanting one, and both bind on pickup, so nobody can make them for you. Enchanting also puts the spell power enchants on your own gear. The goggles in the head slot need 270 Engineering to wear: without it, take the best head you can wear and keep your two professions. Alchemy pays for itself once you raid every week, because Fire drinks a Major Mana Potion on every boss.

      Levels 1 to 19

      Fireball is trained at 1 with new ranks at 6, 12 and 18, so visit the trainer every even level. Frostbolt at 4 is your slow, Fire Blast at 6 is the instant finisher, Frost Nova at 10 buys distance and Flamestrike at 16 hits packs.

      From 10 to 14 put five points into Incineration, 10% more crit on Fire Blast, then Accelerant at 15 to cut 0.1 sec off Fireball, and Ignite from 16 to 19 so every Fire crit burns for more.

      Wand mobs down from half health to save mana: Lesser Magic Wand at 5 and Greater Magic Wand at 13 come from Enchanting, and Riptear’s Spare Arm (Rear Guard Patrol, Horde) or Elven Wand (Alliance, Bashal’Aran, Darkshore) are early quest wands. Your trainer’s level 10 class quest pays a staff or an orb; take the staff and retire the starter one. Wear cloth quest rewards over vendor gear, Intellect and Stamina first.

      Levels 20 to 60

      Pyroblast comes at 20 and Ignite’s last point at 21, then three more points of Accelerant at 22 to 24 make Fireball a 3.1 second cast. Fireball stays your nuke through the 20s, with Fire Blast on cooldown: at the ranks you have in the 20s Fireball does more damage for its mana than Scorch, and mana is what runs out first. Improved Scorch’s first point at 27 lands a stack only one Scorch in three, so at 27 build the stacks only on a mob or elite that lives a minute or more. From 28, with two points, Scorch to 5 stacks first on anything that lives 20 seconds. From 30, with Heating Up in, Scorch and Fire Blast take over on single targets: every Scorch crit is a Heating Up stack, two Scorches roll twice the crits of one Fireball, and every few crits turn into a fast Pyroblast. On leveling mana the cheap ranks win here, because each one earns the same Heating Up stack for less: Scorch rank 1 from 28 to 39, Fire Blast rank 2 to 35 and rank 3 from 36 to 39, and Pyroblast rank 2 to 37, except rank 3 at 34 and 35. Open on a mob with Pyroblast from range, Fireball (Scorch from 30) as it closes, and finish it with Fire Blast. Keep Frost Nova and Blink for anything that reaches you, and Frostbolt for kiting: none of your Fire spells slow until Frostfire Bolt at 40, and Frostbolt is still the cheaper slow after that. Flamestrike into Arcane Explosion clears packs once you have the Stamina for it. Train Mage Armor at 34 and wear it for the mana; switch to Ice Armor when you are taking melee hits in the open world.

      After Combustion at 40, Wand Specialization at 41 and 42 adds a quarter to the wand you finish mobs with. Accelerant’s last point at 43 and two points of Burning Soul at 44 and 45 keep a mob’s hits from pushing back half your Fire casts, then Improved Channeling, a point of Arcane Subtlety, Arcane Concentration from 50 and Missile Barrage at 55 and 56, which procs off Fireball and Arcane Blast and so waits for 57. Arcane Blast at 57 changes the loop: Fireball is the filler again, as in a raid, with the Arcane Blast buff kept up and every Missile Barrage proc spent on a fast Arcane Missiles. Precision takes the last three points for 6% hit.
