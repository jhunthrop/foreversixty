      • Real today: the character (Battle.net import), the band-20 upgrades and their DPS deltas (BiS file; worn items are real runners-up), the band-30 talent build, trainer spells and dungeon picks (talents, trainables and BiS files), the 9 Dec raid list (dates.json).

      • The hero is the one focal moment: crest, name in class colour, the sim figure; the journey track and unlock row sit inside the band.
      • Upgrades become a strip of six tiles; the green bar is the delta against the largest, so the order reads at a glance.
