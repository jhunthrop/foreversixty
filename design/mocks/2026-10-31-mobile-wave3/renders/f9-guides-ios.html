Elemental Shaman
Updated 24 Sep 2026 · Recommended races: Dwarf, Orc
OverviewTalentsRotationStatsGearRaces
Role
Ranged spell damage

Overview
Elemental is Shaman's ranged spellcasting spec, dealing damage with Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, Flame Shock and Lava Burst, backed by fire totems.
Strengths
Forever's baseline cast-time cut to Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning
Lava Burst hits harder when Flame Shock is already on the target
Weaknesses
Slower leveling than Enhancement, because its damage depends on cast times
Hard casts leave it idle while moving

F9.3 Talents. Every talent has its icon, rank and the game's own text.
Guides
OverviewTalentsRotationStatsGearRaces
Talents
Concussion 5 ranks
Increases the damage done by your Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, and Earth Shock spells by 5%.
Call of Flame 3 ranks
Increases the damage done by your Fire Totems and by your Flame Shock, Fire Nova, and Lava Burst spells by 15%, as well as increases the duration of your Flame Shock spell by 9 sec.
Elemental Fury 5 ranks
Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your Searing and Magma Totems and your Fire, Frost, and Nature spells by 100%.
Lightning Overload 3 ranks
Gives your Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning spells a 10% chance to cast a second, similar spell on the same target at no additional cost that causes half damage and no threat.
Elemental Alacrity 3 ranks
Reduces the cast time of your Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, and Lava Burst spells by 0.50 sec.
Lava Burst 1 rank
You hurl molten lava at the target, dealing 114 to 145 Fire damage. If your Flame Shock is on the target, Lava Burst deals 20% increased damage.
Elemental Mastery 1 rank
Gain mastery over Fire and Lightning, reducing the cast time of Lightning Bolt and Lava Burst by 0.5 sec, and removing the cooldown from Chain Lightning. Lasts 15 sec.
