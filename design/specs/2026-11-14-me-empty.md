# Me tab, no site activity (spec, 2026-11-14)

Extends 2026-11-13-addon-hub.md sections 3, 4.1 and 8. Scope: `MeTab.model` and `MeFrame.draw` only. No new window, no new control.

## 1. Question and answer

Question: "I have no logs yet. What do I look like right now, and what is next?"

The answer is visible at once, with no scroll, at 740 px wide (the hub never scrolls) and no taller than the active column:
- Level and experience.
- The build and its next talent point.
- Gear at your band: "9 of 16 best in slot", then the top three upgrades with their DPS gains.

Every figure comes from the client or the shipped data. Nothing is invented. A missing source drops its row.

## 2. State and what it replaces

The state applies when all three are true: no score record, no `last_raid`, no `sim_result`. The ratings pack and companion state do not matter.

| Region | Normal | This state |
|---|---|---|
| Left 1, Forever Score | score and five parts | removed |
| Left 2, Last raid | table | removed |
| Left 1 (new) | none | Where you stand |
| Left 2 (new) | none | Gear |
| Left foot (new) | none | one site line |
| Right, Right now | unchanged | unchanged |
| Right, Set up | unchanged | unchanged |
| Right, Your sim | per spec 8 | unchanged |

Right now keeps every act-now item, including a point waiting, a bag upgrade and tonight's raid. The new left sections state facts and never carry client gold or a tab link. If Right now already shows a row for a tab, the matching left row stays but is plain body and muted text.

The model returns `standing`, `gear` and `site`. `score` and `lastRaid` are nil. When any of the three records exists, the state ends and the normal layout returns in the same draw.

Reference: the in-game character panel (level, XP bar, rested), the tooltip's "Equipped" comparison for the gear rows, and Wowhead's BiS table rows for the icon, name and gain. We borrow the XP bar, rested wording and the icon-name-number row. We borrow no frames or boxes.

## 3. Layout, left column (388)

Order: Where you stand, Gear, site line. `sectionGap` 16 between sections. Headings are `BuildsWindow.buildSection` through the local `heading()`: title face, cream, with a muted right-hand note. No boxes, cards, rules or edge bars.

### 3.1 Where you stand

Right note: `Level 25 Hunter`. Rows are 18 px (`G.partRow`). The label is muted at x 0, width 86 (`G.partLabel`). The value is body text from x 86. A muted note is right-aligned to 388.

| Row | Label | Value | Right note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Experience` | 8 px track, 130 wide (`G.track`, `G.trackMax`), filled to XP share in body colour, then muted `62%` | `Rested 40%` muted, only when `GetXPExhaustion` is above 0 |
| 2 | `Build` | build name, body, cut to fit | `27 of 33 on plan` muted |
| 3 | `Next point` | talent spell icon 16 px and name, body | `Level 26` muted |
| 4 | `Next` | one unlock line, muted | none |

Row notes:
- Row 1's right note is the rested share of the level: `GetXPExhaustion() / UnitXPMax()`, rounded to a whole percent.
- Row 2 reads `Progress.build` and the points on plan from `BuildsPane.figures`. Hover shows the build's lead (`BuildDialog.lead`) in the client tooltip.
- Row 3 is the talent from `Progress.nextPoint` and `nextPointLevel`. Its spell icon has the client's spell tooltip. When a point waits now, the note reads `Now` in body colour, not gold. Right now owns the gold.
- Row 4 is the next unlock: `Gear.bandFor` for the next band, or the first talent point.

At 60, row 1 is replaced (below). Rows with no source are left out and the rows beneath move up.

### 3.2 Gear

Shown only when `GearTab.read` has a model at the character's band (level 10 or more). Right note: `9 of 16 best in slot`, from `GearTab.tally` and `ownBand`. Rows are 20 px (`G.gainRow`), at most 3 upgrades, then at most 1 enchant row.
- Upgrade row: `itemCell` 16 px with the quality edge, the item name in its quality colour, and the gain right-aligned in success green with the existing `L.meGain` (`+12.4 DPS`). Tooltip and shift-click are `itemCell`'s own. The order is `GearTab.upgrades`, largest first.
- Enchant row (`unenchantedLine`): no icon, `2 enchants missing` in warning, then the slot names muted and cut to fit, for example `Weapon, Chest`.
- Fewer than 3 upgrades: show what exists. No padding rows.
- No upgrades and no missing enchants: one muted row, `Nothing to chase at 30.`, with the band number.
- No gear model at all (no list for the band): the Gear section is left out.

### 3.3 The site line

One muted line at `y + 2`, then one gold link on the next line. The link is the existing `MeFrame.openApp` copy box.
- No companion: line `Your score, raid logs and sim show here once the companion app brings them in.` Gold link `foreversixty.gg/app`.
- Companion running, nothing in the inbox yet: line `Your score and raid logs show here after your first logged raid.` No link.
- First-open Set up showing (`model.setup`): the whole site line is left out. Step 3 carries the address once.

## 4. Level cases

**Level 5, no spec, no band:**
- Where you stand shows Experience, then `Build` with `No build followed` muted. There is no Next point row.
- Row 4 reads `Next` / `Level 10, your first talent point`.
- The Gear section is left out.
- The Gear tab is not shown (it opens at level 10), so Gear never appears here.
- This is the thinnest case by design: three honest rows and the site line. Right now carries Set up (step 1, `Pick your spec`).

**Level 25, build followed, band 20:**
- All four rows show, row 4 reading `Next` / `Level 30, a new gear list`.
- Gear shows band 20's figures.
- Example: `Build` `Beast Mastery leveling` / `11 of 16 on plan`, then `Next point` / `Improved Aspect of the Hawk` / `Level 26`.

**Level 60 fresh:**
- Row 1 reads `Level` / `60` in body colour, with the note `Max level` muted. There is no track and no rested note.
- Row 2 reads `51 of 51 on plan`.
- Rows 3 and 4 are left out when no point waits and no band is above.
- Gear is the band-60 section.
- The site line is the no-companion variant.

## 5. States

Rows are text and read-only. They are not buttons.

| Element | Default | Hover | Focus | Loading | Empty / error |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Section heading and rows | as 3.1 | none | none | n/a | an omitted row leaves no gap |
| Build name (row 2) | body text | client tooltip with the build lead | none | n/a | build missing: `No build followed` muted |
| Talent icon and name (row 3) | spell icon, body name | client spell tooltip | none | n/a | no talent left: row omitted |
| Item cell | icon with quality edge | client item tooltip | none | icon `Theme.UNKNOWN_ICON`, name from `data.bisNames` until the client loads it | n/a |
| Item cell click | n/a | n/a | n/a | n/a | shift-click links the item; plain click does nothing |
| Site link | gold | `goldHover`, 1 px underline | 1 px gold ring | n/a | n/a |

Touch parity does not apply, because the addon has no touch. Keyboard parity: the link is a `Button`, and Tab order is link then close. Tooltips need hover only. Every fact in a tooltip is also in the row text.

## 6. Hierarchy

- Primary: the Gear section's gains (success green against body text). At 60 it is the Gear note.
- One level of emphasis: the XP fill. Everything else is body or muted.
- The site line is secondary: muted, with one gold link.
- Colours follow 3.5. Gold is used only for the link. Green is gain only. Warning is missing enchants only. Item names carry quality colours.
- No client gold appears in the new sections.

## 8. Limits and omissions

- Where you stand: 4 rows maximum. Gear: 3 upgrade rows and 1 enchant row.
- Left column height at level 25 is about 250 px (heading 22 + 72 + 16 + heading 22 + 80 + 16 + 2 lines of site text 30). This is less than the right column with four Right now rows (164 px plus Your sim).
- The window height still comes from `Hub.fitHeight`. The tab must not make the window taller than the shortest of today's Me layouts.
- Left out when the data is missing: rested note (no rested XP), Next point (no talent left or no build), Next (nothing ahead), Gear (no list for the band), the enchant row (none missing), the site link (companion present or Set up showing).
- Professions are not in this pass: the addon does not read `GetSkillLineInfo` yet, and a row with no source is not shown.

## 9. Build and polish rules

- Budget about 150 lines of drawing in `MeFrame.lua`, using `text`, `place`, `heading`, `itemCell`, `paintCell` and `link`. Build all regions once in `MeFrame.build`. Do not create regions in `draw`.
- A redraw must not Hide or Show a control under the cursor. Row regions are built once, and `draw` only sets text, texture, colour and width through `fit`. A row that disappears is hidden only when the model removes it. An item cell that stays in use keeps its position and visibility, and only `itemId` and the texture change. The XP fill and the rested note update in place on `PLAYER_XP_UPDATE`, at most once a second, with no Hide or Show.
- No layout shift on open: the first draw already has the final rows. The item name falls back to `data.bisNames` before the client loads it, so there is no empty-name flash.
- No missing-icon flash: `paintCell` sets the icon and the edge in one call, and the unknown icon is `Theme.UNKNOWN_ICON`.
- The XP track and fill use `Theme.texture` and `Theme.wholePixels`, so edges are whole pixels.

## 11. Build amendments (2026-11-14, lane addon-me-tab-empty)

- Gear sits in the RIGHT column, under Right now (or Set up) and above Your sim, not in the left. With no logs the right column otherwise holds one Right now row or none, and the left would run long while the right stood empty. Left: Where you stand, then the site line.
- The Builds tab's calm lead leaves Right now in this state (`MeTab.QUIET_SAID`), because Where you stand already says the build and its points on plan. A Builds item that waits (a point to spend, builds from the site) still shows there in client gold.
- The Where you stand heading carries no right note: the header's figures line already reads `Level 25 Hunter`.
- The build row has no hover tooltip and the talent row's icon carries no spell tooltip in this pass: every fact is in the row text (section 5's own rule).
