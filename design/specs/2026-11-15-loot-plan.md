# Loot plan before the raid: experience spec

2026-11-15. Inputs: design/reviews/2026-11-15-guild-diff/00-facts.md (verified), 01-plan.md, 02-officer-plan.md, 03-brief.md.
Where the facts and the plan differ, the facts win. Fixture world: Olympus XXVII, Alliance, 38 members; Thandrel, Fury Warrior 60
(officer and raider); raid: Onyxia's Lair, Wed 9 Dec (the only raid open this phase; data/builds/1.60.1.70938/loot.json `opens: raids-1`, 1 boss, 22 table entries of which 16 are gear).
Every other raid in loot.json is `later`: the page is built per boss and holds for all of them.

## 1. The question and the answer above the fold

Officer: "This drops tonight. Who should it go to, and is that fair?" Raider: "Which drops tonight are mine?"

Visible with no scroll on 360 and 1280:
- Officer: the selected drop (icon, quality-coloured name, slot), then the Damage group's first three rows, each with rank, name, gain
  band, and the fairness line. The Healers and Tanks group heads sit under it, counts visible, never hidden behind a tab.
- Raider: `Your drops`, up to five rows, each drop with your status word and your own band. No other raider appears.

The plan is a recommendation. The council decides (the loot council's award flow is unchanged and stays the only place an award is written).

## 2. Reference

- Wowhead BiS table: item rows with icon, quality colour, slot, source boss. Borrowed: the ItemRow shape and boss grouping.
- In-game master loot window and RCLootCouncil's response list: one row per candidate, gear they wear in the slot, loot received,
  response (MS/OS). Borrowed: the candidate row's content order (replaces, awards, response). Not borrowed: one mixed list.
- The addon's own session (addon/ForeverSixty/OfficerLoot.lua, `hub-officer-loot`): drops, rows per answering member, Won this week,
  attendance. The plan is that session's rows, computed before the raid, grouped by role.
- Existing web council (`web/src/components/guild/loot/*`: LootDrops, LootCandidates, LootCandidate, MemberFlag): reused, see section 4.

## 3. Rules that bind every surface

1. Rank inside a role only: Damage (DPS), Healers (HPS), Tanks (score). Three groups, three headings, never one list, never one unit.
   A raider's role is the sign-up role when signed up, else the logged role. `roleOf(row)` in loot_candidates.go returns only the logged role today, so the sign-up override is new code (lane L1): a tank signed up as damage lands in Damage.
2. Gain is a band, never a point figure. Damage rows: the server rounds the loot value (sims.LootValue.Value) to a 10-wide band: `30 to 40 DPS`, `under 10 DPS`.
   The group heading carries `estimate`. Healers and tanks get no absolute band until D3 gives floors (tanks have no dps-per-point.json entry and
   score is not DPS; healer HPS is noisy). Until then their band is relative to the group's own top gain for that drop: `Top` (within 10 percent of the
   group's top gain), `Close` (within half of it), `Smaller` (under half). Never a number, never compared across groups. D3 replaces these with absolute floors.
3. Ties: rows whose bands match share the same rank number and show `Even`. The band wins; inside a band the order is source (rule 4), then
   won this phase ascending, then last-4 attendance descending, then name. The row's own fairness line carries the reason; no caption restates it.
   Lock pins a row at its current rank, across recomputes; the pinned row still prints its true band, so a lock that now sits above a higher band is visible.
   Swap exchanges the row with the pick above it; across a band boundary the moved row becomes locked (glyph shown) because the order no longer follows the sort.
   A lock or skip on a raider who withdraws from the raid is kept but muted (`Withdrew`) and ignored by the ranking; it returns if they sign up again.
4. Gear source rank: addon export (newest, enchants, suffixes) > signed-in Blizzard (3 h active or 20 h idle, enchants yes) > directory
   (up to 72 h, NO enchants, NO suffix). The band wins; source breaks ties only inside a band, so a directory row in a higher band ranks above a fresher row in a lower band and says so with its age.
   Every row carries the source and its age as one muted text, `Addon 2 h` (the site's shared duration format), not a chip. A directory row also reads `Enchants unknown`. Age text turns amber when older than the last raid; there is no separate Old gear chip.
   "Missing" is never printed for anything a directory row cannot know.
5. Tier 0 (the drop is in the raider's BiS band) and tier 1 (item level upgrade, no band) are the council's. Tier 1 rows sit under one
   `Item level upgrades` caption in the same role group, with no band and no second label.
6. Not candidates (existing rule, loot_candidates.go): wrong class or level, slotless drop, no gear on file. Those raiders are not listed in rows.
   They are counted: `3 signed up with no gear on file` under the group, names for officers only (D5).
7. Two-slot items: rings, trinkets and one-hand weapons compare against the worse of the two worn pieces, and `replaces` names that piece
   (Vis'kag the Bloodletter replaces the worse of two weapons). Shamans never dual wield in Forever: compare against the single weapon, never an off-hand.
   Survival hunters compare as melee. Non-gear table entries (Scale of Onyxia, Onyxia Hide Backpack, both Heads of Onyxia, Mature Black Dragon Sinew,
   Draconic for Dummies) are not ranked: the filter is class weapon or armor with an equip slot (items.json `class_id` 2 or 4, `inventory_type` not 0).
   They show in one muted `Not ranked` line under the list, each with its chance.
8. Drop chance: every drop row and every raider row shows the chance from loot.json `item_chances` (`8%`, `100%`); an entry with none on file shows `Chance unknown`.
   Onyxia's gear mostly sits at 8%, so the page never prints a count of drops as what is coming.
9. Chips: at most two per candidate row. `BiS` only when true, MS/OS only when declared; nothing else is a chip.
10. Names to officers only. Raiders never receive another raider's row; the API strips it server-side, the page is not a CSS hide.

Existing page region it replaces: the empty space under the planner's rail. Components (web/src/components): PageHeader, TabStrip, ControlBar,

- Notice (muted, one line, dismissible per officer, the only disclaimer on any surface): `A recommendation. The loot council decides.`

The row links to the council's existing LootCandidate detail, and shows the council's vote tally when the drop is open on a night. Under the list one muted `Not ranked` line names the 6 non-gear entries with their chances. Selected row has the 2 px gold left rule (the site's selection token). Primary element: the selected row.
  - Right Plate: the drop head (ItemRow 48, with the GameTooltip on hover), then three RankGroups: `Damage · 11`, `Healers · 4`, `Tanks · 2`.
    Each group: caption line, rows (first 3, `Show all 11` ShowAll), then a muted tail for the not-candidates count.
- Candidate row (one line at 1280, three at 360): rank numeral (muted; gold when locked), crest 28 (circular ringed HD crest only), name in class colour, `Fury Warrior`,
  gain band in the group's unit, `replaces Dal'Rend's Sacred Charge` (what they wear in the slot, ItemName in quality colour, `Empty slot` when none),
  `BiS` chip only when true, MS/OS chip only when declared (a derived flag shows as muted text), fairness line `Won 2 this phase · 3 of 4 raids`,
  source and age as muted text `Addon 2 h`, `Blizzard 3 h`, `Directory 2 d`, and the officer actions.
- Officer actions, one click each, icon buttons with a visible text label from 1024 px, in an overflow `...` Sheet below 768:
  `Skip` (takes the raider out of this item's plan, row collapses to a muted `Skipped by Karl · Undo`), `Swap` (exchanges this row with the pick above it, 8 s undo toast), `Lock` (pins the row at its current rank; a lock glyph, survives recompute; `Unlock` toggles).
  No confirm dialog on any of the three: each is undoable for 8 s and the council's award is the real decision. A mark records officer and time (`Locked by Karl, 14:20`) as a title on the glyph and in the row's Disclosure.
- Hierarchy: the first row of each group is the group's one emphasised row (name 16 px, band in the 16 px gold figure style); other rows at the body size, band in ink. Fairness line and source Tag are the muted tier (12 px minimum, muted ink token). Nothing else is gold.

### 5.2 Raider view (member, same route)
- Header and Notice as above. No Plate of every drop: one Plate `Your drops`: ItemRows (up to 5, `All 9 ›`), one per gear drop the raider is a candidate for, each with the chance, `replaces <your item>` in its quality colour, the item level change (`+3 item level`), your status and your own band.
- Status words, server-computed: `Likely upgrade` (tier 0 and, for damage, a band above `under 10`; for healers and tanks, `Top` or `Close`), `Possible upgrade` (tier 1 or `Smaller`), `No upgrade` (shown only in `All`), `No gear on file`. The chance is on the row, so a status on an 8% item does not read as a promise.
- The raider's own fairness line is shown (`You have won 2 this phase`). Rank, other names and other bands never leave the server.
- A raider not signed up: `Sign up to see your drops` with the sign-up Segmented (existing).

## 8. Surface D: the raid leader's addon

How the plan reaches the game, honestly: the addon makes no network calls. The companion writes a new inbox message `loot_plan` (contract 3, beside `loot_values`) into the SavedVariables inbox. The addon reads the inbox at login or /reload only.
So the plan in game is as fresh as the leader's last login or reload after the companion last wrote. The panel shows that age: `Plan from 14:02`, and when the site has a newer mark than the stamp the panel cannot know it (it says nothing; the age line is the honesty).
No companion: no plan, and the panel line reads `No plan. Run the companion to get one.` (never an error). Message body per drop: top 3 candidates per role, name, band, locked and dropped flags, source and age, awards this phase, last 4. Raider rows beyond that are not sent (size and privacy).
- Where: OfficerTab's loot session (surfaces/OfficerTab.lua, OfficerLoot.lua). Above the live rows for the selected item, one `Plan` strip: `Plan  Damage Thandrel, Brenn  Healers none  Tanks Kaelra  (locked)`. Below it the existing live rows.
- Live compare is unchanged: `loot?` goes out on RAID or PARTY, `loot!` answers fill in rows with the raider's CURRENT gear. Live rows are labelled `Live`, the strip `Plan`; when they disagree the panel states neither is right (`Plan uses gear from 14:02. Live uses what they wear now.` as the strip's tooltip).
- Fix owed (polish, in lane L6): OfficerLoot.view ranks `largest first` across units. Group live rows by unit (dps, hps, score) with the same headings as the site; never one mixed order.
- States: no plan for this item (strip hidden, one muted line `Not in the plan`), plan older than 24 h (`Plan from Mon 14:02` in the amber stale tone), item not in the plan but live answers exist (live only), in combat (no change; the Officer tab opens only out of combat as today).
- Raiders' addons never receive the plan.

## 10. Officer objections, by number (02-officer-plan.md section 3)

1. Public gear without consent: sections 3 and 4. Directory is off by default, behind D1 plus a per-guild setting, with `directory_optouts` honoured. Signed-in data is used only at gear consent.

## 11. Performance and polish limits
- No layout shift on hydration: group heights reserved from the counts in the first payload; Skeleton rows at the final 56 px; the drop list's width fixed at 420 px.
- No missing-icon flash: ItemIcon and the crest come from the baked sprite as on the Loot tab; icons for every drop are in the first paint (web/public/data icons, no runtime fetch).
- web/lighthouserc.json budgets unchanged; the plan page must not add a route-level JS chunk over the budget (the island loads with the raid page's own).
- Plan payload under 40 KB per raid for 40 raiders and 16 gear drops (top 3 shown plus the rest as counts; `Show all` fetches the group).
- Server-side strip for raiders, test with a raider token: the body holds only own rows.
