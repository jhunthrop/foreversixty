# The Guild tab: experience spec (lane guild-tab)

2026-11-10, owner-approved. The Raid tab tells a level 36 player `24 levels to go` and nothing else, and the first raids open
on 9 Dec, so for most players today the tab is dead. The site's nav says Guild. The tab becomes **Guild** on both apps: the
guild you are in, at any level, with the raid at its centre once you can go.

Mocks: `design/mocks/ws_app_guild.py` (`guild-tab*` screens, `python3 design/mocks/ws_app.py guild-tab`) into
`design/mocks/app/<platform>-guild-tab*.html`. Reviews: `design/reviews/2026-11-10-guild-tab/`.

Carried over: the flows spec (2026-11-08-app-flows.md sections 3 and 4.2), round 5's panels on the ground, game words,
no explainer lines, no em dashes, one crest language, every figure from data.

## 1. The tab

- Label **Guild**, the second of four: Home, Guild, Gear, Activity. Same badge rule as the Raid tab had: your unanswered
  next raid (at 60) or an officer's open items. Below 60 there is no badge for the raid (you cannot answer it).
- Icon: the site's Guild door (`design/assets/icons/ui/guild.svg`, the glyph web's header draws for Guild), baked by
  `web/scripts/gen-ui-icons.mjs` into `tab-guild` (iOS template image, Android VectorDrawable) next to the other three tab
  vectors, at 24 pt / 24 dp, with a thicken stroke only if it needs one to match the others' weight at 24. Tinted by the
  tab bar exactly as the others (selected gold, unselected the muted ink, same opacity), light and dark. `tab-raid` and
  `ic_tab_raid` are deleted.
- Deep links keep working: `/app/raid/{guild}/{raid}` and `/night` push the raid page onto the Guild tab's stack;
  `/app/guild/{guild}` and the applicant link open the Guild tab. A raid push opens the raid inside the Guild tab.

## 2. The four states

The fixture world: Olympus XXVII, US PvP Alliance, 38 members. Thandrel, Fury Warrior 60 (member and, in the officer
state, officer). Felsnap, Beast Mastery Hunter 36, in the same guild for the below-60 state; with no guild for that state.

### 2.1 In a guild, below 60 (`guild-tab-leveler`)
Order, top to bottom:
1. **Guild head**: the guild crest (or the faction mark when it has none), the name, `38 members · Alliance`.
   Under it the level spread: `12 at 60 · 9 close · you're 36` (close = 55 to 59).
2. **Next raid**, viewable: the zone icon, the raid, `Wed 9 Dec, 8:00 pm CST`, the counts `14 in, 2 late` and the roles
   `2 tanks · 4 healers · 8 damage`. The four answers are drawn disabled; one quiet line under them: `Raids open at 60`.
   Never a whole-tab blocker. No raid planned: one line, `No raid planned yet`.
3. **Roster**: `Recently on` (the five most recently seen members: crest, name in class colour, `60 · Fury Warrior`,
   `Seen 2 h ago`), then `Close to 60` (the members at 55 to 59 with their level, highest first, up to five). Action
   `Roster ›` to the roster page.
4. **Recent logs**: the guild's last reports from the trailing week (title, zone, `4 of 6 kills · 1 h 08 min`, the day),
   each opening the report. Empty: the section is left out.
5. **Progression**: the tier name and `0 of 1 bosses down`, pulls this tier; before the first pull the line says
   `First raids open Wed 9 Dec` (from the bundled key dates).
6. The guild's pages the sections above do not open already: Calendar, Loot history (Guild sims is a 60's page).

### 2.2 In a guild, at 60 (`guild-tab`)
Today's raid experience first, unchanged from the Raid tab (flows spec 4.2): the one-line raid head with your answer and
the four answers, Who is coming, Before you pull; raid night leads while a raid is live; the Live Activity hooks are
untouched. Then: the **guild head** (compact, as a section title row: crest, name, `38 members · 31 at 60`), **Roster**
(Recently on, as above; no Close to 60 row when it is empty), **Loot** (last raid's pieces from the raid badge, `Loot
history ›`), **Recent logs**, **Progression**, the pages left (Calendar, Guild sims).

### 2.3 Officer (`guild-tab-officer`, at 60; an officer below 60 gets the same rows, not drawn)
The member's tab plus **Officer** right after the raid block (after Needs you, which stays):
- `Plan a raid` opens the guild page's Raids tab on the site, where Plan a raid lives (`{guild page}#raids`).
- `Applications` with the pending count (`3 waiting`), opening the applicant list; each applicant one more tap.
- `Setup` with the checklist's progress (`5 of 8 done`), opening the site's setup checklist (`{guild page}/setup`).
  Left out once finished.
Each one tap from the tab. Shown when the guild home's `viewer.officer_tools` is true.

### 2.4 No guild (`guild-tab-none`, `guild-tab-none-leveler`)
1. **Guilds recruiting a Fury Warrior** (as today) with Apply; a guild you already applied to shows `Applied` and the
   day instead of the button.
2. **Find your guild**: `Search guilds on the site ›` (the site's guild finder, `/guild`) and Claim your guild (as today).
3. Below 60, one quiet line under the head: `Raids open at 60`; the rest is the same.
Signed out: sign in, as today.

## 3. Data mapping (only what the API already serves the web guild pages)

| Element | Call | Field |
|---|---|---|
| Guild head | `GET /v1/guilds/{id}/home` (`getGuildHome`, already generated) | `guild.name`, `guild.faction`, `guild.crest_url`, `counts.on_roster` |
| Level spread | same | `members[].level` (count at 60, at 55 to 59), the viewer's own level from `/v1/me` |
| Recently on | same | `members[]` sorted by `seen_at`, `class`, `spec_name`, `level` |
| Close to 60 | same | `members[].level` 55 to 59 |
| Next raid (below 60) | same | `next_raid` (title, zone, `starts_at`, `guild_timezone`, `counts`) |
| Recent logs | same | `reports[]` (title, zone, `created_at`, `fight_count`, `kill_count`, `duration_ms`) |
| Progression | same | `summary.tier` (name, `down`, `named_encounters`), `summary.pulls_this_tier`; key dates for the first raids |
| Officer flag | same | `viewer.officer_tools` |
| Applications | `GET /v1/guilds/{id}/applications` (already used) and `/raid-badge` | pending rows, `applicants` |
| Setup progress | `GET /v1/guilds/{id}/setup` | `done`, `total`, `finished` (documented in openapi with the `app` tag in this lane; the route exists) |
| Loot | `GET /v1/guilds/{id}/raid-badge` (already used) | `last_raid_loot` |
| Raid at 60 | unchanged | `/v1/me`, `/raids`, `/raid-nights`, `/ready` |
| Recruiting | `GET /v1/guilds/recruiting` (already used) | rows |
| Your applications | `GET /v1/guilds/{id}/applications/mine` for each recruiting guild shown | `rows[].status`, `created_at` |

The guild home is one more call when the tab loads; a failure leaves its sections out, never the tab (as the extras do today).

### Needs data (not built)
1. An account-wide list of your applications (`/v1/me/applications`): today the tab can only mark guilds in the
   recruiting list; an application to any other guild is not shown.
2. Guild kills by boss with dates on the guild home (the home carries report kill counts and the tier's down count, not
   which boss died when; the progression call has it per encounter but the tab keeps to one line).
