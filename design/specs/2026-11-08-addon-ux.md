Status: design (spec, mocks, persona review). No addon code changes in this lane.

## 2. Tooltip redesign

### 2.1 The grammar, one for every tooltip

- **G1. One answer, then at most one supporting line.** No header, no blank row. Our lines follow the client's last line directly, like its own coloured lines. Everything else is behind Shift.
- **G2. Colour is meaning**, in the client's own font colours:
  - green `GREEN_FONT_COLOR` (1aff1a): do this (an upgrade, in your bags);
  - gold `NORMAL_FONT_COLOR` (ffd100): yours (your best in slot, your job tonight);
  - white `HIGHLIGHT_FONT_COLOR` (ffffff): information (next upgrade, no upgrade until);
  - grey `Theme.tooltipMuted` (b8b8b8, lighter than the client's 808080 for contrast on the tooltip ground): no, or detail;
  - item names always in their quality colour; gains always green.
- **G3. The green arrow means upgrade everywhere**: the client's `bags-greenarrow` atlas at 12 px (`|A:bags-greenarrow:12:12|a`) leads every green upgrade line, as it marks the bag item, the paper-doll slot, the quest reward and the roll frame.
- **G4. Shift is details**, the same key the client uses to compare. No tooltip ever prints a "hold Shift" hint; the welcome teaches it once.
- **G5. Silence over noise.** No data, not gear, not loaded yet: no line at all. Never `…`, never a zero.

### 2.2 Item verdicts, exact text

Precedence top to bottom; the first that applies is the verdict. `N` is the next band's level; gains are `Progress.roundedGain` with the existing 1.0 floor (`Progress.MIN_GAIN`), one decimal for DPS and HPS, none for score.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Client has not sent the item, or its stats | none until it arrives, then redraw (today's `pending`) | | |

Unit: DPS for damage specs, HPS for healers, `score` for specs with neither (tanks); the word follows the number.

### 2.3 The Shift block, exact order

Appended under the support line, all grey unless named:

1. The figure's breakdown: `+1 Agi, +3.4 weapon DPS` (no brackets).
2. Alternatives, at most two, one per line: `Also good: <item> +2.2 (needs bullets)`, the second aligned under the first item name.
3. `In your build's gear list` when the loaded build lists the item.
4. A cap: `Hit capped: more is wasted`.
5. `Enchant not counted` when the worn enchant could not be read.
6. Last, dimmer (8a8a8a): `Forever Sixty · Beast Mastery estimate` or `Forever Sixty · Beast Mastery, your sim`.

Holding or releasing Shift redraws the tooltip through the client's own `SetHyperlink` from an event frame (the mechanism `PlayerTooltip.lua` already uses). No `OnUpdate`.

### 2.4 Quest reward and loot roll

- On the quest frame's choices: the green arrow on the icon of the reward with the best gain ≥ 1.0 (only one); `Best in slot` in gold under the name of the reward that is the band's pick (may be the same one). When no reward helps: `Sells for most`, muted, under the reward with the highest client sell price; nothing on the others.
- The reward's hover is the item tooltip (§2.2).
- The roll frame: one short line beside the item, from the same verdict: arrow `+3.5 DPS` (green), `Best in slot` (gold), `Sidegrade`, `Downgrade`, `Not for you` (grey).
- Overlays stay addon-owned frames parented to the client's buttons (the L4.1 taint rule).

## 3. First-run welcome and the chip pulse

### 3.1 The welcome panel (`welcome.png`)

- Shown once per account: `ForeverSixtyDB.welcomedAt` (the field `SiteStatus.welcomeDue` already reads; `ForeverSixtyDB` is a per-account SavedVariable). It replaces the three chat lines.
- When: 5 s after the first `PLAYER_ENTERING_WORLD` of that login, never in combat (waits for `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED`), never inside an instance (waits for the next zone change outside).
- Frame: 380 wide, the client's dark frame with the class crest portrait (one crest language), title `Forever Sixty is on`, close X, centred a third of the way down the screen, Escape closes (`UISpecialFrames`).
- Three pointers, each an illustration drawn from the real textures, a gold heading and one white sentence:
  1. A bag slot with Ranger Bow and the green arrow: **A green arrow on a bag item.** `It beats what you wear. Hover it to see by how much.`
  2. A talent icon with the gold ring and a plate: **A gold ring in your talents.** `Your next point. The numbers are your build's order.`
  3. The chip with its ring: **This line by your minimap.** `Your next step. Click it for upgrades, talents and rotation.`
- Then: `Hold Shift over any tooltip for the details.` (Shift in gold).
- Footer: muted `/fs help shows this again` left; `Got it` button right. `Got it`, X and Escape all stamp `welcomedAt`; a `/reload` before any of them shows it again.
- `/fs help` opens this panel and prints the command list to chat below it (the list is `settingsCommands`, unchanged). `/fs welcome` (testers) stays as an alias.

### 3.2 The chip pulse (`chip-pulse.png`)

- Until the chip is clicked once on the account (`ForeverSixtyDB.chipOpenedAt` unset), a gold ring (ffd100) breathes around the chip: alpha 0.3 to 1.0 and back in 1.6 s, ease in and out, through an `AnimationGroup` (no `OnUpdate`), looping.
- It stops for good on the first click (stamping `chipOpenedAt`), and pauses while the chip is hidden (combat, talent window open, right-click hidden).
- The existing new-point ring (a few seconds when a point arrives) still plays after that.
- The chip itself: full opacity always; never an icon without words. Idle text: the next talent level (`Next talent at 25`), else `Forever Sixty`.

### 3.3 Settings

The `Show the minimap button` switch stays, default off for new installs (existing installs keep their choice). The `Show the Forever Sixty line` switch's hint becomes `Your next step beside the minimap. Click it to open Forever Sixty. Shift-drag moves it.`

## 4. The addon panel, the in-game home

### 4.1 What it is

One frame that answers "what should I do next on this character", opened from the chip. It replaces the character-panel flyout's content and becomes the target of the keybind and the compartment entry.

### 4.2 Frame

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | 340 wide; height fits the content, at most 560; never scrolls (regions shed rows in the order in §4.4) |
| Look | The 1.60 client's dark frame: bronze rim, title bar `Forever Sixty` in gold, class crest portrait over the top-left corner, red close X (the character pane's own look) |
| Head | Name in class colour; muted fact line `Level 24 Hunter · Employed Millennials` |
| Anchor, from the chip | TOPRIGHT to the chip's BOTTOMLEFT, clamped to the screen |
| Anchor, from the character pane | TOPLEFT to the pane's TOPRIGHT (the flyout's anchor today, clearing `CharacterFrameModeTabs`) |
| Movable | Drag the title bar; the spot is saved per account in `ForeverSixtyDB.panel = { point, x, y }` and wins over both anchors once set. Settings' `Reset positions` clears it |
| Closable | X, Escape (`UISpecialFrames`), the chip click again, the keybind again |
| Keybind | The existing `bindingToggle` ("Toggle Forever Sixty", `Bindings.xml`) toggles the panel; unbound by default |
| Combat | Closes at `PLAYER_REGEN_DISABLED`; cannot open in combat (the chip is hidden then; the keybind prints nothing and does nothing). Equip buttons are disabled while the client refuses to equip |
| Strata | `MEDIUM`, below the client's dialogs |
| Built | On first open, never at load; redraws on the chip's events while shown |

### 4.3 Contents, in priority order

Each region has a gold heading and a muted right-hand note; one rule between regions.

1. **Tonight** (only when the inbox holds a raid starting within 18 hours, or running). `raid_assignment` gives the zone, title, start, slot label and purpose, and `with`.
   - Heading note: `8:00 pm · in 3 h 12 min` (the client's local clock; `RaidAssign.epochOf`).
   - Row: the raid's icon, zone name, and the sign-up answer right-aligned: `You're In` (green), `Late` (gold), `Bench` (white), `Out` (grey), `Not answered` (gold). Under it, muted: `as Damage · answer on the app or the site` (the addon never sends; E7: who and as what).
   - `Your job: <label>` in gold, the purpose in muted text, then `With Thornhide, Brightwen and 7 more.`
   - **Contract gap:** today the inbox carries only `raid_assignment` (one message per slot held). The sign-up answer, a raid with no job yet, and "as Damage" need a new flat inbox message, proposed here as `raid_signup` `{ raid_id, raid, title, starts_at, answer: in|late|bench|out|none, role, late_minutes? }`, one per upcoming raid of the character's guild in the next 7 days. Until the API and companion carry it, the region shows only when a job exists and the answer row reads `On the roster` (an assignment is only sent to In, Late or Bench, so that is true).
   - The day after a raid (no upcoming raid, a `last_raid` message under 2 days old): the region is **Last raid** instead: date, each boss's percentile in parse colours, the biggest loss for the worst boss (today's chip `lastnight` state and the flyout's region).
2. **Upgrades.** At most three rows: icon, name in quality colour, gain right in green, muted line `<slot> · <source>` (the tooltip's case 16 source). A bag upgrade leads with the arrow `In your bags ·` and an `Equip` button (today's chip action). Note right: `Beast Mastery estimate` or `your sim · 2 days ago`. Under fewer than three: `Your other slots are best for your level.` Below the first band: `Gear picks start at level 20. Until then, the green arrow on your bags marks anything that beats what you wear.` Rows are the same `ItemTooltip.sectionFor` Next lines the paper doll reads (one verdict source).
3. **Talents.** Note right: `12 of 15 on plan · 3 off plan` (off plan in the warning colour, hover names the talents). Row: the talent's client icon, `Level 25: Lethal Attacks`, `1 of 5` right, muted build name, and a `Talents` button (opens the talent window, which shows the ring). With points waiting: `Spend 2 now`. Finished: a single heading row `Beast Mastery raid · 51 of 51 on plan`. Not 10 yet and no build: replaced by **Pick your path** (three spec rows with their tab icons, the one-line playstyle from `specPlaystyle`, a `Pick` button each, and `Loads that path's leveling build. Your first talent point comes at level 10.`).
4. **Rotation at <level>.** The top four lines of `Rotation.model` for the build's spec and level: spell icon, name (rank muted where the client shows ranks), and the condition's first clause; the full condition on hover. One dimmed row for the next ability learned (`Rapid Fire · At level 26`). Note right: `hover for the full rule`.
5. **Forever Score.** One line: `Forever Score 1840` in the tier colour, right `Epic · as of 7 Nov` muted; hover gives the five parts. Absent with no record.
6. **Footer.** Two buttons: `Change build` (the build dialog's paste tab) and, under 60, `Copy for the site` (the export); at 60 `Sim me` (queues a sim through the outbox; becomes `Sim queued` muted).

### 4.4 Height budget

When the content would pass 560: rotation drops to three rows, then upgrades to two, then the rotation's next-ability row goes. Tonight and the next talent never drop.

### 4.5 Empty states

| State | Panel shows |
|---|---|
| Fresh install, under 10, no build | Pick your path; Rotation at your level (the lowest band covers it); Upgrades' "picks start" sentence; a muted closing line `Your raids and Forever Score show here once the companion app has run.` |
| No companion data yet (no ack in the inbox) | Everything that comes from Data.lua (upgrades, talents, rotation); no Tonight, no Score; the closing line above |

| No guild | No Tonight region (never an empty card) |
| In a guild, no raid planned | No Tonight region |
| Level 60, build complete, all slots best | Upgrades reads `Every slot is best for your build.`; Talents is the single finished row |

### 4.6 The character panel tab: merge

Recommendation: **merge**. The character pane's `Forever Sixty` button stays and opens this same panel docked to the pane's right edge; the flyout and its own region code go. Reasons: one home cannot drift from another; the flyout's targets, rotation and build are this panel's regions 2 to 4 already; its Sim and Last raid regions move into the footer and the Tonight region. The docked panel follows the pane's show and hide (it is anchored, not parented, so the taint rule holds: the addon still never writes into `CharacterFrame`).

### 4.7 The minimap and the chip

The chip is the addon's one presence beside the minimap. The minimap edge button is off by default (§1.6). The addon compartment entry opens the panel. Right-click on the chip keeps `Hide until next level` (at 60: until `/fs chip`).

## Appendix A: purpose audit

Each surface: its moment and the 3-second answer, then one line per element: **element**; the player (R reads, D decides, T taps or clicks); what breaks without it.

**Item tooltip** (hovering gear anywhere; "is this better for me, and by how much")
- **Verdict line**; R, D (equip, keep, sell, roll); the addon has no answer and the player reads stats by hand.
- **Green arrow on the verdict**; R; the tooltip and the bag mark stop reading as one signal.
- **Support line: source under a next upgrade**; R, D (where to go); the player knows what to want but not where it drops.
- **Support line: `Your best in slot until level N`**; R; the player cannot tell a good upgrade from the one to keep for ten levels.
- **Support line: `Better: <pick>`**; D (equip now or hold out); a player equips a small upgrade and stops looking for the real one.
- **Support line: `At level N: <pick>` under a worn pick**; R; the next chase is invisible until the band changes.
- **`over <worn>` on two-slot items**; D (which ring to replace); the player swaps out the wrong ring.
- **Shift: breakdown**; R when doubting a number; a sceptic cannot check the figure.
- **Shift: alternatives**; D when the pick is out of reach; the player has no fallback without the site.
- **Shift: attribution**; R; nobody knows whether a number is the shipped estimate or their own sim.
- **Shift: cap, enchant, build-list lines**; R; rare caveats, wrong conclusions without them.
- **Compare tooltip titled Next upgrade**; R; the next upgrade is a name without its real stats (tenet 2).

**Quest reward** (turning in; "which one do I take")
- **Green arrow on the best reward**; D, T; the player hovers all three and compares by hand.
- **`Best in slot` label**; D; a band pick looks like any other upgrade.
- **`Sells for most`**; D, T; with nothing useful, the player opens three tooltips to find the vendor price.

**Player tooltip** (hovering someone in town or LFG; "is this player any good")
- **`Forever Score 1240` in tier colour**; R, D (invite or not); the addon's one social signal disappears.
- **Date when older than 7 days**; R; an old score reads as current.
- **Shift: five parts**; R; a leader cannot see whether the score is gear or parses.
- **Shift: live line, link hint**; R, T; the guildmate's current gap and the way to their page are lost.

**Bag and paper-doll marks** (bags or character pane open; "what should I put on")
- **Green arrow**; R, T; upgrades sit unnoticed in the bags.
- **Gold corner**; R; a slot worth chasing looks the same as a finished one.

**Chip** (any time out of combat; "what is my next step")
- **The line**; R; the next step needs a window open.
- **The pulse until first click**; R, T; the owner's own failure: nobody finds it.
- **`+N` badge**; R; more than one waiting thing reads as one.
- **Hover's three lines**; R; the click's meaning and the way to hide it are unknown.

**Welcome** (first login; "what did I just install")
- **Three pointers**; R; the arrow, ring and chip are unexplained marks.
- **Shift sentence**; R; every detail behind Shift is unreachable.
- **`/fs help shows this again`**; R; a dismissed welcome is gone for good.
- **Got it**; T; no way to close it but the X.

**Addon panel** (chip clicked, in town or between pulls; "what should I do next on this character")
- **Head (crest, name, level, guild)**; R; on an alt, the player is unsure whose panel this is.
- **Tonight: time and countdown**; R; raid time needs the app or the calendar.
- **Tonight: the answer row and `as Damage`**; R; the player cannot tell whether they answered (E7).
- **Tonight: job and purpose**; R; the job is only in a chat line at zone-in.
- **Tonight: with whom**; R; the player does not know who to coordinate with.
- **Last raid (the day after)**; R; the chip's one line is the only report in game.
- **Upgrades rows with source**; R, D; the next session has no target.
- **`In your bags` row with Equip**; T; the fastest gain sits in the bags.
- **Upgrades note (estimate or your sim)**; R; the source of the gains is unknown.
- **Talents row and button**; R, T; the next point needs the talent window.
- **Off-plan count**; R, D (respec); off-plan points go unnoticed.
- **Pick your path**; D, T; a new player has no build and the addon has nothing to say.
- **Rotation rows**; R; the player at a new level does not know what changed.
- **Next-ability dimmed row**; R; a new spell arrives unannounced.
- **Forever Score line**; R; the player's own standing needs the site.
- **Change build**; T; switching builds needs `/fs`.
- **Copy for the site / Sim me**; T; the export and the sim request need commands.
- **Companion line (fresh install)**; R; empty regions look broken rather than pending.

**Talent marks** (talent window open; "where does my next point go")
- **Gold ring**; R, T; the next node is found by reading the build.
- **Plates on points still to take**; R; the order beyond the next point is lost.
- **Red outline off plan**; R, D; mistakes are invisible.
- **Strip**; R; the plan's progress needs the panel.
- **Node tooltip line**; R; which points of the plan sit on this node is unknown.

**Group panel and guild tab** (raid leader before the pull; officer reviewing the roster)
- Rows and ready check as audited in §1.9; R, D; the leader checks each member by hand.
