## Copy rule (owner, 2026-11-08)

"Adding unnecessary explanations is a dead giveaway it's AI." A number gets its unit and nothing else; any qualifier the context already makes clear is cut. On the tooltips: the green arrow and the figure are the upgrade (`+3.5 DPS`, never `Upgrade +3.5 DPS`); a loss is its figure (`−1.3 DPS`); `Best in slot`, never `Your best in slot`; a level refusal is its reason (`Mail armor at level 40`); `over <worn>` only on rings and trinkets. Where a table below still shows an older wording, this section wins. Every surface passes ai-slop-reviewer alongside ux-designer and wow-player before it lands.

## 2. Tooltip redesign

### 2.1 The grammar, one for every tooltip

- **G1. One answer, then at most one supporting line.** No header, no blank row. Our lines follow the client's last line directly, like its own coloured lines. Everything else is behind Shift.
- **G2. Colour is meaning**, in the client's own font colours:
  - green `GREEN_FONT_COLOR` (1aff1a): do this (an upgrade, in your bags);
  - gold `NORMAL_FONT_COLOR` (ffd100): yours (your best in slot, your job tonight);
  - white `HIGHLIGHT_FONT_COLOR` (ffffff): information (next upgrade, no upgrade until);
  - grey `Theme.tooltipMuted` (b8b8b8, lighter than the client's 808080 for contrast on the tooltip ground): no, or detail;
  - item names always in their quality colour; gains always green.
- **G3. The green arrow means upgrade everywhere**: the client's `bags-greenarrow` atlas at 12 px (`|A:bags-greenarrow:12:12|a`) leads every green upgrade line, as it marks the bag item, the paper-doll slot, the quest reward and the roll frame.
- **G4. Shift is details**, the same key the client uses to compare. No tooltip ever prints a "hold Shift" hint; the welcome teaches it once.
- **G5. Silence over noise.** No data, not gear, not loaded yet: no line at all. Never `…`, never a zero.

### 2.2 Item verdicts, exact text

Precedence top to bottom; the first that applies is the verdict. `N` is the next band's level; gains are `Progress.roundedGain` with the existing 1.0 floor (`Progress.MIN_GAIN`), one decimal for DPS and HPS, none for score.

### 2.3 The Shift block, exact order

Appended under the support line, all grey unless named:

1. The figure's breakdown, every stat that moves including Stamina and Armor: `+7 Agility, +2 Stamina, +6 Armor` in the client's own stat words (no brackets); under a worn item's next upgrade it names that item, `Ranger Bow: +1 Agility`. A weapon's own DPS is never listed (owner, 2026-11-08): `+3.4 weapon DPS` under a `+3.5 DPS` headline read as one number twice. Armor and Stamina are listed even though the weights price them at nothing for a damage spec, so a leveller sees what the swap costs in survival.
2. Alternatives, at most two, one per line: `Also good: <item> +2.2 DPS (gun)` (an alternative under the 1.0 floor is left out), the second aligned under the first item name; a row never crosses the tooltip's border (it shortens the name to the client's own widest line). A ranged item whose type differs from the worn one carries its type in the game's words, `(gun)`, `(bow)`, `(crossbow)`, `(thrown)`, `(wand)`, on alternatives and on the Next line alike; `(thrown)` marks a thrown weapon in the ranged slot (item subclass 16, e.g. Cracked Blacksmith Hammer, inventory type 25 in the 1.60 data).
3. (Cut by the copy rule: the attribution already says whose numbers these are.)
4. A cap: `Hit capped: more is wasted`.
5. `Enchant not counted` when the worn enchant could not be read.
6. Last, dimmer (8a8a8a): `Forever Sixty · Beast Mastery` or `Forever Sixty · Beast Mastery, your sim`.

Holding or releasing Shift redraws the tooltip through the client's own `SetHyperlink` from an event frame (the mechanism `PlayerTooltip.lua` already uses). No `OnUpdate`.

## 3. First-run welcome and the chip pulse

### 3.1 The welcome callout (`welcome.png`)

- Shown once per account: `ForeverSixtyDB.welcomedAt` (the field `SiteStatus.welcomeDue` already reads; `ForeverSixtyDB` is a per-account SavedVariable). It replaces the three chat lines.
- When: 5 s after the first `PLAYER_ENTERING_WORLD` of that login, never in combat (waits for `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED`), never inside an instance (waits for the next zone change outside).
- Form: a callout in the client's tooltip style (`GameTooltip`'s backdrop, the same ground as every tooltip), 300 wide, docked to the left of the chip with a pointer at it. No portrait, no window chrome, no modal: it never covers the middle of the screen and nothing under it stops working.
- Content, in order:
  1. `Forever Sixty is on` in gold.
  2. `This line is your next step. Click it for your upgrades, talents and rotation.` (the chip beside it is the illustration).
  3. A bag slot with Ranger Bow and the green arrow: `Green arrow: it beats what you wear. Hover for how much.`
  4. A talent icon with the gold ring and a plate: `Gold ring: your next talent point.`
  5. `Hold Shift on a tooltip for the details.` (Shift in gold).
  6. Muted `/fs help shows this again` left; `Got it` right.
- Dismissed for good (stamps `welcomedAt`) by `Got it`, Escape, or a click on the chip. Entering combat hides it without stamping; it comes back after combat once, then not again that login.
- `/fs help` shows the callout again and prints the command list to chat (`settingsCommands`, unchanged). `/fs welcome` (testers) stays as an alias.

### 3.2 The chip pulse (`chip-pulse.png`)

- While `ForeverSixtyDB.chipSeenAt` is unset, a gold ring (ffd100) breathes around the chip after each login: alpha 0.3 to 1.0 and back in 1.6 s, ease in and out, five cycles (8 s), then still. An `AnimationGroup` with a loop count, no `OnUpdate`.
- `chipSeenAt` is stamped by the first click on the chip or the first hover over it; after that the first-run pulse never plays again on the account. If neither happens, the pulse gives up after its third login (`ForeverSixtyDB.chipPulses` counts them).
- Never in combat; it does not play while the chip is hidden (talent window open, right-click hidden).
- The existing new-point ring (a few seconds when a point arrives) keeps working after that.
- The chip itself: full opacity always; never an icon without words. Idle text: the next talent level (`Next talent at 25`), else `Forever Sixty`. An actionable state (`Take`, `Equip`, the raid) always outranks the idle line.
- New chip state, highest priority from 18 hours before a raid's start until 6 hours after it (`RaidAssign.SHOWN_FOR_SECONDS`): `Tonight 8:00 pm: Onyxia's Lair`, from the same inbox data as the panel's Tonight region. Once the panel has been opened that day, the raid drops back to normal priority for the session until one hour before the pull.
- Hover, two dim lines: `Click: open Forever Sixty` and `Right-click: hide until level N` (`hide until /fs chip` at 60). The chip's own line repeats first only when the chip had to shorten it.

### 3.3 Settings

- The `Show the minimap button` switch stays, default off for new installs (existing installs keep their choice).
- The `Show the Forever Sixty line` switch's hint becomes `Your next step beside the minimap. Click it to open Forever Sixty. Shift-drag moves it.`
- New, first on the page: `Tooltips only`, hint `Keeps the gear and score lines on tooltips and turns off the line by the minimap, the marks and the welcome.` Off by default. On, it sets the chip, talent-mark and bag-mark switches off in one step (their own switches show the result and can be turned back on one by one).

## 4. The addon panel, the in-game home

### 4.1 What it is

One frame that answers "what should I do next on this character", opened from the chip. It replaces the character-panel flyout's content and becomes the target of the keybind and the compartment entry.

### 4.2 Frame

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | 340 wide; height fits the content, at most 520; never scrolls (regions shed rows in the order in §4.4) |
| Look | The 1.60 client's dark frame: bronze rim, title bar `Forever Sixty` in gold, class crest portrait over the top-left corner, red close X (the character pane's own look) |
| Head | Name in class colour, `Forever Score 1240` right-aligned and muted with the number in its tier colour (absent with no record or no rated fights); muted fact line `Level 24 Hunter · Employed Millennials` |
| Anchor, from the chip | TOPRIGHT to the chip's BOTTOMLEFT, clamped to the screen |
| Anchor, from the character pane | TOPLEFT to the pane's TOPRIGHT (the flyout's anchor today, clearing `CharacterFrameModeTabs`) |
| Movable | Drag the title bar; the spot is saved per account in `ForeverSixtyDB.panel = { point, x, y }` and wins over both anchors once set. Settings' `Reset positions` clears it |
| Closable | X, Escape (`UISpecialFrames`), the chip click again, the keybind again |
| Keybind | The existing `bindingToggle` ("Toggle Forever Sixty", `Bindings.xml`) toggles the panel; unbound by default |
| Combat | Closes at `PLAYER_REGEN_DISABLED`; cannot open in combat (the chip is hidden then; the keybind does nothing). Equip buttons are disabled while the client refuses to equip |
| Strata | `MEDIUM`, below the client's dialogs |
| Built | On first open, never at load; redraws on the chip's events while shown |

No footer. The build dialog is one click away from the Talents row (`Change`), the export runs on its own at logout for the companion (and `/fs copy` remains), and the sim request sits in the Upgrades note.

### 4.3 Contents, in priority order

Each region has a gold heading and a muted right-hand note; one rule between regions.

1. **Tonight** (only when the inbox holds a raid starting within 18 hours, or running).
   - Heading note: `8:00 pm · in 3 h 12 min` (the client's local clock; `RaidAssign.epochOf`).
   - Row: the raid's icon, zone name, the answer right-aligned: `You're In` (green), `Late` (gold), `Bench` (white), `Out` (grey), `Not answered` (gold); under it, muted, `as Ashvale, Damage` (E7: who and as what).
   - Not answered: four buttons `In`, `Late`, `Bench`, `Out` under the row (the apps' and Discord's four answers, in their order). The addon never touches the network: a click writes a `raid_answer` entry (below) to the companion outbox (`ForeverSixtyDB.outbox`, contract 4, the path `Sim me` already uses) and the answer reads `In, not sent yet` (white) with a muted line `Sends when the companion app runs` under `as Ashvale, Damage`, and the buttons go, until the inbox's next sign-up message confirms it, then `You're In`. Answered: no buttons and no line about the app.
   - `Your job: <label>` in gold on one line, then `· with <name>` muted when the slot has one or two other names (`with`), nothing when it has more. The slot's purpose is the row's hover, not printed.
   - **Contracts** (the API and companion lanes build them; the addon reads them behind a feature check and renders without them). Stated once in `docs/contracts/addon-v2.md` (contract 3 `raid_signup`, contract 4 `raid_answer`); summarised here:
     - Inbox `raid_signup`, one per raid of the character's guild that is planned or locked, not over, and starts within the next 7 days, whether or not the character answered. Flat strings and numbers, so today's companion carries it through as an unknown type:
       `{ type = "raid_signup", character = "us/normal/obnoxious", raid_id = 42, raid = "Onyxia's Lair", title = "Onyxia", starts_at = "2026-12-10T02:00:00Z", answer = "in" | "late" | "bench" | "out" | "none", role = "tank" | "healer" | "dps" | nil, late_minutes = 15 | 30 | 60 | nil, answered_at = "2026-12-08T19:04:00Z" | nil }`
       `answer = "none"` when the character has no sign-up row; `role` is the stored sign-up's role (absent with no row); `late_minutes` only with `late` and only when the answer said; `answered_at` is the row's `updated_at`, RFC 3339 UTC, absent with no row.
     - Inbox top level `accepts = { "raid_answer" }`: the companion writes it once it uploads `raid_answer` entries. This is the feature check: the addon draws the buttons only when the inbox lists `raid_answer` in `accepts` **and** holds a `raid_signup` for that raid.
     - Outbox `raid_answer`, a list, one entry per raid answered in game since the companion last cleared it (a second click for the same raid replaces the first):
       `raid_answer = { { character = "Obnoxious-Yell", raid_id = 42, answer = "in" | "late" | "bench" | "out", late_minutes = nil, answered_at = 1796753040 }, ... } | nil`
       `character` is the export's `<name>-<realm>` form (contract 4); `answered_at` is the client's `time()` (Unix seconds, UTC). The panel's `Late` sends no `late_minutes`. The API applies an entry only when the raid is still open to the character and `answered_at` is later than the stored row's `updated_at` (a newer answer from the app or Discord wins); the role is the stored row's, else the character's spec role. The companion uploads the list, then removes the entries the site accepted or refused; it never edits the rest of the outbox.
     - Confirmation: the panel's pending answer clears when a `raid_signup` for the raid carries an `answered_at` at or after the click (the answer shown is then the inbox's, whatever won), or when the raid is over.
   - Without the contracts (no `accepts`, or no `raid_signup` for the raid): the region shows only when a `raid_assignment` exists, the answer reads `On the roster` (an assignment is only sent to In, Late or Bench) and no buttons show.
   - The day after a raid (no upcoming raid, a `last_raid` message under 2 days old): the region is **Last raid** instead: date, each boss's percentile in parse colours, the biggest loss for the worst boss (today's chip `lastnight` state and the flyout's region).
2. **Upgrades.** At most three rows: icon, name in quality colour, gain right in green, muted line `<slot> · <source>` (the tooltip's case 16 source), plus `· level 27` when the item's required level is above the character's. A bag upgrade leads with the arrow `In your bags ·` and an `Equip` button (today's chip action). Note right: nothing on the shipped estimate (the leveller read "estimate" as jargon); `your sim · 2 days ago · Sim again` once the character has a sim, where `Sim again` queues one through the outbox and becomes `Sim queued`. Under fewer than three: `Your other slots are best for your level.` All best: `Every slot is best for your build.` Below the first band: `Gear picks start at level 20. Until then, the green arrow on your bags marks anything that beats what you wear.` Rows are the same `ItemTooltip.sectionFor` Next lines the paper doll reads (one verdict source). Rows are never cut with an ellipsis: a source too long for one line wraps to a second.
3. **Talents.** Note right: `12 of 15 on plan · 3 off plan` (off plan in the warning colour, hover names the talents). Row: the talent's client icon, `Level 25: Lethal Attacks`, `1 of 5` right, muted `<build name> · Change` (Change opens the build dialog), and a `Talents` button (opens the talent window, which shows the ring). With points waiting: `Spend 2 now`. A finished build on plan: the region is not shown. Finished with points off plan: the heading and its off-plan note only. Under 10 with no build: **Pick your path** (three spec rows with their tab icons, the one-line playstyle from `specPlaystyle`, a `Pick` button each, and `Loads that path's leveling build. Your first talent point comes at level 10.`).
4. **Rotation at <level>.** Under 60: every line of `Rotation.model`'s band for the build's spec and level (six at most): spell icon, name, and the condition's first clause; the full condition on hover. One dimmed row for the next ability learned (`Rapid Fire · At level 26`). Note right: `hover for the full rule`. At 60: a single collapsed heading `Rotation at 60 · show` (expands in place, remembered per character), except for the first open after a new rank is learned, when it opens expanded.
5. There is no separate Score region: the score sits in the head.

Accuracy note for the build lane (tenet 8): the levelling mock's rotation is `Data.lua` `rotations["hunter-beast-mastery"]` band 22 verbatim (Forever's hunters have Aimed Shot in that band), and `Lethal Attacks` is the Beast Mastery tier-1 talent 105011 in `data/builds/1.60.1.70790/talents/hunter.json` (icon `ability_searingarrow`). The leveller persona questioned both from retail Classic memory; the client data is the source. Checked 2026-11-08 against `data/builds/1.60.1.70790/trainables/hunter.json` (built from `SkillLineAbility`): Aimed Shot is a trainer spell on the Marksmanship line (`source: skill_line_ability`, rank 1 at level 20, AcquireMethod not 3), not a talent, so a Beast Mastery hunter has it at 22 and the row stays. The gate is general, not a one-off: no rotation row or talent step reaches the panel unless its spell is a trainable at or below the character's level (`skill_line_ability`, AcquireMethod 0 or 2) or a talent the loaded build has taken by that level; a row that fails is fixed in the data (the Data.lua generator and its test), never only hidden in the panel. A condition's first clause names what a shared cooldown is shared with (`When its cooldown, shared with Multi-Shot, is up`). Spec lines on Pick your path wrap to two lines rather than end in an ellipsis.

### 4.4 Height budget

At most 520 tall. When the content would pass it: the rotation drops to four rows, then upgrades to two, then the rotation's next-ability row goes. Tonight and the next talent never drop.

### 4.5 Empty states

| State | Panel shows |
|---|---|
| Fresh install, under 10, no build | Pick your path; Rotation at your level (the lowest band covers it); Upgrades' "picks start" sentence |
| No companion data yet (no ack in the inbox) | Everything that comes from Data.lua (upgrades, talents, rotation); no Tonight, no score in the head |
| Data addon missing or old | No score in the head; nothing else changes |
| No guild | No Tonight region (never an empty card) |
| In a guild, no raid in the next 18 hours | No Tonight region |
| Level 60, build complete on plan, all slots best | Upgrades reads `Every slot is best for your build.`; no Talents region; Rotation collapsed |
| In combat | Closed |

### 4.6 The character panel tab: merge

Recommendation: **merge**. The character pane's `Forever Sixty` button stays and opens this same panel docked to the pane's right edge; the flyout and its own region code go. Reasons: one home cannot drift from another; the flyout's targets, rotation and build are this panel's regions 2 to 4 already; its Sim region becomes the Upgrades note's `Sim again` and its Last raid region the Tonight region's day-after form. The docked panel follows the pane's show and hide (it is anchored, not parented, so the taint rule holds: the addon still never writes into `CharacterFrame`).

### 4.7 The minimap and the chip

The chip is the addon's one presence beside the minimap. The minimap edge button is off by default (§1.6). The addon compartment entry opens the panel. Right-click on the chip keeps `Hide until next level` (at 60: until `/fs chip`).

## Appendix A: purpose audit

Each surface: its moment and the 3-second answer, then one line per element: **element**; the player (R reads, D decides, T taps or clicks); what breaks without it.

**Item tooltip** (hovering gear anywhere; "is this better for me, and by how much")
- **Verdict line**; R, D (equip, keep, sell, roll); the addon has no answer and the player reads stats by hand.
- **Green arrow on the verdict**; R; the tooltip and the bag mark stop reading as one signal.
- **Support line: source under a next upgrade**; R, D (where to go); the player knows what to want but not where it drops.
- **Support line: `Best in slot until level N`**; R; the player cannot tell a good upgrade from the one to keep for ten levels.
- **Support line: `Better: <pick>`**; D (equip now or hold out); a player equips a small upgrade and stops looking for the real one.
- **Support line: `At level N: <pick>` under a worn pick**; R; the next chase is invisible until the band changes.
- **`over <worn>` on rings and trinkets**; D (which ring to replace); the player swaps out the wrong ring.
- **Shift: breakdown**; R when doubting a number; a sceptic cannot check the figure.
- **Shift: alternatives**; D when the pick is out of reach; the player has no fallback without the site.
- **Shift: attribution**; R; nobody knows whether a number is the shipped estimate or their own sim.
- **Shift: cap, enchant, build-list lines**; R; rare caveats, wrong conclusions without them.
- **Compare tooltip titled Next upgrade**; R; the next upgrade is a name without its real stats (tenet 2).

**Loot roll** (a drop in a group; "do I need this")
- **Green arrow on the icon**; R, D; the Need decision waits for a tooltip.
- **`+5.8 DPS`, `Best in slot`, `Not for you` under the name**; D; the player hovers mid-fight or rolls blind.

**Quest reward** (turning in; "which one do I take")
- **Green arrow on the best reward**; D, T; the player hovers all three and compares by hand.
- **`Best in slot` label**; D; a band pick looks like any other upgrade.
- **`Sells for most`**; D, T; with nothing useful, the player opens three tooltips to find the vendor price.

**Player tooltip** (hovering someone in town or LFG; "is this player any good")
- **`Forever Score 1240` in tier colour**; R, D (invite or not); the addon's one social signal disappears.
- **Date when older than 7 days**; R; an old score reads as current.
- **Shift: five parts**; R; a leader cannot see whether the score is gear or parses.

**Bag and paper-doll marks** (bags or character pane open; "what should I put on")
- **Green arrow**; R, T; upgrades sit unnoticed in the bags.
- **Gold corner**; R; a slot worth chasing looks the same as a finished one.

**Chip** (any time out of combat; "what is my next step")
- **The line**; R; the next step needs a window open.
- **The bounded pulse (five cycles a login, until clicked or hovered, three logins at most)**; R, T; the owner's own failure: nobody finds it.
- **Raid-day line**; R; a raider's next step on raid day is invisible without opening anything.
- **`+N` badge**; R; more than one waiting thing reads as one.
- **Hover's three lines**; R; the click's meaning and the way to hide it are unknown.

**Welcome callout** (first login; "what did I just install")
- **Docked to the chip**; R; the chip it explains is the illustration, and nothing in the middle of the screen blocks play.
- **Two pointers (arrow, ring)**; R; the bag and talent marks are unexplained.
- **Shift sentence**; R; every detail behind Shift is unreachable.
- **`/fs help shows this again`**; R; a dismissed welcome is gone for good.
- **Got it**; T; no way to close it but the X.

**Addon panel** (chip clicked, in town or between pulls; "what should I do next on this character")
- **Head (crest, name, level, guild)**; R; on an alt, the player is unsure whose panel this is.
- **Score in the head**; R; the player's own standing needs the site (one number, no region of its own).
- **Tonight: time and countdown**; R; raid time needs the app or the calendar.
- **Tonight: the answer row and `as Ashvale, Damage`**; R; the player cannot tell whether they answered, or for which character (E7).
- **Tonight: In, Late, Bench, Out (unanswered only)**; T; answering means alt-tabbing to the phone mid-session.
- **Tonight: the job line**; R; the job is only in a chat line at zone-in.
- **Tonight: `with` one or two names**; R; the player does not know their partner for the job.
- **Last raid (the day after)**; R; the chip's one line is the only report in game.
- **Upgrades rows with source**; R, D; the next session has no target.
- **`In your bags` row with Equip**; T; the fastest gain sits in the bags.
- **Upgrades note, `your sim · Sim again` (only with a sim)**; R, T; a raider cannot tell whether the gains are theirs, or refresh them.
- **Talents row and button**; R, T; the next point needs the talent window.
- **`Change` on the build line**; T; switching builds needs `/fs`.
- **Off-plan count**; R, D (respec); off-plan points go unnoticed.
- **Pick your path**; D, T; a new player has no build and the addon has nothing to say.
- **Rotation rows under 60**; R; the player at a new level does not know what changed.
- **Rotation collapsed at 60**; R when a rank changes; a raider sees four rows they know by heart.
- **Next-ability dimmed row**; R; a new spell arrives unannounced.

**Talent marks** (talent window open; "where does my next point go")
- **Gold ring**; R, T; the next node is found by reading the build.
- **Plates on points still to take**; R; the order beyond the next point is lost.
- **Red outline off plan**; R, D; mistakes are invisible.
- **Strip**; R; the plan's progress needs the panel.
- **Node tooltip line**; R; which points of the plan sit on this node is unknown.

**Group panel and guild tab** (raid leader before the pull; officer reviewing the roster)
- Rows and ready check as audited in §1.9; R, D; the leader checks each member by hand.
