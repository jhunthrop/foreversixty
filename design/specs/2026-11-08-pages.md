## 1. Diagnosis of the live pages (production 2026-11-08 at 390, 1440, 1920, 2000; `before/`, `measure.json`)

**/changelog**
1. **The entries are cut to their titles.** `changelog.astro` renders each entry as a `FeedRow` (date, source pill, title, `note`) and never renders the markdown body. 34 of 38 entries have a body (5,449 words in the folder); **22 have no `note`, so the reader sees only a one-line title** ("The log coach is here." with nothing under it). All 14 site entries are titles only. The page reads like a feed of headlines with the story thrown away.
2. **The title is a link to the first source**, so a site entry's headline opens `/guild` or `/premium` and a game entry's opens Wowhead or Blizzard in place; a second or third source (14 entries carry 2 to 5) is not reachable at all.
3. **A 900 px column** (`measure-wide`) in one gold-edged box at every width: at 2000 it starts at x 550 and uses 45% of the screen; at 1440 x 270 to 1,170.
4. **Old type:** a 44 px caps condensed H1 (`WHAT CHANGED`), 11 px caps pills, 13 px dates. 38 `THIS SITE` / `BLIZZARD` / `COMMUNITY` pills in 2 colours carry more ink than the entries.
5. **No way through it:** no grouping by month, no filter (game news and site changes are interleaved: 13 site rows sit above the first game row), no anchor to link one entry (each row has an `id`, nothing exposes it), no year (`20 Sept` will be ambiguous next September).
6. Signed in changes nothing. 1440: 13 rows in the first screen, page 3,325 px; 390: 6 rows, 6,235 px.

**Content pages** (`/terms`, `/privacy`, `/refunds`, `/about`, `/sources`, `/roadmap`)
7. **A caps eyebrow over a caps condensed title** (`LEGAL` / `PRIVACY POLICY`, `ABOUT` / `ROADMAP`), 26 px caps condensed section heads (8 on privacy), the old system.
8. **One 615 px column flush left in the 1,344 column** (x 96 to 711 at 1440; at 2000 x 376 to 991, 31% of the screen) with nothing beside it. Privacy is 2,337 px at 1440 and 3,559 at 390 with no contents list: the eighth section ("Requests and contact", where the email is) starts at y 1,716 at 1440 and y 2,300 at 390.
9. **The Log coach section has no anchor:** `CoachPrivacySection.astro` writes `<h2>` without an `id`, unlike every markdown heading (gap PG3).
10. **The three legal pages do not link to each other** above the footer; a player reading Terms who wants Refunds scrolls to the footer.
11. **Roadmap is stale and orphaned:** `Updated 14 Sept`, rows for a beta that ended and a launch that happened; no link on the site reaches `/roadmap` (not in the footer, header or any page). Content, not frame: question Q7.
12. **The `Content.astro` confidence sentence and Sources footer never draw:** no page in `src/content/pages` carries `confidence`, so `showSources` is false on all six. The machinery is dead on these pages (gap PG4).
13. What is right and stays: the markdown as written, the one Updated stamp, the article JSON-LD and `article:modified_time`, inline links in prose, the roadmap's table.

**404** (`/this-page-does-not-exist`, status 404)
14. **Two gold-edged cards with icon chips and caps titles** (`PICK YOUR CLASS`, `LOOK UP A CHARACTER`) under an eyebrow `NOT FOUND`.
15. **The class picker is nine 44 px crests with 10 px names** (Barlow 700 10 px: under the system's 12 px floor) and no specs; at 390 it wraps 5 + 4.
16. **Signed in changes nothing:** a signed-in Felsnap gets the same "Pick your class" as a stranger; the page never offers the player's own guide or BiS (tenet 14). `404-in-*` is pixel-identical to `404-*` below the header.
17. **The address that failed is not shown**, so a mistyped link cannot be spotted, and nothing suggests the page the player probably meant.
18. 1440: 1,156 px, both cards in the first screen; 390: 1,918 px, the Look up button at y 795.
19. **Unknown character, guild and report do not reach this page.** `/character/...`, `/guild/...` and `/reports/...` are Worker shells; their islands answer "not found" in place (`scoreCopy.notFoundLine`, `guildCopy` not-found, `GuildNotFound.svelte`). Only a report's data file falls through to the static 404 (`worker.ts` `serveReportFile`), which no player opens directly. So this spec covers an unknown path; the shells' not-found states belong to their own page specs.

**/b-unavailable** (served with 503 for `/b/:id` when the API is down)
20. A 720 px column centred inside the 1,344 column (x 360 at 1440, the only page that centres its text) with a caps condensed H1 and two 15 px muted paragraphs. Right in substance; wrong frame.

## 3. Reference surfaces, and what is borrowed

- **Blizzard's hotfix and patch-notes pages** (what a player opens for "what changed"): dated headings, newest first, each change in full under its date. Borrowed: newest first, the month as the heading, every entry's text shown. Not borrowed: the one long undifferentiated column.
- **GitHub's release pages** (the reference for a dated log on a wide screen): date and tag in a narrow left column, notes in the middle, assets at the side, a permalink per release. Borrowed: the `Dated` grammar (date column 120 | text 720 | lane), the date as the entry's permalink. Not borrowed: collapsed older releases.
- **Stripe's and GitHub's legal documents**: a sticky contents list at the left, numbered sections with anchors, one "Last updated" line, sibling policies one click away. Borrowed: the contents rail from 1024, heading anchors, the legal TabStrip. Not borrowed: a second date per section.
- **The in-game Character Info and the class picker players already trust** (our home's `Pick your class` rows): crest, class name in class colour, its specs. Borrowed for the 404 plate, row for row.
- **Our own system as shipped**: `PageHeader` with `lead` and the answer at the right (guides), `ControlBar` with counts in its segments (tiers), `Toc` and the bar's `On this page` (guides), `ReadingSection`, `Prose`, `RowsSlim`, `LaneBlock`, `Tag`, `TabStrip`, `Disclosure`, `Notice`, `FormRow`, `Field`, `Select`, `Button`, `Skeleton`, `RowGrid`, `Plate`.

## 4. The pages in primitives

### 4.1 /changelog (`Page layout="reading"`, contents from 1760)
Region order: `head` (`head-title`, `head-meta`), `controls`, then one `section-<month-year>` per month (`section-november-2026`, `section-october-2026`, `section-september-2026`), or `notes` when a filter is empty.
- **PageHeader.** Title `What changed` (the shipped H1, `--color-strong`). Meta `Updated {d MMM}` (`latest` of the entries' `updated`, as shipped; the one stamp). Lead: the page's meta description, verbatim (`Everything that changed in World of Warcraft: Forever and on this site, dated and sourced.`).
- **ControlBar** (`Changelog choices`, sticky). Group `Show` (label from 1280): segmented `All 38`, `Game 24`, `Site 14` (the count is the segment's `n`, computed at build time from the collection). Game = `blizzard`, `community`, `datamined`; Site = `site`. `end`: the `On this page` select (below 1760 only) opening a `Menu` of the months, the current one marked. Phone: one 48 px line `{All changes | Game changes | Site changes} · {count}` with `Change ▾`; open, the segmented control (44 px items) and the months as slim rows, stacked in place (`web-changelog-bar-390`). URL: `?show=game` / `?show=site` (`replaceState`; absent = All).
- **No Plate** (a reading page).
- **Sections:** one `DatedGroup` per month, newest first; head `{Month} {year}` (`November 2026`), meta `{n} change(s)` in the current filter. Each entry is a `Dated`:
  - Date: `formatDate` (`5 Nov`), `<time datetime="2026-11-05">`, a link to `#<file id>` (the shipped `id`, unchanged).
  - Tags: `Tag` muted with `pillLabelFor(kind)` (`This site`, `Blizzard`, `Community`); a returning visitor's entry adds the gold `Tag` `New` (4.1.1).
  - Title: the frontmatter `title`, not a link (decision CL2).
  - Body: the markdown body through the content pipeline (`render(entry)`), `Prose`. An entry with no body shows its `note` as the body, 16/26 (4 entries, all 12 Sept). An entry with a body shows its `note` in the lane as a caveat (Small, `--muted`) under the sources (12 entries: "Reported by one outlet…", "Supersedes the Sept 12 entry…").
  - Lane: `LaneBlock` `Sources` (`guidesCopy.sources`), one slim `Row` per unique source URL: the label as a gold 600 link (verbatim, `This site, Guild`), led by a muted `Tag` of the source's kind **only when it differs from the entry's kind** (a Blizzard entry's Wowhead source reads `Community`; its Blizzard source has no tag, the date column says it).
- **Contents rail** (from 1760, sticky 76 under the top, the guides' rail): `Toc` `On this page` with the months shown under the current filter; the month in view gold (`aria-current="location"`).

#### 4.1.1 New since your last visit (proposed, decision CL4)
On leaving the page the script stores the newest entry's date in `localStorage` `fs.changelogSeen`. On the next visit every entry dated after it gets `Tag` `New`. First visit, or storage blocked: no tags. No account, no API; signed in and out alike. The tag is set by a small inline script after the list (before first paint of the entries below the fold, and inside a column whose height the tag does not change: the date column is shorter than the text from 1280; below 1280 the tag sits on the date line), so nothing shifts.

### 4.4 404 (`Page layout="ledger"`)
Region order: `head` (`head-title`, `head-meta`, `head-answer` signed in), `notes` (only with a suggestion), `section-pick-your-class` (holding the `plate`), `section-look-up-a-character`.
- **PageHeader.** Title `No page here` (404.astro's PageHead title). Meta: the address that failed, `location.pathname` (Small, set by a two-line inline script before paint; empty for a no-script reader). Lead: `Nothing exists at this address. It may have moved as the game changes.` (404.astro's `job`). Answer (signed in): `CharacterAnswer`, read from the header selector's character (`selectedCharacter(readCurrent(), me)`, never switching it):

Signed out: no answer, no reserve.
- **Did you mean (proposed N3):** when the failed path is within edit distance 3 of a known page path, a `Notice` (neutral) `Did you mean` + a gold link `{page title} ›` (`web-404-suggest`: `/guides/hunter/beastmastery` -> `Beast Mastery Hunter guide ›`). The known paths are a build-time list (the static routes, every guide, class landing and BiS spec page, about 120 paths, under 5 KB inlined); the script runs inline before the plate paints, so the line never pushes the page after paint.
- **`Pick your class`** (`Section`, 18/24), holding **the page's one Plate**: `RowGrid` of 3 columns from 1024 (2 from 768, 1 below), nine class `Row`s in `classes.json` order: `ClassCrest` 44 (the ringed HD crest), the class name 15/20 700 in its class colour, sub the specs in tree order (`specs.json`, 13/18 muted), each row a link to `/guides/<class>` (as shipped). Accessible name `Pick your class`.
