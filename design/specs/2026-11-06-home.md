Author: home lane, 2026-11-06, build mode (no reviewer gate). The system is `2026-11-05-web-layout.md` (type §3.3, primitives §3A, the BiS worked example §5, guild §7). This page is built from the `ui/` primitives only and carries the mock's `data-region` names (`design/parity.md`).

## 1. Persona rulings (wow-player, 2026-11-06), and the lane's decisions on them

H1. **Signed in, the page is the selected character.** The Title is the character's name in the class colour; the meta line is `Level 34 Beast Mastery Hunter`, `Alliance Dwarf`, `Item level 31` (the facts the API has; a missing one is left out). No "Welcome back".
H2. **One next-step line** (the `summary` region, gold), the most urgent of, in order: the raid sign-up when the character's guild has a raid and the character is not In (`Next: Onyxia's Lair is Thursday and you are Late. Confirm your sign-up.`); unspent talent points (`Next: spend your 2 talent points, then get Hand of Edward the Odd.`); the top upgrade (`Next: get Hand of Edward the Odd from Zul'Farrak.`); else `Nothing waiting. You wear every pick for levels 30 to 39.` One link at its end to the page that owns the fix.
H3. **Upgrades: the top three**, each the BiS pick with its icon, slot, source and the DPS it adds over what the character wears, then `All 9 on best in slot ›`. The unit is DPS as on BiS (the persona's `%` is overruled: the site has one unit, decision D4). This is the page's one Plate.
H4. **Raid first at 60 in a guild.** A `Next raid` section above the upgrades: the raid row (title, local day and time, countdown, In and Late counts, the character's state as a Tag) linking to the raid page, then `Before Thursday` as a Checklist of the guild's `needs_before_next_raid`. Home links to the raid page for the sign-up; it does not write it (one job per page).
H5. **Talents only when points wait.** A `Talents` section with one row (`2 points to spend`, the earned and spent counts) linking to the planner. With nothing to spend it is left out. The next talent's name is shown only below 20, where the leveling plan names it (data gap above 20: the ladder carries ids only).
H6. **Simulator: the last sim, one row** (title, when, its DPS), or `Run your first sim` linking to the simulator.
H7. **Rail (from 1024): Guild, Your logs, Set up.** Guild only when in one (name, rank, attendance; links to the guild). Your logs: the character's own three newest reports, or one row that says how to upload. Set up: a Checklist of the Battle.net import and the addon export with their age (the export older than 7 days warns: upgrades are read from it). **Owner correction (2026-11-06):** Battle.net is the primary source of characters and gear and the upgrades are read from the imported gear; the addon is optional. Set up shows the gear source as a check (the Battle.net import; with no import, the addon export), then the addon as a neutral optional line with no status, `Addon and companion (optional)`, `Rates every item in your bags with your weights, and uploads your logs as you play.`, `Get the addon and companion ›`. Only no gear source at all warns: `No gear yet`, `Sign in with Battle.net again to import your characters, or paste an addon export.`, `Get set up ›`. No copy on home says the upgrades need the addon; `Paste an addon export` stays only as a fallback in the no-character state, and the signed-out step reads `Install the addon and the companion`. Owner rule (2026-11-06): every addon call to action names the addon and the companion (the shared words land in `web/src/lib/addon/cta-copy.ts`; home uses the same literals until then).
H8. **Signed out: crests are the front door.** Title `Play your class better.`, meta `Pick your class.` and `First raids 9 Dec` (from `dates.json`, gone once the date passes), the one primary button `Sign in with Battle.net`. Then `Pick your class`: nine Rows (crest, class name in its colour, its specs) in three columns, each to `/guides/<class>`.
H9. **Signed out: one class, every band.** The Plate is `Marksmanship Hunter, level 20 to 60`: one ItemRow per band (20 to 29 ... 60 raid-ready) with the band's ranged pick, its source and the set's DPS, to show what the product does from leveling to raid without a feature list. The data starts at band 20 (no band below 20 is ranked; named, not built). The class is the fixture-backed one so the gate runs on CI's fixture data.
H10. **How to start, two steps** (rail): `1 Sign in with Battle.net` (imports your characters, gear and level), `2 Install the addon and the companion` (the addon rates the items in your bags, the companion uploads your logs).
H11. **Signed in, no character:** Title `Play your class better.`, the summary `No character yet. Battle.net lists a character after you play it and log out.`, a `Get your character in` section (Check again as the one primary action, Paste an addon export, Get the addon and companion), then `Pick your class`. No empty cards, no zeroes.
H12. **Cut from home:** the launch countdown (raid time lives in the raid row), Look up a character (the header search owns it; the full box with region and ruleset moves to its own page, `/characters`, where "Look up another character" and the missing-character page now land, restyled when its turn comes), recent reports of everyone, Top guilds, the Latest feed and Help build this (footer links), the Another class row (the header selector switches character), the sky band, the dates strip, the example card.

- Phone: one column, head, summary, main sections, rail sections. The RowGrid is one column below 768, two to 1023, three from 1024.
- Wide: the one site column (1344 below 1760, 1760 at 1920, 1792 at 2000), rail 340 then 400. Nothing stretches: rows are full-width lists, the Plate is a list.
- New primitive: `RowGrid` (ui/RowGrid.svelte, test): a list of open Rows in `cols` columns with the Row hairlines, for the class picker and later any short link grid.
- `ws_prims` gains `ItemRow` and `RowGrid`, mock twins of the shipped primitives, and its `Checklist` mark now carries the shipped `role="img"` and its word.
- `ItemRow` (shipped) now prints its gain's `unit` after the figure (no caller passed one before).
- The upgrades read the BiS file through `fetchBisFileStrict` (a 404 is "no list", any other failure rejects), so the error state is real; the planner's hover keeps the quiet `fetchBisFile`.

## 4. Behaviour
- The signed-out page is server-rendered. The signed-in page is one island (`HomeSignedIn.svelte`, `client:idle-after-load`) over the session read the header uses (`createCharacterCardState`); with the session hint on (`html[data-session='1']`) the signed-out page is hidden before paint and the island's skeleton holds the place. A failed or empty `/v1/me` shows the signed-out page.
- Reads: `/v1/me`; the BiS band and item table through `cachedUpgradesFor` (cached an hour); `listMySims`; `listMyReports`; `fetchGuildHome(guild.id)` only when the character has a guild. Each section shows its own skeleton and its own failure; only the upgrades failure is mocked (the others fall back to their empty row).
- The header selector changes the character in place.
