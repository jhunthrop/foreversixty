Author: ux-designer, 2026-10-28. Product design (binding): `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-log-coach-design.md` §3, §5, §14, §16, §17. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-log-coach.md` Tasks 30–36 and Rulings 6 and 8. Mock: `design/mocks/log-coach/gen_log_coach.py`, boards in `design/mocks/renders/log-coach-*.png`. ## 0. Decisions

| # | Decision | Why (reference) |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | The Coach panel is a **tab** (`coach`) in `TABS`, directly after `rating`, so it sits in ModeBar's `More` menu next to Rating. It is not a drawer. | The report page's whole state is its query string (`url.ts`). Discord, the account line and the History link all need a URL that reopens the panel: `?fight=3&tab=coach&source=GUID`. A drawer needs its own state, a focus trap and a second scroll container inside `ReportView.svelte` (2220 lines). Rating already proves the pattern: a lazy tab with a reserved height (`REPORT_LAZY_MIN_H`). On a 360 px phone a drawer is a full screen anyway. |
| D2 | The row line is a **sibling strip under the viewer's own row**, inside the `li` of `ActorRow.svelte`, between the row `button` and `row-detail`. It is not inside the button. | The row is one `button` with `aria-expanded` that toggles the abilities. A link inside a button is invalid and breaks keyboard and touch. The strip is its own `a` and its own target. |
| D3 | The account "last raid" line is the **first child of the Characters `StatePanel`**, above the list, in a reserved slot. | The line is news about the account, so it comes before the rows. It loads separately (`GET /v1/me/coach/recent`). |
| D4 | The account line **does not hide** when there is no raid in 14 days. It shows `Last raid · no log in the last 14 days`, muted (ruled by the controller 2026-10-28: shown, never hidden). | Product §5.6 says hidden. A hidden line collapses a reserved slot after a fetch and shifts the panels below it (CLS budget 0.05, `web/lighthouserc.json`). One muted 13 px line costs nothing. This changes §5.6 (ruled). |
| D5 | Officers open another player's review from a **`Coach review` button in the opened row** (`row-detail`), never a line on every row. No fetch happens until the press. | Ruling 6 forbids up to 40 fetches. A line on 40 rows is a wall of equal-weight text (tenet 11). |
| D6 | The row line fetch is **read-only**: it never takes a lease or queues the model. | Product §3 and §15: the model runs only when a Premium owner or an officer opens a review. Task 35 fetches the fight route for the row line and Task 30's route queues generation, so every page view would spend the cap. The API needs `line=1` on the route: it serves the stored review or the rule-only rendering and queues nothing. Opening the panel uses the route without it. |
| D7 | The review is one column inside the report's content column at every width: the card fills the column, and the rail (player, Couldn't check, evidence footer, History) is a 2-up grid with a 20 px gap under it (player and Couldn't check left, footer and History right; one column on phone). Two columns, a 760 px review beside a 320 px rail, apply only when the content column is 1120 px or wider (a container query). Nothing may extend past the column's right edge. (Amended by the gate, 2026-10-28: the content column beside the fight list is 916 px at every desktop width, so the 760 + 40 + 320 layout ran past it.) | Details! keeps the verdict wide and the evidence close under it. 1-up cards keep their width at 2000 px. |
| D8 | Officer view uses the header `{Name}'s {Spec} review`, never `Your …`. The API's `persona.label` is ignored when `access` is `officer`. | The text is written to the player as "you". An officer reading `Your Fury coach` is told something false. |

## 2. Reference, and what we borrow

- **Details!'s player breakdown and the in-game combat summary.** Borrowed: the player's own row is the way in, one click opens it in place, the ability icons and numbers are one step down. Not borrowed: a second window.
- **Wowhead's class guide "mistakes" lists.** Borrowed: a short ranked list, the biggest first, each item with the icon of the spell it is about. Not borrowed: a score or a grade. The coach never shows one.
- **Our Rating tab** (`RatingTab.svelte`, `RatingPanel.svelte`). Reused: the lazy tab, the `min-h` reserve, `momentHref` evidence links, the whole-night line.
- **Our `StatePanel`, `EmptyState`, `LoadError`, `Skeleton`** (`web/src/components/ui/`) for the shell and the loading, empty and error states. No control is invented. The anchor chip is the report page's ability-link recipe (icon 16 px, 12 px text, 1 px `--line` border).
- **`BisTooltipHost.svelte` and the shared item tooltip** for every item name (§3.1, names inline).

### 3.1 The Coach tab (`CoachPanel.svelte`)

Mounted in `ReportView.svelte` beside Rating through `createLazyComponent`, with `REPORT_LAZY_MIN_H.coach = 'min-h-[640px]'` on desktop and `min-h-[420px]` on phone, on the tab wrapper (the card is always its natural height; the reserve below it is bare `--bg`). The tab draws no pace chart and no phone `Show chart` bar, and opening it leaves the fight list where it sits on every other tab. The tab exists in `TABS` for everyone, but ModeBar lists it only for a signed-in viewer who owns a character in the report or holds an officer view. Everyone else sees nothing about the coach. Adding an entry to the `More` popover shifts nothing in the strip. Whole night selected: §8 `night`.

**Player.** The review is for one player, named by `source`. With `source=friendlies` and one owned character in the fight, that character is chosen. With several, a row of 44 px crest buttons (the circular ringed crest, 32 px, class-colour ring) picks one, and the choice writes `source`. With none owned (an officer), §8 `officerPick`.

**Review card** (`card`: `--raised`, 1 px `--line`, 6 px radius, 1 px gold top highlight, padding 24 desktop and 18 phone). Regions, top to bottom:

1. **Header row**, 44 px. Left: the title (§8 `titleModel`, `titleRules`, `titleOfficer`, `titleStarter`). Right: a status slot, 13 px muted, fixed in the row, so a change never moves a line. It holds the `writing` text with an 8 px gold dot, or the `pill-site` `Officer view`.
2. **Hint line** (starter only): 13 px muted under the header. **Busy line** (busy only): 13 px muted, 2 px `--line-warm` left rule, 12 px left padding. Both are known on first paint.
3. **Opening.** 15 px / 22 px `--text`, max 720 px. No quotation marks, no avatar.
4. **Fix first**, the one primary element. A 2 px `--gold` left rule, 16 px left padding, 20 px above. A `label` `Fix first` in `--gold`. The title row: the spell icon 32 px (from the first anchor that carries a `spell_id`) and the title, 20 px / 28 px `--strong`. With no spell anchor, no icon and no gap. Then the why, 15 px. Then a 1 px `--line-soft` rule, the `label` `Practice` and the drill text, 14 px. Then the anchor chips.
5. **Link lines (rule-only rendering only).** Under a Fix first or Then why, one 13 px muted line per computed link, led by a 12 px `--gold` link glyph: the pack's sentence word for word (§10.4). The model's review has none; its prose carries the connection.
6. **Then.** A `label`, then up to three items on 1 px `--line-soft` dividers, 14 px padding. Each item: title 16 px 600 `--strong` (icon 24 px when an anchor carries a spell), why 14 px, chips. Smaller in type and weight than Fix first. This is the visible second level.
7. **Doing well.** A `label` in `--kill`, then one or two lines, 14 px, each led by the 14 px `--kill` tick (the planner's tick glyph, hidden from assistive tech).
8. **Rail: a 2-up grid under the card (two columns only from a 1120 px content column, D7).** `Couldn't check`: a `label`, then the pack's lines word for word, 13 px muted, never behind a disclosure (tenet 4). The footer `Reviewed 10 Dec · Based on: …`, 12 px muted. `History` (Premium only), a 44 px underlined link. On desktop the rail opens with the player card: crest 40 px, name in class colour 15 px 600, `Fury Warrior · Onyxia (40) · Kill 3:52` 13 px muted.

**Anchor chips.** One `a` each, from `coachAnchorHref`. 36 px tall on desktop and 44 px on touch (`min-h-11 md:min-h-9`), 10 px side padding, 12 px text, 1 px `--line`. Label grammar is §8 `anchor*`: the spell icon 16 px when there is a spell, the name, then `· Casts`, `· Buffs`, `· Debuffs`, `· Healing`, `· Timelines` or `· Resources` in muted; a death reads `Death · 3:20`. Times are `m:ss` from the pull. An anchor id missing from `anchors` renders no chip. A chip never opens a new tab.

**Names inline (tenets 2 and 3).** In titles, why, doing-well lines and Then items, every spell and item name that the build's name dictionaries resolve exactly (longest match first; the validator's own dictionaries, Task 27) renders as a token: 16 px icon and the name, items in their quality colour (the lightened text variant the item tooltip uses), a rank in muted when the name carries one. Items open the shared item tooltip on hover, focus and tap. Spells open the report's ability tooltip. The drill and the opening stay plain text. A name the dictionaries do not hold stays plain. A token keeps the 22 px line height.

### 3.2 The row line (`CoachRowLine.svelte`)

A full-width `a` strip inside the row's `li`, after the `button`. Height 36 px desktop, 52 px phone (room for a two-line title at 13 px / 18 px). Left padding 100 px desktop, so the text starts under the name column (8 + 28 + 12 + 40 + 12), and 48 px phone. Right padding 8 px. Background `#0b0f17` (one step under `--raised`), 1 px `--line-soft` rule on top. Text 13 px: the lead in `--muted`, the title in `--strong` 600 with the spell icon 20 px before it, a `›` in `--muted` at the far right. Only the viewer's own rows carry it (Ruling 6). The strip opens the Coach tab for that player and fight (`tab=coach&source=GUID`), moves focus to the panel heading and scrolls it into view. It never expands the row.

The free line has two targets. `{n} things to fix · First: {title}` opens the Coach tab's teaser. `Read the full review with Premium` is a gold underlined link to `/premium`. On phone they are two strips of 44 px.

### 3.3 The account line (`CoachLastRaidLine.svelte`)

The first child of the Characters `StatePanel`, under the heading row and above the first `CharacterRow`. Height 57 px desktop and 77 px phone (two lines at 13 px / 20 px on desktop, three on phone, 8 px padding; amended from 44/64 by the gate, 2026-10-28), the text top-aligned with the spare height under it, a 1 px `--line-soft` rule under it. Segments are separated by a muted ` · `. `Last raid` is muted, the zone and date are `--strong` 600, `Open` and `See your coach's review with Premium` are `--gold` underlined. Dates are `ddd d MMM` in the viewer's zone.

### 3.4 Discord

- `/report` ephemeral: one field named `Your coach` on the head embed, after `Kill order` and before `Loot`. One line per character, three at most. With two or more characters each line opens with the character name in bold and ` · `. The link text is the markdown link `Open your coach's review`. `Share in channel` re-renders without the field. A free viewer, or a viewer who owns nothing in the log, gets no field. The pending form links the whole sentence.
- Raid-night post (`report_ready`): the description gains one sentence (§8 `raidNight`) when any raider in the log holds `log_coach` on an owned character. It names and counts nobody.

### 3.5 History (`History` link, Premium only)

The link goes to the character page's `#coach` list: 56 px rows on `--line-soft`, each `{date} · {Boss} · {Spec} · {title}` (`fix_first.title`, or `Nothing to fix` when null), a muted `Wipe` tag on a wipe and a muted `Log only` tag when `source` is `rules`, the whole row a link to `?fight=N&tab=coach&source=GUID` on that report. Twenty rows, then a 44 px `Show older` button (§10.3).

## 4. States

### 4.1 Reserved heights (the no-shift rule)

Text slots in the review card have a minimum height of `lines x 22 px`. The line counts come from the model's caps (product §9.3) at the measure each width gives (desktop: 100 characters a line in the opening and 96 inside the Fix first rule; phone: 44 and 42 characters a line; secondary text is 14 px so phone holds 45 and 41).

| Slot | Cap | Desktop lines | Phone lines |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | 280 characters | 3 (66 px) | 7 (154 px) |
| Fix first, why | 320 | 4 (88 px) | 8 (176 px) |
| Fix first, drill | 240 | 3 (66 px) | 6 (132 px) |
| Then, why (each) | 200 | 3 (66 px) | 5 (110 px) |
| Doing well (each line) | 160 | 2 (44 px) | 4 (88 px) |
| Chips (each item) | 4 chips | 1 row (36 px) | 2 rows (94 px) |
| Link lines (each item) | 2 links, 13 px / 20 px | 2 lines (40 px) | 4 lines (80 px) |

Rules:

1. A review whose `status` is `writing` is laid out at these minimums from its first paint, filled with the rule-only rendering. The mock draws them as dashed boxes (`log-coach-states.png`, the Writing cell). The real panel draws no outline.
2. When the model's review arrives it replaces the text inside the same slots. Titles are the pack's words in both renderings, so they do not change. The header title changes from `Your {Spec} review` to `Your {Spec} coach` on the same 24 px line. Nothing below moves: Playwright asserts the `y` of the `Then` label, the `Doing well` label and the card's bottom edge are equal before and after (tolerance 0 px).
3. The slot counts follow the rule-only rendering's items. If the model returns fewer Then or Doing well items, the unused slots stay empty at their reserved height until the next open. If a text is longer than its slot (a validator miss) the card grows once; the test fails the build.
4. After the swap the minimums stay for that visit. Removing them would shift.
5. A review that is `ready` on the first answer (stored, or the switch is off) is laid out at natural height. There is no swap to protect.
6. The tab's own reserve holds while loading, so the footer never jumps: `min-h-[640px]` desktop, `min-h-[420px]` phone.
7. The status slot sits at the right of the 44 px header row on desktop. On phone it is its own 20 px row under the title, present in every review state and empty when idle (the mock draws it inline; this rule governs).
8. Icons carry `width` and `height` attributes and come from the build's icon sprites, preloaded with the lazy chunk, so no icon appears late.

## 5. Hierarchy

- **Coach tab.** One primary element: the Fix first block (gold rule, 20 px title, 32 px icon). One level of emphasis below it: the Then titles at 16 px with no rule. Doing well, Couldn't check and the footer are visibly secondary: smaller, muted, or both. The opening is plain text and never a card.
- **Row line.** One primary element: the title in `--strong` 600. The lead is muted. Nothing else on the strip is coloured except the free Premium link in `--gold`.
- **Account line.** One primary element: the first thing to fix, as plain `--text`. The zone and date are `--strong`. `Open` is the only gold.
- **Whitespace.** 20 px above Fix first, 24 px above Then, 20 px above Doing well, 20 px between rail cards. No region has a border of its own inside the card except the 2 px Fix first rule and 1 px dividers.
- **Voice.** The panel speaks as the coach. The row strip, the account line and Discord speak as the site and use the pack's words (product §16). The teaser panel states counts and a title and does not speak as the coach.

## 7. Performance and polish

- `CoachPanel`, `CoachReviewBody` and `CoachTeaser` load through `createLazyComponent` only when `tab=coach`. The row strip and the account line are small and eager. Nothing is imported into the signed-out bundle: the strip mounts only when `me` says the row is owned or the viewer is an officer.
- No layout shift (budget `cumulative-layout-shift` 0.05 in `web/lighthouserc.json`; this lane's target is 0). The strip's slot is reserved the moment `me` resolves, by entitlement (36 px desktop; 52 px Premium or 88 px free on phone), before the combatant list's first paint for an owned row. If `me` can resolve after the list paints, the list gates its body on `me` for at most 150 ms. Trash pulls and pulls under `MinFightMS` (30 s) get no slot: the report knows both before any fetch. The account slot is reserved at 57 px or 77 px from the first paint of the Characters panel.
- No missing-icon flash: spell and item icons come from the build's sprites with explicit size. The chunk preloads them with the lazy import.
- No font swap: Cinzel and Barlow are already loaded by the page. The panel adds no font.
- Lighthouse fixtures are signed out, so they do not exercise the coach. The Playwright acceptance below measures layout shift with a `PerformanceObserver` on `layout-shift` entries.
- `Cache-Control: private, no-store` on every coach response (product §17.3): no service worker caching of a review.

## 8. Copy, final

Voice: a mentor who has played the spec. One idea a sentence. No exclamation marks. Never "heal more", never a grade, never "you failed". Strings with a `{field}` are filled by `Fill` (Go) or `fill` (web). Titles are under eight words with at most one number. The tests read the second column of each row by the first column's key.

## 10. API notes and rulings (controller, 2026-10-28)

1. **Row strip is read-only (Task 30).** The fight route gains `line=1`: it returns the stored review's line, or the rule-only line, and never takes a lease, never calls `Caps` and never queues the model. The strip always sends it. Opening the panel sends the route without it.
2. **Teaser carries the icon (Tasks 30 and 35).** The teaser's `fix_first` is `{ title, code, spell_id }`. The web shows the spell's icon from `spell_id`. `code` alone cannot name a spell, so the pack's first anchor spell goes with it. Nothing else is sent.
3. **History items gain `boss` and `kill` (Task 31).** A history row reads `{date} · {Boss} · {Spec} · {title}`, with a muted `Wipe` tag on a wipe.
4. **Link sentences.** In the model's review, links appear only inside the model's prose. In the rule-only rendering the computed links render as link chips under the item's why: a 13 px muted sentence led by a 12 px link glyph in `--gold`, one line per link, the sentence being the pack's `Link.Sentence` word for word. The rule-only answer therefore carries `links[].sentence` per item (Task 30).
5. **Account and raid-night copy rulings.** `lastRaidNone` is shown, never hidden. `raidNight` stands as a guild-wide sentence that does not open with "Premium raiders". The bosses clauses and the night's same-stem counting stand.
6. **Officer header.** `persona.label` is ignored when `access` is `officer`; the web builds `titleOfficer` from the roster name.
8. **Anchor spell names (gate, 2026-10-28).** Each anchor with a `spell_id` also carries `spell_name`, the build's class spell data name, so a chip reads `{Spell} · Casts` and the prose tokenises a talent the web's name tables lack. A chip whose spell still does not resolve reads the kind word alone (`Casts`).
7. **Accuracy to check.** `DetailAdherence` shows `Heroic Strike (31), Slam (12) and Rend (9)` only as a format example. The F8 rotation lists decide the real names; Heroic Strike is a Fury rage dump and Rend is not a Fury cast.
