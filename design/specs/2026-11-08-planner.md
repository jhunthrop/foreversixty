## 1. Diagnosis of the live page (production 2026-11-08 at 390, 1440, 1920, 2000; `before/`, `before/measure.json`)

1. **Nothing uses the width.** The page sits in the 1,248 px column at every width. At 2000 the head starts at x 376 and the rail ends at x 1,624: 62% of the screen, 752 px of empty gutters (`planner-out-2000-fold.png`). At 1920 the same, from x 336.
2. **The trees start below a 400 px head.** At 1440 the head band runs y 109 to 481: a caps eyebrow `BUILD PLANNER`, a condensed caps title `HUNTER TALENTS`, the job paragraph (`Spend your points, compare them...`), the meta line, the Class and Race selects, then four bordered StatTiles (Points left, Spent, Level, DPS). The first talent is at y 579 and the trees end at y 1,036: the last tier is below the 1,000 px first screen.
3. **On a phone the trees are 1.5 to 2 screens down.** At 390 signed out the head is 653 px tall (the four tiles alone 235 px in a 2 × 2 grid), then the Level build card (333 px) sits above the trees; the tree tabs are at y 1,102 and the first talent at y 1,243. Signed in, the `Your character` card grows the head to 937 px and the first talent moves to y 1,630. The page is 3,295 px signed out, 3,703 px signed in.
4. **Eight framed objects.** Three tree cards, the Level build card, the Point order card, the Sim this build outline button as a full-width bar, the Share card and the Import card: each a gold-edged box with an icon chip and a caps title (`LEVEL 60 BUILD`, `POINT ORDER`, `SHARE THIS BUILD`, `IMPORT FROM ADDON`). The measure counts 8 bordered blocks over 60 px in `main` (9 signed in, with the character card).
5. **A second character card.** Signed in, `CharacterHeader` in its `card` layout draws `YOUR CHARACTER`, the crest, `Felsnap`, `Dwarf Beast Mastery Hunter`, `Level 34 · Alliance`, `Addon · 2 days ago`, then `SEND THIS BUILD TO FELSNAP` and `SIM THIS BUILD` as two equal outline buttons. The header selector already shows Felsnap: system 3.6 retires this card on every page. Signed out its column holds one line, `Sign in or paste an export to see your character here`, a 440 px column of nothing beside the head (`planner-guide-1440.png`).
6. **Two DPS numbers for one build, a factor of three apart.** On the Survival guide link the head tile reads `335 DPS ± 1.3` and the Level 60 build card beside the trees reads `966.6 DPS` (`Preview: the level 60 gear set, fully equipped. Not your DPS.`). The tile sims the planner's own gear map, which is empty for every link and every fresh build: it is a naked character's damage, shown in gold as the build's answer. Neither number says what gear it uses on its face.
7. **The arrival from a guide says something false.** `Load this build` on a guide opens `/planner?code=...`; the page then says `Talents loaded from a character. The order points were spent in is not recorded in game...` (no character was involved) and, in the head, `A linked build · Survival Hunter` in gold: two lines about where the build came from, one of them wrong (bug B1).
8. **The level cap is not a cap.** Signed in at level 34, the facts read `25 Points left`, `0/25`. At 25 of 25 every unspent talent still glows gold as spendable; a 26th click is accepted and the counters become `26/26` (`pointsAvailable` is `max(level − 9, spent)`). Nothing says the points past 34 are planned ahead (gap G2).
9. **Three cell languages on one site.** The planner rings an available talent gold and a partial or maxed one green; the sim's and the guides' `Trees` ring a maxed talent gold and a partial one green; the game's TalentFrame shows a talent you can learn green, a maxed one gold, a locked one grey. A player reading a guide tree and then the planner tree sees gold mean two opposite things.
10. **The order strip scrolls sideways.** Point order is a 44 px strip of icons with a level under each; 51 points scroll horizontally inside a 395 px card, so at 1440 only the first seven are visible (`planner-guide-1440.png`). The order is the planner's unique fact (a leveling plan), and it is mostly hidden (tenet 4).
11. **Utilities sit at the same weight as the job.** Share (title, Share, Copy for the game, the hint, the text summary, Copy as text, the sim switch) is a 425 px card; Import is a full-width 821 × 249 card under the trees from 1280. `Copy for the game`, the one control that puts the build in the game for a visitor, is a 156 px outline button inside the Share card at y 1,155 (1440) and y 2,043 (390).
12. **What is right and stays:** the trees with the client's art, arrows and rank pills; click to add, right-click or long-press to remove, arrows and Backspace on the keyboard; the tooltip with this rank and the next; the refusal line in words (`Needs 10 points in Survival. You have 5.`); the band compare (differs, still to take, matches, beyond the guide; Load with a confirm; See what differs; the gold dot on cells that differ; the band line in the tooltip); the URL as truth (`?class`, `?race`, `?code`, `?talents`, `?spec`); the code notes; send to the addon with its full state machine; Copy for the game; Share with the confirm that lists what goes public; Import with the class switch; the read-only `/b/:id` with Fork; the CLS reserve discipline.

## 3. Reference surfaces, and what is borrowed

- **The game's TalentFrame (1.12):** a tree's art behind a grid of talent buttons, arrows between prerequisites, the rank on every button, a talent you can learn green, a maxed one gold, a locked one grey; "Talent Points: n" as the one number; tabs per tree. Borrowed: the art, arrows, rank pills, the colours (decision P2), tabs on a phone, Points left as the head's gold figure.
- **Wowhead's Classic talent calculator:** the class icons in a row over the calculator, three trees side by side with each tree's points in its head, the required level, the link that updates as you click. Borrowed: the class row (`ClassPicker`), trees side by side from 768, the points per tree in the tree head, the URL as truth. Not borrowed: per-tree reset icons, ads, the print and export menus.
- **Talented (the in-game template addon):** templates applied to the character and a send to the game. Borrowed: the build as something you send, one primary action named for the character (`Send this build to Felsnap`).
- **Icy Veins leveling orders:** the order a talent point is taken, level by level. Borrowed: Point order as rows of ten levels with a socket per level (`PointOrder`).
- **Our system as shipped:** `PageHeader` with the answer at the right (tiers, BiS), `ControlBar` (BiS, tiers, guides), `Plate`, open `Section`s, `Button`, `Field`, `Skeleton`, `Notice`, `Tag`; the guides' `TalentTrees` language for the colours.

## 4. The page in primitives

### 4.2 ControlBar (`Planner choices`, sticky, 56 px)

### 4.3 Notes (`notes`, one Notice line, neutral)
One sentence when the build arrived with something to say, never more than one:
- a link with no character (a guide's `Load this build`, a shared `?code=`): `linkedNote` (proposed, replaces the false "Talents loaded from a character" for this case, bug B1);
- a character's export or addon import: the shipped reconstructed sentences (with `minus {n} that no legal order reaches`, `This code is from an older client build; check the talents.`);
- a band load: `bandLoadedNote` (`Loaded the 60 build. The order shown is ...`) or, from the panel, `Loaded at level 34 · 21 points` (`bandCompareCopy.loadedLine`);
- a character with more points than any legal order: `keptNote`;
- a gear-only log link: `This link carries no talent ranks.`;
- a code that does not read: the decoder's own sentence (`That code is FS9; this site reads FS1.`), or `That code names a class this planner does not have: {slug}.` The decoder messages keep their wording (pinned by `fs1.test.ts`).

### 4.4 The one Plate: the trees (`TalentEditor`, accessible name `Talent trees`)
- From 768: three tree columns (four never occurs; `--n` is the tree count). Below 768: the three tree tabs and the open tree; the tab opens on the tree with the most points (first tree when none).
- Each cell is a button: click adds a point, right-click or a 500 ms press removes the last one, Enter adds, Backspace or Delete removes, arrows move between cells (`moveFocus`), Escape closes the tooltip. Hover or focus opens `TalentTip` after 150 ms.
- Colours (decision P2): can spend `avail`, partial `part`, maxed `max`, locked `lock`. With no points left at a character's level (decision P3) the unspent cells read `lock` and their tooltip says why (`No points left at level 34.`, proposed).
- After a band has loaded or while a band is compared, a cell whose rank differs carries the gold dot.
- Foot (hairline above, 12 / 16 px padding): the status line (`role="status"`, 14/20 `--strong`, `--wipe` for a refusal, 20 px reserved: the `messages` sentence or a failed estimate), then Small `Talent trees read from the game client, build 1.60.1.70790.`, and at the right `Reset…` (gold link; hidden at 0 spent). Reset open: Small `Clear every point in this build?`, small Buttons `Clear all points` and `Keep the build` (focus moves to Keep).
