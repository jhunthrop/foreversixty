## 1. Diagnosis of the live pages (measured 2026-11-07, `before/`)

**/setup**
1. **Three gold-edged cards with numbered icon tiles and condensed caps titles** ("GET SET UP", "1 SIGN IN", "2 THE ADDON", "3 THE COMPANION"), a caps eyebrow "ADDON AND COMPANION" over a caps H1. Card-everything, the look section 2 of the system retires.
2. **State is said three times per step**: a progress chip in the head (`Sign in` / `Addon` / `Companion`, a ring or a gold tick), a tag in each card head (`TO DO`, `OPTIONAL`, `DONE`), and a line under the button (`Signed in as Hunthrop#1894`). The chips are a second navigation for a page that fits in three rows.
3. **The three actions are far apart.** At 1440 the Sign in button ends at y 420, Install from CurseForge at y 595, Download for Windows at y 1,220. At 390 the companion's button is at y 1,900 of a 2,986 px page. The answer to "what do I still need to do" is never in one screen.
4. **Signed in with no character, the gold button is wrong.** The first step not done is the addon (gold `INSTALL FROM CURSEFORGE`), while the account has no character at all; the line under it says `No character yet. Refresh from Battle.net, or install the addon and the companion, then paste an export.` and the page has no Refresh button. The real next step (Battle.net) has no control (`before/setup-new-1440.png`).
5. **The character never appears.** Signed in with a Battle.net character, the page never shows it (tenet 14): the payoff of setting up is invisible except in the header chip.
6. **An outage reads as signed out.** `lib/setup/state.ts` turns a failed `/v1/me` into `me: null` (`fetchMeOnce().catch(() => null)`), so a signed-in player is told to sign in when the API is down.
7. **Three disclosures inside the addon card** (`Try an export`, `Manual install` open, `Everything the addon does`). The paste box, the Mac and Korea path, is folded; the eleven in-game surfaces, the best evidence of what the addon is worth, are folded; the zip is linked twice (the button row and the manual).
8. **The companion's signed download is unexplained.** `web/src/data/companion.json` carries `firstRun` ("Signed by Commish LLC. If SmartScreen still asks …"); nothing renders it. A player who meets SmartScreen has no answer on the page.
9. **The current-character chip on the page** (`CurrentCharacterBar`, "Felsnap · Beast Mastery Hunter · Open in planner · Open in simulator · Forget") is a second character control beside the header selector (3.6 and one-way-to-switch).
10. **Width:** one 900 px column at 1440, 1920 and 2000 (`measure-wide`); at 2000 the page uses 45% of the screen.
11. **What is right and stays:** the three steps and their order, one gold button on the first step not done (W.10), every done line from `progress.ts`, the companion Status capture with its Example tag, the eleven surfaces with real client captures, the commands read from the addon's own `Locale.lua`, the handshake, the hand-install path and build number, every download through `/dl/`, the `?next=` way back to a guild claim.

**/connect**
12. **A centred 420 px card in an empty page**, a 40 px condensed caps H1 that wraps to three lines ("CONNECT JHUNT-PC TO HUNTHROP#1894?"), the device panel a box inside the box (`border-line-warm`).
13. **Success says it twice:** title `Connected.`, body `Connected. Go back to the companion; …` (B3).
14. **The device panel does not move between signed out and pending** (88 px, shipped and tested): right, kept as a rule.
15. **Signing in can fail off-site:** a Battle.net callback that fails (`api/internal/auth/handler.go` `fail`, line 200) answers the browser with a JSON 500 body; the player never comes back to /connect or /setup (G2).

## 7. Every state of every interactive element

All Buttons are the system Button (gold primary 44 px, secondary outline, sm 36 px; case as the system), links the system link (gold 600, `›` where it navigates). Focus-visible everywhere: 2 px gold outline, 2 px offset. Touch: every target at least 44 px on a phone (links in rows carry 44 px min-height below 768).

Gaps (named, not invented):
- **G1. No import-running signal.** Sign-in imports for up to 5 s (`api/internal/auth/importer.go` `importBudget`) and continues in the background for up to 2 min (`importContinueBudget`); `/v1/me` cannot say which. A new player who signs in during a slow import sees "no character" (the warn row) for up to two minutes. Needed: `GET /v1/me` `bnet_import: { state: 'running' | 'done' | 'failed', at }` (or `bnet_import_running: bool`); the page polls `/v1/me` every 5 s while running. `web-setup-importing` is drawn to that proposed field.
- **G2. A failed Battle.net callback never returns to the site.** `handler.go` `fail` writes a JSON 500 to the browser. Needed: redirect to `safeNext(next)` with `signin=failed` (and log as now); /setup and /connect read it. `web-setup-signin-failed` is drawn to it.
- **G3. Refresh from /setup.** The refresh snapshot and the `?refreshed=1` reading live in `Account.svelte`; /setup needs them as a shared module (`lib/account/refresh-snapshot.ts` already is) and `battlenetStartUrl('/setup?refreshed=1')`.
- **G4. Platform detection** for the Mac variant (U10): one helper in `lib/setup/`, server-safe (renders the Windows variant, swaps after hydration inside the reserved row; the row height is the same).
- **G5. Device platform reads like the pipeline.** `deviceStatusLine` prints `JHUNT-PC · windows/amd64 · last seen 3 minutes ago` (the device's raw `platform`); it must read `Windows` (tenet 7). A `platformLabel()` mapping (`windows/*` -> `Windows`, `darwin/*` -> `macOS`, `ios`, `android`) shared with /connect, which already receives `Windows` from the lookup.
- **G6. Three pairing strings inside `LogsCompanion.svelte`** (`PAIR_ACTION`, `PAIR_FAILED`, the code line) move to `pairingCopy`.
- **G7. A parity fixture for the guild-claim return** (4.6): a `me` with a guild and a `?next=` claim path.

Bugs for the build:
