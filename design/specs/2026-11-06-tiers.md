## 1. Diagnosis of the live page (captures at 390, 1440, 1920, 2000; `design/reviews/2026-11-06-tiers/before/`)

Measured on production on 2026-11-06.
1. **The list starts below a header that says what the page is three times.** It has a gold caps eyebrow ("Level 60 · Raid-ready, Phase 1"), a caps title "TIER LIST", a job sentence, the role tabs and the faction pills. Then come three note paragraphs (90 px at 1440, 168 px at 390), then a Card head "DPS TIER LIST · Alliance". The Card head restates the selected tab and the selected pill. The first row sits at y 533 at 1440 and at y 583 at 390.
2. **Six rows in the first screen at 1440.** Rows are 72 px tall. In the first 1,000 px a player sees S, A and four of the eight B specs. At 390 rows are 128 px, so one and a half specs fit in the first screen.
3. **The page is the old card system.** The list is a gold-edged `Card` with a trophy icon chip. "How to read this" is a second bordered box, 820 px wide, at y 2,023. The signed-in callout is a third box, with a gold border. That makes three framed objects where the system allows one.
4. **Width.** The column is 1,344 px at every width, so content is 1,248 px. At 1920 the page uses 65% of the screen; at 2000 it uses 62%. The bar column is about 370 px at all widths, and the Details!-style bar, which is the page's picture, never grows.
5. **Two visual languages for controls.** The role tabs are outlined 44 px boxes. The faction pills are caps, filled in the faction colour, behind a divider. That is not the system's segmented control, and selection reads as two different colours.
6. **80 caps links.** Every DPS row ends in "BIS GUIDE PLANNER SIM", 12 px caps with tracking. The 20 rows make an 80-link caps column at the right edge, and it is the loudest text on the page after the names.
7. **Notes that point away.** The first DPS note says "What it counts and what it leaves out is in the box below". That box is 1,500 px further down. The healer notes are three lines of 13 px across 112 ch.
8. **Type.** The title and headings are Barlow Condensed caps. Column heads are two-line 12 px ("Damage per / second", "Gap to the top, / %"). Figures are mono on the shipped CSS. The system's decided type (Marcellus title once, Barlow 700 normal-case heads, Barlow tabular figures) is not applied.
9. **Signed-in reserve.** A 72 px skeleton row is reserved under the job line whenever a session is hinted. It is a bordered box even while loading.
10. **Nav.** The header highlights "Logs" on the tier list. The tier list is not a log page.
11. **What is right and stays:** the rules (letters on DPS only, 1% ties, the healer error rule, the tank sort on damage taken), the class-colour bars, the crest per row, race under the name, "Top" and "Least", green best-of-column on tanks, both factions prerendered with a pre-paint faction script, and no island above the fold.

Borrowed: the bar as the row's picture, the leader-scaled length and the class colour. At 1760 and up the bar is a fixed 560 px, so bars compare at a glance. Not borrowed: the name drawn on the bar. ## 4. The page in primitives

`Page layout="ledger"`. Order, top to bottom: `PageHeader`, `ControlBar`, `Notice` (the caption), one `Plate` holding a `RankList`, one open `Section` "How to read this", then the footer.

### 4.1 PageHeader
- `title="Tier list"`. Marcellus 40/46 (32/38 on phone), `tone="strong"`, which is `--color-strong` per decision D8. No crest, because the page has no single class.
- `meta`: one Small line (13/20, `--color-muted`): `Level 60 · {presets.raid.label} · Updated {d MMM}`. This is the page's one Updated stamp (provenance quiet). Build and engine go in the stamp's `title` attribute, as shipped.
- `answer` (signed in only; section 4.6). Below 900 px it stacks under the title.
- The eyebrow, the job sentence and the bordered callout are removed.

### 4.2 ControlBar
- `groups`:
  - `Role` is `segmented`, `param` unused. Options carry `href` (`/tiers`, `/tiers/tank`, `/tiers/healer`, keeping `?faction=` when set). Each label carries a `count`: `DPS 20`, `Tank 3`, `Healer 5`, the lengths of the lists.
  - `Faction` is `segmented`, `param="faction"`. Options are `Alliance` and `Horde`, each with an `icon` (the faction emblem at 20 px, `FactionMark`).
- Selection is gold for both groups (system 3.4). The faction colour fill retires; the emblem in the item carries the faction.
- `label="Tier list choices"`. `labelsFrom=1280`.
- `summary` on phone: `{Role} · {Faction}`, for example `DPS · Alliance`, then `Change ▾`.
- `end`: none.
- Faction behaviour is unchanged from today. Both factions' lists are in the HTML. The pre-paint inline script reads `?faction=horde` and sets `data-tier-faction`. A choice writes `?faction=` with `history.replaceState` and swaps which list shows. Nothing reloads and nothing shifts.

### 4.3 The caption (`Notice tone="neutral"`, 15/24, `--color-text`, one line at 1280 and up)
One sentence per role, with figures from data (section 6). Signed out, it ends with `Sign in` (a gold link) and ` to see your spec marked.`

### 4.4 The one Plate: `RankList`
New shared primitives, in the mock's `ws_prims.py`. They are a design-review item under tenet 10; see decision T6.
- **`RankList`** (`n_figs`, `grouped`, `links`): one CSS grid template, shared by the heads and every row.
  - Columns: `28 rank | 36 crest | name | bar | figure × n | 88 gap | links`.
  - Name is `minmax(180px, 220px)` and bar is `minmax(120px, 1fr)` below 1760.
  - From 1760 the name is `minmax(240px, 1fr)` and the bar is a **fixed 560 px** (section 6 of the system spec). Measured: bar 244 px at 1440 and 560 px at 1920 and 2000.
  - Figure columns are 112 px (124 px on tank).
  - Links are 206 px for four links and 156 px for three.
  - Column gap is 16 px.
- **`ColumnHeads`**: one row of 12 px caps `Label`s, `--color-muted`, 40 px tall, with a `--line-soft` hairline under it. On DPS it is indented by the letter column. It is hidden below 1024.
  - DPS: `#`, `Spec`, `Share of the top spec's damage`, `DPS`, `Gap`.
  - Healer: `#`, `Spec`, `Share of the top healer's healing`, `HPS`, `Gap`.
  - Tank: `#`, `Spec`, `Share of the least taken`, `Taken per second`, `Effective health`, `Threat per second`, `More taken`.
  - Numeric heads are right-aligned.
- **`RankGroup`** (DPS only): the letter column.
  - From 1100 it is 184 px wide, with grid areas `letter | band` over `letter | count`.
  - Letter: Barlow 700 32/36 in the tier colour. S `#ff8000`, A `#b866f5`, B `#3d94f0`, C `#1eff00`, D `#9d9d9d`: the quality colours, as shipped.
  - Band: 13/18 `--color-text`. Count: 13/18 muted.
  - The rail is 3 px in the tier colour at x 0, full group height. A 1 px `--line-soft` rule sits on the right. A 1 px `--line` rule divides groups.
  - The column takes the plate ground. There is no tint and no separate surface.
  - Below 1100 it becomes a header strip: the letter at 22/24, the band, and the count at the right, 8 px vertical padding, with a hairline under it.
  - A one-spec group is one row high. The label fits 56 px, which fixes the live page's 78 px S and A groups.
- **`RankRow`** (a `Row` preset, as `ItemRow` is). Min height `--row-h` (56 px), padding 8 x 16, `--line-soft` divider. The row has **no `href`**: its next steps are links inside it, which follows the Row rule "a link or an action, never both".
  - Rank: Barlow 700 15/20 tabular, `--color-muted`, right-aligned. A tied healer reads `=2`. An unranked healer list shows no rank.
  - Crest: `ClassCrest` 36 px (32 px on phone), the circular ringed HD crest, never a square.
  - Name: the spec name as the BiS link, Barlow 700 16/20 in the class colour. The primitive applies the colour from `classSlug`. Under it, Small `{Class} · {Race}`. When `weights_low_confidence` is set, a third line reads `Stat weights less certain` (13/18, muted, dotted underline, the shipped `title`).
  - Bar: `Meter tone="class"` (new tone; the fill is the class colour from a prop). It is 8 px tall on `--line-soft`, with length `fraction` (value over top; tank: best over value).
  - Figure: Barlow 700 16/20 tabular, `--color-strong`, with its unit inline at 12 px 600 muted. Units: `DPS`, `HPS`, and on tank `taken / s`, `HP` and `threat / s`. On a tank row the best of each column is `--color-kill`, with `title="Best of the {n} tanks"`, so colour is not the only signal.
  - Gap: Barlow 600 15/20 tabular, `--color-text`, right-aligned: `−8.2%` (tank: `+40.7%`). The leader reads `Top` (tank: `Least`) in 700 `--color-strong`. A tie adds `≈ tie` under it, 700 12/16 `--color-strong`, with the shipped `title`.
  - Links: `BiS` in `--color-gold`, then `Guide`, `Planner` and `Sim` (DPS only) in `--color-nav`. All are 600 14/20, normal case, 16 px apart, right-aligned, with a 40 px hit height (44 px on phone). The 12 px caps link strip retires.
- **Phone (below 1024).** The row is a four-area grid, `22 | 32 | 1fr | auto`:
  - Line 1: rank, crest, name and class · race, then the figure at the right.
  - Line 2: the gap, under the figure, with `≈ tie` inline after it.
  - Tank only: `Effective health 49,794 HP` and `Threat 1,633 threat / s`, under the name.
  - Then the bar, full width from the name's left edge.
  - Then the links on one 44 px line, left-aligned under the name.
  - Measured: 113 px a row on DPS and healer (live: 128 px), 176 px on tank.

### 4.5 The open Section "How to read this"

The body has three columns at 1024 and up, `--gap` apart, and one column on phone. Each column is a 12 px caps `Label` over Body 15/24 `--color-text`, max 62 ch. It has no box. The columns replace both the three paragraphs above the list and the bordered box below it.

  - Ground `#e5b9551a`.

**Gaps (named, not invented):**
- G1. **No per-spec DPS error.** `set_dps` carries no error, so DPS ties stay on the fixed 1% gear-adoption margin (as today). Healers have a measured error; DPS does not. Needed: an error on `set_dps` from the ranking sim.
- G2. **No tank fight length.** `tank-encounter.json` has no duration, so the tank boss line names none (unchanged since 2026-10-09).
- G3. **No faction on the selected character in local storage.** `CurrentCharacter` (`lib/current-character.ts`) stores `source`, `ref`, `label`, `classSlug` and `savedAt`; it has no faction. The page therefore cannot pre-paint a Horde character's faction without a flash. Until a `faction` field is added, a signed-in Horde player with no `?faction=` lands on Alliance, as today. Decision T3.
- G4. **Data present but not shown** (decision T4): healer `metrics.mana_lasts_sec`, `overheal_pct`, `hpm`; tank `metrics.tmi`, `chance_of_death`, `dps`.
- G5. **The working copy's active build is behind** (1.60.1.70532 here, 1.60.1.70790 on origin/main and production). That is not a page gap. The mocks were rendered against 70790 through `FS_DATA_ROOT` so that they match production.
