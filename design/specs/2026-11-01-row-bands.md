# Row bands (2026-11-01)

Owner, on the guild Roster tab: the viewer's own row ("Zulmara (you)" in the fixture) sat on a
shaded stripe that started flush at the card's content edge, so the round crest overhung it; it
had no side padding, so the row's right-hand text sat hard against its edge; and it was a square
lighter rectangle inside a rounded card, so it read as a misplaced highlight, not a row.

## Root cause

There was no shared treatment for a lit row. Each list painted its own ground on the row box it
already had: `bg-[rgba(229,185,85,.06)]` (roster, readiness), an inline `background:` from
`OWN_ROW_WASH` (raid report blocks, attendance, sign-ups), `hover:bg-card-top` (planned raids,
match picker, coach history, kill order) and the fight table's own `MARKED` / `target:` strings.
Inside a padded card body those row boxes have no side padding of their own (or 4 px), and none
had a radius, so every one of them drew a square stripe from the content edge.

The 2026-10-30 BiS fix (8639e7756) took the tint off rows where it only restated a pill; the
2026-10-31 tier fix (1fd853eae) kept the you-row state but drew it as part of the list's own
structure. A row that must stay lit (the viewer's own row, a marked fight, a hover) now follows the
same idea: the band is the row, not a stripe laid over the list.

## The band

- One recipe in `web/src/styles/global.css` (`.row-band`, `.row-band-own`, `.row-band-mark`,
  `.row-band-target`, `.row-band-hover`) and its class strings in `web/src/lib/ui/row.ts`.
- Corners take `--radius-control` (8 px).
- Content sits 12 px inside the band. A row whose list pads it by less reaches out by the
  difference (`ROW_BAND_REACH`: 0 -> `-mx-3 px-3`, `px-1` -> `-mx-2 px-3`, `px-2` -> `-mx-1 px-3`),
  so its content keeps the list's left edge and the band grows into the gutter.
- While a band is lit, the hairlines on either side of it step back, so it never shows a rule
  cutting across its rounded corners.
- Grounds: own row `--row-hover` (gold 6%); marked or addressed fight gold 8% with the 2 px gold
  edge; link-row hover `--color-card-top`, press `#171d2b`.

## Out of scope (already contained)

Rows that run edge to edge inside a clipped card keep their full-bleed ground: the account rows,
the rankings list (own row with the gold left edge), the tier list, the BiS class cards, the
report's fight selector (a nested tree with its own gold rail) and the home Latest rows. The card
clips them to its radius, and their content carries its own padding.
