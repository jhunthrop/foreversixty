# Rankings page redesign: experience spec

Author: ux-designer. 2026-10-11. Route `/rankings` and `/rankings/{encounter-slug}` (`web/src/pages/rankings.astro`, `web/src/components/Rankings.svelte`, `web/src/lib/rankings/*`). Mock: `design/mocks/gen_rankings.py`; renders `design/mocks/renders/rankings{,-signed-out,-2000,-1024,-phone,-states}.png`; header states in `character-header-states.png`. Header: `2026-10-11-character-header.md`. References: `docs/tenets.md` 1, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 14; the one-job-per-page rule; the one crest language; provenance stays quiet; the tier list spec.

## 1. The one job

**"Where does my character stand among characters like it?"** Signed in, the page leads with the shared header: `Your rank: 14th of 212 Fury Warrior kills, on Onyxia, Normal ruleset`, action `See your parses →`. The board follows with your best kill pinned. Signed out, it is the board with a class and spec choice under the shared signed-out band (one muted line; no sign-in control of the page's own).

**Ownership.** The tier list owns spec rankings (which spec is strongest); this page owns character rankings (where this character is among its own spec). The hero says so with one pointer line, `Comparing specs, not characters? Tier list →`. Guild rankings stay on the Guilds tab (unchanged here); home keeps its top-guilds panel.

## 2. What the board shows today

Per encounter (the slug), per metric (Damage, Healing, Damage taken, Execution), one row per ranked kill: rank, character (class colour, link to `/character/...`), guild with region, raid size, the metric, Executed %, length, date, talent split (link to `/b/{build_id}`), Report. Filters: board (Characters, Guilds), Today only, metric, ruleset, region, phase, faction; class and spec only by URL. Page size 100. Today the page opens with the encounter name as an h1 (a bare `/rankings` shows a picker and nothing else), a `CurrentCharacterBar` spine, and, pre-filled from the pointer, only class and ruleset. A `You` pill marks your row and moderation states are pills.

## 3. Decisions

1. **Layout, top to bottom.** Real header bar; the band (eyebrow `Characters`, h1 `Rankings`, job line `Where your character stands among characters like it.`, the tier pointer, the one `Updated {date}` stamp from `updated_at` at the right of the h1); the shared header (signed in); the board heading `Fury Warriors` with the quiet `Another class or spec` link and, under it, the one-line colour legend `Rank colour is the percentile: 99+ 95+ 75+ 50+ 25+ under 25` (each in its parse colour; no count, the header already says it); the Characters/Guilds tabs at the right of that row; one filter panel; the board; the pager. The encounter is no longer the h1: it is the first and widest control, because it is the one filter every answer needs.
2. **Selector versus filters (binding).** The header selector sets **class and spec** (and, as a default, **ruleset** and **region**'s absence: the cohort is all regions). It is the only character switch; the page has no switcher. The filters narrow the cohort: **Encounter, Ruleset, Region, Phase, Faction, Metric, Today only.** Signed in there is no class or spec filter; the heading names the scope, and `Another class or spec` (quiet underlined text, not hidden: it opens the Class and Spec selects in the filter panel) lets a player look at someone else's board. While another spec is shown the header's answer stays about the character, a one-line note under it reads `Showing Frost Mages. Your rank is on the Fury Warrior board. Back to Fury Warriors`, and no row is pinned. Signed out the Class and Spec selects are the first two controls and the heading reads `Every class and spec`.
3. **"Realm" does not exist in the data.** Forever has rulesets per region and no realms (`web/src/lib/rankings/api.ts`, the API's `region` and `ruleset` enums), so the realm filter is **Region** (US, EU, KR, TW, CN). Say so if the owner asks for realm.
4. **Defaults.** Encounter: the one the character most recently ranked on (from `/v1/characters/...` `history[0]`); with none, the encounter with the most ranked kills (until the API orders that way, the first of `/v1/encounters`); the picker only when nothing is ranked at all. Ruleset: the character's own (`Every ruleset` stays an option). Region: every region. Phase: the current phase (`phaseAt(now)`); the options read `Phase 1` and `All phases` (the select label never carries `Raids, phase…`, which does not fit; `PHASES` labels in `lib/rankings/phases.ts` need the shorter wording, with the region option `All regions`). Metric by role: Damage for damage specs, Healing for healers, Damage taken for tanks. Replaces `applyCurrentCharacterPrefilter` (class and ruleset only) with class, spec, ruleset, encounter, metric.
5. **The pin.** **Pin only when the row is off the visible page** (round 2). Then it is the first row of the panel, under the column labels and the label `Your best kill`, with its true rank (even 204), a 2 px gold left edge and 6% gold tint, a 1 px rule, then the board from `#1`. When your row is on the visible page nothing is pinned: the same gold edge and tint mark it in place (`rankings.png`, rank 14). No pills: the `You` pill and the moderation pills go. A removed or at-risk kill gets one plain 12 px muted line under the name (`Removed by a moderator`).
6. **Columns** keep `RANKING_ROW_GRID` (single source of truth): `# · Character · Guild · Size · {Metric} · Executed · Length · Date · Build · Report`. The rank numeral keeps its parse-ladder colour (grey, green, blue, purple, orange, pink from the percentile). **Build is plain body-colour text with an underline on hover; Report is the gold link** (two links, two weights). From 1100 down Size and Length drop and the guild moves under the name; below 600 a row is rank, name with the figure, `Report` (44 px) and a muted second line `40-man · 2:41 · 94% · 31/20/0` (the split is the plain Build link). Dates are relative inside two weeks (`3 days ago`), else `Nov 25`, the same rule as Logs. No column is tinted but rank.
7. **One crest language.** One rule, signed in or out: a board whose rows are all one class shows no crest in rows (the header or heading carries it once; 100 identical rings are noise, tenet 11); a board with more than one class shows a 28 px ringed crest before each name, never a square class icon. Names are class-coloured always.
8. **Wide and narrow.** 1344 column centred, header band full-bleed (`rankings-2000.png`); at 1024 the header stays three columns and the filter panel is 3 up; at 390 the header stacks and **the filters collapse to one summary line** (`Onyxia · Normal · All regions · Phase 1`, wrapping to a second line rather than truncating, and a 44 px `Filters` button) that opens the 2-column panel (shown open in `rankings-states.png`); rows as in 6.

## 4. Data this needs (contract asks for the API lane; nothing is invented in the mock beyond examples)

- **One row per character, not per kill.** `Store.Rankings` today ranks every `fight_metrics` row, so one character can hold five ranks and `M` would count kills, not characters. The board and the header need the character's **best kill** per `player_key` (a `distinct on` or `group=character`). Until that exists the copy must say kills: `14th of 212 Fury Warrior kills` (tenet 8).
- **Own-rank lookup.** The API has no per-character rank (`current-character-prefilter.ts` says so). Ask: `GET /v1/rankings?...&character={region}/{ruleset}/{name}` returning `character_rank { rank, row }` or null. The percentile endpoint is not enough (a percentile is not an `Nth`).
- Both are `api/**` changes outside this task. The page falls back to no header answer (`Your rank did not load.`) and no pin until they land.

## 5. States

Rendered in `rankings-states.png` and the header sheet: pinned row beyond the page; row rest, hover (ground only, nothing moves), own row; removed row; board loading (reserved `min-h-[440px]`); board empty (`No ranked fights yet for this filter. Rankings fill in as reports are uploaded.` + `Upload a log →`); board failed (`Try again`); header empty (`Not ranked yet on Onyxia. Upload a log of a kill to get on the board.`); header with another spec shown; encounter picker. Every filter select: rest, hover, focus-visible, disabled (none), loading (selects stay usable), 44 px tall everywhere. Keyboard: header identity link, header action, tabs, filters in order, pinned row, rows, pager.

## 6. Copy

Eyebrow `Characters`; h1 `Rankings`; job line `Where your character stands among characters like it.` (signed out: `How characters rank on each encounter, kill by kill.`); pointer `Comparing specs, not characters?` + `Tier list →`; answer label `Your rank`, figure `{ordinal}`, unit `of {M} {Spec} {Class} kills` (it becomes `of {M} {Spec} {Class}s` only when the API dedupes to each character's best kill and has the own-rank lookup, section 4; never earlier, at the same size), note `On {encounter}, {Ruleset} ruleset`; action `See your parses →`; heading `{Spec} {Class}s` / `Every class and spec`; no count under the heading; no sign-in prose or button on the page (the shared signed-out band only)

## 7. Not this page

Not a spec ranking (tier list), not a gear or talent page, not a switcher, no provenance pills, no per-row crest on a single-class board.

## 8. Acceptance

Captures at 1440 signed in and out, 2000, 1024, 390; the pinned row at rank 204; another spec shown; every state above; side by side with `rankings.png`. Tests: no `pill` class on the page, one `Updated`, no `CurrentCharacterBar`, header follows the selector, filters in the URL, `/bis` and `/logs` headers identical in markup. The examples in the mock (Obnoxious Yell, the 16 rows, guilds, DPS, sizes of 10, 20 and 40, lengths, dates, `212`) are invented; the encounter (Onyxia, a launch raid), classes, specs, phases and rulesets are the site's own. Onyxia is a 40-man board, so every row is 40 and only the lengths vary; other sizes are shown on the states sheet's dungeon board (5 and 4). Real rows carry the API's `size` and `duration_ms`.

## 9. Open questions

1. Per-character best kill and own-rank lookup (section 4): does the API lane take them before launch?
2. Guilds tab: the header's answer on it (the character's guild rank) is not designed; Characters is the default.
3. A mixed-class default for signed out vs. the most-played spec.

## 10. Round 2 (WoW player round-one review, decisions applied)

Header answer reads `kills` until the API change (section 4 kept); the own row is pinned only off-page; colour legend added; phone filters collapse to a summary line; the heading no longer repeats the count; the mock's sizes and lengths vary and the encounter is Onyxia so they are plausible; Build plain, Report gold; no prose or button sign-in (the shared band). Encounter is 1.3x the other selects at 1440 (the review preferred the 1024 proportion). Not done: the Guilds tab's header answer is still undesigned.
