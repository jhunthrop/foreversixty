## 1. The one question and the one job

**"Where does my spec stand?"** Answer, then the next step: the spec's BiS list, guide and planner.

Visible with no scroll:

| | 360 px phone | 1280 px desktop |
|---|---|---|
| Signed in, character on this role | Eyebrow, h1, job line, the "Your spec" callout (rank, gap, Your best in slot button), role tabs | Same, plus the faction pills in the same row and the first caption line |
| Signed out | Eyebrow, h1, job line, role tabs, faction pills, the S tier header and its first row | Same, the S and A tiers in full |

The page does not show gear, stat weights, talents, rotations or a spec's reasons. It links.

## 2. Reference

- **Wowhead and Icy Veins tier lists.** Borrowed: one row per spec, class colour on the name. Not borrowed: the S to D letters (no per-spec error to back them) or a bare icon grid with no number. Ours carries the sim number, the gap and three next steps per row.
- **Details! damage window.** Borrowed: the horizontal bar scaled to the leader, number at the right, rank at the left, class colour on the bar. Ours scales to the top spec, not to the raid total.
- **BiS index and the class header.** Borrowed: eyebrow, the 44 px tab and faction-pill family, the circular ringed crest, the `panel` box.

## 3. Settled design questions

1. **No letters, anywhere.** Round 1 anchored S to D letters to the top spec, which is flagged "stat weights less certain", and split Fury (4.8% behind, S) from Demonology (5.5% behind, A) on 0.7 points with no error shown. The data cannot defend a letter: the band entries carry no per-spec error on `set_dps` (only per-stat weight errors). The choice was "show the error and treat gaps inside it as ties" or "drop letters and headline the gap". Letters need an error we do not have, so the page headlines the gap and does three things instead:
   - Sorted bars, rank number, and **Gap to the top, %** as the headline figure.
   - **Rulers**, not grades: thin labelled lines inside the DPS list at 10, 20 and 30% behind the top (`10% or more behind the top`). They help the eye and make no claim about a spec.
   - **Ties**: a row within 1% of the row above shows `≈ tie` under its gap. 1% is the site's own adoption margin for gear picks (a pick must gain at least 1% to replace another). Chain rule: each row against the one above it. If the nightly later publishes a per-spec error, the tie threshold becomes that error and the rule text changes with it.
2. **Stat weights less certain** is no longer a pill beside the name. It is plain 11 px muted text with a dotted underline on its own line under the class and race, never on the headline, never in a callout. Five words: `Stat weights less certain`. Tooltip adds the longer sentence. It stays visible on phone because it is text, not a tooltip.
3. **Tank sort key: damage taken per second, lower is better.** Round 1 sorted on effective health while the Taken column disagreed (Paladin 3rd took less damage than the Bear 2nd). Reasons for the change: damage taken is the sim's outcome against the boss; effective health is only the hit-point pool going in; a player reading "Taken" next to a rank that disagrees loses trust. The bar is `best / value` (longer is better, like every other bar), the gap column is `More taken than best` (`+15.2%`), the top row reads `Least`. Effective health and threat per second are plain columns; the best of each of the three columns is green (`#7fd48a`) on desktop and phone. Consequence to track: the BiS index headlines effective health for tanks (`tankCopy.indexSpecSummary`); the two pages should not rank differently, so either the BiS index adds damage taken or this is accepted as two views. Raised in section 12.
4. **Units on every column head.** DPS: `Damage per second` with `DPS` under each number. Healer: `Healing per second` with `HPS`. Tank: `Damage taken per second, lower is better`, `Effective health (HP)`, `Threat per second`, `More taken than best`. Gap: `Gap to the top, %`. Bar heads say what the bar is (`Bar: share of the top spec's damage`). No word repeats three times.
5. **Role and faction.** Unchanged: role is the path (`/tiers`, `/tiers/tank`, `/tiers/healer`), faction is `?faction=horde` with both factions prerendered and an inline pre-paint script. What the toggle changes is now visible: every number, and the **race** under each spec name (`band.race`, e.g. Gnome for Alliance warlocks, Troll for Horde). A caption line says `Each spec is simmed as its best {Faction} race, named under it.` Race differs by spec, so it sits on the row, not in one header line.
6. **Row anatomy.** See 4.C. The spec name is the BiS link.
7. **Honesty.** One short line each above the list, the full box below (4.B, 4.D).
8. **Header, wide screen.** Unchanged: full-bleed `.band`, 1344 px centred container, rows keep their width at 2000.

## 4. Layout, region by region

Tokens: `--bg #07090d`, `--raised #0d111a`, `--line #262e40`, `--line-soft #1c2230`, `--gold #e5b955`, `--muted #9a9484`, body `#c9c2b2`, `--strong #f2eee4`. Type: Cinzel 700 for the h1 (30 px desktop, 26 phone) and spec names (15 px); Barlow body 13 to 14 px; JetBrains Mono 500 for every number; `.label` 11 px uppercase for headings and column heads. Class colours from `classes.json` (shaman uses the site's lightened blue `#3f8fe0` for contrast, as `mocklib`). Components: `ArtPanel`-style band, `ClassCrest` 36 px, `FactionMark`, `panel`, `pill`, `btn`, `UpdatedStamp` wording, `Skeleton` and `LoadError`/`StatePanel` from `components/ui`. No new control is invented; the row and the tier header are page markup built from these.

### 4.A Header band
1. Eyebrow (`label`, gold, 28 px rule): `Level 60 · {presets.raid.label}` (from data).
2. h1 `Tier list`.
3. Job line, 14 px body colour, 62ch max.
4. **Your-spec callout** (signed in, the character's spec is on this role's list): `panel`, 1 px gold border, 6% gold tint, max 820 px, crest 36, sentence, one `btn-gold` "Your best in slot →". On the tank list the second line reads `{x.x}% more damage taken than {top}.`; the top spec reads `Takes the least damage.`
5. **Cross-role pointer** (signed in, the character's spec is on another role's list; shown on the other two role pages, new in round 2): one 44 px line, crest 36, `{Spec} {Class} is on the {role} list. See where it stands →`, linking that tab. No panel, no button.
6. Controls row (role tabs, divider, faction pills).

### 4.B Notes above the list
Three short 13 px lines in the body colour, 112ch max, 6 px apart, before the column heads. One fact per line, in the order a raider asks. Text per role is in section 6. The full "How to read this" box stays below (4.D). The lines are what a player must see before reading a number; the box is the rest.

### 4.C List
One `panel` per role, one column-head row above it (`label` 10 px, units per section 3.4).

**DPS:** a single panel of 20 rows (round 1's tier cells are gone). Ruler rows (28 px, `#0a0d15`, 10 px `label`, a hairline filling the rest) are inserted before the first row at or past 10, 20 and 30% behind. A ruler with no row after it is not shown.

**Healer:** 5 rows, no rulers. **Tank:** 3 rows with two extra columns.

**Row (desktop, 60 px min, taller only for the less-certain note):** grid `32 | 36 | 248 | 1fr | 108 | [104 | 104 for tank] | 84 | 210`, 14 px gaps.
- Rank: mono 13 px muted.
- Crest: `ClassCrest` 36 px.
- Name: Cinzel 15 px, class colour, **a link to the spec's BiS page** (underline on hover, gold focus ring). Line 2: `{Class} · {Race}` 12 px muted. Line 3, only when `weights_low_confidence`: `Stat weights less certain`. Gold `Your spec` pill beside the name when signed in.
- Bar: 10 px track, fill in class colour at 90%, zero-based, width = value / top (tank: best / value).
- Number: mono 16 px `--strong` with the unit label (10 px) under it. Tank: the top row's number is green.
- Gap: mono 13 px; `Top` (tank `Least`) in gold bold on row 1, else `−x.x%` (tank `+x.x%`) muted; `≈ tie` 10 px muted beneath when within 1% of the row above.
- Tank columns: effective health and threat per second, mono 14 px, best of column green and bold, others muted.
- Links: `BIS` gold, `GUIDE` and `PLANNER` in the body colour `#c9c2b2` (was muted), 12 px uppercase, 44 px hit height. The spec name and BIS go to the same page by design; the name is the large target, BIS the labelled one. The planner link's `title` is `Open the {spec} level 60 build in the planner`.
- Your row: 2 px gold left edge, 6% gold tint.

### 4.D How to read this
Below the list, `panel`, max 820 px: `label`, two bullets, one muted `Updated {date}` and the link `How we check the sim →` to `/sim/specs`. Repeats nothing the notes did not already say in short; it is the long form for a player who scrolls. No source pills, no build in view (build and engine in the stamp's `title`).

### 4.E Footer
Site footer. Nothing page-specific.

## 7. Phone (390; 360 minimum)

Order: nav, band (eyebrow, h1, job line, callout stacked with a full-width button, role tabs full width equal thirds, faction pills full width equal halves), caption, list, How to read this, footer. The container gutter is 18 px.
- DPS is one `panel` with the same ruler rows. The notes sit above it, three lines.
- Row, three lines: (1) rank, crest 36, name and marks, number with unit; (2) bar plus gap; (3) a three-cell link strip `BIS | GUIDE | PLANNER`, each 44 px tall, hairline dividers. Tank rows add a line `Effective health {n}  Threat/s {n}` between the bar and the links, best of each green; the damage-taken number is the headline number, green on the top row. The less-certain note is a third text line in the name block and wraps nowhere else.
- Never collapses: all three links on every row, the gap, the unit, the tier rule, the honesty lines. Nothing is behind a disclosure (tenet 4).
- Cost: the DPS list is long on phone. The signed-in answer is in the band and the row is marked; that is the mitigation, not a truncated list.

## 8. Performance and polish

- Static prerender of all three role pages with both factions in the HTML; the only script is the inline faction selector (no island above the fold).
- Row height is fixed at 60 px desktop; skeleton rows use the same grid, so CLS stays at or under 0.05. The list is server-rendered, so loading and error states only exist for the your-spec callout and a failed data build; the states sheet shows them.
- Crests are the 128 px WebP set already shipped (4 to 7 KB each), eight classes visible, `width` and `height` set. No icon fetch beyond them. The page needs no spec icons.
- Lighthouse: `/tiers.html`, `/tiers/tank.html`, `/tiers/healer.html` added to `web/lighthouserc.json` under the default matrix (performance, accessibility, SEO at least 0.95, LCP at most 1700 ms, TBT at most 100 ms, CLS at most 0.05). LCP element is the h1.
- Contrast: muted `#9a9484` on `#0d111a` is used for secondary text only; the gap, rank and class name are 12 px or larger.

## 9. Data contract

Read at build time, never typed: for each of the 28 spec files in `data/builds/<active build>/bis/<spec>.json`, the entry with `band == 60`, `preset == "raid"`, `faction` = `alliance` and again `horde`.
- `role` (absent means dps), `race` (shown under the name), `set_dps` (DPS; for healers effective HPS, equal to `metrics.hps`), `talents` (planner link), `weights_low_confidence` (the Stat weights less certain note).
- Tank: `metrics.dtps` (sort key, ascending), `metrics.effective_health`, `metrics.tps`.
- Sort: DPS and healer descending by `set_dps`; tank ascending by `dtps`; ties by name. Gap = (1 − value / top) × 100 (tank: (value / top − 1) × 100), one decimal. Bar = value / top (tank: top / value). Tie = within 1% of the row above.
- Names, class, role: `data/curated/specs.json` (`name`, `class_slug`, `spec_slug`, `role`); class names and colours: `classes.json`.
- Eyebrow: `presets.raid.label`. Updated: the latest `generated_at` across the files, one stamp. `build` and `engine_version` in the stamp's `title` only.
- Not used on this page: `kept_incumbent`, `unconfirmed_within_error_held` (per-slot gear facts; they belong to the BiS page).
- Tank `set_dps` is the tank's own damage, not a rank figure; it is never shown here.
- Also read: `data/curated/heal-profile.json` and `data/curated/tank-encounter.json` for the profile lines. Prose never quotes a nightly figure. The rulers (10, 20, 30) and the 1% tie margin are rules, not nightly numbers.

## 10. What this page is not

Not a letter-graded ranking (no S to D; the data carries no per-spec error to back one). Not a gear page (no slots, items, weights), not a talent page, not a guide, not a promise about raid performance. No hero, no feature pitch, no source pills, no build or engine counters in view. No class filter and no search (28 rows fit one page). No monk row (no monk in this build's catalogue). No PvP or leveling tiers.

## 12. Open questions for the owner and the wow-player

1. **Tie margin.** 1% is the site's gear adoption margin, not a measured per-spec DPS error. If the nightly can publish the sim's error for `set_dps`, the page should show it per row and use it for ties. Until then the rule is stated on screen.
2. **Tank fight length.** `tank-encounter.json` gives the boss swing and level but no duration, so the tank note names no length. Needs a data field before the page can say one.
3. **Tank sort versus the BiS index.** The index headlines effective health; this page sorts on damage taken. Align one to the other or accept both.
4. **Raid utility, mana, one strongest utility line per spec.** The review wants them. They are not in the BiS files; adding them restates the guide and risks tenet 8. Left out, the notes say so for healers and DPS.
5. **Navigation.** No nav item yet. Link from the BiS index, the guides index and the home picker footer.
