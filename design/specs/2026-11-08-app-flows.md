# App flows: the away-from-PC companion

Owner, 2026-11-08:
- "think deeply about all of the user flows we'll need in the app and design the app to be incredibly efficient for those flows";
- a slide-out profile menu like X's ("yes do it") and the priority ruling R0;
- every existing screen stays ("we shouldn't throw the work away that some might find value in"), secondary, never the primary flow;
- the tabs are the app's jobs, not the site's sections: **Home · Raid · Gear · Activity**;
- "make sure the workflows and app layout are absolutely optimized for our users";
- "Everything in the design should be incredibly thoughtful about how our users will interact with the application. Every single thing has a purpose and reason for being there."

That last ruling is a gate (section 6): every element on every mocked screen has a line in the purpose audit (appendix A), every screen states its moment and its 3-second answer, every control is preselected, and what the walkthroughs never touch is cut (appendix C lists the cuts).

This spec sits above `2026-11-07-mobile-redesign.md`. Where the two disagree this one wins: R1 (five tabs), R2 (Home order), R6 (BiS as a tab), R10 (the Guild tab) and R15 (the switcher sheet) are replaced by sections 3 and 4. The visual system, tokens, parity and every other ruling stand.

## 0. Who opens the app, and when

Nobody plays Forever on a phone. The app is opened in the hours away from the PC, or beside the PC on raid night.

| Moment | Who, where | What they want within 3 seconds | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 Raid day, away | a raider at work or on the couch | answer "are you in tonight", see who's coming | 0 taps (the notification) |
| M2 Raid night | at the PC, the phone by the keyboard | countdown, who is in, what to bring; afterwards, the summary | 0 taps (Live Activity), 1 to open |
| M3 Something changed | anywhere, a push arrived | what it means for me: loot, a sim, a report, a tier move, a guildmate passing me | 1 tap to the answer |
| M4 Planning the next session | a few idle minutes away from the PC | the next upgrade, where it drops, what to run tonight | 1 tap from launch |
| M5 Someone sent a link | Discord, a guildmate | the list, build, sim, report or character linked | 1 tap (the link opens the screen) |
| M6 Keep the raid full | an officer | who hasn't answered, ping them, bench or mark in, review an applicant | 2 taps from launch, 1 from the push |
| M7 The casual check-in | opens the app twice a week, pushes mostly off | "what happened since I last looked", then the next upgrade | 0 taps (Home), 1 for the whole list |

Levelers (under 60) live in M3, M4 and M7: the band, its upgrades, the milestones.

Efficiency rules every flow obeys:
- **E1 Zero taps first.** An answer that fits on the lock screen (notification action, widget, Live Activity, control widget) goes there.
- **E2 One tap from launch** to each primary moment. Home is an answer that changes with the time and the player's state, not a menu.
- **E3 The selected character is global.** Every screen answers for the character picked in the menu; switching re-answers in place; no screen asks "which character".
- **E4 Every push and link lands on its answer**, its Back the tab it belongs under (table F4). Never a list the player must search.
- **E5 Offline is not a dead end.** The last answers show with their time; a sign-up made offline queues ("Will send when online").
- **E6 Ask at the moment of value.** The notification permission is asked when the player first answers a raid or picks their main, never at launch.
- **E7 Say who and when.** A raid answer names its character (`as Thandrel, Damage`) only when the account has more than one character in that guild (owner ruling, 2026-11-09); otherwise the answer alone (`You are In`). Every raid time carries the zone (`8:00 pm CST`).
- **E8 Defaults over choices.** Every control opens preselected for the selected character (appendix B); a control nobody changes in the walkthroughs is cut.

### F1 Answer the raid (M1)
- Trigger: `raid.posted`, `raid.signup_nudge` (raid day, unanswered, at the player's reminder hour), `raid.changed`.
- The notification: `Onyxia's Lair · Wed 6 Jan · 8:00 pm CST`, `Are you in, as Thandrel, Damage? 18 in, 3 late`, actions **In, Late, Bench, Out** (iOS on a long press, Android expanded). The answer goes without opening the app (iOS notification action with an authenticated background request; Android action intent), then a quiet confirmation naming the character.
- **Late** asks how late with three chips (15, 30, 60 min): iOS through a Notification Content Extension, Android in the expanded notification; with no choice it records Late. In the app Late opens the same chips under the answers.
- The answers stay on screen after answering, the current one selected: a change of plan is one tap (two for Late with minutes).
- Also from: Home (raid card on raid day, next-raid line on other days), the Raid tab, the next-raid widget, the control widget / QS tile.
- To build: the aps-environment entitlement and categories (iOS), FCM (Android), the Late chips, the offline queue (E5).

### F2 Raid night (M2)
- `raid.starting` (60 min before) starts the Live Activity / ongoing notification: `Starts 8:00 pm CST · 18 in, 3 late · 2 tanks, 5 heals`, your answer, the four buttons in the expanded island.
- The Raid tab leads with **Raid night** while a raid is live (start to start + 4 h): who is in by role with names, then **Ready check**, the private checklist (attunement, flask, food, enchants, resist gear), ticked by the addon's last export and stamped with it (`From your last log-out, 9 h ago`), each missing item with where to get it. Before the pull the same list is Home's `2 things before you pull`.
- After the raid the activity ends on `Onyxia's Lair done · 1 kill · you got Deathbringer · report ready`; the next morning Home leads with **Last night**.

### F3 Keep the raid full (M6)
- Triggers: `raid.short`, `roster.pending`, `raid.late_change`, an applicant.
- The push opens the raid page filtered to **Not answered**, the short role first. The primary button mentions the short role only (`Mention the 2 healers in Discord`), the second everyone unanswered (`Mention all` (4 once Grunvald is marked In)); the preview shows the message (with no bot, `Connect the Discord bot` opens the site). Per row **Mark In** (it confirms on the row with Undo), Bench, Discord DM.
- The filters are Not answered (preselected), In, Late, Bench, Out, All. When the unanswered cannot close the gap (healers short by 3, two healers unanswered) the page lists **Healers on the bench** with Mark In.
- The Raid tab's badge is the officer's open count (unanswered plus applicants, 6 in the mocks), the same number as **Needs you**; a member's badge is 1 while their own next raid is unanswered.
- The applicant push offers **Review** and **Decline**, never Accept: Review opens the applicant (Forever Score, sim, item level, attunement, enchants with the missing slots named, best-in-slot gap, history, their note) with **Accept into guild** / Decline anchored above the tab bar (the raid role comes from her spec when she answers a raid); after Accept, `/ginvite Mirelle` with Copy.
- Creating and editing raids stays on the site ("Edit on the site").

### F4 Something changed (M3, M7)
Every event lands on its answer and is a row in **Activity** (section 4C), pushes on or off.

| Event | Push (example) | Lands on | Back |
|---|---|---|---|
| `loot.awarded` | `You got Deathbringer · main hand, +12.6 DPS` | the item sheet over Gear | Gear |
| `bis.changed` | `Fury Warrior BiS changed: a new pick for Hands` | Gear, the slot open | Gear |
| `band.entered`, `game.milestone` | class-aware: `Level 40: mail, your mount and your 40 to 49 list` (plate for a warrior or paladin) | Gear at the new band | Gear |
| `sim.finished` | `Sim done: 948.6 DPS ±6 (+21 vs your last)` | the sim result | Activity |
| `report.ready`, `coach.ready` | `Onyxia's Lair report ready: 87th percentile` | the report's player view, the coach first | Activity |
| `ranking.best` | `New best: Onyxia, 94th percentile` | the ranking, your row pinned | Activity |
| tier move (weekly, new) | `Fury Warrior moved to S` | Tiers, your spec marked | Activity |
| `guildsim.passed` (new) | `Kaelra passed you in the guild sims` | the guild sims ladder | Raid |
| raid events | as F1 to F3 | the raid page | Raid |

Notification settings (menu): **Raids**, **Gear and sims**, **Logs** on by default; **Guild ladder**, **Tiers** off; **Officer** on for officers. Raids, Guild ladder and Officer show only to a guild member. Two more settings: **Let officers DM me on Discord** (a guild member's, `officer_dms`, off by default; with it off Not answered offers no Discord DM for that member; coordinator 2026-11-08), and the raid-day reminder hour (default 3:00 pm your time).

### F5 Plan the next session (M4)
- Home's **Next upgrades**: three, each with its gain and source (a reputation names the standing it needs), and one **Best run** line naming the best dungeon pick with its gain and chance (`Best run: Blackrock Spire, Blackcrow +1.9 DPS, 8%`). The line is left out on raid day and when there is no dungeon pick.
- **Gear** answers in full: the list it compares against (your spec, band and faction, preselected), **Where to go tonight** (every upgrade grouped by where it comes from: instance, profession, reputation), then every slot against the list. A tap on an item opens the sheet: the client's tooltip lines, where to get it with its chance, the runner-up, who in the guild has it or needs it.
- Where to go needs no new endpoint: it is computed from the static list and loot files (`/data/{build}/bis/{spec}.json`, `loot.json`) the apps already cache, as the web's `lib/bis/page-model` does.

### F6 Open a link (M5)
- Universal links open the matching screen pushed under its tab: `/bis/...` under Gear, `/guild/.../raids/...` under Raid, `/sim/...`, `/reports/...`, `/character/...`, `/guides/...`, `/tiers`, `/b/:id` under Home.
- Anything else opens the site in an in-app browser sheet. Share sends the web URL.
- To build: widen the AASA paths, the router entries.

### F7 Look someone up
- The search button (Home, Raid and Gear toolbars) finds characters, items, specs and guilds in one field, recent searches first.

### F8 First run
1. Signed-out Home (R3): `Play your class better.`, **Sign in with Battle.net**, Pick your class, the raid countdown.
2. Sign in: Battle.net: **Pick your main** (the highest level preselected; it says raid sign-ups are for the main and the head switches it).
3. In a guild: `You're in` on the small line, the guild's name alone as the title (`Olympus XXVII`; a long name wraps over two balanced lines, coordinator 2026-11-08, so no guild name's length decides how the head breaks), `Get raid sign-ups here...` → **Turn on notifications** / Not now. Not in a guild: the ask comes in context (the first sim: "Tell me when my sim is done").
4. Home answers for the main.

### F9 Switch character, F10 account chores
- The profile button at the top left of every tab root opens the menu: Switch character at the top, one tap switches and closes it.
- Notifications, Plan, Devices, Offline data and Settings are menu rows.

## 2. Secondary tools (kept, restyled, never primary)
Guides, Tiers, Patch notes, Sims, Logs and reports (meters, coach), the Planner and shared builds, Rankings. Each is reached from the menu, from an Activity row or a link that names it, never from a tab or the top of Home. Pushed from the menu, they sit under the tab the menu was opened on.

## 3. Navigation: four tabs and the profile menu

**Tabs: Home · Raid · Gear · Activity** (owner, 2026-11-08). Badges: Raid, your unanswered next raid (1) or an officer's open items; Activity, the unread count.

**The profile menu** (the X pattern), opened from a tab root, never from a pushed screen (where the left-edge swipe is iOS Back):
- **The profile button**, the same on all four tab roots, Home included, always at the top left of the navigation bar (owner, 2026-11-08: one trigger in one place; the earlier Home exception, the head's crest and name, is withdrawn). It shows the selected character's **Battle.net portrait** (`avatar_url` on `/v1/me` characters, captured at import by `bnetimport/capture.go`, migration 0026) as a 32 px circle inside the 44 pt / 48 dp target, in a 2 px ring of the faction colour (tokens `color.faction.allianceBar` and `hordeBar`), with the **faction emblem** as a badge on the top right (40% of the diameter, the tier list's faction toggle mark `factionMarkSrc`, a 1 px background-coloured outline so it reads on the ring).
  - Signed in with a portrait: the portrait, the faction ring, the badge.
  - Signed in without a portrait (a new character, a failed media fetch): the default profile image, the neutral person glyph, in the faction ring, with the badge. Never the class crest: the class is Home's header crest and is never drawn twice.
  - Signed out, or with no character: the person glyph alone, no ring, no badge; it opens the menu.
- **Owner exception to the one crest language** (DESIGN-SYSTEM.md tenet 2, apps/shared/README.md): the profile button and the menu's character rows (You and Switch character) use the Battle.net portrait with the faction ring and badge, so the button and the menu match; everywhere else stays the crest (Home's header, Pick your main, rosters, rankings, widgets, the Live Activity).
- Anywhere on a tab root by a swipe from the left edge.
- iOS: a modal drawer, 85% of the width, over the dimmed screen and the tab bar. Android: Material 3 `ModalNavigationDrawer`.
- Contents: **You** (the portrait with ring and badge at 56 px, name in the class colour, `Fury Warrior · 60 · Olympus XXVII`, the Main tag, `Updated 2 h ago from Battle.net · Refresh`); **Switch character** (the others at level 10 and up, each with its portrait, ring and badge at 40 px (the person glyph without a portrait), `Show low-level characters (N)`); **Tools** (Guides, Tiers, Patch notes (the owner's What changed: the game and the site; renamed so it never reads as Home's New for you), Sims, Logs and reports, Planner, Rankings; labels alone, no line under each, owner ruling 2026-11-09); **Account** (Notifications, Plan, Devices, Offline data, Settings); the footer (version and data build, Help, Privacy). Signed out: Sign in with Battle.net and the tools that work signed out.

## 4. The four tabs

### 4.1 Home is time-aware (M1, M2, M4, M7)
`/v1/home` `sections` composes it (`homesections.MomentOf`).

| Moment | When | Order |
|---|---|---|
| Raid live | a guild raid started under 4 h ago | Head · Raid live card (`Pulled 1 h 12 min ago`, counts, your answer, Open raid night) · New for you (the raid group) |
| Raid day | a guild raid later today, in the guild's raid time zone | Head · Next raid card (countdown, your answer, who's coming, what is not ready; an officer's open items: the unanswered with the short role, the applicants) · Next upgrades (no Best run line) · New for you |

| Any other day, in a guild | otherwise, in a guild | Head · Next upgrades · Next raid (one line with its answers, the current one selected) · New for you |
| Leveling | under 60, no raid moment | Head (its fact line says the levels to the next band) · Next upgrades · New for you |
| No guild | at 60, no guild | Head · Next upgrades · New for you · Find a guild (before 9 Dec its line carries the first raids date) |

- **One head on every Home**: the class crest, the name in the class colour and the fact line; no figures. The profile button above it is the portrait, a different image, so nothing is drawn twice. The 3-second answer of each moment (the raid card, Raid live, Last night, Next upgrades) follows the head directly, as before.
- **New for you** is the three newest Activity rows, unread ones dotted, `All activity (N) ›` to the tab with the unread count: the casual's "since I last looked".
- Section types: `head`, `upgrades` (limit 3; the app adds the Best run line from the cached list and loot files and leaves it out when the Home carries a `next_raid` card), `next_raid` (`card` or `line`), `raid_live`, `raid_summary`, `activity` (limit 3, `group` on raid night, `exclude_kinds` the day after: loot.awarded, report.ready, coach.ready, ranking.best), `guild_cta`, `notice`. HOME_SECTIONS rules: `raid_moments`, `leveling`, `find_a_guild`.
- Signed out: the R3 Home, unchanged, with its Forever right now feed (with no character there is nothing of the player's own to show; the feed is cut only for a signed-in player); loading, error and no-character states as mocked.

### 4.2 Raid (M1, M2, M6)
- In a guild (owner ruling, 2026-11-09: the tab does not repeat Home's card; Home keeps the full card):
  - The top is one compact line: the raid, its time, your answer as a chip, with the four answers under it (Late opens its minutes there, F1).
  - Then the tab's content: **Who is coming** (the sign-ups by role with names and the comp, `Healers 5 of 8 · 3 short`), **Before you pull** (the private checklist).
  - Then for an officer **Needs you** (the unanswered with Mention in Discord, the applicants).
  - Then the guild's pages: Roster, Calendar, Loot history, Guild sims.
- During a raid: **Raid night** leads (roles with names), then Before you pull.
- No raid planned: one line (`Your officers plan raids on the site; a push tells you the moment one is posted`), then the pages.
- No guild: **guilds recruiting your spec** with Apply, then Claim your guild. Under 60: `Raids start at 60, 26 levels to go` with Find a guild, no badge. Signed out: Sign in.

### 4.3 Gear (M4)
- The summary line names the list: `Fury Warrior · 60 raid · Alliance`, `4 of 17 slots on the list`, **Change** (the picker: class, spec, band, faction; preselected to the character).
- **Where to go tonight** (instance, profession, reputation), **Every slot** (the list's pick per slot, Worn or the gain). The top three are Home's; Gear does not repeat them.
- Signed out: the class picker, then spec and band.

### 4.4 Activity (M3, M7)
- Every notification event, newest first, grouped Today, Yesterday, This week; each row its title, line and time (the group shows in the icon and the filter, not a label); unread rows marked; **Mark all read**; filters **All** (preselected), Raids, Gear and sims, Logs, Guild ladder, Tiers (the notification groups; Officer rows sit under Raids for an officer).
- Each row opens its answer (the F4 table). The list is the push inbox: a player with pushes off sees the same.
- Signed out: Sign in. Empty: one line on what arrives here.
- API: built in this round (`api/internal/events`, migration 0084): `GET /v1/me/activity?group=&before=&limit=` answers `{ rows: [{ id, kind, group, title, body, link, occurred_at, read }], unread, next_before }`, `POST /v1/me/activity/read { through }` answers `{ unread }`; every routed event is recorded per recipient whatever the push preferences; rows older than 90 days are pruned. The routes mount with `APP_NOTIFICATIONS_ENABLED`.

## 6. The gate
- Every element on every mocked screen has a line in appendix A: the flow it serves, what the user does with it (reads, decides, taps), what breaks without it. An element with no line is not in the mock.
- Every screen states its moment and its 3-second answer (appendix A, each screen's first line); that answer is at the top and nothing above it competes.
- Every control is preselected (appendix B); a control nobody changed in the walkthroughs is cut.
- The persona reviewers (raider, officer, leveler, casual) get appendix A with the captures, and each names the elements they would never look at; those are cut or demoted before SHIP. Appendix C records every cut and why.

## Appendix A: purpose audit
