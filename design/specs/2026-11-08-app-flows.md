# App flows: the away-from-PC companion

Owner, 2026-11-08: "think deeply about all of the user flows we'll need in the app and design the app to be incredibly efficient for those flows." Also: a slide-out profile menu like X's for navigation beyond the bottom tabs ("yes do it"), and the priority ruling R0. Every existing screen stays: "we shouldn't throw the work away that some might find value in", but those screens are secondary, never the primary flow.

This spec sits above `2026-11-07-mobile-redesign.md`. Where the two disagree, this one wins: R1 (five tabs) and R15 (the switcher sheet) are replaced by section 3 here, and R2 (Home order) by section 4. The visual system, tokens, parity and every other ruling stand.

## 0. Who opens the app, and when

Nobody plays Forever on a phone. The app is opened in the hours away from the PC, or beside the PC on raid night. Five moments cover almost every open:

| Moment | Where the player is | What they want | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 Raid day, away | at work, on the couch | answer "are you in tonight", see who's coming | 0 taps (from the notification) |
| M2 Raid night | at the PC, the game open, the phone beside the keyboard | countdown, who's online and signed, what to bring, then the summary | 0 taps (Live Activity), 1 to open |
| M3 Something changed | anywhere, a push arrived | what it means for me: an upgrade dropped, a sim finished, a report is ready, my tier moved, a guildmate passed me | 1 tap to the answer |
| M4 Planning the next session | away from the PC, a few idle minutes | the next upgrade, where it drops, what to run tonight | 1 tap from launch |
| M5 Someone sent a link | Discord, a guildmate | the BiS list, build, sim, report or character they linked | 1 tap (universal link opens the screen) |

Officers add M6: **keep the raid full** (who hasn't answered, ping them, bench or confirm, review an applicant). Levelers (no guild, under 60) live in M3 and M4: the next band, its upgrades, and milestones.

Efficiency rules every flow obeys:
- **E1 Zero taps first.** If an answer fits on the lock screen (notification action, widget, Live Activity, control widget), it goes there. Opening the app is the fallback.
- **E2 One tap from launch** to each primary moment. Home is an answer that changes with the time and the player's state, not a menu.
- **E3 The selected character is global.** Every screen answers for the character picked in the menu (section 3). Switching re-answers in place; no screen asks "which character".
- **E4 Every push and link lands on its answer**, with Back going to the screen it belongs under (a raid push: Back to Guild; a sim push: Back to Home). Never to a list the player must search.
- **E5 Offline is not a dead end.** The last answers show with their time. A sign-up made offline queues and is sent on reconnect, marked "Will send when online".
- **E6 Ask at the moment of value.** The notification permission is asked when the player first answers a raid or picks their main, with one line on what they'll get. It is never a launch popup.

## 1. The flows

Each flow names its trigger, the shortest path, the screens it touches, and what exists today (API events in `api/internal/events/catalogue.go`, screens in the redesign spec section 4.2).

### F1 Answer the raid (M1), member
- Trigger: `raid.posted` (a new raid), `raid.signup_nudge` (raid day, unanswered), `raid.changed`.
- Path: the notification shows `Onyxia's Lair · Wed 9 Dec · 8:00 pm` and four actions: **In, Late, Bench, Out**. Tapping one answers without opening the app (iOS notification action with an authenticated background request; Android action intent). A haptic and a quiet confirmation notification follow ("You're In for Onyxia, 8:00 pm").
- **Late** asks how late with three chips (15, 30, 60 min) in the expanded notification; with no choice it records Late without a time.
- Also from: the Guild tab head (the same four buttons), the next-raid widget, the control widget / QS tile, the raid page.
- Exists: the events, the `RAID_SIGNUP` category, sign-up endpoints, the four-button Segmented. To build: the iOS aps-environment entitlement, the notification categories and their handlers on both platforms, the Late chips, the offline queue (E5).

### F2 Raid night (M2), member
- Trigger: `raid.starting` (60 min before) starts the Live Activity / ongoing notification.
- Lock screen and Dynamic Island: countdown, `14 in · 2 late · 3 heals · 2 tanks`, your status, and the four buttons in the expanded island.
- Tap: the **Raid night** screen. Your status, then **Before you pull**: the private readiness checklist (flask, food, resist gear, consumables, from D12 and the addon export D14), each ticked by the player or by the addon's last export. Then who's coming by role, and the late notes.
- After the raid: `raid.summary` and `report.ready` replace the activity with `Raid done · Report ready · 3 upgrades looted` and a tap opens the summary (F4).
- Exists: Raid night screen, Live Activity (R13), livesurface API. To build: the readiness checklist rows on raid night, the end state.

### F3 Keep the raid full (M6), officer
- Triggers: `raid.short` ("2 healers short for tonight"), `roster.pending`, `raid.late_change`, an applicant.
- Path: the push opens the raid page filtered to **Not answered** with one primary action, **Mention in Discord** (D16: @mention non-signers in the raid channel), and per row: Confirm, Bench, Message (opens Discord DM if opted in).
- The Guild tab carries a badge with the officer's open count (unanswered for the next raid, pending applicants).
- Applicant push: an applicant card (crest, spec, level, Forever Score, sim DPS, guild history) with **Accept / Decline**, both available as notification actions.
- Creating and editing raids, templates and assignments stay on the site; the app links there ("Edit on the site").
- Exists: the events, the guild roster and raid pages, recruit (s5). To build: the Not answered filter, the badge count (`/v1/me` or the guild home), the applicant actions.

### F4 Something changed for me (M3)
One notification per change, each landing on its answer:

| Event | Push text (example) | Lands on | Back |
|---|---|---|---|
| `loot.awarded` (you) | `You got Onslaught Girdle · BiS waist · +18 DPS` | the item in your paperdoll, the gain shown | Home |
| `bis.changed` | `Fury Warrior BiS changed: new pick for Hands` | BiS, the changed slot open | BiS |
| `band.entered` / `game.milestone` | `Level 40: mail and your 40-49 BiS` | BiS at the new band, your gear marked | Home |
| `sim.finished` | `Sim done: 842 DPS ±6 (+21 vs last)` | Sim result | Home |
| `report.ready` / `coach.ready` | `Molten Core report ready: 87th percentile` | the report's player view; the coach card first when ready | Home |
| `ranking.best` | `New best: Ragnaros, 94th percentile` | the ranking row, your row pinned | Home |
| tier move (weekly) | `Fury Warrior moved to S in raid tiers` | Tiers, your spec marked | Guides |
| guild sim passed (new event `guildsim.passed`) | `Kael passed you in the guild ladder (+12 DPS)` | Guild sims ladder | Guild |

- The same items are the **feed** on Home (Forever right now, Your guild), so a player who turned pushes off still finds them.
- Notification settings (in the menu) group them: **Raids** (on by default), **Your gear and sims** (on), **Logs** (on), **Guild ladder** (off), **Tier lists** (off), **Officer** (on, officers only). No per-event toggles beyond these six.
- Exists: every event but `guildsim.passed` and the weekly tier move; the feed. To build: the two events, the landing routes, the six settings groups.

### F5 Plan the next session (M4)
- Path: launch → Home → **Next upgrade** (the top non-raid section): the best three upgrades for the selected character, each with its gain, source (dungeon or raid boss, quest, rep, craft) and drop chance. A tap opens the item sheet: tooltip, where to get it, runners-up, who in the guild needs it too.
- One more tap: **Where to go** on BiS (R6): the upgrades grouped by instance, so "run UBRS tonight: 3 upgrades" is one glance.
- Levelers: the head shows levels to the next band; the upgrades are for the current band; the next band's preview is one row.
- Exists: Home upgrades, BiS paperdoll and Where to go, item sheet. To build: "who in the guild needs it too" on the item sheet (guild BiS data), the instance grouping summary line on Home.

### F6 Open a link (M5)
- Universal links (`applinks:foreversixty.gg`) open the matching screen for: `/bis/...`, `/guides/...`, `/b/:id` (shared build), `/sim/...`, `/reports/...`, `/character/...`, `/guild/.../raids/...`, `/tiers`.
- The screen opens pushed onto its tab's stack (a BiS link under BiS, a raid link under Guild), so Back is meaningful.
- Any other foreversixty.gg path opens the site in an in-app browser sheet.
- **Share** on those screens sends the web URL (R12).
- Exists: the AASA file (paths `/app/*`, `/b/*`) and the entitlement as of build 7. To build: widen the AASA components to the paths above, the router entries.

### F7 Look someone up
- The search button (Home, BiS, Guides toolbar) finds characters, items, specs and guilds in one field (recent searches first). A character opens their character page; an applicant's page shows the officer actions (F3).
- Exists: search screen. To build: guilds in the results, recent searches.

### F8 First run
1. Launch → signed-out Home (R3): `Play your class better.`, **Sign in with Battle.net**, Pick your class (browse without an account), the raid countdown, the feed.
2. Sign in → the characters from Battle.net (level 10+, R15) → **Pick your main** (one tap; the highest level is preselected).
3. In a guild: `You're in Olympus XXVII. Get raid sign-ups here?` → **Turn on notifications** (system prompt) / Not now. Not in a guild: the same prompt when they first view an upgrade or finish a sim ("Tell me when my sim is done").
4. Home answers for the main. Total: 3 taps from install to an answering Home.
- Exists: sign-in, switcher. To build: Pick your main as a step, the contextual permission prompt.

### F9 Switch character
- Tap the crest top left (or swipe from the left edge on a tab root): the menu opens with your characters at the top (section 3). One tap switches; every screen re-answers (E3). The menu closes on the switch.

### F10 Account chores
- Menu → Notifications, Plan, Devices, Offline data, Settings (sign out, delete account). Rare, so behind the menu, never on a tab.

## 2. Secondary tools (kept, restyled, never primary)
Talent planner and shared builds, Your sims and running a sim, Logs and reports with meters and coach, Rankings, Tiers, What changed. Each is reached from (a) the menu, (b) a Home "Next" row or a feed row that names it, and (c) links (F6). None has a tab; none sits at the top of Home.

## 3. Navigation: four tabs and the profile menu

**Tabs: Home, BiS, Guides, Guild.** Guides behind its feature switch (three tabs while it is off). You leaves the tab bar; its contents move into the menu. The Guild tab badge counts the member's unanswered next raid (1) or the officer's open items.

**The profile menu** (the X pattern):
- Opened by the character crest at the top left of every tab root's navigation bar, or a swipe from the left edge on a tab root (not on pushed screens, where the edge swipe is iOS Back).
- iOS: a custom drawer covering 85% of the width over a dimmed screen, the crest top left. Android: Material 3 `ModalNavigationDrawer`.
- Contents, top to bottom:
  1. **You**: the selected character's crest, name in the class colour, `Fury Warrior · 60 · Olympus XXVII`, then `Updated 2 h ago from Battle.net · Refresh` (R15's stamp).
  2. **Your characters**: the others (level 10+), one row each (crest, name, spec, level, Main tag); `Show low-level characters (N)` at the end. A tap switches and closes.
  3. **Tools**: Your sims · Logs and reports · Talent planner · Rankings · Tiers · What changed (each a Row with its glyph; Tiers and What changed also stay segments in Guides).
  4. **Account**: Notifications · Plan · Devices · Offline data · Settings.
  5. Footer: `Version 0.1.0 (7) · data 1.60.1.70658`, Help, Privacy.
- Signed out: the menu shows **Sign in with Battle.net** at the top and the Tools rows that work signed out (Tiers, What changed, Talent planner).

## 4. Home is time-aware

The server picks the order (`/v1/home` sections, O3); the rules below replace R2's order. The phone has one column, so the top section is the answer to "why did I open the app now".

| State | Order |
|---|---|
| Raid day, before the raid, in a guild | Next raid (countdown, your sign-up buttons, who's coming) · Next upgrade · Feed · Guild sims (top 5) |
| Raid in progress (start to start + 4 h) | Raid night card (opens F2) · Feed (Your guild) |
| The day after a raid | Raid summary (loot you got, report ready, your percentile) · Next upgrade · Feed |
| Any other day, in a guild | Next upgrade · Next raid (one line, sign-up state) · Feed · Guild sims |
| No guild | Next upgrade · Feed · Your spec this week · First raids countdown (one line) |
| Leveling (under 60) | Head with levels to the next band · Next upgrade (this band) · Feed |

The head stays three figures (R2) but drops to one line when a raid card leads. Secondary sections (Your spec this week, Top movers) move below the feed.
