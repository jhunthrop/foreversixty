## 6. /reports/[id] and /reports/sample

### 6.1 Frame (every tab)
`Page layout="split"`: main 1fr and the rail (340 px from 1024, 400 from 1760).
- **PageHeader.** Title: the report's title (Marcellus, `--color-strong`). Meta line: `{zone} · {d MMM yyyy} · {n} fights`, then a muted `Tag` `Not credited` whose `title` carries the shipped credit sentence (decision R8), or `Sample report` on the sample followed by `reportCopy.sampleNote`. A credited report reads `Logged by {name}` in place of the Tag. Status `Live` (kill colour) or `Quiet` while live.
- **Answer (decision R1 as ruled).**
  - **Trash pull:** Label `Trash pull · started {hh:mm:ss}`. Figure `big` `{mobs died}` in `--color-strong` with unit `mobs · {m:ss}` (`177 mobs · 12:43`; `1 mob · 0:09`). Caption `{Your group | Group | Everyone in range} · {n} players · {no deaths | n death(s)}`. Never a DPS figure.
  - **Boss pull, the viewer's character in it:** Label `{boss} #{attempt} · {Kill | Wipe at n%} · {m:ss}`. Figure `big` gold `{dps}` unit `DPS`, then ` · ` and the parse `{p} parse` in its parse colour (`73.3 DPS · 87 parse`); a parse not yet known reads `· parse …`, unreachable `· parse ?`. Caption `{name} · {spec} {class} · {damage} damage`.
