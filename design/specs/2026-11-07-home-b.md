## 1. Rulings

### The feed ("Forever right now")
- P1 Log upload (public reports only): `<Name> uploaded <Zone>, <n> bosses in <m> min`; `a full <Zone>` when every boss of the zone is down. Tag LOGS, or YOUR SPEC when the uploader's spec and band are the viewer's. Links to the report. An anonymised owner's character is never named: `<Zone> uploaded, <n> bosses in <m> min`.
- P2 Notable kills: the first kill of each raid boss on the site, from a public report: `<Name> downed <Boss> in <Zone>, the first kill on Forever`. Tag LOGS (YOUR SPEC on a match). Loot is not in report data (it lives in guild-private `raid_drops`), so loot rows are not built (data gap, named).
- P3 Guild raid post, the viewer's own guild only, never signed out: `<Guild> posted <Raid>, <Day date>: <n> in, <m> late`. The counts update as members sign up; the row keeps its post time. Tag YOUR GUILD.
- P4 Rankings, from the hourly board job: a new #1 reads `<Name> took #1 for <Spec>, <band>`; a climb of 5 or more places ending in the top 10, or any move into the top 3, reads `<Spec>, <band>: <Name> climbed <n> places to #<r>`. Tag YOUR SPEC on the viewer's spec and band, else LOGS.
- P5 Best in slot, written by the API at startup from the deployed BiS files (diff against the last seen picks): one change reads `Best in slot changed for <Spec> <band>: <Item> is the new <slot>`; two or more read `Best in slot refreshed for <Spec> <band>: <n> new picks`, one row per spec, band and preset per refresh. Tag YOUR SPEC on a match, else GUIDES. The first run seeds the state and writes no rows.
- P6 Guide updates and site events are floor rows (section 1, Floor).
- P7 Guild sims (owner addition, 2026-11-07), guild members only: `<Name> simmed <figure> <unit>, #<r> of <N> <role> in <Guild> (<Spec>, <band> <preset>)`. With 3 or 4 in the group the rank is left out of the sentence; below 3 the row has no rank at all. A row posts only on a real change: a first sim, a figure 2% or more off the member's last posted figure, or a rank change; at most one row per member per day (a later sim the same day updates that row). Owner over persona: the auto-sim on import and on a gear change posts too (the persona would have left out the first import sim). Tag YOUR GUILD. Links to the saved sim when there is one, else the character.
- P8 Filters: All, Your spec (the spec AND the band), Your guild (hidden with no guild; with a guild and nothing posted it says `Your guild has nothing posted yet.`), Logs (uploads, kills, rankings). Signed out: All only, no filter bar, never a guild row.
- P9 7 rows on home. The API keeps rows 30 days and the feed reads the last 7 days. No `Everything ›` link until a feed page exists.
- P10 The player's own rows win: within the same hour a YOUR SPEC or YOUR GUILD row sorts above a LOGS or PATCH row.
- P11 Each row: an HD icon (the class crest for a character row, the item icon in its quality colour for an item row, the raid or zone icon otherwise), the sentence, the relative time (`4 min ago`, `2 h ago`, `3 d ago`, updating each minute) and the tag.

### Floor (empty states never look dead)
- F1 When a filter has fewer than 5 rows, the island fills to 5 with real dated site events, newest first, marked by their own tag and never as player activity: BiS refreshes per spec (`Best in slot refreshed for <Spec>`, the file's `generated_at`), guide updates (`<Spec> guide updated`, the guide's `updated`), site changelog entries (`<title>`, tag PATCH), addon releases (`Addon <version> released`, tag PATCH) and the first raid date (`First raids open Wed 9 Dec: Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit, Onyxia's Lair`, tag PATCH, shown with its date, not a relative time). Your spec floors with the viewer's spec only. Your guild never floors. No `Example` rows in production.

### Head (P11 to P14)
- The compact head: crest, the name in the class colour, `Level 20 Fury Warrior`, faction and race, `<Guild>`; then three figures:
  1. `<n> DPS`, label `Simmed on import`, caption `Simmed 6 Nov`. While the run is queued: `Sim pending`. Failed: `Sim failed`, link `Run it yourself ›` to /sim. No gear on the import: `No gear imported`, link `Import your character`.
  2. `+<n> DPS`, label `<n> upgrades` (the BiS gains; as today).
  3. Below 60: `<n> levels`, label `To band <next>`. At 60: `<n> of 17`, label `On best in slot`.

### Countdowns (P15 to P17)
- Raid panel: `First raids open · Wed 9 Dec` (eyebrow), days, hours and minutes to `raids-1` in `phases.json`, the three raid icons with their names. Seconds only in the final hour. Once open: `Raids open now` and the three raids, no clock.
- Guild panel: `<Guild> · next guild event`, the clock to the next planned raid, its title, local day and time, `<n> in`, `you: <state>` and `Sign up on the raid page ›`. A guild with no raid planned: `<Guild>: no raid planned`, `Raid page ›`. No guild: `Find a guild`, `Guilds ›`.
- The live dot pulses; under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it is still and nothing slides.

### Rail
- R1 `Your upgrades`: the top 3 (icon, quality name, slot and source, `+gain`), meta `+<total> DPS`, action `Best in slot ›`.
- R2 `Guild sims` (in a guild, the persona put it second): meta `<Role>, <band> <preset>`; the top 5 of the viewer's role, band and preset by each member's latest sim (crest, name, figure, `up n`/`down n` only once last week's rank exists), the viewer's row pinned below when outside the top 5, `You: no sim yet` with `Run a sim ›` when they have none. Hidden with fewer than 3 members in the group. Members whose account is anonymised are left out of the ladder, the count and the feed. Sims older than 30 days drop out. Action `Guild page ›`.
- R3 `Your spec this week`: the grade letter in its tier colour, `<Spec> at <band>`, `Up from B last week` / `Down from A last week` / `Same as last week` only when last week's grade exists, then `#<place> of <n> damage specs` (tanks, healers: no letter, the place only). Action `Tier list ›`.
- R4 `Top movers` for the spec and band, since last week's board (the reset week, Tuesday 15:00 UTC): rank, crest, name, `▲ n`. With no last-week board yet: `Top 3 for <Spec>, <band>` with no arrows. Action `Rankings ›`.

### Signed out (P22)
- PageHeader `Play your class better.`, meta `Pick your class.` and `First raids 9 Dec`, the primary `Sign in with Battle.net`. Then `Pick your class` (9 crests), then the raid countdown panel, then the split: the feed (All, no filters) and the rail `How to start` (ctaCopy wording). The BiS example plate is cut.

### Auto-sim on import (P23)
- When the Battle.net import or an addon sync brings gear that differs from the character's last import-simmed gear, the API queues one server run of that character's gear and talents: the bare preset, the default iterations and encounter, every account (free included). At most one per character per gear change, and at most 12 a day per account. The figure is stored per character for home and the guild ladder.

## 2. API contract

### `GET /v1/feed`
Query: `filter=all|spec|guild|logs` (default all), `spec=<class>-<spec>` (e.g. `warrior-fury`), `band=20|30|40|50|60`, `cursor`, `limit` (1 to 30, default 7). Public; a session adds the viewer's verified guilds' rows to `all` and enables `guild` (signed out `guild` is 401). Cached: anonymous `public, max-age=30, stale-while-revalidate=300`; signed in private. Router-wide rate limit like every public read.

```json
{ "rows": [ {
    "id": "e_123", "kind": "log_upload", "at": "2026-11-06T17:56:00Z",
    "tag": "logs", "icon": "spell_shadow_raisedead", "crest": "warrior", "quality": null,
    "href": "/reports/abc", "date_only": false,
    "segments": [ { "text": "Nightfall", "style": "name", "class": "warrior" },
                  { "text": " uploaded Shadowfang Keep, 8 bosses in 41 min" } ] } ],
  "next_cursor": null }
```
- `kind`: `log_upload`, `boss_kill`, `guild_raid`, `ranking_top`, `ranking_climb`, `bis_change`, `guild_sim`.
- `tag`: `your_spec`, `logs`, `guides`, `your_guild`, `patch`, computed for the viewer from `spec` and `band`.
- `segments[].style`: absent (plain), `strong`, `name` (with `class`), `item` (with `quality`, `item_id`).

### `GET /v1/home?character=<key>`
Session required; the character must be the viewer's.

```json
{ "import_sim": { "state": "done", "dps": 26.2, "simmed_at": "2026-11-06T12:00:00Z", "sim_id": "s_1" },
  "spec_week": { "spec": "warrior-fury", "band": 20, "faction": "horde", "role": "dps",
                 "grade": "A", "last_week_grade": "B", "place": 4, "of": 23 },
  "movers": { "spec": "warrior-fury", "band": 20, "baseline": true,
              "rows": [ { "rank": 3, "name": "Grukk", "class": "warrior", "key": "us/forever/grukk", "delta": 6 } ] },
  "guild_sims": { "guild_id": 2, "guild_name": "Olympus XXVII", "role": "dps", "band": 60, "preset": "raid",
                  "unit": "DPS", "of": 14,
                  "rows": [ { "rank": 1, "name": "Thalrin", "class": "warrior", "key": "…", "value": 612.4,
                              "delta": null, "simmed_at": "…", "you": false } ],
                  "you": null } }
```
- `import_sim.state`: `pending`, `done`, `failed`; `null` when the character has no imported gear.
- Any block is `null` when it has nothing true to say; `delta` and `last_week_grade` are `null` until a previous week exists.

### Storage (migration 0079)
- `activity_events`: one row per feed event (`kind`, `occurred_at`, `spec`, `band`, `guild_id` (set = guild-only), `character_key`, `tag_base`, `icon`, `crest`, `quality`, `href`, `segments jsonb`, `dedupe_key unique`). Purged after 30 days by the hourly loop.
- `bis_pick_state`: the last seen pick per spec, band, faction, preset and slot (the startup diff).
- `tier_grade_weeks`: per reset week, spec, band, faction: role, grade, place, of; upserted hourly so a week's row is its last observed state.
- `spec_board_weeks`: per reset week, spec, band, character: rank and score; the board is each character's average rank-percentile over the band's instances (instance band from `data/curated/instance-levels.json`, the level range's midpoint), public non-flagged kills only.
- `character_sims`: a character's server sims with what the ladder compares (`role`, `spec`, `band`, `preset`, `gear_hash`, `kind` import or addon, `posted_value`), joined to `sims` for the figure.

## 3. Data gaps (named, not built)
- Loot rows: report data carries no loot; guild `raid_drops` is private.
- Guide changes have no per-guide changelog; the floor row says only that the guide was updated.
- A "Share my sims with my guild" toggle: no per-character sharing setting exists today; the ladder honours the account's anonymise switch.
- Tier grades below 60 use the tier list's rules on each band's set DPS; the tier list page itself still shows 60 only.
