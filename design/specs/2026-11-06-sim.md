## 1. Diagnosis of the live pages (measured 2026-11-06)

Captures: `before/sim-landing-*`, `sim-loaded-*`, `sim-running-*`, `sim-live-*` (production), `sim-saved-*` (fixture build).

**Landing (/sim, signed out).**
1. **Four boxes before an answer.** A caps "SIMULATOR" title with a "TOOLS" eyebrow, then a paste card, then "Your characters", "From a build" and "From a logged fight" cards, each gold-edged with an icon chip and a caps title. Three visual weights of card on one screen.
2. **Width.** The page column is 1,248 px at 1440, 1920 and 2000. The wide token (`--page-max` 1792 from 1760) is not applied on /sim: at 1920 the content uses 65% of the screen, at 2000 62% (main x 336 and 376).
3. **Nothing to do without an export.** A signed-out visitor with no export sees four inputs and no zero-input next step, though every published BiS set already has a one-click "View ranking sim" link (the tier list carries 20 of them).

**Signed-out run from the tier list (the live result).**
4. **`run=1` did not start the run.** The tier list's Sim link carries `run=1` ("the run starts once the code's character has loaded", SimView.svelte 538). In headless Chromium, signed out, at all four widths, 45 s after load, the page sat at "Run sim" with "— DPS". Pressing Run produced the result in about 2 s. Engineering confirms or closes this (it may be specific to the headless browser); it is listed as gap G10.
5. **The answer is below the fold.** The run control sits at y 1,116 at 1440 under a full 17-slot paperdoll (the character strip). The result band starts at y 1,245; the damage table at y 1,742. At 390 the band is at y 2,065 and the table at y 2,837. The first 1,000 px of a 1440 screen show the gear, never the DPS.
6. **Three DPS figures, two different errors.** The run control reads "980 ± 2.0 DPS", the band reads "980.1 DPS ± 1.0 · 0.10% error", and the sticky nav repeats "980.1 DPS". The control's ± is `confidenceBand` (1.96 × standard error, `lib/sim/estimate.ts`); the band's is the standard error (`lib/sim/result-band.ts bandError`). One run, two margins on one screen.
7. **The gear is drawn twice.** The character strip's 17-slot paperdoll at the top, then the report's Gear card's 17-slot paperdoll 3,000 px lower.
8. **The settings come after the answer.** Fight style, length, targets, buffs, More settings, the raw request and the engine sha render after the whole 7,700 px report (y about 9,400 at 1440). To change the fight and run again, a player scrolls the full page.
9. **Every region is a card.** Nine gold-edged Cards (Worth knowing, Damage by ability, DPS spread, Timeline, Talents, Rage, Buffs and debuffs, Rotation, Settings), each with an icon chip, a caps label and a caps title. The page is 9,969 px at 1440 and 16,633 px at 390.
10. **The section nav marks the wrong section.** After scrolling to the end and back to the top, "Settings" stays underlined while the band is in view (`sim-live-1440.png`). Observed once per width; listed for engineering.
11. **Data holes on production.** The Extra attacks row reads "From Windfury Totem 19 · An unnamed spell 2"; Deep Wounds, Gloves of Holy Might and Firebolt rows draw empty icon boxes. Spec 2026-11-04 ruling R1 says no empty box at merge (gap G11).

**Saved run (/sim/<id>, fixture build).**
12. **The band is right; the frame is not.** The band (crest, title, 64 px gold figure, error, two buttons, a seven-fact row) and the sticky section nav are the best part of the shipped page. Below them the same nine Cards; four ability rows are in the first 1,000 px at 1440. The page is 7,670 px at 1440 and 13,055 at 390.
13. **The wide screen is unused.** At 1920 and 2000 the report is the 1,248 px column; Damage by ability and DPS spread sit 8 : 4 side by side and every other card is full width.

**What is right and stays:** every figure and its definition (spec 2026-11-04), the timeline (lanes, Execute wash, rage line, close up, keyboard), real icons and the shared tooltips, the honest fallbacks for older runs, "Open in the sim" and "Copy link", Worth knowing, the paste-first path for a cold visitor, the browser engine with no account.

## 3. Reference

- **Details! damage meter.** One row per ability: icon, name, a bar scaled to the top row, the number. Borrowed: the bar as the row's picture, DPS as the sorted column, white swings as their own rows. At 1760 and up the bar column is a fixed 280 px so bars compare across runs at a glance.
- **Warcraft Logs, damage done and casts.** Borrowed: Avg hit and Crit over landed hits, the outcome split under the name, the casts timeline with buff bars on one time axis.
- **SimulationCraft report.** Borrowed: the DPS distribution, mean, error, min, max, iterations, the resource gained by source and spent by ability, the fight settings stated with the result.
- **Raidbots' quick sim form.** Borrowed: the character, the fight and Run on one line above the result, so re-running with a changed fight never leaves the result. Not borrowed: a full-page form before the answer.
- **The in-game character panel** through the shared Paperdoll (`worn` mode): what the character wore for this run.
- **Our tier list and BiS page as shipped.** Borrowed: PageHeader with the answer right-aligned (label, Figure, links), the ControlBar grammar (group labels, outlined segmented control with gold selection, selects with a caret), open Sections under one Plate, and the rail at 1760 and up.

## 4. The pages in primitives

### 4.1 Shared frame
- `Page layout="split"`: main 1fr and a rail of 340 px from 1024, 400 px from 1760 (`--rail-w`). Content 1,248 px at 1440, 1,632 at 1920, 1,664 at 2000, left edge x 96, 144 and 168. The rail is not sticky (it is longer than the screen).
- **PageHeader** for every state. Title Marcellus 40/46 (32/38 phone), `tone="strong"`, once. The crest is `ClassCrest` 56 px (the ringed HD crest). The answer is right-aligned from 900 px with `min-width:360px`; below 900 it stacks under the title.
- **Tool tabs** (`TabStrip`, route mode) in `PageHeader.tabs` on /sim only: `Quick sim`, `Top gear`, `Droptimizer`, `Talents`, `Weights`, `Spec support`. 14 px 600 normal case; the current tab `--color-strong` with a 2 px gold underline. They replace `SimTabs.astro`'s caps strip and `SimPageHead`'s eyebrow and job line. Not on /sim/<id> (decision S5).
- **ControlBar** (the run line) on /sim whenever a character is loaded. Sticky under the site header, 56 px, full-bleed hairlines. Groups: `Fight` select (the shipped `styleLabel` values), `Length` select, `Targets` select, `Buffs` segmented (`Raid`, `None`, `Custom`), `Precision` select (the shipped `precisionLabel` values). `end`: the page's one primary Button. Group labels show from 1920 (`labelsFrom=1920`, as BiS). `More settings` (execute phase, target level and armor, consumables, rotation choice, the raw request) opens from a `More` link in `end` as an Overlay sheet (`SettingsSheet`, existing); the bar stays one line. Phone: the 48 px summary line `Patchwerk · 3:00 · 1 target · Raid · Normal`, then `Change ▾`, then the primary Button at 36 px; Run never collapses.
- **The one Plate** holds the page's primary object: the paste box (no character), the paperdoll (a character, not run), the ability table (running and after). Everything else is an open Section.

### 4.6 The fidelity note
A spec in progress or not yet measured keeps the shipped note, moved into the answer as a caption under the error: `Not yet` Tag (muted) and the shipped sentence, linking `/sim/specs`. It is one line, never a card. A validated spec carries none.

### 4.7 `StatTable` (new primitive) and the ability table
- One CSS grid shared by the heads and every row: `28 icon | name minmax(200px,1fr) | bar minmax(72px,120px) | figures × n at 62 px`, 12 px gaps. From 1760: bar 280 px, figures 80 px. Measured: the six figure columns and the bar fit the 884 px main at 1440 and the 1,200 px main at 1920.
- Heads: 12 px caps `Label`s at .06em tracking, `--color-muted`, 40 px; the sorted head `--color-strong` with a gold `▾`; numeric heads right-aligned. The heads are buttons with `aria-sort` (shipped).
- Row: min 56 px, padding 8 x 16, `--line-soft` divider; hover S2. Name 15/20 700 `--color-strong`, rank 13 px muted, the weapon in its AA-safe quality colour; sub line 13/18 muted with each "Label value" pair `nowrap`. The first figure (DPS) is 700 `--color-strong`, the rest 600 `--color-text`; a dash is muted. The Total row is 44 px, 13 px muted.
- Phone (below 1024): three columns, icon, name and sub, DPS over Share. The sub line gains `24 casts · 1,493 avg hit · crit 52.2%` (each pair nowrap, wrapping between pairs). The bar is hidden.
- Open mode (no Plate): the same grid without side padding, the bar under the name on phone: Rage sources, aura uptimes.
- Replaces `AbilityTable`'s table markup, `SimResourceCard`'s rows, `AuraTable variant="bars"`. The logs report's `ActorRow` mounts the same component.
