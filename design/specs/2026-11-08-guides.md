## 3. Reference surfaces, and what is borrowed

- **Icy Veins and Wowhead spec guides** (what a Classic player opens for "how do I play X"): a summary box of talents, rotation and stats at the top, then sections with a sticky contents list. Borrowed: the summary on top, the section order, the sticky contents. Not borrowed: screenshots of trees, prose and tables in one column with the sides empty, ads.
- **The in-game talent frame**: three trees side by side, tier rows, rank badges on learned talents, unlearned talents greyed. Borrowed for the At a glance build and the leveling lane (`TalentTrees`), with the real game tooltip on hover.
- **The spellbook and the action bar's tooltip**: spell icon, name, rank, then one line of when. Borrowed for every rotation row.
- **WeakAuras and ElvUI option panels**: labels in 12 px caps over open rows, no box per group. Borrowed for the lane blocks.
- **Our own system as shipped**: `PageHeader` with the answer at the right (tiers, setup), `ControlBar` with icon segments (BiS spec), `Plate`, open `Section`s and `Row`s, `RowGrid` (the home class picker), `ItemRow`, `StatTable`, `Facts`, `Trees` (sim talents), `Tag`, `Button`, `Skeleton`, `Menu`.
