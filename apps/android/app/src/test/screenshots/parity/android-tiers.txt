How to read this
How we check the sim ›
What the number is
Damage per second from our own sim against one target for 180 seconds. Every spec is simmed in its best Raid-ready, Phase 1 gear, with enchants a fresh level 60 can buy, raid buffs and consumables, as the guide’s recommended Alliance race, named under it.
What it leaves out
Cleave, add fights, movement and what a spec brings the raid are not counted, so a spec can sit lower here than it plays in your raid. For how specs do in real raid logs, see the log rankings. At a fresh 60 the order differs: see the leveling bands.
Letters and ties
S is within 5% of the top, A within 10%, B within 20%, C within 30%, D beyond. ≈ tie names the specs within 1% of a spec; a tie across a line names the other letter, and the two are a tie.
