# Forever Sixty apps: release notes

The notes for both apps, newest first. `tools/release-apps.sh` reads the `Unreleased` section
as the store's "What's new" text, then renames it to the version it cuts. Write for players:
one line per change, what they can now do, no ticket numbers. Play caps the text at 500
characters; TestFlight's "What to Test" at 4,000.

## Unreleased

- The Raid tab is now the Guild tab: your guild at any level, the next raid with your answer and who is coming, your assignments and your job for the pull, the roster, the newest loot night, recent logs and progression.
