Mage is pure ranged damage with the best toolkit in the game around it: Polymorph, Counterspell, Frost Nova, Blink, Remove Lesser Curse, food and water for the group, teleports and portals to the capitals, and Arcane Brilliance for every caster in the raid.

## What changed from 1.12

Forever keeps that whole kit and rebuilds the three trees underneath, so a 1.12 mage will recognise the spells and still have to relearn the rotation.

- **Personal debuffs.** Improved Scorch and Winter's Chill now only boost your own spells. Every Fire mage keeps their own Scorch stacks, and nobody else's Scorches help you.
- **Ice Block is a talent.** It sits in the Frost tree at 15 points. Fire mages never get there, and the Arcane builds have no spare point for it, so only Frost mages carry Ice Block.
- **No Molten Armor and no Invisibility.** Mage Armor is the raid armor (half your mana regeneration keeps running while you cast) and Ice Armor is for when things hit you. Threat is handled with Arcane Subtlety, Burning Soul and Frost Channeling, and by not out-casting your tank.
- **One hit stat.** Hit is a single stat for every school, and Precision in the Frost tree adds 6% hit to every spell, which is why every build dips three points there.
- **New spells.** Arcane Blast, Arcane Wrath, Ice Lance on an 8 second cooldown, Heating Up, Fingers of Frost and Missile Barrage are new talents, Frostfire Bolt is trained at 40, 50 and 60, and Teleport: Dalaran at 50.

## Races

- **Orc Mage** is new for Forever and gives the Horde a third mage race, with Blood Fury's spell power on a 2 minute cooldown.
- **Skyborne** can be mages too, but only the Alliance High Order Skyborne, and the race is a paid unlock; the Horde Windshapers cannot.
- **Gnome** is the Alliance pick for Arcane and Frost: Eureka! makes your spells 10% stronger and 10% cheaper for 8 seconds, every 2 minutes. Arcane Power comes back every 3, so pair Eureka! with it whenever both are ready. **Human** is the Alliance pick for Fire, a hair ahead of Gnome: Ruthless adds 1% crit, which Fire turns into Ignite. On the Horde, **Undead** is the pick for all three specs, with Orc close behind.

## Raids and leveling

All three are raid specs. Fire builds its Scorch stacks and burns into a big Combustion and needs every gem, potion and rune on a long fight, Arcane puts the most damage into its Arcane Power windows, and Frost is the easiest to level and the safest in a bad pull.
