## Overview

### Strengths

- Ice Barrier is a shield and your biggest damage buff in one button
- Fingers of Frost lets Ice Lance and Frostbolt shatter a boss that cannot be frozen
- Frost Channeling makes every Frostbolt 15% cheaper and 30% lower in threat
- Ice Block, Cold Snap, Frost Nova and the Frostbolt slow make it the safest spec to level and to raid

### Weaknesses

- Little burst: there is no Icy Veins in Forever, and your only window is a Fingers of Frost proc
- Cone of Cold wants you within 10 yards of the boss, where most bosses punish you
- Frostbolt is a long cast, so movement fights cost Frost more than Fire

### In a raid

- **Role:** Steady ranged caster DPS that rarely pulls threat and rarely dies
- **Brings:** Arcane Brilliance, Remove Lesser Curse, Polymorph and Counterspell. Winter's Chill now only feeds your own Cone of Cold.

## Talents and builds

- **Ice Barrier** (1 point): the capstone is a damage talent. The cast adds 15% Frost damage for a minute and another 5% while the shield holds, and you take no pushback while shielded.
- **Fingers of Frost** (2 points): Frostbolt's chill has a 15% chance to treat your next 2 spells as if the target were frozen, for 15 seconds.
- **Ice Lance** (1 point): four times its damage on a frozen target, and Fingers of Frost makes a boss count as frozen. It has an 8 second cooldown in Forever.
- **Shatter** (3 points): 50% more crit on frozen targets, so both Fingers of Frost spells crit most of the time.
- **Ice Shards** (5 points): doubles the bonus of your Frost crits.
- **Improved Frostbolt** (5 points)
- **Frost Channeling** (3 points): 15% cheaper Frost spells and 30% less threat from them.
- **Ice Block** and **Cold Snap** (2 points): 10 seconds immune to everything on a 5 minute cooldown, and Cold Snap finishes the cooldown so you have a second one.
- **Missile Barrage** (2 points, Arcane): Frostbolt procs it 20% of the time, and the proc makes your next Arcane Missiles free and halves its channel.
- **Precision** (3 points): 6% hit on every school. Every mage build takes it.

Frost takes 31 points to Ice Barrier in both builds, with one point each in Frostbite and Permafrost and two in Frost Warding to fill the rows, and two in **Winter's Chill**, the toll to reach the 30 points Ice Barrier asks for; each Frostbolt then has a 40% chance to add a stack for your next Cone of Cold. Fifteen go into Arcane: **Arcane Concentration** (a free spell 10% of the time), Missile Barrage, and three ranks of **Arcane Impact** for 6% more crit on the Missiles it fires, with Wand Specialization and Improved Channeling as the toll to get there.

The last five points are where the raid build and the level 60 build part. In a raid they go into Fire for five ranks of **Incineration**, which adds 10% crit to Ice Lance. Out of a raid, in dungeon gear with no Mana Spring under you, mana runs out before the fight does, so the level 60 build puts three of them into **Arcane Meditation**, which keeps half your mana regeneration going while you cast, and the other two into Improved Channeling, the last Arcane points worth taking: with all five, your Arcane Missiles never lose casting time when you take a hit. Swap them back into Incineration when you start raiding.

If a boss lets you stand within 10 yards, more points in Winter's Chill and Improved Cone of Cold turn Cone of Cold into a real nuke. Most bosses cleave, breathe or stomp at that range, so the build stays at range.

## Rotation and priority

**Pre-pull.** Mage Armor, Arcane Brilliance and your food buff go up. Conjure a Mana Ruby and a Mana Citrine and drink back to full. Put Ice Barrier up before the pull, then start a Frostbolt 2.5 seconds out so it lands just after the tank's first hit.

**Procs.** Every Frostbolt can roll the next one. The Fingers of Frost charge Ice Lance leaves goes into your next Frostbolt with the same crit bonus, and Missiles go out the moment Missile Barrage lights up.

**Cooldowns.** Frost has no damage cooldown. Use your on-use trinkets and your racial on the pull and again whenever they come back. Save Cold Snap for a second Ice Block or for an Ice Barrier that broke early on a fight that hits you.

**Mana in a 3 minute fight.** A geared Frost mage rarely needs Evocation in a raid. Use a Mana Ruby when you are 1,200 mana down and the Citrine two minutes later. Gems and Demonic Runes share one 2 minute cooldown, and the Robe of the Archmage has its own 5 minute one: use it whenever you are 625 mana down. If you do run dry, Evocation is once a fight, near empty with more than 30 seconds left. The one rank-1 Frostbolt worth keeping on your bars is for slowing a runner or an add for a few mana.

**AoE.** Blizzard from range on packs the tank holds; it channels for 8 seconds and costs about 1,200 mana even with Frost Channeling, so it is for big pulls. On a pack that comes to you, Frost Nova, step back out of their reach, then Cone of Cold and Arcane Explosion while they are rooted, and Blink out when the root breaks. Frostbite's freezes let your Ice Lance and Frostbolt shatter trash too.

**Threat and Ice Block.** Frost Channeling cuts 30% of the threat from your Frostbolts, but there is no Invisibility in Forever, and Ice Block does not wipe your threat. It makes you immune for 10 seconds while the boss turns to the next target, and when you come out you still hold your threat. Use it to survive a breath, a bomb or a pull gone wrong, and click it off early when the danger has passed. If you pull threat, stop casting and let the tank taunt.

**Movement.** Spend a Fingers of Frost charge on Ice Lance on the run, Blink through what you need to cross and use Fire Blast. Frost Nova and Cone of Cold are instant too, when the boss is close.

Frostfire Bolt is not a Frost filler: it hits for less than Frostbolt, costs more, and Improved Frostbolt does not shorten it, so it stays a 3 second cast.

## Stat priority

Hit matters up to the hit cap: a missed Frostbolt is a missed Fingers of Frost chance and no chill on the boss. Precision gives you 6% and your gear finds the rest. Frost power is full value for nearly everything you cast on a boss: only Missiles and the odd Fire Blast on the move miss out. Crit is where Frost's damage piles up: Ice Shards doubles the crit bonus of your Frost spells, and Shatter adds 50% crit to every spell cast on a Fingers of Frost charge.

## Best in slot

## Enchants and consumables

Frost spell power on gear and an Elixir of Frost Power are close to full value. Frost rarely needs a mana potion, so the potion timer goes to a Major Spellblasting Potion on the pull.

## Races

Of the Classic Alliance races, take a **Gnome** for Eureka! and Expansive Mind's mana. **Human** adds 1% crit and plays about the same as a Gnome. **Skyborne**, Alliance only for mages, is level with a Gnome, since Wind Blessed's haste never turns off, so pick either.

On the Horde, take **Undead** for Touch of the Grave and a fear break. **Troll** and **Orc** are close behind: press Berserking or Blood Fury on the pull and on cooldown after that.

## Professions

Take Tailoring and Enchanting, since your raid set needs both: Robe of the Archmage is a Tailoring craft and Frozen Heart of the Mountain an Enchanting one, and both bind on pickup, so nobody can make them for you. Enchanting also puts the spell power enchants on your own gear. The goggles in the head slot need 270 Engineering to wear: without it, take the best head you can wear and keep your two professions. Frost drinks fewer potions than the other specs, so it can skip Alchemy.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Frostbolt at 4 is your spell from the moment you have it, with new ranks at 8, 14 and 20. Train Conjure Water with it, Fire Blast at 6 as the instant finisher and Frost Nova at 10.

From 10 to 14 the points go into Improved Frostbolt for a Frostbolt 0.5 sec faster, then Precision from 15 to 17 for 6% hit and Ice Shards at 18 and 19. Frost Warding only adds armor to Frost Armor, so it waits until the deeper rows need its points.

Wand mobs down from half health to save mana: Lesser Magic Wand at 5 and Greater Magic Wand at 13 come from Enchanting, and Riptear's Spare Arm (Rear Guard Patrol, Horde) or Elven Wand (Alliance, Bashal'Aran, Darkshore) are early quest wands. Your trainer's level 10 class quest pays a staff or an orb; take the staff and retire the starter one. Wear cloth quest rewards over vendor gear, Intellect and Stamina first.

### Levels 20 to 60

Frost is the easiest way to level a mage. Open with Frostbolt from range, Frostbolt again as the mob walks in slowed, Frost Nova when it reaches you, step back and finish it.

Frostbolt rank 5 arrives at 26, but stay on rank 4 through 28: the bigger bolt costs more mana than the gear of those levels can carry. When you move up depends on your race:

- **Gnome:** Frostbolt rank 5 from 29.
- **Orc or Undead:** Frostbolt rank 5 at 29 and 30, back to rank 4 at 31 and 32, and rank 5 for good from 33.
- **Human, Troll or Skyborne:** Frostbolt rank 4 through 33.

Frostbolt rank 6 arrives at 32 but only pays from 34, with Mage Armor.

Every point goes into Frost to 40:

- **20 to 22:** Frost Channeling, so every Frostbolt costs 15% less and you drink less.
- **23 to 25:** the rest of Ice Shards.
- **26:** Frostbite.
- **27 and 28:** Shatter.
- **29:** Ice Lance.
- **30 and 31:** Fingers of Frost.
- **32:** the last point of Shatter.
- **33 to 37:** Frost Warding, Permafrost, Ice Block and Cold Snap.
- **40:** Ice Barrier.

After Ice Barrier the points go to Arcane: Wand Specialization at 41 and 42, Improved Channeling at 43 to 45, Arcane Concentration at 46 to 50, Missile Barrage at 51 and 52 and Arcane Impact at 53 to 55. Then Arcane Meditation at 56 to 58, and the last two points of Improved Channeling at 59 and 60.

After a Frost Nova or a Frostbite freeze, Ice Lance hits a mob for four times its damage, and with Shatter it crits more often than not. Fingers of Frost makes Ice Lance a single-target nuke too.

From level 26, when Cone of Cold arrives, AoE grinding packs with Frost Nova, Blizzard and Cone of Cold is the fastest experience in the game for a careful player. It wants Improved Blizzard to keep the pack slowed, so respec into it while you farm and back out for dungeons. Wear Mage Armor from 34 for the mana, and Ice Armor when you are taking melee hits in the open world, since it chills anything that hits you.
