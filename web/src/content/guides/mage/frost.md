## Overview

Frost casts Frostbolt behind Ice Barrier, spends Fingers of Frost on Ice Lance, fires free Missiles on Missile Barrage, and has Ice Block when things go wrong.

### Strengths

- Ice Barrier is a shield and your biggest damage buff in one button
- Fingers of Frost lets Ice Lance and Frostbolt shatter a boss that cannot be frozen
- Frost Channeling makes it the cheapest and lowest-threat mage in the raid
- Ice Block, Cold Snap, Frost Nova and the Frostbolt slow make it the safest spec to level and to raid

### Weaknesses

- Little burst: there is no Icy Veins in Forever, and your only window is a Fingers of Frost proc
- Cone of Cold and Winter's Chill want you in melee range, where most bosses punish you
- Frostbolt is a long cast, so movement fights cost Frost more than Fire

### In a raid

- **Role:** Steady ranged caster DPS that rarely pulls threat and rarely dies
- **Brings:** Arcane Brilliance, Remove Lesser Curse, Polymorph and Counterspell. Winter's Chill now only feeds your own Cone of Cold.

## Talents and builds

- **Ice Barrier** (1 point): the capstone is a damage talent. The cast adds 15% Frost damage for a minute and another 5% while the shield holds, and you take no pushback while shielded.
- **Fingers of Frost** (2 points): Frostbolt's chill has a 15% chance to treat your next 2 spells as if the target were frozen, for 15 seconds.
- **Ice Lance** (1 point): four times its damage on a frozen target, and Fingers of Frost makes a boss count as frozen. It has an 8 second cooldown in Forever.
- **Shatter** (3 points): 50% more crit on frozen targets, so both Fingers of Frost spells crit most of the time.
- **Ice Shards** (5 points): doubles the bonus of your Frost crits.
- **Improved Frostbolt** (5 points): Frostbolt is a 2.5 second cast.
- **Frost Channeling** (3 points): 15% cheaper Frost spells and 30% less threat from them.
- **Ice Block** and **Cold Snap** (2 points): 10 seconds immune to everything on a 5 minute cooldown, and Cold Snap finishes the cooldown so you have a second one.
- **Missile Barrage** (2 points, Arcane): Frostbolt procs it 20% of the time, and the proc makes your next Arcane Missiles free and halves its channel.
- **Precision** (3 points): 6% hit on every school. Every mage build takes it.

One build does it all at 60, in a raid or out. Frost takes 31 points to Ice Barrier, with one point each in Frostbite and Permafrost and two in Frost Warding to fill the rows, and two in **Winter's Chill**, the toll to reach the 30 points Ice Barrier asks for; each Frostbolt then has a 40% chance to add a stack for your next Cone of Cold. Twelve go into Arcane for **Arcane Concentration** (a free spell 10% of the time) and Missile Barrage. The last eight go into Fire: five ranks of **Incineration**, which adds 10% crit to Ice Lance, two of Flame Throwing for 6 more yards on Fire Blast, and one in Accelerant.

If a boss lets you stand within 10 yards, more points in Winter's Chill and Improved Cone of Cold turn Cone of Cold into a real nuke. Most bosses cleave, breathe or stomp at that range, so the build stays at range. [Open the planner](/planner?class=mage) to change the build.

## Rotation and priority

**Opener.** Mage Armor, Arcane Brilliance and your food buff go up before the pull. Conjure a Mana Ruby and a Mana Citrine and drink back to full. Put Ice Barrier up before the pull, then start a Frostbolt 2.5 seconds out so it lands just after the tank's first hit.

**The loop.** Frostbolt is the filler. Ice Lance whenever Fingers of Frost is up and Ice Lance is off its cooldown: it hits for four times its damage with Shatter's crit. Fingers of Frost gives you two charges and Ice Lance takes one, so your next Frostbolt spends the other with the same crit bonus. Missiles the moment Missile Barrage lights up. Recast Ice Barrier the moment it breaks or runs out: the 15% Frost damage only lasts a minute from the cast. Never stop casting to wait for a proc; every Frostbolt can roll the next one.

**Cooldowns.** Frost has no damage cooldown. Use your on-use trinkets and your racial on the pull and again whenever they come back: Berserking on a Troll, Blood Fury on an Orc, Eureka! on a Gnome. Save Cold Snap for a second Ice Block or for an Ice Barrier that broke early on a fight that hits you.

**Mana in a 3 minute fight.** Frost is the cheapest spec, so a geared Frost mage barely touches Evocation in a raid. Use a Mana Ruby when you are 1,200 mana down and the Citrine two minutes later. Mana gems and Demonic Runes share one 2 minute cooldown, and the Robe of the Archmage has its own 5 minute one, but using any of the three locks the other two out for a minute. If you do run dry, Evocation is once a fight, near empty with more than 30 seconds left. Downranking Frostbolt to stretch mana loses more damage than it saves at every level: drink before pulls instead. The one rank-1 Frostbolt worth keeping on your bars is for slowing a runner or an add for a few mana.

**AoE.** Blizzard from range on packs the tank holds; it channels for 8 seconds and costs about 1,200 mana even with Frost Channeling, so it is for big pulls. On a pack that comes to you, Frost Nova, step back out of their reach, then Cone of Cold and Arcane Explosion while they are rooted, and Blink out when the root breaks. Frostbite's freezes let your Ice Lance and Frostbolt shatter trash too.

**Cone of Cold on a boss.** Frostbolt and Fingers of Frost Ice Lances stack Winter's Chill on the boss, and Cone of Cold spends 5 stacks for double damage. Cast it only when you can stand within 10 yards (12 with Arctic Reach) and your mana is above half. At 30 yards it never comes up.

**Threat and Ice Block.** Frost Channeling makes you the lowest-threat mage in the raid, but there is no Invisibility in Forever, and Ice Block does not wipe your threat. It makes you immune for 10 seconds while the boss turns to the next target, and when you come out you still hold your threat. Use it to survive: a breath, a bomb, a pull gone wrong, a mechanic you cannot outrun. Click it off early when the danger has passed. If you pull threat, stop casting and let the tank taunt.

**Movement.** Frostbolt needs you standing still. Spend a Fingers of Frost charge on Ice Lance on the run, Blink through what you need to cross and use Fire Blast. Frost Nova and Cone of Cold are instant too, when the boss is close.

Frostfire Bolt is not a Frost filler: it hits for less than Frostbolt, costs more, and Improved Frostbolt does not shorten it, so it is still close to a 3 second cast.

## Stat priority

Hit and spell power are your two best stats until the hit cap: a missed Frostbolt is a missed Fingers of Frost and Missile Barrage chance. Frost spell power is full value for nearly everything you cast on a boss: only Missiles and the odd Fire Blast on the move miss out. Frost needs less Intellect than Arcane or Fire, since it is the spec that does not run out.

## Enchants and consumables

Frost casts Frostbolt and Ice Lance for nearly all its damage, so Frost spell power on gear and an Elixir of Frost Power are close to full value. A Major Mana Potion is enough for most bosses.

## Races

**Gnome** is the best Alliance Frost mage: Eureka! is 10% more damage and 10% cheaper spells for 8 seconds on a 2 minute cooldown, and Expansive Mind adds 5% mana. **Human** adds 1% crit and is just behind. **Skyborne** edges a Gnome by a hair in a raid with Wind Blessed, 2% haste that never turns off, but it needs the paid Skyborne Heroic Pack, so take it only if you already own it. On the Horde, **Undead** is the pick: Touch of the Grave gives your spells a 30% chance to drain 5% of your maximum health from the boss, at most once every 15 seconds, and Will of the Forsaken breaks fears. **Troll** and **Orc** are close behind: Berserking adds 10% cast speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes and Blood Fury 10% spell power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes, so press either on the pull and on cooldown after that.

## Professions

Tailoring and Enchanting are the standard pair: Tailoring makes the early cloth sets and Enchanting puts the spell power enchants on your own gear. Frost drinks fewer potions than the other specs, so it can skip Alchemy. Gnomes who want the explosives take Engineering.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

- Frostbolt at 4 is your spell from the moment you have it, with new ranks at 8, 14 and 20. Train Conjure Water with it, Fire Blast at 6 as the instant finisher and Frost Nova at 10.
- At 10 and 11 the points go into Frost Warding, 30% more armor from Frost Armor, then Improved Frostbolt from 12 to 16 for a Frostbolt 0.5 sec faster, and Precision from 17 to 19 for 6% hit.
- Wand mobs down from half health to save mana: Lesser Magic Wand at 5 and Greater Magic Wand at 13 come from Enchanting, and Riptear's Spare Arm (Rear Guard Patrol, Horde) or Elven Wand (Bashal'Aran, Darkshore) are early quest wands. Your trainer's level 10 class quest pays a staff, an orb or a wand; take the staff and retire the starter one. Wear cloth quest rewards over vendor gear, Intellect and Stamina first.

### Levels 20 to 60

Frost is the easiest way to level a mage. Open with Frostbolt from range, Frostbolt again as the mob walks in slowed, Frost Nova when it reaches you, step back and finish it. Every point goes into Frost to 40: Ice Shards in the early 20s, Frostbite, Permafrost and Frost Channeling in the late 20s, Ice Lance at 30 and Ice Block at 31, because it saves the run more than once. After a Frost Nova or a Frostbite freeze, Ice Lance hits a mob for four times its damage, and once Shatter is in by 34 it crits. Fingers of Frost at 36 makes Ice Lance a single-target nuke too, and Ice Barrier lands at 40. From level 26, when Cone of Cold arrives, AoE grinding packs with Frost Nova, Blizzard and Cone of Cold is the fastest experience in the game for a careful player. It wants Improved Blizzard to keep the pack slowed, so respec into it while you farm and back out for dungeons. Train Mage Armor at 34 for the mana; wear Ice Armor instead when you are taking melee hits in the open world, since it chills anything that hits you.
