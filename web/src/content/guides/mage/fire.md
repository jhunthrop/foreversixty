## Overview

Fire spams Scorch, keeps its own Improved Scorch stacks up, turns crits into fast Pyroblasts with Heating Up, and cashes Combustion into a run of Ignites.

### Strengths

- Scorch is 150 mana for a 1.5 second cast, so you land two for every Fireball
- Every Scorch and Fire Blast crit pays twice, once through Ignite and once through Heating Up
- Fire Power rewards you for every burn you keep on the boss
- Fire Blast is instant and a 3-stack Pyroblast is short enough to cast between steps, so you keep doing damage through movement

### Weaknesses

- Runs out of mana before a long boss dies without gems, potions and runes
- No Presence of Mind and no Ice Block: they sit in the Arcane and Frost trees, which this build only dips into
- Fire-immune and Fire-resistant bosses, Ragnaros first among them, shut the spec down

### In a raid

- **Role:** Sustained ranged caster DPS with a Combustion window every 3 minutes
- **Brings:** Arcane Brilliance, Remove Lesser Curse, Polymorph and Counterspell. Improved Scorch now only boosts your own Fire damage, so every Fire mage stacks their own.

## Talents and builds

- **Improved Scorch** (3 points): every Scorch that lands adds 3% Fire damage taken from you, up to 5 stacks for 30 seconds. A missed Scorch adds no stack.
- **Heating Up** (1 point): Scorch, Fire Blast and Fireball crits cut your next Pyroblast's cast by 25%, up to 3 stacks, and every Fire Blast hit adds a stack on top. Three stacks make Pyroblast a 1.5 second cast.
- **Pyroblast** (1 point): the payoff for Heating Up, and the spell you cast before the pull.
- **Ignite** (5 points): every Fire crit burns the boss for another 40% of the hit over 4 seconds.
- **Fire Power** (3 points): 3% more Fire damage for each of your Fire effects on the target, up to 12%. Improved Scorch, Ignite and Pyroblast's burn are three of them.
- **Incineration** and **Critical Mass** (8 points): 10% more crit on Scorch and Fire Blast and 6% more on every Fire spell, which is what keeps Ignite and Heating Up rolling.
- **Master of Elements** (3 points): every Fire crit refunds 30% of the spell's base cost. It is the steady trickle that makes the gems and potions last.
- **Improved Fire Blast** (2 points): Fire Blast every 6 seconds instead of 8, and 15 yards more range. With Heating Up, that is a Pyroblast stack every 6 seconds.
- **Combustion** (1 point): each Fire hit adds 10% Fire crit until three non-periodic crits land, on a 3 minute cooldown.

Fire takes exactly 31 points to Combustion and skips Blast Wave and Improved Flamestrike, which are AoE only. Combustion asks for 30 points above it, and the damage talents plus two ranks of **Flame Throwing** (6 more yards on every Fire spell) come to 28, so two more points are the toll to reach it.

The other 20 points buy mana and hit. Seventeen in Arcane reach **Arcane Concentration** (5 points, a free spell 10% of the time) and two ranks of **Arcane Meditation** (a third of your mana regeneration keeps running while you cast). With Mage Armor on top, most of your regeneration runs through the whole fight. Three go into **Precision** in Frost for 6% hit.

The 60 build pays that toll with a point in **Impact**, a 3% chance to stun a mob for 2 seconds while you farm, and a point in Accelerant. The raid build pays it with two ranks of **Burning Soul** instead: 20% less threat from your Fire spells and a 47% chance to keep a cast from being pushed back. It also moves the point of Magic Absorption into a third rank of Arcane Resilience. [Open the planner](/planner?class=mage) to change the build.

## Rotation and priority

**Opener.** Mage Armor, Arcane Brilliance and your food buff go up before the pull. Conjure a Mana Ruby and a Mana Citrine and drink back to full. Start Pyroblast 6 seconds before the pull so it lands just after the tank's first hit: the full cast happens while nothing else is going on, and its burn starts your Fire Power count. Then Scorch to 5 stacks. Fire Blast whenever it is ready.

**The loop.** Keep 5 Improved Scorch stacks rolling: every filler Scorch refreshes them, so you only think about the refresh after a movement break or a target swap. Fire Blast every time it is off cooldown. Pyroblast only on 3 stacks of Heating Up. Everything else is Scorch. A Pyroblast at the full 6 seconds, or on 1 or 2 stacks, loses to Scorch, and Fireball as a filler loses to Scorch even while your mana is full.

**Combustion.** Press it once the boss carries 5 stacks, together with your on-use trinkets and your racial: Eureka! on a Gnome, Blood Fury on an Orc, Berserking on a Troll. A fresh tank needs a few seconds before you do. Combustion lasts until three crits land, and every Scorch or Fire Blast crit in it is an Ignite and a Heating Up stack, so the window usually ends in a 1.5 second Pyroblast.

**Ignite and threat.** Ignite's damage is your damage and your threat. Fire mages pull more than anyone else in the first twenty seconds, mostly off a Combustion window that crits three times in a row. You have no Ice Block and no Invisibility, so the only fix is not to pull: watch your threat, hold Combustion on a fresh tank, and stop casting for a global if you are close. Burning Soul takes some off the top.

**Mana in a 3 minute fight.** Fire runs dry on a long fight, though not as fast as Arcane. In order: a Mana Ruby when you are 1,200 mana down and a Major Mana Potion, then the Mana Citrine or a Demonic Rune two minutes after the Ruby. Mana gems and Demonic Runes share one 2 minute cooldown, so it is a gem or a rune, never both at once. The Robe of the Archmage has its own 5 minute cooldown, but using any of the three locks the other two out for a minute. Evocation is an 8 minute cooldown and comes once a fight: channel it near empty with more than 30 seconds left, never in the last 30. Keep Fire Blast on cooldown even when you are short: every cast is a Heating Up stack, and the mana you would save is worth less than the Pyroblasts you would lose. Scorch stays your filler when you are short too: you land two of them in the time of one Fireball, and each is a roll for Heating Up and Ignite.

**AoE.** Flamestrike is a 3 second cast that burns the ground for 8 seconds, so cast it where the tank is going to stand, then follow with Arcane Explosion in the pack or a Blizzard from range. Flamestrike costs 990 mana, so two of them on a big pull eat a third of your bar before the Arcane Explosions start. Let the tank get a few seconds of threat first, and Frost Nova and Blink out if the pack turns on you.

**Execute and movement.** Fire has no execute. On the move, keep Fire Blast on cooldown and save a Pyroblast on 3 Heating Up stacks for the next stop. Scorch's stacks last 30 seconds, so a short movement phase does not drop them.

Frostfire Bolt is not a Fire filler. It costs more than Scorch, takes twice as long and its burn mixes Frost and Fire, which Fire Power does not count.

## Stat priority

The table above gives the order for your gear; this is why each stat counts. Hit matters up to the hit cap: every missed Scorch is a missed stack, a missed Ignite and a missed Heating Up chance. Precision gives you 6%, your gear finds the rest, and once your gear reaches the cap more hit adds nothing. Spell power and Fire power are the same stat to you, because everything you cast on a boss is Fire. Crit feeds Ignite, Heating Up and Master of Elements at once, and Critical Mass and Combustion stack more of it on top of your gear. Intellect is your mana bar and adds spell crit, which Fire turns into Ignite and Heating Up. Fire drinks a Major Mana Potion on every boss, so a deeper bar keeps you casting to the end.

## Best in slot

## Enchants and consumables

Fire casts only Fire spells on a boss, so any Fire-only spell power on gear is full value for you. Greater Arcane Elixir is your elixir. Bring Major Mana Potions and Demonic Runes to every boss; you will use both.

## Races

**Human** is the Alliance pick for Fire, a hair ahead of Gnome: Ruthless adds 1% crit with all spells, which Fire turns into Ignite and Heating Up, and The Human Spirit adds 10% Spirit. **Gnome** is just behind: Eureka! is 10% more damage and 10% cheaper spells for 8 seconds on a 2 minute cooldown, and Expansive Mind adds 5% mana, so press Eureka! with Combustion on the pull and again the moment it is back. On the Horde, **Undead** is the pick: Touch of the Grave gives your spells a 30% chance to drain 5% of your maximum health from the boss, at most once every 15 seconds, and Will of the Forsaken breaks fears. **Orc** is next: Blood Fury adds 10% spell power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes, so press it with Combustion on the pull and on cooldown after that. **Troll** Berserking adds 10% cast speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes and lines up with every Combustion, but falls behind both. **Skyborne** is a paid unlock and only Alliance for mages, and played without Read Leyline it does the least Fire damage of the lot.

## Professions

Tailoring and Enchanting are the standard pair: Tailoring makes the early cloth sets and Enchanting puts the spell power enchants on your own gear. Alchemy pays for itself once you raid every week, because Fire drinks a Major Mana Potion on every boss. Gnomes who want the explosives take Engineering.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Fireball is trained at 1 with new ranks at 6, 12 and 18, so visit the trainer every even level. Frostbolt at 4 is your slow, Fire Blast at 6 is the instant finisher, Frost Nova at 10 buys distance and Flamestrike at 16 hits packs.

From 10 to 14 put five points into Incineration, 10% more crit on Fire Blast, then Accelerant at 15 to cut 0.1 sec off Fireball, and Ignite from 16 to 19 so every Fire crit burns for more.

Wand mobs down from half health to save mana: Lesser Magic Wand at 5 and Greater Magic Wand at 13 come from Enchanting, and Riptear's Spare Arm (Rear Guard Patrol, Horde) or Elven Wand (Alliance, Bashal'Aran, Darkshore) are early quest wands. Your trainer's level 10 class quest pays a staff or an orb; take the staff and retire the starter one. Wear cloth quest rewards over vendor gear, Intellect and Stamina first.

### Levels 20 to 60

Fireball is your nuke until Scorch at 22, then Scorch and Fire Blast take over on single targets. Fire levels best from 30, once Improved Scorch and Heating Up are in, and Impact now and then stops a caster or a runner for you. Open on a mob with Pyroblast from range, Scorch or Fireball as it closes, and finish it with Fire Blast. Keep Frost Nova and Blink for anything that reaches you, and Frostbolt for kiting: none of your Fire spells slow until Frostfire Bolt at 40, and Frostbolt is still the cheaper slow after that. Flamestrike into Arcane Explosion clears packs once you have the Stamina for it. Train Mage Armor at 34 and wear it for the mana; switch to Ice Armor when you are taking melee hits in the open world.
