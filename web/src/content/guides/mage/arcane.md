## Overview

Arcane stacks Arcane Blast for spell damage, fires free Missiles on Missile Barrage and saves its burst for Arcane Power. In a raid it fills with Fireball; leveling, with Frostbolt.

### Strengths

- The best burst a mage has: Arcane Power's 15 second window, with Presence of Mind and your trinkets stacked into it
- Arcane Blast turns 12% of your Intellect per stack into spell damage, up to 48% at four stacks
- Missile Barrage makes a big share of your damage free
- On the way to 60, the Frostbolt slow keeps adds off you while you blast

### Weaknesses

- The most mana-hungry spec: a stacked Arcane Blast costs five times a fresh one
- No Ice Block: the leveling build spends its 16 Frost points on Precision and the Frostbolt filler, and the raid build keeps only Precision in Frost
- Arcane Power's burst pulls threat on a tank who is still building

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged caster DPS with a big opening and a big window every 3 minutes
- **Brings:** Arcane Brilliance, Remove Lesser Curse, Polymorph and Counterspell. Arcane Subtlety's resistance cut helps only your own spells, not the raid's.

## Talents and builds

- **Arcane Blast** (1 point): the spec. Each cast gives you 12% of your Intellect as spell damage and raises the next Blast's cost by 100%, up to 4 stacks for 18 seconds from the first Blast.
- **Missile Barrage** (2 points): Arcane Blast procs it 40% of the time, and Arcane Wrath, Fireball and Frostbolt 20%. The proc makes your next Arcane Missiles free (30% of its cost inside Arcane Power), halves its channel and fires a missile every half second.
- **Arcane Concentration** (5 points): 10% of your damage spells make the next one free, and that includes a 4-stack Arcane Blast.
- **Arcane Meditation** (3 points): half your mana regeneration keeps running while you cast. With Mage Armor on top, all of it runs, and on a boss that regeneration is what pays for the fourth and fifth Blast.
- **Presence of Mind** (1 point): your next spell under 10 seconds is instant. In a raid spend it on Pyroblast, your longest cast, from 54; before that and with the leveling build, spend it on Frostbolt. Never spend it on a 1.5 second Arcane Blast that the global cooldown already caps.
- **Arcane Power** (1 point): 30% more spell damage for 15 seconds at 30% more mana per cast, on a 3 minute cooldown.
- **Arcane Mind** and **Arcane Instability** (8 points): 10% Intellect, which Arcane Blast turns into spell damage, and Arcane Mind also doubles the bonus on your Arcane crits. Arcane Instability adds 3% damage and 3% crit.
- **Improved Frostbolt** and **Ice Shards** (10 points, leveling build): Frostbolt is half your casts while you level, and once both are in, by 57, a 2.5 second Frostbolt with full crit damage is the filler that pays for the Blast stacks.
- **Arcane Wrath** (1 point, raid build): a 3.3 second Arcane bolt with Accelerant on a 6 second cooldown that adds 5% of your maximum mana as spell damage. Cast it every time it is ready while you hold 3 Blast stacks.
- **Ignite**, **Pyroblast**, **Improved Fire Blast** and **Heating Up** (9 points, raid build): Fire Blast every 6 seconds, and every Fire Blast hit and every Fire Blast or Fireball crit cuts your next Pyroblast's cast by 25%, up to 3 stacks. Three stacks make Pyroblast a 1.5 second cast, and every Fire crit burns for another 40% through Ignite.
- **Precision** (3 points): 6% hit on every school. Take it in every build.

The leveling build spends 31 points in Arcane for Arcane Power, then goes to Frost for the filler: **Frost Channeling** (3 points: 15% cheaper Frostbolts and 30% less threat from them), all five ranks of Ice Shards, and four ranks of **Incineration** in Fire for Arcane Blast crit. Improved Channeling stays at four ranks so mobs hitting you do not push back every Blast, and one point of Arcane Geometry adds 3 yards to Arcane Blast and Missiles, so you can blast from a little further out.

The raid build puts 32 points in Arcane and takes its second tree from Fire. Both ranks of **Arcane Subtlety** cut 30% of the threat from your Arcane spells, which is what lets you press Arcane Power early in a pull. They and Arcane Wrath are paid for by a rank of Improved Channeling, the point of Arcane Geometry and one point brought over from Frost, which makes 32 in Arcane. Bosses do not push back your casts the way packs of mobs do, and a boss you already reach from 30 yards needs no extra range, so neither of those two costs you anything. All three ranks of Arcane Meditation stay in, since a raid fight is paid for in mana. Sixteen go into Fire: five ranks of Incineration for Fire Blast and Arcane Blast crit, two of **Accelerant** to take 0.2 seconds off Fireball and Arcane Wrath, then Ignite, Pyroblast, Improved Fire Blast and Heating Up. Precision's three points are all it keeps in Frost.

Neither build takes Fire's Impact: its stun does nothing to a boss. [Open the planner](/planner?class=mage) to change the build.

## Rotation and priority

**Opener.** Mage Armor, Arcane Brilliance and your food buff go up before the pull. Conjure a Mana Ruby and a Mana Citrine and drink back to full. In a raid, start Pyroblast 6 seconds before the pull so it lands just after the tank's first hit: the whole cast happens while nothing else is going on, and if it crits, that is your first Ignite. With the leveling build, start a Frostbolt 2.5 seconds out instead; it can roll Missile Barrage before the fight starts. Blast twice while the tank puts a couple of Sunders up, then press Arcane Power, Presence of Mind, your trinkets and your racial together, put the instant Pyroblast out (an instant Frostbolt with the leveling build) and go back to blasting.

**The loop in a raid.** Blast to 3 stacks. Missiles the moment Missile Barrage lights up, Pyroblast on 3 Heating Up stacks when it is a 1.5 second cast, Fire Blast every time it is ready, and Arcane Wrath every time it comes off its 6 second cooldown while you hold 3 stacks. Past 3, keep blasting only while your mana bar is fuller than the boss's health bar and you are above 20% mana; otherwise Fireball fills until the 18 seconds run out. When you are too low on mana for a Fireball, cast a Frostbolt, which costs a third less; it feeds no Ignite or Heating Up, so it only stretches the last of your mana. On the way to 60 there is no Arcane Wrath and no Pyroblast: Blast to 3 stacks, Frostbolt as the filler, and Fire Blast only to finish a mob or on the move. The stacks do not refresh, so the next Blast starts a fresh, cheap set. Missiles go out the moment Missile Barrage lights up, whatever you were doing. A Clearcasting proc makes the next spell free, so a Clearcasting on 3 stacks is a free fourth Blast.

**Arcane Power windows.** Arcane Power and Presence of Mind are both 3 minute cooldowns, so press them together with your on-use trinkets and your racial: Eureka! on a Gnome, Blood Fury on an Orc, Berserking on a Troll. In a 3 minute fight you get one window, on the pull. The second is ready as the boss dies, so on a longer fight hold it for a phase where the boss takes extra damage or the adds are down.

**Mana in a 3 minute fight.** You will run dry. In order: a Mana Ruby when you are 1,200 mana down, a Major Mana Potion the next time you are well down, then the Mana Citrine or a Demonic Rune two minutes after the Ruby. Mana gems and Demonic Runes share one 2 minute cooldown, so it is a gem or a rune, never both at once. The Robe of the Archmage has its own 5 minute cooldown, but using any of the three locks the other two out for a minute. Evocation is an 8 minute cooldown and comes once a fight: channel it when the bar is near empty with more than 30 seconds left, and never in the last 30 seconds, where the mana comes back too late to spend.

**AoE.** Arcane Explosion is instant and hits everything within 10 yards, so you stand in the pack with the tank and spam it. It costs 390 mana a cast, which drains a full bar in under a minute: let the tank get two or three seconds of threat, Frost Nova when the pack turns on you and Blink out. On spread packs or anything that hits hard, stand back and Blizzard instead.

**Threat.** You out-burst the tank in the first ten seconds. The leveling build's Frost Channeling cuts 30% of the threat from your Frostbolts, and the raid build's Arcane Subtlety does the same for your Arcane spells. Your Fire spells get no cut, since the raid build has no Burning Soul. There is no Invisibility, and the build has no spare point for Ice Block: if the boss turns, stop casting, let the tank taunt and wait. On a fresh pull, give the tank a Sunder or two before Arcane Power.

**Movement.** Arcane Blast, Arcane Wrath, Fireball, Frostbolt, Missiles and a 1.5 second Pyroblast all need you standing still. If you know a move is coming, hold Presence of Mind for an instant Pyroblast on the run (an instant Frostbolt with the leveling build), Blink through anything you need to cross, and use Fire Blast, or Arcane Explosion when the boss walks into you.

## Stat priority

The table above gives the order for your gear; this is why each stat counts. Hit matters up to the hit cap: a missed Blast deals nothing but still costs the full stacked price and still raises the cost of the Blast after it. Precision gives you 6%, your gear finds the rest, and once your gear reaches the cap more hit adds nothing. Spell power goes into every spell you cast. Arcane power covers your Blasts, Arcane Wrath and Missiles but not your Fire spells in a raid or your Frostbolts on the way to 60. Each Arcane Blast stack adds 12% of your Intellect as spell damage and Arcane Mind adds 10% more Intellect, so for you Intellect is spell damage on top of the spell crit and the mana bar every mage gets from it. Crit hits harder on your Arcane spells, because Arcane Mind doubles their crit bonus, and in a raid every Fire Blast or Fireball crit is an Ignite and a Heating Up stack.

## Best in slot

## Enchants and consumables

Arcane casts spells of two schools, Arcane and Fire in a raid and Arcane and Frost on the way to 60, so spell power that names one school covers only part of your damage: Arcane power covers your Blasts, Arcane Wrath and Missiles, and Fire power only your Fire Blasts, Pyroblasts and Fireballs. Bring Major Mana Potions and Demonic Runes to every boss; you will use both.

## Races

**Gnome** is the best Alliance Arcane mage: Eureka! makes your damage spells, all but Arcane Wrath, hit 10% harder and cost 10% less for 8 seconds on a 2 minute cooldown, and Expansive Mind adds 5% mana. Press it with Arcane Power on the pull and again the moment it is back. **Human** is close behind with 1% crit and 10% Spirit, and Arcane Meditation and Mage Armor keep all of that Spirit's regeneration running while you cast. On the Horde, **Undead** is the pick: Touch of the Grave gives your spells a 30% chance to drain 5% of your maximum health from the boss, at most once every 15 seconds, and Will of the Forsaken breaks fears. **Orc** is next: Blood Fury adds 10% spell power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes, so press it with Arcane Power on the pull and on cooldown after that. **Troll** Berserking adds 10% cast speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes and lines up with every Arcane Power. **Skyborne** is a paid unlock and only Alliance for mages. Played without Read Leyline (50% more health and mana regeneration for 15 seconds, 2 minute cooldown), Wind Blessed's 2% haste alone does less damage than a Gnome or a Human.

## Professions

Tailoring and Enchanting are the standard pair: Tailoring makes the early cloth sets and Enchanting puts the spell power enchants on your own gear. Alchemy pays for itself once you raid every week, because you will drink a Major Mana Potion every boss. Gnomes who want the explosives take Engineering.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Train Frostbolt and Conjure Water at 4, Fire Blast at 6, Arcane Missiles at 8, Frost Nova at 10 and Arcane Explosion at 14. From 8, open with Frostbolt so the mob walks in slowed, channel Missiles as it closes and finish with Fire Blast.

At 10 the first point goes into Wand Specialization for 13% more wand damage, then 11 to 14 into Improved Channeling, so most hits no longer push back your Missiles, and 15 to 19 into Arcane Concentration for free casts.

Wand mobs down from half health to save mana: Lesser Magic Wand at 5 and Greater Magic Wand at 13 come from Enchanting, and Riptear's Spare Arm (Rear Guard Patrol, Horde) or Elven Wand (Alliance, Bashal'Aran, Darkshore) are early quest wands. Your trainer's level 10 class quest pays a staff or an orb; take the staff and retire the starter one. Wear cloth quest rewards over vendor gear, Intellect and Stamina first.

### Levels 20 to 60

Every point goes into Arcane up to 40, where you take Arcane Power. From 41 the points go into Incineration, then Improved Frostbolt, Precision, Ice Shards and Frost Channeling for the Frostbolt filler. Arcane Blast comes at 20, Arcane Resilience at 21 for armor from your Intellect, and Missile Barrage at 22 and 23. From 20 to 22, until both ranks of Missile Barrage are in, Arcane Missiles still does more damage for its mana than Blast, so lead with Missiles; from 23 you level on Blast and free Missiles. Arcane levels fast on single pulls but burns mana, so drink after every two or three mobs and keep Frost Nova and Blink for anything that reaches you. Frostbolt's slow is your kiting tool at every level. From level 22 to the 40s, AoE grinding with Arcane Explosion after a Frost Nova is the fastest way to level, but it needs gear with Stamina and a careful pull. Train Mage Armor at 34 and wear it for the mana; switch to Ice Armor when you are taking melee hits in the open world.
