For the new Skyborne race, Shaman is available only to characters who choose the Horde at creation.

A Dwarf Shaman combination was shown in a hands-on demo, according to one outlet; this has not been confirmed directly by Blizzard and is treated as single-source. Beyond the class being faction-locked for Skyborne, and the general talent revamp Blizzard has said applies across classes, no other Shaman-specific detail has been announced.

This site's leveling routes, talent builds, and gear priorities for Shaman will be written after we play the class in beta. See the [roadmap](/roadmap) for beta dates.
