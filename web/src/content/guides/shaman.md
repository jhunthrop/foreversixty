## New race pairs

**Dwarf Shaman** is confirmed by Blizzard as one of six new combinations; it was also shown on the BlizzCon demo floor. For the Skyborne, Shaman is available only to characters who choose the Horde at creation, and those Skyborne work with the Earthen Ring.

## Talent structure

Talent trees keep the familiar shape: seven rows per tree and 51 points to spend. The one-point "gold medal" talents at 11, 21, and 31 points are still there, and Forever adds a fourth at the 16-point mark, so every tree has four of them. Principal Designer Kris Zierhut confirmed the 16-point addition in an interview.

Talents that existed mainly to unlock a group buff are becoming baseline abilities instead, which frees room in the trees for talents you notice. Some talents are unchanged from 1.12.

One outlet's tally from the demo trees counts 15 new, 25 changed, and 10 unchanged Shaman talents — the fewest unchanged of any class except Warlock.

## What changed in the demo

**Baseline abilities**, read off level-38 demo spellbooks on stream:

- **Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning both cast half a second faster than Classic** before any talent. Lightning Bolt rank 7 is a 2.5-second cast; Chain Lightning rank 1 is a 2-second cast, 6-second cooldown, 3 targets, each jump 30% weaker. Because the base cast is already faster, maxing Elemental Alacrity brings deep Elemental to roughly where Classic ended up.
- **Ghost Wolf's base cast is 2 seconds**, down from 3, and Improved Ghost Wolf takes a second off per rank and makes it work indoors, so 2/2 is instant.
- **Fire Nova is a spell, not a totem.** 30-yard range, instant, 10-second cooldown, detonating everything within 10 yards of your active Fire totem; rank 3 did 206 to 231 at level 38. Improved Fire Nova trims 2 seconds off the cooldown.
- **Totemic Projection** is new: pick a spot up to 30 yards away and your totems move there rather than to your feet. Totemic Recall is still in.
- **Call of the Elements** is new: drop up to four totems from your Totem Bar in a single 3-second cast.
- **Windfury Weapon** rank 1 is a 20% chance of two extra attacks with 103 attack power, lasting 60 minutes, and it suppresses your own Windfury Totem benefit on that hand. Flametongue Weapon carries the same line about Flametongue Totem.

**New talents:** Lava Burst in Elemental is a high-damage nuke that hits harder with Flame Shock on the target. Maelstrom Weapon in Enhancement gives melee attacks a chance to stack a reduction to the cast time and mana cost of your next Lightning Bolt. Spirit Weapons in Enhancement grants parry chance and modifies threat depending on whether Rockbiter is active. Water Shield in Restoration drops globes that restore mana when you are hit or when a heal crits.

**Changed talents:** Call of Flame now boosts Flame Shock, Fire Nova, and Lava Burst rather than only Fire Totems. Elemental Fury has moved earlier and is multi-rank with a lower first-rank value, where Classic had a single 100% crit damage talent. Stormstrike now increases the Nature damage you personally deal, rather than amplifying the next two Nature sources on the target.

Unified hit and crit is a large indirect buff to Enhancement, which previously had to gear for melee and spell hit separately.

## Where these come from, and what they are worth

Except where a line is credited to Blizzard, everything in the section above was read off BlizzCon demo footage and show-floor screenshots by fan sites, mostly at level 38, which was the demo cap. Numbers can be wrong, ranks can be misread, and anything can change before launch. Nothing here is datamined.

Associate Production Director Clayton Stone has also said that Forever's launch is a deliberately conservative take after Season of Discovery, and that the team is starting by filling out skills classes did not have rather than reinventing them.
