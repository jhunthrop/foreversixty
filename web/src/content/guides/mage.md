## New race pairs

**Orc Mage** is confirmed by Blizzard as one of six new combinations. For the Skyborne, Mage is available only to characters who choose the Alliance at creation, and those Skyborne work with the Kirin Tor.

## Talent structure

Talent trees keep the familiar shape: seven rows per tree and 51 points to spend. The one-point "gold medal" talents at 11, 21, and 31 points are still there, and Forever adds a fourth at the 16-point mark, so every tree has four of them. Principal Designer Kris Zierhut confirmed the 16-point addition in an interview.

Talents that existed mainly to unlock a group buff are becoming baseline abilities instead, which frees room in the trees for talents you notice. Some talents are unchanged from 1.12.

One outlet's tally from the demo trees counts 6 new, 32 changed, and 16 unchanged Mage talents. That is the fewest new talents and the most changed of any class, so expect the shape of Mage to be familiar but the numbers to move a lot.

## What changed in the demo

**New abilities**, read off demo footage:

- **Frostfire Bolt**, a bolt that counts as both Fire and Frost. Four talents in the trees reference it.
- **Comprehend Scroll**, a small utility ability that deciphers an untranslated scroll. It was spotted on a Skyborne Mage, so it may be tied to the race or the Zephras Isle questing rather than to the class.

**New talents:** Arcane Blast is a build-up spell that enables stronger follow-ups, with specific Arcane Missiles interactions. Hot Streak in Fire stacks non-periodic crits to reduce Pyroblast's cast time.

**Changed talents:** Improved Counterspell's silence is guaranteed at rank 1 but short, rather than a chance at a longer one.

The general itemization pass matters to Mage in two ways: hit is now one stat, and caster weapons now grant spell damage and healing, so Mages get level-up weapon upgrades the way Warriors and Rogues always have.

## Where these come from, and what they are worth

Except where a line is credited to Blizzard, everything in the section above was read off BlizzCon demo footage and show-floor screenshots by fan sites, mostly at level 38, which was the demo cap. Numbers can be wrong, ranks can be misread, and anything can change before launch. Nothing here is datamined.

Associate Production Director Clayton Stone has also said that Forever's launch is a deliberately conservative take after Season of Discovery, and that the team is starting by filling out skills classes did not have rather than reinventing them.
