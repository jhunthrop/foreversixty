For the new Skyborne race, Mage is available only to characters who choose the Alliance at creation.

Beyond the class being faction-locked for Skyborne, and the general talent revamp Blizzard has said applies across classes, no other Mage-specific detail has been announced. This site's leveling routes, talent builds, and gear priorities for Mage will be written after we play the class in beta. See the [roadmap](/roadmap) for beta dates.
