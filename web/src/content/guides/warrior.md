## New race pairs

None of Blizzard's six new combinations is a Warrior. Warrior is available to every race, including both factions' Skyborne.

## Talent structure

Talent trees keep the familiar shape: seven rows per tree and 51 points to spend. The one-point "gold medal" talents at 11, 21, and 31 points are still there, and Forever adds a fourth at the 16-point mark, so every tree has four of them. Principal Designer Kris Zierhut confirmed the 16-point addition in an interview.

Talents that existed mainly to unlock a group buff are becoming baseline abilities instead, which frees room in the trees for talents you notice. Some talents are unchanged from 1.12.

One outlet's tally from the demo trees counts 11 new, 30 changed, and 13 unchanged Warrior talents. Warrior has the second-most changed talents of any class, after Mage.

## What changed in the demo

**Baseline abilities**, read off level-38 demo spellbooks on stream:

- **Victory Rush** is baseline, in the Arms tab. Instant, 30-second cooldown: a melee hit that heals 10% of your maximum health, usable within 20 seconds of killing something non-trivial.
- **Tactical Mastery** is a passive rather than a talent.
- **Slam** reaches rank 3 by level 38, where Classic reached it at 46, and now has a 15-second cooldown.
- **Thunder Clap** works in Battle or Defensive Stance. Rank 4 slows attacks 20% for 22 seconds and hits up to 4 targets, where Classic was 10% for 30 seconds.
- **Shield Wall** is a 15-minute cooldown reducing damage 60% for 12 seconds, where Classic was 30 minutes, 75%, and 10 seconds. In one stream it was available while Retaliation was on cooldown, which suggests the shared cooldown between Shield Wall, Retaliation, and Recklessness is gone.
- **Shield Block** has 2 charges and lasts 7 seconds baseline, where Classic was one block for 5 seconds. Still 10 Rage, 5-second cooldown, Defensive Stance.
- **Bloodrage** is still in the Protection tab, and **Intercept** is still in the game — the Fury tree has Improved Intercept — but it was not in the level 38 spellbook, so it is probably trained later than in Classic.

**New talents:** Spearing Strike in Arms is an instant weapon attack with bonus damage against Giants, Dragonkin, and mounted targets, and it dismounts. Bloodthrill in Arms lets melee attacks on a Rend target proc Overpower. Raging Blows in Fury makes Whirlwind also strike with the off-hand and cuts Cleave's Rage cost. Vanguard in Protection lets you Charge while in Defensive Stance.

**Changed talents:** Improved Slam now reduces the global cooldown and cast time and preserves your melee swing timer, rather than only cutting the cast. Death Wish keeps its 20% physical damage and Fear immunity, but its downside is 5% more damage taken instead of 20% less armour and resistance. Improved Shield Wall reduces the cooldown rather than extending the duration.

## Where these come from, and what they are worth

Except where a line is credited to Blizzard, everything in the section above was read off BlizzCon demo footage and show-floor screenshots by fan sites, mostly at level 38, which was the demo cap. Numbers can be wrong, ranks can be misread, and anything can change before launch. Nothing here is datamined.

Associate Production Director Clayton Stone has also said that Forever's launch is a deliberately conservative take after Season of Discovery, and that the team is starting by filling out skills classes did not have rather than reinventing them.
