## Overview

Restoration heals with time: Regrowth and Rejuvenation on the tank all fight, Swiftmend as an extra heal, Bloom when the HoTs run out, and Healing Touch for the holes.

### Strengths

- Swiftmend no longer eats the heal over time in Forever, so it is a second heal on a 15 second cooldown
- Bloom heals again every time a Rejuvenation or Regrowth runs its full duration
- HoTs keep ticking while you move, so you keep healing through movement
- Innervate on yourself, with the Spirit you stack, buys a lot of mana

### Weaknesses

- HoTs are slow against a sudden spike: you have to be ahead of the damage, not behind it
- Healing Touch is a long, expensive cast, so it is for real holes only
- Your big group heal, Tranquility, is on a long cooldown, and Wild Growth costs a capstone

### In a raid

- **Role:** Tank healer, with HoTs on whoever takes damage
- **Brings:** Gift of the Wild, Innervate, a battle rez, and Remove Curse and Abolish Poison

## Talents and builds

The build is 40 points of Restoration and 11 of Balance. It is built for a tank-healing fight that runs your mana down.

- **Gift of Nature** (5 points): 10% more healing on your heals. In Forever it does not reach Tranquility.
- **Improved Rejuvenation** (3 points): 15% more from your most-cast spell.
- **Bloom** (1 point): when a Rejuvenation or Regrowth runs out on its own, the target is healed for your Spirit. With HoTs doing most of your healing, it is the best single point you take.
- **Swiftmend** (1 point): an instant heal for the whole of your own Regrowth or Rejuvenation on the target (another druid's does not count), and in Forever the HoT keeps ticking.
- **Nature's Swiftness** (1 point): your next Healing Touch is instant, the save for a dying tank.
- **Naturalist** (5 points): Healing Touch and Wild Growth half a second faster.
- **Reflection**, **Tranquil Spirit** and **Living Spirit**: half your regeneration keeps going while you cast, a cheaper Healing Touch and Tranquility, and 15% more Spirit.
- **Improved Tranquility** (2 points): Tranquility with no threat and its cooldown cut from 5 minutes to 2.
- **Heart of the Wild** (5 points): 10% more Intellect.
- **Genesis**, **Nature's Majesty** and **Nature's Splendor**: from Balance, 10% more on every HoT tick, crit, and longer Rejuvenation and Regrowth. Moonglow (3 points) is the price of reaching Nature's Splendor, which stretches Rejuvenation by 3 seconds and Regrowth by 6. Pay it and move on.
- **Improved Regrowth** (1 point): 10% more crit on the Regrowth you keep on the tank.

**Wild Growth.** The capstone heals a whole party over 7 seconds, but even with Naturalist it is a 2.5 second cast on a 6 second cooldown with a high mana cost, and on a tank fight Rejuvenation and Bloom do more for the mana. On a fight with heavy group damage in bursts it is worth trying: drop Improved Tranquility, put one point in Wild Growth and the other in a second point of Improved Regrowth.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Rejuvenation on the tank a few seconds before he pulls. Keep Gift of the Wild up on the raid.

**The tank.** Regrowth and Rejuvenation stay up on the tank for the whole fight. Let each one run out before you recast it: a HoT that runs its full duration triggers Bloom, and a refresh on top of it throws that heal away.

**Swiftmend** goes on the tank whenever he is under 75%. In Forever it does not consume the HoT, so all it costs is a global and 20% of your base mana. Cast it on cooldown whenever there is damage to heal, not only in an emergency.

**Healing Touch** fills the real holes: a tank under about half, while your mana is ahead of the fight. If your mana is behind, let the HoTs and Swiftmend carry it.

**Nature's Swiftness** into Healing Touch is the save for a tank who is nearly dead.

**The party.** A hurt party member gets a Rejuvenation, you included. Tranquility goes out when several of your party are hurt at once; with Improved Tranquility it is off cooldown often enough to plan around.

**Ranks.** Rank 1 Rejuvenation is not a cheap heal at 60. In Forever a rank learned far below your level loses its share of your healing power, and rank 1 keeps none of it. Your last three ranks (8, 9 and 10) keep all of it and rank 7 keeps most of it, so on a hurt party member cast rank 7: it costs little more than half the top rank, and on a long fight your mana runs out before your healing does. On the tank, use the top rank in a raid. While leveling, your healing power is still low and most of a Rejuvenation is its base heal, so rank 1 barely dents a tank's damage; a middle rank such as rank 4 keeps the heal sizeable and your mana going. Use the top ranks of Regrowth and Healing Touch.

**Mana.** Innervate is yours from level 40. Cast it on yourself when you are low: it multiplies your Spirit regeneration, and a Restoration druid carries a lot of Spirit. A Major Mana Potion and a Demonic Rune cover the rest. An Alliance Skyborne also presses Read Leyline every time it is ready: 50% more mana regeneration for 15 seconds.

## Stat priority

The table above gives the order for your gear. A long tank fight runs you out of mana before it runs out of time, which is why the mana stats count so much for you. Healing power is every heal, and your HoTs take a big share of it per tick. Crit makes your direct heals land bigger. Spirit is mana through Reflection and Innervate, and it is your Bloom heal. MP5 is mana that never stops. Intellect is your pool and a little crit. Spell power with no healing on it adds nothing to your heals.

## Enchants and consumables

Potions and runes work in any form in Forever, though you heal in caster form anyway. A Brilliant Mana Oil on your weapon is healing and mana at once.

## Races

Night Elf brings Elune's Light, 10% crit for 15 seconds, which is crit on your Regrowths and Healing Touches alike. Tauren is the free Horde pick: Endurance is 5% more health, which keeps you alive through raid damage. If you already own the paid Skyborne Heroic Pack, Skyborne bring Wind Blessed, 2% haste that never turns off, on either faction, which is faster casts, and the Alliance ones add Read Leyline: 50% more health and mana regeneration for 15 seconds on a 2 minute cooldown. Cast it once beside a leyline and you are Attuned for an hour, which doubles it wherever you are. Press it every time it is ready on a long fight.

## Professions

Herbalism and Alchemy is the healer pair: your own mana potions and elixirs.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Healing Touch comes at level 1, Rejuvenation at 4, Regrowth at 12 and Cure Poison at 14. Revive at 12 raises a dead groupmate out of combat. Solo, you kill with Wrath and Moonfire.

Heart of the Wild takes your points from 10 to 14: 10% more Intellect, 20% more Stamina in Bear Form. Reflection from 15 to 17 keeps half your mana regeneration going while you cast, and Nature's Focus at 18 and 19 saves a heal from pushback more than a quarter of the time.

At 10, Heeding the Call from your trainer in Darnassus or Thunder Bluff (The Great Ursera Spirit on Zephras Isle for Skyborne) teaches Bear Form for the pulls that go wrong.

Druids cannot use wands, so swap the starter weapon for the first quest staff with Intellect. Leather or cloth quest rewards with Intellect and Spirit beat vendor gear, and the Aquatic Form quest at 16 pays the Aquarius Belt or the Idol of Shifting Tides.

### Levels 20 to 60

Restoration is the slowest druid to quest with, because it is built to keep others alive, not to kill. Level as Feral and switch at 60, or heal dungeons on the way up: Rejuvenation and Regrowth carry a group from the low levels, and Healing Touch covers the big pulls.
