## Overview

### Strengths

- Dire Bear Form adds a flat block of health and multiplies the armor on your gear, and Heart of the Wild adds 20% Stamina on top
- Bear Form adds 115% to all the threat you generate, and Primal Bite and Lacerate both carry high threat on top, so a bear holds a boss on very little rage
- Defense on gear now works for a bear as it does for other tanks, and Thick Hide adds armor for every point of Defense over five times your level
- Potions, weapon stones and racials all work in Bear Form now, so you never shift to drink

### Weaknesses

- No Parry and no Block: Dodge is your only avoidance, so damage comes in spikes
- No Shield Wall or Last Stand style button: Barkskin and Frenzied Regeneration are your cooldowns
- Threat follows rage, so a bear the boss is not hitting starves; Natural Reaction's 5 rage a dodge softens a dodge streak but does not replace the hits

### In a raid

- **Role:** Main tank or off-tank in Dire Bear Form
- **Brings:** Leader of the Pack, 3% critical strike chance for everyone in range, and a battle rez

## Talents and builds

The build is 41 points of Feral and 10 of Restoration. It tanks from level 10 to 60 with no respec.

- **Thick Hide** (3 points): more base armor for your level and for every point of Defense over five times your level, all multiplied by the form.
- **Natural Reaction** and **Feral Swiftness**: 9% Dodge, and 5 rage every time you dodge, which keeps your threat going on a dodge streak.
- **Furor** (1 point): a 20% chance of 10 rage when you shift into Bear Form. A tank seldom shifts in a fight, so the build keeps one point and puts the rest into Naturalist.
- **Lacerate** (1 point): your bleed. It stacks five times, each cast hits for more per stack already up, and it carries high threat.
- **Primal Bite** (1 point): the capstone. Weapon damage plus a bonus and very high threat, on a 6 second cooldown for 15 rage.
- **Ferocity** (5 points): Maul, Swipe and Primal Bite 5 rage cheaper.
- **Shredding Attacks** (3 points)
- **Savage Fury**, **Feral Instinct** and **Rend and Tear**: more damage, so more threat, from Maul and Swipe, and 15% more from your melee abilities while Lacerate bleeds the target.
- **Sharpened Claws**, **Natural Instinct** and **Blood Frenzy**: crit, bigger crits, and 5 rage on every crit.
- **Leader of the Pack** (1 point)
- **Heart of the Wild** and **Naturalist** (5 points each): 20% more Stamina in Bear Form, and 10% more damage of every kind, which is threat.

Left out: Brutal Impact (a shorter Bash cooldown, nice for trash), Feral Charge (a charge and interrupt, take it if your raid needs a bear to close on casters), and the cat's Predatory Discipline and Shifting Power.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Be in Dire Bear Form. Enrage a second before you pull: it gives you rage up front and 2 a second for 10 seconds. It also costs armor for those 10 seconds, so the dip is over 9 seconds into the fight.

**Pull.** Faerie Fire from range is your pull and your threat on the run in. Then Primal Bite, Lacerate, and Maul queued on your swing.

**Demoralizing Roar.** Demoralizing Roar whenever the boss does not have it, and again in its last 3 seconds, unless a warrior's Demoralizing Shout is already on it.

**Rage.** Maul replaces a white swing, so it is the lowest-value use of your rage. Below 25 queue it at 15. From 25 to 29 hold it until you have 35 rage. From 30 to 35, and again from 40 to 59, hold it until you have 45, so Lacerate, and from 40 Primal Bite, get your rage first and seldom wait on it. From 36 to 39 queue it at 25: before Dire Bear Form your health is thin, and Frenzied Regeneration needs that rage. At 60, raid gear and buffs keep the rage coming, so 25 is enough.

**Adds.** In a raid at 60, on three or more targets, Swipe goes to the top. On two targets, and while leveling, keep it below Primal Bite, Lacerate and Maul. Put Lacerate on the target the raid will kill last. Faerie Fire is a free ranged threat tool on a loose add. Challenging Roar is your panic button when the pack runs to the healers.

**Cooldowns.** Barkskin takes 20% off physical damage for 15 seconds every minute, and Forever lets you cast it in Bear Form. Press it on cooldown; when you know a big hit is a few seconds away, hold it for that. Frenzied Regeneration turns up to 10 rage a second into health for 10 seconds, and in Forever a full 100 rage of it heals your whole health bar. Use it under half health with rage banked, not at the pull.

**There is no Savage Defense in Forever.** Your mitigation is armor, Dodge, Barkskin and your healers.

## Stat priority

Heart of the Wild adds 20% to your Stamina in form. Agility is Dodge, armor and crit at once, and Dodge is the only avoidance you have. Hit is threat, not survival, and adds nothing past the cap. Expertise, which only a few late sets carry, keeps the boss from dodging or parrying your Maul and Lacerate, which is threat and rage. Armor is multiplied by the form, and its share off each physical hit shrinks as you stack it. Strength and attack power are threat, and so is your weapon: your paw hits with the weapon's DPS every 2.5 seconds, so a low-DPS caster staff costs you threat. Defense means fewer crits taken, and Thick Hide turns it into a little armor. Crit is threat, and through Blood Frenzy it is rage too.

## Best in slot

Wolfshead Helm is druid-only in Forever and gives 5 more rage from Enrage (and 5 more energy from Shifting Power). Leatherworkers make it. It is a level 40 blue with only 10 Spirit beside the bonus, so wearing it trades a helm's stats for the rage.

## Enchants and consumables

Forever lets a druid drink in form. Mighty Rage Potion is the bear's threat potion and goes down in Dire Bear Form without breaking it. Swap to a Greater Stoneshield Potion on a fight where you die before you lose threat. An Elemental Sharpening Stone or a weightstone counts on your paw in form, so sharpen your weapon. The old Engineering bombs still do not work in form; the EZ-Thro and SAF-T ones do, and an EZ-Thro Thorium Grenade is a pack stun from Dire Bear Form.

## Races

Your faction picks your race: Night Elf on the Alliance, Tauren on the Horde, and both tank well. Night Elf takes slightly less damage and holds a little more threat, through Quickness and Elune's Light, which is threat when you press it on the pull. Tauren's health is steadier through Endurance, and War Stomp works in form, a great trash pull. Skyborne can be bears on either faction, with Wind Blessed.

## Professions

No craft in your raid set binds on pickup, so no profession is needed for it: the Leatherworking and Tailoring pieces bind when equipped and can be bought. Much of the best 20 to 50 leather, the Defender's, Brawler's and Warden's pieces, binds when picked up, so a leveling bear needs Leatherworking for it. Skinning and Leatherworking pay for themselves, and Alchemy keeps you in Stoneshield and Mighty Rage Potions.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Before 10 you are a caster: Moonfire from 4, then Wrath. At 10, Heeding the Call from your trainer in Darnassus or Thunder Bluff (The Great Ursera Spirit on Zephras Isle for Skyborne) teaches Bear Form, and Maul, Growl and Demoralizing Roar train the same level. Enrage follows at 12, Bash at 14, Swipe at 16 and a second Maul rank at 18.

Your points from 10 to 14 go into Ferocity, so Maul and Swipe cost 5 less Rage. Furor takes one point at 15, for a 20% chance of 10 Rage when you shift into Bear Form. Thick Hide takes 16 to 18 for more armor in Bear Form, and Feral Swiftness at 19 adds 2% dodge. Feral Instinct only adds to Swipe, which a single target never sees, so it waits for the end of the build.

Your paw hits with your weapon's DPS, so replace the starter weapon with the highest-DPS staff or two-handed mace a quest gives you. Wear leather quest rewards with Stamina and Agility over vendor gear, and take the Idol of Shifting Tides from the Aquatic Form quest at 16.

### Levels 20 to 60

Dire Bear Form comes at 40. A bear is the safe way to level through big pulls and the fastest way into dungeons, at the cost of kill speed: Maul and Swipe on a pack, Demoralizing Roar to blunt it, and Frenzied Regeneration from 36 to skip drinking. Lacerate arrives at 25 and Primal Bite at 40, and until then Maul, Swipe and Demoralizing Roar hold a dungeon pack.

- **22 to 35:** Sharpened Claws at 22 and 23, Blood Frenzy at 28 and 29, Leader of the Pack at 30, Predatory Strikes at 31 to 33 and Natural Instinct at 34 and 35.
- **36 on:** Rend and Tear, which Lacerate's bleed turns on, with Feral Swiftness's second point at 39, before Dire Bear Form's bigger pulls.
- **After Rend and Tear's last points:** Natural Reaction, then Heart of the Wild and Naturalist.
