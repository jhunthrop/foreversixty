## Overview

### Strengths

- Rip at 5 combo points bleeds the boss for its whole duration, Rake's hit and its bleed both grow with your attack power, and the bleeds keep Rend and Tear on for every Shred and Bite
- Shifting Power turns mana into 40 energy every 8 seconds
- Potions, Thistle Tea, weapon stones and Windfury Totem all work in Cat Form now
- Your paw hits with your weapon's damage, so a real two-hander is a real upgrade

### Weaknesses

- Shred and Ravage need you behind the target, so you lose damage on a boss that turns or moves
- No Tiger's Fury: Forever removed it, and Shifting Power is the only energy button
- Leaving Cat Form in combat costs you your energy bar unless you take Furor

### In a raid

- **Role:** Melee DPS in Cat Form
- **Brings:** Leader of the Pack, 3% critical strike chance for everyone in range, on spells and attacks alike; Innervate and a battle rez in an emergency

## Talents and builds

The leveling build takes Brutal Impact for a longer Pounce stun and Feral Charge, which in Forever is two buttons: the Bear Form charge and interrupt, and a Cat Form leap behind the target on a 30 second cooldown, which puts you straight into Shred position. Your 31 Feral points are done at 40. From 41 take Genesis, then Nature's Majesty and Nature's Reach in Balance, then Heart of the Wild, Naturalist and a point of Natural Shapeshifter in Restoration. At 60, respec into the raid build: Brutal Impact, Feral Charge and that Natural Shapeshifter point finish Rend and Tear and fill Feral Instinct.

- **Shredding Attacks** (3 points): Shred drops to 42 energy, and Shred is most of your globals.
- **Savage Fury** (2 points): 10% more damage on Shred, Claw and Rake, the bleed included.
- **Rend and Tear**: 3% more damage a point from your melee abilities against a bleeding target, 15% at 5. Your auto attacks do not get it. Rip keeps the boss bleeding, so it is on for every Shred, Rake and Bite after your first finisher, and a Rake or Rip you put on a target that already bleeds carries it on every tick.
- **Sharpened Claws**, **Natural Instinct** and **Blood Frenzy**: crit, bigger crits, and a bonus combo point on every builder crit, with both points in either build. Crit is what makes your 5 points come quickly.
- **Predatory Strikes** (3 points): 150% of your level as attack power in Cat Form.
- **Predatory Discipline** (1 point): Ferocious Bite keeps the bonus damage from your extra energy but no longer spends it. It also opens Shifting Power.
- **Shifting Power** (1 point): the capstone, 40 energy for mana on an 8 second cooldown. It replaces Tiger's Fury, and without it the cat runs out of things to press.
- **Leader of the Pack** (1 point): the raid's crit aura. Take it in every build.
- **Heart of the Wild** and **Naturalist**: Heart of the Wild is 10% more Strength in Cat Form at five points; Naturalist is 10% more damage of every kind. These are why a cat goes into Restoration at all.

The raid build skips Furor. In Forever it no longer hands you 40 energy on a shift; it gives back the energy you left Cat Form with, plus 10 for each second out of form that you did not spend as a bear. If you shift out a lot (Innervate a healer, battle rez, decurse), Furor saves the energy you would lose. Both builds' Balance points are Genesis, 10% more periodic damage (a bigger Rip), Nature's Majesty, 4% crit, and Nature's Reach, 4% hit on your attacks as well as your spells. Natural Shapeshifter only makes your shifts and Shifting Power cheaper, so the raid build spends that point on Rend and Tear. Ferocity's five points take Rake to 35 energy. The extra Feral Instinct points add nothing to a cat's damage; with Brutal Impact gone, they are the two points that bring the tree to the ten that open the next row. Primal Bite and Lacerate are bear buttons, Brutal Impact is for questing, and Feral Charge is a leap you can buy back for a boss you keep losing your back to.

## Rotation and priority

**Open.** Prowl, walk behind the boss and Ravage once the tank has two or three globals on it. Open from a full bar so Poised, a passive you learn at 32, makes it cheaper. Pounce needs Prowl and your position behind the target, exactly like Ravage, so it is never the fallback for a boss you cannot get behind. Use it on trash you want stunned; on a boss, Ravage hits much harder.

**Finishers.** Ferocious Bite is for 5 points while Rip still has 5 seconds or more; between 2 and 5 seconds, keep Shredding and hold the points for the Rip. Do not pool energy for the Bite. Never finish on fewer than 5 points against a boss.

**Rake.** Keep it on the boss. Its hit and every tick of the bleed take a share of your attack power on top of the rank's bonus, so its 35 energy buys more than the same energy as Shred, and it is a combo point. Put it up right after a finisher and again whenever it has fallen off.

**Shred.** Stand behind the target, every other global.

**Energy.** Above 60 energy when Shifting Power comes up, Shred first and press it on the next global. Clearcasting from Omen of Clarity nearly always lands on a Shred, which needs no button of its own.

**Mana.** Shifting Power costs 55% of your base mana, and mana decides how many you get. With a paladin's Judgement of Wisdom on the boss, your hits refill most of it over a fight. Without one, or if you run dry, drink a mana potion in Cat Form without shifting.

**Adds and movement.** You have no cleave. On a pack, Rip and Rake the target that will live longest and Shred down the kill order. When the boss turns to you, or you cannot get behind, Rake and Claw in place of Shred until you can; neither needs your position. Rip and Rake tick while you run.

**Pull aggro.** Leader of the Pack crits and Rend and Tear push your threat high. Open late, and if you pull, Cower.

## Stat priority

Weapon DPS matters in Forever. Your Cat Form paw swings at a fixed 1.0 speed and hits for your weapon's DPS, and Shred and Ravage hit for a multiple of that swing, so a high-DPS two-hander beats a caster staff with more Strength and Agility on it. Heart of the Wild adds 10% to your Strength. A missed or dodged Shred hands back most of its energy, but you still lose the global and the combo point; hit adds nothing past the cap. Through Blood Frenzy, crit is combo points too. Attack power reaches Rip, Rake and Ferocious Bite as well as your paw, so Strength and attack power gear count for more than they did.

## Best in slot

Wolfshead Helm is druid-only in Forever and now gives 5 more energy on every Shifting Power (and 5 more rage on Enrage). Leatherworkers make it. It is a level 40 blue with only 10 Spirit beside the bonus, so wearing it trades a helm's stats for the energy.

## Enchants and consumables

Forever changed the old cat rules: almost every potion works in Cat Form without breaking it, rage potions and Thistle Tea work for druids, and stones, weightstones and weapon enchants count on your paw. So the cat drinks and sharpens like a rogue. Thistle Tea is a free bar of energy: drink it when you are nearly empty, never on top of a full bar. The exception is the old Engineering bombs, which you still cannot throw in form; the new EZ-Thro and SAF-T ones you can.

## Races

**Night Elf.** Racials are usable while shapeshifted in Forever, so line Elune's Light up with your potion and trinkets.

**Tauren** on the Horde: War Stomp is for trash, not damage.

**Skyborne** on either faction: Wind Blessed's haste is faster swings in Cat Form.

## Professions

Your raid set asks for one profession, Enchanting. Molten Heart of the Mountain, and on the Alliance Frozen Heart of the Mountain beside it, are bind-on-pickup trinkets only an enchanter can make. The Leatherworking pieces in your set bind when equipped, so buy them; Skinning and Leatherworking still pay for themselves. Engineering works for a cat in Forever: the EZ-Thro and SAF-T bombs and grenades throw from Cat Form, though the old bombs still do not.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Until 20 you are a caster: Moonfire from 4, then Wrath. Your mana runs out before a long fight does, so keep the cheap ranks:

- **Wrath:** rank 1 at 10 and 11, then rank 2 (a Night Elf keeps it through 15, while a Tauren takes the new rank 3 at 14). Every druid takes the newest Wrath at 16.
- **Moonfire:** rank 1 from 10 to 12 (a Night Elf does a little better with rank 2 at 11), then rank 2 through 19 even after rank 3 trains at 16.

At 10, Heeding the Call from your trainer in Darnassus or Thunder Bluff (The Great Ursera Spirit on Zephras Isle for Skyborne) teaches Bear Form, with Maul and Demoralizing Roar; shift when a pull gets heavy. Bash trains at 14, Swipe at 16.

Ferocity takes your points from 10 to 14: Maul and Swipe cost 5 less Rage now, Claw and Rake less Energy later. Feral Swiftness at 15 and 16 adds 4% dodge now, and Cat Form run speed once you learn Cat Form at 20, Feral Instinct at 17 adds 10% to Swipe, and Brutal Impact at 18 and 19 makes Bash stun longer and come back 30 seconds sooner.

Your bear hits with your weapon's DPS, so swap the starter weapon for the highest-DPS quest staff or two-handed mace. Leather quest rewards beat vendor gear, and the Aquatic Form quest at 16 pays the Idol of Shifting Tides. Druids cannot use wands.

### Levels 20 to 60

Cat Form arrives at 20. Solo mobs face you, so Shred will not land: Claw builds your points and Rake, from 24, is your best button on anything that lives through its bleed, since the hit and the bleed both grow with your attack power. In a group with a tank, keep Rake up and Shred from behind from 21, once the second point of Shredding Attacks has it at 48 energy; at 20, Claw buys more damage for its energy.

Open from Prowl behind the mob: Ravage once you learn it at 32, or Pounce from 36 when you want the stun. When you cannot get behind, open with Claw. Rip at 5 points on anything with a lot of health, Ferocious Bite, from 24, on the rest. From 25, Feral Charge's Cat Form leap lands you behind a mob for a Shred, and in Bear Form it is the charge you pull dungeon packs with.

- **20 to 30:** Shredding Attacks at 20 to 22, Sharpened Claws at 23 and 24, Feral Charge at 25, then Savage Fury and Predatory Strikes.
- **31 to 40:** Leader of the Pack, Blood Frenzy, Natural Instinct and Rend and Tear before Predatory Discipline, which only changes Ferocious Bite; it comes at 39, in time to open Shifting Power at 40.
- **60:** respec into the raid build.
