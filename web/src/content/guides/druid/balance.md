## Overview

### Strengths

- Moonkin Form gives everyone within 45 yards 3% crit, on spells and attacks alike
- Vengeance doubles your Arcane and Nature crit bonus, so crit is worth more to you than to most casters
- Eclipse turns every Wrath into two faster Starfires, a loop that is easy to play and hard to break
- Hurricane has no cooldown in Forever, so you have a real AoE

### Weaknesses

- Starfire is a long cast: moving costs you more than it costs a mage
- Two DoTs to keep up on top of the Eclipse loop
- Moonkin Form cannot heal, so an emergency heal means shifting out (Rebirth, Innervate and Remove Curse all cast from form)

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged caster DPS
- **Brings:** Moonkin Form's 3% crit aura, Faerie Fire, Innervate and a battle rez

## Talents and builds

The leveling build takes Heart of the Wild and Reflection, because mana is what runs out while questing, plus all five points of Naturalist (2% damage a point) and Nature's Grace once you reach it. The raid build moves Heart of the Wild and Reflection into Improved Starfire, Eclipse and Subtlety: the full Starfire loop. At full ranks, Improved Starfire makes Starfire a 3 second cast, and each Wrath banks two Eclipse charges (up to four), each taking another half second off one Starfire. Nature's Grace makes every non-periodic crit speed up your casting and your global cooldown for 3 seconds.

- **Vengeance** (5 points): the crit damage bonus of your Arcane and Nature spells is doubled.
- **Moonfury** (5 points)
- **Improved Wrath** (5 points): Wrath at 1.5 seconds and half the mana. It is what makes the Eclipse Wrath cheap enough to cast every loop.
- **Improved Moonfire**, **Nature's Splendor** and **Insect Swarm**: a Moonfire worth its global, longer DoTs, and the second DoT.
- **Nature's Reach** (2 points): 4% hit and longer range. Hit is the stat a fresh 60 Moonkin is shortest on.
- **Moonglow** (3 points)
- **Moonkin Form** (1 point)

From Restoration, Nature's Focus saves your Starfire from pushback 70% of the time when you take damage, which matters on a boss that hits the raid, and Subtlety cuts the threat of your Nature and Arcane spells, which keeps your crit streaks off the tank's threat.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Shift into Moonkin Form and stay there; every spell in the loop casts from form. Start a Wrath so it lands after the tank's first hit, then Moonfire, Insect Swarm and Starfire on the charges it banked.

**Clearcasting.** Wrath never uses up a Clearcasting proc in Forever, so it always lands on a Starfire or a DoT.

**Mana.** Moonglow makes every damage spell 25% cheaper, Clearcasting procs twice as often in Moonkin Form, and Judgement of Wisdom carries the rest. Innervate goes on you when you will otherwise run dry before the boss dies. On a fight you can finish without it, give it to a healer with high Spirit: theirs regenerates more under it than yours.

**Faerie Fire.** In Moonkin Form it costs a global and a little mana, so put yours up only when no feral druid has it on the boss.

**AoE.** Hurricane has no cooldown in Forever. Channel it on a pack that will live through it; on a pack that dies fast, Moonfire the tough ones and get back on the boss.

**Movement.** Refresh Moonfire and Insect Swarm on the move, and save a few seconds of DoT time before a known move.

## Stat priority

A resisted Starfire is 3 seconds of casting for nothing, and a resisted Wrath banks no Eclipse charges. Hit adds nothing past the spell hit cap. Arcane damage feeds Starfire and Moonfire; Nature damage feeds Wrath, Insect Swarm and Hurricane, never Starfire or Moonfire. Crit pays twice, through Vengeance and Nature's Grace.

## Best in slot

## Enchants and consumables

Potions work in Moonkin Form in Forever, so you never shift out to drink. On a long fight, drink your mana potion the first time you are down that much, then the Demonic Rune on its own cooldown; the Rune costs health, so not while the raid is taking damage.

## Races

**Night Elf.** Vengeance makes Elune's Light's crit worth more to you, and racials work in Moonkin Form in Forever, so press it with your trinkets.

**Tauren** on the Horde: survival and a stun, no damage.

**Skyborne** on either faction, for Wind Blessed's haste. The Alliance ones add Read Leyline: cast it once beside a leyline and you are Attuned for an hour, which doubles its regeneration wherever you are, but entering a raid removes it for good. Press it when your mana starts to run low, between Innervates, since Innervate replaces it.

## Professions

Tailoring makes some of the best early caster gear, and Enchanting puts spell power on it. Alchemy keeps you in mana potions.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Wrath is yours from level 1, with new ranks at 6 and 14. Moonfire trains at 4, Entangling Roots at 8 to hold a second mob, Nature's Grasp at 10 and Faerie Fire at 18.

Until Starfire at 20 the fight is simple: from 4, Moonfire the mob, then Wrath until it dies, and drink when the bar runs low. On a long fight your mana runs out first, so the cheap old ranks do more than the new ones:

- **10 and 11:** Wrath rank 1.
- **12 to 16:** Wrath rank 1 with Moonfire rank 1, except rank 2 of both at 14 (a Horde Skyborne stays on rank 1 of both at 14 too).
- **17:** Wrath rank 2 with Moonfire rank 1.
- **18:** rank 2 of both. A Skyborne druid takes Wrath rank 3 a level early, with Moonfire back on rank 1.
- **19:** Wrath rank 3, with Moonfire still on rank 2.

Improved Wrath takes your points from 10 to 14: Wrath 0.5 sec faster for half the mana. Genesis at 15 and 16 adds 4% to Moonfire's ticks and Rejuvenation, and Moonglow from 17 to 19 makes damage spells 25% cheaper.

At 10, Heeding the Call from your trainer in Darnassus or Thunder Bluff (The Great Ursera Spirit on Zephras Isle for Skyborne) teaches Bear Form for the pulls that go wrong.

Druids cannot use wands, so swap the starter weapon for the first quest staff with Intellect. Leather or cloth quest rewards with Intellect and Spirit beat vendor gear, and the Aquatic Form quest at 16 pays the Aquarius Belt or the Idol of Shifting Tides.

### Levels 20 to 60

Balance levels fine with Wrath and Moonfire, and Moonkin Form at 40 makes it smooth. Feral is faster: Cat Form kills without drinking, and Bear Form survives the bad pulls. As Balance, Moonfire every pull and Wrath until the mob dies; until 35, Starfire is for the opener on a mob that is not yet on you.

Eclipse is yours at 35, but until Moonkin Form and Innervate at 40 your mana keeps you on Wrath most of the time, so the loop starts to pay at 40. The single Eclipse point you level with takes only 0.17 seconds off a Starfire per charge, too little to pay for the Wrath on its own; the loop still wins because the Wrath is cheap and leaves more mana for Starfires.

After Moonkin Form, Heart of the Wild takes 42 to 46 and Naturalist 47 to 51, 10% more damage from every spell, then Reflection at 52 to 54; Nature's Focus only saves casts from pushback, so it waits for 55.

Mana runs out before mobs do in your 20s and 30s, so the newest rank is not always the best:

- **Moonfire:** rank 2 through 28 (at 26 alone rank 3 edges it), rank 3 from 29 through 34 and rank 4 from 35 through 39.
- **Wrath:** rank 3 at 22 and 23, then rank 4 from 24 all the way through 39, and rank 6 at 46 and 47.
- **Insect Swarm**, your talent at 27: rank 1 through 38.

Everywhere else, cast the newest rank.
