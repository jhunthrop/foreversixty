## Overview

Balance is the Moonkin: two DoTs on the boss, Wrath to bank Eclipse charges, and fast Starfires to spend them, all from a form that gives your group crit.

### Strengths

- Moonkin Form gives everyone within 45 yards 3% crit, on spells and attacks alike
- Vengeance doubles your Arcane and Nature crit bonus, so crit is worth more to you than to most casters
- Eclipse turns every Wrath into two faster Starfires, a loop that is easy to play and hard to break
- Hurricane has no cooldown in Forever, so you have a real AoE

### Weaknesses

- Starfire is a long cast: moving costs you more than it costs a mage
- Two DoTs to keep up on top of the Eclipse loop
- Moonkin Form cannot heal, so an emergency heal means shifting out (Rebirth, Innervate and Remove Curse all cast from form)

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged caster DPS
- **Brings:** Moonkin Form's 3% crit aura, Faerie Fire, Innervate and a battle rez

## Talents and builds

The leveling build takes Heart of the Wild and Reflection, because mana is what runs out while questing, plus all five points of Naturalist (2% damage a point) and Nature's Grace once you reach it. The raid build moves Heart of the Wild and Reflection into Improved Starfire, Eclipse and Subtlety: the full Starfire loop. At full ranks, Improved Starfire makes Starfire a 3 second cast, and each Wrath banks two Eclipse charges (up to four), each taking another half second off one Starfire. Nature's Grace makes every non-periodic crit speed up your casting and your global cooldown for 3 seconds.

- **Vengeance** (5 points): the crit damage bonus of your Arcane and Nature spells is doubled.
- **Moonfury** (5 points): 10% more Arcane and Nature damage.
- **Improved Wrath** (5 points): Wrath at 1.5 seconds and half the mana. It is what makes the Eclipse Wrath cheap enough to cast every loop.
- **Improved Moonfire**, **Nature's Splendor** and **Insect Swarm**: a Moonfire worth its global, longer DoTs, and the second DoT.
- **Nature's Reach** (2 points): 4% hit and longer range. Hit is the stat a fresh 60 Moonkin is shortest on.
- **Moonglow** (3 points): your damage spells cost 25% less.
- **Moonkin Form** (1 point): the capstone and the crit aura.

From Restoration, Nature's Focus saves your Starfire from pushback 70% of the time when you take damage, which matters on a boss that hits the raid, and Subtlety cuts the threat of your Nature and Arcane spells, which keeps your crit streaks off the tank's threat.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Shift into Moonkin Form and stay there. Every spell you cast in this loop works in form. Start a Wrath so it finishes as the tank pulls; it lands after the tank's first hit.

**Open.** After that Wrath, Moonfire, then Insect Swarm, then Starfire on the Eclipse charges the Wrath banked.

**The loop.**

1. Keep **Moonfire** and **Insect Swarm** ticking. Recast each one when it falls off.
2. With no Eclipse charge banked, cast **Wrath**. A Wrath and two quick Starfires do more damage per second than plain Starfires.
3. **Starfire** everything else, spending the charges.

**Clearcasting.** Wrath never uses up a Clearcasting proc in Forever, so it always lands on a Starfire or a DoT. You do not need to do anything about it.

**End of the fight.** Once there is less time left than a Starfire takes, cast Wrath. Once there is less than a Wrath, cast Moonfire for its instant hit.

**Mana.** Moonglow makes every damage spell 25% cheaper, Clearcasting procs twice as often in Moonkin Form, and Judgement of Wisdom carries the rest. If you fall behind, lean on Wrath: with Improved Wrath it costs a fraction of a Starfire's mana and takes half its cast. Innervate goes on you when you will otherwise run dry before the boss dies. On a fight you can finish without it, give it to a healer with high Spirit, because Innervate multiplies Spirit regeneration and theirs is higher than yours.

**Faerie Fire.** If no feral druid has it on the boss, put yours up. In Moonkin Form it is not free: it costs a global and a little mana, so only do it when no one else will.

**AoE.** Hurricane has no cooldown in Forever. On a pack that will live through the channel, Hurricane is your AoE; on a pack that dies fast, Moonfire the tough ones and get back on the boss.

**Movement.** Moonfire and Insect Swarm are instant. Refresh them while you move, and save a few seconds of DoT time before a known move.

## Stat priority

The table above gives the order for your gear; here is why each stat counts. Spell power is damage on every cast. Hit matters a lot to a moonkin: a resisted Starfire is 3 seconds of casting for nothing, and a resisted Wrath banks no Eclipse charges. It counts up to the spell hit cap and adds nothing past it. Arcane damage is spell power for Starfire and Moonfire, your Arcane spells. Nature damage feeds Wrath, Insect Swarm and Hurricane, never Starfire or Moonfire. Crit pays twice, through Vengeance and Nature's Grace. Intellect is your mana pool and a little crit.

## Enchants and consumables

Potions work in Moonkin Form in Forever, so you never shift out to drink. On a long fight, drink your mana potion the first time you are down that much, then the Demonic Rune on its own cooldown; the Rune costs health, so not while the raid is taking damage.

## Races

Night Elf brings Elune's Light, 10% crit for 15 seconds on a 3 minute cooldown, and Vengeance makes that crit worth more to you. Racials work in Moonkin Form in Forever, so press it with your trinkets. Tauren is the free Horde pick: Endurance is 5% more health and War Stomp stuns up to 5 enemies for 2 seconds, so its racials bring survival and a stun, not damage. If you already own the paid Skyborne Heroic Pack, Skyborne bring Wind Blessed, 2% haste that never turns off, on either faction, and the Alliance ones add Read Leyline: 50% more health and mana regeneration for 15 seconds on a 2 minute cooldown. Cast it once beside a leyline and you are Attuned for an hour: Attuned doubles Read Leyline's regeneration wherever you are, but entering a raid removes it for good. Press it when your mana starts to run low. Innervate replaces it, so press it between Innervates.

## Professions

Tailoring makes some of the best early caster gear, and Enchanting puts spell power on it. Alchemy keeps you in mana potions.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Wrath is yours from level 1, with new ranks at 6 and 14. Moonfire trains at 4, Entangling Roots at 8 to hold a second mob, Nature's Grasp at 10 and Faerie Fire at 18.

Improved Wrath takes your points from 10 to 14: Wrath 0.5 sec faster for half the mana. Genesis at 15 and 16 adds 4% to Moonfire's ticks and Rejuvenation, and Moonglow from 17 to 19 makes damage spells 25% cheaper.

At 10, Heeding the Call from your trainer in Darnassus or Thunder Bluff (The Great Ursera Spirit on Zephras Isle for Skyborne) teaches Bear Form for the pulls that go wrong.

Druids cannot use wands, so swap the starter weapon for the first quest staff with Intellect. Leather or cloth quest rewards with Intellect and Spirit beat vendor gear, and the Aquatic Form quest at 16 pays the Aquarius Belt or the Idol of Shifting Tides.

### Levels 20 to 60

Balance levels fine with Wrath and Moonfire, and Moonkin Form at 40 makes it smooth. Feral is faster: Cat Form kills without drinking, and Bear Form survives the bad pulls. If you level as Balance, Moonfire every pull and Wrath until the mob dies. Mana runs out before mobs do in your 20s and 30s, so the newest rank is not always the best: Moonfire rank 2 does more for its mana through your 20s, rank 3 through 35 and rank 4 through 39; Wrath rank 3 holds from 22 to 26 and rank 4 through your 30s; Insect Swarm stays at rank 1 through 38. Everywhere else, and from 40 on, cast the newest rank. Starfire is for the opener on a mob that is not yet on you.
