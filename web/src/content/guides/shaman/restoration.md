## Overview

### Strengths

- Chain Heal cast on a target with your Riptide heals it 25% more
- Chain Heal heals three people for one cast
- Water Shield turns the hits you take and your critical heals into mana, three globes at a time
- Nature's Swiftness makes your biggest Healing Wave instant when the tank is about to die

### Weaknesses

- Chain Heal only jumps to people standing close to its target, so it does little when the raid is spread out
- Your Water totems only reach your own party, and they stay put until you spend Totemic Call to bring them to you
- Without raid gear and buffs your mana does not last a long fight

### In a raid

- **Role:** Healer for the tank and the group around it
- **Brings:** Healing Stream or Mana Spring and Mana Tide for your party, plus whatever Earth and Air totems your raid leader assigns

## Talents and builds

Forty-one points in Restoration and ten in Enhancement. Thundering Strikes is 5% crit on your heals, because in Forever it raises spells as well as attacks, and crits are what fire Water Shield. Ancestral Knowledge is 10% more Intellect. Nothing in Elemental helps a heal.

- **Improved Healing Wave** (5 points): Healing Wave casts in 2.5 seconds instead of 3.
- **Tidal Focus** (5 points)
- **Mindfulness** (3 points): half your mana regeneration keeps running while you cast.
- **Water Shield** (1 point): your mana engine. Three globes, each returning 2% of your maximum mana when you are hit or a heal crits.
- **Healing Focus** (3 points): a 70% chance not to lose cast time when you take damage.
- **Tidal Mastery** (5 points): 5% more crit on your heals, and so more Water Shield returns.
- **Restorative Totems** (5 points): Mana Spring 25% stronger, Healing Stream 50% stronger.
- **Mana Tide Totem** (1 point): mana for your whole party for 12 seconds, every 5 minutes.
- **Healing Way** (3 points): Healing Wave heals 25% more, always on.
- **Nature's Swiftness** (1 point): one instant Healing Wave every 3 minutes.
- **Purification** (5 points)
- **Riptide** (1 point): an instant 15 second heal over time on a 12 second cooldown, and Chain Heal heals its target 25% more.

Ancestral Healing is three points for 25% more armor on your target for 15 seconds after a crit heal, which is a real help to the tank.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Water Shield, then Healing Stream Totem with your party. Water Shield lasts 10 minutes but only holds three globes, and only one globe fires every few seconds; recast it when the globes are gone.

**Tank first.**

1. Riptide on the tank when it is hurt and your last Riptide has run out: it is ready again at 12 seconds but heals for 15, so an early recast loses its last tick. No single-target heal gives more healing for its mana, and it sets up the Chain Heal. It heals over its 15 seconds with nothing up front, so it never answers a big hit on its own.
2. Chain Heal on the tank as soon as anyone near it is hurt, as long as your mana is ahead of the fight. Riptide's 25% applies to the target you cast it on, and the two jumps go to the most injured people near it, each for half the heal before. Cast on someone in your own party, it only jumps to other members of your party, so with the tank in your party your Chain Heals on it stay in your party.
3. Healing Wave when the tank is down to 60%. Drop a rank when your mana is behind the fight, but never below rank 8. Lesser Healing Wave is the fast heal when it is down to 30% and the next hit could kill it.
4. Nature's Swiftness into your highest rank of Healing Wave when the tank is under 25%. Keep it for exactly that, and never spend it on a low rank.

**Low ranks.** A rank keeps your whole healing bonus until you are 17 levels past the level you learned it, then loses a twentieth of it every level after, so at 60 a rank learned at 23 or earlier gets none of your gear. Check the tooltip on a geared 60. The ranks that keep all of it at 60 are Healing Wave 8 to 10, Lesser Healing Wave 4 to 6 and Chain Heal 2 and 3. Healing Wave 7 and Chain Heal 1, both learned at 40, keep most of it, Lesser Healing Wave 3 about two thirds and Healing Wave 6 under half. Anything learned at 23 or earlier heals for its tooltip number and nothing more, however much healing gear you wear.

**Everyone else.** Heal them directly only while your mana is above half: Riptide when one is under 50%, Lesser Healing Wave under 25%. Otherwise Chain Heal is how they get healed.

**Chain Heal ranks.** Rank 3 costs about 385 mana with Tidal Focus for a 490 base heal, rank 2 about 300 for 335, and both take your whole healing bonus. With a raid's healing gear rank 2 heals the most per point of mana and rank 3 the most per cast. Rank 1, learned at 40, starts losing your bonus at 58, and at 60 it is never better than rank 2. Cast rank 3 while your mana is ahead of the fight, and drop to rank 2 on fights where you would run dry.

**Your party matters.** Healing Stream, Mana Spring and Mana Tide only reach your party. Ask to be grouped with whoever takes the damage, usually the tank and the melee.

**Water totem.** Healing Stream heals your party every 2 seconds for 5 minutes. Mana Spring is the other choice, but two Mana Springs do not stack, so if another shaman in your group already has one down, Healing Stream is the one that adds something. Mana Tide replaces either for 12 seconds; drop it under 25% mana, then put your Water totem back.

**Cleansing.** Poison Cleansing Totem and Disease Cleansing Totem take the Water slot and clear your party every few seconds; drop one on fights that poison or disease. Cure Poison and Cure Disease reach anyone the totems do not. Ancestral Spirit brings a dead player back after the fight.

**Earth and Air.** Strength of Earth, Grace of Air and Windfury reach the whole raid within 30 yards, so one shaman covers the melee. If you are the only shaman near them, those totems are yours to drop.

## Stat priority

Healing power makes every heal bigger, from any source, including items that add damage and healing; damage-only spell power and Nature power do nothing for a heal. Mp5 keeps you casting through a long fight. Intellect is your mana pool, and Water Shield returns a share of that pool with every globe. Crit fires Water Shield on top of the bigger heal. Spirit only adds mana regeneration, and Mindfulness keeps half of that running while you heal.

## Best in slot

Your raid set leans on crafts. Dormant Heart of the Mountain is an Enchanting craft that binds on pickup, so you make that one yourself; every other craft in it binds when equipped, so you can buy it. Carry a healing staff, or a one-hander with a shield or held off-hand item, and at 60 the staff usually wins.

## Enchants and consumables

Brilliant Mana Oil on your weapon: mp5 and healing in one. Use your Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune as soon as each fits under your mana cap. They are on separate cooldowns, and using them early is what gets you a second use of each. Demonic Rune costs you health, so use it when nobody is hitting you.

## Races

The Dwarf shaman is new in Forever and is the only Alliance shaman. Stoneform cuts physical damage by 10% and clears poisons, diseases and bleeds, which keeps you alive through a cleave or a stray add.

On the Horde, take Orc: Blood Fury increases attack power and spell power by 10% for 15 seconds every 2 minutes, and its spell power covers your heals. Troll's Berserking, 10% casting speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes, heals a little less. Tauren's Endurance, 5% more health, is the pick when you are the one taking the damage. Windshaper Skyborne brings Wind Blessed's 2% haste, always on.

## Professions

Your raid set asks for one profession, Enchanting: Dormant Heart of the Mountain binds on pickup, so nobody can make it for you. Pair it with Herbalism or Alchemy for Major Mana Potions and flasks, which is most of what a healer spends gold on. Every other craft in your set binds when equipped and can be bought.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

You quest like any shaman: Lightning Bolt to pull, Earth Shock from 4, Flame Shock and Searing Totem from 10, and your weapon to finish, since a shaman has no wand. Do the Call of Earth class quest at 4 and Call of Fire at 10: they give the Earth Totem every Earth totem needs and the Fire Totem every Fire totem needs, carried in your bags, and without them those totems do not cast. Talents go Tidal Focus from 10 to 12, 1% cheaper heals per point, and Improved Healing Wave at 13 and 14, a tenth of a second off Healing Wave per point: a faster Healing Wave only burns your mana sooner at these levels. Mindfulness takes 15 to 17 so mana keeps coming back while you cast, and Tidal Focus is full at 19. Healing Wave gets new ranks at 6, 12 and 18; train each one before a dungeon, but at 18 and 19 heal the tank with rank 1, which heals more than any bigger rank before your mana runs out. Tremor Totem at 18 breaks the sleeps in Wailing Caverns. Take quest rewards with Intellect over vendor gear, wear leather, and replace your starter weapon with the first quest staff or mace that carries spell damage.

### Levels 20 to 60

Lesser Healing Wave and Healing Stream Totem come at 20; do the Call of Water quest the same day, since every Water totem needs the Water Totem in your bags. Water Shield is in at 20, Improved Healing Wave is full at 23, and Mana Tide Totem comes at 25, the first level the fourth row opens. The talent teaches its first rank; train the others at 38, 48 and 58, each one a big step up. Restorative Totems from 26 and Healing Way from 30 come next, a stronger Healing Stream and Mana Spring and a quarter more on every Healing Wave, and you can heal any dungeon the day you walk in. Then one point of Tidal Mastery and Purification by 39 open Riptide at 40. Your everyday Healing Wave in a dungeon is not your top rank once you wear healing gear: that gear's bonus is worth far more for its mana on a cheaper rank, so a cheaper one heals more before your mana runs out. Cast rank 2 from 20 to 22 (a dwarf or Windshaper takes rank 3 at 22), rank 3 at 23 and 24, rank 2 at 25 (a dwarf stays on rank 3), rank 3 until 36, rank 4 until 41, rank 5 from 42 to 45, rank 6 from 46 to 55, rank 7 from 56 to 58 and rank 8 at 59, and from 55 keep your top rank for Nature's Swiftness. Chain Heal comes at 40, and rank 1 stays your Chain Heal until 59 even after you train ranks 2 and 3: it costs 247 mana with Tidal Focus to about 300 and 385 for ranks 2 and 3, and the cheaper heal keeps you casting longer. Riptide, the bottom of the tree, is in at 40. Train its second rank at 50 and its third at 60; the talent only teaches the first. From 41, Ancestral Knowledge and Thundering Strikes take ten points from Enhancement, then the rest of Tidal Mastery, Nature's Swiftness at 55, and Ancestral Healing and Healing Focus last.
