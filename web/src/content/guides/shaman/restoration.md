## Overview

### Strengths

- Chain Heal cast on a target with your Riptide heals it 25% more
- Chain Heal heals three people for one cast
- Water Shield turns the hits you take and your critical heals into mana, three globes at a time (four with Totem of Sustaining)
- Nature's Swiftness makes your biggest Healing Wave instant when the tank is about to die

### Weaknesses

- Chain Heal only jumps to people standing close to its target, so it does little when the raid is spread out
- Your Water totems only reach your own party, and they stay put until you spend Totemic Call to bring them to you
- Without raid gear and buffs your mana does not last a long fight

### In a raid

- **Role:** Healer for the tank and the group around it
- **Brings:** Healing Stream or Mana Spring and Mana Tide for your party, plus whatever Earth and Air totems your raid leader assigns

## Talents and builds

Forty-one points in Restoration and ten in Enhancement. Thundering Strikes is 5% crit on your heals, because in Forever it raises spells as well as attacks, and crits are what fire Water Shield. Ancestral Knowledge is 10% more Intellect. Nothing in Elemental helps a heal.

- **Improved Healing Wave** (5 points): Healing Wave casts in 2.5 seconds instead of 3.
- **Tidal Focus** (5 points)
- **Mindfulness** (3 points): half your mana regeneration keeps running while you cast.
- **Water Shield** (1 point): your mana engine. Three globes, each returning 2% of your maximum mana when you are hit or a heal crits.
- **Healing Focus** (3 points): a 70% chance not to lose cast time when you take damage.
- **Tidal Mastery** (5 points): 5% more crit on your heals, and so more Water Shield returns.
- **Restorative Totems** (5 points): Mana Spring 25% stronger, Healing Stream 50% stronger.
- **Mana Tide Totem** (1 point): mana for your whole party for 12 seconds, every 5 minutes.
- **Healing Way** (3 points): Healing Wave heals 25% more, always on.
- **Nature's Swiftness** (1 point): one instant Healing Wave every 3 minutes.
- **Purification** (5 points)
- **Riptide** (1 point): an instant 15 second heal over time on a 12 second cooldown, and Chain Heal heals its target 25% more.

Ancestral Healing is three points for 25% more armor on your target for 15 seconds after a crit heal, which is a real help to the tank.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Water Shield, then Healing Stream Totem with your party. Water Shield lasts 10 minutes but only holds three globes (four with Totem of Sustaining), and only one globe fires every few seconds; recast it when the globes are gone.

**Tank first.** Riptide heals over its 15 seconds with nothing up front, so it never answers a big hit on its own; it is there to set up the Chain Heal, whose two jumps go to the most injured people near the tank, each for half the heal before. Keep Nature's Swiftness for the tank under 25%, and never spend it on a low rank.

**Low ranks.** A rank keeps your whole healing bonus until you are 17 levels past the level you learned it, then loses a twentieth of it every level after, so at 60 a rank learned at 23 or earlier gets none of your gear. The ranks that keep all of it at 60 are Healing Wave 8 to 10, Lesser Healing Wave 4 to 6 and Chain Heal 2 and 3. Healing Wave 7 and Chain Heal 1, both learned at 40, keep most of it, Lesser Healing Wave 3 about two thirds and Healing Wave 6 under half.

**Everyone else.** Heal them directly only while your mana is above half: Riptide under 50%, Lesser Healing Wave under 25%. Otherwise Chain Heal heals them.

**Chain Heal ranks.** Rank 3 costs about 385 mana with Tidal Focus for a 490 base heal, rank 2 about 300 for 335. With a raid's healing gear rank 2 heals the most per point of mana and rank 3 the most per cast. At 60, rank 1 is never better than rank 2. Cast rank 3 while your mana is ahead of the fight, and drop to rank 2 on fights where you would run dry.

**Your party matters.** Healing Stream, Mana Spring and Mana Tide only reach your party. Ask to be grouped with whoever takes the damage, usually the tank and the melee.

**Water totem.** Mana Spring is the other choice, but two Mana Springs do not stack, so if another shaman in your group already has one down, Healing Stream is the one that adds something.

**Cleansing.** Poison Cleansing Totem and Disease Cleansing Totem take the Water slot and clear your party every few seconds; drop one on fights that poison or disease. Cure Poison and Cure Disease reach anyone the totems do not. Ancestral Spirit brings a dead player back after the fight.

**Earth and Air.** Strength of Earth, Grace of Air and Windfury reach the whole raid within 30 yards, so one shaman covers the melee. If you are the only shaman near them, those totems are yours to drop.

## Stat priority

Healing power counts from any source, including items that add damage and healing; damage-only spell power and Nature power do nothing for a heal. Water Shield returns a share of your mana pool with every globe, and crit fires it on top of the bigger heal. Spirit only adds mana regeneration, and Mindfulness keeps half of that running while you heal.

## Best in slot

Your raid set leans on crafts. Dormant Heart of the Mountain is an Enchanting craft that binds on pickup, so you make that one yourself; every other craft in it binds when equipped, so you can buy it. Carry a healing staff, or a one-hander with a shield or held off-hand item, and at 60 the staff usually wins.

The totem slot has three real choices. Tidal Totem, sold by Pix Xizzix, makes Healing Wave 5% cheaper. Totem of Sustaining, from the Scholomance bosses, gives Water Shield a fourth globe: in Forever that is its whole effect, and the Lesser Healing Wave healing it carried in Classic is gone. Totem of Urgency, an Enchanting recipe that opens with the raids, puts 4% crit on Lesser Healing Wave. The best in slot list picks between them for your gear.

## Enchants and consumables

Brilliant Mana Oil on your weapon: mp5 and healing in one. Use your Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune as soon as each fits under your mana cap. They are on separate cooldowns, and using them early is what gets you a second use of each. Demonic Rune costs you health, so use it when nobody is hitting you.

## Races

The Dwarf shaman is new in Forever and is the only Alliance shaman. Stoneform keeps you alive through a cleave or a stray add.

On the Horde, take Orc: Blood Fury's spell power covers your heals. Troll's Berserking heals a little less. Tauren's Endurance is the pick when you are the one taking the damage. Windshaper Skyborne brings Wind Blessed's 2% haste, always on.

## Professions

Take Enchanting for Dormant Heart of the Mountain, which nobody can make for you. Pair it with Herbalism or Alchemy for Major Mana Potions and flasks, which is most of what a healer spends gold on.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

You quest like any shaman: Lightning Bolt to pull, Earth Shock from 4, Flame Shock and Searing Totem from 10, and your weapon to finish, since a shaman has no wand. Do the Call of Earth class quest at 4 and Call of Fire at 10: without the Earth Totem and Fire Totem they give, carried in your bags, those totems do not cast.

- **10 to 12:** Tidal Focus, 1% cheaper heals per point.
- **13 and 14:** Improved Healing Wave, a tenth of a second off Healing Wave per point. A faster Healing Wave only burns your mana sooner at these levels.
- **15 to 17:** Mindfulness, so mana keeps coming back while you cast.
- **18 and 19:** Tidal Focus, full at 19.

Healing Wave gets new ranks at 6, 12 and 18; train each one before a dungeon, but at 18 and 19 heal the tank with rank 1, which heals more than any bigger rank before your mana runs out. Tremor Totem at 18 breaks the sleeps in Wailing Caverns. Take quest rewards with Intellect over vendor gear, wear leather, and replace your starter weapon with the first quest staff or mace that carries spell damage.

### Levels 20 to 60

Lesser Healing Wave, Healing Stream Totem and Water Shield come at 20; do the Call of Water quest the same day, since every Water totem needs the Water Totem in your bags.

- **20:** Water Shield, the first level the third row opens; it goes up before every pull from here.
- **21 to 23:** Improved Healing Wave, full at 23.
- **24:** a point of Ancestral Healing.
- **25:** Mana Tide Totem, the first level the fourth row opens. The talent teaches its first rank; train the others at 38, 48 and 58, each one a big step up.
- **From 26:** Restorative Totems, then Healing Way from 30, and you can heal any dungeon the day you walk in.
- **By 39:** one point of Tidal Mastery and Purification, which open Riptide at 40. The talent only teaches the first rank; train its second at 50 and its third at 60.
- **From 41:** Ancestral Knowledge and Thundering Strikes take ten points from Enhancement, then the rest of Tidal Mastery, Nature's Swiftness at 55, and Ancestral Healing and Healing Focus last.

Once you wear healing gear, your everyday Healing Wave in a dungeon is not your top rank: that gear's bonus is worth far more for its mana on a cheaper rank, so a cheaper one heals more before your mana runs out. From 55 keep your top rank for Nature's Swiftness. Cast:

- **20 to 22:** rank 2 (a dwarf or Windshaper takes rank 3 at 22).
- **23 and 24:** rank 3.
- **25:** rank 2 (a dwarf stays on rank 3).
- **26 to 36:** rank 3.
- **37 to 39:** rank 6, trained at 32: with your talents at these levels it heals enough more than rank 4 to pay for its mana.
- **40 to 43:** rank 5. Rank 7 arrives at 40 and costs more than it gives back, and rank 5 is cheaper than 6 for the same job.
- **44 to 55:** rank 6 again, once your healing bonus outgrows rank 5.
- **56 to 58:** rank 7.
- **59:** rank 8.

Chain Heal comes at 40, and rank 1 stays your Chain Heal until 59 even after you train ranks 2 and 3: it costs 247 mana with Tidal Focus to about 300 and 385 for ranks 2 and 3, and the cheaper heal keeps you casting longer.
