## Overview

Enhancement is the shaman's melee spec: one weapon with Windfury on it, Stormstrike on cooldown, and a free instant Lightning Bolt every time Maelstrom Weapon reaches five stacks.

### Strengths

- Windfury Weapon's extra swings are free attacks with bonus attack power
- Maelstrom Weapon turns melee swings into free, instant Lightning Bolts
- Hit on gear covers your swings and your spells
- Strength of Earth and Grace of Air reach the whole raid in Forever

### Weaknesses

- No dual wield: Forever's shamans fight with one weapon, so every point of Windfury rides on it
- Totems stay where you drop them, and bosses move
- Shocks and totems make mana a real limit without raid buffs

### In a raid

- **Role:** Melee damage
- **Brings:** Strength of Earth and Grace of Air for every raid member in range, or Windfury Totem when no other shaman has it down

## Talents and builds

Both builds go deep Enhancement for Stormstrike, Maelstrom Weapon and Rage of the Farseer, and both take Tidal Focus in Restoration: its 5% hit counts for your swings as well as your spells. The leveling build puts its Elemental points into Convection, Concussion, Elemental Alacrity and Call of Flame for cheaper, faster, harder Lightning Bolts and a stronger Searing Totem. The raid build moves them to Concussion, Reverberation, Elemental Devastation and Elemental Focus: Earth Shock hits harder and comes back sooner, its crits give your swings 9% crit for 10 seconds, and now and then a spell is free. Both builds take Shamanistic Focus, which cuts the cost of your shocks by 45%, and Improved Ghost Wolf, which makes Ghost Wolf instant and usable indoors: the fastest way across a raid room when the boss moves. The raid build pays for its twelfth Elemental point and the third point of Mindfulness with two points of Ancestral Knowledge.

- **Thundering Strikes** (5 points): 5% crit on every attack and spell, and crits are what start Flurry.
- **Ancestral Knowledge** (5 points, 3 in the raid build): up to 10% more Intellect. With Mental Dexterity, Intellect is attack power.
- **Mental Dexterity** (3 points): your attack power goes up by one and a half times your Intellect. This is why Enhancement wants Intellect on its gear.
- **Elemental Weapons** (3 points): Windfury Weapon's extra attacks carry 40% more attack power.
- **Flurry** (5 points): 30% attack speed for your next three swings after a melee crit.
- **Stormstrike** (1 point): an instant strike for 150% weapon damage on a 6 second cooldown that makes your next Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Earth Shock on the target hit 20% harder.
- **Mental Quickness** (2 points): 30% of your Intellect as spell power, for your shocks and bolts.
- **Maelstrom Weapon** (5 points): your melee hits build up to five stacks, each taking 20% off your next Lightning Bolt's cast time and mana. At five it is instant and free.
- **Rage of the Farseer** (1 point): 30% attack speed for 25 seconds every 3 minutes. Press it with your trinkets.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Put Windfury Weapon on your weapon. Shamans in Forever never dual wield: you carry a two-hander, or a one-hander with a shield or a held off-hand item, and Windfury goes on the weapon. Drop Strength of Earth Totem and your Air totem where the tank will hold the boss, a few seconds before the pull, so they are up when you get there. If your party has no other shaman, put Mana Spring Totem in your Water slot as well. If the boss moves more than 30 yards from them, stand where it is now and Totemic Call brings them to you.

**On the pull.** Rage of the Farseer, trinkets, Blood Fury and your potion go together on the pull and again on cooldown. Then Stormstrike.

**The loop.**

1. Keep Strength of Earth Totem and your Air totem down.
2. Stormstrike on cooldown.
3. Lightning Bolt the moment Maelstrom Weapon shows five stacks. It is instant and free, so it costs you nothing. If Stormstrike is a second or two from ready, use it first and let the bolt take the 20%.
4. Searing Totem when it is down, unless the boss is about to die.
5. Fire Nova when Clearcasting makes it free.
6. Earth Shock on cooldown while you are above half mana. It takes Stormstrike's 20% when no bolt is ready.

**Which Air totem.** Windfury Weapon on your own weapon turns off any Windfury Totem's bonus for you, so you never drop Windfury for yourself. In Forever Windfury Totem, Grace of Air and Strength of Earth reach every raid member within 30 yards, not just your party. One Windfury Totem near the boss covers every warrior and rogue within 30 yards of it, and it is worth far more to them than Grace of Air is to you. If another shaman already has it down, take Grace of Air. There is nothing to twist: Windfury Totem in Forever is an aura around the totem, not a buff that lingers on the weapon, so it ends the moment you replace the totem.

**Fire Nova on Clearcasting.** In the raid build, Elemental Focus sometimes makes your next damage spell free. Spend it on Fire Nova: in melee your Searing Totem stands within its 10 yards of the boss. Outside a proc, Fire Nova on its own cooldown costs more mana than its damage is worth on one target, and so does Flame Shock.

**Packs.** Magma Totem and Fire Nova both hit within a few yards of your Fire totem, and in melee you are standing at it. On a big pack, drop Magma Totem, use Fire Nova on its 10 second cooldown and Chain Lightning while your mana lasts, and keep spending five Maelstrom stacks on Lightning Bolt.

**Mana.** Without raid buffs, stop using Earth Shock below half mana and let your swings and free bolts carry you.

**Lightning Shield** only fires when something hits you, which behind a boss is never. Keep it for leveling and for adds that turn on you.

## Stat priority

Hit covers your swings, your Stormstrike and your shocks at once in Forever, so it is never wasted on half your kit. Intellect is worth far more to you than to any other melee: Mental Dexterity turns every point into one and a half attack power, Mental Quickness turns part of it into spell power for your shocks, and it is your mana and your spell crit. Crit keeps Flurry up. Agility gives crit and armor. Strength is attack power twice over.

## Best in slot

Your raid set leans on Leatherworking crafts that bind when equipped, a crafted Blacksmithing axe and two Enchanting trinkets. Shamans wear leather until 40 and mail from 40. Off hand, a shield or a held item; never a second weapon. The full list, with alternatives for every slot, is on the [Enhancement BiS page](/bis/shaman/enhancement).

## Enchants and consumables

Windfury Weapon is your weapon's temporary enchant, so there is no room for a sharpening stone or weighted stone on it. Keep Windfury Weapon up all fight, and use the weapon enchant and consumables listed here.

## Races

The Dwarf shaman is new in Forever and is the only Alliance shaman. Artisan's Precision's 1% hit with spells and attacks is your racial. Stoneform's physical damage cut is a free cooldown for cleaves and add phases.

On the Horde, Orc is the pick: Blood Fury is 10% attack power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes, and you press it with Rage of the Farseer. Troll's Berserking adds 10% attack speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes and lines up with Rage of the Farseer just as well. Windshaper Skyborne's 2% haste is always on. Tauren's racials do nothing for your damage.

## Professions

Take Enchanting. Frozen Heart of the Mountain and Molten Heart of the Mountain are Enchanting crafts that bind on pickup, and your raid set wears both. Your Leatherworking pieces and the Blacksmithing axe bind when equipped, so buy them or ask a guildmate; your second profession can be a gathering one that pays for them.

## Leveling

Enhancement is the shaman's best leveling spec. You hit hard in melee, take hits well, and spend little time drinking. Keep Lightning Shield up while you quest: mobs hit you, so it does damage. Rockbiter Weapon is your only imbue until Flametongue at 10; Windfury Weapon at 30 replaces both and stays on for good. Take Stormstrike as soon as the tree opens it. Strength of Earth Totem from 10 and Searing Totem from 10 go down on every pull worth fighting; skip them on a single mob that dies in seconds. Ghost Wolf at 20 is how you travel, instant once you have Improved Ghost Wolf, and leather turns to mail at 40.
