## Overview

Enhancement is the shaman's melee spec: one weapon and its imbue, Stormstrike on cooldown, and a free instant Lightning Bolt whenever Maelstrom Weapon reaches five stacks.

### Strengths

- Windfury Weapon's extra swings are free attacks with bonus attack power
- Maelstrom Weapon turns melee swings into free, instant Lightning Bolts
- Hit on gear covers your swings and your spells
- Strength of Earth and Grace of Air reach the whole raid in Forever

### Weaknesses

- No dual wield: Forever's shamans fight with one weapon, so every point of Windfury rides on it
- Totems do not follow you: when the boss moves, Totemic Call brings them back, for a global and a quarter of your base mana
- Shocks and totems make mana a real limit without raid buffs

### In a raid

- **Role:** Melee damage
- **Brings:** Strength of Earth and Grace of Air for every raid member in range, or Windfury Totem when no other shaman has it down

## Talents and builds

Both builds go deep Enhancement for Stormstrike, Maelstrom Weapon and Rage of the Farseer, and both take Tidal Focus in Restoration: its 5% hit counts for your swings as well as your spells. The leveling build puts its Elemental points into Convection, Concussion, Elemental Devastation and Call of Flame: cheaper shocks and bolts, harder Lightning Bolts and Earth Shocks, 9% melee crit for 10 seconds after a spell crits, and a stronger Searing Totem. The raid build keeps Elemental Devastation, fills Concussion and moves the rest to Reverberation and Elemental Focus: Earth Shock hits harder and comes back sooner, and now and then a spell is free. Both builds take Shamanistic Focus, which cuts the cost of your shocks by 45%, and Improved Ghost Wolf, which makes Ghost Wolf instant and usable indoors: the fastest way across a raid room when the boss moves. The raid build pays for its twelfth Elemental point and the third point of Mindfulness with two points of Ancestral Knowledge.

- **Thundering Strikes** (5 points): 5% crit on every attack and spell, and crits are what start Flurry.
- **Ancestral Knowledge** (5 points, 3 in the raid build): up to 10% more Intellect. With Mental Dexterity, Intellect is attack power. If your raid never asks you to run, the two Improved Ghost Wolf points go here instead for a little more damage.
- **Mental Dexterity** (3 points): your attack power goes up by one and a half times your Intellect. This is why Enhancement wants Intellect on its gear.
- **Elemental Weapons** (3 points): Windfury Weapon's extra attacks carry 40% more attack power, and Rockbiter Weapon's attack power is 20% bigger.
- **Flurry** (5 points): 30% attack speed for your next three swings after a melee crit.
- **Stormstrike** (1 point): an instant strike for 150% weapon damage on a 6 second cooldown that makes your next Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Earth Shock on the target hit 20% harder.
- **Mental Quickness** (2 points): 30% of your Intellect as spell power, for your shocks and bolts.
- **Maelstrom Weapon** (5 points): each melee hit has a chance to add a stack, up to five, each taking 20% off your next Lightning Bolt's cast time and mana. At five it is instant and free; spend it inside 30 seconds.
- **Rage of the Farseer** (1 point): 30% attack speed for 25 seconds every 3 minutes. Press it with your trinkets.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Imbue your weapon: Rockbiter Weapon in a raid where a Windfury Totem reaches you, Windfury Weapon everywhere else. Drop Strength of Earth Totem and your Air totem where the tank will hold the boss, a few seconds before the pull, so they are up when you get there. If your party has no other shaman, put Mana Spring Totem in your Water slot as well. If the boss moves away from them, follow it and press Totemic Call: it relocates your active totems to your location.

**On the pull.** Rage of the Farseer, trinkets, any damage racial (Blood Fury or Berserking) and your potion go together on the pull and again on cooldown. Then Stormstrike.

**The loop.**

1. Keep Strength of Earth Totem and your Air totem down.
2. Stormstrike on cooldown.
3. Lightning Bolt the moment Maelstrom Weapon shows five stacks, once you have all five points: it is instant and free then, so it costs you nothing. With fewer points even five stacks leave the bolt a cast that holds up your next swing, so keep swinging instead. If Stormstrike is a second or two from ready, use it first and let the bolt take the 20%.
4. Searing Totem when it is down, unless the boss is about to die.
5. Fire Nova when Clearcasting makes it free.
6. Earth Shock on cooldown while you have more than a quarter of your mana. It takes Stormstrike's 20% when no bolt is ready.

**Which imbue.** Windfury Weapon on your main hand turns off any Windfury Totem's bonus for you. So in a raid where a Windfury Totem reaches you, yours or another shaman's, put Rockbiter Weapon on instead: Forever's Rockbiter is a large flat attack power bonus, it keeps the totem's extra swings working for you, and Elemental Weapons makes it bigger. It also adds threat to your swings, so watch your meter early in a fight. Solo and anywhere without that totem, use Windfury Weapon.

**Which Air totem.** Windfury Totem, Grace of Air and Strength of Earth reach every raid member within 30 yards, not just your party. One Windfury Totem near the boss covers every warrior and rogue within 30 yards of it, and for them it is worth far more than Grace of Air's Agility. If another shaman already has it down, take Grace of Air; if you drop it yourself, keep Rockbiter on so its swings count for you too. There is nothing to twist: Windfury Totem is an aura around the totem, not a buff that lingers on the weapon, so it ends the moment you replace the totem.

**Fire Nova on Clearcasting.** In the raid build, Elemental Focus sometimes makes your next damage spell free. Spend it on Fire Nova: in melee your Searing Totem stands within its 10 yards of the boss. Outside a proc, Fire Nova on its own cooldown costs more mana than its damage is worth on one target, and so does Flame Shock.

**Packs.** Magma Totem and Fire Nova both hit within a few yards of your Fire totem, and in melee you are standing at it. On a big pack, drop Magma Totem, use Fire Nova on its 10 second cooldown and Chain Lightning while your mana lasts, and once you have all five points of Maelstrom Weapon keep spending five stacks on Lightning Bolt.

**Mana.** Stop shocking at a quarter of your mana: that last quarter pays for Stormstrike, which hits harder than the shock you skip, and for dropping your totems again. Your swings and free bolts carry you until your mana comes back. In a raid, Judgement of Wisdom and Mana Spring bring in enough mana that you never run dry, so Earth Shock never needs to wait and you shock freely down to that quarter.

**Lightning Shield** only fires when something hits you, which behind a boss is never. Keep it for leveling and for adds that turn on you.

## Stat priority

Strength is attack power twice over. Intellect does more for you than for any other melee: Mental Dexterity turns every point into one and a half attack power, Mental Quickness turns part of it into spell power for your shocks, and it is your mana and your spell crit. Crit keeps Flurry up. Agility gives crit and armor. Plain attack power adds to every swing, and plain spell power to your shocks. Hit covers your swings, your Stormstrike and your shocks at once. Your swings stop missing a raid boss at 9% hit and your spells at 16%, so past 9% hit only helps your shocks, Fire Nova, totems and free bolts, a small share of your damage. ## Best in slot

Your raid set leans on crafts. Frozen Heart of the Mountain is an Enchanting craft that binds on pickup, and an Alliance set adds Molten Heart of the Mountain, which does too, so you make those yourself; every other craft in it binds when equipped, so you can buy it. Shamans learn mail at 40, but a leather piece with better stats still wins its slot. Carry a two-hander, or a one-hander with a shield or a held item; never a second weapon. The full list, with alternatives for every slot, is on the [Enhancement BiS page](/bis/shaman/enhancement).

## Enchants and consumables

Your imbue is your weapon's temporary enchant, Rockbiter Weapon under a raid's Windfury Totem and Windfury Weapon without it, so there is no room for a sharpening stone or a weightstone on it. Keep it up all fight. Elixir of the Mongoose, Juju Power, Juju Might and R.O.I.D.S. are your raid buffs, with a Smoked Desert Dumpling for food.

## Races

The Dwarf shaman is new in Forever and is the only Alliance shaman. Artisan's Precision's 1% hit with spells and attacks is your racial. Stoneform's physical damage cut is a free cooldown for cleaves and add phases.

On the Horde, Orc is the pick: Blood Fury is 10% attack power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes. Press it with Rage of the Farseer on the pull and on its own cooldown after that. Troll's Berserking adds 10% attack speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes and lines up with Rage of the Farseer just as well. Windshaper Skyborne, the paid Heroic Pack race, brings Wind Blessed's 2% haste, always on. Tauren's racials do nothing for your damage.

## Professions

Take Enchanting. Frozen Heart of the Mountain is an Enchanting trinket that binds on pickup, and so is Molten Heart of the Mountain, which an Alliance raid set wears beside it, so nobody can make them for you. Every other craft in your set binds when equipped, so buy them or ask a guildmate; your second profession can be a gathering one that pays for them.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Rockbiter Weapon goes on at 1 and Earth Shock comes at 4. Lightning Shield at 8 stays up while you quest: mobs hit you, so it does damage. Rockbiter stays on your weapon until Windfury Weapon at 30; below 20 its attack power is worth more than Flametongue Weapon. Do the Call of Earth class quest at 4 and Call of Fire at 10: they give the Earth Totem every Earth totem needs and the Fire Totem every Fire totem needs, carried in your bags, and without them those totems do not cast. From 10, Searing Totem and Strength of Earth Totem go down on every pull that lasts. Talents open with Thundering Strikes from 10 to 14, 1% crit with spells and attacks per point, then Ancestral Knowledge from 15 to 19, 2% Intellect per point. Replace the starter weapon with the first slow two-hander a quest gives you: staves are trained already, and a weapon master teaches two-handed maces and axes. Quest rewards beat vendor gear, you wear leather until 40, and you never dual wield.

### Levels 20 to 60

Enhancement is the shaman's best leveling spec. You hit hard in melee, take hits well, and spend little time drinking. Windfury Weapon at 30 replaces Rockbiter and stays on while you level. Stormstrike sits in the fourth row and you can press it the moment you learn it: from 20 this build fills Mental Dexterity, puts two points in Elemental Weapons and takes Stormstrike at 25, the first level the fourth row opens. Then it finishes Elemental Weapons, takes Shamanistic Focus and fills Flurry by 32, with Improved Ghost Wolf at 33 and 34. Maelstrom Weapon starts at 37 and is full at 42; spend its stacks on Lightning Bolt from 42, when the bolt is instant and free, and until then let them fall off and keep swinging. Ghost Wolf at 20 is how you travel, instant once you have Improved Ghost Wolf, and leather turns to mail at 40.
