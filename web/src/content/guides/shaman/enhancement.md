## Overview

### Strengths

- Windfury Weapon's extra swings are free attacks with bonus attack power
- Maelstrom Weapon turns melee swings into free, instant Lightning Bolts
- Hit on gear covers your swings and your spells
- Strength of Earth and Grace of Air reach the whole raid in Forever

### Weaknesses

- No dual wield: Forever's shamans fight with one weapon, so every point of Windfury rides on it
- Totems do not follow you: when the boss moves, Totemic Call brings them back, for a global and a quarter of your base mana
- Shocks and totems make mana a real limit without raid buffs

### In a raid

- **Role:** Melee damage
- **Brings:** Strength of Earth and Grace of Air for every raid member in range, or Windfury Totem when no other shaman has it down

## Talents and builds

Both builds go deep Enhancement for Stormstrike, Maelstrom Weapon and Rage of the Farseer, and both take Tidal Focus in Restoration: its 5% hit counts for your swings as well as your spells. The leveling build puts its Elemental points into Convection and Elemental Devastation: shocks and bolts a tenth cheaper, and 9% melee crit for 10 seconds after a spell crits. It also takes both points of Improved Stormstrike, which keeps half your regeneration running for 15 seconds after every Stormstrike, and all three of Mindfulness, which adds half whenever you cast: with no Mana Spring and no paladin beside you, that is what pays for your shocks on a long pull. The raid build drops Convection and spends its Elemental points on Concussion, Elemental Devastation, Reverberation and Elemental Focus: Earth Shock hits harder and comes back sooner, and now and then a spell is free. Both builds take Shamanistic Focus, which cuts the cost of your shocks by 45%, and Improved Ghost Wolf, which makes Ghost Wolf instant and usable indoors: the fastest way across a raid room when the boss moves. The raid build pays for four more Elemental points with two of Ancestral Knowledge and both of Improved Stormstrike.

- **Thundering Strikes** (5 points): 5% crit on every attack and spell, and crits are what start Flurry.
- **Ancestral Knowledge** (5 points, 3 in the raid build): up to 10% more Intellect. With Mental Dexterity, Intellect is attack power. If your raid never asks you to run, the two Improved Ghost Wolf points go here instead for a little more damage.
- **Mental Dexterity** (3 points): attack power equal to a third of your Intellect per point, all of it at three.
- **Elemental Weapons** (3 points): Windfury Weapon's extra attacks carry 40% more attack power, and Rockbiter Weapon's attack power is 20% bigger.
- **Flurry** (5 points): 30% attack speed for your next three swings after a melee crit.
- **Stormstrike** (1 point): an instant strike for 100% weapon damage on an 8 second cooldown that makes your next Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Earth Shock on the target hit 20% harder.
- **Mental Quickness** (2 points): 30% of your Intellect as spell power, for your shocks and bolts.
- **Improved Stormstrike** (2 points, leveling build only): every Stormstrike keeps half your mana regeneration running while you cast for 15 seconds, and each time you dodge or parry, Stormstrike's cooldown resets. Judgement of Wisdom and Mana Spring make it dead weight in a raid.
- **Maelstrom Weapon** (5 points): your main hand adds a stack six times a minute with one point, ten with five, so a slow weapon gets more out of every swing. Each stack takes 20% off your next Lightning Bolt's cast time and mana at every rank, so five make it instant and free; spend them inside 30 seconds.
- **Rage of the Farseer** (1 point): 30% attack speed for 25 seconds every 3 minutes. Press it with your trinkets.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Put Windfury Weapon on your weapon, in a raid or out. Drop Strength of Earth Totem and your Air totem where the tank will hold the boss, a few seconds before the pull, so they are up when you get there. If your party has no other shaman, put Mana Spring Totem in your Water slot as well. If the boss moves away from them, follow it and press Totemic Call: it relocates your active totems to your location.

**On the pull.** Rage of the Farseer, trinkets, any damage racial (Blood Fury or Berserking) and your potion go together on the pull and again on cooldown. Then Stormstrike.

**Five stacks.** If Stormstrike is a second or two from ready when Maelstrom Weapon hits five, use it first and let the Lightning Bolt take the 20%. Searing Totem when it is down, unless the boss is about to die.

**Which imbue.** Windfury Weapon from 30, raid or not. It works alongside a Windfury Totem: each has its own chance on your swings, so under another shaman's totem you get both.

**Which Air totem.** One Windfury Totem near the boss covers every warrior and rogue within 30 yards of it, and for them it is worth far more than Grace of Air's Agility. If you drop it yourself, its extra swings count for you too, on top of your weapon's. There is nothing to twist: Windfury Totem is an aura around the totem, so it ends the moment you replace the totem.

**Fire Nova and Flame Shock.** Outside a Clearcasting proc, Fire Nova on its own cooldown costs more mana than its damage is worth on one target, and so does Flame Shock.

**Mana.** Shock on cooldown until your bar is down to about a sixth, then hold that for Stormstrike and your next totem drop. Keeping a quarter back throws shocks away now that Stormstrike comes every 8 seconds and spends less mana over a fight: about 3% of your damage at 60 in a five-man. Shocking all the way to empty adds a little more, but at 40 you spend about a third of a long fight dry, with no Stormstrike and no totems. In a raid, Judgement of Wisdom and Mana Spring keep the bar high enough that you shock on cooldown and never reach the reserve.

**Packs.** Magma Totem and Fire Nova both hit within a few yards of your Fire totem, and in melee you are standing at it. On a big pack, drop Magma Totem, use Fire Nova on its 10 second cooldown and Chain Lightning while your mana lasts, and keep spending five Maelstrom Weapon stacks on Lightning Bolt.

**Lightning Shield** only fires when something hits you, which behind a boss is never. Keep it for leveling and for adds that turn on you.

## Stat priority

Strength is two attack power a point. Intellect is one through Mental Dexterity once you have all three points, and it also feeds your shocks through Mental Quickness and your mana pool. Crit keeps Flurry up. Hit covers your swings, your Stormstrike and your shocks at once. Your swings stop missing a raid boss at 9% hit and your spells at 16%, so past 9% hit only helps your shocks, Fire Nova, totems and free bolts, a small share of your damage.

## Best in slot

Your raid set leans on crafts. Frozen Heart of the Mountain is an Enchanting craft that binds on pickup, and an Alliance set adds Molten Heart of the Mountain, which does too, so you make those yourself; every other craft in it binds when equipped, so you can buy it. A leather piece with better stats still beats mail in its slot. Carry a two-hander, or a one-hander with a shield or a held item; never a second weapon.

## Enchants and consumables

Your imbue is your weapon's temporary enchant, Windfury Weapon in a raid or out, so there is no room for a sharpening stone or a weightstone on it. Keep it up all fight. Elixir of the Mongoose, Juju Power, Juju Might and R.O.I.D.S. are your raid buffs, with a Smoked Desert Dumpling for food.

## Races

The Dwarf shaman is new in Forever and is the only Alliance shaman. Artisan's Precision's 1% hit covers your spells and your attacks, and Stoneform is a free cooldown for cleaves and add phases.

On the Horde, take Orc for Blood Fury's attack power: press it with Rage of the Farseer on the pull and on its own cooldown after that. Troll's Berserking lines up with Rage of the Farseer just as well. Windshaper Skyborne brings Wind Blessed's 2% haste, always on. Tauren's racials do nothing for your damage.

## Professions

Take Enchanting for Frozen Heart of the Mountain, and on the Alliance Molten Heart of the Mountain, which nobody can make for you. Your second profession can be a gathering one that pays for the rest.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Rockbiter Weapon goes on at 1. From 10, run Flametongue Weapon: it beats Rockbiter by a point or two, and by 29 it is a coin flip, so a new Rockbiter rank is not worth a trip to town; swap to Rockbiter on fire-immune mobs. Windfury Weapon takes over at 30. Earth Shock comes at 4, and Lightning Shield at 8 stays up while you quest, since mobs hit you. Do the Call of Earth class quest at 4 and Call of Fire at 10: without the Earth Totem and Fire Totem they give, carried in your bags, those totems do not cast. From 10, Searing Totem and Strength of Earth Totem go down on every pull that lasts.

- **10 to 14:** Thundering Strikes, 1% crit with spells and attacks per point.
- **15 to 17:** Mental Dexterity, a third of your Intellect as attack power per point.
- **From 18:** Ancestral Knowledge, 2% Intellect per point.

Replace the starter weapon with the first slow two-hander a quest gives you: staves are trained already, and a weapon master teaches two-handed maces and axes. Quest rewards beat vendor gear.

### Levels 20 to 60

Enhancement is the shaman's best leveling spec. You hit hard in melee, take hits well, and spend little time drinking. Windfury Weapon at 30 replaces whatever imbue you were running and stays on while you level. Ghost Wolf at 20 is how you travel, instant once you have Improved Ghost Wolf, and you wear leather until 40 and mail from 40.

- **20 to 25:** fill Ancestral Knowledge, two points in Elemental Weapons, and Stormstrike at 25, the first level the fourth row opens. Press it the moment you learn it.
- **26 to 32:** finish Elemental Weapons, take Shamanistic Focus and fill Flurry by 32.
- **33 and 34:** Mental Quickness.
- **35 to 39:** Maelstrom Weapon, full at 39. Five stacks make the bolt instant and free from your first point, so spend them.
- **40:** Rage of the Farseer.
- **41 and 42:** Improved Ghost Wolf.
- **43 to 48:** a point of Convection, then Tidal Focus at 44 to 48 for 5% more hit on your swings.
- **49 and 50:** Improved Stormstrike. Half your regeneration keeps running while you cast for 15 seconds after every Stormstrike, and Stormstrike comes every 8 seconds, so it never drops: you shock all the way through a long pull instead of running dry.
- **51 to 53:** Mindfulness, half your regeneration while casting on top of Improved Stormstrike.
- **54 to 57:** fill Convection.
- **58 to 60:** Elemental Devastation, 9% melee crit for 10 seconds after a spell crits.
