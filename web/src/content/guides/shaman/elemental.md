## Overview

Elemental is the shaman's caster spec: Flame Shock kept ticking, Lightning Bolt for almost every cast, Lightning Mastery with your cooldowns, and Chain Lightning on more than one target.

### Strengths

- Call of Thunder and Elemental Fury make your Lightning Bolts crit often and crit for double
- Forever's Lightning Bolt is a 2.0 second cast with Elemental Alacrity, 1.5 under Lightning Mastery
- Chain Lightning and Magma Totem handle packs without a separate setup
- Shocks and totems are instant, so you keep doing damage on the move

### Weaknesses

- Mana decides which Lightning Bolt rank you can afford
- Searing Totem only reaches 20 yards, so you stand closer than other casters
- The raid build needs Restoration points for spell hit

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged spell damage
- **Brings:** A second set of totems for your party, and a Mana Spring for the casters in it

## Talents and builds

The leveling build spends every point in Elemental until Lightning Mastery at 40. The next five go to Thundering Strikes, 5% crit on every spell. From there the rest go to Restoration: Tidal Focus for 5% spell hit and all three points of Mindfulness, so half your mana regeneration keeps running while you cast, then Totemic Focus and Improved Reincarnation: cheaper totems and a shorter Reincarnation cooldown. Tidal Focus comes first because a Lightning Bolt that misses throws away its mana and its cast time; Improved Healing Wave only speeds up a heal your damage rotation never casts. The raid build keeps the same 31 Elemental points, Thundering Strikes and Tidal Focus, and trades Totemic Focus for Ancestral Knowledge (10% more Intellect).

Lightning Mastery is the 31st point, and Call of Thunder above it is the point that opens it. For 15 seconds every 3 minutes Lightning Bolt casts half a second faster and Chain Lightning has no cooldown. With Elemental Alacrity that makes Lightning Bolt a 1.5 second cast.

- **Convection** (5 points): 10% cheaper shocks, Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning. This is your mana talent.
- **Concussion** (5 points): more damage on Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning and Earth Shock, which is most of what you cast.
- **Elemental Alacrity** (3 points): half a second off Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning. Both go from 2.5 to 2.0 seconds.
- **Call of Flame** (3 points): Flame Shock lasts 21 seconds instead of 12, and Flame Shock, Fire Nova and your fire totems hit 15% harder. One shock now covers three and a half shock cooldowns.
- **Elemental Focus** (1 point): any Fire, Frost or Nature damage spell has a 10% chance to make your next damage spell free.
- **Call of Thunder** (1 point): 5% more crit on Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning, and the way to Lightning Mastery.
- **Lightning Overload** (3 points): a 30% chance for Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning to fire a free, smaller copy of the spell with no threat.
- **Elemental Fury** (5 points): doubles the crit bonus of your spells and your Searing and Magma Totems.
- **Lightning Mastery** (1 point): 15 seconds of faster Lightning Bolts and Chain Lightning with no cooldown, every 3 minutes.

Four more Elemental points are the toll to reach Elemental Fury, and both builds spend them the same way: two in Trial by Fire and two in Elemental Warding. Trial by Fire makes Fire Nova hit 20% harder on a 6 second cooldown and keeps Magma Totem burning for 40 seconds, so Fire Nova and Magma Totem are your damage on dungeon packs. Elemental Warding cuts the Fire, Frost and Nature damage you take by 7%. Elemental Devastation is melee crit, and you never swing a weapon, so skip it.

Mindfulness keeps half your regeneration running while you cast. Improved Reincarnation (2 points) adds 4% maximum health, cuts Reincarnation to a 40 minute cooldown and brings you back with more health and mana.

## Rotation and priority

Drop Mana Spring Totem a few seconds before the pull, then time a Lightning Bolt so it lands just after the tank's first hit; on a pull you do not trust, wait for the tank instead. Then Lightning Mastery with your cooldowns, Flame Shock, Searing Totem, and Lightning Bolt for the rest of the fight.

**Flame Shock stays up.** Forever's Flame Shock hits hard up front and ticks hard after, and with Call of Flame one global keeps it burning for 21 seconds. Reapply it the moment it falls off, ahead of everything but your cooldowns. Earth Shock stays on your bar for interrupts. It costs about twice a Lightning Bolt's mana and shares the shock cooldown with Flame Shock, so never let it delay a Flame Shock.

**Lightning Mastery goes with everything else.** Press it on the pull with your trinkets and Blood Fury, and again every 3 minutes; Blood Fury comes back sooner, so use it whenever it is ready. For its 15 seconds every Lightning Bolt is a 1.5 second cast, so you fit more bolts into the same window your trinkets are up. A Troll presses Berserking the moment it ends, for 15 more seconds of faster bolts.

**Totems.** Searing Totem is your single-target Fire totem. Its bolts reach 20 yards, so drop it within 20 yards of the boss and stand there; refresh it when it expires, every 55 seconds. Mana Spring Totem returns 10 mana every 2 seconds to your party for 5 minutes and pays for itself many times over, but two Mana Springs do not stack: if a Restoration shaman already has one down for your group, skip yours. Strength of Earth, Grace of Air and Windfury reach the whole raid within 30 yards in Forever, so leave the Earth and Air slots to whichever shaman your raid leader assigns them to.

**Fire Nova** is a pack spell for you. It only hits within 10 yards of your Fire totem, and on a boss your Searing Totem stands with you, up to 20 yards out. Let a Clearcasting proc go to your next Lightning Bolt instead.

**Lightning Bolt ranks.** With Convection, rank 10 costs 198 mana and rank 9 costs 171 for about 16% less base damage, with the same spell power bonus. In a raid, cast rank 10 the whole fight: raid buffs carry its mana close enough to the end that the bigger bolt is worth it. Without raid buffs, rank 10 alone runs you dry on a long fight, so pace it: cast rank 10 while your mana bar is fuller than the share of the fight left (above half mana at the halfway point, for example), and drop to rank 8 the moment it is not. Rank 8 is the cheapest bolt that still takes all of your spell damage at 60. That spends your spare mana on the bigger bolt without ever standing around empty.

**Packs.** On two or more targets drop Magma Totem in the pack (40 seconds with Trial by Fire), and cast Chain Lightning and Fire Nova each on its 6 second cooldown while you stand close enough to place the totem, with Lightning Bolt between. Under Lightning Mastery Chain Lightning has no cooldown at all; save Mastery for the pack if the pull is worth it.

**Moving.** Every shock and every totem is instant. If you have to run, refresh Flame Shock, drop a totem or use Earth Shock as an interrupt, and start casting again the moment you stop.

## Stat priority

Hit matters up to the cap and adds nothing past it: a bolt that misses does nothing. Spell hit caps at 16% against a raid boss. Tidal Focus gives you 5% and a Dwarf's Artisan's Precision 1%, and your gear has to find the rest. Spell power adds to every damage spell you cast. Nature power adds to Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning and Earth Shock, which is nearly everything you cast, but Flame Shock and Searing Totem are fire, and it does nothing for them, where plain spell power covers both. Crit feeds Elemental Fury's doubled bonus, on top of the 5% Call of Thunder already gives your bolts. Intellect is your mana pool and your spell crit at once, and Ancestral Knowledge makes every point 10% bigger. The table above gives the order for your level and gear. Every caster stat lives on one hit and one crit rating in Forever, so a piece with hit or crit and no spell power still works on your spells.

## Best in slot

Your raid set leans on crafts. Frozen Heart of the Mountain and Totem of Thunder are Enchanting crafts that bind on pickup, so you make those yourself; every other craft in it binds when equipped, so you can buy it. The full list, with alternatives for every slot, is on the [Elemental BiS page](/bis/shaman/elemental).

## Enchants and consumables

Your weapon imbues only proc on melee swings, so an Elemental's weapon carries Brilliant Wizard Oil instead. Flask of Supreme Power and Greater Arcane Elixir go on together; a flask stacks with your elixirs. Drink your Major Mana Potion and use your Demonic Rune as soon as each fits under your mana cap. They are on separate cooldowns, and using them early is what lets you use each one twice in a long fight. Demonic Rune costs you health, so never use it while you are taking damage.

## Races

The Dwarf shaman is new in Forever and is the only Alliance shaman. Artisan's Precision's 1% hit is the racial that matters for Elemental, since every point of hit counts until your gear reaches the cap. Stoneform's physical damage cut is a nice extra on trash and in PvP.

On the Horde, Orc is the damage pick: Blood Fury raises your spell power by 10% for 15 seconds every 2 minutes. Press it with Lightning Mastery on the pull and on its own cooldown after that. Troll's Berserking, 10% casting speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes, goes outside Lightning Mastery: inside it your bolts are already a 1.5 second cast, and casting speed never shortens the 1.5 second global cooldown. Windshaper Skyborne, the paid Heroic Pack race, brings Wind Blessed's 2% haste, always on. Tauren's racials do nothing for your damage.

## Professions

Enchanting is the profession for an Elemental. Frozen Heart of the Mountain and Totem of Thunder are both Enchanting crafts and both bind on pickup, so nobody can make them for you. Every other craft in your set binds when equipped and can be bought, so keep your second profession for Alchemy or gathering.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Enhancement levels faster: it fights in melee, lives through bad pulls and spends less time drinking. If you level as Elemental anyway, pull with Lightning Bolt, add Earth Shock from 4 and finish in melee to save mana; a shaman has no wand. Do the Call of Earth class quest at 4 and Call of Fire at 10: they give the Earth Totem every Earth totem needs and the Fire Totem every Fire totem needs, carried in your bags, and without them those totems do not cast. Searing Totem goes down on every pull from 10. Flame Shock also comes at 10, and Fire Nova at 12 bursts around that Searing Totem when two mobs reach you. Your first talent points go to Convection from 10 to 14, 2% off the mana cost of your shocks and Lightning Bolt per point, then Concussion from 15 to 19, 1% more Lightning Bolt and Earth Shock damage per point. Wear leather or cloth with Intellect, take quest rewards over vendor gear, and replace your starter weapon with the first quest staff or mace that carries spell damage.

### Levels 20 to 60

From 20, Lightning Bolt and Flame Shock carry you the whole way. On packs keep dropping Searing Totem for Fire Nova until Magma Totem takes its place at 26, Chain Lightning from 32 handles pairs, and Lightning Mastery at 40 is a 15 second burst for the pull that goes wrong. Cast the top rank of Lightning Bolt when you drink after every pull. On a long fight where your mana, not the mob, runs out first, drop well down the ranks: rank 3 from 20 to 27, while each new rank still costs more than it is worth, back to the top rank at 28 and 29, then rank 3 again from 30 to 33, rank 4 until 38, rank 5 until 44, rank 6 until 50, rank 7 until 56 and rank 8 from 57. Each lower rank costs far less than your best bolt and still keeps most of your spell damage, so it does more in total before you run dry. A rank further down loses more of your gear's bonus with every level. Learn Ghost Wolf at 20: its 3 second cast is slow, but the 40% speed is how a shaman travels until a mount. Shamans wear leather until 40 and mail from 40, and Chain Heal comes at 40 as well.
