## Overview

### Strengths

- Call of Thunder and Elemental Fury make your Lightning Bolts crit often and crit for double
- Forever's Lightning Bolt is a 2.0 second cast with Elemental Alacrity, 1.5 under Lightning Mastery
- Chain Lightning and Magma Totem handle packs without a separate setup
- Shocks and totems are instant, so you keep doing damage on the move

### Weaknesses

- Mana decides which Lightning Bolt rank you can afford
- Searing Totem only reaches 20 yards, so you stand closer than other casters
- The raid build needs Restoration points for spell hit

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged spell damage
- **Brings:** A second set of totems for your party, and a Mana Spring for the casters in it

## Talents and builds

The leveling build spends every point in Elemental until Lightning Mastery at 40: Call of Thunder at 30 and Lightning Overload at 31 to 33 come before Trial by Fire's second point at 34. The next five go to Tidal Focus for 5% spell hit, then Thundering Strikes, 5% crit on every spell. The rest go to Restoration: all three points of Mindfulness, so half your mana regeneration keeps running while you cast, then Totemic Focus for cheaper totems and Improved Reincarnation (2 points) for 4% more maximum health, a 40 minute Reincarnation cooldown and more health and mana when you come back. Tidal Focus comes ahead of Improved Healing Wave because a Lightning Bolt that misses throws away its mana and its cast time. The raid build keeps the same 31 Elemental points, Thundering Strikes and Tidal Focus, and trades Totemic Focus for Ancestral Knowledge (10% more Intellect).

Lightning Mastery is the 31st point, and Call of Thunder above it is the point that opens it.

- **Convection** (5 points): 10% cheaper shocks, Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning. This is your mana talent.
- **Concussion** (5 points): more damage on Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning and Earth Shock, which is most of what you cast.
- **Elemental Alacrity** (3 points): half a second off Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning. Both go from 2.5 to 2.0 seconds.
- **Call of Flame** (3 points): Flame Shock lasts 21 seconds instead of 12, and Flame Shock, Fire Nova and your fire totems hit 15% harder. One shock now covers three and a half shock cooldowns.
- **Elemental Focus** (1 point): any Fire, Frost or Nature damage spell has a 10% chance to make your next damage spell free.
- **Call of Thunder** (1 point): 5% more crit on Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning, and the way to Lightning Mastery.
- **Lightning Overload** (3 points): a 30% chance for Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning to fire a free copy of the spell at half damage, with no threat.
- **Elemental Fury** (5 points): doubles the crit bonus of your spells and your Searing and Magma Totems.
- **Lightning Mastery** (1 point): 15 seconds of faster Lightning Bolts and Chain Lightning with no cooldown, every 3 minutes.

Four more Elemental points are the toll to reach Elemental Fury, and both builds spend them the same way: two in Trial by Fire and two in Elemental Warding. Trial by Fire makes Fire Nova hit 20% harder on a 6 second cooldown and keeps Magma Totem burning for 40 seconds, so Fire Nova and Magma Totem are your damage on dungeon packs. Elemental Warding cuts the Fire, Frost and Nature damage you take by 7%. Elemental Devastation is melee crit, and you never swing a weapon, so skip it.

## Rotation and priority

Drop Mana Spring Totem a few seconds before the pull, then time a Lightning Bolt so it lands just after the tank's first hit; on a pull you do not trust, wait for the tank instead.

**Flame Shock stays up.** Forever's Flame Shock hits hard up front and ticks hard after. Reapply it the moment it falls off, ahead of everything but your cooldowns. Earth Shock stays on your bar for interrupts. It costs about twice a Lightning Bolt's mana and shares the shock cooldown with Flame Shock, so never let it delay a Flame Shock.

**Cooldowns.** Lightning Mastery goes on the pull with your trinkets and Blood Fury, and again every 3 minutes. Blood Fury comes back sooner, so use it whenever it is ready.

**Totems.** Drop Searing Totem within 20 yards of the boss and stand there; refresh it when it expires, every 55 seconds. Strength of Earth, Grace of Air and Windfury reach the whole raid within 30 yards in Forever, so leave the Earth and Air slots to whichever shaman your raid leader assigns them to.

**Fire Nova** is a pack spell for you. Let a Clearcasting proc go to your next Lightning Bolt instead.

**Mana.** In a raid, cast Lightning Bolt rank 10 the whole fight. Without raid buffs, cast rank 10 while your mana bar is fuller than the share of the fight left (above half mana at the halfway point, for example), and drop to rank 8 the moment it is not. Rank 8 is the cheapest Lightning Bolt that still takes all of your spell damage at 60.

**Packs.** Drop Magma Totem in the pack and stand close enough to place it, then cast Chain Lightning and Fire Nova each on its 6 second cooldown, with Lightning Bolt between. Lightning Mastery takes Chain Lightning's cooldown away, so save it for the pack if the pull is worth it.

**Moving.** Every shock and every totem is instant. On the run, refresh Flame Shock, drop a totem or interrupt with Earth Shock, and start casting again the moment you stop.

## Stat priority

Spell hit caps at 16% against a raid boss. Tidal Focus gives you 5% and a Dwarf's Artisan's Precision 1%, and your gear has to find the rest. Nature power adds to Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning and Earth Shock, but Flame Shock and Searing Totem are fire, and it does nothing for them, where plain spell power covers both. Crit feeds Elemental Fury's doubled bonus, on top of the 5% Call of Thunder already gives your bolts. Ancestral Knowledge makes every point of Intellect 10% bigger. Every caster stat lives on one hit and one crit rating in Forever, so a piece with hit or crit and no spell power still works on your spells.

## Best in slot

Your raid set leans on crafts. Frozen Heart of the Mountain and Totem of Thunder are Enchanting crafts that bind on pickup, so you make those yourself; every other craft in it binds when equipped, so you can buy it.

## Enchants and consumables

Your weapon imbues only proc on melee swings, so an Elemental's weapon carries Brilliant Wizard Oil instead. Flask of Supreme Power and Greater Arcane Elixir go on together; a flask stacks with your elixirs. Drink your Major Mana Potion and use your Demonic Rune as soon as each fits under your mana cap. They are on separate cooldowns, and using them early is what lets you use each one twice in a long fight. Demonic Rune costs you health, so never use it while you are taking damage.

## Races

The Dwarf shaman is new in Forever and is the only Alliance shaman. Artisan's Precision's 1% hit counts until your gear reaches the cap, and Stoneform is a nice extra on trash and in PvP.

On the Horde, take Orc for Blood Fury's spell power: press it with Lightning Mastery on the pull and on its own cooldown after that. Troll's Berserking goes outside Lightning Mastery, since casting speed never shortens the 1.5 second global cooldown. Windshaper Skyborne brings Wind Blessed's 2% haste, always on. Tauren's racials do nothing for your damage.

## Professions

Take Enchanting for Frozen Heart of the Mountain and Totem of Thunder, which nobody can make for you. Keep your second profession for Alchemy or gathering.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Enhancement levels faster: it fights in melee, lives through bad pulls and spends less time drinking. If you level as Elemental anyway, pull with Lightning Bolt, add Earth Shock from 4 and finish in melee to save mana; a shaman has no wand.

Do the Call of Earth class quest at 4 and Call of Fire at 10: without the Earth Totem and Fire Totem they give, carried in your bags, those totems do not cast. Searing Totem goes down on every pull from 10, and Fire Nova at 12 bursts around it when two mobs reach you. Flame Shock also comes at 10; when rank 2 arrives at 18, a dwarf keeps rank 1 through 19, while every Horde shaman takes the new rank.

- **10 to 14:** Convection, 2% off the mana cost of your shocks and Lightning Bolt per point.
- **15 to 17:** Elemental Alacrity, Lightning Bolt a sixth of a second faster per point.
- **From 18:** Concussion, 1% more Lightning Bolt and Earth Shock damage per point.

Wear leather or cloth with Intellect, take quest rewards over vendor gear, and replace your starter weapon with the first quest staff or mace that carries spell damage.

### Levels 20 to 60

From 20, Lightning Bolt and Flame Shock carry you the whole way. On packs keep dropping Searing Totem for Fire Nova until Magma Totem takes its place at 26, Chain Lightning from 32 handles pairs, and Lightning Mastery at 40 is a 15 second burst for the pull that goes wrong. Learn Ghost Wolf at 20: its 3 second cast is slow, but the 40% speed is how a shaman travels until a mount. You wear leather until 40 and mail from 40, when Chain Heal comes as well.

Cast the top rank of Lightning Bolt when you drink after every pull. On a long fight where your mana, not the mob, runs out first, a lower rank costs far less, keeps most of your spell damage and does more in total before you run dry. A rank further down loses more of your gear's bonus with every level, so step down to:

- **20 to 33:** rank 3, while each new rank still costs more than it is worth.
- **34 to 38:** rank 4, and at 39 too for a Windshaper Skyborne.
- **39 to 44:** rank 5, from 40 for a Windshaper Skyborne.
- **45 to 50:** rank 6.
- **51 to 56:** rank 7.
- **From 57:** rank 8.
