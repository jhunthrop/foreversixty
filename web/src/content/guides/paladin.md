Blizzard has confirmed an Undead Paladin race and class combination for Forever, shown in an official cinematic.

Beyond that, one press roundup reports that Paladin is getting a Seal usable for tanking, deeper group healing, and a fuller Retribution talent tree. This has not been confirmed directly by Blizzard and is treated as single-source. The same roundup reports reworked racials, including an Undead spell-drain racial, also single-source.

This site's leveling routes, talent builds, and gear priorities for Paladin will be written after we play the class in beta. See the [roadmap](/roadmap) for beta dates.
