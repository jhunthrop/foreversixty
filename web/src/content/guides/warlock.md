## New race pairs

**Troll Warlock** is confirmed by Blizzard as one of six new combinations. Warlock is not available to Skyborne.

## Talent structure

Talent trees keep the familiar shape: seven rows per tree and 51 points to spend. The one-point "gold medal" talents at 11, 21, and 31 points are still there, and Forever adds a fourth at the 16-point mark, so every tree has four of them. Principal Designer Kris Zierhut confirmed the 16-point addition in an interview.

Talents that existed mainly to unlock a group buff are becoming baseline abilities instead, which frees room in the trees for talents you notice. Some talents are unchanged from 1.12.

One outlet's tally from the demo trees counts 23 new, 26 changed, and only 3 unchanged Warlock talents. That is the largest rework of any class by a wide margin.

## What changed in the demo

**Curses and Banes are now separate categories**, which means you can run one Curse and one Bane on the same target. Curse of Agony is renamed **Bane of Agony** and Curse of Doom is **Bane of Doom**.

**Renames and new summons**, read off level-38 demo spellbooks:

- **Subjugate Demon** is Enslave Demon renamed, reaching rank 1 by level 38.
- **Incubus** is a new summon, the male counterpart to the Succubus with its own spell rather than a cosmetic option. Four Demonology talents name it.

**New talents:** Pandemic grants a large critical strike damage bonus to Corruption, the Banes, Drain Soul, Drain Life, Siphon Life, and a new Drain Hope. Drain Hope is a channelled Shadow spell that also increases your other Shadow damage-over-time effects on the target. Malevolence lets damage-over-time effects critically strike at all, which they could not in Classic.

Warlock spellbooks were also read at the Goldshire trainer on stream, so the level each rank is trained at is known for Warlock ahead of the other eight classes.

## Where these come from, and what they are worth

Except where a line is credited to Blizzard, everything in the section above was read off BlizzCon demo footage and show-floor screenshots by fan sites, mostly at level 38, which was the demo cap. Numbers can be wrong, ranks can be misread, and anything can change before launch. Nothing here is datamined.

Associate Production Director Clayton Stone has also said that Forever's launch is a deliberately conservative take after Season of Discovery, and that the team is starting by filling out skills classes did not have rather than reinventing them.
