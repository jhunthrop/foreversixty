## Overview

### Strengths

- Two weapons and Flurry mean a constant stream of swings and rage
- Bloodthirst and Whirlwind are two strong buttons on short cooldowns
- Death Wish and Recklessness stack into a huge execute phase
- Whirlwind hits up to four targets with both weapons on trash

### Weaknesses

- Needs two good weapons, so it gears up slower than Arms
- Death Wish raises the damage you take by 5%, which hurts on cleave bosses
- No healing cut or debuff of its own for the raid

### In a raid

- **Role:** Dual-wield melee damage
- **Brings:** Battle Shout, which reaches every party and raid member within 20 yards

## Talents and builds

- **Flurry** (3 points): 30% more attack speed for your next three white swings after any melee crit. A Heroic Strike takes the place of a main-hand swing and comes out at the faster speed without using one up. With two weapons and Cruelty it is up most of the fight, and every extra swing is extra rage.
- **Bloodthirst** (1 point): an instant hit that scales with 45% of your attack power, every 6 seconds. Outside the execute phase, it is the button you never delay.
- **Death Wish** (1 point): 20% more physical damage and Fear immunity for 30 seconds, at the cost of 5% more damage taken.
- **Dual Wield Specialization** (5 points): 25% more off-hand damage and 50% more rage from off-hand swings. The core talent of a two-weapon build.
- **Cruelty** and **Unbridled Wrath** (10 points): 5% crit, and a 60% chance at an extra rage point on every white swing that lands, from either hand. A Heroic Strike takes the place of a main-hand swing and never procs it. These are your first points: Cruelty from 10 to 14, then Unbridled Wrath from 15 to 19.
- **Furious Precision** (3 points): 10% more hit with the off hand, which cuts the off-hand misses that cost you rage.
- **Improved Execute** (2 points): Execute costs 10 rage instead of 15, so the rage you hold goes into damage.
- **Improved Whirlwind** (2 points): Whirlwind comes back every 8 seconds instead of 10 and hits the closest enemy 30% harder. You press Whirlwind every time it comes off cooldown, and on a single boss the closest enemy is the boss.
- **Reckless Swings** (1 point): a button on a 15 second cooldown and a 1 second global that makes your next four white swings within 8 seconds bring 5 more rage each. It costs a quarter of your base health, which the healers cover without noticing, and 20 rage every 15 seconds is a Heroic Strike you would not otherwise have.
- **Enrage** (2 points): a 30% chance at 4% more physical damage for 12 seconds after you get hit. Any damaging attack rolls it, so cleaves, raid-wide damage and every dungeon pull keep it coming up.
- **Improved Heroic Strike** (3 points): Heroic Strike costs 3 less. It is your rage dump, and you press it a lot.
- **Deep Wounds** and **Impale** (8 points in Arms, with **Improved Rend** as the toll to reach Deep Wounds; Fury never casts Rend): every crit leaves a bleed for 60% of your weapon's average damage, and your ability crits hit for 220% of a normal hit instead of double. Fury crits a lot, so this is the best use of the Arms points.
- **Improved Bloodrage** (2 points in Protection): half again as much rage from every Bloodrage.
- **Anger Management** (1 point, behind 3 in **Improved Tactical Mastery**): a point of rage every 3 seconds in combat, 10 rage every half minute. Improved Tactical Mastery is the way in. In a raid you never leave Berserker Stance, but on trash it lets a Charge's rage come with you when you swap back.

The build also puts two points in **Improved Berserker Rage**, so every Berserker Rage brings 10 rage and breaks any slow on you, and two in **Improved Charge**, part of the points the rows above Deep Wounds ask for; the 6 extra rage on every Charge on trash, in dungeons and out in the world comes free. It skips Deflection and Improved Intercept: you walk into a raid boss instead of Intercepting it, and a boss you stand behind never gives you a parry.

## Rotation and priority

**The pull.** Bloodrage a few seconds before the tank pulls, then Battle Shout with that rage. If a raid warrior's shout is already on you, skip yours and keep the rage for Bloodthirst; solo and in a small group, always shout. Stay in Berserker Stance and walk in: the early Bloodrage has already put you in combat, so there is no Charge to take, and with Improved Bloodrage it brings more rage than a Charge would. On trash and while leveling, Charge or Intercept in freely.

**Rage.** Never hold Reckless Swings; the health it costs is the healers' to cover. Below 60 rage, hold it for Bloodthirst and Whirlwind instead of queueing Heroic Strike. With a shaman's Windfury Totem on you, Hamstring right after Reckless Swings while the target is above 25%: ten rage for a main-hand hit that can trigger the totem's strike. From 25% down that rage is your first Execute. Without Windfury, never Hamstring for damage.

**Fears.** Berserker Rage breaks fear and keeps you immune to it for 10 seconds, which is why it waits on Magmadar, Onyxia and Nefarian for Panic, Bellowing Roar and the fear. Death Wish can be pressed while you are feared too: it breaks the fear and keeps you immune for its 30 seconds, so if Berserker Rage is down and you have the 10 rage, it is your second fear break.

**Cooldowns.** Recklessness goes with the execute-phase Death Wish. Trinkets, Blood Fury, Berserking and Eureka! go on cooldown from the pull; when one comes up close to Death Wish, wait the few seconds and press them together. Elune's Light the same, but never inside Recklessness.

**Two or more targets.** Swap Heroic Strike for Cleave with two or more mobs in front of you.

**Moving.** Intercept closes the gap in Berserker Stance. Bloodthirst first when you land.

## Stat priority

Hit matters up to the hit cap, and dual wielding has two caps: your specials reach theirs long before your white swings do, and past the special cap every point still trims your white misses, which are rage. Crit feeds Flurry, Deep Wounds and Impale, and a critical swing brings twice the rage. Weapon skill from gear, Edgemaster's Handguards and the like, is worth chasing now that no race brings it: it takes the sting out of glancing blows and trims your misses.

## Best in slot

Both hands matter. The main hand carries Heroic Strike and the off hand swings all fight behind Flurry. Your rage comes from how often you land, not how hard you hit, so off-hand hit is rage and off-hand damage is damage.

## Enchants and consumables

Mighty Rage Potion goes on the pull and again when it comes back. Enchant and sharpen both weapons.

## Races

Night Elf is the Alliance pick and Undead the Horde pick among the Classic races. Flurry turns Elune's Light's crit into extra swings: press it on the pull and every time it comes back, with Death Wish when they line up, and never inside Recklessness, which already makes every hit crit. On the Alliance, Skyborne is level with Night Elf; on the Horde, Skyborne and Undead are level. With two weapons hitting, Touch of the Grave comes back almost as soon as it can.

The gap between races is small. On the Alliance, Human and Gnome are next, and Dwarf comes in behind them. On the Horde, Orc is next. Troll's Berserking only covers 15 seconds in every 3 minutes, so it trails Undead and Orc, and Tauren brings nothing for damage and comes last. Blood Fury and Berserking also stack with Recklessness; a crit racial does not.

## Professions

Enchanting changes your gear: Frozen Heart of the Mountain, and on the Alliance Molten Heart of the Mountain beside it, are bind-on-pickup trinkets only an enchanter can make. Blacksmithing makes melee weapons and armor, but the ones on your list bind when equipped, so buy them rather than train it. Engineering brings Goblin Sapper Charges and Dense Dynamite for every trash pack, and grenades in PvP.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Charge and Rend train at 4, Thunder Clap at 6, Hamstring at 8 and Overpower at 12. Rend's bleed ignores armor and grows with your attack power, so put it on anything that will live through it. Tactical Mastery at 14 lets you keep 10 Rage through a stance swap.

At 10, Bloodrage trains, and your race's warrior quest teaches Defensive Stance, Sunder Armor and Taunt; its follow-up rewards a one-hander worth keeping for when you dual wield.

- **10 to 14:** Cruelty, 5% melee crit.
- **15 to 19:** Unbridled Wrath, a 60% chance at an extra Rage on your white swings.
- **20:** Furious Precision starts, for off-hand hit, at the level Dual Wield trains.

Until then, swap the starter weapon for the first quest or dungeon two-hander you can get, and take quest rewards over vendor gear. You wear mail until plate at 40.

### Levels 20 to 60

Fury levels fastest once Bloodthirst and Whirlwind are in, but it needs two good one-handers to get there. Charge every pull, Bloodrage on cooldown, and Victory Rush whenever it is up: it heals 10%, works for 20 seconds after a kill and comes back every 30 seconds, so when the next mob is close, press it right after the Charge.

Until Whirlwind arrives at 36 you fight in Battle Stance, so press Overpower whenever it lights. Berserker Stance and Berserker Rage train at 30, but Berserker Rage only works in that stance, so leave it on the bar until 36: from there Whirlwind makes Berserker Stance your home, Berserker Rage joins the rotation, and pulls of two or three go quick.

- **From 35:** Flurry.
- **38 and 39:** Improved Whirlwind, for the Whirlwind you train at 36.
- **40:** Bloodthirst.
- **41:** the second point of Improved Berserker Rage, once Berserker Rage is in the rotation.

In Arms, take Anger Management and Deep Wounds before the second point of Improved Charge, which only pays the row for Impale.
