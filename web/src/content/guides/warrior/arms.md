## Overview

Arms is the two-handed warrior: Rend, Overpower and Mortal Strike in Battle Stance, a stance swap for Recklessness, and the biggest Executes in the game.

### Strengths

- Mortal Strike is a big hit every 6 seconds that halves the healing on the target
- Bloodthrill turns plain swings into extra Overpowers on a bleeding target
- Rage held going into the execute phase becomes one huge Execute
- Sweeping Strikes copies five hits onto a second target and Whirlwind hits up to four, so trash packs go down fast

### Weaknesses

- Rage starved without a raid around you, so every point has a job
- Lives in Battle Stance, so Whirlwind and Recklessness cost a stance swap
- One slow two-hander means fewer swings and lumpier rage than a dual-wielder

### In a raid

- **Role:** Two-handed melee damage
- **Brings:** Mortal Strike's 50% healing cut for the bosses that heal, and Battle Shout, which reaches every party and raid member within 20 yards

## Talents and builds

- **Bloodthrill** (2 points): while your Rend is on the target, every main-hand hit has a 20% chance to open Overpower. This is the talent the whole spec is built around, so Rend is never optional.
- **Improved Overpower** (2 points): 50% more crit chance on Overpower. With Bloodthrill feeding you Overpowers, this is where your crits come from.
- **Weaponmaster** (5 points): a bonus that depends on your two-hander. Swords give a 5% chance at an extra attack, axes and polearms 5% crit, maces and staves ignore 15% of armor. Take all five whatever you swing.
- **Improved Rend** and **Deep Wounds** (3 points each): Rend hits 35% harder and every crit adds a bleed for 60% of your weapon's average damage. Deep Wounds needs the Improved Rend points anyway.
- **Impale** (2 points): your ability crits hit for 220% of a normal hit instead of double. Overpower crits most of the time, so this pays every few seconds.
- **Two-Handed Weapon Specialization** (3 points): 6% more damage with the weapon you always carry.
- **Improved Tactical Mastery** (3 points): you keep 25 rage through a stance change instead of 10. This is what makes the swap to Berserker Stance for Recklessness and Whirlwind free.
- **Anger Management** (1 point) and **Improved Charge** (2 points, level 60 build only): a point of rage every 3 seconds in combat, and 21 rage from Charge instead of 15. Rage is the limit for Arms, and both pay from the first second of the pull; the raid build skips Improved Charge, since you charge once per boss.
- **Sweeping Strikes** and **Mortal Strike** (1 point each): Sweeping Strikes is the stepping stone to Mortal Strike and your two-target button. Mortal Strike is the spec.
- **Improved Slam** (2 points): Slam's cast and global drop to one second, your swing timer keeps running through it, and its cooldown falls from 15 seconds to 10. That puts a Slam between your Mortal Strikes without costing you a swing.
- **Improved Bloodrage** (Protection): two points in the level 60 build for half again as much rage from every Bloodrage, one point in the raid build for a quarter more.
- **Cruelty** and **Unbridled Wrath** (10 points in Fury): 5% crit and a 60% chance at an extra rage point on every white swing that lands. Heroic Strike and Cleave take the place of a swing and never proc it.

The level 60 build puts one point in **Improved Heroic Strike**, three in **Deflection** and five in **Iron Will**, which takes 15% off every stun and fear on you.

The raid build drops Iron Will, Deflection and Improved Charge. Two more points of **Improved Heroic Strike** make Heroic Strike, your rage dump, cost 3 less. **Spearing Strike** is a second weapon strike, 15 rage on a 20 second cooldown in Battle Stance, that hits twice as hard on dragons and giants. Fifteen points in Fury reach **Reckless Swings**: for 8 seconds your white swings bring 5 more rage each, which is two or three swings with a slow two-hander, at a quarter of your base health. The same points reach **Improved Execute**, which takes Execute down to 10 rage. **Enrage** is the way there, and it pays whenever raid damage lands on you. One point of **Improved Bloodrage** stays.

## Rotation and priority

**The pull.** Charge on the pull. In a raid that means once the tank has engaged the boss, never before. Out in the world, Charge is how you pull. Charge only works out of combat, and Bloodrage puts you in combat, so a Bloodrage before the pull costs you the Charge. Spend the Charge's rage on Battle Shout as you arrive, unless a raid warrior's shout is already on you; then press Bloodrage as your first swing lands. That is a shout, a Rend and a Mortal Strike in your first three globals, or Rend and Mortal Strike in the first two when another warrior's shout is already on you.

**Battle Stance.** Keep Rend ticking at all times: Bloodthrill only lights Overpower on a bleeding target. Press Overpower the moment it lights up, even ahead of Mortal Strike: it is only usable for a few seconds after a dodge or a Bloodthrill proc, and Mortal Strike will still be there a second later. Mortal Strike goes on cooldown otherwise, and Reckless Swings right behind it in the raid build: for 8 seconds your white swings bring 5 more rage each, which is two or three swings with a slow two-hander. It costs a quarter of your base health, so tell your healers. With Improved Slam, Slam fills the gap between Mortal Strikes, and Spearing Strike takes any global that is still free; it only works in Battle Stance, so it stops at the execute swap.

**Rage.** Bloodrage on cooldown whenever you are under 80 rage. Heroic Strike only when you are about to cap at 90 or more: below that, keep the rage for Mortal Strike, Overpower and Execute, which lose damage every time they wait for it. If a shaman drops Windfury Totem in your group, Hamstring on any free global: it is a cheap main-hand hit that can trigger the totem's extra attack. Without Windfury, never Hamstring for damage.

**Execute.** From 25% stop Hamstring and let the rage build. At 20%, Execute in Battle Stance first: it spends your whole bar, so that first Execute is your biggest hit of the fight. Then swap to Berserker Stance (Improved Tactical Mastery keeps 25 rage) and press Recklessness. From there, Mortal Strike on cooldown and Execute on every other global until the boss dies; in a dungeon without raid buffs, Execute goes first. Overpower and Rend do not work in Berserker Stance, so stay put.

**Two or more targets.** Sweeping Strikes in Battle Stance, swap to Berserker Stance, Whirlwind. Whirlwind hits up to four targets and every Sweeping Strikes charge copies a hit onto a second one. Swap back for Overpower and Rend once Whirlwind is on cooldown. Use Cleave instead of Heroic Strike when two mobs are in front of you.

**Moving.** Rend first on anything new, because it is what lights your Overpowers. Charge is out of combat only, so on a boss that moves you walk back in; that is a lost swing, not a reason to leave Battle Stance.

## Stat priority

The table above gives the order for your gear; this is why each stat counts. Every point of Strength is two attack power for a warrior. Crit feeds Deep Wounds, Impale and Overpower, and a critical swing brings twice the rage. Agility carries crit and armor with it. Hit matters up to the hit cap, because a missed Mortal Strike or Overpower is a lost global and lost rage; once your gear reaches the cap it adds nothing. Haste adds swings, and swings are rage. Weapon skill takes the sting out of glancing blows and trims your misses, and no race brings it any more. For a two-hander it only comes on the weapon itself, like Obsidian Edged Blade's two-handed sword skill; Edgemaster's Handguards and the other skill gear raise one-handed skills and do nothing for you.

## Enchants and consumables

Mighty Rage Potion goes on the pull and again when it comes back. Pre-pull, take Elixir of the Mongoose, R.O.I.D.S., Winterfall Firewater and Smoked Desert Dumplings: crit, Strength and attack power all stack with the raid's buffs.

## Races

Night Elf is the Alliance pick and Undead the Horde pick among the races every account has. Elune's Light is 10% crit for 15 seconds every 3 minutes: press it on the pull and every time it comes back, but never inside Recklessness, which already makes every hit crit. Undead's Touch of the Grave drains the target for damage and a little health, at most once every 15 seconds. Skyborne is level with Night Elf on the Alliance and edges Undead by a hair on the Horde, since Wind Blessed is 2% haste that never turns off, but it needs the paid Skyborne Heroic Pack, so take it only if you already own it.

If you want a different look, the gap is small. On the Alliance, Dwarf comes out level with Night Elf, and Human's Ruthless, a flat 1% crit, and Gnome's Eureka!, 10% more damage and 10% less rage on your non-periodic damaging abilities for 8 seconds every 2 minutes, sit just behind them. On the Horde, Orc and Undead are close: Orc's Blood Fury is 10% attack power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes, so press it on the pull and every time it comes back, and it still adds damage inside Recklessness. Troll's Berserking only covers 15 seconds in every 3 minutes, so it trails Undead and Orc; Tauren brings nothing for damage and comes last.

## Professions

Enchanting is the one that changes your gear: Frozen Heart of the Mountain is a bind-on-pickup trinket only an enchanter can make. Blacksmithing and Mining are the other pair, but Arcanite Champion binds when equipped, so buy one rather than train it for the sword. Engineering brings Goblin Sapper Charges and Dense Dynamite for every trash pack, and grenades in PvP.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Charge and Rend train at 4, Thunder Clap at 6, Hamstring at 8 and Overpower at 12. Rend's bleed ignores armor, so put it on anything that will live through it. Tactical Mastery at 14 lets you keep 10 Rage through a stance swap.

At 10, Bloodrage trains, and your race's warrior quest teaches Defensive Stance, Sunder Armor and Taunt; its follow-up rewards a one-hander, a good pick if your two-hander is still the starter.

Your first point at 10 makes Heroic Strike cost 1 less Rage. Three points of Deflection follow for parry, then Improved Rend from 14 to 16 for a much bigger bleed, and Improved Charge at 17 and 18 for 6 more Rage on every opener.

Weapon damage is nearly all your damage, so swap the starter weapon for the first quest or dungeon two-hander you can get, and take quest rewards over vendor gear. You wear mail until plate at 40.

### Levels 20 to 60

Level as Arms from the start: Rend, Charge and Overpower carry you to 40, and Mortal Strike makes everything after that quick. Charge every pull, and Victory Rush whenever it is up after a kill: it heals 10% and comes back every 30 seconds, so open the next pull with it if the last one died fast. Sweeping Strikes into Whirlwind once you have both for pulls of two or three. Improved Overpower comes in at 22 and Bloodthrill at 33, after Deep Wounds and Impale; once both are in, keep Rend on everything and every pull turns into Overpower spam. Fury levels at least as fast with two good one-handers; Arms only needs one weapon, which makes it easier to keep up to date.
