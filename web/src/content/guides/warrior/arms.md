## Overview

### Strengths

- Mortal Strike is a big hit every 6 seconds that halves the healing on the target
- Bloodthrill turns plain swings into extra Overpowers on a bleeding target
- Rage held going into the execute phase becomes one huge Execute
- Sweeping Strikes copies five hits onto a second target and Whirlwind hits up to four, so trash packs go down fast

### Weaknesses

- Rage starved without a raid around you, so every point has a job
- Lives in Battle Stance, so Whirlwind and Recklessness cost a stance swap
- One slow two-hander means fewer swings and lumpier rage than a dual-wielder

### In a raid

- **Role:** Two-handed melee damage
- **Brings:** Mortal Strike's 50% healing cut for the bosses that heal, and Battle Shout, which reaches every party and raid member within 20 yards

## Talents and builds

- **Bloodthrill** (2 points): while your Rend is on the target, every main-hand hit has a 20% chance to open Overpower. This is the talent the whole spec is built around, so Rend is never optional.
- **Improved Overpower** (2 points): 50% more crit chance on Overpower. With Bloodthrill feeding you Overpowers, this is where your crits come from.
- **Weaponmaster** (5 points): a bonus that depends on your two-hander. Swords give a 5% chance at an extra attack, axes and polearms 5% crit, maces and staves ignore 15% of armor. Take all five whatever you swing.
- **Improved Rend** and **Deep Wounds** (3 points each): Rend hits 35% harder and every crit adds a bleed for 60% of your weapon's average damage. Deep Wounds needs the Improved Rend points anyway.
- **Impale** (2 points): your ability crits hit for 220% of a normal hit instead of double. Overpower crits most of the time, so this pays every few seconds.
- **Two-Handed Weapon Specialization** (3 points): 6% more damage with the weapon you always carry.
- **Improved Tactical Mastery** (3 points): you keep 25 rage through a stance change instead of 10. This is what makes the swap to Berserker Stance for Recklessness and Whirlwind free.
- **Anger Management** (1 point) and **Improved Charge** (2 points, level 60 build only): a point of rage every 3 seconds in combat, and 21 rage from Charge instead of 15. Rage is the limit for Arms, and both pay from the first second of the pull; the raid build skips Improved Charge, since you charge once per boss.
- **Sweeping Strikes** and **Mortal Strike** (1 point each): Sweeping Strikes is the stepping stone to Mortal Strike and your two-target button. Mortal Strike is the spec.
- **Improved Slam** (2 points): Slam's cast and global drop to one second, your swing timer keeps running through it, and its cooldown falls from 15 seconds to 10. That puts a Slam between your Mortal Strikes without costing you a swing.
- **Improved Bloodrage** (Protection): two points in the level 60 build for half again as much rage from every Bloodrage, one point in the raid build for a quarter more.
- **Cruelty** and **Unbridled Wrath** (10 points in Fury): 5% crit and a 60% chance at an extra rage point on every white swing that lands. Heroic Strike and Cleave take the place of a swing and never proc it.

The level 60 build puts one point in **Improved Heroic Strike**, two in **Deflection** and five in **Iron Will**, which takes 15% off every stun and fear on you. Both builds take **Spearing Strike**, a second weapon strike, 15 rage on a 20 second cooldown in Battle Stance, that hits twice as hard on dragons and giants.

The raid build drops Iron Will, Deflection and Improved Charge. Two more points of **Improved Heroic Strike** make Heroic Strike cost 3 less. Fifteen points in Fury reach **Reckless Swings**: for 8 seconds your white swings bring 5 more rage each, which is two or three swings with a slow two-hander, at a quarter of your base health. The same points reach **Improved Execute**, which takes Execute down to 10 rage. **Enrage** is the way there, and it pays whenever raid damage lands on you.

## Rotation and priority

**The pull.** In a raid, Charge once the tank has engaged the boss, never before; out in the world, Charge is how you pull. Bloodrage puts you in combat and Charge only works out of combat, so a Bloodrage before the pull costs you the Charge. Spend the Charge's rage on Battle Shout as you arrive, unless a raid warrior's shout is already on you; then press Bloodrage as your first swing lands.

**Battle Stance.** Rend never falls off: Bloodthrill only lights Overpower on a bleeding target. Mortal Strike on cooldown, and Reckless Swings right behind it in the raid build; tell your healers about the health it costs. With Improved Slam, Slam fills the gap between Mortal Strikes, and Spearing Strike takes any global that is still free; it only works in Battle Stance, so it stops at the execute swap.

**Rage.** Heroic Strike only when you are about to cap at 90 or more. Hamstring is for a group with a shaman's Windfury Totem; without Windfury, never Hamstring for damage.

**Execute.** From 25%, stop Hamstring and let the rage build. At 20%, Execute in Battle Stance first: it spends your whole bar, so that first Execute is your biggest hit of the fight. Then swap and press Recklessness, and stay in Berserker Stance until the boss dies, Mortal Strike on cooldown and Execute on every other global. In a dungeon without raid buffs, Execute goes first.

**Two or more targets.** Sweeping Strikes in Battle Stance, swap to Berserker Stance, Whirlwind. Swap back for Overpower and Rend once Whirlwind is on cooldown. Cleave instead of Heroic Strike when two mobs are in front of you.

**Moving.** Rend first on anything new. On a boss that moves you walk back in, since Charge is out of combat only; that is a lost swing, not a reason to leave Battle Stance.

## Stat priority

Crit feeds Deep Wounds, Impale and Overpower, and a critical swing brings twice the rage. Hit counts up to the hit cap and adds nothing past it. Weapon skill takes the sting out of glancing blows and trims your misses, and no race brings it any more. For a two-hander it only comes on the weapon itself, like Obsidian Edged Blade's two-handed sword skill; Edgemaster's Handguards and the other skill gear raise one-handed skills and do nothing for you.

## Enchants and consumables

Mighty Rage Potion goes on the pull and again when it comes back. Pre-pull, take Elixir of the Mongoose, R.O.I.D.S., Juju Power, Juju Might and Smoked Desert Dumplings: crit, Strength and attack power all stack with the raid's buffs.

## Races

Night Elf is the Alliance pick and Undead the Horde pick among the Classic races. Skyborne's Wind Blessed is level with Night Elf on the Alliance and as good as Undead on the Horde. Press Elune's Light on the pull and every time it comes back, but never inside Recklessness, which already makes every hit crit.

If you want a different look, the gap is small. On the Alliance, Dwarf comes out level with Night Elf, and Human and Gnome sit just behind them. On the Horde, Orc is close to Undead: press Blood Fury on the pull and every time it comes back, and it still adds damage inside Recklessness. Troll's Berserking trails Undead and Orc; Tauren brings nothing for damage and comes last.

## Professions

Enchanting changes your gear: Frozen Heart of the Mountain is a bind-on-pickup trinket only an enchanter can make. Blacksmithing and Mining are the other pair, but Arcanite Champion binds when equipped, so buy one rather than train it for the sword. Engineering brings Goblin Sapper Charges and Dense Dynamite for every trash pack, and grenades in PvP.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Charge and Rend train at 4, Thunder Clap at 6, Hamstring at 8 and Overpower at 12. Rend's bleed ignores armor, so put it on anything that will live through it. Tactical Mastery at 14 lets you keep 10 Rage through a stance swap.

At 10, Bloodrage trains, and your race's warrior quest teaches Defensive Stance, Sunder Armor and Taunt; its follow-up rewards a one-hander, a good pick if your two-hander is still the starter.

- **10 to 12:** Improved Rend, a much bigger bleed on everything you fight.
- **13:** Improved Heroic Strike, so Heroic Strike costs 1 less Rage.
- **14:** a first point of Deflection to open the next row.
- **15 and 16:** Improved Overpower, so the Overpowers your target's dodges open crit far more often.
- **17:** a second point of Deflection, for parry.
- **18 and 19:** Improved Charge, 6 more Rage on every opener.

Swap the starter weapon for the first quest or dungeon two-hander you can get, and take quest rewards over vendor gear. You wear mail until plate at 40.

### Levels 20 to 60

Level as Arms from the start: Rend, Charge and Overpower carry you to 40, and Mortal Strike makes everything after that quick. Charge every pull, and Victory Rush whenever it is up: it heals 10%, works for 20 seconds after a kill and comes back every 30 seconds, so when the next mob is close, press it right after the Charge. Sweeping Strikes into Whirlwind for pulls of two or three.

From 20, Improved Tactical Mastery, Anger Management and Deep Wounds, then take Spearing Strike at 27 and press it on any free global in Battle Stance. Bloodthrill comes in at 33, after Deep Wounds and Impale; once it is in, keep Rend on everything and every pull turns into Overpower spam. Fury levels at least as fast with two good one-handers; Arms only needs one weapon, which is easier to keep up to date.
