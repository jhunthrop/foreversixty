## Overview

### Strengths

- Revenge is huge threat for almost no rage, and Shield Slam carries a very high amount of threat
- Defensive Stance raises all your threat by 80%, and Defiance stacks another 20% on top with a shield on
- Shield Block is off the global cooldown and up most of the fight, and every block, dodge and parry pays rage back through Shield Specialization and Master of Defense
- Last Stand and Shield Wall give the healers time when a fight goes wrong

### Weaknesses

- Very little damage of its own; solo play is slow
- Several targets at once means tabbing Sunder and Revenge around, not one button
- Rage is thin in the first seconds of a pull, before the boss has hit you

### In a raid

- **Role:** Main tank
- **Brings:** Sunder Armor stacks, Thunder Clap's attack speed slow and Demoralizing Shout

## Talents and builds

- **Shield Slam** (1 point): a shield hit that adds your block value to its damage, with a very high amount of threat. Your biggest button.
- **Bastion** (5 points): 10% more damage while a shield is equipped, which is 10% more threat from everything that hits.
- **Defiance** (3 points): 20% more threat in Defensive Stance while a shield is equipped, on top of the stance's own 80%, so the two together come to about 2.2 times your threat.
- **Shield Specialization** (5 points): 5% more block chance, and every block gives you 5 rage.
- **Master of Defense** (2 points): 5 rage on every dodge and parry with a shield on. With Shield Specialization, a tank that is being hit is never short of rage.
- **Anticipation** (5 points): 20 Defense Skill, so fewer hits land and fewer crit.
- **Improved Revenge**, **Improved Thunder Clap** and **Improved Sunder Armor** (9 points): Revenge hits 60% harder, Thunder Clap costs 6 less and Sunder Armor 3 less. These are the three buttons you press most after Shield Slam.
- **Focused Rage** (3 points): every offensive ability costs 3 less, which makes Revenge nearly free.
- **Last Stand** (1 point): 30% of your maximum health for 20 seconds, lost again when it ends. Your emergency button.
- **Improved Bloodrage** (2 points): half again as much rage from every Bloodrage.
- **Concussion Blow** (1 point): a 5 second stun, and the point that opens Shield Slam.

The Arms points go to **Deflection** for 5% parry, **Improved Heroic Strike** for a cheaper rage dump, and **Improved Rend** into **Deep Wounds**. Improved Tactical Mastery and Anger Management are left out: a tank who stays in Defensive Stance gets more from Bastion's last ranks and Improved Bloodrage.

Take **Vanguard** (Charge in Defensive Stance) for trash and dungeons where you pull with Charge, **Improved Shield Bash** for a boss with a cast you must silence, and **Iron Will** for a fight full of stuns and fears. On progression bosses, take two points of **Improved Shield Wall** out of Improved Thunder Clap: Shield Wall comes back every 4 minutes instead of every 15, so a long fight gets it two or three times, and each Thunder Clap only costs 4 more rage.

## Rotation and priority

**The pull.** Bloodrage before you pull so your first Sunder Armor or Shield Slam is paid for, and Battle Shout with it unless a raid warrior's shout is already up. Open with Shield Slam or Sunder Armor, and let the damage dealers start after your first two or three globals. Taunt only to take a target back; it does nothing for you on a boss you already hold.

**Shield Block.** It is your mitigation and your rage engine at once, so it never waits for a big hit: keep it up.

**Demoralizing Shout.** If a druid or another warrior already holds it, leave it to them: two do not stack.

**Emergencies.** On a fight you know, press Shield Wall before the big hit lands rather than after.

**Adds.** Thunder Clap hits up to four targets and slows all of them. Sunder and Revenge the add the healers can least afford, and Taunt anything that peels off. When a whole pack breaks loose onto the healers, Challenging Shout makes everything around you attack you for 6 seconds on a 10 minute cooldown: Thunder Clap and Sunder them before it runs out.

## Stat priority

Dodge and parry each light Revenge and, with Master of Defense, bring rage. Strength is threat and also adds block value, which Shield Slam adds to its damage. Crushing blows come from the boss's level, not your defense; Shield Block on top of your dodge, parry and block is what pushes them off the table. Weapon skill from gear like Edgemaster's Handguards cuts how often the boss dodges and parries you, which is threat and rage, not survival. Expertise, which only a few late sets carry, does the same job.

## Best in slot

## Enchants and consumables

Mighty Rage Potion is threat at the pull. Pre-pull, the rum and Elixir of Fortitude are your health bar, and the dumplings' Strength is threat and block value; a tank that dies holds no threat.

## Races

Night Elf is the Alliance pick and Undead the Horde pick. Quickness's dodge is every hit it turns away: a Night Elf takes less damage than any other Alliance race, and its health swings less than a Dwarf's. Touch of the Grave's drain is damage, and damage is threat: an Undead holds more threat than any other Horde race, with a little health back on every drain. No race's health swings less than a Tauren's when the big hits stack, so take Tauren when your healers need the bigger health bar more than the raid needs your threat lead.

Dwarf is the button race. Stoneform only helps when you press it, so save it for the hit you know is coming, or for the bleed or poison stack that would otherwise get a healer to panic.

## Professions

Blacksmithing and Mining. Blacksmithing makes plate and shields, and Mining feeds it. Engineering adds Goblin Sapper Charges, Dense Dynamite and grenades for trash and add pickups.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Charge and Rend train at 4, Thunder Clap at 6 and Hamstring at 8. At 10, Bloodrage trains, and your race's warrior quest teaches Defensive Stance, Sunder Armor and Taunt; its follow-up rewards a one-hander to pair with a shield. Shield Bash comes at 12, Revenge and Demoralizing Shout at 14, and Shield Block at 16: two attacks blocked, and every block lights Revenge.

- **10 and 11:** Improved Bloodrage, half again the Rage.
- **12 to 16:** Shield Specialization, more block, and from the fifth point every block gives 5 Rage.
- **17 to 19:** Improved Revenge, so Revenge hits 60% harder.

Take quest rewards over vendor gear: a one-hander and the best shield you can find beat the starter weapon. You wear mail until plate at 40.

### Levels 20 to 60

Solo, Arms or Fury levels faster; keep a shield in your bags, because with one on you can tank every dungeon on the way up, even in damage talents.

- **20 to 22:** Defiance, the moment its row opens.
- **23 and 24:** Master of Defense.
- **25 to 27:** Improved Sunder Armor.
- **28 to 30:** Improved Thunder Clap.
- **31 and 32:** Focused Rage.
- **33 to 35:** Anticipation's Defense.
- **36:** Last Stand.
- **37 and 38:** Anticipation's last points.
- **39:** Concussion Blow, which opens Shield Slam.
- **40:** Shield Slam, the 31st point.
- **41:** the last point of Focused Rage, then Bastion.

The levels after that go to Improved Heroic Strike, Deflection, Improved Rend and Deep Wounds in Arms.
