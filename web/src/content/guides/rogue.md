## New race pairs

None of Blizzard's six new combinations is a Rogue. Rogue is available to both factions' Skyborne.

## Talent structure

Talent trees keep the familiar shape: seven rows per tree and 51 points to spend. The one-point "gold medal" talents at 11, 21, and 31 points are still there, and Forever adds a fourth at the 16-point mark, so every tree has four of them. Principal Designer Kris Zierhut confirmed the 16-point addition in an interview.

Talents that existed mainly to unlock a group buff are becoming baseline abilities instead, which frees room in the trees for talents you notice. Some talents are unchanged from 1.12.

One outlet's tally from the demo trees counts 10 new, 25 changed, and 18 unchanged Rogue talents.

## What changed in the demo

**Weapons:** Rogues can use **one-handed axes** in Forever. The Hack and Slash talent lists Axe and Sword together as one weapon group.

**New talents:** Mutilate in Assassination is a dual-wield attack generating 2 combo points, stronger against poisoned targets. Venom in Assassination is a finisher that boosts poison damage and application chance for a duration scaled by combo points. Restless Blades in Combat lets damaging finishers reduce the remaining cooldowns on Adrenaline Rush, Blade Flurry, Evasion, and Vanish. Cutthroat in Subtlety gives Backstab a chance to let the next Ambush skip the Stealth requirement.

**Changed talents:** Improved Expose Armor now reduces Energy cost and refunds a combo point on a 5-point cast, rather than increasing the armour reduction. Improved Kidney Shot's damage amplification applies only to your own attacks and poisons, with fewer ranks but more personal value. Weapon Expertise now reduces enemy dodge and parry chance, replacing weapon skill. Hemorrhage now increases your Rupture damage rather than all physical damage taken by the target.

**Tree layout:** four prerequisite arrows in the Rogue trees were corrected after reviewers went back over stream footage — Deflection to Riposte, Precision to Dual Wield Specialization, Blade Flurry to Weapon Expertise, and Dirty Deeds to Quietus. Any build sketched before those were spotted will need a point moved.

Blade Flurry and Seal of Command were initially reported as baseline and are in fact still talents.

The itemization change that matters most to Rogues: hit and crit are now single stats, weapon skill on items is weaker, and a new stat reduces enemy dodge and parry.

## Where these come from, and what they are worth

Except where a line is credited to Blizzard, everything in the section above was read off BlizzCon demo footage and show-floor screenshots by fan sites, mostly at level 38, which was the demo cap. Numbers can be wrong, ranks can be misread, and anything can change before launch. Nothing here is datamined.

Associate Production Director Clayton Stone has also said that Forever's launch is a deliberately conservative take after Season of Discovery, and that the team is starting by filling out skills classes did not have rather than reinventing them.
