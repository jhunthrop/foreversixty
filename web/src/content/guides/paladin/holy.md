## Overview

Holy heals the tank: Oath of Light on them, cheap Flash of Lights on top, and Divine Favor into Holy Light when they are in trouble.

### Strengths

- Oath of Light adds a flat amount to every Holy Light, Flash of Light and Holy Shock you cast on the tank, as long as the rank is close to your level, so a Flash of Light a rank or two below your top one heals for most of what the top rank does and gets more healing out of every point of mana
- Holy Shock is an instant heal for movement and emergencies, and Divine Favor guarantees a crit when it counts
- Illumination refunds half the cost of every critical heal
- Spiritual Focus and Concentration Aura keep your casts going while you take damage

### Weaknesses

- No heal over time and only one party heal, Light's Vigil, which costs almost as much as ten top-rank Flash of Lights
- Long fights test your mana: you heal off pace, not off the health bars
- Oath of Light is one target, so most of your strength goes to one player

### In a raid

- **Role:** Tank healer first, then your own party
- **Brings:** Greater Blessings (Wisdom or Might, and Kings where no hunter's Heart of the Lion covers the raid), an aura for your group, Blessing of Protection every 3 minutes and Lay on Hands

## Talents and builds

The build is 31 Holy, 12 Protection and 8 Retribution.

- **Spiritual Focus** (5 points): every hit you take while casting Flash of Light, Holy Light or Light's Vigil has a 70% chance not to push the cast back.
- **Healing Light** (3 points): 12% more healing on Holy Light, Flash of Light and Holy Shock.
- **Reverence** (2 points): 30% of your regeneration keeps running while you cast.
- **Illumination** (5 points): every critical heal gives back half the spell's base cost. It also keeps half your Spirit regeneration running while you cast for 15 seconds. It is the reason crit is a mana stat for you.
- **Divine Favor** (1 point): your next Flash of Light, Holy Light or Holy Shock is a sure crit, on a 2 minute cooldown.
- **Infusion of Light** (2 points): a Flash of Light or Holy Shock crit makes your next Holy Light within 15 seconds a second faster.
- **Oath of Light** (1 point): sworn on one ally for 5 minutes, it adds a flat amount to every Holy Light, Flash of Light and Holy Shock you put on them, as long as the rank is close to your level. It took Blessing of Light's place, and it is the biggest point in the tree.
- **Holy Shock** (1 point): an instant heal on a 10 second cooldown.
- **Boundless Light** (2 points): 30% more crit on Holy Shock on anyone without your Oath.
- **Holy Power** (5 points): your critical heals land for 175% instead of 150%. It no longer adds crit chance.
- **Light's Vigil** (1 point): the capstone. The next Holy Shock on its target has no cooldown and heals that target's whole party.
- **Benediction** (5 points) and **Conviction** (3 points): Holy Shock, Cleanse, your Blessings and your Seals and Judgement cost 10% less, and you get 6% crit on your heals and everything else you do.
- **Divine Intellect** (5 points): 10% Intellect, in Protection's first row now.

The rest is utility. **Guardian's Favor** takes 2 minutes off Blessing of Protection and adds 6 seconds to Blessing of Freedom, **Sacred Duty** brings Divine Shield back a minute sooner, **Purifying Power** makes Cleanse cheaper, **Divine Precision** is the point that opens Holy Power's row, and **Precision** fills out the Protection points, since nothing else in those rows heals.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Greater Blessings are an hour long in Forever, so they go out before the first pull and after a wipe, not between trash packs. Swear Oath of Light on the tank a second or two before the pull. Concentration Aura if nobody else in your group runs it.

**Your heals, in the order you reach for them:**

1. **Oath of Light** kept on the tank. It lasts 5 minutes: renew it in a long fight, and move it when the tank changes.
2. **Divine Favor**, then **Holy Light** (rank 8), when the tank drops below 40%. It is the only heal you can keep casting that outheals a tank by itself, and the crit is guaranteed.
3. **Holy Light** on the tank below 70%, and **Holy Shock** on anyone below 70%, but only with mana to spare: keep your mana percent no more than 5 points under the percent of the fight left (halfway through, above 45% mana). Once you fall more than 5 points under, heal with Flash of Light alone until you catch up.
4. **Flash of Light rank 5** on the tank below 90%. This is your staple. Oath of Light adds the same flat amount to every rank close to your level, and rank 5 carries all of your healing power at 60 for less mana than rank 6. Without raid buffs, when mana is the limit, rank 4 does the same job for less again. Rank 1 is a trap at 60: Forever takes the healing power off ranks learned far below your level, and the Oath's bonus goes with it, so rank 1 heals for little more than its base.
5. **Flash of Light rank 5** on everyone else below 80%, yourself included. In a raid, Blessing of Wisdom, Mana Spring and the raid's Intellect buffs pay for the bigger heal. Without them, rank 4 is the best balance of heal size and mana, as it is from 50 while you level, even once you have rank 5.

**Ranks.** Bind the ranks you use. Holy Light rank 8 is the top rank a trainer teaches, and the rank 9 book has no known source in Forever yet, so rank 8 is your top rank. Use Holy Shock rank 3 or 4. Rank 1 costs half as much, but it is a level 30 rank, so at 60 it keeps only part of your healing power.

**Mana.** You heal off pace, not off health bars: if your bar is ahead of the fight, spend it on Holy Light and Holy Shock; if it is behind, heal with Flash of Light alone. Every crit refunds half its base cost through Illumination, which is why Holy Shock on the party, where Boundless Light adds 30% crit, gets a large part of its cost back. Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune go on cooldown as soon as your bar has room for them.

**Light's Vigil.** It costs 1,340 mana at 60. Keep it for a fight where your whole party takes big damage at once. On an ordinary boss it is mana you need for the tank.

**Movement.** Holy Shock is your only instant heal outside Lay on Hands, and Flash of Light is a 1.5 second cast. Stand where you reach the tank and your party, so you never have to move to heal.

**Silences.** Voice of Truth makes you immune to silences and interrupts for 6 seconds, every 3 minutes: save it for the caster pack or boss that would lock you out.

**Emergencies.** Divine Favor into Holy Light first. Lay on Hands heals your target for as much as your own maximum health and gives them 550 mana at its top rank, on a 20 minute cooldown. It drains all of your own mana, and your regeneration keeps running. Use it on someone about to die whom Divine Favor and Holy Light could not have saved, ideally late in the fight. Blessing of Protection saves a player who has pulled aggro from a physical attacker, but never use it on the tank: they drop the boss.

## Stat priority

Mana per 5 seconds keeps you casting, and you run out of mana long before you run out of heals. Intellect is your mana bar and your crit, and crit is mana through Illumination and healing through Holy Power. Healing power adds the same amount to every rank close to your level, but ranks learned far below your level lose most or all of it, so at 60 your Flash of Light is rank 4 or 5 and your Holy Shock rank 3 or 4, never rank 1. Spirit only works once you have gone five seconds without a cast, apart from Reverence's 30% and the 50% Illumination gives you for 15 seconds after every critical heal, which is one more reason crit is a mana stat for you. Spell damage does nothing for a heal. The table above has the order.

## Races

**Human** brings Ruthless, 1% crit on every heal and so more Illumination refunds, and The Human Spirit, 10% Spirit. **Dwarf** has Stoneform for a hit you cannot heal through. **Undead** is new to paladins in Forever and the Horde's only paladin healer: Will of the Forsaken breaks a fear and gets you casting again (it no longer makes you immune, so press it after the fear), and Cannibalize restores mana as well as health between pulls.

## Professions

Herbalism and Alchemy keep you in mana potions, Flasks of Distilled Wisdom and elixirs, which is most of what a healer spends gold on. Enchanting gives you the healing enchants yourself, and it is the only way to wear Dormant Heart of the Mountain, which binds when picked up.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Holy Light and Seal of Righteousness come at 1, Judgement at 4, Holy Strike at 6, Purify at 8 and Lay on Hands at 10. New Holy Light ranks come at 6 and 14, and Blessing of Wisdom at 14 is your mana blessing once you heal groups. Until 17, rank 3 is your Holy Light for everything. From 18, while you level, put Holy Light (Rank 1) on its own key and heal with it: at 35 mana against rank 3's 110 it heals far more per point of mana, even though it keeps a little less of your healing power each level from 18, and over a whole dungeon you heal more before you drink. Rank 3 still heals more per cast, so keep it for the hit the tank must survive. Rank 1 stays your Holy Light through 24, with Flash of Light beside it from 20, and from 25 to 29 rank 2 does the same job. From 30 your newest rank takes over for most of your leveling; at 40 to 42 and 47 to 49 keep rank 4, which heals as much for less mana.

At 12, the class quest from your trainer teaches Redemption. A healer without it is a long corpse run for the group.

Most Holy paladins level as Retribution and respec for raids; the Retribution guide has those points. If you level as Holy, your first five points, 10 to 14, go into Spiritual Focus: at 5/5, 70% of the hits you take do not push back Holy Light, or Flash of Light once you learn it at 20. Healing Light takes 15 to 17 for 12% bigger heals, then Reverence at 18 and 19 keeps 30% of your mana regen going while you cast.

You still kill things with your weapon and your Seal, so take quest rewards over vendor gear and replace your Battleworn Hammer with the first better slow two-hander you find. Paladins cannot use wands. You wear mail until plate at 40.

### Levels 20 to 60

Level as Retribution and heal dungeons on the way: Flash of Light at level 20 is enough to heal most dungeons at your level, even in a Retribution build. Respec Holy for raids. If you do level as Holy, take Oath of Light at 25, the first point you can spend in its row, ahead of Divine Precision and Infusion of Light: it is the biggest heal in that row from the day you get it, and Divine Precision only adds Holy spell hit and a little silence reduction, nothing for your heals. Questing as Holy is slow either way.

Common mistakes:

- Healing the tank with rank 1 Flash of Light at 60. It has lost your healing power and the Oath's bonus with it; rank 4 or 5 under Oath of Light does the work for less mana than the top rank.
- Letting Oath of Light lapse after 5 minutes, or leaving it on the old tank after a swap.
- Holy Shock on the Oath target when the tank is fine. On anyone else it crits far more often and refunds mana.
- Casting Light's Vigil on an ordinary boss.
- Topping players to full with Holy Light. Let Flash of Light handle anything that is not an emergency.
