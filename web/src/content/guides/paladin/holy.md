## Overview

### Strengths

- Oath of Light adds a flat amount to every Holy Light, Flash of Light and Holy Shock you cast on the tank, as long as the rank is close to your level, so a Flash of Light a rank or two below your top one heals for most of what the top rank does and gets more healing out of every point of mana
- Holy Shock is an instant heal for movement and emergencies, and Divine Favor guarantees a crit when it counts
- Illumination refunds half the cost of every critical heal
- Spiritual Focus and Concentration Aura together stop every pushback: under the aura, a hit while you cast never costs you casting time

### Weaknesses

- No heal over time and only one party heal, Light's Vigil, which costs almost as much as ten top-rank Flash of Lights
- Long fights test your mana: you heal off pace, not off the health bars
- Oath of Light is one target, so most of your strength goes to one player

### In a raid

- **Role:** Tank healer first, then your own party
- **Brings:** Greater Blessings (Wisdom or Might, and Kings where no hunter's Heart of the Lion covers the raid), an aura for your group, Blessing of Protection every 3 minutes and Lay on Hands

## Talents and builds

The build is 31 Holy, 12 Protection and 8 Retribution.

- **Spiritual Focus** (5 points): every hit you take while casting Flash of Light, Holy Light or Light's Vigil has a 70% chance not to push the cast back.
- **Healing Light** (3 points): 12% more healing on Holy Light, Flash of Light and Holy Shock.
- **Reverence** (2 points): 30% of your regeneration keeps running while you cast.
- **Illumination** (5 points): every critical heal gives back half the spell's base cost. The tooltip also promises 15 seconds of Spirit regeneration while you cast; that part does not happen, so count Illumination as a mana refund and nothing more. It is the reason crit is a mana stat for you.
- **Divine Favor** (1 point): your next Flash of Light, Holy Light or Holy Shock is a sure crit, on a 2 minute cooldown.
- **Infusion of Light** (2 points): a Flash of Light or Holy Shock crit makes your next Holy Light within 15 seconds a second faster.
- **Oath of Light** (1 point): sworn on one ally for 5 minutes, it adds a flat amount to every Holy Light, Flash of Light and Holy Shock you put on them, as long as the rank is close to your level. It took Blessing of Light's place, and it is the biggest point in the tree.
- **Holy Shock** (1 point): an instant heal on a 10 second cooldown.
- **Boundless Light** (2 points): 30% more crit on Holy Shock on anyone without your Oath.
- **Holy Power** (5 points): your critical heals land for 175% instead of 150%. It no longer adds crit chance.
- **Light's Vigil** (1 point): the capstone. The next Holy Shock on its target has no cooldown and heals that target's whole party.
- **Benediction** (5 points) and **Conviction** (3 points): Holy Shock, Cleanse, your Blessings and your Seals and Judgement cost 10% less, and you get 6% crit on your heals and everything else you do.
- **Divine Intellect** (5 points): 10% Intellect, in Protection's first row now.

The rest is utility. **Guardian's Favor** takes 2 minutes off Blessing of Protection and adds 6 seconds to Blessing of Freedom, **Sacred Duty** brings Divine Shield back a minute sooner, **Purifying Power** makes Cleanse cheaper, **Divine Precision** is the point that opens Holy Power's row, and **Precision** fills out the Protection points, since nothing else in those rows heals.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Greater Blessings are an hour long in Forever, so they go out before the first pull and after a wipe, not between trash packs. Swear Oath of Light on the tank a second or two before the pull, and move it when the tank changes. Run Concentration Aura unless another paladin already has it on your group: its 35% and Spiritual Focus's 70% add up, so nothing that hits you pushes a cast back, where Devotion Aura lets three hits in ten through, the first costing you a second of casting.

**Pace, not health bars.** Divine Favor into Holy Light is for the tank at 40%, the heal that must land. Holy Light and Holy Shock otherwise spend only mana you are ahead on; fall behind and it is Flash of Light alone until you catch up. Holy Shock on the party, where Boundless Light adds 30% crit, gets a good share of its cost back through Illumination.

**Ranks.** Bind the ranks you use. A rank keeps all of your healing power and the Oath's bonus until you are 17 levels past the level you learned it, then loses a twentieth a level, so a cheap rank wins only while that gap is small; past the breakpoints in the leveling section, move up. Flash of Light rank 5 carries all of your healing power at 60 for less mana than rank 6, and rank 4 does the tank's job for less again outside a raid. Rank 1 is a trap at 60: Forever takes the healing power off ranks learned far below your level, and the Oath's bonus goes with it, so rank 1 heals for little more than its base. Use Holy Shock rank 3 or 4. Rank 1 costs half as much, but it is a level 30 rank, so at 60 it keeps only part of your healing power.

**Light's Vigil.** At 1,340 mana it waits for a fight where your whole party takes big damage at once.

**Movement.** Holy Shock is your only instant heal outside Lay on Hands, so stand where you reach the tank and your party without moving.

**Silences.** Voice of Truth makes you immune to silences and interrupts for 6 seconds, every 3 minutes: save it for the caster pack or boss that would lock you out.

**Emergencies.** Lay on Hands heals your target for as much as your own maximum health and gives them 550 mana at its top rank, on a 20 minute cooldown. It drains all of your own mana, and your regeneration keeps running. Use it on someone about to die whom Divine Favor and Holy Light could not have saved, ideally late in the fight. Blessing of Protection saves a player who has pulled aggro from a physical attacker, but never use it on the tank: they drop the boss.

## Stat priority

Intellect brings crit with it, and crit is mana through Illumination and healing through Holy Power. Healing power adds the same amount to every rank close to your level, and ranks learned far below your level lose most or all of it. Spirit only works once you have gone five seconds without a cast, apart from Reverence's 30%. Illumination pays you in mana on a crit, not in regeneration. Spell damage does nothing for a heal.

## Races

**Human** for Ruthless, whose crit means more Illumination refunds, and The Human Spirit for mana. **Dwarf** has Stoneform for a hit you cannot heal through. **Undead** is new to paladins in Forever and the Horde's only paladin healer: Will of the Forsaken gets you casting again after a fear (it no longer makes you immune, so press it after the fear), and Cannibalize restores mana as well as health between pulls.

## Professions

Herbalism and Alchemy keep you in mana potions, Flasks of Distilled Wisdom and elixirs, which is most of what a healer spends gold on. Enchanting gives you the healing enchants yourself, and it is the only way to wear Dormant Heart of the Mountain, which binds when picked up.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Holy Light and Seal of Righteousness come at 1, Judgement at 4, Holy Strike at 6, Purify at 8 and Lay on Hands at 10. New Holy Light ranks come at 6 and 14, and Blessing of Wisdom at 14 is your mana blessing once you heal groups.

From 18, put Holy Light (Rank 1) on its own key: at 35 mana against rank 3's 110 it heals far more per point of mana, even though it keeps a little less of your healing power each level, and over a whole dungeon you heal more before you drink. Rank 3 heals more per cast, but over a fight rank 1 heals more, even on the tank below 40%. Your Holy Light rank while you level:

- **To 17:** rank 3 for everything.
- **18 to 28:** rank 1, with Flash of Light beside it from 20.
- **29:** rank 2.
- **From 30:** your newest rank, except at 40 to 42 and 47 to 49, where rank 4 heals as much for less mana.

At 12, the class quest from your trainer teaches Redemption. A healer without it is a long corpse run for the group.

Most Holy paladins level as [Retribution](/guides/paladin/retribution) and respec for raids. If you level as Holy:

- **10 to 14:** Spiritual Focus, so hits stop pushing back Holy Light, and Flash of Light once you learn it at 20.
- **15 and 16:** Reverence.
- **17 to 19:** Healing Light.

You still kill things with your weapon and your Seal, so take quest rewards over vendor gear and replace your Battleworn Hammer with the first better slow two-hander you find. Paladins cannot use wands. You wear mail until plate at 40.

### Levels 20 to 60

Flash of Light at level 20 is enough to heal most dungeons at your level, even in a Retribution build. The rank to bind, by level:

- **On the tank:** rank 1 through 42 (a Dwarf keeps rank 1 at 43), rank 2 from 43, rank 3 from 50, rank 4 from 54 (an Undead takes rank 4 at 53). At 60 it is rank 4 on your own buffs and rank 5 in a raid.
- **On the rest of the group:** rank 1 through 41. Rank 2 from 42 as a Human, from 43 as a Dwarf, from 44 as an Undead; rank 2 stays through 49, and rank 4 takes over from 50.

Questing as Holy is slow either way. If you do level as Holy:

- **20 to 24:** Illumination.
- **25:** Oath of Light, the first point you can spend in its row, ahead of Divine Precision and Infusion of Light. It is the biggest heal in that row from the day you get it, and Divine Precision only adds Holy spell hit and a little silence reduction, nothing for your heals.
- **After 25:** Divine Intellect from Protection for a bigger mana bar, Divine Favor at 30, then Benediction and Conviction from Retribution by 39, whose crit means more Illumination refunds.
- **40 to 44:** Purifying Power, Divine Precision and Infusion of Light, only to open Holy Shock's row.
- **45:** Holy Shock. It costs more mana than a leveling dungeon lets you spend until Holy Power stands behind it.
- **By 52:** Boundless Light and Holy Power.
- **53:** Light's Vigil.
- **Last:** Precision, Guardian's Favor and Sacred Duty, which do nothing for your heals.

Common mistakes:

- Healing the tank with rank 1 Flash of Light at 60. Rank 4 or 5 under Oath of Light does the work for less mana than the top rank.
- Letting Oath of Light lapse after 5 minutes, or leaving it on the old tank after a swap.
- Holy Shock on the Oath target when the tank is fine. On anyone else it crits far more often and refunds mana.
- Casting Light's Vigil on an ordinary boss.
- Topping players to full with Holy Light. Let Flash of Light handle anything that is not an emergency.
