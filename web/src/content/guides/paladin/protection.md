## Overview

### Strengths

- Righteous Fury turns your Holy damage into huge threat, and Holy Shield adds more on every block
- Holy Shield and Redoubt keep your block chance high, and Shieldsworn fires Holy Shield's damage on every Holy Strike while it is up and lets you block spells
- Judgement of Fury taunts, and Swift Judgement gives you a second one
- Three defensives (Templar's Bulwark, Divine Shield, Divine Protection) plus Lay on Hands

### Weaknesses

- Mana, not cooldowns, limits what you press: run dry and your threat stops
- Templar's Bulwark, Divine Shield and Divine Protection share Forbearance, so you get one at a time
- Forever has no Blessing of Salvation, so your damage dealers keep all their threat and you have to out-threat them without that help

### In a raid

- **Role:** Main or off tank. You hold the boss, and you swap with Judgement of Fury when the fight calls for it
- **Brings:** Devotion Aura for your group, Greater Blessing of Might (or Kings where no hunter's Heart of the Lion covers the raid), and a Lay on Hands for the raid's worst moment

## Talents and builds

The build is 10 Holy, 33 Protection and 8 Retribution.

- **Redoubt** (5 points): a hit that damages you has a chance to add 30% block for 10 seconds or 5 blocks.
- **Precision** (3 points): 3% hit, so the Judgement and Holy Strike that carry your threat land.
- **Sacred Duty** (2 points): a minute off Divine Shield, Divine Protection and Templar's Bulwark, and 10 seconds off Forbearance.
- **Templar's Bulwark** (1 point): an absorb worth your whole health bar for 8 seconds. It leads to Swift Judgement.
- **Shield Specialization** (2 points): 30% more absorbed by your shield, and a block gives back 4% of your mana, at most once every 3 seconds. This is where your mana comes from.
- **Swift Judgement** (1 point): a second Judgement of your Seal on its own one minute cooldown, free and off the global cooldown.
- **Improved Righteous Fury** (3 points): 9% less damage taken while Righteous Fury is on. It is a mitigation talent now, not a threat one.
- **Anticipation** (5 points)
- **Holy Shield** (1 point): 30% more block for 10 seconds or 4 blocks, with Holy damage on each block and 20% extra threat on that damage. It sits on the 21 point row now.
- **Iron Creed** (3 points): 30% more threat on Holy Strike, and while Righteous Fury is on each Holy Strike cuts your damage taken by 6% for 6 seconds.
- **Shieldsworn** (1 point): the capstone. Holy Strike comes back every 6 seconds, which keeps Iron Creed up, and each strike fires Holy Shield's damage and Shield Specialization's mana without spending a charge. For 6 seconds after a strike you can block spells too.

**Toughness** (10% armor from items) and a point of **Divine Intellect** fill the tree. In Retribution, **Deflection** is 5% parry, the only parry talent a paladin has, and **Conviction** is 6% crit on everything you do. In Holy, **Divine Strength** is 10% Strength and **Improved Seals** adds 15% to Seal of Fury and its Judgement.

Left out: Reckoning (extra attacks after a block or a crit; it adds a little threat, but paying for it with Anticipation's Defense gets you killed on hard pulls), Stoicism, Guardian's Favor and Improved Hammer of Justice. Pick those up for PvP or utility. There is no Blessing of Sanctuary in Forever, so the tree gives you no blessing of your own.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Righteous Fury is a 30 minute buff, not a stance: cast it before the raid (it costs 30% of your base mana, so do it before you drink) and keep it on all night. Get Greater Blessing of Kings on yourself unless a hunter's Heart of the Lion already covers you: the two are the same 10% to every stat and do not stack, so with a hunter in the raid take Might instead. Keep Devotion Aura on.

**The opener.** Put up Seal of Fury about 3 seconds before the pull. Pull with Judgement: under Seal of Fury it taunts, so the boss comes straight to you. Press Holy Shield about 2 seconds before the boss reaches you, so all four charges are there for its first swings. Then Swift Judgement, Holy Strike and Hammer of the Righteous in the first few globals, and give your damage dealers a few seconds: there is no Blessing of Salvation to drop their threat.

**Mana.** You run on blocks, and Holy Strike under Shieldsworn triggers Shield Specialization too. Hold Holy Shield's cost in reserve at all times. If you are going dry, cut Consecration first, then Hammer of the Righteous, and never Holy Shield. Below 35% health Consecration goes down anyway, because mana held back then may never get used. At 60 without raid buffs, Consecration rank 1 goes down far more often and does more than the higher ranks.

**Taunts and swaps.** Judgement under Seal of Fury makes the target attack you for 3 seconds, from 10 yards, and Swift Judgement judges the same Seal, so it is a second taunt on its own minute. On a fight with a tank swap, hold one of them for the swap. Judgement is a ranged attack: it lands on your ranged hit chance and nothing dodges, parries or blocks it. Judgement of Fury replaces your own Judgement of the Crusader on the target, so leave the Crusader debuff to a Retribution or Holy paladin.

**Adds.** Consecration and Hammer of the Righteous are your multi-target threat; Holy Shield and Shieldsworn's block keep the damage down. Judge the add that is loose, Holy Strike your main target.

**Defensives.** Templar's Bulwark comes first, then Divine Shield and Divine Protection once Forbearance is gone. All three put Forbearance on you, so plan one per bad moment, not three in a row. Lay on Hands heals you to full on a 20 minute cooldown, but it takes every point of your mana with it. Your mana keeps regenerating afterwards, but until it comes back you have no Holy Shield and little threat, so save it for the swing that would kill you and have a mana potion ready. A Dwarf's Stoneform takes a global but puts no Forbearance on you.

## Stat priority

More block chance adds nothing you can measure at 60, but keep the shield: your blocks still power Holy Shield's damage and Shield Specialization's mana. Hit counts up to the cap and nothing past it, and Precision already gives you 3% of it. Expertise, which only a few raid sets carry, lowers the chance the boss dodges or parries your swings and Holy Strike, which is threat.

## Enchants and consumables

## Races

On the Alliance, take a **Dwarf**. Dwarf and Human take hits and hold threat about the same, so Stoneform tips it: a defensive that costs no mana on a hard pull, with Artisan's Precision adding hit. **Human** brings Will to Survive to get you back on the boss after a stun and Ruthless for a little more threat; The Human Spirit does little for a tank. **Undead** is new to paladins in Forever and the only Horde option. Will of the Forsaken gets a feared tank back to the boss before it turns on the raid, but it no longer makes you immune, so press it after the fear lands.

## Professions

Mining and Blacksmithing make plate, shields and the tanking weapons. Engineering gives you explosives for trash threat. With Alchemy, Elixir of Holy Power adds to Holy Shield, Holy Strike, Seal of Fury, your Judgements and Consecration. Hammer of the Righteous hits off your weapon, so the elixir does nothing for it. First Aid is free, and you will use bandages between pulls while leveling.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Devotion Aura comes at 1, Judgement at 4, Holy Strike at 6 and Hammer of Justice at 8. At 10 you get Righteous Fury and Seal of Fury: with a shield on, every swing under Seal of Fury gives you an absorb, and judging it hits for Holy damage and taunts.

At 12, the class quest from your trainer teaches Redemption. Holy Strike rank 2 also arrives at 12: put it on your bar straight away, the extra threat is worth its mana.

Most tanks level as [Retribution](/guides/paladin/retribution) and pick these up when they respec. If you level as Protection:

- **10:** Divine Intellect for 2% Intellect.
- **11 to 15:** Redoubt.
- **16 to 18:** Precision.
- **19:** the first point of Sacred Duty, 30 seconds off Divine Protection.

Take quest rewards over vendor gear. To tank, pair a one-hander with a shield as soon as you have both, since Seal of Fury's absorb needs the shield. Paladins cannot use wands. You wear mail until plate at 40.

### Levels 20 to 60

Level as Retribution and tank dungeons in it: Righteous Fury and Seal of Fury are enough to hold a dungeon group from Ragefire Chasm and the Deadmines on. Switch to Protection when you are tanking more than you are questing. Holy Shield comes at 30, with new ranks at 40, 50 and 60, and Hammer of the Righteous at 40. If you tank in Protection:

- **20:** Templar's Bulwark before a second point of Sacred Duty. A full health bar of absorb keeps a low-level tank alive far more often than a shorter cooldown on it.
- **21 and 22:** Shield Specialization, since your blocks are your mana.
- **23 and 24:** the second point of Sacred Duty and a point of Toughness.
- **25:** Swift Judgement, the first level its row opens. A second Judgement and a second taunt are worth more threat than the rest of Toughness.
- **26 to 29:** the rest of Toughness.

While you level, Consecration rank 1 does a little more than rank 2 at 30 to 38, because it costs less and you can drop it far more often on a dungeon pull; from 39 use your newest rank, and at 60 without raid buffs go back to rank 1.

Common mistakes:

- Forgetting Righteous Fury after a death or a long break. Check your buffs before every pull.
- Spending mana on Consecration and having none for Holy Shield.
- Pressing Holy Shield while the boss is still running in, so its charges are gone before the first swing.
- Burning both Judgements on threat right before a tank swap.
- Using Divine Shield and Templar's Bulwark back to back: they share Forbearance, so the second one is not there.
