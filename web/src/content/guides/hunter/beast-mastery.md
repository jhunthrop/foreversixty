## Overview

### Strengths

- The pet takes 10% of your attack power and all of your crit, so every upgrade you wear makes it hit harder
- Bestial Wrath twice in a three minute boss fight, the first on the pull with Rapid Fire
- The pet holds adds and soaks hits while leveling, so you rarely stop to eat
- Careful Aim turns Intellect into attack power for you and the pet

### Weaknesses

- A dead or idle pet is a large part of your damage gone
- The pet takes no stat buffs from other players in Forever, only your own Heart of the Lion, so your gear is what makes it stronger
- Mana runs out on long fights if you keep firing Multi-Shot on every cooldown

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged physical DPS with a pet
- **Brings:** Heart of the Lion (10% stats and a flat attack power bonus for everyone near you), Trueshot Aura (100 ranged attack power at 60 for your party and raid), Hunter's Mark on the boss

## Talents and builds

Thirty-one points in Beast Mastery buys every talent that makes the pet hit harder, then twenty more buy what makes you hit harder: sixteen in Marksmanship and four in Survival's Improved Tracking in the level 60 build, all twenty in Marksmanship in the raid build.

- **Unleashed Fury** (5 points): 15% more damage for the pet on every hit, white or special
- **Ferocity** (5 points): 10% pet crit on top of the crit it already copies from you, and every crit feeds Frenzy
- **Frenzy** (5 points): a pet crit always grants 30% attack speed for 8 seconds, which with Ferocity is close to permanent
- **Focused Fire** (2 points): 2% more damage for you and the pet while the pet is out, the only flat damage bonus to your own shots in the Beast Mastery tree
- **Bestial Wrath** (1 point): 50% more pet damage for 18 seconds every two minutes, and the pet ignores fear while it lasts
- **Deadly Aspects** (5 points): auto shots under Aspect of the Hawk have a 10% chance to speed your ranged attacks by 30% for 12 seconds
- **Careful Aim** (5 points): attack power equal to your Intellect, so a mail Intellect piece is an attack power piece for you and the pet
- **Efficiency** (5 points, level 60 build): 15% off the mana of your shots and stings, so you keep shooting through a chain of pulls
- **Improved Tracking** (4 points, level 60 build): 4% more damage against the creature type you track, so set Track to your target's type before the pull
- **Mortal Shots** (4 points, raid build): your shot crits hit for 224% of a normal hit instead of double

Endurance Training (4 points) keeps the pet alive through raid damage, Bestial Discipline (2 points) keeps half your mana regeneration running while you shoot, and Intimidation (1 point) opens Bestial Wrath and stuns trash: press it to stop a runner or to peel an add off a healer, since bosses cannot be stunned. Bestial Swiftness gets the cat to the target sooner after every pull. In the raid build, Improved Stings (2 points) adds Serpent Sting damage and is the way down to Mortal Shots. Hawk Eye, Improved Concussive Shot and Deflection add nothing to raid damage, so the build skips them.

The raid build moves two points out of Efficiency and the four in Improved Tracking into Improved Stings and Mortal Shots. With Judgement of Wisdom on the boss and a Major Mana Potion you do not run dry, and Mortal Shots beats the mana. Out in the world, keep the level 60 build: all five points of Efficiency keep you shooting, and you will run out of mana long before you run out of targets.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Mark the boss with Hunter's Mark first, so the mana is back by the pull. Put up Aspect of the Hawk and leave it up all fight: under Falcon, Deadly Aspects only procs from melee swings, never from your auto shots. Set the pet to passive while you walk in.

**The opener.** Time Aimed Shot so it lands just after the tank's first hit. Started any earlier, it is the first threat on the boss and the boss comes for you; on a fresh tank, give him a second or two more. Send the pet in once the tank has the boss, and press Bestial Wrath as it connects.

**Auto Shot.** It does more damage than any button you press, so protect it:

- Stand still. Moving while an auto shot winds up cancels it.
- Fire your instants right after an auto shot leaves, so they never push the next one back.
- Stay outside 8 yards. Inside it you cannot shoot at all.

**Mana.** Refresh Serpent Sting when it is about to drop and fill the gaps with Arcane Shot to the end of the fight: both carry your ranged attack power in Forever, the sting is the best damage for its mana you have and Arcane Shot is next. When the mana runs low, Multi-Shot goes first. Drink a Major Mana Potion before you need it, not at 5%.

**The pet.** Raid with a cat: Claw and Bite on a fast attacker, steady damage with nothing to manage. Train it the top ranks of Claw, Bite and Growl as you level, then turn off Growl in raids (right-click it on the pet bar) or it will pull the boss off your tank. Send it to the boss's back so it is not parried or cleaved. If it is taking raid damage, a Mend Pet between pulls is cheaper than a Revive Pet in the middle of one.

**Threat.** A Beast Mastery hunter with Bestial Wrath up can pass a fresh tank in the opening seconds. Watch your threat and Feign Death before you reach the tank, not after the boss turns. Feign Death can be resisted: if it says Resisted, you still have all your threat. The pet keeps its own threat, which is fine, since it sits behind the boss.

**Trash.** Multi-Shot hits three targets and is your AoE on most packs. Volley is a mana-heavy channel, worth it only on a big pack that lives through it. Lay Freezing Trap before the pull; any damage breaks it. Explosive Trap under a pack the tank is holding hits everything within 10 yards, and at 60 it costs even more than Volley, so save it for the same big packs.

**Utility.** Tranquilizing Shot (bought from your trainer at 60, so you do not wait on Lucifron's tome) removes a boss's frenzy, and on a frenzying boss it is a raid job that someone is assigned to: if it is you, it comes before any damage. Distracting Shot puts a big lump of threat on a loose add, enough to pull it off a healer and onto you. Bring it to your tank and let him take it from you. Freezing Trap takes care of anything loose in the back line.

## Stat priority

Hit matters up to 9%, the cap against a raid boss, auto shots included; past the cap it adds nothing. Crit is better than it looks because the pet copies all of your crit chance. Intellect is attack power through Careful Aim and mana on top. Plain attack power counts for a little more than it did in Classic, because Arcane Shot and Serpent Sting carry a share of it.

## Best in slot

A strong leather piece often beats the mail you can get, so your list mixes both at 60. Your ranged weapon is your real weapon, the melee weapon is there for its stats, so a two-hander with Agility or Intellect beats a fast one-hander with damage.

## Enchants and consumables

Put a scope on the ranged weapon: Sniper Scope from Engineering, and Biznicks 247x128 Accurascope once your raid's engineer can make it. Agility on gloves, boots and cloak, the best stats enchant on the chest, and Intellect on bracers: no bracer enchant carries real Agility, Strength does nothing for your shots, and Careful Aim makes Intellect count as attack power.

**Ammo and quiver.** Carry the best ammo your level can use and plenty of it: arrows for a bow, bullets for a gun. Jagged Arrows from 40, Ice Threaded and Thorium Headed Arrows from the early 50s, or Thorium Shells from an engineer. The quiver or ammo pouch is a free 10 to 15% ranged attack speed, so upgrade it every time you can: Quickdraw Quiver from 40 and a 15% bag near 60. Run out of ammo in a raid and you do almost nothing.

Forever's damage potions share the two-minute potion cooldown with mana potions, so a Beast Mastery hunter drinks Major Mana Potions. Elixir of the Mongoose is the elixir to keep up, with Poached Sunscale Salmon and Ground Scorpok Assay alongside it.

## Races

Night Elf for the Alliance: Elune's Light lines up with Bestial Wrath and Rapid Fire on the pull, and your pet copies that crit. Human's Ruthless is next, and Dwarf comes last.

Orc for the Horde. Command makes your pet hit harder all fight, and Blood Fury's two minute cooldown comes up with every Bestial Wrath, so you press them together twice in most boss fights. Troll is a fine second: Berserking stacks with Rapid Fire on the pull.

## Professions

Your raid set asks for Enchanting. Frozen Heart of the Mountain is an Enchanting trinket that binds on pickup, so nobody can make it for you. Legionite Glaive is a Blacksmithing polearm that also binds on pickup. If you are not a blacksmith, take the next two-hander on your list. Every other craft in your set binds when equipped, the Engineering rifle and the Leatherworking mail included, so buy them. Engineering brings Thorium Grenades for trash, and the target dummies and explosives that save wipes. Skinning pays for your leveling, since you kill beasts all day.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Train Heart of the Lion at level 1 and keep it up all the way to 60: 10% more stats and extra attack power (280 at 60) for everyone near you, and another 10% stats for you. Raptor Strike and Auto Shot carry the first levels. Train Serpent Sting and Aspect of the Monkey at 4, Arcane Shot and Hunter's Mark at 6, Concussive Shot at 8, Aspect of the Hawk at 10, which replaces Monkey for good, and Wing Clip at 12.

Multi-Shot comes at 18. It is the worst damage for its mana you fire, so once you are under a quarter of your mana, hold it, and from 20 Aimed Shot takes the shared cooldown when you can afford it. Keep Serpent Sting rolling and Arcane Shot firing on cooldown even then, without waiting on the shared cooldown.

At 10 your trainer starts the taming quests, which end by teaching Tame Beast. Tame a beast at your level, feed it to keep it happy, and train Mend Pet at 12.

Talents:

- **10 to 14:** Deadly Aspects. With Aspect of the Hawk up, each point is a 2% chance on Auto Shot for 30% ranged attack speed over 12 sec.
- **15 and 16:** Focused Fire, the moment its row opens: 2% on every shot and every pet hit is worth more on single pulls than pet health, so Endurance Training starts at 17.
- **20 to 29:** Unleashed Fury 20 to 24 and Ferocity 25 to 29, before the fourth point of Endurance Training.
- **30 to 39:** Bestial Discipline at 30 and 31, Bestial Swiftness and Intimidation at 32 and 33 to open the way to Bestial Wrath, and Frenzy 35 to 39.

Take quest rewards over vendor gear, Agility first. Replace your starting bow or gun with the first ranged quest reward you find: your ranged weapon is most of your damage. Wear leather until 40. From 40 you can wear mail, but keep a leather piece whenever it is the better one.

### Levels 20 to 60

Beast Mastery is the best way to level a hunter. Keep the pet within a few levels of you. The pet takes the hits, you shoot, and you rarely stop to eat.

Mark the target and send the pet in first; once it has hit and Growled, open with Serpent Sting, then Arcane Shot and auto shot. If something reaches you, Wing Clip and step back out to range; the 8 yard dead zone is where a hunter dies. Feign Death at 30 resets a bad pull. Aspect of the Cheetah at 20 is your run speed between quests, but it dazes you when you are hit, so never wear it into a fight.

Aimed Shot comes at 20 and shares its cooldown with Multi-Shot. When rank 2 arrives at 28, leave it in your spellbook and keep rank 1 on your bar all the way to 60; training a new rank upgrades the bar button on its own, so drag rank 1 back from the book. Rank 1 still hits for well over half of what the newest rank does, since most of the shot is your weapon, for far less mana, and out in the world that keeps you shooting instead of drinking over a chain of pulls. In a raid at 60, with Judgement of Wisdom on the boss and a mana potion, cast the top rank.

Arcane Shot costs 11% of your base mana at every rank, so always cast the newest one. Serpent Sting is different: every rank adds a share of your ranged attack power, so a low rank keeps most of the damage for a fraction of the mana. Drag the old rank back onto the bar after every trainer visit.

- **Until 25:** the newest Serpent Sting.
- **26 to 60:** Serpent Sting rank 1, all the way up: it keeps most of the damage for a fraction of the mana. In a raid at 60, with Judgement of Wisdom on the boss, cast the top rank.

Buy arrows in stacks before you leave town, and replace your quiver as you level. Mail at 40 is the biggest armor upgrade you will get, and Bestial Wrath comes the same level.
