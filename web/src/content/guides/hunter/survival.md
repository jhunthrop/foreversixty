## Overview

Survival is a melee spec in Forever: two weapons, Raptor Strike queued every time it comes off cooldown, Mongoose Bite whenever it lights up, and no pet.

### Strengths

- Lone Wolf's 20% damage on every attack, with no pet to keep alive
- Expose Prey opens Mongoose Bite off your own hits on a marked target, so it fires even on a boss that never swings at you
- Resourcefulness takes 60% off your melee and trap costs, so you do not run dry
- Rapid Fire speeds your melee swings as well as your shots

### Weaknesses

- You stand in melee with the rogues and warriors, so cleaves, frontal breaths and melee-range mechanics are yours to dodge
- Two weapons and a white hit cap well above a ranged hunter's make hit hard to fill

### In a raid

- **Role:** Melee DPS without a pet
- **Brings:** Heart of the Lion (more stats and attack power for everyone near you) and Hunter's Mark on the boss
- **What the raid adds:** Forever's Strength of Earth Totem gives less Strength than Era's and Curse of Recklessness strips less armor, so a full raid lifts a Survival hunter less than it did in Era.

## Talents and builds

Thirty-five points in Survival take every melee damage talent, eleven in Marksmanship take Careful Aim and Lone Wolf, and five in Beast Mastery take Deadly Aspects.

- **Lone Wolf** (1 point): 20% more damage with every attack while no pet is out. Forever's pets copy only a tenth of your attack power and get no raid buffs, so for a melee hunter the talent beats the pet
- **Expose Prey** (2 points): your hits on a target with Hunter's Mark have a 10% chance to open Mongoose Bite for 10 seconds, the only way it opens on a boss that is not attacking you
- **Lacerating Strikes** (1 point): Mongoose Bite also bleeds the target for 40% of its damage over 21 seconds
- **Predator's Edge** (5 points): 30% more crit damage on your melee specials and 50% more off-hand damage
- **Savage Strikes** (2 points): 4% crit on your melee specials
- **Lightning Reflexes** (5 points): 15% Agility
- **Deadly Aspects** (5 points): under Aspect of the Beast, every white swing has a 10% chance to speed your melee attacks by 30% for 12 seconds
- **Resourcefulness** (2 points): 60% off melee and trap costs, and your special crits keep half your mana regeneration running

Careful Aim (5 points) turns Intellect into attack power and is the way to Lone Wolf. Lethal Attacks (5 points) is 5% crit on everything. Surefooted is 3% hit, Strider Kick is a third melee special, and Clever Traps and Survivalist's Discipline turn Immolation Trap into a damage button. Counterattack comes with Deterrence on the way down. Improved Tracking (5 points) is 5% damage against the creature type you track, so track the boss's type before every pull.

The Marksmanship shot talents do nothing for you. Mortal Shots is ranged only, Hawk Eye is range on a bow you never fire, and Efficiency is wasted next to Resourcefulness. The same build is the raid build: with Judgement of Wisdom on the boss you could take Rapid Killing for a shorter Rapid Fire, but it does not beat the points it would cost.

## Rotation and priority

Dismiss your pet before the pull; Lone Wolf only works with no pet out. Put up Aspect of the Beast and leave it up. At 60, Aspect of the Falcon gives the same melee attack power as Beast (it copies your best Aspect of the Hawk, 10 more once you own Hawk's Ahn'Qiraj rank), and Deadly Aspects procs from your swings under either one, so Falcon is no upgrade for you. Before 60 Beast is the only melee aspect you have. Mark the boss with Hunter's Mark. Expose Prey needs a mark on the target, so if no other hunter has marked it, you do.

Walk in behind the boss with the rogues once the tank has it. On the pull, press Rapid Fire, your trinkets and Berserking or Blood Fury together: Rapid Fire speeds your melee swings in Forever, so it is a melee cooldown for you. Its five minute cooldown gives you one in a normal boss fight.

Then play the priority above. Raptor Strike replaces your next white hit instead of costing a global, so the only mistake is letting it sit unqueued. Mongoose Bite and Counterattack are the buttons you watch for: Expose Prey opens Mongoose Bite off your own hits, while Counterattack needs a parry, and from behind a boss you will not parry, so it is mostly an add and trash button. Keep Lacerate's bleed on the boss between Mongoose Bites; it crits like every bleed in Forever, and Strider Kick and Immolation Trap fill what is left. Wing Clip is only a filler while a Windfury Totem reaches you; in a group without a shaman, leave it alone.

You fire no shots in this spec. Your bow or gun is there for its stats.

**Threat.** You sit on a fresh tank's heels in the opener. Disengage trims some threat on a 5 second cooldown, and Feign Death drops all of it if it lands; check the message, because either can miss or be resisted. Deterrence is your emergency button when something turns on you.

**Trash.** Survival has little cleave of its own. Explosive Trap under the pack is your AoE, and Engineering's explosives are the rest. Freezing Trap is your crowd control, laid before the pull.

**Movement.** Stay on the boss's back. Every second you are not swinging is lost white damage and lost Expose Prey procs.

## Stat priority

Hit first until the special attack cap shown above. Your white swings have a much higher cap that you will not reach, and hit past the special cap still raises white damage. Then crit, which Predator's Edge and Savage Strikes make strong, and Agility, which Lightning Reflexes raises by 15%. Intellect is attack power through Careful Aim. Strength and plain attack power are the weakest per point. Haste is rare on gear but strong when you find it.

## Best in slot

Hunters wear leather until 40 and mail from 40. Survival dual-wields, and Predator's Edge's 50% off-hand bonus makes the off hand worth almost as much as the main hand. The ranged slot is a stat stick. The full lists by level are on the [Survival best in slot page](/bis/hunter/survival).

## Enchants and consumables

Agility on gloves, boots and cloak, the best stats enchant on the chest, and Intellect on bracers. Intellect shows up on a weapon in the table too: through Careful Aim it is attack power for you.

Forever's damage potions share the two-minute potion cooldown with mana potions. Survival's mana holds with Resourcefulness, so it drinks a Major Frenzy Potion on the pull instead. Keep Elixir of the Mongoose up, with the food above.

## Races

Dwarf for the Alliance: Artisan's Precision is 1% hit, which is worth more to a dual-wielder than to anyone. Night Elf's Elune's Light (10% crit for 15 seconds) lines up with Rapid Fire, and Human's Ruthless is 1% crit. Humans can be Hunters for the first time in Forever.

Troll for the Horde: Berserking is 10% attack speed for 15 seconds and stacks with Rapid Fire on your swings. Orc's Blood Fury is the other good pull cooldown.

## Professions

Engineering gives Survival the AoE it lacks: grenades and explosives on trash. Skinning and Leatherworking pay for your leveling, and Blacksmithing makes melee weapons worth carrying before raid drops.

## Leveling

Survival levels in melee. Keep a pet out while you level: Lone Wolf is one of the last points this build takes, and until then the pet holds aggro and adds damage. Dismiss it the moment you take Lone Wolf.

Open with Hunter's Mark, send the pet, then walk in and queue Raptor Strike. Press Mongoose Bite whenever it lights up and keep Lacerate on anything that will live long enough to bleed. Wing Clip a runner. Feign Death at 30 resets a bad pull. Aspect of the Cheetah at 20 is your run speed between quests, but it dazes you when you are hit, so never wear it into a fight.

Mail at 40 is the biggest armor upgrade you will get, and from 20 you can carry two weapons.
