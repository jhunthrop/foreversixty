## Overview

Survival is a melee spec in Forever: two weapons, Raptor Strike queued every time it comes off cooldown, Mongoose Bite whenever it lights up, and no pet in a raid.

### Strengths

- Lone Wolf's 20% damage on every attack, with no pet to keep alive
- Expose Prey opens Mongoose Bite off your own hits on a marked target, so it fires even on a boss that never swings at you
- Resourcefulness takes 60% off your melee and trap costs, so you do not run dry
- Rapid Fire speeds your melee swings as well as your shots

### Weaknesses

- You stand in melee with the rogues and warriors, so cleaves, frontal breaths and melee-range mechanics are yours to dodge
- Two weapons and a white hit cap well above a ranged hunter's make hit hard to fill

### In a raid

- **Role:** Melee DPS, with your pet dismissed
- **Brings:** Heart of the Lion (more stats and a flat attack power bonus for everyone near you) and Hunter's Mark on the boss
- **What the raid adds:** Forever's Strength of Earth Totem gives less Strength than Era's and Curse of Recklessness strips less armor, so a full raid lifts a Survival hunter less than it did in Era.

## Talents and builds

Thirty-five points in Survival take every melee damage talent, eleven in Marksmanship take Careful Aim and Lone Wolf, and five in Beast Mastery take Deadly Aspects.

- **Lone Wolf** (1 point): 20% more damage with every attack while no pet is out. Forever's pets copy only a tenth of your attack power and take no stat buffs from other players, so in a raid the talent beats the pet for a melee hunter. Out in the world the pet is worth a little more than the talent and takes the hits, so keep it out there; the point waits for your next raid pull
- **Expose Prey** (2 points): your hits on a target with Hunter's Mark have a 10% chance to open Mongoose Bite for 10 seconds, the only way it opens on a boss that is not attacking you
- **Lacerating Strikes** (1 point): Mongoose Bite also bleeds the target for 40% of its damage over 21 seconds
- **Predator's Edge** (5 points): your melee special crits hit for 230% of a normal hit instead of double, and your off hand does 50% more damage
- **Savage Strikes** (2 points): 4% crit on your melee specials
- **Lightning Reflexes** (5 points): 15% Agility
- **Deadly Aspects** (5 points): under Aspect of the Beast, every white swing has a 10% chance to speed your melee attacks by 30% for 12 seconds
- **Resourcefulness** (2 points): 60% off melee and trap costs, and your special crits keep half your mana regeneration running

Careful Aim (5 points) turns Intellect into attack power and is the way to Lone Wolf. Lethal Attacks (5 points) is 5% crit on everything. Surefooted is 3% hit, Strider Kick is a third melee special, and Clever Traps and Survivalist's Discipline turn Immolation Trap into a damage button. Counterattack comes with Deterrence on the way down. Improved Tracking (5 points) is 5% damage against the creature type you track. Track covers Beasts, Demons, Dragonkin, Elementals, Giants, Humanoids and Undead, which takes in most raid bosses, so set it to the boss's type before the pull whenever Track covers it.

The build skips the Marksmanship shot talents: Mortal Shots is ranged only, Hawk Eye is range on a bow you never fire, and Efficiency's 15% comes on top of Resourcefulness's 60%, so it saves little. The same build is the raid build. Rapid Killing sits on Lone Wolf's row, and Lethal Attacks and Careful Aim are exactly the ten points that open it, so its two points would have to come out of the Survival talents or Deadly Aspects, and two fewer points of Deadly Aspects cost you more than a shorter Rapid Fire gives back.

## Rotation and priority

In a raid, dismiss your pet before the pull; Lone Wolf only works with no pet out. Mark the boss with Hunter's Mark first, so the mana is back by the pull, then put up Aspect of the Beast and leave it up. At 60, Aspect of the Falcon gives the same melee attack power as Beast (10 more once you own Hawk's Ahn'Qiraj rank), and Deadly Aspects procs from your swings under either one, so Falcon is not a clear upgrade. From 30 to 59 Beast is the only melee aspect you have. Expose Prey needs a mark on the target, so if no other hunter has marked it, you do, and on a fight longer than two minutes you mark it again when it falls off.

Walk in behind the boss with the rogues once the tank has it. On the pull, press Rapid Fire, your trinkets and your racial (Elune's Light, Blood Fury or Berserking) together. Rapid Fire speeds your melee swings in Forever, so it is a melee cooldown for you. Its five minute cooldown gives you one in a normal boss fight.

Then work through your melee priority: Raptor Strike, Counterattack, Mongoose Bite, Lacerate, Strider Kick, Immolation Trap. Raptor Strike replaces your next white hit instead of costing a global, so the only mistake is letting it sit unqueued. Mongoose Bite and Counterattack are the buttons you watch for: Expose Prey opens Mongoose Bite off your own hits, while Counterattack needs a parry, and from behind a boss you will not parry, so it is mostly an add and trash button. Keep Lacerate's bleed on the boss between Mongoose Bites; it crits like every bleed in Forever, and Strider Kick and Immolation Trap fill what is left. Wing Clip is only a filler while a Windfury Totem reaches you; in a group without a shaman, leave it alone.

You fire no shots in this spec. Your bow or gun is there for its stats.

**Threat.** You sit on a fresh tank's heels in the opener. Disengage trims some threat on a 5 second cooldown, but it turns off your auto attack, so start swinging again the moment it lands. Feign Death drops all of your threat if it lands; check the message, because either can miss or be resisted. Deterrence is your emergency button when something turns on you.

**Trash.** Survival has little cleave of its own. Explosive Trap under the pack is your AoE, and Engineering's explosives are the rest. Freezing Trap is your crowd control, laid before the pull.

**Movement.** Stay on the boss's back. Every second you are not swinging is lost white damage and lost Expose Prey procs.

## Stat priority

The table above gives the order for your gear; here is why each stat counts. Hit matters up to 9%, the cap for your special attacks against a raid boss; Surefooted gives you 3% of it, so you need 6% from gear, or 5% as a Dwarf. Your white swings have a much higher cap that you will not reach, and hit past the special cap still raises white damage. Crit pays off on your melee specials: Predator's Edge makes their crits hit for 230% of a normal hit instead of double, and every special crit keeps half your mana regeneration running through Resourcefulness. Savage Strikes adds 4% crit chance to those specials on top of what your gear gives. Agility is attack power and crit together, and Lightning Reflexes raises it by 15%. Intellect is attack power through Careful Aim, and Strength is attack power directly. Plain attack power gives only itself. Haste is rare on gear but strong when you find it.

## Best in slot

Hunters wear leather until 40 and can wear mail from 40, but leather pieces stay in your best gear after it: a strong leather piece often beats the mail you can get, so your list mixes both at 60. Survival dual-wields, and Predator's Edge's 50% off-hand bonus lifts the off hand to three quarters of a main-hand swing, so it is a real weapon: pick it for damage and speed, not just stats. The ranged slot is a stat stick. The full lists by level are on the [Survival best in slot page](/bis/hunter/survival).

## Enchants and consumables

Agility on gloves, boots and cloak, since it is attack power and crit together and Lightning Reflexes raises it, and the best stats enchant on the chest, which lifts every stat you use. On bracers and both weapons the Strength, Intellect and Agility enchants are all attack power for you, Strength directly, Intellect through Careful Aim and Agility with crit on top, so take the ones the enchant table on this page picks for your gear.

Forever's damage potions share the two-minute potion cooldown with mana potions. Survival's mana holds with Resourcefulness, so it drinks a Major Frenzy Potion on the pull instead. Keep Elixir of the Mongoose up, with Poached Sunscale Salmon.

## Races

Night Elf for the Alliance: Elune's Light is 10% crit for 15 seconds, and Predator's Edge makes your special crits hit harder, so press it with Rapid Fire on the pull. Human's Ruthless is next, and Dwarf's 1% hit comes last even for a dual-wielder. Humans can be Hunters for the first time in Forever.

Orc and Troll are level for the Horde. Blood Fury is 10% attack power for 15 seconds every two minutes, so it goes on the pull with Rapid Fire and again at two and four minutes. Berserking is 10% attack speed for 15 seconds every three minutes and stacks with Rapid Fire on your swings.

Skyborne, a paid unlock with the Heroic Pack, can be Hunters on either faction: Wind Blessed's 2% haste speeds both of your weapons, and it lands in the middle of the pack, so it is the paid option, never the pick.

## Professions

Enchanting is the profession your best in slot list asks for. Molten Heart of the Mountain and Frozen Heart of the Mountain are Enchanting trinkets that bind on pickup, so nobody can make them for you. Heartseeker, the blacksmith's dagger in your off hand, binds when equipped, so buy one and do not train Blacksmithing for it. Engineering gives Survival the AoE it lacks: grenades and explosives on trash. Skinning pays for your leveling.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Train Heart of the Lion at level 1 and keep it up all the way to 60: 10% more stats and extra attack power (280 at 60) for everyone near you, and another 10% stats for you. Raptor Strike from level 1 is your main button, with new ranks at 8 and 16. Train Aspect of the Monkey and Serpent Sting at 4, Hunter's Mark at 6, Wing Clip at 12 and Mongoose Bite at 16, which lights up after you dodge.

At 10 your trainer starts the taming quests, which end by teaching Tame Beast. Tame a beast at your level, feed it to keep it happy, and train Mend Pet at 12.

Your first five points go into Improved Tracking, 1% damage per point against whatever you are tracking: Track Beasts from level 1, Track Humanoids at 10, Track Undead at 18. Keep the right one on. Savage Strikes at 15 and 16 adds 2% crit to your melee abilities per point, then Survivalist from 17 adds 2% health per point.

Take quest rewards over vendor gear, Agility first. Your melee weapon is your damage: replace the starting one with the first slow two-handed quest reward you find, since Raptor Strike adds its damage on top of the weapon hit. Wear leather until 40. From 40 you can wear mail, but keep a leather piece whenever it is the better one.

### Levels 20 to 60

Survival levels in melee. Keep a pet out while you level: Lone Wolf is the last point this build takes, at 60, and until then the pet holds aggro and adds damage. Lone Wolf pays off in a raid, where you get the raid buffs and your pet gets none, so your own hits carry more of the damage. Out in the world at 60 a pet still adds a little more damage than Lone Wolf does and takes the hits for you, so keep it out while you quest and farm. The talent does nothing while your pet is out, so dismiss the pet before a raid pull and call it back afterwards.

Open with Hunter's Mark, send the pet, then walk in and queue Raptor Strike. Press Mongoose Bite whenever it lights up, and from 30 keep Lacerate on anything that will live long enough to bleed. Wing Clip a runner. Wear Aspect of the Monkey until 30: its dodges open Mongoose Bite. At 30 switch to Aspect of the Beast and keep it up. Throw Immolation Trap under anything that will live long enough to burn. Feign Death at 30 resets a bad pull. Aspect of the Cheetah at 20 is your run speed between quests, but it dazes you when you are hit, so never wear it into a fight.

At 26 Immolation Trap rank 2 arrives, and with a trap on every pull your mana runs short. From 26 to 29 keep Raptor Strike rank 3 on your bar instead of rank 4: most of the hit is your weapon, so it does nearly what rank 4 does for less mana, and the mana it saves goes into traps. If you still run dry over a chain of pulls, rank 2 is better again at 26 and 27. Drag the lower rank onto your bar from the spellbook at 26 and put rank 4 back at 30.

Mail at 40 is the biggest armor upgrade you will get, and from 20 you can carry two weapons.

At 46 the new Immolation Trap rank costs more, and with a trap on every pull your mana starts to run short over a chain of them. Rank 6 of Raptor Strike, trained at 40, is the one to use until then. For 46 and 47, keep rank 5 on your bar instead: most of the hit is your weapon, so it does nearly what rank 6 does for less mana, and the mana it saves goes into traps. Drag rank 5 back from the spellbook as you did at 26. At 48 put rank 7 on the bar, unless your mana still runs out over a chain of pulls; then rank 5 stays the better button until 50.
