## Overview

### Strengths

- Barrage, Mortal Shots and Ranged Weapon Specialization all boost Aimed Shot and Multi-Shot, the shots on your shared cooldown
- No pet to keep alive in a raid: Lone Wolf puts 20% on everything you do instead
- Improved Tracking adds 5% damage against most raid bosses, whenever you track the boss's type
- Careful Aim turns Intellect into attack power

### Weaknesses

- One big cooldown, Rapid Fire, and it comes once in a normal boss fight
- Auto shot is the biggest share of your damage and stops whenever you move, so a fight full of movement costs you more than it costs a Beast Mastery hunter, whose pet keeps fighting

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged physical DPS
- **Brings:** Heart of the Lion (10% stats and a flat attack power bonus for everyone near you), Trueshot Aura (100 ranged attack power at 60 for your party and raid), Hunter's Mark on the boss

## Talents and builds

There are two builds. You level with the pet: thirty-one points in Marksmanship take the talents that raise your big shots and your auto shot, plus Rapid Killing for a shorter Rapid Fire and Rapid Recuperation to keep your mana coming back while you shoot; fifteen in Beast Mastery take Deadly Aspects and the pet talents, and five in Survival take Improved Tracking. At 60, for raids, the pet goes: thirty-three points in Marksmanship trade Rapid Killing and Rapid Recuperation for Lone Wolf, Sniper Shot, the last two ranks of Barrage and two points of Improved Stings, five in Beast Mastery keep Deadly Aspects, and thirteen in Survival take Improved Tracking, Survivalist and Surefooted.

- **Mortal Shots** (5 points): your shot crits hit for 230% of a normal hit instead of double
- **Barrage** (3 points in the raid build): 15% more damage on Multi-Shot, Aimed Shot and Volley, 5% with the 1 point you level with
- **Ranged Weapon Specialization** (5 points): 5% more damage on everything your bow or gun does, auto shot included
- **Careful Aim** (5 points)
- **Lethal Attacks** (5 points)
- **Efficiency** (5 points): 15% off every shot, so you reach the end of a long fight still shooting
- **Deadly Aspects** (5 points): auto shots under Aspect of the Hawk have a 10% chance to speed your ranged attacks by 30% for 12 seconds
- **Improved Tracking** (5 points): 5% more damage against the creature type you are tracking
- **Lone Wolf** (1 point, raid build): 20% more damage with every attack while no pet is out, in the raid build
- **Improved Stings** (2 points, raid build): 13% more Serpent Sting damage
- **Surefooted** (3 points, raid build): 3% hit in the raid build
- **Sniper Shot** (1 point, raid build): weapon damage plus 590 at rank 3 on a 4 second cast and its own 15 second cooldown, the hardest hit you have
- **Rapid Killing** (2 points, leveling build): two minutes off Rapid Fire, and your next shot after a kill hits 20% harder
- **Rapid Recuperation** (2 points, leveling build): after your Serpent Sting lands, half your mana regeneration keeps running while you cast for 15 seconds, all of it once Rapid Killing is spent

Improved Tracking only works while you track the target's type, and Track covers Beasts, Demons, Dragonkin, Elementals, Giants, Humanoids and Undead. Most raid bosses are one of those; the boss's tooltip names its type, and on a boss of any other type the talent does nothing. Set it before the pull, and swap on trash with mixed types.

In a raid the pet does less than Lone Wolf. Forever's pets copy only a tenth of your attack power and take no stat buffs from other players, so the 20% on everything you do is worth more than the cat with Unleashed Fury behind it. Survivalist is the way to Surefooted. While leveling, keep the pet: it holds what you pull.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** In a raid, dismiss your pet: Lone Wolf only works with no pet out. Mark the boss with Hunter's Mark first, so the mana is back by the pull, then put up Aspect of the Hawk and leave it up: under Falcon, Deadly Aspects only procs from melee swings, never from your auto shots.

**The opener.** Time Aimed Shot so it lands just after the tank's first hit. Started any earlier, it is the first threat on the boss and the boss comes for you; on a fresh tank, give him a second or two more.

**Auto Shot.** It does more damage than any button you press, so protect it:

- Stand still. Moving while an auto shot winds up cancels it.
- Fire your instants right after an auto shot leaves, so they never push the next one back.
- Stay outside 8 yards. Inside it you cannot shoot at all.
- On a movement phase, fire Arcane Shot and Multi-Shot on the run while the mana holds, and plant your feet the moment you can.

**Mana.** Keep Serpent Sting rolling and refresh it only as it runs out, and fill the gaps with Arcane Shot. Both carry your ranged attack power in Forever. When the mana runs low, Multi-Shot goes first; keep Sniper Shot and Arcane Shot going and the sting rolling to the end, since the sting is the best damage for its mana you have and Arcane Shot is next. Drink a Major Mana Potion before you need it.

**The pet.** Level with a cat and train it the top ranks of Claw, Bite and Growl as you go. In a raid with the raid build, leave it dismissed. If you raid with the leveling build instead, turn off Growl (right-click it on the pet bar) and send the cat to the boss's back.

**Threat.** Feign Death when you get close to the tank's threat, not after the boss turns. It can be resisted: if it says Resisted, you still have all your threat.

**Trash.** Multi-Shot hits three targets. Volley is a mana-heavy channel, worth it only on a big pack that lives through it. Lay Freezing Trap before the pull. Explosive Trap under a pack the tank is holding hits everything within 10 yards, and at 60 it costs even more than Volley, so save it for the same big packs.

**Utility.** Tranquilizing Shot (bought from your trainer at 60, so you do not wait on Lucifron's tome) removes a boss's frenzy, and on a frenzying boss it is a raid job that someone is assigned to: if it is you, it comes before any damage. Distracting Shot puts a big lump of threat on a loose add, enough to pull it off a healer and onto you. Bring it to your tank and let him take it from you. Freezing Trap takes care of anything loose in the back line.

## Stat priority

Hit matters up to 9%, the cap against a raid boss, auto shots included. Surefooted and good raid gear take you there, and past the cap hit adds nothing; if your character sheet is still short of it, hit matters until you reach it. Mortal Shots makes your ranged crits hit harder. Intellect is attack power through Careful Aim and mana on top. Plain attack power counts for a little more than it did in Classic, because Arcane Shot and Serpent Sting carry a share of it.

## Best in slot

A strong leather piece often beats the mail you can get, so your list mixes both at 60. Your ranged weapon is your real weapon, the melee weapon is there for its stats.

## Enchants and consumables

Put a scope on the ranged weapon: Sniper Scope from Engineering, and Biznicks 247x128 Accurascope once your raid's engineer can make it. Agility on gloves, boots and cloak, the best stats enchant on the chest, and Intellect on bracers: no bracer enchant carries real Agility, Strength does nothing for your shots, and Careful Aim makes Intellect count as attack power.

**Ammo and quiver.** Carry the best ammo your level can use and plenty of it: arrows for a bow, bullets for a gun. Jagged Arrows from 40, Ice Threaded and Thorium Headed Arrows from the early 50s, or Thorium Shells from an engineer. The quiver or ammo pouch is a free 10 to 15% ranged attack speed, so upgrade it every time you can: Quickdraw Quiver from 40 and a 15% bag near 60. Run out of ammo in a raid and you do almost nothing.

Forever's damage potions share the two-minute potion cooldown with mana potions, so a Marksmanship hunter drinks Major Mana Potions. Keep Elixir of the Mongoose up, with Poached Sunscale Salmon and Ground Scorpok Assay alongside it.

## Races

Night Elf for the Alliance: Elune's Light lines up with Rapid Fire on the pull. Human's Ruthless is next, and Dwarf comes last.

Troll for the Horde: Berserking stacks with Rapid Fire on the pull, and more auto shots is what Marksmanship lives on. Orc is close behind, but Command does nothing for you with the pet dismissed.

## Professions

Your raid set asks for Enchanting. The Heart of the Mountain trinkets are Enchanting crafts that bind on pickup, so nobody can make them for you. Legionite Glaive is a Blacksmithing polearm that also binds on pickup. If you are not a blacksmith, take the next two-hander on your list. Every other craft in your set binds when equipped, the Engineering rifle and the Leatherworking mail included, so buy them. Engineering brings Thorium Grenades for trash and the explosives that save a bad pull. Skinning pays for your leveling.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Train Heart of the Lion at level 1 and keep it up all the way to 60: 10% more stats and extra attack power (280 at 60) for everyone near you, and another 10% stats for you. Raptor Strike and Auto Shot carry the first levels. Train Serpent Sting and Aspect of the Monkey at 4, Arcane Shot and Hunter's Mark at 6, Concussive Shot at 8, Aspect of the Hawk at 10, which replaces Monkey for good, and Wing Clip at 12.

Multi-Shot comes at 18. It is the worst damage for its mana you fire, so once you are under a quarter of your mana, hold it, and from 20 Aimed Shot takes the shared cooldown when you can afford it. Keep Serpent Sting rolling and Arcane Shot firing on cooldown even then, without waiting on the shared cooldown.

At 10 your trainer starts the taming quests, which end by teaching Tame Beast. Tame a beast at your level, feed it to keep it happy, and train Mend Pet at 12.

Your first five points go into Lethal Attacks, 1% crit on every attack per point. Careful Aim from 15 turns 20% of your Intellect into attack power a point, and Efficiency from 20 cuts the mana cost of your shots and stings by 3% a point, which is fewer drinking breaks.

Take quest rewards over vendor gear, Agility first. Replace your starting bow or gun with the first ranged quest reward you find: your ranged weapon is most of your damage. Wear leather until 40. From 40 you can wear mail, but keep a leather piece whenever it is the better one.

### Levels 20 to 60

Marksmanship levels well, a little slower than Beast Mastery because more of the work is yours. Keep a pet out all the way: it holds what you pull while you shoot. Switch to the raid build and dismiss the pet when you start raiding at 60.

- **25 and 26:** Rapid Killing takes two minutes off Rapid Fire, which you train at 26, so you have it for most hard pulls.
- **33 and 34:** Rapid Recuperation keeps your mana coming back while you shoot, once your Serpent Sting is on the target.
- **From 41:** Deadly Aspects, then Focused Fire at 46 and 47, ahead of the Endurance Training points that only open Unleashed Fury.

Mark the target and send the pet in first; once it has hit and Growled, open with Serpent Sting, then Arcane Shot and auto shot. If something reaches you, Wing Clip and step back out of the 8 yard dead zone. Feign Death at 30 resets a bad pull. Aspect of the Cheetah at 20 is your run speed between quests, but it dazes you when you are hit, so never wear it into a fight.

Aimed Shot comes at 20 and shares its cooldown with Multi-Shot. Out in the world, keep rank 1 on your bar all the way to 60: training a new rank upgrades the bar button on its own, so drag rank 1 back from the book each time, at 28, 36, 44, 52 and 60. Rank 1 still hits for most of what the newer ranks do, because most of the shot is your weapon, for a fraction of the mana, and since you fire it every time the shared cooldown comes up, the expensive ranks drain you fastest of all over a chain of pulls. In a raid at 60, with Judgement of Wisdom on the boss and a mana potion, cast the top rank.

Arcane Shot costs 11% of your base mana at every rank, so always cast the newest one. Serpent Sting is different: every rank adds a share of your ranged attack power, so a low rank keeps most of the damage for a fraction of the mana. Drag the old rank back onto the bar after every trainer visit.

- **Until 25:** the newest Serpent Sting.
- **26 to 32:** Serpent Sting rank 1.
- **33 to 41:** the newest Serpent Sting. Rapid Recuperation is in by 34 and keeps your mana up.
- **42 to 49:** Serpent Sting rank 3. From 42 the new ranks cost more than Rapid Recuperation gives back.
- **50 to 59:** Serpent Sting rank 4.
- **60, out in the world:** rank 1 again. In a raid, with Judgement of Wisdom on the boss, cast the top rank.

Buy arrows in stacks before you leave town, and replace your quiver as you level. Mail at 40 is the biggest armor upgrade you will get.
