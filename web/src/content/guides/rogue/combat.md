## Overview

Combat is the steadiest rogue in a raid: a dagger in each hand, Backstab from behind, Eviscerate at five points and Adrenaline Rush with Blade Flurry for the burst.

### Strengths

- The most forgiving raid rotation of the three trees: one builder, two finishers
- Adrenaline Rush doubles your energy regeneration for 15 seconds, and Blade Flurry cleaves every swing onto a second target
- Weapon Expertise and Precision cover dodge and hit inside the tree
- Plays the same on every boss: stay behind, keep Slice and Dice up, spend at five

### Weaknesses

- Wants daggers at 60, so Backstab means standing behind the boss every second
- Burst lives on two long cooldowns: a fight that needs damage in the last minute may find them still recharging
- Little crowd control or stealth play in the raid build

### In a raid

- **Role:** Sustained single-target melee damage, with the best two-target cleave a rogue has
- **Brings:** Kicks, Kidney Shots on trash and its own damage; no raid buff

## Talents and builds

The raid build goes 12/34/5: Combat to Adrenaline Rush with all five points of Flash of Steel, Assassination for Malice, Ruthlessness, Improved Slice and Dice and Lethality, and five points of Opportunity for Backstab. The level 60 build is the one you level and run dungeons with: 14/34/3: Improved Gouge and Remorseless Attacks in place of Lethality and two points of Improved Slice and Dice, Master of Deception in place of Opportunity, a third point of Ruthlessness, and the fifth points of Hack and Slash and Lightning Reflexes in place of the fifth Flash of Steel and the second Efficient Strikes. That pays off on trash and on the way up, not on a boss. Respec to the raid build for the raid.

- **Adrenaline Rush** (1 point): double energy regeneration for 15 seconds on a 5 minute cooldown. Every Backstab you fit into it is a free one.
- **Blade Flurry** (1 point): 20% attack speed for 15 seconds, and every melee hit also strikes a second nearby enemy. Two-target fights are where Combat beats the other trees.
- **Hack and Slash** (5 points, 4 in the raid build): Forever folds the old weapon specializations into one talent. At five points daggers and fists get 5% crit, swords and axes a 5% chance at an extra attack, maces ignore 15% of armor.
- **Flash of Steel** (5 points, 4 in the level 60 build): Shank gives 15 more energy and comes back 2 seconds sooner. At five points that is 40 energy for 5 every 8 seconds, the best energy in the tree.
- **Weapon Expertise** (2 points): 2% less chance to be dodged or parried. Every dodged Backstab is a wasted global and a combo point you did not get.
- **Dual Wield Specialization** (2 points): 50% more off-hand damage on every off-hand swing.
- **Puncturing Wounds** (3 points): 30% more crit on Backstab and a 45% chance at a second combo point. This is what makes daggers the Combat weapon in Forever.
- **Coup de Grace** (3 points): Eviscerate hits 20% harder.

In the raid build, Lethality takes Backstab crits from 200% to 220%, and with Puncturing Wounds and Hack and Slash more than half your Backstabs crit. Opportunity adds 20% to Backstab. Aggression adds 15% to Eviscerate and Sinister Strike, not to Backstab, so on daggers it is a finisher talent. Flawless Execution takes 15 energy off Eviscerate, and Ruthlessness and Improved Slice and Dice feed the finishers. Efficient Strikes takes energy off Sinister Strike, 3 at one point and 5 at two: your builder's discount in the level 60 build. In the raid build its two points and four of Lightning Reflexes are the toll to reach Flawless Execution, Weapon Expertise and Aggression.

## Rotation and priority

### The pull

Check your poisons and stones before the pull. Stealth before the pull, walk in behind the boss, and open with **Garrote** once the tank has the boss. Garrote is a bleed, so the boss having no Sunders on it yet costs it nothing, and it gives you the combo point for Slice and Dice. Ambush and Backstab from stealth both hit into armor that has no shred on it yet, and neither leaves a bleed behind. Cheap Shot is for trash: bosses are immune to stuns.

Every rogue picks up **Poised** at 18: at full energy out of combat, your next ability costs less. Stand at full energy in stealth before you open.

### The priority

**Shank** on cooldown, right behind your racial and trinket: an off-hand stab for 5 energy that cannot miss and gives 40 back with Flash of Steel. Keep Slice and Dice up with one or two combo points every time it drops, Eviscerate at five, and Backstab in between. Backstab needs a dagger in the main hand; with any other weapon there, Sinister Strike is your builder. Do not refresh Slice and Dice early with five points. Let it fall, put one or two points back into it and keep five-point sets for Eviscerate. Rupture is not worth a global on a boss.

### Energy

Backstab costs 60 and you are energy-limited all fight, so you spend the moment you can and wait on energy rather than clip anything. Never sit at full energy: anything over the cap is lost. Press **Adrenaline Rush** low on energy, never at the cap: on the pull, Garrote and one Backstab first, then Adrenaline Rush. Blade Flurry costs 25 and can go at any energy. Shank is energy too: never let it sit ready, and press it before Backstab when both are up. Before an add you know is coming, let energy climb a little so the first cleaved swings have Backstabs behind them.

**Thistle Tea** gives 100 energy on a 5 minute cooldown. Drink it when you are near empty, so none of the 100 is lost to the cap.

### Cooldowns and adds

On a single target, Adrenaline Rush goes a few seconds into the pull with Blade Flurry, your racial, a trinket and a potion. On a 3 minute kill you get two Blade Flurries and one Adrenaline Rush, so do not hold them. If a fight brings adds in the first minute, hold Blade Flurry for them and keep Adrenaline Rush with it.

Blade Flurry hits anything next to your target. Never turn it on next to a Sapped, Polymorphed, Shackled or Feared mob: one cleave breaks it and the pull goes wrong. Cancel the buff if you have to.

### Trash, threat and survival

- **Kidney Shot** costs 25 energy, sits on a 20 second cooldown and stuns longer per combo point, up to 6 seconds at five. Use it on casters and on the mob beating on your healer. **Kick** stops a cast for 25 energy. **Gouge** buys a few seconds on a loose add.
- **Redirect** (trained at level 1) lifts your combo points off a target and hands them to the next enemy you hit within 15 seconds. When a trash mob is about to die with points on it, Redirect and finish the next one.
- **Feint** on cooldown on any boss where you sit near the tank's threat. **Vanish** drops you from the threat table entirely, but a bleed or DoT ticking on you puts you straight back in combat, so make it your emergency button.
- **Evasion** does nothing against spells. Pop it when a melee trash mob turns on you and walk it back to the tank.

## Stat priority

Hit counts until your attacks stop missing, and adds nothing past that: a missed Backstab is a wasted global and a lost combo point, Precision only gets you part of the way, and your white swings with two weapons miss far more often than your specials. Agility gives attack power and crit together. Strength and attack power add straight damage to every swing. A crit makes one hit bigger, and Lethality makes your Backstab crits bigger still, but Combat has no Seal Fate, so a crit never adds a combo point, and no bleed of yours runs all fight for a crit to tick on. Haste is rare on gear and good when you find it. The table above gives the order for your gear.

## Best in slot

Combat wants **daggers at 60**. Hack and Slash gives daggers crit, and Backstab, Puncturing Wounds, Lethality and Opportunity stack on top of that, so daggers beat swords and axes in Forever. Keep a sword or axe for leveling and for any fight where you cannot stay behind the target. The off hand only needs to be fast and hit hard; Dual Wield Specialization makes it worth a real upgrade.

## Enchants and consumables

Forever lets a sharpening stone sit on a weapon beside a poison, so use both: **Deadly Poison** on the main hand, **Instant Poison** on the off hand, and a stone on each hand. Deadly Poison on the main hand stacks fast off your Backstabs and stays up; Instant Poison goes on the off hand. Two Instants lose to that pair, and so does the same pair the other way round. A shaman's Windfury Totem is an aura on you, not a weapon enchant, so it stacks with poisons and stones.

## Races

Gnome on Alliance and Undead on Horde. **Eureka!** makes your non-periodic damaging abilities cost 10% less and deal 10% more for 8 seconds every 2 minutes, and **Expansive Mind** gives you 5% more energy. Undead's **Touch of the Grave** gives your attacks a chance to drain up to 5% of your maximum health from the target, at most once every 15 seconds, and that free damage puts it first for Horde. Orc, Troll and Night Elf all land a step behind and within a hair of each other. Forever has no weapon-skill racials, so a Human gets nothing extra from swords.

## Professions

**Enchanting** is the one that changes your gear: Molten Heart of the Mountain and Frozen Heart of the Mountain are bind-on-pickup trinkets only an enchanter can make. **Engineering** brings grenades and dynamite for trash. **Skinning** and **Leatherworking** pay for themselves, since Devilsaur and the Stormshroud pieces are leatherworking crafts. Heartseeker is a blacksmith's dagger that binds when equipped: when it is on your list, buy one, do not train the profession for it.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Sinister Strike, Eviscerate and Stealth are there from level 1. Gouge trains at 6, Evasion at 8, Slice and Dice and Sprint at 10, Kick at 12, Garrote at 14 and Ambush at 18. Slice and Dice at 10 is the big one: from then on, a short one on anything that will live, Eviscerate on the rest.

Poised at 18 takes 10 Energy off your first ability when you start a pull at full Energy out of combat, so never pull half empty.

Your points from 10 to 12 go into Coup de Grace for 20% more Eviscerate damage. Efficient Strikes at 13 takes 3 Energy off Sinister Strike, Lightning Reflexes from 14 to 18 gives you 5% dodge, and Puncturing Wounds from 19 only pays off when you have a dagger in the main hand and get behind the mob for Backstab.

Wear leather and take quest rewards over vendor gear. Replace the Worn Dagger with the first quest sword, axe or mace that beats it, and keep throwing knives for pulling.

### Levels 20 to 60

Level as Combat with a sword, axe or mace and Sinister Strike: mobs face you while you level, and Backstab needs you behind the target. Open with Cheap Shot or Garrote from stealth, Slice and Dice at one or two points on anything that will live long enough, Eviscerate at five, and Gouge to step behind when you have a dagger in the main hand. Shank comes at 24: press it on cooldown from then on. Aggression by 30 makes every Sinister Strike hit harder. Improved Gouge and Remorseless Attacks come in the mid 40s and speed up the Gouge trick and back-to-back pulls from there. Poisons arrive at 20 with Instant Poison and at 30 with Deadly Poison. Sprint, Evasion and Vanish get you out of the pulls that go wrong.
