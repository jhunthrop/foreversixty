## Overview

Assassination is the Forever rogue built on Mutilate: two daggers, two combo points a strike, Seal Fate crits for a third and a Cold Blood Eviscerate whenever it is up.

### Strengths

- Mutilate has no facing requirement, so you never lose damage chasing a boss's back
- Combo points come fast: two a Mutilate, three on a Seal Fate crit
- Cold Blood turns an Eviscerate into a guaranteed crit every three minutes
- Hard-hitting poisons with Vile Poisons and Improved Poisons behind them

### Weaknesses

- Needs a dagger in both hands; one sword or mace and Mutilate will not cast
- Little cleave: no Blade Flurry, and poisons only land on what you hit
- Mutilate's bonus needs your poison on the target, so a cleanse or immunity hurts more than it does Combat

### In a raid

- **Role:** Single-target melee damage
- **Brings:** Kicks, Kidney Shots on trash and its own damage; no raid buff

## Talents and builds

The raid build goes 31/18/2: down Assassination to Mutilate and all five points of Seal Fate, into Combat for Precision, Puncturing Wounds and Flawless Execution, and two points of Opportunity. The level 60 build is the one you level with. It keeps a single point of Seal Fate and spends the rest on Improved Gouge, Remorseless Attacks and Vile Poisons, which shine on trash and while leveling. Respec to the raid build for the raid.

- **Mutilate** (1 point): strikes with both daggers for 75% weapon damage plus a flat bonus each, 20% more against a poisoned target, for two combo points.
- **Seal Fate** (5 points in the raid build): a crit from a builder adds a combo point. Mutilate can only trigger it once a cast, even when both hands crit.
- **Cold Blood** (1 point): your next Mutilate or Eviscerate is a guaranteed crit, on a 3 minute cooldown. Save it for an Eviscerate at four or more points.
- **Lethality** (5 points, 3 in the raid build): Mutilate crits hit up to 20% harder, and with Malice and Puncturing Wounds you crit a lot.
- **Vile Poisons** (5 points, 2 in the raid build): up to 20% more poison damage.
- **Improved Poisons** (5 points): poisons apply more often and often keep their charge.

Murder only works on Humanoid and Giant targets: check the boss's type before you count on it, and enjoy it on every humanoid trash pull. Ruthlessness and Relentless Strikes keep finishers flowing. Improved Sinister Strike and Lightning Reflexes in the raid build are there to reach Flawless Execution, not for what they do.

**Venom** is the new Assassination finisher: more poison damage and more poison procs for a few seconds per combo point. It is a trap on a boss. Every combo point you spend on Venom is one not spent on Slice and Dice or Eviscerate, and poisons are too small a share of your damage for a poison buff to pay that back. Skip the talent and never press it on a boss.

## Rotation and priority

### The pull

Stealth, get behind the boss and open with **Garrote** once the tank has the boss. The boss has no Sunders on it yet at the pull, and a bleed does not care. Ambush hits harder on paper, but it lands into that armor and gets none of Lethality's bonus. Cheap Shot is for trash only: bosses are immune to stuns.

Every rogue learns **Poised** at 18: at full energy out of combat, your next ability costs less. Open from full energy.

### The priority

At four or more combo points, Cold Blood if it is ready, then the finisher: Slice and Dice when it is down or about to fall off, Eviscerate otherwise. Mutilate builds. Cold Blood is off the global cooldown and costs nothing, so it never delays the Eviscerate.

Spend at four, not five. Mutilate gives two points and a Seal Fate crit gives a third, so you go 0, 2 or 3, then 4 to 6: waiting for exactly five wastes a Mutilate. Rupture and Expose Armor do not make the list. Forever's Expose Armor does not stack with Sunder, so leave it to your warriors.

### Energy

Mutilate costs 60, so you will often sit on four points waiting for energy. That is fine: finish first, then build. Do not cap. **Thistle Tea** gives 100 energy on a 5 minute cooldown; drink it when you are empty, ideally right before Cold Blood comes up. Line up Cold Blood with your racial, trinket and potion.

### Poisons

**Deadly Poison** on the main hand, **Instant Poison** on the off hand, with a sharpening stone beside each in Forever. Mutilate's bonus needs a poison sitting on the target, and Deadly Poison is the one that stays there, so never run two Instants. Refresh poisons before every pull; a Mutilate into an unpoisoned boss is a weak one.

### Trash, threat and survival

- **Kidney Shot** costs 25 energy, sits on a 20 second cooldown and stuns longer per combo point, up to 6 seconds at five: use it on casters and on whatever is hitting your healer. Kick, Gouge and Blind handle the rest.
- **Redirect** (trained at level 1) lifts your combo points off a dying mob and hands them to the next enemy you hit within 15 seconds.
- **Feint** on cooldown on bosses where you sit near the tank's threat. **Vanish** drops you from the threat table, but a bleed or DoT on you puts you back in combat, so keep it for emergencies.
- **Evasion** is for the melee trash mob that turns on you. It does nothing against spells.

## Stat priority

Hit first: a missed Mutilate is a wasted global and two combo points you did not get. Agility next, for attack power and crit together. Crit is worth more to you than to most melee, since every builder crit is a Seal Fate point and Lethality makes it bigger.

## Best in slot

Two daggers, always. The main-hand dagger matters most: it carries Deadly Poison and the bigger half of every Mutilate. Mutilate swings both, though, so the off-hand dagger is a real upgrade too.

## Enchants and consumables

Poisons and stones go on together in Forever: Deadly Poison and a stone on the main hand, Instant Poison and a stone on the off hand. A shaman's Windfury Totem is an aura on you, so it stacks with both.

## Races

Night Elf on Alliance and Troll on Horde. **Elune's Light** gives 10% crit for 15 seconds, which pairs with Seal Fate and Cold Blood; **Berserking** gives 10% attack speed and more poison procs. Orc's Blood Fury is close behind for Horde. Human's passive 1% crit is the Alliance fallback; Elune's Light beats it for raiding. Forever has no weapon-skill racials.

## Professions

**Enchanting** makes Molten Heart of the Mountain and Frozen Heart of the Mountain, bind-on-pickup trinkets only an enchanter can wear, and both are pre-raid picks. **Engineering** gives you grenades and dynamite for trash. **Skinning** and **Leatherworking** cover the Devilsaur and Stormshroud crafts.

## Leveling

Level Combat or Assassination with Sinister Strike until 30. Once Mutilate comes at 30, Assassination levels well: it has no facing requirement, so two daggers work on mobs that face you, and two points a strike means an Eviscerate every few seconds. Open with Cheap Shot, which gives two combo points, or Garrote. Instant Poison comes at 20, Deadly Poison at 30. Remorseless Attacks and Improved Gouge make chain pulls quicker; Sprint, Evasion and Vanish get you out of the bad ones.
