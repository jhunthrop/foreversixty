## Overview

Assassination is the Forever rogue built on Mutilate: two daggers, two combo points a strike, Seal Fate crits for a third and a Cold Blood Eviscerate whenever it is up.

### Strengths

- Mutilate has no facing requirement, so you never lose damage chasing a boss's back
- Combo points come fast: two a Mutilate, three on a Seal Fate crit
- Cold Blood turns an Eviscerate into a guaranteed crit every three minutes
- Hard-hitting poisons with Vile Poisons and Improved Poisons behind them

### Weaknesses

- Needs a dagger in both hands; one sword or mace and Mutilate will not cast
- Little cleave: no Blade Flurry, and poisons only land on what you hit
- Mutilate's bonus needs your poison on the target, so a cleanse or immunity hurts more than it does Combat

### In a raid

- **Role:** Single-target melee damage
- **Brings:** Kicks, Kidney Shots on trash and its own damage; no raid buff

## Talents and builds

The raid build goes 36/10/5: down Assassination to Mutilate, with Seal Fate, Venom and all five points of Relentless Strikes on the way, into Combat for Coup de Grace, Puncturing Wounds and Precision, and five points of Opportunity. The level 60 build is the one you level with. It trades Vigor, Blood Splatter, a point of Improved Slice and Dice and four points of Opportunity for Improved Gouge, Remorseless Attacks, a third point of Precision and a point of Master of Deception, which shine on trash and while leveling. Respec to the raid build for the raid.

- **Mutilate** (1 point): strikes with both daggers for 90% weapon damage plus 79 each at the top rank (the off-hand strike takes the usual half weapon damage, the flat 79 does not), 20% more against a poisoned target, for two combo points. It sits below Venom in the tree.
- **Seal Fate** (3 points): every crit from a builder adds a combo point. Mutilate can only trigger it once a cast, even when both hands crit.
- **Venom** (1 point): every Shank adds 30% to your poison damage, on top of Vile Poisons, and 20 percentage points to each poison's chance to land for 6 seconds, so Instant Poison procs on half your off-hand hits. It is a passive on Shank, with no finisher or global of its own, and Mutilate needs it.
- **Relentless Strikes** (5 points): every finisher has a 20% chance per combo point to give 25 energy back, so a four-point Eviscerate refunds it four times in five. This is what lets a 60 energy builder keep up.
- **Cold Blood** (1 point): your next Mutilate or Eviscerate is a guaranteed crit, on a 3 minute cooldown. Save it for an Eviscerate at four or more points.
- **Lethality** (2 points): Mutilate crits deal 220% instead of 200%, and with Malice and Puncturing Wounds you crit a lot.
- **Vile Poisons** (5 points): 20% more poison damage.
- **Improved Poisons** (5 points): poisons apply more often and often keep their charge.
- **Coup de Grace** (3 points): Eviscerate hits 20% harder.

Opportunity in the raid build adds 20% to Mutilate and Garrote. Blood Splatter keeps your opening Garrote ticking 9 seconds longer and every Rupture 6 seconds longer, which is what puts Rupture in your raid rotation, and the point of Vigor is 5 more energy to pool into. Ruthlessness keeps finishers flowing. Two points of Lightning Reflexes with Coup de Grace are the toll to reach Puncturing Wounds and Precision.

## Rotation and priority

### The pull

Stealth, get behind the boss and open with **Garrote** once the tank has the boss. The boss has no Sunders on it yet at the pull, and a bleed does not care. Ambush hits harder on paper, but it lands into that armor and gets none of Lethality's bonus. Cheap Shot is for trash only: bosses are immune to stuns.

Every rogue learns **Poised** at 18: at full energy out of combat, your next ability costs less. Open from full energy.

### The priority

**Shank** on cooldown, right behind your racial and trinket: 5 energy for 25 back, it cannot miss, and with Venom, for the next 6 seconds your poisons get another 30% on top of Vile Poisons and Instant Poison lands on half your off-hand hits. At four or more combo points, Slice and Dice first if it is down or about to fall off. In the raid build, Rupture next whenever it is not ticking: Blood Splatter makes it last 6 seconds longer, and its ticks can crit in Forever. Otherwise press Cold Blood if it is ready and Eviscerate straight after. Mutilate builds. Cold Blood is off the global cooldown and costs nothing, so it never delays the Eviscerate.

Spend at four, not five. Mutilate gives two points and a Seal Fate crit gives a third, and Ruthlessness often leaves you one after a finisher, so two Mutilates usually put you on four or five: waiting for exactly five wastes a Mutilate. Expose Armor does not make the list: Forever's Expose Armor does not stack with Sunder, so leave it to your warriors. Without Blood Splatter, in the level 60 build, skip Rupture too.

### Energy

Mutilate costs 60, so you will often sit on four points waiting for energy. That is fine: finish first, then build, and Relentless Strikes will hand most of the finisher's cost back. Never let Shank sit ready; it is the cheapest energy you have. Do not cap. **Thistle Tea** gives 100 energy on a 5 minute cooldown; drink it when you are empty. Line up Cold Blood with your racial, trinket and potion.

### Poisons

**Deadly Poison** on the main hand, **Instant Poison** on the off hand, with a sharpening stone beside each in Forever. Mutilate's bonus needs a poison sitting on the target, and Deadly Poison is the one that stays there, so never run two Instants. Check both coats before every boss pull and re-apply any that is close to running out; a Mutilate into an unpoisoned boss is a weak one.

### Trash, threat and survival

- **Kidney Shot** costs 25 energy, sits on a 20 second cooldown and stuns longer per combo point, up to 6 seconds at five: use it on casters and on whatever is hitting your healer. Kick, Gouge and Blind handle the rest.
- **Redirect** (trained at level 1) lifts your combo points off a dying mob and hands them to the next enemy you hit within 15 seconds.
- **Feint** on cooldown on bosses where you sit near the tank's threat. **Vanish** drops you from the threat table, but a bleed or DoT on you puts you back in combat, so keep it for emergencies.
- **Evasion** is for the melee trash mob that turns on you. It does nothing against spells.

## Stat priority

Hit counts until your attacks stop missing, and adds nothing past that: a missed Mutilate is a wasted global and two combo points you did not get, and your white swings with two weapons miss far more often than your specials, so they keep wanting hit after Mutilate stops missing. Agility gives attack power and crit together. Crit counts for more in this tree than for most melee, since every builder crit is a Seal Fate point and Lethality makes it bigger. Strength and attack power add straight damage to every hit. Haste is rare on gear; take it when it comes. The table above gives the order for your gear.

## Best in slot

Two daggers, always. The main-hand dagger matters most: it carries Deadly Poison, your full-weight white hits and the bigger half of every Mutilate, because the off-hand strike only gets half of its dagger's weapon damage. The flat bonus still lands in full on both hands, so a good off-hand dagger is a real upgrade, just never ahead of the main hand.

## Enchants and consumables

Poisons and stones go on together in Forever: Deadly Poison and a stone on the main hand, Instant Poison and a stone on the off hand. A shaman's Windfury Totem is an aura on you, so it stacks with both.

## Races

Undead on Horde and Night Elf on Alliance. **Touch of the Grave** gives your attacks a chance to drain up to 5% of your maximum health from the target, at most once every 15 seconds: free damage all fight, and nothing else a Horde rogue gets matches it. Orc's **Blood Fury** is next and Troll's **Berserking** last. On Alliance, Night Elf's **Elune's Light** (10% crit for 15 seconds every 3 minutes, which pairs with Seal Fate: more Mutilate crits, more third points), Dwarf's passive 1% hit and Human's passive 1% crit all land in the same place, so Night Elf wins only by a hair: play the one you like. Forever has no weapon-skill racials.

## Professions

**Enchanting** makes Molten Heart of the Mountain and Frozen Heart of the Mountain, bind-on-pickup trinkets only an enchanter can make; your best in slot table shows which of them your set wants. **Engineering** gives you grenades and dynamite for trash. **Skinning** and **Leatherworking** cover the Devilsaur and Stormshroud crafts.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

You start with Sinister Strike, Eviscerate and Stealth. Backstab trains at 4, Gouge at 6, Evasion at 8, then Slice and Dice and Sprint at 10, Garrote at 14 and Ambush at 18. With a dagger in hand, Gouge, step behind and Backstab: it is the hardest hit you have this early.

Poised also comes at 18: at full Energy out of combat your next ability costs 10 less, so walk into every pull capped and open with Ambush or Garrote.

Your first three points, 10 to 12, go into Improved Gouge, which holds the mob 1.5 seconds longer and gives you time to get behind it. Remorseless Attacks at 13 and 14 adds 40% crit to your next Sinister Strike, Backstab or Ambush after a kill, then Malice from 15 to 19 gives you 5% crit.

Wear leather and take quest rewards over vendor gear. Drop the Worn Dagger the moment a quest offers a better dagger, so Backstab and Ambush stay open to you, and keep throwing knives for pulling.

### Levels 20 to 60

From 20 to 40, Sinister Strike is your everyday builder on mobs that face you, with Gouge into Backstab whenever you can get behind. Shank comes at 24: press it on cooldown from then on. Once Mutilate comes at 40, Assassination levels well: it has no facing requirement, so two daggers work on mobs that face you, and two points a strike means an Eviscerate every few seconds. Open with Cheap Shot, which gives two combo points, or Garrote. Instant Poison comes at 20, Deadly Poison at 30, and Venom at 30 makes every Shank feed them. Relentless Strikes comes late, at 39 to 44, and from then on most finishers hand their energy back. Remorseless Attacks and Improved Gouge make chain pulls quicker; Sprint, Evasion and Vanish get you out of the bad ones.
