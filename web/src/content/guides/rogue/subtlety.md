## Overview

Subtlety is the bleed rogue in Forever: a Premeditation opener, Hemorrhage and Boot Knife Shanks to build, Rupture as the main finisher and Death Mark to cash the bleeds in.

### Strengths

- No facing requirement anywhere in the level 60 rotation: Hemorrhage, Ghostly Strike and Rupture all work from the front
- A four-point opener from stealth with Premeditation and Initiative
- Bleeds ignore armor, so Rupture is full value from the first second of the pull
- The best rogue for PvP and world play: Preparation, a short Vanish and Blind with Elusiveness

### Weaknesses

- Bleeds carry its damage, so a boss that cannot bleed takes Garrote and Rupture off your bar and leaves every mark with less to store
- More moving parts than Combat: a debuff, two bleeds, a mark and three short cooldowns to track
- Its strongest tools (Vanish and Blind) do little on a raid boss, and Preparation is worth only one extra round of combo points there

### In a raid

- **Role:** Single-target melee damage, at its best on bosses that make you move
- **Brings:** Kicks, Kidney Shots on trash and its own damage; no raid buff

## Talents and builds

One build does everything: 12 in Assassination for Malice, Ruthlessness, Improved Slice and Dice, Blood Splatter and a point of Lethality, 8 in Combat for Coup de Grace, Efficient Strikes and Precision, then 31 down Subtlety to Death Mark.

- **Hemorrhage** (1 point): your builder. 145% weapon damage with a dagger, one combo point, and the target takes 15% more damage from your Rupture and Garrote.
- **Serrated Blades** (3 points): ignore 9% of the target's armor, and Rupture deals 30% more.
- **Boot Knife** (5 points): every Shank adds two combo points. Shank is 5 energy for 25 back on a 10 second cooldown, so this is two free combo points every 10 seconds.
- **Death Mark** (1 point): a 25 energy finisher on a 10 second cooldown. It marks the target for 6 seconds and stores 12% per combo point of the damage you deal it, Rupture and Garrote at twice that, then deals it all at once, up to a cap set by your attack power and crit.
- **Coup de Grace** (3 points): Death Mark stores 20% more and its cap rises 20%, and Eviscerate hits 20% harder.
- **Ghostly Strike** (1 point): 180% weapon damage with a dagger and 15% dodge for 7 seconds, on a 20 second cooldown. A Ghostly Strike that lands also lets your next Ambush or Garrote within 7 seconds go in without Stealth.
- **Premeditation** (1 point): two combo points on a 2 minute cooldown. In Forever it needs no Stealth, so it opens the fight and comes back in it.
- **Initiative** (3 points): Garrote, Ambush and Cheap Shot add a combo point.

Opportunity (5 points) adds 20% to Garrote, and to Ambush and Backstab when you hold a dagger. Blood Splatter keeps Rupture up 6 seconds longer and every Garrote 9, which is more bleed for every mark to store. Efficient Strikes takes 5 energy off Hemorrhage. Both points of Dirty Deeds take 20 energy off Garrote and Cheap Shot, on the opener and on every Garrote after a Ghostly Strike, and let Garrote go in from the front. Elusiveness, Camouflage and Master of Deception are for the world and PvP, and they are what gets you down the tree. Improved Ambush is skipped: Ambush is not your boss opener.

## Rotation and priority

### The pull

Stealth. A couple of seconds before the pull, press **Premeditation** for two combo points; they last 20 seconds, so do not press it early. It needs no Stealth in Forever, so press it again every 2 minutes in the fight whenever you have three combo points or fewer, and spend them inside the 20 seconds. Open with **Garrote** once the tank has the boss: Initiative makes it a fourth combo point, and a bleed does not care that the boss has no Sunders on it yet. Ambush needs a dagger in the main hand, so with a sword it is off your bar. Then **Ghostly Strike** for a fifth point, **Rupture** right away, and **Death Mark** as soon as you have four or more again with Rupture ticking. On trash that will die fast, open with **Cheap Shot**, which Initiative makes three combo points and which holds the mob still for 4 seconds, then Eviscerate.

Every rogue learns **Poised** at 18: at full energy out of combat, your next ability costs less, so open from full energy.

### The priority

**Shank** on cooldown, right behind your racial and trinket, while you have three combo points or fewer: with Boot Knife it is two points for 5 energy. At four or five, finish first and Shank right after; a 10 second cooldown loses nothing to one global.

Ghostly Strike goes on cooldown too, just never at five points. Every Ghostly Strike that lands is followed by a **Garrote** at four points or fewer, even with the old one still ticking: the strike lets it in without Stealth, a fresh Garrote is worth more than the ticks it replaces, and with Initiative it gives two combo points, which Hemorrhage does not. At exactly four with a Ghostly Strike just landed, Garrote first: its window is 7 seconds and Rupture can wait one global.

Rupture at four or more combo points whenever it is not ticking. **Death Mark** at four or more points while Rupture ticks. Between marks, every point Rupture and Slice and Dice do not need waits for the next mark, never Eviscerate, even with Death Mark on cooldown. While Rupture ticks, spend three or more on Slice and Dice when that is down or about to fall off. Hemorrhage builds, and waits a moment when Ghostly Strike is about to come off cooldown unless you are close to full energy. After a Ghostly Strike it waits for the Garrote: every Hemorrhage spends energy the Garrote needs, and if you press it the Garrote never gets its 30 energy before the window closes.

Rupture before Death Mark, always: Hemorrhage's debuff and Serrated Blades both feed it, and Death Mark stores every tick at twice the rate. A mark with no Rupture under it has lost its best source, so never press it into a lapsed bleed. Mark at four, and do not hold it for a fifth point: it comes back every 10 seconds, so a mark you hold back delays every mark after it.

### Energy

Shank is your energy: 5 to press, 25 back, and two combo points with Boot Knife, so a Shank left on cooldown costs you energy and points at once. Efficient Strikes keeps Hemorrhage cheap. Do not cap energy. **Thistle Tea** gives 100 energy on a 5 minute cooldown; drink it when empty.

### Cooldowns

Death Mark is your only damage cooldown and it comes back every 10 seconds, so there is nothing to hold: line your racial, trinket and potion up at the pull, where the first mark stores Garrote and Rupture together. **Preparation** finishes every other rogue cooldown, once every 10 minutes. On a boss, spend Premeditation, Shank and Ghostly Strike first, then Preparation and press all three again for another five combo points. Keep it for a second Vanish only when the fight needs one.

### Trash, threat and survival

- **Kidney Shot** costs 25 energy, sits on a 20 second cooldown and stuns longer per combo point, up to 6 seconds at five. Use it on casters. Kick, Gouge and Blind handle the rest; with Elusiveness, Blind comes back every three and a half minutes.
- **Redirect** (trained at level 1) lifts your combo points off a dying mob and hands them to the next enemy you hit within 15 seconds.
- **Feint** on cooldown on bosses where you sit near the tank's threat. **Vanish** drops you from the threat table, but a bleed or DoT on you puts you back in combat.
- **Evasion** and Ghostly Strike's dodge are for melee trash that turns on you. Neither helps against spells.

## Stat priority

Hit counts until your attacks stop missing, and adds nothing past that. Your white swings with two weapons miss far more often than Hemorrhage, Ghostly Strike and your finishers, so they keep wanting hit after your specials have stopped missing. Agility gives attack power and crit together. Crit is better than it looks: your Rupture and Garrote ticks crit, and Death Mark's cap rises with your crit chance. Strength and attack power add straight damage to every hit. Haste is rare on gear; take it when it comes. The table above gives the order for your gear.

## Best in slot

Subtlety does not need daggers at 60. Hemorrhage and Ghostly Strike hit harder with a dagger, but most of your damage comes from Rupture, Garrote, Death Mark, Shank and your white swings, and none of them care what kind of weapon you hold. Take the weapons in the table above, dagger or not. The off hand carries your Instant Poison and every Shank, so it wants to hit hard too.

## Enchants and consumables

Deadly Poison and a stone on the main hand, Instant Poison and a stone on the off hand: Forever lets them sit together. A shaman's Windfury Totem stacks with both.

## Races

Undead on Horde and Night Elf on Alliance. **Touch of the Grave** gives your attacks a chance to drain up to 5% of your maximum health from the target, at most once every 15 seconds, which is free damage all fight. **Elune's Light** is 10% crit for 15 seconds on a 3 minute cooldown, so press it at the pull with the first mark. Orc's Blood Fury and Human's passive 1% crit are the next best.

## Professions

**Enchanting** makes Molten Heart of the Mountain and Frozen Heart of the Mountain, bind-on-pickup trinkets only an enchanter can make. **Engineering** is the PvP and trash pick: grenades, dynamite and gadgets. **Skinning** and **Leatherworking** cover the Devilsaur and Stormshroud crafts.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Combat levels faster with a sword, so if you only want Subtlety for raids, level Combat and respec at 60. If you level Subtlety, here is how it starts. You start with Sinister Strike, Eviscerate and Stealth. Backstab trains at 4, Gouge at 6, Slice and Dice and Sprint at 10, Garrote at 14 and Ambush at 18. Keep a dagger in the main hand while you level: Backstab and Ambush need one, and they are what your first talents build on until Hemorrhage at 30.

Poised at 18 takes 10 Energy off your opener when you are at full Energy out of combat, so Ambush from stealth costs 50.

Your first three points, 10 to 12, go into Master of Deception, which lets you sneak past mobs as if you were three levels higher. Opportunity from 13 to 17 adds 20% to Backstab, Garrote and Ambush. Both points of Dirty Deeds, at 18 and 19, make Garrote 20 Energy cheaper and let you use it from the front.

Wear leather and take quest rewards over vendor gear. Swap the Worn Dagger for the first better quest dagger, and keep throwing knives for pulling.

### Levels 20 to 60

Premeditation comes at 20, Ghostly Strike at 26, Hemorrhage at 30, Boot Knife from 35 and Death Mark at 40. Stealth and Camouflage, from 24, keep you out of trouble on the way. Shank comes at 24 for every rogue: press it on cooldown. Until Hemorrhage, Backstab with a dagger is your builder whenever you are behind the target, and Sinister Strike when you are not. Open with Garrote, or Cheap Shot from 26, keep Slice and Dice up on anything that lives, and Eviscerate at five on mobs. On anything bigger, Rupture at four and Eviscerate at three while it ticks, until Death Mark comes at 40. From 26, follow every Ghostly Strike with a Garrote on anything that will live through it: the strike lets it in without Stealth. From 40, keep Rupture ticking on anything that will live, and put four or more points into Death Mark while it ticks; Eviscerate a mob that will die before the mark goes off.
