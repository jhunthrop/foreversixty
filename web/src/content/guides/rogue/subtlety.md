## Overview

Subtlety is the bleed rogue in Forever: a Premeditation opener, Hemorrhage and Boot Knife Shanks to build, Rupture as the main finisher and Death Mark to cash the bleeds in.

### Strengths

- No facing requirement anywhere in the rotation: Hemorrhage, Ghostly Strike and Rupture all work from the front
- A four-point opener from stealth with Premeditation and Initiative
- Bleeds ignore armor, so Rupture is full value from the first second of the pull
- The best rogue for PvP and world play: Preparation, a short Vanish and Blind with Elusiveness

### Weaknesses

- The lowest raid damage of the three trees
- More moving parts than Combat: a debuff, a bleed, a mark and three short cooldowns to track
- Its strongest tools (Vanish and Blind) do little on a raid boss, and Preparation is worth only one extra five-point finisher there

### In a raid

- **Role:** Single-target melee damage, at its best on bosses that make you move
- **Brings:** Kicks, Kidney Shots on trash and its own damage; no raid buff

## Talents and builds

One build does everything: 12 in Assassination for Malice, Ruthlessness, Improved Slice and Dice, Blood Splatter and a point of Lethality, 8 in Combat for Coup de Grace, Efficient Strikes and Precision, then 31 down Subtlety to Death Mark.

- **Hemorrhage** (1 point): your builder. 145% weapon damage with a dagger, one combo point, and the target takes 15% more damage from your Rupture and Garrote.
- **Serrated Blades** (3 points): ignore 9% of the target's armor, and Rupture deals 30% more.
- **Boot Knife** (5 points): every Shank adds two combo points. Shank is 5 energy for 25 back on a 10 second cooldown, so this is two free combo points every 10 seconds.
- **Death Mark** (1 point): a 25 energy finisher on a 10 second cooldown. It marks the target for 6 seconds and stores 12% per combo point of the damage you deal it, Rupture and Garrote at twice that, then deals it all at once, up to a cap set by your attack power and crit.
- **Coup de Grace** (3 points): Death Mark stores 20% more and its cap rises 20%, and Eviscerate hits 20% harder.
- **Ghostly Strike** (1 point): 180% weapon damage with a dagger and 15% dodge for 7 seconds, on a 20 second cooldown. A Ghostly Strike that lands also lets your next Ambush or Garrote within 7 seconds go in without Stealth.
- **Premeditation** (1 point): two combo points on a 2 minute cooldown. In Forever it needs no Stealth, so it opens the fight and comes back in it.
- **Initiative** (3 points): Garrote, Ambush and Cheap Shot add a combo point.

Opportunity (5 points) adds 20% to Garrote and Ambush. Blood Splatter keeps Rupture up 6 seconds longer and the opening Garrote 9, which is more bleed for every mark to store. Efficient Strikes takes 5 energy off Hemorrhage. One point of Dirty Deeds takes 10 energy off Garrote and Cheap Shot. Elusiveness, Camouflage and Master of Deception are for the world and PvP, and they are what gets you down the tree. The point in Improved Ambush only opens the next row; Ambush is not a boss opener for you.

## Rotation and priority

### The pull

Stealth. A couple of seconds before the pull, press **Premeditation** for two combo points; they last 20 seconds, so do not press it early. It needs no Stealth in Forever, so press it again every 2 minutes in the fight whenever you have three combo points or fewer, and spend them inside the 20 seconds. Open with **Garrote** once the tank has the boss: Initiative makes it a fourth combo point, and a bleed does not care that the boss has no Sunders on it yet. Ambush is the opener for anything that will die fast, not for a boss. Then **Rupture** right away at four points, and **Death Mark** as soon as you have five with both bleeds ticking.

Every rogue learns **Poised** at 18: at full energy out of combat, your next ability costs less, so open from full energy.

### The priority

**Shank** on cooldown, right behind your racial and trinket, while you have three combo points or fewer: with Boot Knife it is two points for 5 energy. At four or five, finish first and Shank right after; a 10 second cooldown loses nothing to one global. Ghostly Strike goes on cooldown too, just never at five points. Rupture at four or more combo points whenever it is not ticking. **Death Mark** at five points while Rupture ticks. While Rupture ticks and Death Mark is on cooldown, spend three or more points on Eviscerate, or on Slice and Dice when that is down or about to fall off. Hemorrhage builds, and waits a moment when Ghostly Strike is about to come off cooldown unless you are close to full energy.

Rupture first, always: Hemorrhage's debuff and Serrated Blades both feed it, and Death Mark stores every tick at twice the rate. A mark with no Rupture under it has lost its best source, so never press it into a lapsed bleed. Mark at five points, not four: the storage is per combo point, and the 10 second cooldown means one comes back every few finishers anyway.

### Energy

Shank is your energy: 5 to press, 25 back, and two combo points with Boot Knife, so a Shank left on cooldown costs you energy and points at once. Efficient Strikes keeps Hemorrhage cheap. Do not cap energy. **Thistle Tea** gives 100 energy on a 5 minute cooldown; drink it when empty.

### Cooldowns

Death Mark is your only damage cooldown and it comes back every 10 seconds, so there is nothing to hold: line your racial, trinket and potion up at the pull, where the first mark stores Garrote and Rupture together. **Preparation** finishes every other rogue cooldown, once every 10 minutes. On a boss, spend Premeditation, Shank and Ghostly Strike first, then Preparation and press all three again for another five points. Keep it for a second Vanish only when the fight needs one.

### Trash, threat and survival

- **Kidney Shot** costs 25 energy, sits on a 20 second cooldown and stuns longer per combo point, up to 6 seconds at five. Use it on casters. Kick, Gouge and Blind handle the rest; Elusiveness puts Blind on a short leash.
- **Redirect** (trained at level 1) lifts your combo points off a dying mob and hands them to the next enemy you hit within 15 seconds.
- **Feint** on cooldown on bosses where you sit near the tank's threat. **Vanish** drops you from the threat table, but a bleed or DoT on you puts you back in combat.
- **Evasion** and Ghostly Strike's dodge are for melee trash that turns on you. Neither helps against spells.

## Stat priority

Hit first, so Hemorrhage, Ghostly Strike and the finishers land. Agility next, for attack power and crit together. Crit is better than it looks: your Rupture and Garrote ticks crit, and Death Mark's cap rises with your crit chance.

## Best in slot

Daggers in both hands. Hemorrhage and Ghostly Strike both hit harder with a dagger in the main hand, and the main hand is the one that matters. A fast off-hand dagger carries your Instant Poison.

## Enchants and consumables

Deadly Poison and a stone on the main hand, Instant Poison and a stone on the off hand: Forever lets them sit together. A shaman's Windfury Totem stacks with both.

## Races

Undead on Horde and Night Elf on Alliance. **Touch of the Grave** gives your attacks a chance to drain up to 5% of your maximum health from the target, at most once every 15 seconds, which is free damage all fight. **Elune's Light** is 10% crit for 15 seconds on a 3 minute cooldown, so press it at the pull with the first mark. Orc's Blood Fury and Human's passive 1% crit are the next best.

## Professions

**Enchanting** makes Molten Heart of the Mountain and Frozen Heart of the Mountain, bind-on-pickup pre-raid trinkets. **Engineering** is the PvP and trash pick: grenades, dynamite and gadgets. **Skinning** and **Leatherworking** cover the Devilsaur and Stormshroud crafts.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

- You start with Sinister Strike, Eviscerate and Stealth. Backstab trains at 4, Gouge at 6, Slice and Dice and Sprint at 10, Garrote at 14 and Ambush at 18. Keep a dagger in the main hand: Backstab and Ambush need one, and they are what this tree builds on.
- Poised at 18 takes 10 Energy off your opener when you are at full Energy out of combat, so Ambush from stealth costs 50.
- Your first three points, 10 to 12, go into Master of Deception, which lets you sneak past mobs as if you were three levels higher. Opportunity from 13 to 17 adds 20% to Backstab, Garrote and Ambush. Dirty Deeds at 18 makes Garrote 10 Energy cheaper and lets you use it from the front, and a point of Camouflage at 19 speeds you up in stealth.
- Wear leather and take quest rewards over vendor gear. Swap the Worn Dagger for the first better quest dagger, and keep throwing knives for pulling.

### Levels 20 to 60

Combat levels faster with a sword; respec at 60. If you level Subtlety, Premeditation comes at 20, Ghostly Strike at 26, Hemorrhage at 30, Boot Knife from 35 and Death Mark at 40. Stealth and Camouflage keep you out of trouble on the way. Shank comes at 24 for every rogue: press it on cooldown. Leveling Subtlety, open with Cheap Shot or Garrote, keep Slice and Dice up on anything that lives, and Eviscerate at five.
