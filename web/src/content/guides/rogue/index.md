Rogue is still energy, combo points and stealth, but Forever moves all three trees onto daggers at 60 and gives each one a clear job. If you want one character for launch, level Combat and pick your raid tree at 60.

## What changed from 1.12

- **Daggers win.** Backstab, Ambush, Mutilate and both Subtlety builders want a dagger in the main hand, and Hack and Slash gives daggers crit. Swords, axes, maces and fists are leveling weapons; one-handed axes are new for rogues.
- **Poisons and stones stack.** A sharpening stone sits on a weapon beside a poison. Deadly Poison on the main hand and Instant Poison on the off hand is right for all three trees.
- **Redirect** is trained at level 1: it takes your combo points off one target and gives them to the next enemy you hit within 15 seconds.
- **Poised** comes at 18: at full energy out of combat, your next ability costs less. Open from full energy.
- **Shank** comes at 24 (rank 2 at 44): an off-hand stab for 5 energy that cannot miss, gives 25 energy back at rank 2 and sits on a 10 second cooldown. Every tree presses it on cooldown (a Subtlety rogue finishes first when Boot Knife would overflow the bar), and each has a talent that builds on it: Venom for Assassination, Flash of Steel for Combat, Boot Knife for Subtlety.
- **No weapon-skill racials.** Night Elf's Elune's Light and Troll's Berserking are the burst racials; Human gets 1% crit instead of sword skill.

## Races

Every Classic rogue race is back: Human, Dwarf, Night Elf and Gnome for the Alliance, Orc, Undead and Troll for the Horde, and the Skyborne on both factions. None of Forever's six new race and class pairs is a rogue.

Racials are reworked and none of them adds weapon skill, so pick for the button you press. Night Elf's **Elune's Light** (10% crit for 15 seconds) and Troll's **Berserking** (10% attack speed for 15 seconds), both on 3 minute cooldowns, are the raid picks. Orc's **Blood Fury** is 10% attack power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes and close behind. A Gnome's **Eureka!** makes your attacks 10% cheaper and 10% harder for 8 seconds every 2 minutes. Human's **Ruthless** is a passive 1% crit and Dwarf's **Artisan's Precision** a passive 1% hit. Undead's **Will of the Forsaken** breaks charm, fear and sleep every 2 minutes and **Touch of the Grave** drains a little health from what you hit, and Skyborne get a little haste on everything from **Wind Blessed**.
