Rogue is still energy, combo points and stealth, but Forever puts Combat and Assassination on daggers at 60 and gives each tree a clear job. If you want one character for launch, level Combat and pick your raid tree at 60.

## What changed from 1.12

- **Daggers for Combat and Assassination.** Backstab and Ambush need a dagger in the main hand, Mutilate needs one in each hand, and Hack and Slash gives daggers crit. Hemorrhage and Ghostly Strike hit harder with a dagger too, but Subtlety's damage leans on bleeds, Shank and Death Mark, so it takes the best weapons it can find, dagger or not. One-handed axes are new for rogues.
- **Poisons and stones stack.** A sharpening stone sits on a weapon beside a poison. Deadly Poison on the main hand and Instant Poison on the off hand is right for Combat and Subtlety, and for Assassination until 60, where it swaps them.
- **Redirect** is trained at level 1: it takes your combo points off one target and gives them to the next enemy you hit within 15 seconds.
- **Poised** comes at 18: at full energy out of combat, your next ability costs less. Open from full energy.
- **Shank** comes at 24 (rank 2 at 44): an off-hand stab for 5 energy that cannot miss, gives 25 energy back at rank 2 and sits on a 10 second cooldown. Every tree presses it on cooldown (a Subtlety rogue with 50 energy or more finishes first when Boot Knife would overflow its combo points), and each has a talent that builds on it: Venom for Assassination, Flash of Steel for Combat, Boot Knife for Subtlety.
- **No weapon-skill racials.** Human gets 1% crit instead of sword skill, and the races that win for a rogue are Undead, Night Elf and Gnome.

## Races

Every Classic rogue race is back: Human, Dwarf, Night Elf and Gnome for the Alliance, Orc, Undead and Troll for the Horde. Skyborne, the paid Heroic Pack race, can be a rogue on either faction. None of Forever's six new race and class pairs is a rogue.

Racials are reworked and none of them adds weapon skill. Undead is the Horde pick in every tree: **Touch of the Grave** gives your attacks a chance to drain up to 5% of your maximum health from the target, at most once every 15 seconds, and it adds up. Orc's **Blood Fury** (10% attack power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes) is second and Troll's **Berserking** (10% attack speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes) trails. On Alliance, Night Elf's **Elune's Light** (10% crit for 15 seconds every 3 minutes) leads for Assassination and Subtlety, with Human's **Ruthless** (a passive 1% crit) close behind and Dwarf's **Artisan's Precision** (a passive 1% hit) trailing in Subtlety, and Gnome is the Combat pick: **Eureka!** makes your non-periodic damaging abilities cost 10% less and deal 10% more for 8 seconds every 2 minutes, and **Expansive Mind** makes your energy bar 5% bigger. Undead's **Will of the Forsaken** also breaks charm, fear and sleep every 2 minutes. Skyborne's 2% haste from **Wind Blessed** never beats the best free race, so it is only for players who bought the Heroic Pack for the look.
