## Overview

Holy is the priest who heals everything: Prayer of Mending and Renew on the tank, downranked Greater Heal as the filler, and Prayer of Healing for a hurt group.

### Strengths

- Prayer of Mending is the cheapest healing in the game: five jumps for one cast
- Prayer of Healing heals any five-player group at once, not only your own
- Spiritual Guidance and Meditation turn Spirit into both healing and mana
- Lightwell heals the melee while you heal the tank, and Holy Binding heals you every time you Flash Heal someone else

### Weaknesses

- Mana is the limit on every long fight
- Your shield is untalented and Weakened Soul limits it, so most of your healing answers damage that has already landed
- Slow heals on a spiky tank need you to pre-cast and cancel

### In a raid

- **Role:** Raid and group healer, and a strong second tank healer
- **Brings:** Power Word: Fortitude, Divine Spirit, Fear Ward, Lightwell and Shadow Protection

## Talents and builds

Deep Holy for Prayer of Mending, with Discipline taken to Meditation and Inner Focus.

- **Prayer of Mending** (1 point): the capstone. It heals its holder on the next hit or direct heal, then jumps. Keep it on the tank.
- **Spiritual Healing** (3 points): 10% more healing on everything.
- **Spiritual Guidance** (4 points): a share of your Spirit becomes healing power, so Spirit pays you twice.
- **Holy Specialization** (5 points) and **Divine Fury** (5 points): 5% crit on Holy spells, and Heal and Greater Heal cast half a second faster.
- **Improved Healing** (3 points): 15% off Lesser Heal, Heal, Greater Heal and Prayer of Mending.
- **Improved Renew** (3 points): 15% more on the Renew you keep on the tank.
- **Litany of Light** (2 points): mana back whenever your heal is a different spell from the last one. Put a Renew or a Prayer of Mending between your Greater Heals and it pays all fight; two Greater Heals in a row, even at different ranks, earn nothing.
- **Holy Binding** (1 point): every Flash Heal you put on someone else heals you for half as much. Raid damage hits you too.
- **Twilight Focus** (3 points): 70% of the hits you take no longer push back your casts. On raid-wide damage this is worth more than any throughput point.
- **Meditation** (3 points), **Mental Strength** (5 points) and **Inner Focus** (1 point): regeneration while casting, a deeper bar, and a free spell every 3 minutes.

Spirit of Redemption is only the path to Prayer of Mending. [Open the planner](/planner?class=priest) to change the build.

## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Drop Lightwell where the melee and the tank can reach it and tell the raid it is there: it lasts 3 minutes or 5 clicks, and being hit cancels a click. Put Fear Ward on the tank if the boss fears. Prayer of Mending and Renew go on the tank as he engages.

**On the tank.** Prayer of Mending every time it is ready. Renew always up at rank 7 or higher. Go up to the top rank on a tank who is dropping through it.

**The filler is a downranked Greater Heal** in a raid: rank 1 or 2, whichever fills the hole without overhealing. In a dungeon group or on bare gear, where your bonus is small, Heal rank 4 is the cheaper cast. With Divine Fury both are 2.5 second casts. The top rank of Greater Heal comes out only when the tank needs one big number.

**Low ranks.** In Forever a rank learned far below your level gets less of your healing bonus. At 60, Lesser Heal and the first ranks of Heal and Renew were learned too long ago to scale with your gear and heal for little more than their base, and Renew rank 5 and 6 and Heal rank 4 keep only part of it. Downrank within the ranks you learned from your forties on.

**Flash Heal is for emergencies.** In a raid use the top rank, on a tank who is really low or anyone about to die: it is your dearest heal, so spend it there and let Prayer of Mending, Renew and Greater Heal do the rest. While leveling, a lower rank keeps your bar alive. Holy Binding heals you off every one of them.

**Prayer of Healing.** Cast it when three or more players in one group are missing a fair chunk. In Forever it heals the target and the target's party, so target a member of whichever group took the hit: you are not limited to your own party.

**Overheal.** Start Greater Heal on the tank before the boss swings, and cancel it if he does not need it: a cancelled cast costs no mana. Do not chase small top-ups with Greater Heal; Prayer of Mending and Renew cover those.

**Inner Focus.** Spend it on a Prayer of Healing, your most expensive spell, or on a Greater Heal for a tank who is about to drop: it makes the spell free and adds 25% crit chance. Do not let it fall on a Renew.

**Assignments.** In a raid you will be on a group or on a tank. On a group, Prayer of Healing is your main spell and Prayer of Mending goes on whoever in that group gets hit. On a tank, Renew, Prayer of Mending and Greater Heal, with Flash Heal for the spikes. Either way, never stand where the boss's cleave or the raid's AoE lands on you: Twilight Focus helps, it does not make you immune.

**Mana.** Every heal you skip early is mana for the last minute. Sit out of casting for five seconds when you can and Spirit regeneration comes back in full. Drink a Major Mana Potion and use a Demonic Rune once you are down enough that neither wastes, an Undead uses Dark Sacrifice, and a Gnome's Eureka! goes on a string of Prayers of Healing.

## Stat priority

On a long fight mp5 wins: a point of it buys more healing over five minutes than a point of healing power. Healing power comes next, then Spirit, which Spiritual Guidance and Meditation turn into both healing and mana. Intellect deepens the bar. Critical strike is last for Holy, since Inspiration only adds armor. Spell power with no healing on it does nothing for a heal.

## Best in slot

Forever puts a third of a healing item's bonus on it as spell damage. Only the healing number heals, so read healing gear by that number.

## Enchants and consumables

Flask of Distilled Wisdom for the mana, Greater Mageblood Elixir, Greater Cleric's Elixir and Elixir of Sages, Sage's Tea for food, Brilliant Mana Oil on the weapon, and Major Mana Potions with Demonic Runes in the fight. Forever's Major Mender's Potions add healing for 30 seconds, but they share the potion cooldown with your mana potion, and on a long fight the mana is worth more.

## Races

**Human** is the Alliance pick. The Human Spirit adds 10% Spirit, which Spiritual Guidance turns into healing, and Divine Grace is a free instant heal on an ally under 50% every 10 minutes. Feedback is a PvP spell.

**Undead** is the Horde pick, and the best Holy priest of all, for Dark Sacrifice: once a fight, trade your own health for well over a thousand mana while the raid is quiet, then heal yourself back. Will of the Forsaken breaks fears.

**Gnome** is close behind Human. Eureka! makes your heals 10% stronger and 10% cheaper for 8 seconds every 2 minutes, and Expansive Mind adds 5% mana. Contingency Plan is a ward that shields and heals an ally who drops under 35%: put it on the tank before a hard hit. **Night Elf** has Elune's Light, 10% crit for 15 seconds, **Troll** Berserking, 10% cast speed, and **Dwarf** Stoneform and Desperate Prayer, a free self heal. Those three heal clearly less.

## Professions

Tailoring makes healing cloth you will wear into the first raids. Enchanting saves you the enchanting bill. Alchemy makes the flask and the elixirs you burn every raid, and that is the profession that saves a healer the most gold.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

- Until Holy Fire at 20, Smite is your damage: Shadow Word: Pain at 4 first, Smite, then the wand. Power Word: Shield at 6 and Renew at 8 matter most, and Inner Fire follows at 12.
- Three points of Twilight Focus from 10 to 12 stop your casts from being pushed back when something hits you. Improved Renew takes 13 to 15 for 15% bigger Renews, and Holy Specialization starts at 16 for more crit on your Holy spells.
- Wear cloth, take quest rewards over vendor gear, and swap the starting mace for the first quest staff or caster mace. Get a wand early: a Lesser Magic Wand from an Enchanter at 5, a Greater Magic Wand at 13, or a quest wand such as the Elven Wand (Bashal'Aran, Darkshore) or the Cinder Wand (Arugal's Folly, Silverpine Forest).

### Levels 20 to 60

Level as Shadow and respec at 60, or level in a group as Holy. Solo, Holy is slow: Smite and Holy Fire hit hard, but they cost so much mana that you drink after every pull. If you level Holy, Holy Fire opens, Smite finishes, and Power Word: Shield keeps you casting. In dungeons, keep Renew on the tank, Heal as the filler from level 16, and learn to downrank early: a level 30 priest who casts Heal rank 2 instead of rank 3 finishes the dungeon with mana to spare.
