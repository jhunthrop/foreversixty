## Overview

Discipline heals the tank with Penance on cooldown, a shield to open Renewed Hope, and crits that leave Divine Aegis shields behind.

### Strengths

- Penance is a short channel of three heals every 12 seconds
- A shield that soaks the next hit before it lands
- Renewed Hope and Divine Aegis turn Weakened Soul into crits, and crits into more shields

### Weaknesses

- Less group healing than Holy: no Prayer of Mending, no Spiritual Healing
- Weakened Soul stops you shielding the same target again for a while
- Penance and the shield are single-target: a raid-wide hit falls to Prayer of Healing

### In a raid

- **Role:** Tank healer, and the priest who can give Power Infusion
- **Brings:** Power Word: Fortitude, Divine Spirit, Fear Ward, Lightwell, Shadow Protection and, with the swap below, Power Infusion

## Talents and builds

Deep Discipline for Penance and Divine Aegis, with Holy taken for faster, cheaper heals.

- **Penance** (1 point): your best heal. A two second channel that heals three times, one second apart, on a 12 second cooldown.
- **Renewed Hope** (4 points): Flash Heal, Lesser Heal, Heal, Greater Heal and Penance gain 8% crit chance on a target under Weakened Soul, and each one takes 4 seconds off his Weakened Soul so the next shield comes sooner.
- **Divine Aegis** (3 points): every direct heal that crits leaves a shield for 15% of it.
- **Soul Warding** (1 point): no cooldown on Power Word: Shield and 15% off its cost.
- **Improved Power Word: Shield** (3 points): 20% more on every shield.
- **Twin Disciplines** (5 points) and **Mental Agility** (3 points): Penance and Renew heal 5% more, and Renew and the shield cost 10% less.
- **Inner Focus** (1 point): a free spell with 25% more crit every 3 minutes.
- **Meditation** (3 points) and **Mental Strength** (5 points): regeneration while casting, and a deeper bar.
- **Holy Specialization** (5 points), **Divine Fury** (5 points) and **Improved Healing** (3 points): more crits for Divine Aegis, faster Heal and Greater Heal, and 15% off Lesser Heal, Heal, Greater Heal and Penance.
- **Improved Renew** (3 points) and **Twilight Focus** (3 points): a better Renew on the tank, and most raid hits no longer push back your casts.

**Power Infusion** is not in the build. It works on yourself, 15 seconds of 20% more healing every 3 minutes, but on a mage or warlock's burst it is worth far more to the raid. When you have a caster worth buffing, take it: move two points out of Improved Renew, one into Renewed Hope's fifth rank and one into Power Infusion, and give it to them on their burst. ## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Drop Lightwell and tell the raid it is up. Fear Ward the tank if the boss fears. Renew the tank just before he engages, so it is already ticking when the first hits land.

**Penance on cooldown.** On the tank whenever he is missing health, on a party member who drops low when the tank is fine. It is the heal you never let sit.

**The shield is a switch.** Shield the tank at the top rank when he is hurt, Weakened Soul is off him and your mana is keeping pace with the fight. In Forever a shield no longer costs a warrior any rage, so rage is no reason to hold it back: it soaks the next hit, and the Weakened Soul it leaves is what Renewed Hope needs. Your next heals on him gain 8% crit chance, every crit leaves a Divine Aegis, and each heal shortens the Weakened Soul so you shield again sooner.

**Renew** stays on the tank at rank 7 or higher.

**Fillers.** Greater Heal at the top rank on the tank, where the big hits land. Greater Heal rank 1 on everyone else.

**Low ranks.** In Forever a rank learned far below your level gets less of your healing bonus. At 60, Lesser Heal and the first ranks of Heal and Renew were learned too long ago to scale with your gear and heal for little more than their base, and Renew rank 5 and 6 and Heal rank 4 keep only part of it. Downrank within the ranks you learned from your forties on, and keep Lesser Heal off your bars for good: when even Greater Heal rank 1 is more than you can afford, wait the few seconds for your regeneration rather than cast a heal that no longer grows with your gear.

**Emergencies.** Flash Heal at the top rank on anyone about to die. It takes a second and a half and it costs the most mana for what it heals, so it never becomes the filler.

**Prayer of Healing** when three or more in a group are hurt. It heals the target's party, so target a member of the group that took the damage.

**Overheal.** Shields and Divine Aegis land before the damage and Penance goes on health already missing, so most of your waste is a Greater Heal that arrives after a shield ate the hit. Start it early and cancel it if the tank does not need it.

**Inner Focus** on a Greater Heal or a Prayer of Healing: it makes the spell free and adds 25% crit chance, and a Greater Heal crit leaves the biggest Divine Aegis you can make.

**Mana.** Penance is cheap and the shield soaks more than it costs; the fillers are what drain you. When your bar falls behind the fight, stop topping off the party and heal only real holes. Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune once you are down enough, Dark Sacrifice if you are Undead.

## Stat priority

The table above gives the order for your gear; here is why each stat counts. Mp5 is mana that keeps coming while you cast, and on a long fight running dry is what ends your healing. Healing power is the size of every shield, Penance and heal, short of a rank far below your level. Intellect deepens your bar, and Mental Strength adds 10% to it. Critical strike pays twice for Discipline: every direct heal that crits also leaves a Divine Aegis shield on the target. Spirit is mana, and Meditation keeps half of its regeneration running while you cast. Spell power with no healing on it does nothing for a heal.

## Best in slot

Meditation keeps half your regeneration running while you cast, so Spirit still feeds you mid-fight. Forever puts a third of a healing item's bonus on it as spell damage; only the healing number heals.

## Enchants and consumables

Flask of Distilled Wisdom, Greater Mageblood Elixir, Greater Cleric's Elixir and Elixir of Sages, Sage's Tea, Brilliant Mana Oil, and Major Mana Potions with Demonic Runes. Forever's Major Mender's Potion shares the potion cooldown with your mana potion; on a long fight the mana is worth more.

## Races

**Gnome** is the Alliance pick, for Eureka!: your direct heals are 10% cheaper and 10% stronger for 8 seconds every 2 minutes, and so are Penance's heals (never Renew), so put it on a string of Greater Heals, Flash Heals and a Penance. Expansive Mind adds 5% mana. Contingency Plan is a ward that shields and heals an ally who drops under 35%: put it on the tank before a hard hit.

**Human** is level with Gnome, a hair ahead on a long tank fight, so take whichever you would rather play. Divine Grace is a free instant heal on an ally under 50% that also removes Weakened Soul, so you can shield the tank again right away: hold it for the moment he takes two big hits in a row. The Human Spirit adds 10% Spirit.

**Undead** is the Horde pick for Dark Sacrifice: well over a thousand mana once a fight, paid in your own health while the raid is quiet. Will of the Forsaken breaks fears.

**Night Elf** has Elune's Light for 10% crit (more Divine Aegis), **Troll** Berserking, and **Dwarf** Stoneform and Desperate Prayer. All three heal clearly less than a Gnome or a Human.

## Professions

Alchemy makes the flask and elixirs a healer burns every raid. Tailoring makes healing cloth for the first raids, Truefaith Vestments among it, which binds when you make it. Enchanting saves you the enchanting bill, and it makes the Dormant Heart of the Mountain, the bind on pickup trinket a Horde Discipline priest wears in the raid set.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Train Power Word: Shield at 6 and Renew at 8, then Mind Blast at 10 and Inner Fire at 12. Shadow Word: Pain at 4 opens every pull, Smite does the work until a wand takes over, and Dispel Magic arrives at 18.

Twin Disciplines takes your points from 10 to 14: 5% more damage and healing on your instants, which at this level means Shadow Word: Pain and Renew. Mental Strength takes 15 to 19 for 10% more Intellect, a bigger mana bar between drinks.

Wear cloth, take quest rewards over vendor gear, and swap the starting mace for the first quest staff or caster mace. Get a wand early: a Lesser Magic Wand from an Enchanter at 5, a Greater Magic Wand at 13, or a quest wand such as the Elven Wand (Bashal'Aran, Darkshore) or the Cinder Wand (Arugal's Folly, Silverpine Forest).

### Levels 20 to 60

From 20 you can keep leveling Discipline in dungeons, or respec to Shadow at the trainer for faster solo questing and come back at 60. Penance comes at level 30 and changes how Discipline plays: before that you are a Renew, Heal and shield healer. Shield yourself before every solo pull and wand while it holds; Power Word: Shield is the reason a Discipline priest can quest at all. In dungeons from 40, keep Renew on the tank at rank 4, then rank 5 from 45: they cost far less than the top rank and keep nearly all of your healing bonus. From 50, keep rank 7: rank 8 heals more per cast but costs more for it, and over a dungeon the two come out even. From 56, take Renew rank 9 for your last dungeons; at 60 rank 7 is the tank's Renew again. Heal is the tank's filler until you learn Greater Heal at 40. From then, Greater Heal on the tank stays at rank 1 until you learn rank 4 at 58: it keeps your whole healing bonus until then, and the bigger ranks spend mana on healing a dungeon tank rarely needs. From 40 on, leave Lesser Heal off your bars for good: Greater Heal rank 1 takes its place, by your late forties Lesser Heal has lost your healing bonus anyway, and every cast costs mana regeneration you need for the next Greater Heal.
