## Overview

Discipline heals the tank with Penance on cooldown, a cheap shield to open Renewed Hope, and crits that leave Divine Aegis shields behind.

### Strengths

- Penance is a short channel of three heals every 12 seconds
- A shield that soaks the next hit before it lands
- Renewed Hope and Divine Aegis turn Weakened Soul into crits, and crits into more shields

### Weaknesses

- Less group healing than Holy: no Prayer of Mending, no Spiritual Healing
- Weakened Soul stops you shielding the same target again for a while
- Shields take rage from a warrior tank, so they need judgement on a rage-starved pull

### In a raid

- **Role:** Tank healer, and the priest who can give Power Infusion
- **Brings:** Power Word: Fortitude, Divine Spirit, Fear Ward, Lightwell, Shadow Protection and, with the swap below, Power Infusion

## Talents and builds

Deep Discipline for Penance and Divine Aegis, with Holy taken for faster, cheaper heals.

- **Penance** (1 point): your best heal. A two second channel that heals three times, one second apart, on a 12 second cooldown.
- **Renewed Hope** (4 points): Flash Heal, Lesser Heal, Heal, Greater Heal and Penance gain 8% crit chance on a target under Weakened Soul, and each one takes 4 seconds off his Weakened Soul so the next shield comes sooner.
- **Divine Aegis** (3 points): every direct heal that crits leaves a shield for 15% of it.
- **Soul Warding** (1 point): no cooldown on Power Word: Shield and 15% off its cost.
- **Improved Power Word: Shield** (3 points): 20% more on every shield.
- **Twin Disciplines** (5 points) and **Mental Agility** (3 points): Penance and Renew heal 5% more, and Renew and the shield cost 10% less.
- **Inner Focus** (1 point): a free spell with 25% more crit every 3 minutes.
- **Meditation** (3 points) and **Mental Strength** (5 points): regeneration while casting, and a deeper bar.
- **Holy Specialization** (5 points), **Divine Fury** (5 points) and **Improved Healing** (3 points): more crits for Divine Aegis, faster Heal and Greater Heal, and 15% off Lesser Heal, Heal, Greater Heal and Penance.
- **Improved Renew** (3 points) and **Twilight Focus** (3 points): a better Renew on the tank, and casts that raid damage rarely pushes back.

**Power Infusion** is not in the build. It works on yourself, 15 seconds of 20% more healing every 3 minutes, but on a mage or warlock's burst it is worth far more to the raid. When you have a caster worth buffing, take it: move two points out of Improved Renew, one into Renewed Hope's fifth rank and one into Power Infusion, and give it to them on their burst. ## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Drop Lightwell and tell the raid it is up. Fear Ward the tank if the boss fears. Renew the tank as he engages.

**Penance on cooldown.** On the tank whenever he is missing health, on a party member who drops low when the tank is fine. It is the heal you never let sit.

**The shield is a switch.** Shield the tank at a low rank whenever Weakened Soul is off him. A small shield costs little and soaks one hit, and the Weakened Soul it leaves is what Renewed Hope needs: your next heals on him gain 8% crit chance, every crit leaves a Divine Aegis, and each heal shortens the Weakened Soul so you shield again sooner. A full-rank shield on a warrior tank eats the rage he needs at the start of a pull; save the big one for a hit he cannot take.

**Renew** stays on the tank at rank 7 or higher.

**Fillers.** Greater Heal at the top rank on the tank, where the big hits land. Greater Heal rank 1 or 2 on everyone else.

**Low ranks.** In Forever a rank learned far below your level gets less of your healing bonus. At 60, Lesser Heal and the first ranks of Heal and Renew were learned too long ago to scale with your gear and heal for little more than their base, and Renew rank 5 and 6 and Heal rank 4 keep only part of it. Downrank within the ranks you learned from your forties on.

**Emergencies.** Flash Heal at the top rank on anyone about to die. It is your fastest heal and your dearest, so it never becomes the filler.

**Prayer of Healing** when three or more in a group are hurt. It heals the target's party, so target a member of the group that took the damage.

**Overheal.** Shields and Divine Aegis land before the damage and Penance goes on health already missing, so most of your waste is a Greater Heal that arrives after a shield ate the hit. Start it early and cancel it if the tank does not need it.

**Inner Focus** on a Greater Heal or a Prayer of Healing: it makes the spell free and adds 25% crit chance, and a Greater Heal crit leaves the biggest Divine Aegis you can make.

**Mana.** Shields and Penance are cheap; the fillers are what drain you. When your bar falls behind the fight, stop topping off the party and heal only real holes. Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune once you are down enough, Dark Sacrifice if you are Undead.

## Stat priority

Mana first on a long fight: mp5 and Spirit, then Intellect and healing power close together. Critical strike is worth more to you than to Holy because every crit makes a Divine Aegis, but it is still last per point. Spell power with no healing on it does nothing for a heal.

## Best in slot

Meditation keeps half your regeneration running while you cast, so Spirit still feeds you mid-fight. Forever puts a third of a healing item's bonus on it as spell damage; only the healing number heals.

## Enchants and consumables

Flask of Distilled Wisdom, Greater Mageblood Elixir, Greater Cleric's Elixir and Elixir of Sages, Sage's Tea, Brilliant Mana Oil, and Major Mana Potions with Demonic Runes. Forever's Major Mender's Potion shares the potion cooldown with your mana potion; on a long fight the mana is worth more.

## Races

**Gnome** is the Alliance pick. Eureka! makes your heals 10% cheaper and 10% stronger for 8 seconds every 2 minutes: put it on a Penance and the Greater Heals around it. Expansive Mind adds 5% mana. Contingency Plan is a ward that shields and heals an ally who drops under 35%: put it on the tank before a hard hit.

**Human** is a hair behind. Divine Grace is a free instant heal on an ally under 50% that also removes Weakened Soul, so you can shield the tank again right away: hold it for the moment he takes two big hits in a row. The Human Spirit adds 10% Spirit.

**Undead** is the Horde pick for Dark Sacrifice: well over a thousand mana once a fight, paid in your own health while the raid is quiet. Will of the Forsaken breaks fears.

**Night Elf** has Elune's Light for 10% crit (more Divine Aegis), **Troll** Berserking, and **Dwarf** Stoneform and Desperate Prayer. All three heal clearly less than a Gnome or a Human.

## Professions

Alchemy makes the flask and elixirs a healer burns every raid. Tailoring makes healing cloth for the first raids, and Enchanting saves you the enchanting bill.

## Leveling

Level as Shadow and respec, or level in dungeons. Penance comes at level 30 and changes how Discipline plays: before that you are a Renew, Heal and shield healer. Shield yourself before every solo pull and wand while it holds; Power Word: Shield is the reason a Discipline priest can quest at all.
