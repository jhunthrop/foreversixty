## Overview

Shadow keeps two DoTs ticking, hits Mind Blast and Shadow Word: Death on cooldown, and fills with Mind Flay, all from Shadowform.

### Strengths

- The best way to level a priest: DoTs and a wand do the work, and from level 16 Spirit Tap cuts your drinking after every kill
- Vampiric Embrace heals your whole group for a fifth of your Shadow damage
- Shadow Word: Death is a second big instant on an 18 second cooldown
- Every priest now has Devouring Plague, so Shadow carries two DoTs on any race

### Weaknesses

- The leveling build reaches Mind Flay at 28 and Shadowform at 42, so early on you wand between casts
- Mind Blast and Shadow Word: Death throw a lot of threat early in a pull
- Shadowform blocks every healing spell, so helping your healers means leaving the form

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged caster damage
- **Brings:** Vampiric Embrace on your group, Power Word: Fortitude, Divine Spirit, Fear Ward, Shadow Protection and Silence for a caster add

## Talents and builds

You go deep Shadow for Shadowform and spend the rest in Discipline for mana and cheaper instants. Skip Power in Light and Holy Precision: one only touches Smite and Penance, the other only Holy spells.

- **Shadowform** (1 point): 10% more Shadow damage, half the mana on every Shadow spell and double the critical damage bonus. It is the reason to be Shadow.
- **Shadow Weaving** (3 points): every Shadow spell you land adds 2% Shadow damage taken from you, up to 5 stacks. In Forever it only raises your own damage, not a warlock's. It stacks in your first few globals and Mind Flay keeps it up.
- **Darkness** (5 points): a flat 10% on every Shadow spell.
- **Improved Mind Flay** (2 points): each Mind Flay tick hits 20% harder than the one before, which is why you never clip it.
- **Improved Mind Blast** (4 to 5 points): each point takes half a second off Mind Blast's 8 second cooldown.
- **Improved Shadow Word: Pain** (2 points): 6 more seconds of Pain, so fewer refreshes.
- **Shadow Focus** (5 points): 5% Shadow hit, the cheapest hit you will ever get.
- **Spirit Tap** (5 points leveling, none in the raid build): after every kill your Spirit doubles for 15 seconds, with half your regeneration running while you cast. It is your drink between pulls; on a raid boss nothing dies, so the raid build leaves it.
- **Blackout** (2 points leveling, none in the raid build): a 4% chance for your Shadow spells to stun for 3 seconds, which stops a mob that reaches you. Bosses cannot be stunned, so the raid build drops it.
- **Vampiric Embrace** (1 point): the group heal you keep on the boss in a raid. See the rotation for what it costs you.
- **Early Demise** (2 points): 30% more Shadow Word: Death crit chance below 20%, the raid build's execute.
- **Devouring Contagion** (2 points, raid build): Devouring Plague costs half as much, and a target that dies with it jumps it to two enemies within 10 yards.
- **Silence** (1 point, raid build): a 5 second silence that interrupts a cast, for the add your raid needs stopped.
- **Shadow Reach** (2 points, raid build): 20% more range on your Shadow spells, so you stand out of what the melee stand in.
- **Dark Inspiration** (1 point, raid build): Mind Blast and a full Mind Flay give you and your party 3% more Spirit for 10 seconds.
- **Twin Disciplines** (5 points), **Mental Agility** (3 points) and **Inner Focus** (1 point): your instants hit harder and cost less, and Inner Focus is a free Mind Blast every 3 minutes.
- **Meditation** (3 points) and **Mental Strength** (5 points): half your regeneration keeps running while you cast, and 10% more Intellect for a deeper bar.
- **Silent Resolve** (1 point): 10% less threat. Take more of it if you keep pulling aggro.

The leveling build opens with two points in Wand Specialization for the wand that finishes your mobs, and its last points go to Discipline. The raid build trades Wand Specialization, Blackout and Spirit Tap for Early Demise, Devouring Contagion, Silence, both points of Shadow Reach, one of Dark Inspiration and the last point of Improved Mind Blast. ## Rotation and priority

**Before the pull.** Buff the raid, then shift into Shadowform and drink back to full: the form costs mana, so never shift on the pull. Start Mind Blast 1.5 seconds before the tank's pull lands so it hits as the boss is engaged, and do not pre-cast it on a tank who opens slowly.

**Opener.** Mind Blast lands as the boss is engaged, then Shadow Word: Pain, Vampiric Embrace, Devouring Plague and Shadow Word: Death. By then Mind Blast is back: Inner Focus first, so it is free and more likely to crit, then Mind Flay. By the end of that string Shadow Weaving is at five stacks.

**Priority.** Keep Shadow Word: Pain up. Keep Devouring Plague up: it is on a 24 second cooldown and heals you as it ticks. Mind Blast on cooldown. Shadow Word: Death on cooldown. Mind Flay with everything else.

**Never clip Mind Flay.** Improved Mind Flay makes the third tick the biggest, so a Mind Blast that comes off cooldown mid-channel waits for the channel to finish.

**Shadow Word: Death backlash.** Every cast that does not kill the target hits you for 30% of your maximum health. Cast it on cooldown anyway, and keep Vampiric Embrace up so your own damage heals it back.

**Vampiric Embrace.** On the boss, keep it up: it costs a global every 30 seconds, a few percent of your own damage, and in return your whole group heals for 20% of every Shadow spell you land. On a fight with group damage that is a lot of healing, and it pays for Shadow Word: Death. On a fight where your group takes nothing, skip it and push damage. That healing goes to your group, so it never shows on your own damage meter.

**Execute.** Nothing changes below 20%, except that Early Demise makes Shadow Word: Death crit far more often. Keep Shadow Word: Pain up until the last 6 seconds of a fight. It ticks every 3 seconds, and it takes two ticks to out-damage the Mind Flay you would cast instead, so once the boss has less than 6 seconds to live, spend the global on Mind Blast, Shadow Word: Death or Mind Flay.

**AoE and adds.** Shadow has no real AoE. Put Shadow Word: Pain on adds that will live 15 seconds or more and keep your rotation on the kill target. Mind Flay slows by 50%, which is a good way to peel a runner off a healer.

**Moving.** Shadow Word: Pain, Devouring Plague, Shadow Word: Death and Vampiric Embrace are all instant. Save a refresh or Shadow Word: Death for when you have to run.

**Threat.** Mind Blast threatens hard. Fade sheds threat for 10 seconds on a 30 second cooldown; use it the moment you climb past the tank, not after the boss turns. If you keep pulling, move a point from Mental Strength into Silent Resolve.

**Mana.** With raid buffs a 3 minute fight does not drain you. On long fights use a Major Mana Potion and a Demonic Rune once you are down that much, and an Undead uses Dark Sacrifice.

**Cooldowns.** Inner Focus on Mind Blast every time it is up. Trinkets and racials go with your biggest damage: Troll Berserking and Night Elf Elune's Light whenever they are up, Gnome Eureka! on a Mind Blast and a Shadow Word: Death, and fill the rest of it with Mind Flay, whose ticks it also makes 10% stronger. It skips Shadow Word: Pain and Devouring Plague, so never spend it on a refresh.

## Stat priority

The table above gives the order for your gear; here is why each stat counts. Hit matters up to the 16% spell hit cap against a raid boss and adds nothing past it: Shadow Focus gives 5%, your gear finds the rest, and every miss is a lost Mind Blast. Spell power and Shadow power are the same stat to you, and every spell you cast grows with them. Critical strike reaches more than your direct hits: in Forever your DoT ticks and Mind Flay crit, and Shadowform doubles the crit bonus, so a Shadow crit hits for twice a normal hit. Intellect is a little crit and a deeper bar; with raid buffs the mana rarely matters. Spell penetration does nothing against raid bosses.

## Best in slot

Forever puts a third of a healing item's bonus on it as spell damage, so a healer's cloth piece still does some damage. Judge it by that damage number, its Intellect and its Spirit, never by the healing: a healing piece with good Intellect or Spirit can beat a thin caster piece.

## Enchants and consumables

Flask of Supreme Power, Elixir of Shadow Power and Greater Arcane Elixir all stack, so run all three. Brilliant Wizard Oil on the weapon. Open with Forever's Major Spellblasting Potion; a Major Mana Potion and a Demonic Rune come out on a long fight. The Demonic Rune hurts you, so do not stack it with a Shadow Word: Death when your healers are busy.

## Races

**Human** is the Alliance pick. Ruthless adds 1% critical strike chance to all your spells, and Shadowform doubles every crit's bonus. The Human Spirit adds 10% Spirit, which while leveling is more drinking saved while Spirit Tap is up. The Human priest spells are Divine Grace, a free instant heal on an ally under 50%, and Feedback, which is for PvP.

**Undead** is the Horde pick, and the strongest Shadow priest of all. Touch of the Grave gives your spells a chance to drain health from the target, up to 5% of your maximum health, at most once every 15 seconds, which takes the edge off Shadow Word: Death's backlash. Dark Sacrifice trades your health for mana once a fight, and Will of the Forsaken breaks fears and charms.

**Night Elf** is as good as Human: Elune's Light is 10% crit for 15 seconds every 3 minutes. **Dwarf** is close behind with Artisan's Precision, 1% hit, plus Stoneform and Desperate Prayer. **Gnome** trails them a little: Eureka! makes Mind Blast and Shadow Word: Death 10% cheaper and 10% stronger, and Mind Flay's ticks 10% stronger, for 8 seconds every 2 minutes, and Expansive Mind adds 5% mana. **Troll** has Berserking, 10% cast speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes, and falls clearly behind Undead.

## Professions

Tailoring makes a lot of the cloth you wear before raids, so it pays for itself. Enchanting makes its own caster pieces and saves you the enchanting bill. A Gnome who wants Engineering gets more reliable devices from Engineering Specialization.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Smite does your damage until 10: Shadow Word: Pain at 4, Smite, then the wand. Power Word: Shield comes at 6, Mind Blast at 10, Inner Fire at 12 and Psychic Scream at 14, your way out when a second mob joins.

Your first two talent points go into Wand Specialization at 10 and 11, 25% more wand damage, because the wand finishes most of your mobs at these levels. Spirit Tap takes 12 to 16: at five points every kill doubles your Spirit for 15 seconds and keeps half your regeneration running while you cast, so you barely drink. Shadow Focus starts at 17 for more hit on your Shadow spells.

Wear cloth, take quest rewards over vendor gear, and swap the starting mace for the first quest staff or caster mace. Get a wand early: a Lesser Magic Wand from an Enchanter at 5, a Greater Magic Wand at 13, or a quest wand such as the Elven Wand (Bashal'Aran, Darkshore) or the Cinder Wand (Arugal's Folly, Silverpine Forest).

### Levels 20 to 60

Shadow is how a priest levels. Pull with Mind Blast, put Shadow Word: Pain and Devouring Plague up, shield yourself if the mob reaches you, and finish with Mind Flay or a wand. Spirit Tap procs on every kill that gives experience and lets you chain pulls with little drinking.

From 20 the build finishes Shadow Focus at 21, takes Improved Mind Blast to 25 and Improved Shadow Word: Pain at 26 and 27, then Mind Flay at 28. Until 28 the wand is your filler. Improved Mind Flay comes at 29 and 30 and two points of Blackout at 31 and 32, then Vampiric Embrace, Shadow Weaving and Darkness, and Shadowform is your point at 42. The two early points in Wand Specialization cost you those two levels of the form, and they are worth it.

In Shadowform you cannot cast Renew or a heal. Power Word: Shield still works, so shield before the pull and let Vampiric Embrace and Devouring Plague top you up. Leave the form only for a heal you really need. Solo, cast Shadow Word: Death only when it will kill or you are well above half health: the backlash is real.
