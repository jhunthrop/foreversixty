## New race pairs

**Gnome Priest** is confirmed by Blizzard as one of six new combinations. Priest is not available to Skyborne.

## Talent structure

Talent trees keep the familiar shape: seven rows per tree and 51 points to spend. The one-point "gold medal" talents at 11, 21, and 31 points are still there, and Forever adds a fourth at the 16-point mark, so every tree has four of them. Principal Designer Kris Zierhut confirmed the 16-point addition in an interview.

Talents that existed mainly to unlock a group buff are becoming baseline abilities instead, which frees room in the trees for talents you notice. Some talents are unchanged from 1.12.

One outlet's tally from the demo trees counts 14 new, 27 changed, and 12 unchanged Priest talents.

## What changed in the demo

**Baseline abilities**, read off level-38 demo spellbooks:

- **Fear Ward is baseline for every Priest**, on a 3-minute cooldown, rather than a Dwarf racial.
- **Shadow Word: Death** is in as an execute-style Shadow spell, named by the Early Demise talent.
- **Devouring Plague** is a normal Shadow spell rather than an Undead racial, reaching rank 3 by level 38 on a 1-minute cooldown.

**Racial spells are still per-race, and reworked.** Human Priests get a new **Divine Grace**, an instant heal on a friendly target below 50% health that also removes Weakened Soul and cannot be cast on yourself, alongside **Feedback**. Gnome Priests get two new ones: **Confounding Flash**, which confuses up to five enemies within 8 yards for 3 seconds, and **Contingency Plan**, a Holy ward that absorbs and heals when an ally drops below 35% health. Night Elf **Elune's Grace** now reduces melee and ranged hit chance by 50% for 15 seconds or three misses. Troll **Hex of Weakness** now reduces attack power instead of flat melee damage, and also cuts healing received by 20%. Undead get **Dark Sacrifice**, converting health to mana, plus **Touch of Weakness**.

**New talents:** Penance in Discipline is a channelled ability that heals an ally or damages an enemy. Divine Aegis in Discipline turns critical heals into an absorb shield. Litany of Light in Holy returns mana for alternating between different heals. Prayer of Mending in Holy is a jumping heal that triggers on damage or on a direct heal.

**Changed talents:** Meditation gives significantly more mana regeneration while casting at rank 1, 17% against Classic's 5%. Spirit of Redemption lasts 15 seconds rather than 10. Spiritual Guidance now scales healing fully from Spirit but damage only weakly. Vampiric Embrace is shorter but can trigger Spirit Tap on a kill.

Bonus healing on gear now also carries one third as much bonus damage, and appears from level 10, which is aimed squarely at healers questing in the open world.

## Where these come from, and what they are worth

Except where a line is credited to Blizzard, everything in the section above was read off BlizzCon demo footage and show-floor screenshots by fan sites, mostly at level 38, which was the demo cap. Numbers can be wrong, ranks can be misread, and anything can change before launch. Nothing here is datamined.

Associate Production Director Clayton Stone has also said that Forever's launch is a deliberately conservative take after Season of Discovery, and that the team is starting by filling out skills classes did not have rather than reinventing them.
