## New race pairs

None of Blizzard's six new combinations is a Druid. Druid is available to both factions' Skyborne, with sky-blue forms created specifically for the race.

## Talent structure

Talent trees keep the familiar shape: seven rows per tree and 51 points to spend. The one-point "gold medal" talents at 11, 21, and 31 points are still there, and Forever adds a fourth at the 16-point mark, so every tree has four of them. Principal Designer Kris Zierhut confirmed the 16-point addition in an interview.

Talents that existed mainly to unlock a group buff are becoming baseline abilities instead, which frees room in the trees for talents you notice. Some talents are unchanged from 1.12.

One outlet's tally from the demo trees counts 15 new, 23 changed, and 14 unchanged Druid talents.

## What changed in the demo

**Baseline abilities**, read off level-38 demo spellbooks:

- **Omen of Clarity and Nature's Grasp are trainable**, not talents. Both were in the Balance tab at level 38.
- **Revive** is new: a 10-second out-of-combat resurrection. Rebirth keeps its 30-minute cooldown, so Druids no longer have to burn it out of combat.
- **Lacerate**, the Bear bleed from later expansions, is in as a baseline ability, with the Shredding Attacks talent cutting its Rage cost. It was not in the level 38 spellbook, so it is trained later.
- **Cure Poison is gone.** Abolish Poison is the only poison cure.

**New talents:** Genesis in Balance increases periodic damage and healing. Nature's Majesty grants spell and melee critical strike chance. Berserk in Feral is a major cooldown giving multi-target Mangle with no cooldown and 100% crit chance on combo point generators.

**Changed talents:** Feral Charge now has a separate Cat version, a leap that dazes, alongside the Bear interrupt charge. Primal Fury also grants a combo point chance on Cat crits, so it supports both forms. Heart of the Wild has moved much earlier in the tree with adjusted percentages. Furor's Cat energy recovery is now based on time spent out of form.

Two general changes matter a lot to Druid specifically. Improved Mark of the Wild is baseline, which frees points that every Druid used to spend. And bonus healing now carries one third as much bonus damage from level 10, which helps Restoration Druids questing.

## Where these come from, and what they are worth

Except where a line is credited to Blizzard, everything in the section above was read off BlizzCon demo footage and show-floor screenshots by fan sites, mostly at level 38, which was the demo cap. Numbers can be wrong, ranks can be misread, and anything can change before launch. Nothing here is datamined.

Associate Production Director Clayton Stone has also said that Forever's launch is a deliberately conservative take after Season of Discovery, and that the team is starting by filling out skills classes did not have rather than reinventing them.
