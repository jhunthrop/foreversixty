Druid is one of the four default classes available to the new Skyborne race, and Skyborne Druids have sky-blue Druid forms.

Beyond the class being available to Skyborne with a distinct form palette, and the general talent revamp Blizzard has said applies across classes, no other Druid-specific detail has been announced. This site's leveling routes, talent builds, and gear priorities for Druid will be written after we play the class in beta. See the [roadmap](/roadmap) for beta dates.
