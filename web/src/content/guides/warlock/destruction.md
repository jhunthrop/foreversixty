## Overview

Destruction keeps Immolate on the boss, fires Conflagrate and Shadowburn on cooldown to hold both halves of Shadow and Flame, and casts Incinerate in between.

### Strengths

- Conflagrate and Shadowburn are instants on short cooldowns, so you keep casting while you move
- Shadow and Flame keeps 20% more Shadow and 20% more Fire damage running all fight
- Ruin doubles the bonus on every Destruction crit
- No setup before the pull beyond your demon and Demon Armor

### Weaknesses

- Lapsing Immolate costs you Incinerate's bonus and Conflagrate
- Incinerate and Conflagrate are expensive, so you Life Tap often
- Big crits pull threat fast

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged Fire and Shadow damage
- **Brings:** Curse of the Elements when the raid assigns it to you (one curse and one bane per warlock on each target), Bane of Havoc for two-target fights, Healthstones, a Soulstone on a healer, summons and Portal of Summoning

## Talents and builds

The leveling and raid builds are the same Destruction tree. The raid build moves the Grim Reach and Soul Harvest points into Pandemic for bigger Corruption and Doom crits, and adds a point each to Cataclysm and Aftermath.

- **Bane** (5 points): Immolate becomes a 1.5 second cast, Incinerate 2 seconds, Shadow Bolt 2.5 seconds and Soul Fire 4 seconds.
- **Ruin** (5 points): doubles the crit bonus on all your Destruction spells.
- **Agonizing Flames** (3 points): 10% more damage on all your Destruction spells.
- **Aftermath** (3 points, 4 in the raid build): Immolate's first hit deals 30% more, 40% at 4 points.
- **Conflagrate** and **Fire and Brimstone** (4 points): an instant Fire hit on a target with your Immolate, 10 second cooldown, with 25% more crit chance from Fire and Brimstone.
- **Shadowburn** (1 point): an instant Shadow hit, 15 second cooldown, at any health. It costs a Soul Shard.
- **Shadow and Flame** (5 points): Conflagrate gives you 20% more Shadow damage for 20 seconds and Shadowburn 20% more Fire damage for 20 seconds. At 5 points Conflagrate never eats your Immolate and every Shadowburn refunds its shard on the spot.
- **Incinerate** (1 point): your filler from level 40. It deals 25% more while your Immolate is on the target.
- **Suppression** and **Improved Corruption** (10 points): 5% hit on every spell, 20% less threat, and an instant Corruption.

Bane of Havoc, Molten Skin and Destructive Reach are path points for this build. Havoc only earns its slot on fights with two targets.

## Rotation and priority

Before the pull: Demon Armor on, Succubus out, at least one shard spare for Shadowburn, a Healthstone, and Life Tap to full while the healers top you off. You have no Demonology talents, but the Succubus still earns her shard: her melee and Lash of Pain out-damage the Imp's Firebolt.

Opener: when your tank pulls on a timer and opens hard, start Immolate 1.5 seconds before the pull so it lands with the tank. That precast is worth a little damage over the fight. If your tank needs a second to settle, start Immolate as the tank reaches the boss instead. Then Corruption, Curse of the Elements if it is yours, Bane of Doom, Conflagrate, Shadowburn, and Incinerate from there.

Priority after that:

1. Corruption whenever it has fallen off. It is instant and cheap.
2. Bane of Doom the moment the last one fires. In the last minute of the fight a new Doom would never land, so use Bane of Agony instead.
3. Immolate when about 3 seconds are left on it. The cast lands as the old one runs out.
4. Conflagrate on cooldown while Immolate is up.
5. Shadowburn on cooldown, at any boss health.
6. Incinerate.

Conflagrate and Shadowburn on cooldown keep both halves of Shadow and Flame running the whole fight: Conflagrate's Shadow buff feeds Corruption, Doom and Shadowburn, and Shadowburn's Fire buff feeds Immolate, Conflagrate and Incinerate. Do not save Shadowburn for the execute. Its shard comes straight back.

Curses and banes have their own slots in Forever, so Curse of the Elements and Bane of Doom sit on the boss together. When another warlock holds Curse of the Elements, leave it to them: two do not stack. Your curse slot is then free for Curse of Recklessness, Tongues or Weakness if your raid leader asks.

Searing Pain is a threat spell, not a damage spell, and Incinerate does more. Keep it off your bars in raids.

Life Tap below about 10% mana and whenever you have to move. Each tap converts its base amount plus your Spirit, and Improved Life Tap adds 20%. Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune have separate cooldowns, so use both once you are low enough that none of it is wasted.

Soul Shards: walk into a raid with a full bag of them. Each Succubus summon, Soulstone, Healthstone and Spellstone costs one, and Portal of Summoning costs five on a one hour cooldown. In Forever, Drain Soul has a chance to give a shard on every target it damages, not only on a kill, so a few Drain Soul ticks on raid trash refill the bag. Shadowburn needs one in your bag to cast, even though Shadow and Flame hands it straight back.

Threat: you crit big and often, and Conflagrate and Shadowburn come early in the fight. If you climb to the top of the threat meter, stop casting Incinerate and let Corruption and your DoTs tick until the tank pulls ahead.

Movement: Corruption, both banes, Conflagrate, Shadowburn and Life Tap are all instant. When you know a move is coming in the next second or two, hold Conflagrate and Shadowburn for it.

Adds and AoE: on two targets, put Bane of Havoc on the one that must die and spend your casts on the other: 15% of that damage also lands on the Havoc target. Havoc takes your bane slot, so no Doom on that target. Rain of Fire for trash packs that stay put. Hellfire burns you as well.

## Stat priority

Spell power first. Hit matters up to the cap shown above, and Suppression gives you 5% of it. Fire power is the better school stat, since Incinerate, Immolate and Conflagrate are most of your damage. Crit pays double, since Ruin doubles its bonus on every Destruction spell. Shadow power covers Corruption, Doom and Shadowburn. Intellect is mana you need. Spirit only matters through Life Tap.

Flask and elixirs stack as they always have in Classic, and the school elixirs are separate buffs, so run Elixir of Shadow Power for your Shadow spells and Elixir of Fire Power for Incinerate, Immolate and Conflagrate. ## Races

Gnome and Orc are the picks. Gnome's Eureka! gives 8 seconds of 10% more damage and 10% lower cost every two minutes, but only on direct damage: Immolate's first hit, Conflagrate, Shadowburn, Soul Fire and Shadow Bolt among them. It does not touch Incinerate, so press it when Immolate, Conflagrate and Shadowburn are all coming up together. Expansive Mind's 5% more mana means fewer Life Taps. Orc's Blood Fury is 10% spell power for 15 seconds every two minutes, and Command makes your demon hit 5% harder: press Blood Fury at the pull with your trinkets. Troll's Berserking is 10% cast speed on a three minute cooldown, a step behind Orc. Human's Ruthless is 1% crit. Undead's Touch of the Grave drains health from your target now and then.

## Professions

Tailoring and Enchanting. Much of your pre-raid gear is tailored cloth, Enchanting makes some of the best pre-raid caster pieces, and you enchant your own gear. Alchemy is the swap when your guild expects a flask every raid night.

## Leveling

Destruction kills single mobs fast but has no self-heal, so pull one at a time. Before 40, Shadow Bolt is your filler: Immolate, Corruption and Bane of Agony on the mob, Shadow Bolt it down, and finish with Shadowburn. Until Shadow and Flame reaches 5 points, Shadowburn does not always refund its shard and Conflagrate can eat your Immolate, so Drain Soul the last few percent of a mob's health to keep shards coming. From 40, Incinerate replaces Shadow Bolt. Keep the Voidwalker out when pulls get messy, and Life Tap between pulls instead of drinking.
