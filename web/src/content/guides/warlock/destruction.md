## Overview

Destruction keeps Immolate on the boss, fires Conflagrate and Shadowburn on cooldown to hold both halves of Shadow and Flame, and casts Incinerate in between.

### Strengths

- Conflagrate and Shadowburn are instants on short cooldowns, so you keep casting while you move
- Shadow and Flame keeps 20% more Shadow and 20% more Fire damage running all fight
- Ruin doubles the bonus on every Destruction crit
- Little setup before the pull: your demon, Demon Armor and a full mana bar

### Weaknesses

- Lapsing Immolate costs you Incinerate's bonus and Conflagrate
- Conflagrate and Shadowburn on cooldown burn mana fast, so you Life Tap often
- Big crits pull threat fast

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged Fire and Shadow damage
- **Brings:** Curse of the Elements when the raid assigns it to you (one curse and one bane per warlock on each target), Bane of Havoc for two-target fights, Healthstones, a Soulstone on a healer, summons and Portal of Summoning

## Talents and builds

The raid build keeps the leveling build's Destruction core and drops its safety picks: Destructive Reach and Molten Skin come out, Aftermath goes to 5 and Cataclysm to 3. In Affliction the Grim Reach and Soul Harvest points go into a full Pandemic, and three points of Malevolence follow it.

- **Bane** (5 points): Immolate becomes a 1.5 second cast, Incinerate 2 seconds, Shadow Bolt 2.5 seconds and Soul Fire 4 seconds.
- **Ruin** (5 points): doubles the crit bonus on all your Destruction spells.
- **Agonizing Flames** (3 points): 10% more damage on all your Destruction spells.
- **Aftermath** (3 points, 5 in the raid build): Immolate's first hit deals 30% more, 50% at 5 points.
- **Conflagrate** and **Fire and Brimstone** (4 points): an instant Fire hit on a target with your Immolate, 10 second cooldown, with 25% more crit chance from Fire and Brimstone.
- **Shadowburn** (1 point): an instant Shadow hit, 15 second cooldown, at any health. It costs a Soul Shard.
- **Shadow and Flame** (5 points): Conflagrate gives you 20% more Shadow damage for 20 seconds and Shadowburn 20% more Fire damage for 20 seconds. At 5 points Conflagrate never eats your Immolate and every Shadowburn refunds its shard on the spot.
- **Incinerate** (1 point): your filler from level 40. It deals 25% more while your Immolate is on the target.
- **Suppression** and **Improved Corruption** (10 points): 5% hit on every spell, 20% less threat, and an instant Corruption.
- **Pandemic** and **Malevolence** (raid build, 6 points): three points of Pandemic double the crit bonus on Corruption and both banes, and three of Malevolence add 3% crit to your Shadow spells: Corruption ticks, Doom and Shadowburn.

Bane of Havoc is the path to Incinerate, and it only earns a cast on fights with two targets. The leveling build also takes Molten Skin and Destructive Reach for the quests: Molten Skin takes 6% off every hit you take, and Destructive Reach adds 20% range to your Destruction spells, so you can stand clear of cleaves and fire on the floor. At a raid boss the healers cover the first and you rarely need the second, so the raid build spends those points on damage.

## Rotation and priority

Before the pull: Demon Armor on, Succubus out, at least one shard spare for Shadowburn, a Healthstone, and Life Tap to full while the healers top you off. You have no Demonology talents, but the Succubus still earns her shard: her melee and Lash of Pain out-damage the Imp's Firebolt.

Opener: when your tank pulls on a timer and opens hard, start Immolate 1.5 seconds before the pull so it lands with the tank. That precast is worth a little damage over the fight. If your tank needs a second to settle, start Immolate as the tank reaches the boss instead. Then Curse of the Elements if it is yours, so the whole raid gets it from the first global, then Corruption, Bane of Doom, Conflagrate, Shadowburn, and Incinerate from there.

Priority after that:

1. Corruption whenever it has fallen off. It is instant and ticks for 18 seconds off one global.
2. Bane of Doom the moment the last one fires. In the last minute of the fight a new Doom would never land, so use Bane of Agony instead.
3. Immolate when about 3 seconds are left on it. It lands a moment before the old one ends, so it never drops.
4. Conflagrate on cooldown while Immolate is up.
5. Shadowburn on cooldown, at any boss health.
6. Incinerate.

Conflagrate and Shadowburn on cooldown keep both halves of Shadow and Flame running the whole fight: Conflagrate's Shadow buff feeds Corruption, Doom and Shadowburn, and Shadowburn's Fire buff feeds Immolate, Conflagrate and Incinerate. Do not save Shadowburn for the execute. Its shard comes straight back.

Curses and banes have their own slots in Forever, so Curse of the Elements and Bane of Doom sit on the boss together. When another warlock holds Curse of the Elements, leave it to them: two do not stack. Your curse slot is then free for Curse of Recklessness, Tongues or Weakness if your raid leader asks.

Searing Pain is a threat spell, not a damage spell, and Incinerate does more. Keep it off your bars in raids.

Life Tap below about 10% mana and whenever you have to move. Each tap converts its base amount plus your Spirit, and Improved Life Tap adds 20%. Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune have separate cooldowns, so use both once you are low enough that none of it is wasted.

Soul Shards: walk into a raid with a full bag of them. Each Succubus summon, Soulstone, Healthstone and Firestone costs one, and Portal of Summoning costs five on a one hour cooldown. In Forever, Drain Soul has a chance to give a shard on every target it damages, not only on a kill. Shadowburn needs one in your bag to cast, even though Shadow and Flame hands it straight back.

Threat: you crit big and often, and Conflagrate and Shadowburn come early in the fight. If you climb to the top of the threat meter, stop casting Incinerate and let Corruption and your DoTs tick until the tank pulls ahead.

Movement: Corruption, both banes, Conflagrate, Shadowburn and Life Tap are all instant. When you know a move is coming in the next second or two, hold Conflagrate and Shadowburn for it.

Adds and AoE: on two targets, spend your casts on the one that must die and put Bane of Havoc on the other: 15% of everything you deal also lands on it. Havoc takes your bane slot, so no Doom on that target. Rain of Fire for trash packs that stay put. Hellfire burns you as well.

## Stat priority

Hit matters up to the cap and adds nothing past it: Suppression already gives you 5% of it, and a resisted Incinerate is a whole cast lost. Spell power feeds every spell you cast. Fire power feeds Incinerate, Immolate and Conflagrate, which are most of your damage. Crit pays double, since Ruin doubles its bonus on every Destruction spell. Shadow power covers Corruption, Doom and Shadowburn. Intellect is mana you need, plus a little crit, which Ruin doubles like any other. Spirit only matters through Life Tap. ## Best in slot

The pre-raid set leans on Tailoring and Enchanting crafts.

## Enchants and consumables

Flask and elixirs stack as they always have in Classic, and the school elixirs are separate buffs, so run Elixir of Shadow Power for your Shadow spells and Elixir of Fire Power for Incinerate, Immolate and Conflagrate. Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune have separate cooldowns, so use both, and keep a Healthstone for the Life Taps that leave you low. Put Brilliant Wizard Oil or a Firestone on the weapon. A Firestone costs a shard; Ruin doubles the bonus on the crits its 2% adds, and its Fire damage feeds Incinerate, Immolate and Conflagrate.

## Races

Human and Orc are the picks. Ruthless is 1% crit on every spell, and with Ruin every Destruction crit lands for double the usual bonus; The Human Spirit's 10% more Spirit makes every Life Tap bigger. Gnome is a step behind. Eureka! gives 8 seconds of 10% more damage and 10% lower cost on your direct damage spells every two minutes, Immolate's first hit, Conflagrate and Shadowburn among them, but Corruption, the banes and Immolate's burn get nothing from it. Press it as Conflagrate and Shadowburn come off cooldown; Incinerate gets nothing from it, so cast it as you always do. Expansive Mind's 5% more mana means fewer Life Taps. Orc's Blood Fury is 10% spell power for 15 seconds every two minutes, and Command makes your demon hit 5% harder: press Blood Fury at the pull with your trinkets. Troll's Berserking is 10% cast speed on a three minute cooldown, a step behind Orc. Undead is close behind Orc: Touch of the Grave gives your spells a chance to drain up to 5% of your maximum health from the target, at most once every 15 seconds.

## Professions

Tailoring and Enchanting. Tailored cloth carries your pre-raid set, Enchanting adds a few strong caster pieces, and you enchant your own gear. Engineering goggles need Engineering to wear, so they are only yours if you train it in place of Enchanting. Alchemy is the swap when your guild expects a flask every raid night, and it keeps your Fire Power elixirs cheap.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Train Immolate at 1, Corruption at 4, Life Tap at 6 and Bane of Agony at 8. Do the Voidwalker class quest from your warlock trainer the day you hit 10. Drain Life at 14 is your heal.

Your points at 10 and 11 go into Destructive Reach: 20% more range on Shadow Bolt and Immolate, so you open from farther out and get a little more casting done before the mob reaches you. Bane from 12 to 16 cuts half a second off Shadow Bolt and Immolate. Molten Skin from 17 to 19 takes 6% off all damage you take.

Wand the mob down once Immolate and Corruption are ticking; a Lesser Magic Wand at 5 and a Greater Magic Wand at 13 from an enchanter carry you to 20. Swap your starting weapon for the first quest staff or dagger with Intellect or Stamina.

### Levels 20 to 60

Destruction kills single mobs fast but has little sustain: no Siphon Life and no talented drains, so Drain Life and Death Coil are your only heals. Pull one at a time. Before 40, Shadow Bolt is your filler: Immolate, Corruption and Bane of Agony on the mob, Shadow Bolt it down, and from 30, once you take the talent, finish with Shadowburn. A mob that dies within 8 seconds of Shadowburn gives the shard back, so as a finisher it is free; on a dungeon boss, cast it on cooldown for as long as you have shards to spend. Shadow Bolt rank 5 arrives at 28 and is worth casting right away. Shadowburn joins at 30, and on level 30 gear your mana cannot carry the 160-mana rank 5 as well as the 110-mana rank 4, so cast rank 4 at 30 and 31 and go back to rank 5 at 32. Conflagrate joins at 34 and eats your Immolate every time until you take Shadow and Flame, so cast it once Immolate has 6 seconds or less left and recast Immolate right after. From 39, with the first point of Shadow and Flame, cast it on cooldown: the Shadow damage it gives you is worth more than the burn it may eat. Drain Soul the last few percent of a mob when you need shards for stones and summons. Put every point into Destruction until Shadow and Flame is full at 44, with Incinerate at 40 on the way, and only then go back for Improved Life Tap, Suppression and Improved Corruption. From 40, Incinerate replaces Shadow Bolt. Conflagrate rank 3 and Immolate rank 5 arrive with it, but on level 40 gear your mana runs short. At 40 keep casting Immolate rank 4 and move up to rank 5 at 41. At 41 drop Corruption from rank 4 back to rank 3 for that one level. Each is a small gain that costs you nothing, and from 42 the top rank of both wins again. Keep the Voidwalker out when pulls get messy, and Life Tap between pulls instead of drinking.
