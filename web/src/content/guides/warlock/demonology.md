## Overview

Demonology sacrifices the Imp for 15% Shadow damage, keeps a Succubus out for 10% more, and casts Shadow Bolt, with free Soul Fires below 35%.

### Strengths

- The most Shadow bonuses of any warlock build at once: the Imp sacrifice, Master Demonologist and Soul Link, plus Demonic Knowledge's spell damage
- Decimation turns the last 35% of every boss into a Soul Fire burn
- Soul Link sends 30% of the damage you take to your demon
- The demon does real damage of its own

### Weaknesses

- The full setup takes a minute before the pull: two summons, a sacrifice and Soul Link
- A dead Succubus takes Master Demonologist, Soul Link and Demonic Knowledge with it until you resummon
- Mostly Shadow Bolt: few instants for movement

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged Shadow damage with a demon
- **Brings:** Curse of the Elements when the raid assigns it to you (one curse and one bane per warlock on each target), Healthstones, a Soulstone on a healer, summons and Portal of Summoning

## Talents and builds

The leveling build stops at 30 Demonology points, before Demonic Pact, and keeps Improved Corruption and Improved Imp. The raid build goes to Pact and spends its Destruction points on Shadow Bolt.

- **Demonic Sacrifice** (1 point): in Forever the Imp's sacrifice gives 15% more Shadow damage, and the Succubus' gives 15% more Fire. Without Demonic Pact any summon cancels it, so the leveling build takes it as the path to Soul Link.
- **Master Demonologist** (5 points): a buff on you and your demon while it is out. The Succubus or Incubus gives 10% more Shadow damage, which is why the raid build keeps one out.
- **Soul Link** (1 point): you and your demon deal 3% more damage, and the demon takes 30% of the damage you take. It needs a demon out and ends when the demon dies or is dismissed.
- **Demonic Knowledge** (3 points): you and your demon gain spell damage equal to your level while a demon is out, 60 at level 60.
- **Decimation** (2 points): Soul Fire's cooldown drops by 90%. Each Shadow Bolt on a target below 35% health hits 6% harder and gives you 10 seconds of Soul Fires with 40% less cast time and no Soul Shard cost.
- **Master Summoner** (2 points): 4 seconds off every summon and 40% off its mana. A summon becomes a 6 second cast, which is what makes a mid-fight resummon possible.
- **Fel Vitality** (3 points): 15% more mana for you and your demons.

The raid build adds what the leveling build cannot use. Demonic Pact, the 31st point, keeps your sacrifice when you summon a different demon; only a new Imp ends it. So you sacrifice the Imp and still play with a Succubus out. Improved Shadow Bolt makes the target take 20% more Shadow damage from you for 12 seconds after a Shadow Bolt crit, and in Forever that debuff is yours alone. Ruin doubles the crit bonus on Shadow Bolt and Soul Fire, and all 5 points of Unholy Power go into the Succubus.

Improved Health Funnel, Demonic Aegis and Improved Voidwalker add no damage. They are path points that keep you and your demon alive. Without Improved Corruption, Corruption is a 2 second cast in the raid build, and it is still worth it.

Your demon scales from you in Forever, and raid buffs do not land on it. It inherits part of your spell damage, attack power and armor. Its spells hit as often as yours, and its melee gets 8/17 of your hit. Demons almost never crit in melee against a boss. Lash of Pain crits at about a normal caster's rate, more with Malevolence.

## Rotation and priority

The setup, in order, starting about a minute before the pull:

1. Summon your Imp.
2. Cast Demonic Sacrifice on it.
3. Summon your Succubus. Demonic Pact keeps the sacrifice.
4. Put on Demon Armor, make a Healthstone and Life Tap back to full.
5. Cast Soul Link a couple of seconds before the pull.

Each summon is a 6 second cast with Master Summoner, and the Succubus costs a Soul Shard. Send the Succubus to the boss's back once the tank has it.

On the pull: Curse of the Elements first if you hold it, then Corruption and Bane of Doom, then Immolate, then Shadow Bolt for the rest of the fight. Curses and banes have their own slots in Forever, so Curse of the Elements and Doom sit on the boss together. When another warlock holds Curse of the Elements, leave it to them: two do not stack. Your curse slot is then free for Curse of Recklessness, Tongues or Weakness if your raid leader asks.

Bane of Doom hits once, for a big chunk, when its minute runs out, so recast it the moment it fires. A Doom cast with less than a minute left never lands, so in the last minute put Bane of Agony up instead. Doom's damage scales hard with your spell damage, which is why it beats Agony here.

Recast Corruption when it falls off and never clip it. Start Immolate's cast with about 3 seconds left on the old one. Everything else is Shadow Bolt.

Below 35% the fight changes. Every Shadow Bolt now gives you 10 seconds of Decimation. Decimation also cuts Soul Fire's cooldown to 6 seconds all the time, so the burn is Shadow Bolt, Soul Fire, Shadow Bolt, Soul Fire: each Soul Fire is a 2.4 second cast that costs no shard and beats Shadow Bolt, and each Shadow Bolt between them keeps the buff up. Outside Decimation, Soul Fire is a 4 second cast that costs a shard. Never cast it above 35%.

Searing Pain also triggers Decimation, but it carries a lot of extra threat and Shadow Bolt hits harder with your Shadow bonuses. Keep it off your bars in raids.

Life Tap below about 10% mana and whenever you have to move. Each tap converts its base amount plus your Spirit. Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune have separate cooldowns: use both once you are low enough that none of it is wasted.

Soul Shards: walk into a raid with a full bag of them. Each Succubus summon, Soulstone, Healthstone and Spellstone costs one, and Portal of Summoning costs five on a one hour cooldown. In Forever, Drain Soul has a chance to give a shard on every target it damages, not only on a kill, so a few Drain Soul ticks on raid trash refill the bag. Demonology spends more of them than the other specs: every wipe or dead Succubus is another summon.

If the Succubus dies: resummon her (6 seconds, a shard, a big chunk of mana), then cast Soul Link again before you go back to Shadow Bolt. The Imp sacrifice survives the resummon. On fights with heavy raid damage, park her behind the boss and Health Funnel her before she drops.

Movement: Corruption needs you to stand still in this build, and Doom is only due once a minute, so Life Tap is what you cast on the move. Adds: Doom only fires on a target that lives a minute, so Corruption is your spell for adds. Rain of Fire for trash packs that stay put.

## Stat priority

Spell power first. Hit is close behind: Suppression gives you 5%, and the table above shows how much more you need. Shadow power is nearly as good as spell power, but Immolate and Soul Fire are Fire. Crit pays twice through Ruin and Improved Shadow Bolt. Intellect is mana. Spirit only matters through Life Tap.

## Races

Gnome and Troll are the picks. Eureka! gives 8 seconds of 10% more damage and 10% lower cost on your direct damage spells every two minutes. Save it for the Soul Fire burn below 35%. Expansive Mind adds 5% mana. Berserking is 10% cast speed on a three minute cooldown: press it with your trinkets, and again for the Decimation phase if it is back. Orc is the strong Horde alternative for this spec: Blood Fury is 10% spell power for 15 seconds every two minutes, and Command makes your Succubus hit 5% harder. Human's Ruthless is 1% crit. Undead's Touch of the Grave drains health from your target now and then.

## Professions

Tailoring and Enchanting. Much of your pre-raid gear is tailored cloth, Enchanting makes some of the best pre-raid caster pieces, and you enchant your own gear. Alchemy is the swap when your guild expects a flask every raid night.

## Leveling

Demonology levels well with a demon that tanks. Until Demonic Pact at 60, any summon cancels your sacrifice, so solo you keep a demon out for Soul Link, Master Demonologist and Demonic Knowledge. Use the Voidwalker when pulls hurt, since Master Demonologist then cuts the physical damage you take by 10%. Use the Succubus when you want speed. Put Corruption and Bane of Agony on the mob, let the demon hold it, and Shadow Bolt or wand it down. From 48, when you learn Soul Fire, Decimation's free Soul Fires finish every mob below 35%. Drain Soul the last few percent of a mob's health when you need shards, since every resummon costs one.
