## Overview

### Strengths

- Corruption, both banes and Siphon Life are instant, so you keep doing damage while you move
- Every DoT tick can crit, and Pandemic doubles the bonus on all of them but Immolate's burn
- Siphon Life gives you a small, steady heal on top of your damage

### Weaknesses

- Slow start: your first three globals, four when you hold Curse of the Elements, go into putting DoTs up
- Short fights and targets that die fast waste the DoTs you put up
- Life Tap costs health, and a long fight drains it

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged Shadow damage, DoTs plus Wrack
- **Brings:** Curse of the Elements when the raid assigns it to you (one curse and one bane per warlock on each target), Healthstones, a Soulstone on a healer, summons and Portal of Summoning

## Talents and builds

The leveling build carries you on Soul Siphon and Wrack while you quest, fills Malevolence and takes Nightfall once Wrack is yours, and from 47 spends its points on Bane and Aftermath for a faster, harder Immolate. The raid build goes to 35 points in Affliction for all five points of Malevolence, Nightfall, Soul Siphon and Wrack, sacrifices the Imp through Demonic Sacrifice, and spends its Destruction points on Improved Shadow Bolt, so a Nightfall bolt that crits sets up your Wrack and DoTs.

- **Suppression** (5 points): 5% hit on every spell and 20% less threat. Every warlock build takes all five.
- **Improved Corruption** (5 points): Corruption becomes instant and hits 10% harder.
- **Amplify Curse** (1 point): no global cooldown, a 3 minute cooldown and 30 seconds to use it. Your next Bane of Agony or Curse of Weakness is 50% stronger, or your next Curse of Exhaustion 20%. In a raid it goes on your next Bane of Agony refresh once it is off cooldown; never clip a running Agony for it.
- **Pandemic** (3 points): Corruption, both banes, Siphon Life, the drains and Wrack crit for double the usual bonus.
- **Improved Bane of Agony** (2 points): 10% more on the Agony you keep up all fight. The leveling build takes it at 42 and 43, after Wrack.
- **Malevolence** (5 points): 1% crit a point on your Shadow spells, DoT ticks and Wrack included, and Pandemic doubles the bonus on those. The leveling build takes two points at 26 and 27 and the last three at 44 to 46.
- **Nightfall** (2 points, at 43 in the raid build and 52 to 53 while leveling): Corruption and Wrack ticks have a 4% chance to give you a Shadow Trance, which makes your next Shadow Bolt instant, though it still costs full mana. Cast it between two Wracks.
- **Shadow Mastery** (5 points): 10% more Shadow damage on everything you cast.
- **Siphon Life** (1 point): a 30 second DoT that heals you, and one more Affliction effect for Soul Siphon.
- **Soul Siphon** and **Wrack** (4 points): Wrack is a 6 second channel that deals Shadow damage every second, and while it runs your Corruption, Siphon Life and Bane of Agony on that target tick twice as fast. They deal the same ticks, only sooner, so they also run out sooner. Bane of Doom and Immolate keep their own pace. Soul Siphon adds 12% to Wrack and the drains for each of those three on the target, up to 36%; Bane of Doom does not count. With three of your effects on the boss, Wrack and the faster DoTs out-damage Shadow Bolt for a fraction of the mana, so both builds take it and it is your filler from 40.
- **Demonic Sacrifice** (1 point, raid): sacrifice the Imp before the pull for Burning Shadow, 15% more Shadow damage for two hours. Nearly everything you cast on a boss is Shadow, so it is worth more than any demon at your side. Summoning any demon ends it.
- **Improved Shadow Bolt** (5 points, raid): each Shadow Bolt crit makes the boss take 20% more Shadow damage from you for 12 seconds, Wrack and your DoTs included.
- **Bane** and **Aftermath** (10 points, leveling): Immolate casts in 1.5 seconds and its first hit deals 50% more. You keep Immolate up while you level, and nothing left in the Affliction tree pays more for those points once Wrack is yours.

The raid build drops Soul Harvest, Improved Life Tap, Bane, Aftermath and the two points in Unholy Power, and keeps Grim Reach for range. In Demonology it takes Demonic Embrace and Fel Vitality on the way to Demonic Sacrifice, and Demonic Aegis for Demon Armor. With the Imp gone, Immolate is a slow Fire cast that Burning Shadow does nothing for, so the build skips Bane and Aftermath and drops Immolate from the rotation. Neither build takes Improved Drains: it does nothing for Wrack, and Wrack replaces Drain Life from 40.

## Rotation and priority

**Pre-pull.** Demon Armor on and a Healthstone in your bag. Summon the Imp and sacrifice it with Demonic Sacrifice for Burning Shadow, then Life Tap to full while the healers top you off. Any demon you summon after that ends it, so leave the pet slot empty and sacrifice a fresh Imp after a wipe.

**Curses.** Curse of the Elements goes up first if you hold it, since every spell the raid casts hits harder from that global on. When another warlock holds it, your curse slot is free for whatever the raid leader asks: Curse of Recklessness for a melee-heavy raid (505 armor), Curse of Tongues on a caster, Curse of Weakness on a boss that hits the tank hard. Amplify Curse also strengthens Curse of Weakness and goes to whichever of the two you cast first, so if you carry Weakness, press Amplify Curse on your Bane of Agony and cast Weakness after it.

**DoTs.** Keep Corruption, Siphon Life and Bane of Agony up all fight, even at 60: Bane of Doom gets nothing from Wrack or Soul Siphon, so Agony stays your bane. From 40, each Wrack burns through about 12 seconds of those three. Check them before every channel and recast any with 3 seconds or less left; one with a little more lapses partway through the channel, since the channel burns 12 seconds of its clock, and you refresh it right after. Otherwise never clip them: a recast throws away whatever was left on the old one. Immolate, while you level without the sacrifice, is the one you recast early.

**Nightfall.** When a Shadow Trance comes up mid-channel, let the current Wrack finish, then cast the instant Shadow Bolt before the next one.

**Mana.** Improved Life Tap in the leveling build adds 20% to every tap. Drink your Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune on cooldown once you are low enough that none of it is wasted.

**Soul Shards.** Walk into a raid with a full bag of them. Each Succubus summon, Soulstone, Healthstone and Spellstone costs one, and Portal of Summoning costs five on a one hour cooldown. In Forever, Drain Soul has a chance to give a shard on every target it damages, not only on a kill, so a few Drain Soul ticks on raid trash refill the bag.

**Execute.** Affliction has no execute spell. Near the end, stop refreshing any DoT that will not get half its ticks in before the boss dies; your last Bane of Agony wants most of its ticks. Channel Wrack instead while your other DoTs are still up.

**Movement.** Refresh DoTs, or Life Tap with nothing to refresh.

**Adds and AoE.** Put Corruption and Agony on each of two or three adds that live 15 seconds or more. Rain of Fire for trash packs that stay put. Hellfire burns you as well, so use it only with the pack on top of you and a healer ready.

## Stat priority

Suppression gives you 5% of the hit cap, and a missed bane or Corruption is a whole DoT lost. Shadow power feeds nearly every spell you cast, since almost everything Affliction casts is Shadow. DoT ticks crit too, and Pandemic doubles the bonus on all but Immolate's. Intellect adds about 1% crit for every 60 points. Spirit only matters through Life Tap, so let it decide between two otherwise equal pieces.

## Best in slot

The pre-raid set leans on Tailoring and Enchanting crafts.

## Enchants and consumables

Flask and elixirs stack as they always have in Classic, and the school elixirs are separate buffs, so run Elixir of Shadow Power for your Shadow spells. With the Imp sacrificed you cast no Fire spell on a boss, so Elixir of Fire Power does nothing for you. Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune have separate cooldowns, so use both, and keep a Healthstone for the Life Taps that leave you low. Put Brilliant Wizard Oil or a Spellstone on the weapon; a Spellstone costs a shard.

## Races

Human and Orc are the picks. Ruthless is 1% crit on every spell, DoT ticks included, and The Human Spirit makes every Life Tap bigger. Orc's Command does nothing once your Imp is sacrificed; press Blood Fury with your trinkets once the DoTs are up. Gnome is a step behind: whatever its tooltip says about non-periodic abilities, Eureka!'s damage also reaches Wrack and your drains, but not Corruption, the banes or Immolate's burn, so press it as you start a Wrack; Expansive Mind helps a spec that taps. Troll's Berserking is a step behind Orc. Undead is about level with Orc: Touch of the Grave helps a spec that pays for mana in health.

## Professions

Tailoring and Enchanting: tailored cloth and a couple of enchanted caster pieces make up much of your pre-raid set, and you enchant your own gear. Alchemy is the swap when your guild expects a flask every raid night.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Train Corruption at 4, Life Tap at 6 and Bane of Agony at 8. Do the Voidwalker class quest from your warlock trainer the day you hit 10; it is the pet that makes solo play easy. Drain Life comes at 14.

- **10 and 11:** Improved Life Tap, for 20% more mana from every tap.
- **12 to 16:** Suppression.
- **17 to 21:** Improved Corruption. Each point takes 0.4 seconds off Corruption's 2 second cast, and at 21 it is instant. Until then, open with it while the mob is still running at you.

Wands do most of your killing before 20: a Lesser Magic Wand at 5 and a Greater Magic Wand at 13, both made by Enchanting, from an enchanter or the Auction House. Take cloth quest rewards with Intellect and Stamina over anything a vendor sells, and replace your starting weapon with the first quest staff or dagger that gives Intellect or Stamina.

### Levels 20 to 60

Affliction is the easiest warlock to level. Put Corruption, Immolate and Bane of Agony on the mob, then Siphon Life from 30, and let your Voidwalker tank. The Succubus comes at 20 and the Felhunter at 30; keep the Voidwalker out until your gear can take a hit. Drain Life while the DoTs run, and wand the mob down when the DoTs are enough.

From 22, press Amplify Curse on your Bane of Agony whenever it is ready. From 40, channel Wrack where you used to Drain Life: with Soul Siphon full, Corruption, Siphon Life and Bane of Agony on the target add 36% to both, Wrack makes those three tick twice as fast, and it costs far less mana than the Shadow Bolts it replaces.

Cast Drain Soul on the last few percent of a mob's health whenever you need Soul Shards; from 34, Soul Harvest turns each of those kills into mana regeneration, so you drink less.

Talents after Improved Corruption:

- **22:** Amplify Curse.
- **23 to 25:** Pandemic.
- **26 and 27:** Malevolence.
- **28 and 29:** Grim Reach, paying the row for Siphon Life.
- **30:** Siphon Life.
- **31 to 33:** Soul Siphon.
- **34:** the first point of Soul Harvest.
- **35 to 39:** Shadow Mastery.
- **40:** Wrack.
- **41:** the second point of Soul Harvest.
- **42 and 43:** Improved Bane of Agony.
- **44 to 46:** the last three points of Malevolence.
- **47 to 51:** Bane, for a 1.5 second Immolate.
- **52 and 53:** Nightfall. Cast the instant Shadow Bolt between two Wracks.
- **54 to 58:** Aftermath, 50% more on Immolate's first hit.
- **59 and 60:** Unholy Power for your demon.
