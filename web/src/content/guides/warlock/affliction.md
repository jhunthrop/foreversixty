## Overview

Affliction keeps three DoTs and a bane rolling on the boss and fills the gaps with Shadow Bolt, and every one of those ticks can crit in Forever.

### Strengths

- Corruption, both banes and Siphon Life are instant, so you keep doing damage while you move
- Every DoT tick can crit, and Pandemic doubles the bonus on those crits
- Siphon Life gives you a small, steady heal on top of your damage

### Weaknesses

- Slow start: your first five globals go into putting DoTs up
- Short fights and targets that die fast waste the DoTs you put up
- Life Tap costs health, and a long fight drains it

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged Shadow damage, DoTs plus Shadow Bolt
- **Brings:** Curse of the Elements when the raid assigns it to you (one curse and one bane per warlock on each target), Healthstones, a Soulstone on a healer, summons and Portal of Summoning

## Talents and builds

The leveling build is a real Affliction tree: Nightfall, Soul Siphon and Wrack make the drains and the free Shadow Bolts carry you while you quest. The raid build is a Shadow Bolt build that keeps its DoTs up: it stops at 30 points in Affliction and buys Improved Shadow Bolt and Ruin in Destruction.

- **Suppression** (5 points): 5% hit on every spell and 20% less threat. Every warlock build takes all five.
- **Improved Corruption** (5 points): Corruption becomes instant and hits 10% harder.
- **Amplify Curse** (1 point): no global cooldown, a 3 minute cooldown and 30 seconds to use it. Your next Bane of Agony or Curse of Weakness is 50% stronger, or your next Curse of Exhaustion 20%. In a raid it goes on the Bane of Agony you put up in a boss's last minute.
- **Pandemic** (3 points): Corruption, both banes, Siphon Life, the drains and Wrack crit for double the usual bonus.
- **Nightfall** (2 points): each Corruption, Drain Life, Drain Soul or Wrack tick has a 4% chance to make your next Shadow Bolt instant. The leveling build takes it for free instant Shadow Bolts while you quest. The raid build drops it, and its points go toward Malevolence.
- **Shadow Mastery** (5 points): 10% more Shadow damage on everything you cast.
- **Siphon Life** (1 point): a 30 second DoT that heals you, and one more Affliction effect for Soul Siphon.
- **Soul Siphon** and **Wrack** (4 points): Wrack is a 6 second channel that deals Shadow damage every second and makes the target take 10% more from your other Shadow DoTs. Soul Siphon adds 12% to Wrack and the drains for each of your Affliction effects on the target, up to 36%. Cheap and strong solo. In a raid it trails Shadow Bolt without Soul Siphon and is close to it with Soul Siphon.

The raid build trades Nightfall, Soul Siphon, Wrack, Soul Harvest and the three points of Improved Drains (a raid rotation casts no drain), along with Improved Health Funnel and Improved Voidwalker, for Malevolence, Improved Shadow Bolt and Ruin. It also takes one point of Improved Bane of Agony for 5% more Bane of Agony damage, and keeps Grim Reach for range. Malevolence gives your Shadow spells 5% crit, DoT ticks included. Improved Shadow Bolt makes the target take 20% more Shadow damage from you for 12 seconds after a Shadow Bolt crit; in Forever that debuff is yours alone, not the raid's. Four points of Ruin raise the crit bonus of your Shadow Bolt and Immolate by 80%. Its Demonology points are Unholy Power for your Succubus and Demonic Aegis for Demon Armor.

## Rotation and priority

Before the pull, get set up: Demon Armor on, Succubus out, a Healthstone in your bag, and Life Tap to full mana while the healers top you off. The Succubus costs a shard where the Imp is free, but her melee and Lash of Pain do more damage than the Imp's Firebolt, and Unholy Power makes her hit harder still.

Once the tank has the boss: Curse of the Elements first if you hold it, since every spell the raid casts hits harder from that global on, and it lasts five minutes. Then Corruption, Bane of Doom, Siphon Life and Immolate, and Shadow Bolt from then on. Curses and banes have their own slots in Forever, so Curse of the Elements and your bane sit on the boss together. When another warlock holds Curse of the Elements, leave it to them: two of them on one boss do not stack. Your curse slot is then free for whatever the raid leader asks: Curse of Recklessness for a melee-heavy raid (505 armor), Curse of Tongues on a caster, Curse of Weakness on a boss that hits the tank hard. Amplify Curse also strengthens Curse of Weakness and goes to whichever of the two you cast first, so if you hold Weakness, put your last Bane of Agony up before you refresh Weakness.

Bane of Doom is your bane on a boss. It hits once, for a big chunk, when its minute runs out, so recast it the moment it fires. A Doom cast with less than a minute of the fight left never lands, so once the last one has fired, press Amplify Curse and put Bane of Agony up instead. Improved Bane of Agony and Pandemic make that last Agony count.

Never clip a DoT. A recast throws away whatever was left on the old one, and Bane of Agony saves its biggest ticks for the end. Recast Corruption and Siphon Life when they fall off. Immolate is the one exception: start its cast with about 3 seconds left. It lands a moment before the old one ends, and the fresh direct hit is worth the tick you give up.

This build does not take Wrack. Without Soul Siphon behind it, Wrack is a 6 second channel that Shadow Bolt beats, so Shadow Bolt is your filler. The Level 60 build above is the one that channels Wrack, with Nightfall and Soul Siphon behind it. It is built for questing, with Soul Harvest, Improved Drains and Improved Health Funnel where this one takes Malevolence, Improved Shadow Bolt and Ruin, so in a raid take this build and hard cast Shadow Bolt.

Life Tap when you drop below about 10% mana, and Life Tap whenever you have to move with nothing to refresh. Each tap converts its base amount plus your Spirit, and Improved Life Tap adds 20% on top. Drink your Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune on cooldown once you are low enough that none of it is wasted. The health comes from your healers and your Healthstone.

Soul Shards: walk into a raid with a full bag of them. Each Succubus summon, Soulstone, Healthstone and Spellstone costs one, and Portal of Summoning costs five on a one hour cooldown. In Forever, Drain Soul has a chance to give a shard on every target it damages, not only on a kill, so a few Drain Soul ticks on raid trash refill the bag.

Your wand is for when you are out of mana with Life Tap unsafe. A wand shot cannot start during a global cooldown, so it never fits between spells.

Execute and movement: Affliction has no execute spell. Near the end of a fight, stop refreshing any DoT that will not get half its ticks in before the boss dies. Your last Bane of Agony wants most of its 24 seconds, since its big ticks come last. Cast Shadow Bolt instead. While moving, refresh DoTs or Life Tap.

Adds and AoE: Corruption and Agony on two or three adds that live 15 seconds or more beats Shadow Bolt on one of them. Rain of Fire for trash packs that stay put. Hellfire burns you as well, so use it only with the pack on top of you and a healer ready.

## Stat priority

Hit matters up to the cap and adds nothing past it: Suppression already gives you 5% of it, and a missed bane or Corruption is a whole DoT lost. Spell power feeds every spell you cast, and Shadow power feeds nearly all of them, since almost everything Affliction casts is Shadow. Crit is good for Affliction because DoT ticks crit too, and Pandemic doubles the bonus on them. Intellect is mana, plus a little crit: about 1% for every 60 points. Spirit only matters through Life Tap, so let it decide between two otherwise equal pieces. The table above gives the order for your gear.

## Best in slot

The pre-raid set leans on Tailoring and Enchanting crafts.

## Enchants and consumables

Flask and elixirs stack as they always have in Classic, and the school elixirs are separate buffs, so run Elixir of Shadow Power for your Shadow spells and Elixir of Fire Power for your Immolate. Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune have separate cooldowns, so use both, and keep a Healthstone for the Life Taps that leave you low. Put Brilliant Wizard Oil or a Spellstone on the weapon; a Spellstone costs a shard.

## Races

Human and Orc are the picks. Ruthless is 1% crit on every spell, DoT ticks included, and The Human Spirit's 10% more Spirit makes every Life Tap bigger. Gnome is a step behind: Eureka! gives 8 seconds of 10% more damage and 10% lower cost on your direct damage spells every two minutes, but your DoTs get nothing from it, so press it as you start a run of Shadow Bolts; Expansive Mind's 5% more mana helps a spec that taps. Orc's Blood Fury is 10% spell power for 15 seconds every two minutes, and Command makes your demon hit 5% harder for the whole fight: press Blood Fury with your trinkets once the DoTs are up. Troll's Berserking is 10% cast speed on a three minute cooldown, a step behind Orc. Undead is as good as Orc for Affliction: Touch of the Grave gives your spells a chance to drain up to 5% of your maximum health from the target, at most once every 15 seconds, which helps a spec that pays for mana in health.

## Professions

Tailoring and Enchanting: tailored cloth and a couple of enchanted caster pieces make up much of your pre-raid set, and you enchant your own gear. Alchemy is the swap when your guild expects a flask every raid night.

## Leveling

### Levels 1 to 19

Train Corruption at 4, Life Tap at 6 and Bane of Agony at 8. Do the Voidwalker class quest from your warlock trainer the day you hit 10; it is the pet that makes solo play easy. Drain Life comes at 14.

Your points at 10 and 11 go into Improved Life Tap for 20% more mana from every tap, then Suppression from 12 to 16 for 5% hit and 20% less threat. Improved Corruption starts at 17: each point takes 0.4 seconds off Corruption's 2 second cast, and at 21 it is instant. Until then, open with it while the mob is still running at you.

Wands do most of your killing before 20. Get a Lesser Magic Wand at 5 and a Greater Magic Wand at 13, both made by Enchanting, from an enchanter or the Auction House. Take cloth quest rewards with Intellect and Stamina over anything a vendor sells, and replace your starting weapon with the first quest staff or dagger that gives Intellect or Stamina.

### Levels 20 to 60

Affliction is the easiest warlock to level. Put Corruption and Bane of Agony on the mob, then Siphon Life from 30, and let your Voidwalker tank. Drain Life or Wrack while the DoTs run: with three of your effects on the target, Soul Siphon adds 36%, and Wrack costs far less mana than the Shadow Bolts it replaces. Wand the mob down when the DoTs are enough. Cast Drain Soul on the last few percent of a mob's health whenever you need Soul Shards. Soul Harvest's mana regeneration then lets you drink less. Fire every Nightfall proc as an instant Shadow Bolt before its 10 seconds run out. The Succubus comes at 20 and the Felhunter at 30; keep the Voidwalker out until your gear can take a hit.
