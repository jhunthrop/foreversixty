## Overview

Affliction keeps three DoTs and a bane rolling on the boss and fills the gaps with Shadow Bolt, and every one of those ticks can crit in Forever.

### Strengths

- Corruption, Bane of Agony and Siphon Life are instant, so you keep doing damage while you move
- Every DoT tick can crit, and Pandemic doubles the bonus on those crits
- Siphon Life gives you a small, steady heal on top of your damage

### Weaknesses

- Slow start: your first five globals go into putting DoTs up
- Short fights and targets that die fast waste the DoTs you put up
- Life Tap costs health, and a long fight drains it

### In a raid

- **Role:** Ranged Shadow damage, DoTs plus Shadow Bolt
- **Brings:** Curse of the Elements when the raid assigns it to you (one curse and one bane per warlock on each target), Healthstones, a Soulstone on a healer, summons and Portal of Summoning

## Talents and builds

The leveling build is a real Affliction tree: Nightfall, Soul Siphon and Wrack make the drains and the free Shadow Bolts carry you while you quest. The raid build is a Shadow Bolt build that keeps its DoTs up: it stops at 30 points in Affliction and buys Improved Shadow Bolt and Ruin in Destruction.

- **Suppression** (5 points): 5% hit on every spell and 20% less threat. Every warlock build starts here.
- **Improved Corruption** (5 points): Corruption becomes instant and hits 10% harder.
- **Amplify Curse** (1 point): no global cooldown, a 3 minute cooldown and 30 seconds to use it. Your next Bane of Agony deals 50% more.
- **Pandemic** (3 points): Corruption, both banes, Siphon Life, the drains and Wrack crit for double the usual bonus.
- **Nightfall** (2 points): each Corruption, Drain Life, Drain Soul or Wrack tick has a 4% chance to make your next Shadow Bolt instant. It is the leveling pick: free bolts while you move between pulls.
- **Shadow Mastery** (5 points): 10% more Shadow damage on everything you cast.
- **Siphon Life** (1 point): a 30 second DoT that heals you, and one more Affliction effect for Soul Siphon.
- **Soul Siphon** and **Wrack** (4 points): Wrack is a 6 second channel that deals Shadow damage every second and makes the target take 10% more from your Corruption and Bane of Agony. Soul Siphon adds 12% to Wrack and the drains for each of your Affliction effects on the target, up to 36%. Cheap and strong solo, weaker than Shadow Bolt in a raid.

The raid build trades Nightfall, Soul Siphon, Wrack and Soul Harvest for three talents. It also moves the point from Improved Drains to Improved Bane of Agony for 5% more Bane of Agony damage, since a raid rotation casts no drain. Malevolence gives your Shadow spells 5% crit, DoT ticks included. Improved Shadow Bolt makes the target take 20% more Shadow damage from you for 12 seconds after a Shadow Bolt crit; in Forever that debuff is yours alone, not the raid's. Four points of Ruin raise the crit bonus of your Shadow Bolt and Immolate by 80%. Its Demonology points are Unholy Power for your Succubus and Demonic Aegis for Demon Armor.

## Rotation and priority

Before the pull, get set up: Demon Armor on, Succubus out, a Healthstone in your bag, and Life Tap to full mana while the healers top you off. The Succubus costs a shard where the Imp is free, but her melee and Lash of Pain do more damage than the Imp's Firebolt, and Unholy Power makes her hit harder still. Press Amplify Curse a few seconds before the pull. If the pull slips past 30 seconds, it is gone for three minutes, so press it on the countdown, not before.

Once the tank has the boss, put up Corruption, then Bane of Agony while Amplify Curse is still on you, then Siphon Life and Immolate, and Shadow Bolt from then on. If your raid leader gives you Curse of Weakness, put Agony down before it: Amplify Curse goes to whichever of the two you cast first. If you hold the raid's Curse of the Elements, it goes on first and lasts five minutes. Curses and banes have their own slots in Forever, so Curse of the Elements and Bane of Agony sit on the boss together. When another warlock holds Curse of the Elements, leave it to them: two of them on one boss do not stack. Your curse slot is then free for whatever the raid leader asks: Curse of Recklessness for a melee-heavy raid (505 armor), Curse of Tongues on a caster, Curse of Weakness on a boss that hits the tank hard.

Never clip a DoT. A recast throws away whatever was left on the old one, and Bane of Agony saves its biggest ticks for the end. Recast Corruption, Agony and Siphon Life when they fall off. Immolate is the one exception: start its cast with about 3 seconds left. It lands as the old one runs out, and the fresh direct hit is worth the last tick.

Bane of Agony beats Bane of Doom for Affliction. Both crit, and Pandemic doubles both, but Amplify Curse adds 50% to Agony and nothing to Doom, and Agony starts paying at once instead of a minute later. Demonology and Destruction have no Amplify Curse, which is why they run Doom.

Wrack stays off your raid bars. It is a 6 second channel and Shadow Bolt does more in those 6 seconds. Keep Shadow Bolt as your filler.

Life Tap when you drop below about 10% mana, and Life Tap whenever you have to move with nothing to refresh. Each tap converts its base amount plus your Spirit, and Improved Life Tap adds 20% on top. Drink your Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune on cooldown once you are low enough that none of it is wasted. The health comes from your healers and your Healthstone.

Soul Shards: walk into a raid with a full bag of them. Each Succubus summon, Soulstone, Healthstone and Spellstone costs one, and Portal of Summoning costs five on a one hour cooldown. In Forever, Drain Soul has a chance to give a shard on every target it damages, not only on a kill, so a few Drain Soul ticks on raid trash refill the bag.

Your wand is for when you are out of mana with Life Tap unsafe. A wand shot cannot start during a global cooldown, so it never fits between spells.

Execute and movement: Affliction has no execute spell. Near the end of a fight, stop refreshing any DoT that will not get half its ticks in before the boss dies, and Bane of Agony even sooner, since its big ticks come last. Cast Shadow Bolt instead. While moving, refresh DoTs, Life Tap, or fire an instant Shadow Bolt if Nightfall gave you one.

Adds and AoE: Corruption and Agony on two or three adds that live 15 seconds or more beats Shadow Bolt on one of them. Rain of Fire for trash packs that stay put. Hellfire burns you as well, so use it only with the pack on top of you and a healer ready.

## Stat priority

Spell power and Shadow spell power are your stats, with Shadow power almost as good because nearly everything you cast is Shadow. Hit stays valuable right up to the cap shown above, and Suppression already gives you 5% of it. A missed bane or Corruption is a whole DoT lost. Crit is good for Affliction because DoT ticks crit too. Intellect is a small mana gain. Spirit only matters through Life Tap, so let it decide between two otherwise equal pieces.

## Races

Gnome and Troll are the picks. Eureka! gives 8 seconds of 10% more damage and 10% lower cost on your direct damage spells every two minutes. Your DoTs get nothing from it, so press it as you start a run of Shadow Bolts. Expansive Mind's 5% more mana is real for a spec that taps. Berserking is 10% cast speed on a three minute cooldown: press it with your trinkets once the DoTs are up. Orc Blood Fury is 10% spell power for 15 seconds every two minutes, and Command makes your demon hit 5% harder. Undead's Touch of the Grave drains health from your target now and then, which helps a spec that pays for mana in health. Human's Ruthless is 1% crit and The Human Spirit raises Spirit, which feeds Life Tap.

## Professions

Tailoring and Enchanting. Much of your pre-raid gear is tailored cloth, Enchanting makes some of the best pre-raid caster pieces, and you enchant your own gear. Alchemy is the swap when your guild expects a flask every raid night.

## Leveling

Affliction is the easiest warlock to level. Put Corruption and Bane of Agony on the mob, then Siphon Life from 30, and let your Voidwalker tank. Drain Life or Wrack while the DoTs run: with three of your effects on the target, Soul Siphon adds 36%, and Wrack costs far less mana than the Shadow Bolts it replaces. Wand the mob down when the DoTs are enough. Cast Drain Soul on the last few percent of a mob's health whenever you need Soul Shards. Soul Harvest's mana regeneration then lets you drink less. Fire every Nightfall proc as an instant Shadow Bolt before its 10 seconds run out. The Succubus comes at 20 and the Felhunter at 30; keep the Voidwalker out until your gear can take a hit.
