## New race pairs

**Human Hunter** is confirmed by Blizzard as one of six new combinations. Hunter is also available to both factions' Skyborne.

## Talent structure

Talent trees keep the familiar shape: seven rows per tree and 51 points to spend. The one-point "gold medal" talents at 11, 21, and 31 points are still there, and Forever adds a fourth at the 16-point mark, so every tree has four of them. Principal Designer Kris Zierhut confirmed the 16-point addition in an interview.

Talents that existed mainly to unlock a group buff are becoming baseline abilities instead, which frees room in the trees for talents you notice. Some talents are unchanged from 1.12.

One outlet's tally from the demo trees counts 15 new, 16 changed, and 19 unchanged Hunter talents — the most unchanged talents of any class.

## What changed in the demo

**Baseline abilities**, read off level-38 demo spellbooks on stream:

- **Aimed Shot is trainable** rather than a Marksmanship talent, and it is faster: a 2-second cast on a 6-second cooldown shared with Multi-Shot, where Classic was a 3-second cast. It had reached rank 3 by level 38.
- **Aspect of the Beast** now grants 50 melee attack power at rank 1 as well as making you untrackable. In Classic it only made you untrackable.
- **Call Pet is renamed after your pet.** The demo Hunter's read "Call Owl."

**New talents:** Lone Wolf in Marksmanship grants 20% more damage with all attacks while you have no active pet, which makes pet-less Hunter a real build. Summon Hawk is a dive-bomb attack sharing a cooldown with Arcane Shot, with up to two hawks active. Resourcefulness in Survival heavily cuts the mana cost of traps and melee abilities and lets crits allow partial mana regeneration while casting.

**Changed talents:** Endurance Training also grants pet armour. Efficiency's mana cost reduction now covers melee abilities too. Trueshot Aura has moved up the tree and grants ranged attack power only. Entrapment's root is now guaranteed but short, rather than a chance at a longer one.

Hunter also picks up the general itemization change that matters most to the class: hit chance is now one stat across melee, ranged, and spells, which Blizzard says makes traps more reliable.

## Where these come from, and what they are worth

Except where a line is credited to Blizzard, everything in the section above was read off BlizzCon demo footage and show-floor screenshots by fan sites, mostly at level 38, which was the demo cap. Numbers can be wrong, ranks can be misread, and anything can change before launch. Nothing here is datamined.

Associate Production Director Clayton Stone has also said that Forever's launch is a deliberately conservative take after Season of Discovery, and that the team is starting by filling out skills classes did not have rather than reinventing them.
