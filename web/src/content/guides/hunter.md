Blizzard has not announced Hunter-specific talent or ability changes for Forever. Hunter is one of the four default classes available to the new Skyborne race.

Beyond the class being available to Skyborne, and the general talent revamp Blizzard has said applies across classes, no Hunter-specific detail has been announced. This site's leveling routes, talent builds, and gear priorities for Hunter will be written after we play the class in beta. See the [roadmap](/roadmap) for beta dates.
