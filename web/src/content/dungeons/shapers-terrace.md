A dungeon on the slopes of Un'Goro Crater, named by Blizzard as one of the nine new dungeons.

## What is known

- Named by Blizzard in the What's Next panel as one of nine new dungeons at launch.
- Located on the slopes of Un'Goro Crater, per Blizzard's panel recap.
- The level range of 58 to 60 comes from one press roundup and has not been stated by Blizzard.

## What is not known

Bosses, loot, quest givers, and the entrance location. These will be filled in from the beta client after Sept 17.
