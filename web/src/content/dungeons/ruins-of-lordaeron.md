A Horde-side dungeon in the ruins of Lordaeron, where the Forsaken are trying to reclaim the city.

## What is known

- Named by Blizzard in the What's Next panel as one of nine new dungeons at launch.
- One outlet's live log of the panel describes it as **Horde-side**: you help the Forsaken reclaim the city from lingering Scourge and from a necromancer planning an attack on another city.
- The level range of 15 to 20 comes from press coverage of the panel and has not been stated by Blizzard.
- Blizzard has not said which zone the entrance sits in. The ruins stand in Tirisfal Glades in the original world, but that is our inference, not a stated fact.

## What the Deep Dive changed for every dungeon

Blizzard says every dungeon drop has been re-examined, hundreds of new drops have been added, unique dungeon boss items are now rare quality, and set bonuses have been improved. A bind-on-pickup uncommon or rare drop also grants its appearance to every eligible looter, so nobody has to roll against an upgrade just to collect the look.

## What is not known

Bosses, loot tables, quest givers, and the entrance location. These will be filled in from the beta client after Sept 17.
