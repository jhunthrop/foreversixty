A dungeon built around a fight between the Horde and the Alliance for control of the ruins.

## What is known

- Named by Blizzard in the What's Next panel as one of nine new dungeons at launch.
- The level range of roughly 15 to 20 comes from one press roundup and has not been stated by Blizzard.

## What is not known

The exact zone it sits in, bosses, loot, quest givers, and the entrance location. These will be filled in from the beta client after Sept 17.
