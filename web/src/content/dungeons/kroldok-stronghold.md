An outdoor ogre stronghold in the new Riverglades zone, where outrunners are taking locals and something is chanting at night.

## What is known

- Named by Blizzard in the What's Next panel as one of nine new dungeons at launch and described as an ogre fortress.
- One outlet's live log of the panel places it **in the Riverglades** and calls it an **outdoor dungeon**, with ogre outrunners kidnapping locals and strange chanting at night, possibly from cultists.
- **The level range changed on Sept 14.** The panel log gives 40 to 55, recorded off the slide. The roundup we used on Sept 12 gave 35 to 40. Both are single-source; we have switched to the panel log. Blizzard has never published dungeon level ranges.

## What the Deep Dive changed for every dungeon

Blizzard says every dungeon drop has been re-examined, hundreds of new drops have been added, unique dungeon boss items are now rare quality, and set bonuses have been improved. A bind-on-pickup uncommon or rare drop also grants its appearance to every eligible looter, so nobody has to roll against an upgrade just to collect the look.

## What is not known

Bosses, loot tables, quest givers, and the entrance location. These will be filled in from the beta client after Sept 17.
