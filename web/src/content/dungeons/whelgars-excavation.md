An Explorers' League dig above Whelgar's Excavation in the Wetlands that has turned up more than the League expected.

## What is known

- Named by Blizzard in the What's Next panel as one of nine new dungeons at launch. Blizzard's own recap calls it "a mysterious excavation site above Whelgar's Excavation."
- One outlet's live log of the panel adds titan constructs, a strange mist coming down from the mountains, and flashing lights on the horizon.
- The level range of 24 to 29 comes from press coverage of the panel and has not been stated by Blizzard. Two outlets independently give the same range.

## What the Deep Dive changed for every dungeon

Blizzard says every dungeon drop has been re-examined, hundreds of new drops have been added, unique dungeon boss items are now rare quality, and set bonuses have been improved. A bind-on-pickup uncommon or rare drop also grants its appearance to every eligible looter, so nobody has to roll against an upgrade just to collect the look.

## What is not known

Bosses, loot tables, quest givers, and the entrance location. These will be filled in from the beta client after Sept 17.
