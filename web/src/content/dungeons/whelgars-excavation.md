An Explorers' League dig site in the Wetlands, named by Blizzard as one of the nine new dungeons.

## What is known

- Named by Blizzard in the What's Next panel as one of nine new dungeons at launch.
- Located at an Explorers' League excavation in the Wetlands, per Blizzard's panel recap.
- The level range of 24 to 29 comes from one press roundup and has not been stated by Blizzard.

## What is not known

Bosses, loot, quest givers, and the entrance location. These will be filled in from the beta client after Sept 17.
