A dungeon set after the arcane barrier protecting old Dalaran falls, according to Blizzard's panel recap.

## What is known

- Named by Blizzard in the What's Next panel as one of nine new dungeons at launch.
- The level range of roughly 20 to 35 comes from one press roundup and has not been stated by Blizzard.

## What is not known

The exact zone it sits in, bosses, loot, quest givers, and the entrance location. These will be filled in from the beta client after Sept 17.
