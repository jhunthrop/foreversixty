A whole city to explore after the Kirin Tor's arcane barrier comes down and its magic starts misfiring.

## What is known

- Named by Blizzard in the What's Next panel as one of nine new dungeons at launch, set after the arcane barrier protecting old Dalaran falls.
- One outlet's live log of the panel describes enchantments backfiring, constructs going haywire, and a whole city to explore.
- **The level range changed on Sept 14.** A live log of the panel gives 28 to 30, recorded off the slide as it appeared. The roundup we used on Sept 12 gave roughly 20 to 35. Both figures are single-source; we have switched to the panel log because it records what was on screen. Blizzard has never published dungeon level ranges.
- Blizzard has not said which zone the entrance sits in. Dalaran stood in the Alterac Mountains in the original world, but that is our inference, not a stated fact.

## What the Deep Dive changed for every dungeon

Blizzard says every dungeon drop has been re-examined, hundreds of new drops have been added, unique dungeon boss items are now rare quality, and set bonuses have been improved. A bind-on-pickup uncommon or rare drop also grants its appearance to every eligible looter, so nobody has to roll against an upgrade just to collect the look.

## What is not known

Bosses, loot tables, quest givers, and the entrance location. These will be filled in from the beta client after Sept 17.
