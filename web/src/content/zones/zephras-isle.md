A floating island above Azeroth, and the starting zone for the new Skyborne race, levels 1 through 12.

## What is known

- Blizzard confirms Zephras Isle as the Skyborne starting zone, covering levels 1 to 12. Blizzard's own words: an "elemental skybound starting experience," blending Classic Warcraft aesthetics with architecture inspired by **Skywall**.
- It was described on stage as wooden docks in the clouds, with ships anchored by chains to stone spires. One outlet's live log of the panel records it the same way.
- The Skyborne who live there are Highborne who exiled themselves to Skywall, the domain of Al'Akir the Windlord, generations ago. The wind elementals that protected the isle have vanished, ancient structures are crumbling, and the Skyborne are fracturing: some seek answers from the wind spirits, some want their ancestors' arcane traditions back, and, in Blizzard's words, "others have turned to darker powers."
- Blizzard does not count Zephras Isle among the three new leveling zones in its marketing. It is specifically the entry point for the new race.
- Access requires owning one of the paid packs that include the Skyborne race.
- The zone and the Skyborne starting experience were playable on the BlizzCon 2026 show floor.

## What is not known

Full quest list, whether non-Skyborne characters can visit, and how the zone connects to the rest of the world.
