The starting zone for the new Skyborne race, levels 1 through 12.

## What is known

- Blizzard confirms Zephras Isle as the Skyborne starting zone, covering levels 1 to 12.
- Access requires owning one of the paid packs that include the Skyborne race.

## What is not known

Full quest list, whether non-Skyborne characters can visit, and how the zone connects to the rest of the world.
