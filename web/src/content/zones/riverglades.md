A new Eastern Kingdoms frontier zone for characters in the mid-30s to mid-40s.

## What is known

- Blizzard describes Riverglades as a new frontier zone in the Eastern Kingdoms.
- The panel recap reports roughly 150 quests, though this figure has not been corroborated elsewhere and is treated as single-source.
- The level range is reported as mid-30s to mid-40s; this page lists it as 35 to 44 for the level table, and that range is likewise single-source.

## What is not known

The zone's exact borders, its connections to neighboring zones, and confirmation of the quest count from a second source.
