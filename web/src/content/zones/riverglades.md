A new Eastern Kingdoms frontier zone for characters in the mid-30s to mid-40s, built on a piece of the map that was always empty.

## What is known

- Blizzard describes Riverglades as a rugged frontier of rivers, grasslands, trade routes, struggling communities, and old grudges, with more than 150 new quests across much of the mid-30s to mid-40s experience.
- The level range is reported as mid-30s to mid-40s; this page lists it as 35 to 44 for the level table, and that range is single-source.
- One outlet's live log of the panel adds a **neutral goblin port** and places the **Krol'dok Stronghold outdoor dungeon** in the zone. Single-source.

## Where the zone comes from

Lead Game Designer Tim Jones explains the premise: it takes story beats from Warcraft 1, where orcs were chased by humans back across the Red Ridge Mountains into a wild frontier, and reinforcements never came to those settlements. Riverglades shows how they survived a generation later. In his words, "geographically speaking, this place always existed in Vanilla World of Warcraft. It was never a developed space, but that empty space in that part of the Eastern Kingdoms was always there."

## What is not known

The zone's exact borders, its connections to neighbouring zones, and confirmation of the quest count from a second source.
