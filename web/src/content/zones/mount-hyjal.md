An endgame leveling zone on Mount Hyjal, built around the world's recovery after the battle with Archimonde.

## What is known

- Blizzard describes it as an endgame leveling zone.
- It depicts Mount Hyjal recovering after the fight against Archimonde, with new factions to work with.
- It connects to the Hyjal Summit raid and to one of the three entrances into the Barrow Deeps raid.

## How Blizzard approached a zone that was never finished

Lead Software Engineer Ana Resendes describes the team's method as "Found Photographs": you find a few photos taken long ago, they show what the place looked like, but you cannot know what was outside the frame. Traces of Hyjal existed in Classic and the team treats those as the starting point, but for the parts that were never finished it does not feel bound to reproduce modern WoW's version of the lore. Forever, she says, "does not have to retrace the exact history of WoW."

## What is not known

Exact quest count, specific level range, and full faction details. No level range has been given by Blizzard or by press coverage.
