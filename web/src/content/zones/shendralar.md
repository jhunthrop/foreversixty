A new Kalimdor zone sitting between Mulgore and Desolace.

## What is known

- Blizzard and press coverage both place the zone between Mulgore and Desolace on Kalimdor.
- The spelling of the name is inconsistent across sources: Blizzard's own materials and press coverage use both "Shen'dralar" and "Shen'dralas." This site uses "Shen'dralar."

## What is not known

Exact quest count, level range, and how the zone connects to Mulgore and Desolace.
