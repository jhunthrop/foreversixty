Forever itself is included with a normal World of Warcraft subscription or game time. The packs below are optional. Pack contents come from Blizzard's post; the USD prices come from press coverage, since Blizzard's post lists contents but not prices.

| Pack                        | Price  | Includes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Skyborne Heroic Pack        | $29.99 | Skyborne race and the Zephras Isle starting zone, early name reservation, Cerulean Prideclaw ground mount, Shen'dorei Skyseer's Garb, Veteran Adventurer's Rucksack, Windwell toy, housing decor for modern WoW, one Invite-a-Friend launch code |
| Skyborne Epic Pack          | $59.99 | Everything above, beta access from Sept 17, 30 days of game time from Nov 4, a mount, a transmog set, pets, tabards, three Invite-a-Friend codes in total                                                                                        |
| Warcraft Forever Collection | $79.99 | Everything above, Warcraft III: Reforged, the Forsaken Kingdom campaign, and figurine housing decor. Available through Jan 11, 2027                                                                                                              |
| Collector's Edition         | $150   | The Collection as a digital code, a 13-inch statue, mousepad, art prints, pins, and a journal. Physical, ships November 2026                                                                                                                     |

Invite-a-Friend codes grant access from Nov 4 through Nov 11, 2026, and are emailed from Oct 20.

Nothing about the packs changed between the Sept 12 announcement and Sept 14. Three things are worth adding that Blizzard's copy states and press coverage has since clarified.

**You do not have to buy anything to try the beta.** Blizzard opened a free opt-in on the Forever page on Battle.net on Sept 13. Opting in puts you in the pool without guaranteeing a seat. The Skyborne Epic Pack and above guarantee access; the $29.99 Heroic Pack does not include beta access, which has been the most common point of confusion since the announcement.

**Early Name Reservation covers up to three characters**, first come first served, and Blizzard warns that a desired name is not guaranteed. Names in Forever are two-part and unique per region, so there is no realm to fall back on if the one you want is taken. The three-character figure is reported by one outlet and is not in Blizzard's own pre-purchase post.

**The Cerulean Prideclaw still needs the riding skill.** Blizzard's own copy notes that players will need to purchase the riding skill in game to use the mount.
