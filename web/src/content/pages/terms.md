## What Premium and the guild plan are

Premium and the guild plan sell server compute and storage: running simulations on our
servers, keeping raw logs and saved sims longer, and (for the guild plan) tools for a raid
group. Nothing from Battle.net — your characters, your sign-in, anything the game itself
tells us about you — is ever sold or gated behind a subscription.

## Billing and auto-renewal

Premium is $4/month or $40/year; the guild plan is $15/month or $150/year and covers every
member. A subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period until you
cancel. Stripe processes every payment; we never see or store your card.

## Cancelling

Cancelling keeps your access through the end of the period you already paid for. After that,
the account works exactly like a free one — nothing is deleted. Long-retention logs stay at
the two-year retention for thirty days after your subscription ends, then fall back to the
free ninety-day retention.
