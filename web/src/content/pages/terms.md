## What Premium and the guild plan are

Premium and the guild plan sell server compute and storage: running simulations on our
servers, keeping raw logs and saved sims longer, and (for the guild plan) tools for a raid
group. Nothing from Battle.net — your characters, your sign-in, anything the game itself
tells us about you — is ever sold or gated behind a subscription.

## Billing and auto-renewal

Premium is $6 a month or $50 a year; Guild is $15 a month or $150 a year; Raid guild is $25 a
month or $250 a year. Founder prices apply until 6 January 2027, as stated on the pricing page. A subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period until you
cancel. Stripe processes every payment; we never see or store your card.

## Cancelling

Cancelling stops the renewal and keeps your access through the end of the period you already
paid for; a period is paid up front and is not refunded in part (see the
[refund policy](/refunds)). After that, the account works exactly like a free one — nothing is
deleted.

## Price changes

If a price changes, you are told before it affects you, and the new price applies only from
your next renewal after that notice. Nothing you have already paid for is repriced.

## Account termination for abuse

An account or guild found to be abusing the guild plan (for example, an inflated or
fabricated roster meant to split one subscription among unrelated players) may have its
entitlement revoked.
