## What is collected

Your Battle.net sign-in identity; an email address if you sign in with an email link instead;
combat logs and addon exports you upload; and, if you subscribe, a Stripe Customer id. We
never collect, see, or store your card number, its expiry, or your CVC — Stripe holds all of
that directly.

## How Battle.net data is used

Anything sourced from Blizzard's Developer API is kept for at most thirty days, per
Blizzard's own Developer API Terms of Use, and is never sold or placed behind a paywall.

## Stripe

Stripe is our payment processor. Checkout and the billing portal are hosted by Stripe; see
[Stripe's own privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) for what Stripe itself collects
during that process.

## Cookies

`fs_session` and `fs_csrf` are functional cookies only — they keep you signed in and protect
state-changing requests. There are no tracking or advertising cookies, consistent with this
site never running ads, for anyone, ever.

## Data retention

Uploaded combat logs, the reports parsed from them, saved sims and the ratings derived from
them are kept until you delete them or your account; the rankings and a character's history
are built from that record and would be wrong without it. Session rows expire with the
session. If a storage limit ever becomes necessary it will be announced on the changelog
with dates before anything is removed.

## Who is responsible

Forever Sixty is operated by Commish LLC, an Arkansas limited liability company, which is the
party responsible for the data described on this page. Mail reaches it at Commish LLC, c/o
Northwest Registered Agent, LLC, 701 South St., STE 100, Mountain Home, AR 72653, United
States.

## Requests and contact

To see, export, correct or delete what the site holds about you, email
[help@foreversixty.gg](mailto:help@foreversixty.gg) from the address or Battle.net account the
request is about. You get an answer within thirty days, and usually much sooner. Those rights
are yours wherever you live; the site runs no advertising, sells no data and builds no
profiles, so there is nothing to opt out of beyond deleting your account.
