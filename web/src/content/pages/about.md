Forever Sixty is a fan-run reference for World of Warcraft: Forever. It is written and checked by players.

Every fact on the site carries the date it was last checked and where it came from. A source pill says whether a fact comes from Blizzard, from the game client, from verified player reports, or from this site's own work. When only one outlet reports something, the page says so.

The site is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.

To report an error or write a guide, use the links in the footer.
