Skyborne is the new playable race introduced with World of Warcraft: Forever. They are elves descended from Highborne who made a pact with the wind spirits of Skywall, and they live on Zephras Isle.

## Faction is chosen at creation, and the two halves differ

Blizzard is explicit that this is not a neutral race in the Pandaren sense. From the panel transcript: "the Skyborne are not a neutral race. At character creation, players will choose whether their Skyborne joins the Horde or the Alliance."

The split is a story point as much as a mechanic. The Skyborne share an ancestry but, in Blizzard's words, "no longer share a common vision for the future." Horde-aligned Skyborne keep stronger ties to elemental traditions and work with the Earthen Ring. Alliance-aligned Skyborne pursue their ancestors' arcane legacy and work with the Kirin Tor.

## Classes

Six classes across the race, four of them shared:

| Class | Horde Skyborne | Alliance Skyborne |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warrior | Yes | Yes |
| Hunter | Yes | Yes |
| Rogue | Yes | Yes |
| Druid | Yes | Yes |
| Shaman | Yes | No |
| Mage | No | Yes |

Druids get sky-blue forms created specifically for the race. Two hands-on reports from the BlizzCon demo flagged the Shaman and Mage split as something to watch through testing, since a faction-locked class on a single race has no precedent in Classic.

## Racials

Every race in Forever has two active and two passive racials. Skyborne share both passives, and the Alliance and Horde halves differ on the second active.

| Racial | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk on Air | Active | Glide downward through the air for 10 seconds while steering, on a 2-minute cooldown |
| Read Ley Line (Alliance) | Active | 100% increased Health and Mana regeneration for 15 seconds, or 15 minutes if a ley line is nearby |
| Skysight (Horde) | Active | 10% movement and mounted speed for 30 seconds, or 15 minutes near a convergence of elements |
| Wind Blessed | Passive | 1% increased melee, ranged, and spellcasting haste |
| Elemental Insight | Passive | 5% increased damage against Elementals |

Walk on Air, Wind Blessed, and Elemental Insight were seen by two outlets in the demo. Read Ley Line is reported by both, but only one transcription separates the Horde version into Skysight, so the Horde and Alliance difference is single-source and has not been stated by Blizzard.

## Zephras Isle

Zephras Isle is the Skyborne starting zone, covering levels 1 to 12. It is a floating island, described on stage as wooden docks in the clouds with ships anchored by chains to stone spires, blending Classic Warcraft aesthetics with architecture inspired by Skywall.

Its premise: the wind elementals that protected the isle have vanished, ancient structures are crumbling, and the Skyborne are fracturing over what to do about it. Some seek answers from the wind spirits, some want the arcane traditions of their ancestors, and some have turned to darker powers.

Blizzard does not count Zephras Isle among the three new leveling zones in its marketing; it is specifically the entry point for the new race. See the [Zephras Isle zone page](/zones/zephras-isle).

## Lore and scope

Lead Software Engineer Ana Resendes says the Skyborne were created specifically for Forever, with no direct connection to Classic or modern WoW, and there are no plans to add them to modern WoW. Bundles may include mounts or items usable in both modern WoW and Forever, but the race itself is exclusive to Forever for now.

## Price and reaction

Skyborne requires owning at least the Skyborne Heroic Pack, priced at $29.99. It is not available with a base subscription, and the $29.99 minimum to unlock a playable race remains the single largest point of community pushback since the announcement. Nothing about the packs changed between Sept 12 and Sept 14. See the [editions page](/editions).

## What is not known

Full customization options, whether the race will ever be sold separately at a lower price, whether the Shaman and Mage split survives testing, and whether non-Skyborne characters can visit Zephras Isle.
