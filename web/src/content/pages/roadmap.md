Every dated milestone Blizzard has announced for World of Warcraft: Forever, in order.

| Date                    | Content                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sept 13, 10:00 a.m. PDT | Deep Dive panel covering systems and rewards; Hardcore: What's Next panel at 4:15 p.m. PDT               |
| Sept 17 – Oct 21        | Beta, with a level cap of 30                                                                             |
| Oct 27 – Nov 3          | Character name reservation                                                                               |
| Nov 4, 3:00 p.m. PST    | Launch                                                                                                   |
| Dec 9                   | Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit, and Onyxia's Lair open                                                       |
| Winter 2026/27          | Hardcore realms, exact date not yet announced                                                            |
| Spring 2027             | Two raids, two dungeons, new quests and a zone, a legendary questline, and a PvP season                  |
| Summer 2027             | A revamped iconic raid plus one new raid, two dungeons, a PvP season, and professions and Legacy updates |

The beta level cap of 30 is reported by press coverage and has not been restated directly in Blizzard's own text; the rest of this table comes from Blizzard's panel recap.
