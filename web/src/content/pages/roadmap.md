Every dated milestone Blizzard has announced for World of Warcraft: Forever, in order.

| Date                    | Content                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sept 13                 | Deep Dive panel at 10:00 a.m. PDT and Hardcore panel at 4:15 p.m. PDT, both held                         |
| Sept 17, 10:00 a.m. PDT | Live developer Q&A on the Warcraft Twitch and YouTube channels, with a VOD afterwards                    |
| Sept 17 – Oct 21        | Beta, with a level cap of 30                                                                             |
| Oct 20                  | Invite-a-Friend launch codes begin going out by email                                                    |
| Oct 27 – Nov 3          | Early name reservation for pack owners, up to three characters                                           |
| Nov 4, 3:00 p.m. PST    | Launch                                                                                                   |
| Nov 4 – Nov 11          | Invite-a-Friend launch week access                                                                       |
| Dec 9                   | Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit, and Onyxia's Lair open                                                       |
| Winter 2026/27          | The Hardcore ruleset, exact date not yet announced                                                       |
| Spring 2027             | Two raids, two dungeons, new quests and a zone, a legendary questline, and a PvP season                  |
| Summer 2027             | A revamped iconic raid plus one new raid, two dungeons, a PvP season, and professions and Legacy updates |

## Notes on these dates

**There are no realms, so there is no realm list to publish.** Forever is realmless: players pick a ruleset — Normal, PvP, Roleplaying, or Hardcore — rather than a realm. Oct 27 is name reservation only. Names are two-part and unique per region.

**Hardcore is a ruleset, not a set of realms**, and the Sept 13 Hardcore panel gave no date beyond the roadmap's "winter."

**The beta end date is reported two ways.** Blizzard's pre-purchase post says the beta runs through Oct 21, 2026 PST. The roadmap slide shown at BlizzCon reads Oct 22, and Icy Veins reproduces that. Exact start and end times were never given, so the test may run into Oct 22 in other time zones. We list Blizzard's own date.

**Beta access is not paywalled.** A free opt-in is open on the Forever page on Battle.net; it puts you in the pool without guaranteeing a seat. The Skyborne Epic Pack, the Warcraft Forever Collection, and the Collector's Edition guarantee access.

The beta level cap of 30 is reported by press coverage and has not been restated directly in Blizzard's own text; the rest of this table comes from Blizzard's panel recaps and posts.
