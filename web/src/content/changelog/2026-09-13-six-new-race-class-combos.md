The Deep Dive gave the full launch list: Gnome Priest, Human Hunter, Dwarf Shaman, Orc Mage, Troll Warlock and Undead Paladin. Blizzard says more are planned for future updates.

This supersedes announcement-day coverage, which had only Undead Paladin confirmed by cinematic and Dwarf Shaman reported from the demo floor. Night Elf Paladin belongs to retail Patch 12.2 and is not a Forever combination.
