Blizzard's stress test of World of Warcraft: Forever is now on 21 Oct, moved from 19 Oct, and starts at 2 pm PDT in North America (19:00 CEST in Europe). It may end early or run late. The beta is taken offline shortly before it begins and is expected to return shortly after it ends.

When the test ends, every character, name and guild made on the test servers is wiped, ahead of name reservations opening on 27 Oct for pre-purchasers, through 3 Nov.
