The Premium page now lists only what is live today, with the month beside what is coming: character history charts are marked November and Discord notifications December. The guild plan's officer tools read as the loot council helper and the raid readiness board, with more as they ship.

Uploaded logs, the reports parsed from them and saved sims are kept for everyone, with no retention tier on any plan. The terms and the privacy policy now say the same, and the FAQ entry about long-retention logs is gone. The line about comparing more than two builds is retired.
