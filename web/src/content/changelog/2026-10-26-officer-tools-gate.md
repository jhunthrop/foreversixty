The officer tools, the loot council helper and the raid readiness board's Award, Undo and Nudge, are now part of the guild plan. Every guild claimed before today keeps them through the first raid night, until 11 December.

The guild settings page shows what your guild has and until when. An officer of a guild without the plan sees the tools read-only, with a sentence saying how to subscribe.
