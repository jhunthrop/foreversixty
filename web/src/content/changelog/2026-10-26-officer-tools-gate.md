Award and Undo on the loot council helper and Nudge on the raid readiness board are part of the guild plan. Every guild claimed before today keeps them through the first raid nights, until 12 December.

The guild settings page shows what your guild has and until when. An officer of a guild without the plan sees the tools read-only, with a sentence saying how to subscribe.
