Blizzard reworked every race's abilities so each has two actives and two passives, tuned for similar offensive power. Racials that used to grant Weapon Skill now grant critical strike chance with the matching weapon type, which makes them useful to casters as well as melee.

Blizzard's own worked examples are Dwarf and Undead. Stoneform now reduces physical damage taken rather than raising armour, Find Treasure stacks with other tracking, Mace Specialization grants crit, and Big Game Hunter adds damage against Beasts. Will of the Forsaken removes Charm, Fear and Sleep but no longer grants immunity, Cannibalize restores mana as well as health, and Touch of the Grave is new.

The numbers for the other seven races come from outlets that played the BlizzCon demo rather than from Blizzard, and they disagree on a few values.
