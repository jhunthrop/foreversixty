Legacy points come from completing challenges while levelling, such as levelling a particular class or profession. Sixty-five are earnable at launch and each character can spend sixteen of them. Points are earned across the account but spent per character, so a Warrior need not pay for a perk that only a caster wants.

The three launch trees are Adventure, Professions and Resourcefulness. Named perks include Well Rested, Bountiful Harvest and Reagent Economy, which removes the vendor reagent cost from class abilities. A separate Legacy Reward Track grants cosmetics such as a Spectral Bear Cub, and mounts are granted when you learn riding. Blizzard says more trees will follow.

The perk list transcribed from the demo runs to twenty entries and includes two Professions slots whose tooltip reads "To be added in future patch content." It also names a crate turn-in currency called Merchant Favor and two organisations, the Azeroth Commerce Authority and Durotar Supply and Logistics, that Blizzard has not described anywhere.
