Blizzard is hosting a live Q&A with members of the World of Warcraft development team on Thursday, Sept 17 at 10:00 a.m. PDT, on the official Warcraft Twitch and YouTube channels, with a VOD afterwards. Questions are collected in a World of Warcraft forum thread.

It is a World of Warcraft Q&A rather than a Forever-only one, and it falls on the same day the beta opens.
