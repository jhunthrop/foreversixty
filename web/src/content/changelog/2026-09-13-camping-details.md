A Basic Campfire creates a campsite that gives vendors, repairs, profession workspaces and one-hour buffs. You have to sit for a few moments and become rested before the buffs apply, and some parts of Azeroth are better suited to a quiet rest than others.

Up to three crafted objects can be added to a Basic Campfire, one per player, sharing a one-hour cooldown. Every profession has three objects at different skill levels: a Blacksmith's Sharpening Wheel raises Attack Power, a Tailor's Faction Banner raises Spirit, and a Herbalist's Incense Candle raises Intellect. These are variants of the equivalent class buffs and do not stack with them.

The first objects are learned at profession skill 20, from profession trainers or from camping NPCs; advanced objects come from Blueprint recipes that drop from specific dungeon bosses. Alchemists place an Alchemy Lab, Leatherworkers a Tanning Rack needed for advanced recipes, and Cooks can learn upgraded campfires that hold five or ten objects.
