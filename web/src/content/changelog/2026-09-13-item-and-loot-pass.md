Blizzard says every dungeon drop has been re-examined, hundreds of new drops have been added, unique dungeon boss items are now rare quality, and set bonuses have been improved. Quest rewards were expanded so long questlines stop handing casters two-handed swords: Mor'ladim in Duskwood now also grants the Ladimore Heirloom Ring, and Hemet Nesingwary's quests add the Master Hunter's Spellsword.

Trinkets are being pushed toward situational effects, with the Worgenbane Talisman given as an example, and some items now work in specific biomes such as Woodlands, Mountains or Deserts, or against specific creature types. World-drop epics have been improved and hundreds of new rare-creature drops added.

One outlet's live log of the panel also names the Graverobber's Shovel and Sharpened Cutlery as new items, plus updated versions of the Tidal Charm and Whitemane's Chapeau.
