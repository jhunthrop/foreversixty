Premium is $6 a month or $50 a year, one price for everyone. Guilds pick Guild, $15 a month or $150 a year for up to 25 verified members with one Premium seat, or Raid guild, $25 a month or $250 a year for any size with three seats, the loot council helper, the readiness board and consumables. Officers give seats to any verified member and can move each one once every 30 days. A guild's first 7 days are free. Subscribe before 6 January 2027 and you keep the founder price. A guild's history is never locked: a lapsed plan keeps its raids and logs readable and downloadable, and the plan can be handed to another officer.
