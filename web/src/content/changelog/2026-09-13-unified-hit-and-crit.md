Forever combines spell, melee and ranged hit chance into a single hit stat, and does the same for critical strike chance. Blizzard says this helps hybrids such as Retribution Paladins and Enhancement Shaman, and also makes Taunts, Poisons and Traps more reliable for Warriors, Hunters and Rogues.

A new stat reduces the chance for your attacks to be parried or dodged. Weapon skill still works the way it did, but individual items carry less of it so it is no longer the automatic best choice. Bonus healing now also carries one third as much bonus damage and appears on gear as early as level 10, and caster weapons grant spell damage and healing.
