Premium members now see a History section on their own character page, between the Forever Score card and Gear. Score over time draws each day's last rated fight as a dated point on the tier bands, with a labelled gap wherever no fight was rated, and lists the fights behind the selected point. Parse by boss draws the parse of every kill of a boss you pick, with its kills listed underneath. The section includes your private and guild reports, and only you see it.

Free members and visitors keep the sparkline on the Forever Score card, and a free owner sees one line pointing to the plans. The account page's History row now reads Open on your main character for Premium members and Sparkline only for everyone else.
